Key facts

  • The commercial register of the Canton of Lucerne is responsible for the registration of companies
  • The minimum capital for companies in the Canton of Lucerne is CHF 20,000
  • The registration fees for companies in the Canton of Lucerne are 2% of the share capital
  • Insurance obligations for companies in the Canton of Lucerne include liability insurance and professional liability insurance

Choosing the legal form

Companies in the Canton of Lucerne can choose from different legal forms, including limited liability company (SRL), limited liability company (SRI) and partnership. The most common legal form is the SRL, which offers protection for shareholders against company losses.

Registration in the trade register

Registration in the commercial register of the Canton of Lucerne is a mandatory step for all companies. The trade register is responsible for the registration of companies and provides information on registered companies, including name, address and share capital.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the legal forms available for businesses in the Canton of Lucerne?
Companies in the Canton of Lucerne can choose between the limited liability company (SRL), the limited liability company (SRI) and the partnership. The most common legal form is the SRL, which offers protection for shareholders against company losses.
What are the registration fees for companies in the Canton of Lucerne?
The registration fees for companies in the Canton of Lucerne are 2% of the share capital. This means that if the company has a share capital of CHF 100,000, the registration fees will be CHF 2,000.
What are the insurance obligations for companies in the Canton of Lucerne?
Insurance obligations for companies in the Canton of Lucerne include liability insurance and professional liability insurance. These insurances protect the company and its partners against losses and liabilities.
