Operational details

Swiss private life insurance for the border worker: when is it convenient?

The choice to take out a private life insurance policy in Switzerland depends on several factors and could be an important choice for residents at the border, as it offers additional benefits and coverage compared to the third pillar.

First, border workers are entitled to health insurance (LAMal), which covers medical expenses in the event of illness or accident. In particular, LAMal services cover most medical, surgical and dental treatments, as well as hospital admissions and hospitalizations. For example, according to the latest statistics, in 2020, LAMal performance covered around CHF 120 million for general benefits and CHF 30 million for custom benefits.

Another important coverage offered by Swiss private life insurance policies is the reimbursement of medical expenses for family members. This benefit covers basic medical expenses for spouses and children up to the age of 18, as well as specialist benefits and hospital admissions and hospitalizations for the same family members. For example, according to a recent survey, the reimbursement of medical expenses for family members covered about CHF 50 million in 2020.
