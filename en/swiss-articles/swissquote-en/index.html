Swissquote falls sharply on the stock market, weighs on crypto market weakness

Weakness in the bitcoin and other crypto markets has weighed on Swissquote's revenue growth. Revenues rose 2% to CHF 364 million, significantly less than analysts had expected. Pre-tax profit came to 183 million (-1%), while net profits fell by 3% to 154 million.

The Geneva-based company is one of Switzerland's leading online brokers and has a strong presence in the forex and cryptocurrency market. However, the crypto market crisis hit the company hard, which saw its profits fall by 10% in the second quarter compared to the same period a year earlier.

According to analysts, the company was supposed to record a 5% revenue increase in the second quarter, but the weakness of the crypto market weighed on its growth. “The crypto market crisis was deeper than we thought,” said one analyst. “Swissquote has taken a huge hit, but hopefully it can recover in the next quarter.”

The company has sought to mitigate the effects of the crypto market crisis by increasing its presence in the forex market. In the second quarter, forex revenues were up 15% compared to the same period last year. However, this increase was not enough to compensate for the loss of profits in the crypto market.
