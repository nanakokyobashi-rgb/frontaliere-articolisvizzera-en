Naturalization in the Canton of Aargau: requirements and procedure (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

Learn about the requirements and procedure for obtaining naturalization in the Canton of Aargau, including years of residence, integration tests, and taxes.

Context

In brief

  • Ordinary naturalization requires a C permit and 10 years of residence.
  • Integration and language tests are mandatory.
  • Fees vary depending on the municipality.

Key facts

  • What: Ordinary naturalization in the Canton of Aargau
  • When: C permit + 10 years of residence
  • Where: Canton of Aargau
  • Who: Municipalities and the canton
  • Amount: Variable fees

Ordinary naturalization in the Canton of Aargau requires a C residence permit and at least 10 years of continuous residence in the canton. This period can be reduced if you have resided there since youth, with years between the ages of 8 and 18 counting double. In addition to residence, it is necessary to pass an integration test and a language test. Naturalization fees vary depending on the municipality of residence.

Residence Requirements

To obtain naturalization, it is necessary to demonstrate continuous residence in the Canton of Aargau. Years of residence before the age of 10 do not count, while those between the ages of 8 and 18 count double. This means that those who have lived in the canton from a young age can obtain naturalization with a shorter residence period.

Integration and Language Tests

Integration and language tests are mandatory for all naturalization applicants. These tests assess knowledge of the German language and understanding of Swiss culture and laws. It is important to prepare adequately to pass these exams, as their outcome will influence the approval of the naturalization application.

Naturalization Fees

Naturalization fees vary depending on the municipality of residence. It is advisable to inquire at the municipality to find out the exact amount of fees to be paid. The fees cover the administrative costs related to the naturalization procedure and can vary significantly from one municipality to another.

Practical Implications

Obtaining naturalization in the Canton of Aargau has several practical implications. Naturalized citizens can vote and stand for election, access certain social benefits, and have greater stability in the canton. In addition, naturalization can facilitate access to certain jobs and educational opportunities.

The naturalisation procedure in Canton Aargau may vary from other Swiss cantons. For example, some cantons may require a longer period of residence or more stringent integration tests. It is important to compare the procedures and requirements between the different cantons to understand the differences and similarities.
