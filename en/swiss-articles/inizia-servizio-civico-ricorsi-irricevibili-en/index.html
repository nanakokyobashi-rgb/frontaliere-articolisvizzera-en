Civic service initiative, appeals are inadmissible

The Federal Court has ruled that appeals filed by supporters of the 'For a Switzerland that engages' initiative (Civic Service Initiative) are inadmissible. The initiative provided for a compulsory civil service for young people, but the vote was cancelled on 30 November due to serious irregularities. Supporters of the initiative appealed to the Federal Court, but were rejected.

The decision of the Federal Court was taken in the session of February 10, 2024, and is based on the current legislation on referendums and popular initiative, established by the Federal Constitution of April 18, 1999. According to the law, articles 193 and 194 of the Federal Constitution provide that appeals filed against decisions of the Federal Council or the Government concerning referendums and popular initiative are admissible only if filed within 30 days of the publication of the decision.

The decision of the Federal Court was taken after a careful examination of the facts and regulations in force. Supporters of the initiative had filed appeals against the Federal Council's decision to annul the vote, but the Federal Court ruled that the appeals are inadmissible because they were filed after the deadline set by law.
