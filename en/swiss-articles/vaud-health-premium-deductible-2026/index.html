For those living in Switzerland, the guide to KVG health insurance premiums in the canton of Vaud starts with a precise rule: health insurance is mandatory for residents and must be taken out within three months of arrival. For those who arrive in the country during the year, the indicated deadline remains three months from arrival: the comparison begins with the insurance position and the applicable territory, not with a single national tariff.

This distinction also applies to the reading of the offers: geographical reference and year must be consistent, otherwise the comparison between premiums loses value. The 2026 guide is used to sort these items before choosing.

The KVG/KVG system is a private insurance with per capita premiums. It is not a tax and it is not a salary contribution. It should therefore be distinguished in the balance sheet from AHV/AHV and BVG/BVG social security contributions, as well as direct federal tax and VAT. The comparison with the payslip or the costo della vita in Svizzera is clearer when these items remain separate.

The territory changes the comparison

The premiums are linked to the canton and region. For the canton of Vaud, the figure to be used in 2026 must therefore refer to the applicable territory; a national average or the value of another canton does not replace that reference. The average KVG premiums are figures indexed each year: the value useful for the balance sheet is the one updated for the year and area considered.

For adults, the deductible can be CHF 300, CHF 500, CHF 1000, CHF 1500, CHF 2000 or CHF 2500. These are the options to be compared when evaluating a policy. The deductible should not be confused with the premium: they are two distinct elements of the insurance choice.

Then there is the reduction in premiums, referred to as the cantonal subsidy. In the case of the canton of Vaud, the verification must be maintained at the cantonal level, together with the premium linked to the region. In this way, the 2026 guide separates three data that are often mixed: insurance obligation, per capita cost and cantonal support.

Operational details

The national premium comparison 2026 should not be read as a single ranking. The geographical variable comes before the number: a premium referring to a region must be compared with another value referring to the same territorial framework. For a resident of the canton of Vaud, the practical step is to start with the applicable canton and region, then observe the cantonal deductible and reduction.

Three levels to read an offer

The first level is the territory. The premiums per canton and region prevent an average amount from being used as if it were valid everywhere. The average KVG premiums are indexed figures every year: a figure referring to another year should not automatically become the parameter for 2026. The national comparison is useful when it compares homogeneous rules, not when it cancels out cantonal differences.

The second level is the adult deductible. The six available thresholds — CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500 — are the parameter to be added to the per capita premium. When moving from one insurance fund to another, indicating the deductible avoids comparing offers built on different conditions. The choice remains legible only if the threshold is shown next to the premium and the territory, instead of being left implicit.

The third level is the subsidy. The premium reduction is cantonal and should not be treated as an identical national item in every territory. In the canton of Vaud, the support must be checked together with the applicable cantonal situation and the premium linked to the region. This is the item that can affect the overall cost of coverage and should be included in the comparison, not added later.

Separate insurance, taxes and wages

A reliable simulation does not add the KVG to the wage deductions as if it were the same thing. The premium is per capita; AHV/AHV and BVP/BVG belong to social security contributions. Direct federal tax and VAT are also different items. Keeping these categories separate avoids attributing the functioning of a tax or wage levy to the health insurance company and helps to read the cost of living in Switzerland.

For the change of cash insurer, the comparison must use the same three filters: canton or region, adult deductible and cantonal premium reduction. Only in this way can a new offer be read alongside the previous one. The guida LAMal e cassa malati helps to keep the comparison focused on the mandatory coverage and the policy parameters.

Key points

To manage the choice in the canton of Vaud without mixing the entries, it is advisable to follow a short sequence.

1. Set your starting point. If you are a resident and have arrived in Switzerland, consider the obligation to conclude the KVG within three months of arrival. The reference date is the date of arrival.

2. Identify the territory. Indicate the canton of Vaud and the applicable region before reading the premium. Premiums depend on canton and region; using data referring to another area alters the comparison.

3. Put the adult deductibles on the table. Use the six options provided: CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500. Associate each option with the per capita premium of the offer you are reviewing, so the comparison remains readable.

4. Check the premium reduction. The subsidy is cantonal: for the canton of Vaud, the check must be carried out on the correct cantonal reference and kept separate from the policy price. Do not treat the subsidy as an indistinct national rule.

5. Prepare the cash change. Before submitting your request, keep a comparison of the territory, deductible, premium and cantonal reduction. Check the current policy conditions and the applicable official indications, without automatically transferring a choice made to another canton or region.

Final control over the budget

In the balance sheet, record the KVG as a private premium per capita, not as a tax or as a salary contribution. Keep AHV/AHV, LPP/BVG, direct federal tax and VAT separate: they are different items and should not be merged into the same line. The passage also serves to correctly read the cost of living, especially when comparing insurance alternatives and cantonal subsidy.

The average premium figures are indexed each year. For 2026, before closing the choice, use the updated data referring to the canton and region concerned and check the cantonal subsidy available. The procedure remains national in principle, but territorial in application: this is the distinction to be maintained until the application is sent.

Which LAMal franchises are available for adults in 2026?

What should be compared before changing health insurer?
