Hoval Ticino: job opportunities in the technical sector (cross-border guide)

Hoval, a company active in heating and air conditioning, is looking for staff in Ticino. Discover open positions, requirements, and how to apply.

Context

In brief

  • Hoval is looking for staff in Ticino for technical and administrative positions.
  • The company operates in the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning sector.
  • Applications are submitted online via the official website.

Key facts

  • What: Hoval job openings in Ticino
  • Where: Hoval office in Ticino (location not specified)
  • Who: Hoval, a Swiss thermal systems company
  • Sector: Heating, ventilation, air conditioning (HVAC)
  • Application method: Online via company website

Hoval is a Swiss company with a long tradition in the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems sector. The company, part of the international Hoval group, has a strong presence in Ticino and is currently looking for new staff to strengthen its local team.

The open positions mainly involve technical and administrative roles. Among the sought-after profiles are specialized technicians, engineers, project managers, and back-office staff. The company offers permanent contracts with opportunities for internal career growth.

Requirements

To apply for the open positions, Hoval requires:

  • Previous experience in the HVAC sector (heating, ventilation, air conditioning)
  • Knowledge of Swiss regulations on energy efficiency
  • Technical diploma or degree in mechanical/energy engineering
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian; German or French are a plus
  • Willingness to travel within Ticino and occasionally to other Swiss regions

How to apply

Applications must be submitted exclusively through the Hoval careers portal. Unsolicited applications via email or phone are not accepted. The selection process includes an introductory interview with human resources, followed by a technical interview with the department manager.

Operational details

Analysis: What does it mean to work at Hoval Ticino?

Working at Hoval means joining a company that operates in a strategic sector for Switzerland: energy efficiency and climate control. With the rising costs of energy and new federal regulations on building efficiency, the demand for qualified professionals in this field is constantly growing.

Career prospects

Hoval offers structured career paths. Employees can access company-funded professional development courses, with a particular focus on new technologies (heat pumps, hybrid systems, controlled mechanical ventilation systems). For those coming from other cantons or abroad, the company provides support in finding accommodation and managing bureaucratic procedures.

Contractual conditions

The contracts offered by Hoval in Ticino follow the Collective Labour Agreement (CLA) of the metalworking sector or, in some cases, specific company contracts. Compensation is aligned with the standards of the Ticino canton, with the possibility of bonuses tied to the achievement of individual and team goals.

Comparison with other companies in the sector

Unlike small artisan businesses, Hoval guarantees greater stability and growth opportunities. Compared to large multinational corporations, it offers a more familiar environment and greater decision-making autonomy. Employees appreciate the flexibility of working hours and the possibility of hybrid work for some administrative positions.

Practical Guide: How to Apply to Hoval Ticino

Here are the steps to follow to successfully apply for open positions at Hoval in Ticino.

Step 1: Prepare Your Documentation

  • Curriculum vitae in Europass or Swiss format (with a professional photo)
  • Personalized motivation letter for the position
  • Work certificates and diplomas (translated into Italian if in another language)
  • Any relevant training course certificates (e.g. heating system license)

Step 2: Search for Open Positions

Visit the official Hoval website, 'Careers' or 'Work with us' section. Positions are updated periodically. Filter by location: select Ticino or Swiss Italian.

Step 3: Submit Your Application

Complete the online form with your details. Upload your documents in PDF format. Ensure your CV is up-to-date and your motivation letter specifically references the desired position.

Step 4: Prepare for the Interview

If you are selected, you will receive an invitation for an interview. Prepare to:

  • Answer technical questions about the HVAC sector
  • Discuss your previous experience
  • Talk about your language skills (Italian essential, German/French advantageous)
  • Bring any references

Timeframes

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the requirements to work at Hoval Ticino?
The main requirements include experience in the HVAC sector, a technical diploma or degree in engineering, knowledge of Swiss energy efficiency regulations, excellent command of Italian, and willingness to travel within Ticino.
How do I submit an application to Hoval?
Applications must be submitted online through the careers portal on the official Hoval website. Applications via email or phone are not accepted. You need to upload your CV, cover letter, and certificates.
How long does the selection process take?
After submitting your application, Hoval responds within 2-3 weeks. The complete process (interviews and tests) takes about 4-6 weeks. Positions remain open until filled.
Does Hoval offer support for those coming from abroad?
Yes, Hoval provides support for the work visa (L or B permit) and for AVS registration. The company also assists with finding accommodation and handling bureaucratic procedures for those coming from other cantons or abroad.
