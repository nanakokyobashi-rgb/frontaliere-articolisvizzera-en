Operational details

A paradigm shift in relationships. The implications of these scientific findings are profound for those who live with a pet in Switzerland on a daily basis. If in the past training was focused on command and control, today the focus has shifted to mutual understanding. According to Judith Benz-Schwarzburg, an ethologist at the Messerli Forschungsinstitut in Vienna, the gap between human and animal mental faculties is narrowing considerably. Animals today show evidence of a complex inner mental life, characterized by desires, needs and even forms of culture or perception of death, elements that in the past were considered exclusive to humans. This change in perspective forces the Swiss owner to think seriously about the responsibilities that come with owning an animal. The question raised by the research goes beyond education: it asks about the fundamental rights of animals. If the dog or cat are no longer mere tools of companionship but beings endowed with a deep psychic life, how does our duty towards them change? The right to a life free of suffering, the right to life and the right to freedom are themes that, although abstract, condition the daily choices of the owners. Frequently Asked Questions
What is meant by substitution behavior?
Replacement behavior is a signal, such as head scratching, that a dog engages in when it is in a situation of discomfort or difficulty. Correctly interpreting these gestures is essential to understand the emotional state of the animal and intervene before the situation can degenerate or create further frustrations.
Can pets really understand human language?
Recent research, as indicated in the text, shows that dogs can store dozens of names associated with their games and organize them into logical categories. Cats also show surprising abilities, learning to associate words and images with a speed that in certain tests is higher than that of very young children.
Why is paradigm shift in education important?
The shift from obedience-based discipline to collaborative communication reflects the recognition of the mental complexity of animals. Science has shown that a relationship based on listening to the needs of the animal promotes better coexistence, reducing stress and improving the emotional bond between the owner and the pet.
