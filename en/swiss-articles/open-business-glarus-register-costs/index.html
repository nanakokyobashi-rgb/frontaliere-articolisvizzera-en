Operational details

Practical analysis: costs and operational choices

Minimum capital and incorporation costs

The minimum capital for joint-stock companies is set by federal law (Code of Bonds) and does not vary from canton to canton: what changes, from Glarus to Zurich, are notary fees and registration fees in the cantonal register. The latter are determined by the commercial register office of the Canton of Glarus and depend on the legal form and complexity of the deed of incorporation.

For a sole proprietorship, the upfront costs are generally lower: no minimum capital or public deed is required. For a SAGL or an SA, in addition to the federal minimum capital, notary fees, cantonal registration fees and the possible establishment of a occupational pension fund for employees must be provided for.

Tax obligations: three levels

The Swiss tax system is divided into three levels: direct federal tax (DFI), cantonal tax and municipal tax. The IFD is a progressive federal tax, administered under the rules of the AFC and collected by the cantons under federal supervision. The cantonal tax is governed by the tax law of the Canton of Glarus, with a municipal multiplier that varies from municipality to municipality. This means that two companies with the same turnover may have a different tax burden depending on their location.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure to open a business in Glarus

Before registration

1. Choose the right legal form: sole proprietorship, Sagl (GmbH), AG (SA) or another form provided by the Code of Obligations. The choice affects minimum capital, liability and accounting obligations. 2. Check the cantonal requirements: each canton may require specific authorizations for certain activities (professional regulations, licences, permits). 3. Define the registered office: the address must be in Glarus in order to be registered in the cantonal register.

Registration in the Commercial Register

4. Prepare the founding deed: for capital companies a public deed drawn up by a notary is required. For a sole proprietorship, a registration declaration with the required data. 5. Deposit the minimum capital: with a Swiss bank, with issuance of the payment confirmation required for registration. 6. Submit the application to the Commercial Register Office of the Canton of Glarus: with articles of association, founding deed, bank confirmation and identity documents of the founders. 7. Receive the UID number: necessary to operate with federal authorities and for subsequent registrations (VAT, compensation funds, etc.).

Subsequent obligations

8. Registration for VAT purposes (if mandatory or optional) through the competent federal portal. 9. Registration as an employer with the AVS compensation fund, unemployment insurance and SUVA or other LAINF insurer. 10. Registration with the occupational pension scheme if you hire personnel subject to the BVG/LPP. 11. Registration with the cantonal tax office for cantonal and municipal tax, and filing of the DFI/IFD according to federal deadlines.

Frequently Asked Questions
What legal forms can be chosen in Glarus?
Swiss law provides for sole proprietorship, partnership, limited partnership, Sagl (GmbH) and public limited company (SA). The choice affects responsibilities, minimum capital and accounting obligations. For Sagl and SA, the minimum capital is set by the Code of Bonds.
When is registration in the trade register mandatory?
Registration is mandatory for joint-stock companies (Sagl, SA) before the start of business. For the sole proprietorship, the obligation is triggered only when certain thresholds are exceeded, including the annual turnover required by federal law. The cantonal office verifies the documentation and issues the UID number.
How does the corporate tax system work in Glarus?
The Swiss system is divided into three levels: direct federal tax (DFI) administered by the AFC, cantonal tax governed by the tax law of the Canton of Glarus and municipal tax with variable multiplier from municipality to municipality. VAT is federal with uniform rates and must be registered on the portal dedicated to exceeding the established thresholds.
What insurance obligations do those who open a business with employees have?
Employees must register as an employer with the AVS compensation fund, pay AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC, LAINF and, if applicable, LPP contributions. The LPP rates vary by age group: 7% between 25 and 34 years, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54, 18% from 55 years. LAMal is also mandatory for those residing in Switzerland within three months.
What are the procedural steps to open a business in Glarus?
The main steps are: choose the legal form, check the cantonal requirements, define the registered office in Glarus, draw up the deed of incorporation with a notary, deposit the minimum capital with a Swiss bank, submit the application to the cantonal trade register office and receive the UID number for subsequent VAT registrations, compensation funds and tax office.
