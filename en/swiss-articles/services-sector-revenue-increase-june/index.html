Service sector: strong increase in turnover in June

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 1 September 2026

Service sector turnover in Switzerland increased by 6.8% in June compared to 2025.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Service sector turnover in Switzerland increased by 6.8% in June compared to 2025.
  • This is the fourth consecutive month of growth.
  • Commodity trade jumped 20%.

Key facts

  • What: Increase in turnover in the service sector
  • When: June 2025
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: Federal Statistical Office (FSO)
  • Amount: 6.8% growth compared to 2025

The services sector in Switzerland recorded a strong increase in turnover in June, with a growth of 6.8% compared to the same period in 2025. This result, released today by the Federal Statistical Office (FSO), represents the fourth consecutive month in positive territory, after +8.5% in May, +10.2% in April and +3.6% in March. This trend confirms a clear reversal of course compared to -2.7% in February and -0.6% in January.

The strong growth in June was driven in particular by the jump in commodity trade, which registered an increase of 20%. Retail and wholesale also contributed significantly, with a 9% increase, as did the warehousing and transport segment, which saw a 7% growth. At the opposite extreme, hotel and catering activities recorded a decline of 1%.

Operational details

Practical implications

The increase in the turnover of the service sector has significant implications for the Swiss economy. The trade in raw materials, which has seen a jump of 20%, could indicate a higher demand for raw materials at the national and international level. This could have a positive impact on industrial sectors that depend on these raw materials, such as the production of consumer goods and the manufacturing industry.

The retail and wholesale trade, which has seen an increase of 9%, suggests an increase in domestic consumption. This could be due to greater consumer confidence or favorable economic policies. However, it is important to note that the hotel and restaurant activities have registered a decline of 1%, which could indicate a lower demand for tourist or restaurant services.

Comparison with previous months

The month of June saw a growth of 6.8%, which is lower compared to previous months. For example, May recorded an increase of 8.5%, April of 10.2% and March of 3.6%. This trend could indicate a stabilization of the service sector after a period of strong growth. However, it is still too early to draw definitive conclusions.

Future scenarios

If the positive trend continues, the service sector could significantly contribute to the economic growth of Switzerland. However, it is important to monitor the sectors that have registered a decline, such as hotel and restaurant activities, to understand whether the negative trend is temporary or if there are structural factors influencing these sectors.
