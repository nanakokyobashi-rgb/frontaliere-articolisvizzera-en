Bregaglia, compensation for four dismissed nurses (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

CSB awards compensation to four nurses dismissed in February 2025 following a settlement before the District Court.

Context

Key facts

  • Who → Centro Sanitario Bregaglia (CSB)
  • What → compensation for four nurses
  • When → 11 February 2025
  • Where → Bregaglia and Pretura
  • Union → VPOD Ticino
  • Outcome → agreement with compensation, without reinstatement

Four nurses from the Centro Sanitario Bregaglia will receive compensation following the agreement reached before the Pretura. The matter concerns the dismissals announced in February 2025 and results in financial recognition by the CSB.

The source cites Keystone-ATS and reports the statement from the Union of Public and Social-Health Services Employees VPOD Ticino. The ATS acronym therefore appears in the news item together with the VPOD communication, which announced the outcome of the agreement. The case involves a healthcare facility in Bregaglia and four workers, all with long service records.

The sequence of the dispute

The four nurses were individually informed of the termination on 11 February 2025. The VPOD then challenged the dismissals, disputing two aspects of the decision: the manner in which the determinations had been made and the failure to involve staff representatives.

The agreement was reached during proceedings before the Pretura. The point made public is that the CSB recognized compensation for the four workers. The announced outcome therefore concerns payment, without a return to the lost job.

The VPOD considers the agreement partial redress. The settlement highlights the distance between financial recognition and the elimination of the effects of the matter. According to the union, the compensation does not return the lost jobs to the workers and does not erase the months of uncertainty.

“the payment of some compensation is a first step”

This gives rise to the request addressed to the CSB: to assume its responsibilities and reestablish a collaborative relationship with the staff and the trade union representatives. The report therefore presents two distinct elements: the financial outcome of the agreement and the collaborative relationship, which the union is asking to rebuild. Operational details

The practical value of the matter lies not in a new rule applicable to all employment relationships, but in the distinction between three levels: the decision, representation and the outcome of the agreement. The news documents an agreement linked to four dismissals; it does not describe a general change for healthcare personnel or other workers.

Before and after, without conflating the levels

For readers who live or work in Switzerland, the concrete question is what changes after a settlement of the dispute. In the Bregaglia case, the economic outcome changes, while the lost job is not restored. Key points

An operational reading of the case can start from the steps actually described, without turning the agreement into an automatic rule. For someone faced with a termination, the goal is to immediately distinguish the communication received, representation, the dispute and any financial solution.

A five-step working sheet

1. Focus on the communication. In the Bregaglia case, the four female workers were informed individually on 11 February 2025. The date and form of the communication are therefore the first reference point for reconstructing the sequence of events.

2. Clarify the role of the staff. The VPOD's dispute also concerned the failure to involve representatives. In a similar situation, this point must be separated from the assessment of the final decision: it concerns how the decision was made.

3. Involve a representative body. VPOD challenged the dismissals and the matter reached the proceedings before the Pretura. The case shows the transition from individual communication to a dispute supported by staff representation.

4. Define the desired outcome. Compensation and returning to the job are not the same outcome. The CSB agreement includes the recognition of compensation, but according to VPOD it does not restore the lost position: this distinction must be clarified before evaluating a solution.

5. Look at the subsequent relationship. The union's request includes restoring collaboration with the staff and representatives. The financial aspect therefore does not exhaust the organizational issue raised in the note.

These steps help read a concrete dispute without attributing to the case a scope that the source does not assign to it. The Pretura is the venue for the discussion described for this agreement; the central point remains the agreement with compensation and the distance between financial redress and restoration of the position.
