Richemont is facing declining demand for the Montblanc watch brand, based in Le Locle (NE). The company plans to reassign 17 employees to other companies in the luxury group, while 6 employees have been laid off.

The information was reported by Unia to the Awp agency. The union sees the situation at Montblanc as a sign of the crisis affecting the entire watchmaking industry. In its assessment, the pressure is hitting low- and mid-range watches in particular.

Two distinct outcomes for staff

The news describes two different outcomes for staff. The 17 employees affected by the transfer are not described as having been laid off: the text says they are being transferred to other companies belonging to the luxury group. The six employees who were laid off, on the other hand, represent the second measure mentioned in the communication reported by Unia.

The distinction also matters for reading the headline correctly. The number of transfers and the number of layoffs belong to two different measures. It is therefore not correct to describe all the cases as layoffs, because for the 17 employees the text speaks of a transfer to other companies in the group. The six layoffs, on the other hand, refer to the situation at Montblanc, without being presented as a reduction across Richemont as a whole.

The signal highlighted by the union

Declining demand is the starting point of the news. Unia links it to the crisis in the watchmaking industry and points to particular pressure on the low- and mid-range segments. The location given, Le Locle (NE), makes clear that the case concerns that site, but the source does not extend the figures beyond Montblanc.

The picture therefore remains limited in scope: Richemont is dealing with declining demand for Montblanc, arranging 17 transfers and carrying out 6 layoffs. The broader interpretation, that of a crisis affecting the entire industry, is attributed to Unia and concerns chiefly low- and mid-range watches.

Operational details

The practical significance of the news depends on the scale at which it is read. Unia describes it as a sign of the crisis affecting the entire watchmaking industry, but the figures provided concern Montblanc and the Le Locle site. The news does not present 17 transfers and 6 layoffs as statistics for the sector as a whole. For the reader, the figure is therefore a company-specific case accompanied by a union assessment of the sector.
