Neuchâtel transport subscriptions: zones and fares (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

Find out how public transport passes work in the Canton of Neuchâtel: fare zones, half-price, GA and discounts for students and apprentices.

Context

In brief

  • Swiss public transport: zone fare system and national fare communities
  • Annual, monthly, half-fare and GA passes available in all cantons
  • Special discounts for students and apprentices with CartaJuventus and school certificates
  • Ticket validation online, via mobile app or at physical counters

Key facts

  • What: Public transport passes with zone fare system
  • Where: Canton of Neuchâtel, Switzerland
  • When: Current system applicable until 2026
  • Who: Regional and federal transport companies (SBB), fare communities
  • Passes: Annual, monthly, half-fare, unlimited national GA
  • Discounts: Students and apprentices (CartaJuventus and official certificates)

The Swiss public transport system is organized into fare zones defined by distance and geographical area. In the Canton of Neuchâtel, as in the rest of Switzerland, travelers benefit from a coherent fare structure that simplifies the planning of daily trips.

Fare communities form the basis of the organization of Swiss public transport. These associations coordinate fares among different operators (regional trains, buses, trams, boats), ensuring fare uniformity and facilitating transfers between different modes of transport without administrative complications. In the Canton of Neuchâtel, the same fare principles and rules apply as in all other Swiss cantons, with zones extending based on the distance traveled.

How fare zones work

Zones are defined by distance bands: each ticket or pass covers a certain number of zones. A traveler moving within one or two zones pays a lower fare than someone covering greater distances. This system encourages the purchase of passes for regular trips and reduces the overall cost of mobility in the medium to long term.

Operational details

Half-price, GA and fare combinations

The national half-price (Halbtax/Demi-tarif) is a subscription that reduces by 50% all subsequent tickets purchased. Many Swiss travelers choose to activate the half-price (at a fixed annual cost) because the savings quickly amortize with a few trips. In the Canton of Neuchâtel, the half-price combines with any regional subscription and works on all lines operated by the local fare community.

The GA (General-Abonnement) is the most ambitious unlimited subscription: it allows you to travel freely on ALL Swiss public transport (trains, buses, trams, boats, cable cars in many regions) without geographical zone limits. Those who own a GA do not need to purchase additional tickets for any national journey. In the Canton of Neuchâtel, the GA represents a solution for those who travel frequently both within the canton and to other Swiss regions (for work, study, leisure, business).

The combination of half-price + zonal subscription is very common: the traveler purchases the annual subscription for their main travel zone (home-work) and activates the half-price for occasional trips beyond that zone. Some travelers choose the GA alone if their trips are intense and varied across the entire Swiss territory, considering that the GA offers cost predictability and eliminates the problem of fare choice.

Key points

Purchase and validation procedure in 5 steps

Step 1: Identify the main travel zone. Consult the zone map on the cantonal transport company's website or via the app. The zones are numbered and color-coded for easy reading. In the Canton of Neuchâtel, most commuters travel within 2-3 zones.

Step 2: Choose the type of subscription. If the trips are daily (home-work, home-school), an annual subscription is the most economical solution. If the trips are occasional or sporadic, a monthly subscription is better. Evaluate whether the half-price ticket is convenient based on your mobility habits.

Step 3: Check the requirements for discounts. If you are a student or apprentice, prepare the official enrollment certificate. If you intend to purchase a CartaJuventus, check the maximum age (usually up to 25 years) and eligibility based on the type of school or training.

Step 4: Purchase the subscription. The options are:

  • Online through the cantonal transport company's portal (faster, with paper or digital delivery)
  • At the main station counter (direct assistance, immediate validation)
  • Through the official mobile app (for digital subscriptions, instant validation)

Step 5: Validate the ticket before each trip. Depending on the type of subscription:

  • Paper subscription: swipe the card in the validator installed on board the vehicle
  • Digital subscription (app): show the ticket on the smartphone screen to the inspector
  • CartaJuventus: present the physical card or the digital QR code during the check

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between a zone subscription and the GA?
A zonal subscription covers movements within one or more specific areas and has a cost proportional to the areas chosen. The GA (General-Abonnement) allows unlimited travel on ALL Swiss public transport without zone restrictions. The GA is more convenient for those who travel frequently over long distances or in multiple cantons; the zonal subscription is ideal for those who have regular trips within a limited area (e.g. home-work).
Can I combine the half-price with a zonal subscription?
Yes, it is a common and often convenient combination. Half-price reduces the cost of individual tickets purchased beyond the zone subscription by 50%. If, for example, you have a "1 zone" subscription but occasionally move to 2 zones, the half-price allows you to pay for the additional ticket at a reduced price. Evaluate your trips well: if the "extra" trips are few per month, the half-price may not be convenient economically.
How do I claim a student or apprentice discount?
Present an official school enrollment certificate (for students) or an apprenticeship certificate (for those attending vocational training) at the counter where you purchase the subscription. You can also apply for the CartaJuventus, which provides a 25-50% discount on many travel tickets and subscriptions. The verification also takes place via the official app, where your student status is digitally confirmed by the educational institution.
What happens if I don't validate my membership before boarding?
Failure to validate is considered travel without a valid title. If checked during the trip, you will face a sanctioning procedure. Paper subscriptions must be swiped in the validator installed on board; digital subscriptions must be shown on the smartphone screen to the controller. For continuous annual subscriptions, the validation takes place only once (upon initial activation), then the title remains valid for the entire period.
Can I also use my Neuchâtel subscription in other Swiss cantons?
It depends on your subscription type and pricing communities. A Neuchâtel zonal subscription does not automatically allow travel to neighbouring cantons (e.g. Bern, Vaud), unless it is specifically an "intercantonal subscription" purchased specifically for regional mobility. The GA (General-Abonnement), on the other hand, operates throughout Switzerland without restrictions. For occasional trips outside the canton, half-price + single tickets is often the most convenient solution.
