Production and import prices: +0.7% in August 2026 (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 14 September 2026

The producer and import price index rises to 100.4 points in August 2026. On an annual basis, there is a decrease of 0.7% according to UST data.

Context

In brief

  • Producer and Import Price Index: +0.7% compared to July 2026
  • Index level: 100.4 points (base Dec. 2025 = 100)
  • Annual decrease: -0.7% compared to August 2025
  • Data published on September 14, 2026, by the FSO

Key facts

  • Publication date: September 14, 2026
  • Monthly variation: 0.7%
  • Index value: 100.4
  • Annual variation: -0.7%
  • Data source: FSO

Neuchâtel, September 14, 2026. The Federal Statistical Office (FSO) has released updated data on producer and import price trends in Switzerland. The official press release, dated September 14, 2026, reveals that in August 2026, the total producer and import price index recorded an increase of 0.7% compared to the previous month. This increase brings the index level to 100.4 points, using December 2025 as the reference base, set at 100 points.

Market dynamics

The monthly rise was primarily driven by specific industrial and energy sectors. FSO data show a particular increase in the prices of petroleum products, chemical products, as well as those related to crude oil and natural gas. Specifically, the increase in the producer price index compared to the previous month is mainly due to higher prices for petroleum products and pharmaceutical preparations. Price increases were also observed for slaughter pigs, pork, raw milk, rubber and plastic products, as well as plastics in primary forms.

However, the picture is not uniformly positive for costs. The FSO reports a decrease in the prices of basic pharmaceutical products, other chemical products, and electricity for large consumers. On the import side, compared to July 2026, the import price index recorded higher prices especially for petroleum products, organic products of the chemical industry, crude oil, and natural gas. Increases were also recorded for plastics in primary forms, vegetables, melons and potatoes, rubber and plastic products, inorganic products of the chemical industry, other chemical products, paper and paper products, and green coffee. Conversely, a drop in computer prices was observed.

On a trend level, the situation shows stabilization. Compared to August 2025, the price level of the total supply of domestic and imported products fell by 0.7%. This figure indicates that, despite the monthly inflationary push, national price pressure remains below the level recorded a year ago. Full results and further information on the subject can be found on the FSO website, which is the official source for these crucial economic indicators for the Swiss economy. For those operating in the manufacturing or commercial sector, monitoring these variations is essential for planning profit margins and procurement strategies at a sustainable cost of living.

Frequently Asked Questions
What was the change in prices in August 2026?
According to official data published by the Federal Statistical Office (FSO) on 14 September 2026, the total index of producer and import prices recorded an increase of 0.7% compared to July 2026. This increase brought the index to a level of 100.4 points, based on the month of December 2025 set at 100. Despite this monthly growth, the comparison on an annual basis shows that the price level decreased by 0.7% compared to August 2025.
Which sectors drove the monthly rise in the index?
The 0.7% increase recorded in August 2026 was mainly driven by petroleum products, chemicals, crude oil and natural gas. Specifically, for producer prices, increases were noted for pharmaceutical preparations, raw milk, pork and pigs for slaughter. On the import front, higher prices were also recorded for vegetables, potatoes, green coffee, paper and paper products, as well as plastics in primary forms.
Were there any products that recorded a decrease in prices?
Yes, despite the general increase, the UST reported price declines in some specific areas. With regard to domestic production, a decrease was observed for basic pharmaceuticals, other chemical products and electricity destined for large consumers. With regard to the import index, compared to July 2026, there was a significant drop in computer prices, offering a partial counterweight to energy and chemical increases.
