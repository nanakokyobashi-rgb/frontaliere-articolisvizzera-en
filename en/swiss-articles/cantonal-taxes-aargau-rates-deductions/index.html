Aargau Cantonal Taxes: Rates and Deductions (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 2 September 2026

Find out how cantonal and municipal taxes work in the Canton of Aargau, including progressive tax rates, allowable deductions, and how to file your taxes online. A practical guide.

Context

At a Glance

  • Swiss cantonal taxes are divided into three levels: federal, cantonal, and municipal
  • In the Canton of Aargau, each municipality has its own multiplier for cantonal tax
  • Allowable deductions vary by canton and municipality
  • The Aargau online portal allows for digital tax filings

Key Facts

  • What: Three-tier tax structure (federal + cantonal + municipal)
  • Where: Canton of Aargau, northeastern Switzerland
  • Who: Aargau Cantonal Tax Administration, local municipalities
  • Deadlines: Annual tax return (dates vary by canton)
  • Deductions: Professional expenses, mortgage interest, insurance premiums, AVS/LPP contributions

In Switzerland, the tax system is organized into three distinct levels: direct federal tax (IFD), cantonal taxes, and municipal taxes. The Canton of Aargau, located in northeastern Switzerland, follows this national framework, with each municipality responsible for collecting municipal tax by applying a multiplier to the cantonal tax rate.

In the Canton of Aargau, as in all Swiss cantons, the cantonal tax system allows for specific deductions from taxable income: professional expenses, mortgage interest, health insurance premiums, and contributions to pension plans (AVS, LPP, third pillar 3a). The progressive tax bracket structure ensures that the tax increases gradually with income, without sudden jumps.

How the Municipal Multiplier Works

Each municipality applies its own multiplier to the cantonal tax rate, allowing for local flexibility in tax collection. A multiplier of 100 means the municipality collects 100% of the cantonal tax rate; values greater than 100 indicate an additional municipal tax. This structure allows Aargau municipalities to finance local services while maintaining a uniform tax base with the canton.

Operational details

Allowable Deductions and Their Impact

The deductions allowed in the Canton of Aargau reduce taxable income and, consequently, the total amount of tax due. Among the main deductions are professional expenses (costs directly related to work, supplies, work-related transportation, and professional training), mortgage interest (generally deductible on real estate loans), insurance premiums (especially health and supplemental insurance), and social security contributions (payments to AVS/AHV, LPP/BVG, and the third pillar 3a with an annual limit).

The value of a deduction depends on the marginal tax rate: a higher-income worker benefits more from a CHF 100 deduction than a lower-income worker, because each franc saved falls into a higher tax bracket.

Practical Scenarios: Before-and-After Comparison of Deductions

Scenario A: An employee in the Canton of Aargau with a gross annual income of CHF 65,000 deducts CHF 5,000 in mortgage interest and CHF 3,000 in LPP contributions (7% of the coordinated salary, example for the 25–34 age group). Taxable income: CHF 57,000. If the average cantonal tax rate (including the municipal multiplier) were approximately X%, the tax calculated on CHF 57,000 would be lower than that on CHF 65,000.

Scenario B: The same employee without itemized deductions pays the full tax on CHF 65,000, without any tax reduction benefits. The difference between the two scenarios is why it is crucial to track all deductible expenses and report them correctly on the tax return.

Tax Filing Procedure in the Canton of Aargau

To file your cantonal taxes in the Canton of Aargau, follow these steps:

Step 1: Gather Your Documents Gather your wage statements (pay stubs for the year, bank statements for other income), deduction documents (mortgage interest invoices, LPP/third-pillar account statements, insurance receipts, and business expense documents).

Step 2: Access the online portal Visit the official website of the Aargau Cantonal Tax Administration. The platform provides access to the interactive tax return form and documentation on allowable deductions for the current tax year. Registration requires a taxpayer ID number (provided in previous tax documents) and online access.

Step 3: Filling out the tax return Enter your total income, allowable deductions by category, and pension contributions paid. The portal automatically calculates your taxable income based on the canton’s progressive tax brackets and the municipal multiplier specific to your municipality of residence.

Step 4: Review and Submission Review the summary and verify the automatic calculations (applied tax rates, tax brackets). Submit your return digitally by the annual deadline. The system will provide a confirmation receipt.

Step 5: Receiving the Tax Assessment The cantonal tax administration will issue the tax assessment notice within a few months of the deadline. If refunds are due, they will be credited to your bank account within the specified timeframe. If an additional payment is due, you will receive payment instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions
In the Canton of Aargau, how does the municipal multiplier work?
The municipal multiplier is a factor applied to the cantonal rate that allows the municipality to modulate its tax rate. A multiplier of 100 corresponds to 100% of the cantonal rate; higher values mean an additional local levy. Each municipality in Aargau has its own multiplier, visible on the online portal of the tax administration.
What deductions can I claim on my Aargau tax return?
In the Canton of Aargau deductions are allowed for professional expenses, mortgage interest, insurance premiums (including LAMal), social security contributions (AVS/LPP/third pillar 3a). Check the detail on the official portal, as some deductions have specific limits or conditions. Supporting documents must be kept for any administration checks.
When is the tax filing deadline in the Canton of Aargau?
Deadlines vary annually. Generally, the return must be submitted by the end of May, but it is essential to consult the official tax calendar of the Canton of Aargau for the current tax year. Delays without authorisation are subject to sanctions. Requests for extensions must be forwarded to the cantonal tax administration before the deadline.
How do I access the Aargau tax administration's online portal?
The portal is accessible from the official website of the Tax Administration of the Canton of Aargau. Registration requires Swiss taxpayer number (obtainable in previous declarations) and digital identification (e.g. SwissID). Once authenticated, you can download forms, complete the online return, and track the status of your taxation.
How much tax will I have to pay if I have an average income in the Canton of Aargau?
The amount depends on gross income, deductions, progressive cantonal rates (which vary by tier) and municipal multiplier. It is not possible to give a precise amount without the specific data. Use the online calculator or contact the cantonal tax administration desk for a personalised estimate before filling out the return.
