Key points

Opening a business in the Canton of Uri: choice and obligations

Choice of legal form

To open a business in the Canton of Uri, you need to choose the legal form that best suits your needs. There are several legal forms available, including:

  • Limited liability company (SRL)
  • Limited liability company (SRI)
  • Sole proprietorship
  • Cooperative societies

The choice of legal form depends on the type of business, the number of members and financial needs. It is advisable to consult a lawyer or financial advisor to determine the most suitable legal form.

Registration in the commercial register

Registration in the trade register is a mandatory step to open a business in the Canton of Uri. The business owner must submit an application for registration in the commercial register of the Canton of Uri, accompanied by a copy of the declaration of commencement of business.

The application for registration must be submitted within 30 days from the date of commencement of the business. The registration fee is CHF 500 for companies and CHF 200 for sole proprietorships.

Minimum capital

The minimum capital to open a business in the Canton of Uri is CHF 20,000. The capital must be deposited with a Swiss bank and must be sufficient to cover the initial expenses of the business.
