Swiss Chambers: Olympics, bilateral agreements, asylum and ships

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 October 2026

The National Council examines 8,24 million for sport and 200 million for the 2038 Olympics. The Council of States discusses Bilaterals III, ships and asylum.

Context

In brief

  • The National examines 8.24 million for sporting events
  • 200 million for the 2038 Winter Olympics under discussion
  • States will vote on the credit for Erasmus+ in Bilateral III
  • Five motions in an extraordinary session on asylum

Key Facts

  • Chamber → National Council
  • Sports credit → CHF 8.24 million
  • Period → 2027-2029
  • Olympics → federal contribution of CHF 200 million
  • Bilateral III → commitment credit for Erasmus+
  • Navigation → Swiss flag with owner company abroad
  • Asylum → extraordinary session and five motions

The National Council will open the day with the examination of a credit of CHF 8.24 million intended to support international sporting events taking place in Switzerland in the period 2027-2029. The afternoon session will resume discussions in both houses of parliament.

After the examination, the Chamber will vote on the federal contribution to the costs of the 2038 Winter Olympics. The sum indicated is 200 million francs. The order of business therefore combines a credit referring to the period 2027-2029 and a contribution linked to 2038.

The National Council's programme

Also on the agenda is a draft law prohibiting the payment of severance pay to executives in the Federal Administration and semi-public companies. The planned prohibition will not apply to terminations that are not attributable to the employee, for example those related to a reorganization. The rule therefore contains an exception related to the cause of termination.

The Council of States, on the other hand, will complete the examination of the so-called Bilateral III, which began on Monday. In particular, the senators will have to vote on the commitment credit for Switzerland's participation in the Erasmus+ programme. The vote is indicated as one of the steps of the current examination.

The Chamber of Cantons will then continue with the amendment of the Federal Act on Maritime Navigation under the Swiss flag. One of the main innovations is that the shipowner will be able to fly the Swiss flag even when the owner company is based abroad. The aim is to facilitate access to modern financing models, in particular leasing and bank financing by foreign partners.

Finally, the states will hold an extraordinary session dedicated to asylum, with five motions under discussion. Among these is that of Marco Chiesa, UDC/TI, who calls for the rejection of the EU's amendment to the rules on compensation for unemployed cross-border workers. The agenda thus includes credits, draft laws, Erasmus+, maritime navigation and asylum.

Operational details

The practical weight of dossiers

For a reader who wants to understand the scope of the session, the practical point is to separate the dossiers instead of treating them as a single decision. The agenda brings together sports spending, rules on departure allowances, bilateral relations, maritime navigation and asylum, but for each topic it indicates a different step: examination, vote, continuation of work or extraordinary session.

Key points

How to read the agenda

To transform the agenda into a personal reading sheet, it is advisable to follow a fixed sequence and remain adherent to the parliamentary passage indicated for each dossier.

Five operational steps

1. Separate the chambers. In the first block, mark the topics of the National Council: credit for sporting events, contribution to the Winter Olympics and project on starting allowances. In the second block, place Bilateral III, maritime navigation and extraordinary session on asylum, all of which refer to the Council of States.

2. Classify the planned action. Next to each topic, note whether the source speaks of examination, vote, continuation of work or discussion of motions. This distinction helps not to confuse a bill with a vote or a motion with an amendment already described as definitive.

3. Record amounts and periods without merging them. The statement must show CHF 8.24 million for international sporting events in 2027-2029 and CHF 200 million for the federal contribution to the 2038 Winter Olympics.

4. Note exceptions and objectives. For severance indemnities, the exception of terminations not attributable to the employee, such as those related to a reorganization, must be retained. For shipping, both the possibility of Swiss flagging with an owner company abroad and the objective of encouraging foreign leasing and bank financing should be reported.

5. Isolate the motion on cross-border workers. In the asylum blockade, the specific reference is Marco Chiesa's request to reject the EU's amendment to the rules on compensation for unemployed cross-border workers. The motion is one of five discussed in the extraordinary session.

This scheme makes it possible to compare dossiers without mixing the period 2027-2029 with 2038 and without overlapping maritime navigation with the issue of asylum. For those who follow cross-border work, the point to be kept is the precise wording of the motion and its subject: the EU amendment of the rules on compensation for unemployed workers.

The same criterion applies to companies interested in navigation: the source presents both the condition relating to the owner company and the financial objective linked to leasing and foreign banks. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the first appropriation examined by the National Council?
The National Council first examines a credit of 8.24 million francs intended to support international sporting events that will take place in Switzerland during the period 2027-2029. Subsequently, the chamber will vote on the federal contribution to the costs of the 2038 Winter Olympics, indicated in the source as amounting to 200 million francs.
Who would the ban on severance payments apply to?
The bill prohibits the payment of severance allowances to the senior officials of the Federal Administration and parastatal companies. The ban does not apply to terminations not attributable to the employee, including those related to a reorganization. The text therefore considers both the scope of the senior officials and the reason for the termination.
What new development concerns the Swiss flag?
The amendment to the Federal Maritime Navigation Act provides that a shipowner may fly the Swiss flag even if the owning company is based abroad. The stated objective is to facilitate access to modern financing models, particularly leasing and bank financing granted by foreign partners.
What does Marco Chiesa's motion request?
During the special session devoted to asylum, five motions will be discussed. Among them is the one by Marco Chiesa, UDC/TI, which calls for rejecting the EU's amendment to the rules concerning compensation for unemployed cross-border workers. The source presents this request as one of the topics of the Council of States.
