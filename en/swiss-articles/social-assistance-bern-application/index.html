Social assistance in Bern: requirements and application

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 October 2026

In the Canton of Bern, social assistance is based on need: application to the competent office, documents, and deadlines to be verified with the Canton.

Context

TL;DR

  • Social assistance is based on need.
  • Cantons and municipalities generally administer it.
  • In Bern, the office, documents and deadlines must be checked.

Key facts

  • Criterion → need
  • Administration → Cantons and municipalities
  • Application → competent municipal or cantonal office
  • Verification → official source of the Canton of Bern

In the Canton of Bern, social assistance is generally administered by Cantons and municipalities according to need. For anyone who has to submit an application, the first point of reference is therefore the competent municipal or cantonal office; requirements, documents, deadlines and amounts must be verified using the official source of the Canton.

The criterion of need

The stated criterion is a situation of need, with administration organized at cantonal and municipal level. For this reason, the application must be addressed to the office handling the case, without replacing the procedure with generic information taken from other areas of public administration. The office’s specific authority, together with the required documentation, must be checked before submission.

The main federal bodies also have distinct responsibilities. UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS/AHV, AI and LPP/BVG; UST/BFS produces statistics and does not set rates. AFC/ESTV deals with direct federal tax and VAT, while cantonal administrations manage cantonal and municipal taxation. This separation helps prevent an application for social assistance from being confused with a tax, social security or statistical matter.

Items affecting the budget

LAMal/KVG is another separate matter to keep in mind: for residents, health insurance is mandatory and must be taken out within three months of arrival. Premiums are per capita, vary according to canton and region, and are neither a tax nor a wage contribution; premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy. The guide to cassa malati is available for further information on the insurance aspect.

The general framework therefore remains clear: social assistance is linked to need, ordinary administration is handled by Cantons and municipalities, and the application is initiated at the competent office. The operational rules to use for Bern are those published by the Canton, especially to understand which documents to submit and by what deadlines. There is no need to automatically transfer into the application the rules governing direct federal tax, AVS/AHV or LAMal/KVG: these are different systems, with different authorities and purposes.

Within the framework described, the word requirements does not refer to a single list to be applied indiscriminately. It identifies the conditions and checks associated with the competent channel in the Canton. The same applies to the application: the point of access is municipal or cantonal, while the practical framework is defined by the office and the official source of Bern.

Operational details

For those living in the Canton of Bern, the difference between the various channels changes how the budget is prepared, but does not replace the needs-based criterion. In a hypothetical scenario, a person who cannot cover their expenses must distinguish at least three areas: financial hardship, insurance premiums and social deductions from their salary. Confusing them may lead to contacting the wrong office or using amounts belonging to another system.

Disposable income and benefits do not coincide

If the person works, the payslip lists separate items. AVS/AI/IPG amount to 5.3% for the employee and 10.6% overall including the employer. AD/AC is indicated at 1.1% up to the annual ceiling, while LAINF/LAA varies from 0.7% to 1.5% depending on the sector. For LPP/BVG, on the coordinated salary, the indicated rates are 7% between the ages of 25 and 34, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54 and 18% from age 55 until the reference age. These are items to be considered separately from social assistance. The guide to busta paga svizzera can be used to compare deductions.

In the personal budget, LAMal/KVG must also be recorded as a separate item. It is mandatory for residents, provides per-capita premiums differentiated by canton and region, and is neither a tax nor a payroll contribution. The reduction in premiums is a cantonal subsidy. This distinction avoids confusing an insurance cost with an application for social assistance, without anticipating which requirements are applied by the competent office.

Taxation also follows a separate logic: in Switzerland there are three levels, direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each Canton has its own law and its own multiplier; the municipality applies the multiplier to the cantonal tax. AFC/ESTV concerns direct federal tax and VAT, while the cantonal administrations handle cantonal and municipal taxes. This information serves to classify an item correctly and reconstruct expenses, not to replace the social assistance channel.

Key points

A well-organized application is built by following the scope indicated by the Canton, without replacing it with a standard list valid everywhere. The process can be divided into four steps: identify the office, verify the conditions, prepare the required documents, and comply with the cooperation obligations.

From verification to submission

1. Identify the point of access. For the Canton of Bern, the relevant contact is the competent municipal or cantonal office. Before submitting the request, check which of the two channels handles the case and use the contact details published by the Canton's official source.

2. Check the scope. The condition of need is the criterion specified for social assistance. On the official source, verify the applicable requirements, deadlines, documents, and amounts. Do not use figures taken from another Canton or from a different year.

3. Prepare the documentation. Providing the required documents is part of cooperating with the office. The procedure is not completed by replacing the official list with a generic template: each document must be selected based on the instructions received and submitted within the prescribed deadline.

4. Keep the related channels separate. If you are a resident, LAMal/KVG must be taken out within three months of arrival; the premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy. The AVS/AHV, AI, LPP/BVG, and AD/AC items that appear on the payslip help assess the budget, but do not turn the application into a social security or tax procedure.

The verification of figures requires caution. The maximum amounts for pillar 3a, the threshold and coordination deduction for LPP, the AD/AC maximum, average LAMal premiums, and the median salary are indexed every year. When the application requires an up-to-date financial overview, it is preferable to verify the year indicated by the official source instead of reporting uncertain amounts. The same principle applies to any cantonal rules on benefits and services: requirements, deadlines, and amounts must be checked at Canton level.

This method keeps the channels separate and directs the request to the indicated office, with documents and checks in line with cantonal rules. To organize income and deductions before examining the budget, use calcolatore stipendio.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who administers social welfare in the Canton of Bern?
In Switzerland, social assistance is generally administered by cantons and municipalities according to the condition of need. For an application in the Canton of Bern, the operational reference is the competent municipal or cantonal office. The Canton's official source is used to verify applicable requirements, documents, terms and amounts. The practical organization of the practice must therefore be controlled at the cantonal or municipal level.
What documents are needed for the application?
The application must be prepared with the documents indicated by the competent office. The list should not be replaced with generic models: requirements, terms and documentation must be verified at the official source of the Canton of Bern. The applicant must also comply with the cooperation obligations provided for by the procedure and provide what is required by the competent authority.
Does social care and LAMal/KVG coincide?
No. LAMal/KVG is the mandatory health insurance for residents and must be taken out within three months of arrival. Premiums are per capita, vary by canton and region, and are neither a tax nor a wage contribution. The reduction of premiums is a cantonal subsidy, distinct from the application for social assistance.
Which bodies have competences other than social assistance?
UFAS/BSV covers social security, including AVS/AHV, AI and LPP/BVG. AFC/ESTV deals with direct federal tax and VAT, while cantonal administrations handle cantonal and municipal taxes. The UST/BFS produces statistics. For social assistance, the municipal or cantonal office indicated by the Canton remains responsible.
