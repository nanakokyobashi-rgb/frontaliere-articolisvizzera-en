Wages and work Thurgau: sectors, levels and contracts (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 10 September 2026

In the canton of Thurgau there is no federal minimum wage; the weekly hours are 45 or 50 depending on the sector, with AVS/AHV contributions at 5.3% for the employee.

Context

In a nutshell

  • No federal minimum wage, any canton-specific not yet specified
  • Maximum weekly schedule of 45 or 50 hours depending on the sector
  • Employee AVS/AHV contributions 5.3% (10.6% total with employer)
  • LAMal required for all residents
  • Direct federal tax, VAT and three tax levels (federal, cantonal, municipal)

Key facts

  • What: Contributory scheme AVS/AHV, LPP and LAMal
  • When: Current, in force for years without recent changes
  • Where: Canton of Thurgau, valid at national level
  • Who: Employees, employers, cantonal authorities
  • Amount: Employee AVS/AHV contribution 5.3%
  • What: Maximum working hours 45 h (industry, offices, sales) or 50 h (other sectors)
  • When: Minimum holiday 4 weeks (5 under 20)
  • Who: SECO, UST/BFS, BNS/SNB provide statistical data

The canton of Thurgau, like all Swiss cantons, is located in the federal framework in which there is no minimum wage at the national level; the presence of a cantonal minimum, if provided, is not indicated in the source and therefore remains "not yet specified". Working time regulations set a limit of 45 hours per week for industrial, office and sales sectors, while other sectors can reach 50 hours. Holidays are guaranteed for a minimum of four weeks, with an extension to five weeks for workers under twenty.

Operational details

📊 The labour market in Thurgau is characterised by a strong presence of collective agreements, although the source does not list specific agreements. In the absence of a cantonal minimum wage, wages are negotiated between unions and employers, often based on the level of training. The sectors that traditionally employ the most include the mechanical industry, financial services and alpine tourism; these sectors require skills ranging from professional qualifications to specialist degrees.

Without a cantonal minimum, the wage difference between training levels can be more marked than in cantons with a fixed threshold. A worker with vocational training (CAP) could earn a basic salary which, once the AVS/AHV, LPP and LAMal contributions are applied, translates into a net lower than a graduate with the same working hours. However, collective agreements often provide for wage brackets by grade of qualification, reducing the gap.

Key points

💡 For those living or planning to work in the canton of Thurgau, the operational process begins with verifying the collective labor agreements applicable to your sector. Here are the recommended steps:

1. Identify the employment sector – consult the cantonal website or SECO publications to understand which collective agreements are in effect. 2. Retrieve the agreement text – often available on the industry union's portal or the cantonal labor authority's website. 3. Calculate net pay – use the salary calculator by entering gross salary, weekly hours (45 or 50), and mandatory contributions (AVS/AHV 5.3%, AD/AC 1.1%, LAA/LAI 0.7–1.5%). 4. Verify health insurance coverage – within three months of relocation, it is mandatory to take out health insurance; choose the most suitable deductible (300–2500 CHF) and check for any cantonal reductions. 5. Declare taxes – complete the federal direct tax return and the cantonal/municipal return, taking into account the municipal multiplier. For the final calculation, you can use the tax calculator. 6. Monitor updates – changes to collective agreements or salary thresholds are published by UST/BFS and SECO; subscribing to cantonal newsletters is useful for not missing deadlines.

If you hold a residence permit (B, C, or G), you must also verify the validity of the permit with the SEM and, if necessary, renew it before the expiration date.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the AVS/AHV contribution for the employee?
The mandatory AVS/AHV contribution for the employee is 5.3% of the gross salary, while the employer covers the same share, bringing the total to 10.6%.
Is there a minimum wage in the canton of Thurgau?
The source indicates that there is no federal minimum wage; the presence of a cantonal minimum is not specified, so at the moment it is "not yet specified".
