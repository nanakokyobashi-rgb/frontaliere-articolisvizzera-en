Announcement and appointment

On September 10, 2026, the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) announced the visit to Switzerland, scheduled for Tuesday, September 15, 2026, by Mathias Cormann, who heads the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) as secretary-general. The central part will be the presentation of the OECD's latest report on Swiss economic policy. Cormann will present the document at a press conference, while Helene Budliger Artieda, director of SECO, will comment on the findings and recommendations from Switzerland's perspective. The report's areas of focus

The communiqué places the report within the context of the multiple challenges Switzerland must address. According to the OECD, action is needed to preserve the country's competitiveness and innovative capacity over the long term. The interventions listed include reforms in healthcare and old-age provision, reducing the administrative burden borne by companies, making fuller use of the workforce's potential and strengthening competition.

The topics examined also include adaptation to climate change and the availability of housing in Switzerland. The issues therefore concern national economic policy, while the source does not preview the detailed content of the individual recommendations. No amounts, new rules or implementation timetable are specified: these elements are not included in the press invitation.
