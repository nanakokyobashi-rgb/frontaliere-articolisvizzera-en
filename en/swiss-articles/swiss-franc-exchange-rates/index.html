Recent economic developments focus on the Swiss franc, monitored by experts from the Italian economic portal Borsa e Finanza. Since the end of May, the euro franc exchange rate has risen more than two percent, surpassing zero point ninety-three for the first time since last January. This movement was supported by the easing of geopolitical tensions and the rate differential favourable to the euro and the dollar. In recent days, however, the international scenario has shown new elements of uncertainty. The deteriorating situation in the Middle East and the return of US tariffs are pushing investors back into safe-haven assets. The market is torn between rate support and geopolitical risk. # Central bank dynamics and economic impact

Decisions by monetary authorities continue to drive fluctuations in international currency markets. The governor of the BNS, Martin Schlegel, highlighted that in March the institution had increased its willingness to intervene in the foreign exchange market. However, Schlegel himself warned that the risk of strong upward pressure still exists, given that the international geopolitical situation remains characterised by profound uncertainty. An element of attention is represented by the possible rise in oil prices up to one hundred dollars per barrel. Beyond this critical threshold, demand for safe-haven currencies could reactivate, directly affecting the positioning of the Swiss franc on global markets. To monitor the evolution of purchasing power and daily spending in Swiss territory, it is useful to consult the costo della vita data updated periodically by the competent bodies. The contrast between the restrictive policies of the large foreign central banks and the prudent line of the SNB outlines a complex scenario for economic operators and for residents who manage transactions in different currencies. Price stability in Switzerland, confirmed by inflation data, contrasts with the higher rates applied in the euro area and the United States. Frequently Asked Questions
What factors have influenced the euro franc exchange rate recently?
The euro franc exchange rate was influenced by the easing of geopolitical tensions, the euro- and dollar-friendly interest rate differential, and the monetary policies of central banks.
What is the Swiss National Bank's current position on rates?
The Swiss National Bank kept rates at zero percent, with inflation at zero point five percent in June.
What scenarios could cause a further strengthening of the franc?
A possible rise in the price of oil to a hundred dollars a barrel and rising international geopolitical tensions could reactivate demand for safe-haven goods and strengthen the Swiss franc.
