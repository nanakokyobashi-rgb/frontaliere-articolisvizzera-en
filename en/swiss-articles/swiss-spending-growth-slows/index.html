Swiss spend more, but growth slows

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 12 September 2026

In August, the consumption indicator calculated by PostFinance shows an increase of 0.8%. The pace slows down compared to previous months.

Key facts

  • What: Trend in consumption and retail expenses
  • When: August compared to the same period in 2025
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: PostFinance and its 2.4 million customers
  • Amount: Change of 0.8%
  • Method: Monitoring card payments

In August, the consumption indicator calculated by PostFinance based on card payments of its 2.4 million customers rose by 0.8% compared to the same period in 2025. The figure shows a slowdown compared to July (+1.1%) and June (+1.8%). This trend describes a national economic dynamic that sees Swiss consumers continue to spend, but with a progressively less marked rate of increase over the summer months.

Operational details

Spending on hotels and rental cars has now returned to normal, the financial branch of the Swiss Post said in a statement today. This return to the usual levels after the peaks linked to summer tourist flows reflects a stabilization of demand in the travel and temporary mobility sector. Meanwhile, releases for leisure-related items such as computers and electronic devices have progressed, confirming a constant interest in technology and home entertainment.

Growth sectors and stagnant sectors

Alongside the investments in technology, the data show that a little more was also paid for the purchase and repair of cars as well as for groceries. On the other hand, the beauty and well-being segment remained stagnant overall, with no significant changes compared to previous surveys.
