Verification Procedure

Given the available information, the most reliable procedure is a checklist limited to the specified fields. There is no need to invent attachments, personal criteria, or amounts to organize the application: simply distinguish between what the form must contain and what is not included in the provided material.

1. Distinguish between the two types of financial aid. Note whether the application is for a scholarship or a student loan, keeping both categories as specified in the prompt. 2. Identify the requirements. List only the conditions explicitly associated with the program, without adding criteria derived from other cantons or different cases. 3. Determine the maximum amount. Transcribe the limit specified in the form when available, without substituting it with an estimate or a standard value. 4. Check the submission deadlines.
