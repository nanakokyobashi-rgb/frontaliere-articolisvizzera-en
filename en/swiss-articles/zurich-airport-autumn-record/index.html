Zurich Airport: record with 123'000 passengers

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

On Sunday, October 4, Zurich welcomed 123'000 travelers: a still-provisional record linked to the autumn school holidays in several German-speaking Swiss cantons.

On Sunday, October 4, Zurich Airport welcomed 123'000 travelers, the highest number ever recorded in a single day at the airport. The operating company announced the figure, confirming a report published by Tages-Anzeiger.

The record is not yet final. A spokesperson clarified that the available figures are provisional; the final count will be released in mid-November. The wording already limits the scope of the news: the record concerns a single Sunday and, for now, is based on a count that still needs to be completed.

The operating company linked the high passenger numbers to the autumn school holidays that began in several German-speaking Swiss cantons. The source therefore indicates a specific reason for the concentration of travelers: the holiday calendar in part of German-speaking Switzerland. To interpret the record accurately, it must be kept tied to the stated day and to the provisional nature of the count. It is not a description of an entire period, but the result reported for that Sunday.

An airport with a national history

Zurich Airport is described as Switzerland's most important airport. Its origins date back to the period after the Second World War, when aviation activities were transferred from Dübendorf (ZH) to the airport.

The historical reference does not alter the record figure for Sunday, but recalls how aviation activities were transferred from Dübendorf after the war. The first runway entered service on June 14, 1948. That day, a Swissair Douglas DC-4 four-engine aircraft took off for London.

This separation is also useful when writing the news: the airport's history explains its origins, while the record indicates only the volume handled on that day according to the available data.
