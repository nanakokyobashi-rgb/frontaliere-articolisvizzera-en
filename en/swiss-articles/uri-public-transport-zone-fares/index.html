In Canton Uri, public transport revolves around the Uri fare community, which brings together the services of different companies, including PostBus and SBB (SBB/CFF/SBB), under a single zone fare system. The system allows residents, commuters and visitors to move around the cantonal territory with a single ticket, which can be purchased in the form of a single ticket, a monthly subscription or an annual subscription, generally uploaded to the SwissPass.

  • AG makes the zonal subscription for the covered network superfluous

Combination with national offers

An important practical junction is the interaction between the securities of the URI tariff community and the national Halbtax and AG offers. The source indicates that both solutions can be combined with cantonal subscriptions, but does not specify in which technical form the combination takes place. In general, at national level, Halbtax gives the right to purchase individual tickets at a reduced price, while the AG (general subscription) covers almost the entire Swiss network and makes the de facto zonal subscription unnecessary for trips covered by the perimeter of the AG itself.

For a commuter who only uses routes within the Uri Canton, the zonal subscription is therefore often the most economical solution. For a commuter who continues to other cantons or who uses the national network intensively, the AG may be more convenient for the same use.

Step-by-step procedure to buy the pass

1. Identify the zones needed: before purchasing, it is useful to establish how many zones of the Uri system your daily route crosses. The number of zones determines the fare of the annual or monthly pass. 2. Choose the pass type: depending on how frequently you travel, you can opt for the annual pass (maximum savings for regular use) or the monthly pass (flexibility for temporary needs). 3. Buy through an authorised channel: options include SBB ticket counters, PostBus sales points, official sales apps and ticket machines. The pass is loaded onto your SwissPass. 4. Verify discount documents: to access reductions for students and apprentices, you must present a valid school or training document at the time of purchase and keep it for any inspections. 5. Manage renewal: once loaded onto the SwissPass, the pass can be renewed through the same purchase channels. Renewal conditions and the actual duration of the pass are governed by the Uri tariff community and partner transport companies, and must be verified at the time of initial purchase.

Required documents

  • Swiss identity document or valid residence permit
  • For students and apprentices: certificate of registration or training contract
  • Personal SwissPass (to register the pass)

What to do in case of loss

In case of loss or theft of the SwissPass containing the pass, you should contact the SwissPass customer service to block and reissue the card, in order to avoid improper use of the loaded pass.

Frequently Asked Questions
How does the Uri Canton zoning system work?
The Uri tariff community divides the territory into concentric zones, each with its own tariff. The more zones you cross, the more the price increases. The same criterion applies to subscriptions, which give unlimited access to trains, buses and, where provided, boats included in the network, generally loaded on the SwissPass.
What is the difference between annual and monthly subscription in URIs?
The annual pass is designed for those who use public transport regularly, in particular workers and students. The monthly subscription is the most flexible alternative, useful for temporary needs or to test the route before moving on to the annual title. Both can be purchased at SBB ticket offices, authorised points of sale and digital channels.
What discounts are provided for students and apprentices in URI?
The Uri tariff community provides reductions dedicated to students and apprentices. The access conditions generally require a valid school or training document at the time of purchase and any on-board checks. The reduction applies to the cantonal title and is cumulated with any national benefits according to the regulations of the individual transports.
Can Halbtax and AG be combined with URI subscriptions?
According to the source, both national offers can be combined with cantonal URI subscriptions. The Halbtax gives the right to purchase individual tickets at a reduced price, while the AG covers almost the entire Swiss network and makes the de facto zonal subscription unnecessary for trips covered by the AG's perimeter.
What do you need to buy a zonal subscription in URIs?
A valid Swiss identity document or residence permit, the personal SwissPass for the registration of the degree and, for students and apprentices, a valid registration certificate or training contract are required to purchase the subscription.
