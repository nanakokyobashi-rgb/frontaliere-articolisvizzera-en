2027 health insurance premiums, Il Centro calls for more measures

In Ticino, health insurance premiums will rise by 3.7% in 2027, compared with 5% nationally. The Centre calls for strengthening cantonal and federal measures.

2027 premiums and Il Centro's position

In 2027, health insurance premiums in Ticino will increase by 3,7%. The figure remains an increase, but is below the national average of 5%. For Il Centro, the gap between the two figures is not enough to call this a success: the increase cannot be a reason for satisfaction. At the same time, the party considers the figure a sign that the path taken to contain healthcare costs appears to be correct.

The political message is therefore twofold. Il Centro calls for continuing the work already started at cantonal level and strengthening measures at federal level as well, without compromising the quality of care. At cantonal level, it identifies supply management, the appropriateness of services, the organization of care, the shift to outpatient care, hospital planning and prevention as areas for intervention. The position thus brings together several levers, from the organization of care to prevention.

Measures cited

Among the measures already adopted, the party highlights the introduction of caps in eight specialties. It also mentions initiatives in the home care and independent nursing sectors. The call is not to interrupt this process, but to strengthen it as part of cost containment.

According to Il Centro, however, the cantons cannot act alone. The Confederation should strengthen the tools available to address oversupply and excessive increases in spending. The same approach is set out in the five-point plan presented by Centro svizzero: strengthen primary care, orient remuneration more strongly toward quality and results, fully implement the electronic patient record and increase prevention efforts.

«Containing the growth of costs does not mean reducing necessary care or weakening the quality of our healthcare system».

Il Centro summarizes the principle that, in its view, must accompany every intervention: act simultaneously at cantonal and federal levels while maintaining the quality of the healthcare system. The overview can also be followed via the guida alla cassa malati, keeping the data on premiums and containment measures separate.

Operational details

What the comparison between Ticino and the national average indicates

The comparison between 3.7% and 5% should be read on two levels. In the immediate term, in Ticino the increase forecast for 2027 is more contained than the national average. Politically, for Il Centro the figure indicates that the path undertaken may be the right one, but it does not close the discussion: the increase remains and the party calls for continuing.
