Border teachers in Ticino: requirements and recognition (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 20 July 2026

The requirements to work as a teacher in Ticino as a border worker and the conditions for the recognition of the qualification.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Switzerland signed the New Frontier Agreement on 23 December 2020, effective from 1 January 2024.
  • The New Frontier Agreement provides for the exemption for old frontier workers of €7,500 and the transitional regime 2024-2033.
  • The new frontier workers have a deductible of €10,000.

Requirements for border teachers in Ticino

  • Border teachers in Ticino must meet the requirements of the New Border Agreement in order to work in Switzerland.
  • For old frontier workers, the exemption of €7,500 is valid until 2033, while new frontier workers have a deductible of €10,000.
  • Frontier teachers must hold a master's degree or comparable qualification and have at least 3 years of professional experience.

Recognition of the title of doctor

  • To obtain the recognition of the title of doctor, border teachers must submit the documentation required by the Ministry of Education and Vocational Training of the Canton of Ticino.
  • The documentation includes the title of doctor, the master's degree and the declaration of having worked as a border lecturer for at least 3 years.
  • Border teachers may submit documentation to the Ministry of Education and Vocational Training of the Canton of Ticino by 31 December 2024.

Checklist for border teachers

  • Check that you meet the requirements of the New Frontier Agreement.
  • Make sure you are at least 3 years old

Introduction

Switzerland, known for its stable economy and high-level education system, is an ideal place for frontier teachers. Starting from 1 January 2024, the Canton of Ticino will open its doors to border teachers, i.e. teachers from other countries who wish to work in Switzerland. In this article, we will explore the requirements and recognition to work as a teacher in Ticino as a border worker.

Requirements

To work as a teacher in Ticino as a border worker, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Recognised qualification: A qualification recognised by the Federal Ministry of Education and Research (MFIPD) or a Swiss university is required.
  • Work experience: Work experience of at least two years as a teacher in a European country or in a country with an education system similar to the Swiss one is required.
  • Knowledge of the language: A good knowledge of the German, French or Italian language is required, depending on the region in which you want to work.
  • Quality Certificate: A quality certificate issued by a recognized accreditation organization is required.

# Qualification recognition requirements

In order to work as a teacher in Ticino, border workers must obtain recognition of their qualification. This is necessary to ensure that their qualification is valid and recognised in Switzerland. The recognition process may vary depending on the canton and city where you want to work.

For example, in the Canton of Ticino, the process of recognition of the qualification for border workers is managed by the State Office for Vocational Education and Training (UFES). Border workers must submit the required documentation, which includes the original educational qualification, the degree and any certificates of specialization.

Example of required documentation

  • Original qualification
  • Degree
  • Certificates of specialization (if necessary)
  • Language documentation (if the qualification is not in Italian, English or French)
