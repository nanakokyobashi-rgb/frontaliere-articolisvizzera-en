A test in the field

Approximately 3'000 soldiers, 300 wheeled vehicles and 50 armored vehicles participated in MOBILO 26, the exercise with which the Swiss Armed Forces tested their capacity for rapid mobilization. The movement covered approximately 300 kilometers, from Bière (VD) to Frauenfeld (TG), and concluded on Friday.

The operation's motto was to be ready in 24 hours. To achieve this, the movements took place mainly at night and discreetly. The source gives two reasons: tactical considerations and the desire to avoid unnecessary displays of force. Speed was tested together with the ability to move personnel and equipment without making the operation more visible than necessary.

Cooperation between units had a concrete component. Pontoon Battalion 26 assembled a floating bridge to cross a waterway; the first elements of Artillery Group 49 crossed it. This was not a simulation in the field: Artillery Group 49 entered service on Monday and less than 72 hours later was already moving with its vehicles.

Preparation required anticipating weather conditions, planning the assembly of the bridge and coordinating the arrival of the vehicles. The exercise also included mobile communication systems, medical and logistical equipment, and air defense systems. The ADS 15 reconnaissance drone provided information for the safety of the movements.

The commander of the Land Forces, Divisionary Yves Gächter, and his staff tested the procedures under realistic conditions. The route crossed Vaud's Lake Geneva arc, the Fribourg Plateau, the Seeland, the southern foothills of the Solothurn Jura, the Emmental and Upper Aargau.
