Frequently Asked Questions
Why is the Swiss Army considering the redeployment of old bunkers?
The main reason for this is the worsening security policy situation in Europe, which has led to increased requirements for protection, resilience and decentralisation, as well as an increasing need for protected storage space.
What materials are you trying to deposit in the protected spaces?
Protected storage spaces are sought above all for the storage of consumables, spare parts, medicines, electronic equipment, dangerous goods and ammunition.
Who manages the tender for the evaluation of the plants?
The evaluation order and the associated public tender were launched by the Federal Office for Armaments Armasuisse, as communicated by the Department of Defence.
