The Swiss army is considering recommissioning old military bunkers. The reason is the worsening security policy situation in Europe. The security, resilience and decentralization requirements of the military have increased and the need for secure storage spaces has also grown, the Department of Defense said on Saturday after the news was published by the Blick newspaper. In particular, protected storage spaces are sought for the storage of consumables, spare parts, medicines, electronic equipment, dangerous goods or even ammunition.
