According to the Federal Law on Family Benefits (LFPF) of 1 January 2010, family allowances are paid according to the economic situation of the family. The amount of the allowances varies depending on the family income and the number of children.

To claim family allowances in Canton Nidvaldo, you must contact the relevant Compensation Authority. The application procedure and the monthly amounts per child will be communicated later.

Family allowances are a social benefit provided for by the Federal Law of 18 March 1964 on Social Security, which provides for an economic contribution for families with dependent children. Canton Nidvaldo, like all other Swiss cantons, has adopted this law and offers a family allowance to households residing in its territory.

To claim family allowances, you must meet the statutory residency and income requirements. In particular, you must have been resident in the Canton of Nidvaldo for at least one year and have an annual income of less than CHF 150,000. 📊

  • Applicant's identity document
  • Dependent children's identity document
  • Applicant's tax return
  • Income tax return of family members
