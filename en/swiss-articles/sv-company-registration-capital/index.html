The Canton of Swabia, like all Swiss cantons, requires compulsory registration in the Commercial Register for anyone intending to start a business. This procedure is the first administrative step and represents a crucial moment in the startup phase of any company. Before starting business operations, entrepreneurs and company owners must complete registration with the competent cantonal office, which varies depending on the legal form chosen.

Operational details

Three-tier tax system

In the Canton of Schwyz, as throughout Switzerland, the tax system operates on three levels: federal, cantonal, and municipal. Each level has its own legislation and tax rate.

Direct Federal Tax (DFD) is administered by the Federal Tax Administration (FTA) and represents the federal level. Cantonal tax is managed by the cantonal authorities of Schwyz and is calculated according to its own cantonal law. Finally, municipalities apply a multiplier to the cantonal base, which means that local taxation varies depending on the company's domicile.

This three-tier structure requires the entrepreneur to consider not only the federal rate, but also the specific cantonal and municipal rates. This configuration is not found in many countries, where the tax system is centralized at one or two levels. To plan tax aspects, consult the federal tax calculator.

Insurance obligations for employees

If the business involves hiring employees, significant insurance obligations arise immediately. Under Swiss labor law, the employer is required to pay social contributions for each employee.

⚠️ OASI (Old-Age and Survivors' Insurance), DI (Disability Insurance), and EO (Income Compensation Allowance) contributions represent 5.3% of the employee's salary, to which an identical contribution from the employer is added, for a total of 10.6%. These contributions finance the first pillar of the Swiss pension system.

Accident insurance (UVG/LAA) is mandatory for employees working on a primary basis and varies between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the sector of activity. This amount is borne by the employer.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure

To start a business in the Canton of Schwyz, following these steps is essential:

1. Choose the legal form Before taking any other action, define the type of enterprise. Consult an accountant or a lawyer specialized in commercial law to choose the structure that best suits your needs. Remember that the minimum capital varies: zero for sole proprietorships, CHF 20,000 for LLCs (Sagl), CHF 100,000 for corporations (SA).

2. Prepare the documentation Depending on the legal form, gather the necessary documents: Commercial Register extracts from previous experiences, identity documents of the owners, company statutes (for corporations), deed of incorporation, and bank certificates of capital deposit (for LLCs and SAs).

3. Register with the cantonal Commercial Register Contact the Commercial Register office of the Canton of Schwyz with the prepared documentation. Registration takes place at the competent cantonal authority and marks the official start of business activity.

4. Apply for the federal VAT number If the expected annual turnover exceeds CHF 100,000, you must register for VAT with the Federal Tax Administration (FTA/ESTV). This number is necessary to issue invoices and manage federal tax obligations.

5. Register for cantonal and municipal taxes Contact the tax office of the Canton of Schwyz to register for cantonal and municipal taxes. You will receive details on deadlines, rates, and specific payment procedures.

6. Start social security contributions If you will be hiring employees, register with the competent OASI (AVS) Compensation Fund for the payment of social security contributions. Contact an insurance company for mandatory accident insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the minimum capital required to open a limited liability company (SAGL) in the Canton of Schwyz?
The minimum capital required is CHF 20,000 according to Swiss federal law. This capital must be paid in full to a bank before registration with the Commercial Register. Sagl offers limited liability to invested capital and represents a legal form balanced between administrative complexity and asset protection.
What are the main mandatory social contributions for employees in Switzerland?
The employer must pay: AVS/AI/IPG for a total of 10.6% (5.3% borne by the employee + 5.3% by the employer), LAINF/LAA accident insurance between 0.7% and 1.5%, LPP according to age (7-18%), individual LAMal. These costs represent a significant item in the startup budget and need to be carefully evaluated.
When do I need to register for VAT in the Canton of Schwyz?
If the expected annual turnover exceeds CHF 100,000, you are obliged to register for VAT with the Federal Administration of Customs (AFC/ESTV). Registration must be made within the established deadlines and allows you to issue invoices with VAT and deduct the amounts on commercial purchases.
What is the first concrete step to open a business in the Canton of Schwyz?
The first step is to choose the legal form (sole proprietorship, Sagl, SA, etc.). Then, collect the necessary documents and register with the cantonal Trade Register, which represents the official start of business and the most critical administrative step.
How does taxation work in the Canton of Svitto?
Taxation in Switzerland operates on three levels: federal (IFD, administered by AFC), cantonal (laws of the canton of Switzerland) and municipal (multiplier on a cantonal basis). Each level has its own rates and payment deadlines. An accountant can help you understand specific obligations and plan tax management.
