On 6 August 2026, the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) published updated data on the situation of the Swiss labour market. In July 2026, the unemployment rate stood at 3.0%, registering an increase of 0.1 percentage points compared to the previous month. The total number of registered unemployed reached 148'002, marking an increase of 10'122 people compared to the same month of the previous year, equal to an increase of 7.8%. Analyzing the seasonally adjusted data, the number of unemployed grew by 959 units (+0.7%), keeping the seasonally adjusted rate unchanged at 3.1%. These numbers reflect the current dynamics of the national market, in which the demand and supply of labor are compared with the seasonal fluctuations typical of the summer period. Operational details

Employment dynamics and vacancies

In addition to the official unemployment numbers, SECO monitored the overall number of job seekers. In July 2026, the total of these subjects reached 227,200, registering an increase of 1,409 units (+0.6%) compared to the previous month and an increase of 17,759 units (+8.5%) compared to the same period of the previous year. The rate of job seekers remained stable at 4.8%. Net of seasonal variations, the number of job seekers rose to 238,134 (+1.0%), with a seasonally adjusted rate of 5.0%. These data indicate a market in which the pressure on job search remains constant, despite the variation in seasonal flows. The availability of job positions is a crucial indicator to understand the balance of the system. In July 2026, the vacancies announced at the URCs were 45,156, a decrease of 2,088 units (-4.4%) compared to the previous month.
