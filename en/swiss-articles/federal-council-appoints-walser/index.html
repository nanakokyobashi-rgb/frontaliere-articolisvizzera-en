On 2 September 2026, the Federal Council appointed Tobias Walser, an expert in environmental sciences, as the delegate of the university assemblies of the Swiss Federal Institutes of Technology (ETHs) in Zurich and Lausanne to the Council of the ETHs. Walser, who has been the director of the Department of Health Sciences and Technology at the ETH Zurich since 2022, will take office on 1 September 2026. He succeeds Kristin Becker van Slooten, who held the position from 2004 to 2006 and subsequently from 2017.

Tobias Walser studied environmental sciences at the ETH Zurich, earning his doctorate in 2012 in the field of risk assessment of nanomaterials. For his interdisciplinary work on the spread of nanomaterials, he was awarded the Swiss Aerosol Award in 2012. In his current role as director, he is also involved in strategic initiatives that combine technology, regulation, and practice. He has extensive experience in governance, institutional development, and the coordination of various interest groups in the fields of education and research.

The Role of Tobias Walser

Tobias Walser, with his appointment, assumes a crucial role in the governance of the two largest federal universities in Switzerland. His experience and expertise will be essential in guiding the university assemblies of the ETHs in Zurich and Lausanne, contributing to the definition of national academic strategies and policies.
