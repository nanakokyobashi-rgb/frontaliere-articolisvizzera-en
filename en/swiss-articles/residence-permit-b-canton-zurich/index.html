Residence Permit Requirements B

In order to obtain a residence permit B in the Canton of Zurich, foreign nationals must meet certain requirements. These include:

  • Be a foreign citizen with a legal stay in Switzerland
  • Have a livelihood guarantee for a minimum amount of CHF 50,000 (as established by the law on the stay of foreigners of 16 December 2005)
  • Have a subsistence guarantee that covers at least 3 months of expenses
  • Not have a serious criminal offence committed in Switzerland or abroad
  • You have not been expelled from Switzerland or another EU Member State

Residence Permit Requirements B

To obtain a residence permit B in the Canton of Zurich, foreign nationals must meet the following requirements:

  • Living in the Canton of Zurich: The applicant must reside in the Canton of Zurich for at least 6 months before submitting the application.
  • Sufficient income: The applicant must have sufficient income to support themselves and their family.
  • Health insurance: The applicant must have health insurance covering medical expenses in Switzerland.
  • Good Conduct: The applicant must not have a criminal record or other conditions that may cast doubt on their eligibility to obtain a residence permit.

Renewal Procedure

Residence permit B is valid for 1 year and can be renewed. The renewal procedure is similar to that of obtaining the B residence permit. The applicant must submit a new application and provide the necessary evidence to demonstrate that it continues to meet the requirements.
