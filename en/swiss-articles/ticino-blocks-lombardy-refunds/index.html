The State Council of the Canton of Ticino has decided to cautiously suspend half of the Lombard portion of the refreshments

Context

The State Council of the Canton of Ticino announced that it had cautiously suspended half of the Lombard quota as a reaction to the "health tax". No block for Piedmont, which did not apply the measure. The decision was announced at a press conference by the President of the Ticino Government Claudio Zali, together with colleagues Christian Vitta and Raffaele De Rosa.

The measure, introduced by the Lombard Government to finance public health, provides for a tax of 1.5% on invoices issued by health professionals. However, the State Council of the Canton of Ticino decided to suspend the Lombard quota, equivalent to about 50 million francs, in reaction to the measure. The decision was taken after a meeting of the State Council, at which the possible consequences of the measure on Ticino companies were discussed.

The suspension of the Lombard quota does not imply a total blockage of payments, but rather a moratorium on payments until the matter is resolved. The State Council of the Canton of Ticino has decided to suspend payments for a period of 30 days, at the end of which the situation will be assessed and the next move decided.

Operational details

The Lombard portion of the refreshments is 100,442,354 francs, while Piedmont will receive 8,668,106 francs. The Council of State decided to suspend 50,221,177 francs, corresponding to half of the Lombard share. No suspension is foreseen for Piedmont.

The decision was taken following the request for suspension of the refunds presented by the Canton of Ticino, which had expressed concerns about the management of the funds by Lombardy. Lombardy had already received an amount of 50,221,177 francs, representing half of the total quota allocated to the Canton. The State Council has decided to suspend these funds, pending further information and clarification on the management of the restaurants.

The decision of the Council of State was taken into consideration the regulations in force regarding refreshments. The Federal Law on the cantons' own resources, adopted on 20 December 2019, stipulates that the cantons must use the allocated funds for specific purposes, such as the construction of infrastructure and the management of public services. Lombardy had received 100,442,354 francs for the construction of an infrastructure project, which includes the construction of a new motorway.
