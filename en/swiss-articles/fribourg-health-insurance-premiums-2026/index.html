Sick pay premiums Freiburg: reduction and subsidies (cross-border guide)

Guide to compulsory health insurance premiums in the Canton of Fribourg: deductibles, alternative models and procedure for obtaining premium reductions

Context

In brief

  • Health insurance LAMal is mandatory in Switzerland and also valid in Fribourg
  • Available deductibles range from CHF 300 to CHF 2500 for adults
  • The Canton of Fribourg offers subsidies (premium reduction) for residents with low income
  • The registration procedure must take place within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland

Key facts

  • What: Mandatory health insurance with per capita premiums and variable deductibles
  • Where: Canton of Fribourg (national LAMal system applied at cantonal level)
  • Who: All residents in Switzerland must obtain coverage
  • Deadline: Within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland
  • Deductibles: From CHF 300 to CHF 2500 for adults
  • Subsidies: Available for those with insufficient income (cantonal criteria)

The mandatory LAMal system in the Canton of Fribourg

The LAMal health insurance is mandatory in Switzerland and also valid in Fribourg. It is a private insurance with per capita premiums, not a tax or salary contribution. Anyone who moves to Fribourg or any other Swiss canton must obtain coverage within 3 months of arrival, otherwise they risk administrative penalties. Each insured person pays their own premium, regardless of income or age (except for young adults up to 25 years old, who benefit from flat-rate reductions). Premiums vary depending on the canton of residence, the insurance company chosen, and the insurance model subscribed to (standard, with reduced deductible, HMO models, etc.). In the Canton of Fribourg, like in other cantons, mandatory insurances must cover all residents according to LAMal regulations, without exclusions for pre-existing conditions or health conditions. Health insurance is therefore the cornerstone of Switzerland's national health protection system.

Operational details

Fribourg canton, like all Swiss cantons, provides subsidies to reduce health insurance premiums for people with low incomes. The subsidies (also called 'premium payment assistance') are determined at the cantonal level according to income, assets, and household composition criteria.

How premium reduction works in Fribourg

The procedure to obtain the premium reduction in Canton Fribourg follows the national system: those with insufficient income can apply to the competent cantonal office (generally the social welfare office or equivalent). Criteria vary from canton to canton, but typically include:

  • Gross income below a threshold defined by the canton
  • Limited assets
  • Household composition (spouse, dependent children)

The amount of the subsidy depends on the gap between the actual premium and the 'payment capacity' calculated by the canton based on income. It is not possible to provide exact amounts without consulting updated data from Canton Fribourg, but the mechanism is transparent: the subsidy partially or fully covers the difference between what the insured person can afford to pay and the actual cost of the premium.

Deadlines and documentation

Premium reduction applications must usually be submitted by specific deadlines (often coinciding with the year change, in December). Documentation on income (tax return, pay slips, bank statements) and assets must be provided. The decision is notified in writing, and the subsidy is valid for one year, generally requiring renewal every year.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure for registration

Step 1: Finding out about available premiums

Within 3 months of arriving in Fribourg (or at the beginning of each calendar year if already resident), gather your identification documents and contact the insurance companies operating in the canton to receive their premium proposals. You can do this online through the insurers' websites or directly at their offices. The offers must include the monthly premium for each available deductible level.

Step 2: Choosing the model and deductible

Assess which deductible level (CHF 300–2,500) and which model (standard, HMO, family doctor model) suits your anticipated medical spending profile. A young, healthy adult might opt for a high deductible and basic model; a person with chronic conditions might prefer a low deductible and broader coverage.

Step 3: Concluding the contract

Sign the contract with the chosen insurance company. Coverage will take effect on the 1st of the following month (or from the date you specify, according to the contract). Keep copies of all documents and your policy.

Step 4: Verifying eligibility for subsidies

If your income is low, apply for premium reduction at the social welfare office of Canton Fribourg. Be prepared with your tax return, bank statements, and a copy of your LAMal policy. Renewal deadlines for subsidies are generally in November/December for the following year.

Useful tools and contacts

To find insurance companies operating in Fribourg and compare premiums, you can use the national comparison tool from the Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH) or directly consult the websites of major insurers. For information about subsidies specific to Canton Fribourg, contact the competent cantonal office (exact name: check on the canton's official website).

Frequently Asked Questions
How long do I have to take out health insurance after moving to Freiburg?
You have 3 months from arrival in Switzerland. If you don't insure within this timeframe, you risk an administrative fine. It is advisable to inquire about the available premiums and choose the coverage before the deadline, to avoid bureaucratic complications and additional costs.
What is the difference between basic LAMal and reduced-deductible models?
Basic LAMal offers essential coverage; reduced-deductible models (e.g. HMOs) limit your freedom of medical choice-you need to consult a treating doctor before the specialist-but they offer lower premiums. The choice depends on your need for medical flexibility and available budget.
How do I know if I am eligible for the premium reduction subsidy in Freiburg?
It depends on your annual gross income and assets. Contact the social security office of the Canton of Fribourg with your most recent tax return. The office will determine if you fall within the aid criteria and will communicate the amount of the subsidy. The procedure is generally free of charge.
Can I change my insurance and deductible every year?
Yes. Every year (usually by November) you can cancel your current policy and take out a new one with a different company and deductible. Many take advantage of the change in year to optimize the ratio of premium to coverage and reduce costs.
If I change cantons, what happens to my LAMal insurance?
You must notify the insurance company of the change of address and check if the coverage remains valid in the new canton. Rewards may change. You are generally entitled to a special cancellation window in the event of a transfer, allowing you to search for better conditions in the new canton.
