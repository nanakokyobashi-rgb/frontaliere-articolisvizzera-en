Publibike switches to a UK investor

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 August 2026

The Swiss bicycle rental company Publibike is majority-owned by British investor Visionedgeone.

Context

In brief

  • Publibike switches to Visionedgeone
  • Markus Bacher remains CEO and minority shareholder
  • Visionedgeone owns Inurba Mobility

Key Facts

  • What: Majority transfer of Publibike to Visionedgeone
  • When: July 30, 2024
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: Visionedgeone
  • Amount: Not specified

BERN - Swiss bicycle rental company Publibike is passing a majority stake to British investor Visionedgeone. Current CEO Markus Bacher will retain leadership of the company and will remain a minority shareholder. The news was reported on Monday by the CH-Media Group's "Schweiz heute" portal, after the same company had already announced the change of ownership on July 30 on its website. The price of the transaction was not disclosed.

With the transaction, the current shareholders are selling their shares, with the exception of Bacher. Among those retiring is Bern entrepreneur Thomas Binggeli, founder and owner of Thömus, who has said he wants to return to focusing fully on his other activities.

Visionedgeone has made a long-term commitment, for at least five years, Bacher indicated. The investor already owns Inurba Mobility, a bike sharing operator active in several European countries including France, Finland and Poland. The aim is to exploit possible synergies in terms of technology, distribution and management.

Under the new ownership, Publibike intends to strengthen investments in the fleet, services and technology, as well as expand the offer to other Swiss cities and municipalities. Currently, the company operates around 10,500 bicycles and e-bikes in around 120 cities and municipalities spread across ten cantons, with customers including Zurich, Bern, Basel, Biel and Aarau.

The public sector partly supports the business through annual contributions and guarantees to cover the deficit. Publibike was previously controlled by Swiss Post and was sold in 2022 to a group of investors led by Bacher, Binggeli and IT entrepreneur Guido Honegger. Since 2024, the company has been recording positive operating results (EBITDA), according to Bacher.

Operational details

Implications for the Swiss market

The acquisition of Publibike by Visionedgeone could lead to a strengthening of the bike sharing sector in Switzerland. With Visionedgeone's experience in several European countries, it is expected that Publibike will benefit from new technologies and better management practices. This could result in a more efficient service and greater geographical coverage, with potential new customers in other Swiss cities and municipalities.

Synergies and innovation

The goal of leveraging technology and management synergies could lead to significant innovations in the industry. Visionedgeone, with its expertise in France, Finland, and Poland, could introduce new technology solutions that improve user experience and fleet management. This could include improvements in rental apps, more efficient charging systems for e-bikes, and new ways of distribution.

Expansion and investment

With plans to expand its offerings to other Swiss cities and municipalities, Publibike could see a significant increase in its user base. This could lead to increased demand for bicycles and e-bikes, further stimulating the bike-sharing market. Investments in the fleet and services could also improve the quality of service offered, making Publibike an even more attractive choice for consumers.

Public support

Public sector support through annual contributions and guarantees to cover the deficit is a crucial factor in Publibike's success. With the new ownership, Publibike is expected to continue to receive this support, ensuring the financial sustainability of the company and allowing it to keep prices affordable for users.

Procedure for existing users

For existing users of Publibike, the change of ownership should not result in any immediate changes. However, it is advisable to keep an eye on any official communications from Publibike to be informed of any updates or improvements in the services. Users can continue to use the bikes and e-bikes as usual, benefiting from future improvements that will be introduced.

New users

For those interested in becoming a new Publibike user, you can visit the company's official website to register and start using the service. With the planned expansion, there will be new opportunities to rent bikes and e-bikes in several Swiss cities and municipalities.
