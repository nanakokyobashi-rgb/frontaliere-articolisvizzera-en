“I thought about the candidacy for a long time,” Amaudruz said. A possible election would make her the first female member of the UDC to enter the Federal Council, but this possible record still depends on the vote.

A different decision from 2022

In the 2022 elections, following the resignation of Ueli Maurer, Amaudruz, now 47 years old, had not presented his candidacy. Now he has instead publicly declared that he wants to compete for Parmelin's succession; as a reason he reported that he had thought about the candidacy for a long time, without indicating other reasons.
