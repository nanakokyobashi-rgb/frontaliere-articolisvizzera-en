Security deposit rules

The security deposit is an amount that the landlord requests from the tenant as a guarantee for any damage to the property during the lease. According to the Federal Law of 20 June 2003 on the Lease of Real Estate, the security deposit may not exceed 3 months' rent. In the Canton of Obvaldo, the security deposit can be up to CHF 3,600 for a two-bedroom apartment.
