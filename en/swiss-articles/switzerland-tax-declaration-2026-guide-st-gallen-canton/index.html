Impact of the New Frontier Agreement

The new border agreement (applied since 1 January 2024) introduces differentiated deductibles. For Article 9 of the Agreement, “old frontier workers” are people who met the tax requirements and worked in Graubünden, Ticino or Valais on 17 July 2023 or during the period from 31 December 2018 through 17 July 2023. Article 9 keeps covered employment income taxable exclusively at source in Switzerland; other workers follow the ordinary regime, with ordinary taxation in Italy, a credit for Swiss tax and Swiss withholding at 80% of the ordinary rates. The €10,000 figure in Article 4 of Italian Law No. 83/2023 is only the income limit referred to in Article 1(175) of Law No. 147/2013; it is not a general allowance for new frontier workers. Sources: ESTV factsheet (Italian) · Agreement, Art. 9 · Law No. 83/2023, Art. 4. Old cross-border workers remain taxed only in Switzerland (Art. 9 of the 2020 Agreement).
