The reopening announced by SEM

On October 15, 2026, from Bern-Wabern, the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) announced the temporary reopening of the civil protection facility in Niederscherli, in the Municipality of Köniz (BE). The opening is scheduled for mid-November and provides 120 beds. The facility is intended primarily for Ukrainian refugees.

The decision comes after a slight increase, linked to seasonal factors, in the number of new asylum applications. The SEM points out, however, that the level remains contained and markedly lower than last year. The decline had made it possible to close six federal asylum centers in June 2026.

The reopening is not presented as a permanent expansion of the network. If the need for places should grow, the SEM can arrange additional beds by increasing occupancy density in other federal asylum centers. For the Niederscherli facility and any densification measures, the administration will proceed in consultation with the authorities of the municipalities concerned.

The plan also contains a return condition. As soon as the number of new applications starts falling again, the SEM will close Niederscherli again. For the time being, reopening other reception facilities is not planned.

The timetable indicated by the SEM unfolds in three stages: the reopening in mid-November, the quantitative estimate for the end of 2026 and the condition concerning the centers already closed, which extends at least into the summer of 2027. According to the SEM, around 22 000 asylum applications and around 10 000 applications concerning S status are expected by the end of 2026. The six centers closed in June 2026 will remain inoperative at least until the summer of 2027, provided that the number of new applications evolves as expected.

To understand the decision in operational terms, the key point is the flexibility of reception capacity. SEM does not describe a general reopening of the centers closed in June: it identifies a specific facility, with a defined capacity, and leaves open the possibility of increasing the number of places in other centers if needed. The role of the municipalities concerned

The reopening of Niederscherli and any densification in the other centers will be implemented in agreement with the authorities of the municipalities concerned. Coordination with the local area is therefore part of the measure described by SEM.

For the reader, the practical implication is to follow the sequence, not just the overall number. Seasonal growth may activate temporary places; a decline in new applications leads to the closure of the facility; the absence of a new announcement, by contrast, means that the plan for the six closed centers remains in force. Planning remains tied to the trend in applications and the conditions set out by SEM.
