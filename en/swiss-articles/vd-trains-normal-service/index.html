Restoration announced

Rail traffic between Yverdon-les-Bains and Lausanne will resume normal operations tomorrow morning from 05.40. FFS announced the restoration while around one hundred female employees are still actively working to restore service and inform customers.

The company strongly condemns the criminal act responsible for the disruption and announces that it will file a complaint against unknown persons. In the statement released this afternoon, FFS reports that it deployed 20 replacement buses and around 40 customer assistants.

Service until late evening

The connection between Lausanne and Yverdon-les-Bains is ensured, until late this evening, by two long-distance trains per hour in each direction. However, service remains affected by severe delays. Regional service is provided by replacement buses, and an additional bus service connects Yverdon-les-Bains to Geneva Airport.

The disruption was caused by an arson attack that broke out yesterday morning around 03.15 inside a cable duct near Eclépens station, in VD. The fire caused a switching cabin to malfunction, putting the IC5, IC51, R1 and R2 lines out of service, as well as the section between Cossonay-Penthalaz and Chavornay.

The FFS statement therefore describes an operation on several fronts: restoring service, informing customers, providing replacement buses and connecting with Geneva Airport. The operational situation remains distinct until late evening, while the return to normal service between Yverdon-les-Bains and Lausanne is announced from 05.40 tomorrow. Operational details

FFS's communication allows the disruption to be understood in operational terms: the response is not the same for all connections. Those traveling along the route between Lausanne and Yverdon-les-Bains still have long-distance service, but must contend with major delays. Those using a regional connection instead find replacement buses. For Geneva Airport, a separate service from Yverdon-les-Bains is indicated.
