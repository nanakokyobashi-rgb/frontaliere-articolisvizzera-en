20 Minuten closed the first half of 2026 with an operating result (adjusted EBIT) of CHF 11.7 million. This was announced by the group on the occasion of the publication of TX Group's half-yearly data. The result improved by CHF 16.7 million compared to the same period in 2025.

Growth was fostered by new editorial products and the integration of newsrooms. In addition, digital advertising revenues increased by 20% year-on-year. According to official data, the share of online advertising in the first half of 2026 was 40% of total revenues, while in 2025 it was 30%.

20 Minuten's strategy has focused on the creation of quality content and advertising marketing. The group has invested in technology to improve the user experience and increase advertising visibility. In addition, the merger of the German and French Swiss newsrooms has made it possible to reduce production costs and increase efficiency.
