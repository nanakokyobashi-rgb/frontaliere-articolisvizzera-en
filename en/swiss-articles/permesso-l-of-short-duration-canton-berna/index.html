Conditions for obtaining the permit

To obtain the short-term L permit, foreign nationals must meet the following conditions:

  • Be citizens of a non-EU country or a Community country not a member of the European Union
  • Not having a valid residence permit in the Canton of Bern or another Swiss canton
  • Not having a crime committed in the Canton of Bern or another Swiss canton

The main objective of the short-term L permit is to allow foreign nationals to stay in the Canton of Bern for a limited period of time. Permission is granted only to foreign nationals who meet the conditions established by the authorities of the Canton of Bern.

The conditions for obtaining the short-term L permit include the demonstration of a sufficient economic resource for the stay in the Canton of Bern and the presentation of a residence plan. According to the Law on the Residence of Foreigners (LSS) of 2005, the authority of the Canton of Bern may require a financial guarantee to ensure that the foreign national can support himself during the stay.

The financial guarantee may be required for an amount of up to CHF 10,000, depending on the circumstances. For example, if a foreign national wants to stay in Bern for a period of 3 months for work reasons, the authority of the Canton of Bern may require a financial guarantee of CHF 3,000.

The short-term L permit is valid for a maximum period of 3 months. However, the authority of the Canton of Bern may extend the permit for a further maximum period of 3 months, provided that the foreign national meets the established conditions. According to the Law on the Residence of Foreigners (LSS) of 2005, the authority of the Canton of Bern must justify the extension of the permit with a plausible reason.

The validity of the short-term L permit varies depending on the individual situation of the foreign national. In general, the permit is valid for a limited period of time, which can vary from 1 to 3 months. It is important to note that the permit is not automatically renewable and must be requested again for each new stay.
