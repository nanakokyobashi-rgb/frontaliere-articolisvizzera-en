Key points

Action

If you are a cross-border commuter residing in Switzerland and need to perform military service, you can submit a request for deferment of service or exemption via the FTA website or by submitting a formal request to the FTA. It is important to check the needs of the Church and the possibility of deferment of service before submitting the request.

Requirements for deferral of service

To be eligible to submit a request for a deferral of service, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a minimum age of 18 and a maximum of 30 years of age
  • Have a service commitment of at least 18 months
  • Have no criminal record or have a minor infraction
  • Not have a physical or mental incapacity that may prevent you from performing military service

How to request a service referral

To request a service deferral, you can follow these steps:

  • Log in to the FTA website and fill out the service deferral request form
  • Choose the reason for requesting a deferment of service (e.g., family commitments, studies, or work)
  • Click on the "Submit" button to send the request

Alternatively, you can submit a formal request to the FTA by going to the head office or a local office. It is important to submit your application at least 6 weeks in advance of the start date of your military service.
