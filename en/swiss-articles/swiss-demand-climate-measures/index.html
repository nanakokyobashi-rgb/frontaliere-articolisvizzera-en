More Swiss call for measures against global warming (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 September 2026

After the summer heatwave, the Swiss are calling for concrete measures, such as the renovation of buildings or the installation of solar panels.

Context

In brief

  • 73% of Swiss people call for measures against global warming
  • 80% in favor of energy renovation of buildings
  • 62% support more financial support for agriculture

Key Facts

  • What: Survey on measures against global warming
  • When: August 19-20, 2024
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: 20 Minuten and Tamedia
  • Amount: 73% of respondents

After the summer heatwave, the Swiss are calling for concrete measures, such as the renovation of buildings or the installation of solar panels. This is what emerges from a survey by the online newspaper 20 Minuten and the publisher Tamedia published today. For almost three-quarters of respondents, it is urgent to take measures against global warming. A quarter, on the other hand, do not see the need to do more. Support for strengthening environmental policy is significantly higher among women (60%) than among men (43%).

Energy renovation of buildings

The energy renovation of buildings and the improvement of their insulation are the most widely supported. Four out of five respondents are in favour. The measure even meets with unanimous support across political divisions: it is supported by 89% of Green supporters and 72% of SVP supporters.

Financial support for agriculture

Increased financial support from the federal government for agriculture to compensate for losses caused by heat and drought is also convincing for 62% of respondents. In addition, 58% would like the federal government to promote night trains more.

Operational details

Practical analysis

The survey reveals a significant gender split in support for environmental measures. Women are more likely to support a strengthening of environmental policy than men. This could influence future policy decisions and priorities of the federal government.

Implications for the energy renovation of buildings

Strong support for the energy renovation of buildings could lead to new policies and incentives for the renovation and installation of solar panels. This could have a significant impact on the construction sector and employment in this sector.

Support for agriculture

Financial support for agriculture could help farmers recoup losses caused by heat and drought. This could also lead to greater sustainability in the agricultural sector, with the adoption of practices that are more resilient to climate change.

Popular initiatives

Popular initiatives that have received strong support could influence the country's future environmental and financial policies. The collection of signatures for the Green and Traffic initiatives is still ongoing, and their success could lead to significant changes in environmental policies and public transport.
