Border care assistant in Ticino: requirements and recognition

The Agreement between Switzerland and the European Union (EU) provides for the creation of a qualification recognition regime for border workers who have completed a training course as a care assistant. This scheme will be managed by the AFC/ESTV and it will be possible to apply for it within one year of entering Switzerland.

In order to benefit from this scheme, border workers will have to meet certain specific requirements. First, they must have completed a training course as a care assistant in an EU or EFTA (European Agreement on Freedom of Movement of Servants) member country. The training course must be recognised by the AFC/ESTV and must have been completed within one year of entering Switzerland.

In addition, frontier workers must have a good knowledge of the Italian language, which is the official language of the Canton of Ticino, where this regime is applicable. It is also advisable to have a good knowledge of German and French, which are the other two official languages of Switzerland.

Here is a concrete example: if a border worker has completed a training course as a care assistant in Italy and has a good knowledge of the Italian language, he/she may apply for recognition of his/her qualification at the AFC/ESTV within one year of entering Switzerland.

To obtain the G permit, border guards will need to submit an application to the AFC/ESTV and provide the following documents:

  • Copy of passport

The G permit will be valid for a period of 1 year and can be renewed within 30 days of expiry.

The G permit is a necessary document for border workers who wish to work as care assistants in Ticino. This permit is valid only for border workers who reside in the canton of Ticino and wish to work for families or people with disabilities.

Border crossers wishing to obtain the G permit must submit an application to the AFC/ESTV and provide the following documents:

  • Copy of passport
  • Copy of the certificate of residence
  • Copy of the qualification

The G permit will be valid for a period of 1 year and can be renewed within 30 days of expiry. The cost of permit G is CHF 150.00.
