Permit L in Basel Countryside: validity and extension

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 August 2026

The L permit allows work for up to 12 months in Basel-Country. Discover maximum duration, renewal rules, transition to permit B and employer constraints.

Context

TL;DR

  • L permit: maximum validity 12 months, renewable
  • Strictly tied to the specified employer
  • Managed by SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) and the canton
  • Transition to a B permit after 12 months of uninterrupted employment

Key facts

  • What: Short-term L permit for employed foreign workers
  • When: Valid for 12 months, renewable annually
  • Where: Canton of Basel-Landschaft, northern Switzerland
  • Who: SEM and the Cantonal Migration Office
  • Requirements: Employment contract with a Swiss employer, documentation

The short-term L permit allows foreign nationals to work in Switzerland for a maximum of 12 months. In the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, the permit is managed by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) in coordination with the local authorities. It is not an automatic permit: it requires a formal application by the employer to the competent cantonal authorities.

A key characteristic of the L permit is that it is tied to the employer and canton of employment specified in the initial application. The worker cannot freely change employers without a new procedure, nor move to another canton without a request for a change. This restriction reflects the temporary and sector-specific nature of the permit, designed to meet specific labor needs.

Structure and maximum duration

Validity is limited to 12 months from the date of issue. The permit is renewable if the employment relationship continues. However, renewal is not automatic: before expiration, the employer and the Canton of Basel-Landschaft must formally confirm the continuation by submitting a new application to SEM.

After one year of uninterrupted employment, the foreign worker is entitled to transition to a B residence permit, a permit of indefinite duration that is renewable every 5-10 years depending on the canton and nationality. This transition is not automatic but requires a formal application by the employer to the competent authorities.

Operational details

Maximum duration and renewal mechanism

The L permit is intended for temporary and short-term work needs. The 12-month validity period is non-negotiable: each initial application may not exceed this period. If the employment relationship continues, the permit may be renewed multiple times, theoretically without limit, as long as the employer requests its renewal.

Renewal is not automatic. The employer must submit a new application at least 30 days before the current permit expires. If this application is not submitted, the permit expires and the worker loses the right to stay and work in Switzerland. It is the employer’s responsibility (with support from the cantonal authorities) to manage the administrative timelines to avoid interruptions.

Conditions for extension

The extension of the L permit depends on administrative and economic factors. The Canton of Basel-Landschaft assesses the continuity of the employment relationship and its consistency with the needs of the local labor market. If the employer demonstrates that they still need the foreign worker and there are no legal violations, renewal is usually approved.

However, renewal may be denied if the employer ceases operations or changes its legal form, there are outstanding contribution payments (AVS/AI/IPG 5.3% for the employee, 10.6% total with the employer), the worker has violated the conditions of residence (for example, by working for another employer without authorization), or the canton’s economic situation changes significantly.

Each renewal application requires document verification, consultation of demographic and employment data, and coordination between SEM and the cantonal offices.

Transition from the L permit to the B permit

After one year (12 months) of uninterrupted employment, the foreign worker becomes eligible for a B residence permit. This is an important milestone: the B permit is more stable and renewable every 5-10 years (for EU/EFTA citizens generally 5 years; for others, 10 years).

The transition from L to B is not automatic. The employer must submit a new application to the authorities in Basel-Landschaft, specifying that the employment relationship is stable and intended to continue beyond the initial 12 months. SEM assesses the application and, if accepted, issues the B permit.

The B permit offers tangible benefits: longer validity (5-10 years instead of 12 months), greater freedom to change employers (with notification, but with less stringent procedures), and the right to remain even in the event of temporary unemployment, subject to specific limits and deadlines.

Key points

How to apply for the L permit: step-by-step procedure

The application for the L permit always starts with the employer, not the worker. The Swiss employer must initiate the process with the competent authorities of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, generally the cantonal migration office.

Phase 1: Preparation of the documentation. The employer prepares the file containing a copy of the employment contract (with place of work, position, duration and salary), identity documents of the foreign worker (passport, residence certificates if required), certificates of good conduct in some cases, and documentation concerning the company (commercial registration, articles of association).

Phase 2: Submission of the application to the canton. The employer submits the file to the competent cantonal authority in Basel-Landschaft for work permits (usually the Migration Office or Population Office). SEM intervenes in an advisory capacity for non-EU/EFTA citizens.

Phase 3: Assessment and issuance. The cantonal authority examines the application, checks the local labour market situation, and approves or rejects it within 2-6 weeks. If approved, the L permit is issued with a validity of 12 months from the date of issue.

Critical deadlines: what to remember

The L permit has start and end dates clearly indicated on the document. Renewal must be requested at least 30 days before expiry. If the employer forgets this deadline, the permit expires and the worker can no longer work legally in Switzerland.

The renewal procedure is similar to the initial application, but faster (usually 2-4 weeks) because the canton already has the data on file. The employer submits an extension application, attaching confirmation that the employment relationship is continuing (letter from the employer), an updated copy of the contract if it has been modified, and a certificate of contributions paid (AVS/AI/IPG and disability insurance).

Social contributions and administrative obligations

The L permit is issued free of charge by the canton. However, the employer is required to pay social contributions: AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3% for the employee (10.6% total including the employer), AD/AC at 1.1% up to the annual maximum. In addition, the employer must insure the worker with a mandatory cassa malati (LAMal) within 3 months of the start of employment, and pay SUVA contributions (accidents) if relevant to the sector.

These obligations are not specific to the L permit but apply to every lawful employment relationship in Switzerland. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the maximum duration of the L permit in Basel Countryside?
The L permit is valid for a maximum of 12 months and is renewable if the employment relationship continues. The extension is not automatic: the employer must submit a new request to the SEM and the canton of Basel-Country at least 30 days before the deadline to avoid the interruption of the right to work.
Can I change employer with an L permit?
No, the L permit is closely linked to the employer indicated in the initial application. To change employer, a new full permit application is required. Transfer to another canton also requires a formal amendment procedure with the competent authorities.
After 12 months of permit L, can I move to permit B?
Yes, after 12 months of uninterrupted employment the employer can request the transition to permit B, renewable every 5-10 years depending on nationality (5 years for EU/EFTA, 10 years for other citizens). The transition is not automatic: it requires a formal request to the authorities in Basel-Country.
How many renewals can I do with an L permit?
Theoretically, the L permit can be renewed several times as long as the employment relationship continues and the employer requests its renewal. However, after 12 months it is usual and recommended to move to permit B to obtain greater administrative stability and broader rights.
Who handles the application and renewal of the L permit in Basel Countryside?
The request is handled by the employer in coordination with the Migration Office of the Basel-Country Canton. The SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) intervenes at the federal level only for non-EU/EFTA citizens and in the consultative phase.
