Permit L in Basel Countryside: validity and extension (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 August 2026

The L permit allows work for up to 12 months in Basel-Country. Discover maximum duration, renewal rules, transition to permit B and employer constraints.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Permit L: maximum validity 12 months, renewable
  • Closely related to the specified employer
  • Managed by SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) and canton
  • Transition to permit B after 12 months of uninterrupted work

Key facts

  • What: Short-term L leave for foreign employment
  • When: Valid for 12 months, renewable annually
  • Where: Canton Basel-Country, northern Switzerland
  • Who: SEM and Cantonal Migration Office
  • Requirements: Employment contract with Swiss employer, documentation

The short-term L permit allows foreign nationals to work in Switzerland for up to 12 months. In the Basel-Country Canton the permit is managed by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) in coordination with the local authorities. It is not an automatic permit: it requires a formal request from the employer to the competent cantonal authorities.

A key feature of the L permit is the constraint on the employer and the canton of employment specified in the initial application. The worker cannot freely change employer without a new procedure, nor move to another canton without a change request. This constraint reflects the temporary and sectoral nature of the permit, designed to meet specific work needs.

Operational details

Maximum duration and renewal mechanism

The L permit is structured for temporary and short work needs. The 12 months of validity are non-negotiable: any initial request cannot exceed this period. If the employment relationship continues, the permit can be renewed several times, theoretically without limit, until the employer requests its renewal.

Renewal is not automatic. The employer must submit a new application at least 30 days before the expiry of the current permit. If this request is not submitted, the permit expires and the worker loses the right to stay and work in Switzerland. It is the responsibility of the employer (with the support of the cantonal authorities) to manage administrative time to avoid interruptions.

Conditions for extension

The extension of the L permit depends on administrative and economic factors. The Canton of Basel-Country assesses the continuity of the employment relationship and consistency with the needs of the local labour market. If the employer proves to continue to need the foreign worker and if there are no violations of the law, the renewal is usually approved.

Key points

How to request the L permit: step-by-step procedure

The L permit request starts with the employer, not the employee. The Swiss employer must initiate the process with the competent authorities of the Canton Basel-Country, usually the cantonal migration office.

Phase 1: Preparation of the documentation. The employer prepares the file containing a copy of the employment contract (with workplace, job description, duration, salary), the employee's foreign identity documents (passport, residence certificates if required), good conduct certificates in some cases, and company documentation (commercial registration, statutes).

Phase 2: Submission of the request to the canton. The employer submits the file to the competent cantonal authority in Basel-Country responsible for work permits (usually the Migration Office or Population Office). SEM intervenes in a consultative capacity for non-EU/EFTA citizens.

Phase 3: Evaluation and issuance. The cantonal authority examines the request, verifies the local labor market situation, and approves or rejects it within 2-6 weeks. If approved, the L permit is issued with a validity period of 12 months from the date of issuance.

Critical deadlines: what to remember

The L permit has clearly indicated start and end dates on the document. Renewal must be requested at least 30 days before the expiration date. If the employer forgets this deadline, the permit expires, and the employee can no longer work legally in Switzerland.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the maximum duration of the L permit in Basel Countryside?
The L permit is valid for a maximum of 12 months and is renewable if the employment relationship continues. The extension is not automatic: the employer must submit a new request to the SEM and the canton of Basel-Country at least 30 days before the deadline to avoid the interruption of the right to work.
Can I change employer with an L permit?
No, the L permit is closely linked to the employer indicated in the initial application. To change employer, a new full permit application is required. Transfer to another canton also requires a formal amendment procedure with the competent authorities.
After 12 months of permit L, can I move to permit B?
Yes, after 12 months of uninterrupted employment the employer can request the transition to permit B, renewable every 5-10 years depending on nationality (5 years for EU/EFTA, 10 years for other citizens). The transition is not automatic: it requires a formal request to the authorities in Basel-Country.
How many renewals can I do with an L permit?
Theoretically, the L permit can be renewed several times as long as the employment relationship continues and the employer requests its renewal. However, after 12 months it is usual and recommended to move to permit B to obtain greater administrative stability and broader rights.
Who handles the application and renewal of the L permit in Basel Countryside?
The request is handled by the employer in coordination with the Migration Office of the Basel-Country Canton. The SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) intervenes at the federal level only for non-EU/EFTA citizens and in the consultative phase.
