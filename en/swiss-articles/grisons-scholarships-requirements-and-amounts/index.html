# Scholarship Application Requirements

To be eligible for scholarships, candidates must meet the following requirements:

  • Being students of the Canton of Graubünden
  • Have a good academic performance
  • Have no other sources of funding for their training
  • Be a resident of the Canton of Graubünden

How to apply for a scholarship

To apply for a scholarship, applicants must submit an application to the competent cantonal office by the specified date. The application must be accompanied by:
