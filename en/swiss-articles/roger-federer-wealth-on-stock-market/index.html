Key points

For Swiss residents who own shares or corporate participations, it is essential to understand how these assets affect their overall tax position. In Switzerland there is no federal tax on assets, but it is levied at the cantonal and communal level. The change in Federer's wealth, although it occurred on a foreign market such as Wall Street, has direct implications for his tax return and asset declaration in Switzerland. Taxpayers must declare the market value of their securities, and fluctuations like the 19% recorded by On can significantly alter the annual tax burden.

Procedure for declaring securities

When a loss in value occurs in one's portfolio of shares, it is important to follow the procedures set by the cantonal tax authorities. Here are the key steps for Swiss taxpayers: 1. Obtain the official list of courses published by the Federal Tax Administration (AFC/ESTV) for the fiscal year closure. 2. Correctly enter each held security in the list of securities of one's tax return. 3. Check whether losses on the stock exchange can be offset with possible capital gains, keeping in mind that for private individuals, capital gains on movable assets are usually tax-exempt, but losses are not deductible from taxable income. 4. Consult the annual bank statements to ensure that the declared value matches the one recorded on December 31.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why is Roger Federer no longer a billionaire?
According to Forbes estimates, Federer's wealth fell to $949 million due to the collapse of On a Wall Street's stock. The Swiss footwear company, of which Federer is a partner, recorded a drop of up to 19% in a single day after the publication of quarterly revenues below analysts' expectations.
What is Federer's share of the On company?
Roger Federer is estimated to hold an equity stake of between 2.5% and 3% in the Zurich-based company On. The former tennis player joined the company in 2019 as a strategic investor and partner, contributing to its global expansion.
What caused the collapse of the On stock on the stock market?
The stock suffered a sharp decline, losing about 19%, as a result of the dissemination of quarterly financial results. Despite an increase in turnover, overall revenues did not reach the levels expected by investors, causing a negative reaction on international financial markets.
