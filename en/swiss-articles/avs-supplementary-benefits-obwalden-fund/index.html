Swiss social security is based on a three-pillar system, and at the top is the AVS (Old Age and Survivors Insurance), which covers all residents in Switzerland through cantonal or professional compensation funds. In the Canton of Obvaldo, the cantonal compensation fund is the body responsible for the collection of AVS/AI/IPG contributions, the calculation of annuities and the investigation of applications for supplementary benefits (PC), as required by federal law applied at the cantonal level.

Operational details

Analysis: how the first pillar in Obvaldo really works

The first Swiss pillar is a pay-as-you-go system: contributions paid by active workers finance the current income of current retirees. In small cantons such as Obvaldo — just over 38,000 inhabitants — the cantonal clearing house plays a central role not only as an administrative counter, but as a single point of contact for employees, independents and employers. The fund applies the same federal rules that apply in Zurich, Geneva or Lugano, but it does so on a small scale, with a proximity relationship that often simplifies access for users.

Practical implications for those residing or working in Switzerland

The AVS is not an individual capitalization system, but a mandatory insurance with annuity calculated on the years of contribution. For employees, the AVS/AI/IPG deduction — as required by federal law — takes place automatically in the paycheck, with part to be paid by the employee and part to be paid by the employer (see wage contributions in official sources). There are no cantonal exceptions: regardless of whether the salary is paid in Sarnen, Lucerne or Bern, the rate is the same throughout the territory.

Key points

What to do: operational guide for residents and workers

Anyone who works or has worked in Switzerland, even for a few years, should approach the topic of OASI (AVS) and supplementary benefits (PC) in a structured way. Below is a concrete, step-by-step procedure valid for anyone residing or having worked in the Canton of Obwalden.

Check your Individual Account (CI)

The first step is to request an extract of your Individual Account (CI) from the cantonal compensation fund. The CI extract shows all recorded credits, years of contributions, and any gaps. It can be requested online through the fund's portal or by post. It is advisable to do this at least once every five years, or in any case before approaching retirement age.

If the CI extract shows missing income or errors, you can submit corrective documents (pay slips, salary certificates, foreign statements). Corrections are possible as long as the CI has not been crystallised for the calculation of the pension.

Calculating the OASI (AVS) pension

To estimate your future pension, you can use the official calculator provided by the Confederation (available on the FSIO/BSV website). The calculation provides an estimate based on current credits and those still missing until reference age. It is an indicative, not binding tool: the definitive calculation is the one issued by the compensation fund at the time of the pension application.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Canton Obvaldo Compensation Fund?
It is the competent cantonal body for the collection of AVS/AI/IPG contributions, the calculation of annuities and the investigation of applications for supplementary benefits (PC) in the Canton Obvaldo, as required by federal law applied at cantonal level. The same federal rules apply in all Swiss cantons.
How is the AVS annuity calculated in Obvaldo?
The annuity is calculated on the individual account (CI), which records all taxable income and credits for educational or welfare tasks. At the time of application, the cantonal fund examines the CI and applies the scale of annuities provided for by federal law, with bands decided at the national level and periodically adjusted to the mixed wage/price index. The amount of the maximum annuities currently in force is not specified in the source and must be verified year by year on the official web
Is it possible to redeem missing contribution years?
Yes, federal law provides for the possibility of redemption under certain conditions, with effect on the scale of annuities. Obvaldo's compensation fund provides the redemption estimates and manages the related practices. Redemption is not always possible: in the presence of an existing annuity or special personal conditions, the doors may shrink or close.
What is the purpose of complementary performance (PC)?
PCs are the tool with which the Confederation, through the cantons, ensures that the AVS annuity, alone, covers the minimum living. Those who only receive a partial pension or have to pay sickness or hospital expenses can supplement their income with PCs. The application must be submitted to the cantonal fund, which verifies the applicant's income and substance according to the federal provisions of the LPC.
How do I apply for the AVS annuity in Canton Obvaldo?
The application is submitted to the cantonal fund a few months before the age of reference. You should first check your individual account statement (IC), which can be requested online or by mail, to identify any gaps or errors. To estimate the future annuity, you can use the official UFAS/BSV calculator; the final calculation is the one made by the cashier at the time of application.
