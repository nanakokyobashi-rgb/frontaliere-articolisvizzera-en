Frontier workers, tax war with Ticino: Bellinzona blocks 50 million refills

The Council of State of the Canton of Ticino has officially announced the precautionary suspension of 46% of the refunds of taxes at source due to Italy for the current year. A cut of 50 million Swiss francs (compared to a partial payment of 58.8 million), in reaction to the so-called 'health tax' that the Lombardy Region intends to impose on so-called old border workers with G permits.

The decision of the Ticino State Council was taken following an emergency meeting with representatives of the Lombardy Region and the Italian government. "We cannot tolerate a measure that penalizes our compatriots without adequate debate and consultation with the Swiss authorities," said the President of the State Council of Ticino, Guido Ghirardi.

The health tax, which provides for a payment of 500 euros per year for each border worker, has been the subject of criticism from border workers and their representatives. "It is a discriminatory measure and does not respect the rights of border workers," said the President of the Swiss Border Association, Franco Morandi.
