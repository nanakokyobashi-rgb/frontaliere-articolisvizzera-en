Operational details

Finding the ideal internship position

The search for an internship position in the Canton of Vaud takes place through various channels. Cantonal professional associations and chambers of commerce play a central role, often publishing internship offers on their online portals or organizing fairs dedicated to vocational training. Vocational schools themselves also provide active support in the search, making available information about partner companies and available positions. It is advisable to start the search well in advance, usually a year before the expected start of the internship, as the most sought-after applications are often filled quickly. Preparing a complete and personalized application dossier, which includes a detailed curriculum vitae and a convincing motivation letter, is essential to stand out. An orientation interview or a trial period in a company can represent further steps in the selection process, allowing both the candidate and the employer to assess mutual suitability.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I find an internship in the Canton of Vaud?
The search for an internship in the Canton of Vaud is carried out through cantonal professional associations, chambers of commerce, dedicated online portals and vocational schools. It is advisable to start the search well in advance, preparing a complete application dossier.
What rules the internship contract?
The compulsory internship contract defines the profession, duration, time, salary, holidays and training objectives. It must comply with Swiss cantonal and federal regulations.
What is the average salary of an apprentice in Vaud?
The remuneration of an apprentice in the Canton of Vaud varies according to the professional sector, the year of training and the company. It generally increases progressively during the apprenticeship years, starting from a few hundred to over a thousand Swiss francs per month in the early years.
Is it possible to obtain professional maturity after an apprenticeship in the Vaud?
Yes, for many professions it is possible to achieve professional maturity at the end of the apprenticeship in the Canton of Vaud. This degree opens the doors to studies at University Professional Schools (SUPs).
