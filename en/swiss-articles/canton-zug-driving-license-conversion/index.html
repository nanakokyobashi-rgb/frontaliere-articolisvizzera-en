Context

In short

The conversion of the foreign driving license is mandatory for cross-border commuters residing in the Canton of Zug. The Canton of Zug requires cross-border commuters to have their foreign driving license converted in order to drive in the Canton of Zug.

Key facts

  • What: Conversion of foreign driving license
  • Where: Canton of Zug
  • Who: Cross-border workers

The conversion of the foreign driving license is an obligation for cross-border commuters residing in the Canton of Zug.

Why is conversion necessary?

Conversion of your foreign driving license is necessary to ensure road safety and comply with local regulations. This is necessary to ensure that cross-border commuters hold a valid driving license and are able to drive safely in the Canton of Zug.

How does the conversion work?

Converting your foreign driving license is a quick and easy process. Cross-border commuters must present their foreign driving license at the driving section of the Canton of Zug and undergo a knowledge test of Swiss traffic law. Once the foreign driving license has been converted, the cross-border commuter will be able to drive in the Canton of Zug without any restrictions.

What are the conversion costs?

The costs of converting a foreign driving license vary depending on the canton of residence. In the canton of Zug, conversion costs are around 50-100 CHF. It is important to note that these costs may vary depending on the canton of residence and that cross-border commuters should contact the driving section of the Canton of Zug to obtain more precise information on costs.

What are the consequences if you don't undergo conversion?

If a cross-border commuter does not have his foreign driving license converted, he will not be able to drive in the Canton of Zug and may be fined. Furthermore, if a cross-border commuter is stopped by the police for driving without a valid driving license, he or she may be fined and may even lose the right to drive in the Canton of Zug.
