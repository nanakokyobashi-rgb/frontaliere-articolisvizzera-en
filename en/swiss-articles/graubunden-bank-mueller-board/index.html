The Rhaetian Government has chosen Reto Müller for a seat on the Bank Council of Banca cantonale grigione (BCG). With the same decision, Michèle F. Sutter-Rüdisser’s term as a member of the Bank Council was extended by another four years, and she was designated vice president of the body. These are decisions concerning the institution’s highest governing body, announced in a communication from the executive.

For the reader following banche in Svizzera, the central point is the future composition of the Bank Council. The note focuses on the body’s composition and does not address products, services or customer terms. The timetable is already specified precisely, although the changeover is not immediate.

The succession and the dates

Müller will take office on July 1, 2027, and will remain in the position until June 30, 2031. After three terms of office, Christoph Caviezel will leave the position because of the limit set for the duration of the term. The text therefore distinguishes the appointment from the actual start of the term.

The Government expresses its gratitude to Caviezel for his long, valuable and fruitful service as a member and vice president of the Bank Council. In the text, his departure is attributed to the limit set for the duration of the term, not to any other reason.

The profiles set out in the note

Müller’s profile includes many years of experience in the financial sector. He held various managerial positions at Credit Suisse and UBS for more than thirty years. The text does not attribute to him any position other than that of a member of the Bank Council.

Since 2021, Sutter-Rüdisser has been a member of the Bank Council. At the School of Management of the University of St. Gallen, she is a professor of economics and also serves on several boards of directors. The extension of her term and her appointment as vice president renew and broaden her position on the same body.

Operational details

The practical significance of the decision lies in the distinction between continuity and succession. On the one hand, the body retains Sutter-Rüdisser, who has served on it since 2021 and is designated vice president. On the other, it indicates Müller's future entry within a defined time window and Caviezel's departure after three terms. The source allows the changeover to be read as a planned transition, without turning it into an immediate change for those looking at BCG from the outside.
