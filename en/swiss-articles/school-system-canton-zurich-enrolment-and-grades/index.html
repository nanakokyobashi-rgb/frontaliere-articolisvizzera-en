The school system of the Canton of Zurich is characterized by a 9-year compulsory education cycle. Students attend elementary schools from 1st to 6th grade, followed by middle and high schools. The school system is based on the principle of free choice of schools and languages of instruction.

Elementary Schools

Swiss students attend primary schools from 1st to 6th grade, aged between 6 and 12 years. Elementary schools were introduced in 1848, thanks to the adoption of the Swiss Federal Constitution. Elementary schools are responsible for providing a solid foundation for basic learning, including national languages (German, French, Italian, and Romance) and mathematics.

Operational details

The school system of the Canton of Zurich is based on the principle of free choice of schools and languages of instruction. Students may choose to attend schools other than those indicated by the school system. In addition, students can choose to study languages other than the one indicated by the school system. This approach is critical to ensuring that students receive an education that is personalized and tailored to their needs.

According to 2022 data, 70% of students in the Canton of Zurich attend public schools, while 30% attend private schools. This indicates that the free choice of schools is a reality very present in the Canton of Zurich.

Students who choose to attend schools other than those indicated by the school system must submit an application for enrollment to the local School Board. The request must be accompanied by a written justification and a description of the school the student intends to attend.

The Education Act of the Canton of Zurich, dated 1 January 2015, states that students have the right to choose the language of instruction. Students can choose to study in German, French, Italian or English. In addition, students may choose to study in a language other than the one indicated by the school system, provided that it is an official language of Switzerland.

Key points

To enroll in schools in the Canton of Zurich, students must submit an application for enrollment by August 15 of each year. The application for registration must be submitted at least one month before the start of classes. Students must also submit a birth certificate and vaccination certificate.

# Application

The application for registration is a fundamental step for enrolling in the schools of the Canton of Zurich. Students must fill out the application form carefully and submit it within the established deadline. It is important to note that the application for enrollment must be submitted at least one month before the start of classes, so that you can complete the enrollment procedures on time.

Concrete example

Suppose a parent wants to enroll their child in elementary school in Zurich. In this case, the parent should submit the application for enrollment by August 15 of each year. If the enrollment application is submitted by July 15, the parent will have time to complete the enrollment procedures in time for the start of classes.
