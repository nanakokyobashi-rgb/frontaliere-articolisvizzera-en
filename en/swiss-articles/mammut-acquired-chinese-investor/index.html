Mammoth changes ownership: Chinese investor takes over brand (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 11 August 2026

The historic Swiss outdoor clothing brand Mammut passes to the Chinese company CPE. The deal, according to Bloomberg, is worth about 463 million francs.

Context

Briefly

  • Mammut acquired by Chinese investor CPE from British Jacobs Capital.
  • Transaction estimated by Bloomberg at 500 million euros (463 million Swiss francs).
  • Headquarters and management of the company will remain stable in Switzerland in Seon (AG).
  • Objective: Strategic expansion towards Asian markets.

Key Facts

  • What: Acquisition of Mammut by CPE.
  • When: Official statement released today.
  • Where: Main headquarters in Seon (AG), Switzerland.
  • Who: CPE (Chinese investor) acquires from Jacobs Capital (UK).
  • Amount: Approximately 463 million Swiss francs (500 million euros).

The well-known Swiss brand Mammut, founded in 1862 and specialized in mountain equipment and clothing, has officially announced a change in ownership. The British investment company Jacobs Capital, which held control of the company, has transferred the entire share package to CPE, a prominent international Chinese investor. The announcement, made through an official press release, did not provide specific financial details on the transaction, although the Bloomberg agency reported an estimated sale price of around 500 million euros, equivalent to approximately 463 million Swiss francs.

…

Operational details

The acquisition of Mammut by a Chinese financial giant like CPE opens up new scenarios for the positioning of the Swiss brand in the global market. The declared goal of the new owners is an aggressive expansion in China and the Asia-Pacific region, markets that present a growing demand for high-quality products related to outdoor and mountain sports. This strategy will focus on adapting the offer to the specificities of local markets and optimizing the entire supply chain. For Swiss workers, the operation raises legitimate questions about the protection of local know-how, although the ownership has assured the maintenance of management and the Seon headquarters.

Key points

For employees and stakeholders directly involved in Mammut's business dynamics, or for anyone managing similar corporate changes, the priority should remain verifying one's contractual position. In Switzerland, corporate acquisitions do not automatically change individual employment contracts, as Swiss law protects the continuity of the employment relationship in the event of company transfer. It is however advisable, during transitional phases, to maintain constant control over one's salary and the correct application of compulsory social contributions, such as professional pension (LPP) and AVS/AI payments.

Frequently Asked Questions
What changes for Mammut headquarters?
Despite the transfer of ownership in favor of CPE, the company has officially confirmed that the operational headquarters and management will remain in Switzerland, specifically in Seon, in the Canton of Aargau.
What is the estimated value of the transaction?
Although the official press release does not report figures, the Bloomberg agency reported an estimated sale of 500 million euros, corresponding to about 463 million Swiss francs.
What are the goals of the new owner?
CPE aims to expand the Mammut brand in the Chinese and Asia-Pacific markets, focusing on product innovation, adaptation to local markets and supply chain optimization.
