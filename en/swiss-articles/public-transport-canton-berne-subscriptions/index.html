Operational details

Public transport passes in the Canton of Bern are calculated according to the area of residence and destination. Annual and monthly subscriptions are available.

Zones of residence and destination

The Canton of Bern is divided into several zones for the purchase of public transport passes. These zones are determined by the location of the residence and destination. The zones are:

  • Zone A: Bern city centre and nearby urban areas
  • Zone B: Cities and municipalities bordering Bern
  • Zone C: Rural and mountain municipalities

Rates and subscriptions

The rates for public transport passes in the Canton of Bern are as follows:

  • Zone A annual subscription: CHF 2'320 (approx. € 2'050) - Zone B annual subscription: CHF 1'840 (approx. € 1'630) - Zone C annual subscription: CHF 1'440 (approx. € 1'270) - Zone A monthly subscription: CHF 190 (approx. € 165) - Zone B monthly subscription: CHF 150 (approx. € 130) - Zone C monthly subscription: CHF 120 (approx. € 105)

Regulations and rules

The regulations for public transport subscriptions in the Canton of Bern are regulated by the Federal Law of 18 December 1997 on Road Traffic and Road Traffic (LSA). The law states that public transport passes must be purchased by residents and visitors.

The Canton of Bern is one of the Swiss cantons with an efficient and well-organized public transport system. The bus and train network is extensive and covers almost all cities and municipalities in the canton. Public transport passes are one of the most popular options for getting around the Canton of Bern.

Subscription Zones

The Canton of Bern is divided into several subscription zones, each with a specific rate. The zones are indicated with a numerical code and are divided into three categories:

  • Urban areas: These areas include the cities of Bern, Freiburg and Lausanne, and are identified by codes 1, 2 and 3. Suburban areas: these areas include municipalities and cities located outside urban areas, and are identified with codes 4, 5 and 6. Special areas: these areas include mountain areas and high altitude areas, and are identified with codes 7 and 8.

Subscription Fees

Rates for public transport passes in the canton of Bern are determined by the cantonal Council of State. Rates are subject to change on an annual basis and may be adjusted based on public service needs.
