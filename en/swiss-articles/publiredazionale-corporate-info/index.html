The format called #publiredazionale constitutes an information space managed and published by the newspaper laRegione, within which companies have the opportunity to communicate directly with the public of readers. This space, clearly identified within the editorial structure, aims to give a voice to economic realities that wish to share updates, company news or institutional communications that go beyond traditional journalistic reporting.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is meant by publishing on the Region?
It is an information space marked as #publiredazionale, where companies can communicate directly with readers. These contents are managed by advertisers independently and are clearly distinguished from the journalistic activity of the editorial staff of the Region, guaranteeing transparency to the reader who accesses these news.
Is the information in the editorial boards verified by the editorial staff?
The content published in this space represents the point of view of the companies that provide information. Although they are included in the context of the magazine, they do not constitute an independent journalistic analysis. It is always advisable to distinguish between the promotional message of the company and the editorial information, consulting official data to deepen economic or tax issues.
