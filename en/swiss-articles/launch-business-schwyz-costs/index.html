Starting a business in the Canton of Svitto requires compliance with precise administrative procedures and mandatory regulations. Registration in the cantonal trade register is the first mandatory step for those who intend to operate as an entrepreneur in the Swiss territory.

The Swiss Canton, like all Swiss cantons, applies a tax regime divided into three levels: direct federal taxes managed by AFC/ESTV, its own cantonal taxes and municipal taxes that apply specific multipliers on the cantonal value. The choice of the legal form of its business has a profound impact on these administrative obligations and start-up costs.

Operational details

Enrollment procedure and cantonal taxes

Registration in the commercial register of the Canton of Schwyz is an administrative requirement that must be completed before starting the business. Registration fees vary according to the legal form chosen and according to the municipal location within the canton. In Switzerland, taxation works on three distinct levels: the federal direct tax (IFD) managed by AFC/ESTV at the central level, the cantonal taxes determined by the canton of Switzerland, and the municipal taxes where each municipality applies a multiplier on the cantonal rates.

Parallel to the registration, it is necessary to check with the Swiss cantonal authorities what are the specific costs and taxes that will be levied on your business in the specific territory where you intend to operate. For effective management of the initial budget, it is essential to request a detailed estimate of tax charges from the cantonal and municipal administration of your headquarters. Numerous businesses underestimate these costs in the start-up financial plan.

Minimum capital and administrative implications

The minimum capital required depends directly on the legal form chosen. Sole proprietorships have no initial capital requirements, while limited liability companies (Sàrl and SA) have specific requirements under Swiss federal law.

Key points

Step-by-Step Procedure

Step 1: Decide on the most suitable legal form for your business project (sole proprietorship, partnership, Sàrl, SA). This choice affects personal liability, capital requirements, tax burden, and subsequent administrative obligations.

Step 2: Verify the commercial name with the competent office to ensure it is not already used by other operators in the register.

Step 3: Prepare the necessary administrative documentation, which generally includes identification documents, proof of domicile, official statement of business commencement, and in the case of a company, the relevant statutes.

Step 4: File the registration request with the competent registration office of the Canton of Schwyz. Processing times may vary depending on the provided documentation.

Step 5: Obtain confirmation of registration and the company's identification number (CHE) required for subsequent administrative communications.

Step 6: Register with the AVS/AHV compensation fund (basic pension) as required by Swiss law.

Mandatory Insurance and Pension Obligations

Anyone opening a business with employees must comply with specific mandatory insurance and pension obligations under Swiss labor law. Mandatory health insurance (LAMal/KVG) is required for all residents in Switzerland and is a private insurance policy with a per-capita premium managed by authorized private institutions (not a tax or salary contribution). It must be taken out within 3 months of business commencement if the owner is already a resident in Switzerland.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which legal form to choose to open a business in the Canton of Schwyz?
The choice depends on the type of activity and the desired organisational structure. The sole proprietorship is the simplest form (no minimum capital required), suitable for freelancers and small businesses. Companies (SNC, Sàrl, SA) are indicated when you operate in partnership or need limited liability. The Sàrl and SA have minimum capital requirements set by federal law. Consult the cantonal administration for the choice that best suits your business model.
What are the main costs of registering with the commercial register of the Canton of Schwyz?
Registration costs vary according to the legal form and municipal location within the canton. In Switzerland, taxes operate at three levels: federal (AFC/ESTV), cantonal (Svitto has its own rates) and municipal (each municipality applies specific multipliers). It is advisable to request a detailed quote from the competent administrative office of your municipality and the cantonal administration.
What insurance obligations do I have to comply with as an employer with employees?
If you hire employees, you must enroll them in: (1) LAMal/compulsory health insurance (private premium per capita policy, no tax); (2) LPP/BVG occupational pension (7-18% according to age); (3) AD/AC unemployment insurance (1.1%); (4) LAINF/LAA accident insurance (0.7–1.5% according to sector, managed by SUVA); (5) AVS/AI/IPG contributions (5.3% employee, 10.6% total with employer). Check with UFAS/BSV for current details.
Do I need to register for VAT before starting the business in Svitto?
VAT registration with AFC/ESTV is mandatory if the annual turnover exceeds certain thresholds set by federal law. VAT rates are fixed at the federal level and applied uniformly throughout Switzerland. AFC/ESTV centrally manages value added tax. Check with the administration if your expected turnover falls within the threshold limits.
How long does it take to register with the commercial register of the Canton of Switzerland?
Registration times vary depending on the legal form and completeness of the documentation provided. Generally, the procedure can be completed within a few working days of submission of the complete documentation. It is advisable to contact the registration office of the Canton of Schwyz directly to find out the current times and the exact documents required.
