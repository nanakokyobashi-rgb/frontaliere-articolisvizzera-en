Family allowances Internal Appenzello: amounts and application (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 21 August 2026

Complete guide to family allowances in the Inner Appenzell Canton: federal system, monthly amounts per child, legal conditions, compensation fund and step-by-step application procedure.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Family allowances are federal benefits paid for children up to the age of 16, or 25 if in training
  • Amounts vary according to cantonal decisions and are updated annually
  • The application is submitted to the competent cantonal clearing house

Key facts

  • What: Federal benefits for families with children in Switzerland
  • When: Up to 16 years of age of the child, 25 years if in vocational training
  • Where: At the cantonal clearing houses of Appenzello Interno
  • Who: Parents and guardians with lucrative activity in Switzerland
  • Basic: Federal Child Benefit Act

Family allowances are a fundamental social benefit of the Swiss social security system. In the Inner Appenzell Canton, as in all cantons, they are disbursed according to a harmonized federal system but with specific amounts established by the canton itself.

The system is based on the federal law on child benefits, which guarantees minimum protection at the national level. The benefits include both the actual family allowances (for the general maintenance of the child) and the vocational training allowances (for those attending a recognized vocational starting school).

Operational details

How do the amounts vary between cantons?

Unlike income tax (federal + cantonal + municipal DFIs), family allowances do not follow an income-proportional system. Rather, the canton sets a FIXED monthly amount per child, which is paid regardless of the parent's income level. This system guarantees equity for families regardless of their economic situation.

This cantonal variability is significant. A parent who works in Zurich receives very different amounts than those who work in Geneva or in the Inner Appenzell Canton, with the same number of children. The canton decides independently the basic monthly amount for the first child, the incremental amount for subsequent children (often slightly higher to encourage larger families), and the special amount for children in vocational or university training.

In the Inner Appenzell Canton, as in the other cantons of eastern Switzerland, the amounts reflect the cantonal democratic priorities and decisions in the field of family policy. As these amounts are reviewed annually to take into account inflation and the cantonal economy, it is essential to consult the cantonal clearinghouse directly for current data for 2026.

Key points

Procedure for Requesting Family Allowances Step by Step

The process for requesting family allowances in the Canton of Appenzello Interno follows a standardized procedure at the cantonal compensation fund.

Step 1: Identify and Contact the Competent Compensation Fund

The first action is to contact the cantonal compensation fund competent for Appenzello Interno. This institution provides the official application form and detailed instructions specific to the canton. The contact information is available on the official cantonal administration portal.

Step 2: Complete the Official Application Form

The application form requires accurate data related to the applicant's complete identity (name, surname, date of birth, identification number), each child's identification (name, surname, date of birth, identification number if available), the current residence of the child (must be in Switzerland for federal law), the applicant's employment situation, and the AVS identification number.

Step 3: Attach Required Documentation

The required documentation normally includes a recent extract from the child's civil register (birth certificate or legal filiation certificate), a photocopy of the applicant's identity card, passport, or residence permit, and possibly documentation certifying school enrollment or attendance at a recognized vocational course (for training allowances), and a copy of the employment contract or self-employment certificate (if required by the fund).

Step 4: Submit the Application to the Fund

The application must be submitted to the cantonal compensation fund according to the modalities set by the institution itself: paper-based at the offices, online through the cantonal portal, or certified email. The fund communicates the processing times and official channels.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the exact monthly amount of family allowances in the Inner Appenzell Canton for 2026?
Monthly amounts vary by canton and are updated annually to reflect inflation. In the Inner Appenzell Canton, the specific amounts are officially communicated by the competent cantonal clearing house. For the current figures 2026, it is advisable to contact the cashier directly through the administrative portal of the canton or by telephone. There is no single federal amount, as each canton independently manages the amount it disburses to beneficiaries.
What is the difference between family allowances and training allowances?
Ordinary family allowances are paid until the child reaches the age of 16 and are used for his/her general maintenance. Training allowances constitute a benefit extended up to the age of 25, if the child attends a vocational school, an apprenticeship recognized by the canton, or an accredited university. The amounts of the training allowances are often slightly higher than the ordinary family allowances, to compensate for the higher school expenses.
How do I apply for family allowances in the Inner Appenzell Canton?
The application must be submitted to the competent cantonal clearing house using the official form provided by the institution. The personal data of the child (name, date of birth, residence), the AVS identification number of the applicant and the personal documentation (civil registry extract, identity card) are required. The cashier provides detailed instructions and accepts applications in paper format, online via the cantonal portal, or by email in the manner specified.
What happens if my child turns 16 and does not continue formal training?
The right to ordinary family allowances is automatically interrupted at the age of 16. If the child does not enter a recognized training path (apprenticeship, upper secondary school, high school, university), the checks cease. If, on the other hand, the child continues a recognized training, it is necessary to formally notify the cashier and move on to the extended training allowances, which continue until the age of 25.
Can I claim family allowances even if I work part-time or am self-employed?
Yes. Family allowances are payable to anyone who is gainfully employed in Switzerland, regardless of whether they are a full-time, part-time employee, or self-employed person enrolled in the AVS/AHV. The right is not linked to a minimum income level, but to simple enrollment in the federal social insurance system.
