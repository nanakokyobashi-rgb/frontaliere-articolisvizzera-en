Partial deduction on cross-border worker allowances may break Rome deal

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 16 June 2026 · Updated on 31 October 2026

Switzerland plans to partially deduct from cross-border workers’ health tax refunds, a move that could violate agreements with Italy.

Context

Deduction from cross-border worker rebates contrary to agreements with Rome

The Federal Council has responded to a question from National Councillor Piero Marchesi (SVP/TI) regarding the significance of this Italian health contribution for cross-border workers. The Government notes that the health tax has not yet been introduced.

The so-called health tax is the contribution to the Italian National Health Service set by Law 213/2023 (Art. 1, paragraphs 237-239): from 3% to 6% of the net salary earned in Switzerland, with a minimum of 30 and a maximum of 200 euros for each month worked, set each year by the region of residence.
