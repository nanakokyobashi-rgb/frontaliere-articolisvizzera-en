The dynamic of the events involves a motorcycle driven by an 18-year-old girl and a van used for deliveries. Air rescue procedures and management of critical events

The prompt intervention of the Rega on the occasion of the Amlikon-Bissegg road accident highlights the importance of the Swiss air rescue system in situations of severe traumatic emergency. When life-threatening injuries occur on Swiss roads, the rescue chain is activated according to standardized protocols that coordinate ground ambulance and helicopter services. The operations centre assesses the severity of the injuries reported by the first witnesses or paramedics on site, arranging for the helicopter to be sent to minimise transport times to a hospital equipped with an intensive care unit or a burn and trauma centre.
