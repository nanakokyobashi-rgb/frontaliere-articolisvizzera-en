SSR, the Swiss Broadcasting Corporation, is looking for talent in Ticino to enrich its media universe. The company offers numerous job opportunities in almost all professional categories, creating a stimulating and fulfilling environment for its employees. SSR is a universe for talented people, offering them much to find a pleasant environment in which to grow and thrive.

Job opportunities

SSR is seeking talent in various professional categories. This represents a unique opportunity for cross-border workers who wish to work in Switzerland, particularly in the Canton of Ticino. The company offers a dynamic and innovative work environment, ideal for those who want to grow professionally.

Operational details

Working for SSR means being part of a diverse and engaging media universe. The company is always up-to-date and focused on digital, offering its employees the opportunity to express themselves and take on responsibilities in their own way. This is only possible because SSR is open and cultivates personal relationships.

Work environment

The work environment at SSR is stimulating and satisfying. Employees feel they belong to a universe that values and helps them grow professionally. SSR offers many opportunities to find a pleasant place to work and realize themselves.

Benefits for cross-border workers

Working in Ticino for SSR offers numerous benefits for cross-border workers. In addition to a stimulating work environment, cross-border workers can benefit from a competitive salary and professional growth opportunities. Moreover, proximity to Italy facilitates daily commutes.
