Family allowances Lucerne: amounts and procedure (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 August 2026

How to apply for family allowances in Lucerne: who is entitled, monthly amounts per child, necessary documents and application procedure at the compensation fund.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Federal social benefit managed by the cantons with differentiated amounts per canton
  • Rights for employees, the self-employed and farmers with dependent children
  • Automatic monthly payment; application can be submitted at any time
  • Specific amounts 2026 available on the site of the canton Lucerne

Key facts

  • What: Family and training allowances paid monthly for each dependent child
  • Where: Requested at the clearing house of the canton of Lucerne
  • Who: Employees, self-employed, farmers with dependent children, unemployed people with benefits
  • When: Application can be submitted at any time of the year; payment from month of approval
  • Cantons: 26 Swiss cantons, each with its own amounts set by law
  • System: Federal coordination, amounts and administrative management at cantonal level

Family allowances represent a social security benefit that Switzerland provides monthly to families for the support of dependent children. Unlike taxes and fees, family allowances constitute an economic transfer financed by the Swiss public system through a coordinated mechanism between the federal level and the cantons. In the canton of Lucerne, as in all 26 Swiss cantons, the right is recognized and regulated by law, but the specific monthly amounts for each child are determined by the canton itself according to its own rules.

Operational details

Who is Entitled to Family Allowances in Lucerne

The right to family allowances in the canton of Lucerne is recognized for specific categories of people who engage in work activities or are in a situation of protection against unemployment. Those who can request the benefits include dependent workers (salaried employees) working in Lucerne, self-employed individuals and independent workers registered in the canton, as well as direct cultivators and farmers who exercise their activities in the Lucerne territory. Even people who are unemployed and receiving unemployment benefits can maintain the right to family allowances, although they are subject to specific criteria that vary by canton.

The fundamental element to access the benefit is the actual economic dependence of minors or young people in education or training. This means that the applicant must document that their children are effectively in their care from a financial point of view — that is, that they provide them with the main maintenance. For benefits extended to young people who continue their studies or professional training, the presentation of documents is required that certify the enrollment in an educational institution recognized by the competent authorities.

Key points

Request Procedure: How to Submit Your Application

The procedure for requesting family allowances in the canton of Lucerne follows a standardized and relatively simple path. The first step is to obtain the official application form, available from:

  • The cantonal compensation fund of the canton of Lucerne
  • The municipality of residence
  • The canton of Lucerne's website in the section dedicated to family allowances

Once you have obtained the form, you must complete it with your personal data (name, surname, date of birth, AHV insurance number), the data of all dependent children for whom you are requesting the benefit (full names, dates of birth, school or training status if applicable), and your residence in the canton of Lucerne.

Required Documents and Processing Times

The documents that accompany the application are essential for proper verification of eligibility. You must attach the following:

  • Extract from the Civil Register for each dependent child (document attesting filiation and marital status)
  • Personal identity document (identity card, passport, or residence permit)
  • AHV insurance number (the number assigned by the Swiss social insurance system)
  • If your children are attending education or training: certificate of enrollment at a recognized educational or training institution
  • Declaration that you have not already submitted an application to another fund (to avoid double payments)

Frequently Asked Questions
How much are the monthly family allowances for the first child in the canton of Lucerne?
The amount varies by canton and is updated annually. To find out the exact figures valid in 2026 for the canton of Lucerne, go to the canton's official website (www.lu.ch) in the Family Allowances section, or contact the cantonal compensation fund directly. The Swiss cantons publish official tables with the detailed amounts for each child and applicable income bracket.
Up to what age do children receive family allowances in the canton of Lucerne?
Rules on the age limit vary slightly between cantons, but generally the basic right covers children up to an age established by cantonal law. If the child continues a recognized professional, high school or university education, the right may extend beyond this limit. Check the specific rules and age limits applicable in the canton of Lucerne by contacting the competent compensation fund.
How and where do I apply for family allowances in Lucerne?
The application is submitted only once to the competent clearing house in the canton of Lucerne. The official form (downloadable from the website www.lu.ch or available at the register itself) and the extract from the Civil Registry are required for each dependent child. The processing times are generally 2-4 weeks, after which the checks are automatically paid every month to the indicated account.
What should I do if my employment situation changes during entitlement to checks?
If you change employer within the canton, move from employee to independent, or change your professional situation, the right to family allowances may continue without interruption, but it is mandatory to promptly inform the compensation fund of these changes. In the event of unemployment, the right may be maintained if you regularly receive unemployment benefits, according to the criteria set by the canton.
Where can I find the contacts and times of the Lucerne Canton Compensation Fund?
Contacts, reception times and detailed information are available on the official website of the canton of Lucerne (www.lu.ch) in the Social Security section, or at any municipality in the canton. Most cantonal clearing houses offer telephone advice, email support and in-person counselling for direct assistance to applicants.
