The magnitude 7.4 earthquake hit Colombia last Monday, causing damage and casualties. The earthquake's toll rises to 281 dead and about 4,000 injured. According to official sources, the earthquake was felt in several regions of Colombia, including the city of Bogotá, which recorded earthquake tremors of magnitude 6.8.

Colombian authorities have declared a state of emergency and sent rescue teams to assist victims and survivors. The National Unit for Disaster Risk Management (UNGRD) said the earthquake was the most devastating in recent years in Colombia.

The earthquake also caused significant damage to infrastructure, including school buildings, hospitals and roads. According to initial estimates, the economic damages could reach $10 billion.
