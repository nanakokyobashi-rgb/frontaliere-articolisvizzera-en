Rüegsegger UDC candidate for the Federal Council

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

The Canton of Schwyz puts forward André Rüegsegger to succeed Guy Parmelin: it is the first official candidacy following the announcement of his resignation at the end of the year.

The Schwyz section of the UDC has proposed State Councillor André Rüegsegger to the UDC's national search committee. It is the first official candidacy to emerge since President of the Confederation Guy Parmelin announced on Friday his intention to resign at the end of the year.

The proposal comes from the Schwyz UDC section and is being presented to the national party as a candidacy to succeed outgoing Parmelin. Today's statement says that Rüegsegger has the ideal profile and age for the federal executive.

Fifty years old, a native of Brunnen (SZ) and trained as a lawyer, Rüegsegger has been a member of the Schwyz government since 2012. Initially, he headed the Security Department; since 2020, he has been responsible for the Construction Department.

In 2024, he was convincingly re-elected to the cantonal executive, finishing second overall with 37'989 votes. The report, however, concerns the proposal to the national committee, not a federal election result.

The candidacy would also have a distinctive feature: Rüegsegger would be the first Federal Councillor from Schwyz in the federal executive. The source presents this as a possible first for the canton, alongside his biographical profile and experience in the Schwyz government.

The political profile indicated by the party

The UDC's cantonal president, Roman Bürgi, quoted in the statement, considers Rüegsegger particularly well suited to head the Federal Department of Justice and Police, the DFGP. According to the same statement, the State Councillor is also perfectly in tune with the national party.

Another element highlighted in the profile is his position on the agreements with the EU. At the Swiss UDC assembly in January 2025, Rüegsegger argued that the package should be submitted to a double majority of the people and the cantons. In his view, the content and material significance of the treaties clearly place them at the level of the Constitution.

Until now, his name had not appeared among the favourites for a Federal Council candidacy. Attention was focused in particular on Céline Amaudruz, Yvan Pahud and Nicolas Kolly, National Councillors from Geneva, Vaud and Fribourg respectively, or on a first candidacy by a German-speaking Swiss UDC woman: the St Gallen «senator» Esther Friedli or the Zurich State Councillor Nathalie Rickli.

The political reading of the candidacy

The Schwyz proposal does not amount to an appointment that has already been finalized. The fact recorded by the source is that Rüegsegger's name was sent to the national SVP's search committee, after Parmelin's announcement; for the time being, his status is that of a proposed candidate. This distinction is the first practical point for correctly understanding the news.

How to follow the story

For those living or working in Switzerland, following this candidacy means above all distinguishing the steps already documented from the decisions that have not yet been described. The source allows for an orderly verification, without transforming a cantonal proposal into an appointment to the Federal Council.

Four-step checklist

1. Check the status. The primary reference is the search committee of the national SVP: Rüegsegger was proposed there as a candidate. It must not be written that he is already a Federal Councillor or that the succession has been decided, as the source does not state this.

2. Verify the profile. The essential data are: 50 years old, origin in Brunnen (SZ), legal training, and presence in the Swiss Government since 2012. The chronology of departments completes the profile: security first, then construction since 2020.

3. Separate the cantonal result from the federal race. The 37,989 votes obtained in 2024 and the absolute second place refer to the confirmation in the Swiss executive. These are not votes for the Federal Council and should not be presented as such.

4. Read the European position as a political statement. The intervention at the SVP assembly in January 2025 concerns the double majority of people and Cantons on the package of agreements with the EU. This is a position attributed to Rüegsegger, not the announcement of a decision on the package.

The source does not indicate a date for a possible election nor a procedure following the proposal. For this reason, the most useful update to look for will be the one that eventually clarifies whether the candidacy will remain in the party selection and how it will be compared with the names already mentioned. Until then, the reader can use four simple labels: proposal, profile, position, outcome. Only the first three are documented in the text; the outcome is not yet reported.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who is André Rüegsegger and what role does he play in Svitto?
He is a State Councillor of the Canton of Schwyz, he is 50 years old, he is originally from Brunnen (SZ) and has a legal background. He has been part of the Swiss Government since 2012: first he headed the Department of Safety, then, since 2020, the Department of Construction. In 2024 it was confirmed in the cantonal executive in second place overall, with 37,989 votes.
Why is your proposal called the first official application?
The democentrist section of the Canton of Schwyz proposed it to the research commission of the national UDC. The source calls this the first official candidacy after Guy Parmelin announced his intention to step down at the end of the year on Friday. The proposal is not presented as an appointment already decided to the Federal Council.
Which department could Rüegsegger lead?
According to the cantonal president of the UDC Roman Bürgi, Rüegsegger would be particularly suitable to head the Federal Department of Justice and Police, indicated with the initials DFGP. The source adds that the State Councilor is sympathetic to the national party, but does not announce a decision on the distribution of departments.
What is your position on the agreements with the EU?
At the assembly of the Swiss UDC in January 2025, Rüegsegger argued that the package of agreements with the EU must be submitted to the double majority of the people and Cantons. According to its assessment, the content and material meaning of the treaties are at the level of the Constitution. The source reports this position as an element of his political profile.
