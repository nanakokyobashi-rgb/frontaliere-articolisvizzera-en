Residence permit B Schaffhausen: requirements and renewal (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 20 August 2026

Permit B is a renewable permit to reside and work in Switzerland, managed by SEM. Find out how to get it and renew it in the canton of Schaffhausen.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Renewable residence permit to reside and work in Switzerland
  • Managed by SEM (State Secretariat for Migration)
  • Valid for workers and dependent family members
  • Renewable at the end of validity

Key facts

  • What: Residence permit category B
  • Type: Renewable (non-permanent)
  • Who manages: SEM (State Secretariat for Migration)
  • Where: Canton Schaffhausen
  • Validity: Determined on a case-by-case basis by the cantonal authorities

The category B residence permit is one of the four main residence permits in Switzerland and is intended for those who intend to reside and work in the country for a medium-long period. Unlike the L short-term permit, which is limited to one year, the B permit offers greater stability. At the same time, it differs from the residence permit C, reserved for those who achieve specific integration and permanence requirements.

The canton of Schaffhausen, like all Swiss cantons, administers the practices related to residence permits according to the federal regulations established by the SEM. This means that procedures, required documents and deadlines follow a national framework, although the cantonal migration office remains the direct point of contact for residents and applicants.

Operational details

Access requirements and policies

The requirements for obtaining and maintaining a B permit in Switzerland follow federally established criteria. Although the SEM defines the general framework, the cantonal migration office of Schaffhausen assesses each application considering the individual situation of the applicant.

A central element for obtaining the B permit is the availability of a verified job offer. This means that you generally need to have an employment contract or a promise of employment from an employer located in the canton (or Switzerland in general). The migration office will verify that the employment relationship is legitimate and that the salary complies with Swiss market standards. This check is part of the so-called labour market review, aimed at ensuring that the permit does not replace jobs for Swiss residents.

Another relevant aspect concerns the financial and social situation. Applicants must demonstrate that they can support themselves through their earned income. In addition, Switzerland requires that those staying in the country have health insurance coverage (LAMal), which is mandatory for all residents within three months of arrival. The lack of such insurance may result in complications in the granting or renewal of the permit.

Key points

Renewal procedure and deadlines

The renewal of permit B must be started before the expiration of the current validity. There is no fixed date at national level; the time of renewal depends on the expiry date indicated on the permit itself. As a rule, it is advisable to contact the cantonal migration office at least two or three months before the deadline, so that you have enough time to collect the documentation and complete the procedure.

The documentation required for renewal is similar to that submitted for the initial application:

  • Renewal request form (available at the cantonal office or on the official site of the canton Schaffhausen)
  • Updated employment contract or letter from the employer attesting to the continuity of employment
  • Certificate of payment of social contributions (AVS/AHV) and health insurance coverage (LAMal)
  • Valid ID or passport
  • Certificate of absence of criminal convictions (sometimes required, depending on the circumstances)
  • Certification of a permanent residence (lease or certificate of ownership)

With regard to social contributions, each employee in Switzerland pays 5.3% of AVS/AI/IPG contributions and an amount for unemployment insurance. The continuity of these payments is an element that the migration office checks to ascertain the stability of the situation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What exactly is a category B residence permit?
Permit B is a category of residence permit in Switzerland defined as' renewable residence '. It is intended for foreign workers who wish to reside and work in the country on a stable but not yet permanent basis. Unlike the L permit (short duration, up to one year) and the C permit (permanent residence), the B permit offers an intermediate validity and must be renewed at the end of its expiration. How long does a B permit last before you have to renew it?
The duration of the B permit is not established by a single national rule but is determined by the cantonal authorities on a case-by-case basis. It generally varies from 2 to 5 years, depending on the personal and work situation. The exact duration is indicated on the permit document itself. Before this deadline, you must start the renewal procedure. It is advisable to contact the cantonal migration office at least 2-3 months before the deadline to check the procedural times.
What documents are needed to renew permit B?
To renew the B permit in the canton of Schaffhausen it is necessary to submit: the renewal application form, an updated employment contract or letter from the employer, certificate of payment of social contributions (AVS/AHV), valid identity document or passport, updated LAMal coverage, and a certificate of permanent residence. The Cantonal Migration Office provides the complete checklist. Don't wait until the last minute: administrative procedures generally take 4-8 weeks.
What if the permit expires before I complete the renewal process?
If the B permit expires before the renewal is completed, you are technically in an irregular stay situation. To avoid this, it is essential to submit the renewal application in advance, at least 2-3 months before the deadline. If the application has been submitted in a timely manner, the cantonal office will issue you with a provisional document (receipt) attesting to the pending case, guaranteeing the legality of your stay until the final decision.
What happens if my renewal request is rejected?
If the renewal of permit B is refused, you will receive an official communication with the reasons for the decision. In this case, you have the right to lodge an appeal with the competent cantonal authorities within the established deadlines (usually 30 days from the communication). It is important to consult the rejection letter immediately to understand the specific reasons and consider whether to collect further documentation or contact a specialist in foreign law.
