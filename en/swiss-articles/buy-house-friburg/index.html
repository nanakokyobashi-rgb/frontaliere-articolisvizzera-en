The pass-through tax in Freiburg is 3.1%. This means that if you buy a CHF 500'000 home, the carry-over tax will be CHF 15'500. This amount is relatively low when compared to other Swiss cantons, such as the Canton of Ticino, where the transfer tax can reach 4.4%. In addition, the sustainability of the mortgage loan depends on factors such as the price of the house and the interest rate.

For example, if you buy a home from CHF 600'000 with an interest rate of 3%, the mortgage loan will be CHF 360'000. The sustainability of this mortgage will depend on factors such as monthly income and fixed expenses. If you consider a monthly income of CHF 6'000 and fixed expenses of CHF 2'000, the ratio of income to mortgage will be about 1.67. This means that the mortgage will be easily sustainable.

However, if you consider a monthly income of CHF 4'000 and fixed expenses of CHF 2'000, the ratio of income to mortgage will be about 1.33. In this scenario, the mortgage may be less sustainable. To buy a house in Freiburg, you need to have own funds of at least CHF 20,000 for the mortgage loan. Swiss law provides that mortgages are sustainable and that loans do not exceed 90% of the value of the property. In addition, the Transfer Tax Law (Transfer Tax Law, lit) provides that the transfer tax shall not exceed 2% of the value of the property.
