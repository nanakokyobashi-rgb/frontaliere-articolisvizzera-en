The tax law in the Canton of Glarus

The tax law in the Glarus Canton was passed in 2022 and provides for a number of different rates depending on the income bracket. Rates range from 0.5% to 3.5% of taxable income. 📊

Here are some examples of rates for different income brackets:

  • Taxable income from 20'001 to 50'000 CHF: 1.5%
  • Taxable income from 50'001 to 100'000 CHF: 2.5%
  • Taxable income in excess of CHF 100,000: 3.5%

Canton-specific settings in Glarus

Cantonal taxes in the Canton of Glarus are governed by a specific law, approved in 2019 and which came into force on 1 January 2020. The law establishes a system of progressive rates that vary depending on the income bracket. The rates are established as follows:

  • 1.5% on income up to CHF 20,000
  • 2.5% on income between CHF 20,001 and CHF 40,000
  • 3.5% on income between CHF 40,001 and CHF 60,000
  • 4.5% on income between CHF 60,001 and CHF 80,000
  • 5.5% on income above CHF 80,000

Allowable deductions include:

  • Medical expenses: up to CHF 5'000 per year
  • Education costs: up to CHF 2'000 per year
  • Household expenses: up to CHF 10,000 per year

Deductions allowed
