Key facts

  • When: The day of the concert.

The American band The Black Keys has cancelled the concert scheduled for tonight at the Festhalle in Bern. Cases of illness within the musical group led to this decision. The cancellation took place this morning, that is, on the day of the concert, the organizer TAKK-AB Entertainment indicated today.

"We are so sorry to have to cancel tonight's show," the band said. An illness struck them in the middle of the tour. Musicians are not able to offer the performance that the audience deserves, it is specified. The band hopes to be able to continue the tour as planned.

Implications for the public

Tickets for the concert will be refunded at the original point of sale - please contact your supplier for more information.

Operational details

Practical analysis

The cancellation of The Black Keys concert in Bern left many fans disappointed. However, it is important to note that the decision was made to ensure the health and safety of all viewers. The band expressed its regret at what happened and hopes to be able to continue the tour as planned.

Ticket Refund

Tickets for the concert will be refunded at the original point of sale. Viewers are encouraged to contact their supplier for further information. This refund process is a standard procedure in the event of event cancellations, and ensures that viewers do not suffer financial losses.
