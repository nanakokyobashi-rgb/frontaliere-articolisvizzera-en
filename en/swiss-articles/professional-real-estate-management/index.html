The Swiss real estate market requires absolute dedication, leaving little room for distraction. Many professionals in the sector are often approached by curious people asking whether they have seen a competitor’s latest listing or a property manager’s post. The reality is that, for those who seriously manage a property portfolio, the answer is almost always no. There is no interest in following others’ moves because the schedule is packed with concrete commitments. Those who spend the day monitoring social media to see how others photograph a two-room apartment often find themselves in the position of having no properties to manage personally. On the contrary, professionalism is measured by the quality of the time invested.

A professional’s typical day

The working day begins punctually at 08:00, when incoming calls come not from curious people, but from pre-qualified tenants. These people have a clear objective: to view the properties published earlier. At 09:30, the first viewing takes place. The approach is rigorous: the apartment is aired and the shutters are raised. One does not merely show the rooms, but explains the technical details, such as the bathroom’s east-facing exposure. At 11:00, the work shifts to checking solvency. Instead of relying on good faith, debt enforcement extracts, references and payslips are analyzed with the precision of a Swiss sniper. At 14:00, the photo shoot is carried out, avoiding crooked smartphone shots or inappropriate reflections, and then at 16:00 the contract is signed, guaranteeing the owner a secure income.

Operational details

The practical analysis of the real estate business shows that the difference between a standard service and a high-quality one lies in procedural details. While some operators focus on copying digital content, those who operate successfully focus on risk management. The creditworthiness check, carried out before every signing, drastically reduces the likelihood of default. This process is not an option, but a necessity to ensure that the property is a profitable asset rather than a fixed cost. Swiss precision, applied to tenant selection, makes it possible to establish stable and long-lasting rental relationships.

Key points

For those wishing to optimize their real estate situation or understand how to maximize the return on their property, it is essential to follow established procedures. It is not merely a matter of renting out, but of managing a process that starts with a proper valuation and culminates in the signing of a watertight contract. The correct procedure includes an in-depth document analysis phase, in which checking references and debt enforcement extracts serves as a means of protection for the owner. Following these guidelines makes it possible to transform a property from a source of daily concerns into a steady stream of income.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why is Solvency Check Important?
The solvency check is essential to ensure that the lessee is able to meet the contractual obligations. Analysing executions, references and payroll extracts minimises the risk of late payments, ensuring that the property remains an annuity and does not become a cost for the owner.
What does a professional real estate photo shoot include?
A professional photo shoot should avoid crooked smartphones, rough shots, or unpleasant reflections in mirrors. The goal is to present the property in the best possible way, taking care of the order of the rooms and the quality of the light, without neglecting important technical details such as the arrangement of the windows.
What is the secret to success in real estate management?
Success, as indicated by the source, is the result of constant work and sweat, not distraction. The SECRET OF OUR "SUCCESS" (WHICH THEN IS JUST SWEAT) lies in focusing exclusively on the needs of tenants and owners, optimizing every moment of the working day without wasting time in monitoring the work of competitors.
