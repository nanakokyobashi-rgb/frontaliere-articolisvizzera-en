Frequently Asked Questions
Why is Solvency Check Important?
The solvency check is essential to ensure that the lessee is able to meet the contractual obligations. Analysing executions, references and payroll extracts minimises the risk of late payments, ensuring that the property remains an annuity and does not become a cost for the owner.
What does a professional real estate photo shoot include?
A professional photo shoot should avoid crooked smartphones, rough shots, or unpleasant reflections in mirrors. The goal is to present the property in the best possible way, taking care of the order of the rooms and the quality of the light, without neglecting important technical details such as the arrangement of the windows.
