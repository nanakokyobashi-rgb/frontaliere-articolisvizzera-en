Rentals St. Gallen: fees, deposit and tenant's rights (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 12 September 2026

Uniform federal legal framework, maximum deposit 3 months, cancellation only on official form, dispute within 30 days to the conciliation authority.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Identical federal lease right in all cantons (CO art. 253 et seq.)
  • Maximum security deposit of 3 months on a restricted account in the name of the tenant
  • Lessor's cancellation valid only on official cantonal form
  • Dispute initial fee within 30 days to the conciliation authority

Key facts

  • What: Federal Rules on Deposit, Cancellation and Rent Dispute
  • When: Valid without expiry, applicable to each new contract
  • Where: Canton of St. Gallen and all of Switzerland
  • Who: Cantonal landlords, tenants, conciliation authorities
  • Amount: Deposit max 3 rents; cancellation terms 1/2/3 months for seniority

The right to rent in Switzerland is governed at the federal level by the Code of Obligations (CO art. 253 et seq.) and applies identically in the Canton of St. Gallen as in the rest of the country. This means that the rules on security deposit, form of cancellation and contestation of the initial fee do not vary from one canton to another.

Security deposit and escrow account

The law sets a maximum limit: the deposit cannot exceed three months' rent (including ancillary charges). The sum must be paid into a restricted account in the tenant's name with a Swiss bank; the landlord cannot dispose of it freely. Upon termination of the contract, the deposit plus interest is returned except for established credits of the lessor (damages, arrears of rent).

Practical Implications for Home Seekers in the Canton of St. Gallen

Those who move to the Canton of St. Gallen — whether for work, study or family reunification — are faced with a clear regulatory framework but must obtain local market data. Since federal law is uniform, the difference is made by rent levels, which vary between cities (St. Gallen, Rapperswil-Jona, Wil) and suburban municipalities, as well as between new and old buildings.

A tenant who signs a contract for a 3.5 premises in the central area must know that the required deposit cannot exceed three monthly rentals. If the landlord asks for four months or a payment on his own account, the request is illegitimate. Similarly, a cancellation received on plain paper or via WhatsApp has no legal value: only the official cantonal form is authentic.

Comparison with other cantons: same right, different markets

Unlike taxation (where each canton sets its own multiplier and rates), the right to rent knows no cantonal differences. However, the conciliation authorities are organized at the cantonal level: in the Canton of St. Gallen, the "Schlichtungsbehörden in Miet- und Pachtsachen" established in the districts are responsible. The procedure, deadlines and forms may have slight formal variations, but the substantive principles (30-day deadline, burden of proof, benchmarks) remain the federal ones.

Key points

Practice checklist before signing the contract

1. Check that the fee requested is consistent with the reference rents of the municipality (see UST/BFS statistics or tenant associations). 2. Make sure that the deposit does not exceed 3 months and that a restricted account is opened in your name. 3. Read the contract carefully: duration, cancellation period, ancillary charges, clauses on maintenance work. 4. Keep a signed copy of the contract and key delivery report (property status). 5. In the event of a suspicious initial fee, prepare the documentation for any dispute within 30 days.

Deadlines not to be forgotten

  • Initial fee dispute: 30 days from delivery of the property.
  • Tenant cancellation: complies with the contractual terms (usually 3 months at the end of the month, with written notice).
  • Landlord cancellation: only on official cantonal form, with minimum legal terms (1/2/3 months for seniority).
  • Deposit return: within a reasonable time after return, except for documented disputes.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much can the landlord ask for as a security deposit in the Canton of St. Gallen?
The security deposit may not exceed three months' rent (including ancillary charges), as established by CO art. 257a. The sum must be paid into a restricted account in the tenant's name with a Swiss bank.
By when can you dispute the initial fee and who to contact?
The dispute must be filed within 30 days of the delivery of the property to the conciliation authority in matters of lease of the competent district in the Canton of St. Gallen. After the deadline, the fee shall be deemed to have been accepted.
Is the landlord's cancellation valid if it arrives by email or simple letter?
No. The cancellation of the landlord is valid only if notified on the appropriate official form of the Canton of St. Gallen. Informal communications (email, SMS, non-form letter) do not produce legal effects.
Where can I find the average fees per district in the Canton of St. Gallen?
The official sources are the Federal Statistical Office (FSO/BFS), the Statistical Office of the Canton of St. Gallen and trade associations (ASLOCA, HEV) that periodically publish surveys on rent levels by municipality and type.
What are the minimum cancellation terms for the landlord based on the length of the contract?
One month during the first year of lease, two months from the second to the ninth year, three months from the tenth year onwards. The term starts from the end of the month in which the cancellation is received by the tenant on the official form.
