Based in Waldstatt, in the canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Wagner has announced the cessation of its operations. The family-owned company operates in metal die casting and plastic injection molding and supplies, among others, the automotive industry.

The plan calls for a gradual transfer of operations to several external partners. Subsequently, the company will be liquidated in an orderly manner, according to the company. The closure affects 69 jobs: the layoffs will not be concentrated at a single point in time but will take place in stages over time. A social plan has been approved for the affected employees.

Reasons indicated by management

According to management, intensive restructuring efforts and talks with investors failed to identify an economically sustainable solution for the company as a whole. In its statement, the company attributes the outcome to the difficult demand situation, considerable excess capacity, international competitive pressure and framework conditions deemed less than ideal for industrial production in Switzerland.

Silvan Halter, CEO and co-owner cited in the statement, describes the decision as follows:

“For us, the definitive end of the family business represents an extremely painful decision.”

Halter runs the company as the third generation; the company has been operating for over 80 years.

For the next phase, the stated objective is to carefully support the transfer of projects, assist employees in finding employment and ensure fair and orderly procedures. The source does not specify salary amounts, individual deadlines or the contents of the social plan. For an orderly overview of the available compensation components, see busta paga svizzera.

The process described by the company therefore follows a precise sequence: first the gradual transfer of operations, then the orderly liquidation. On the personnel front, the social plan accompanies a workforce reduction distributed over time, while support in finding new employment is listed among management’s objectives. However, the text does not provide the names of the partners or investors, the dates or the figures related to the restructuring.

What to do now

Practical management must follow the sequence announced by the company, not hypothetical calendars. For the employees involved, the process can be organized into five steps, all grounded in the information already available.

A checklist without anticipating data

1. Separate the time of the announcement from that of one's own dismissal. Since the departures will be staggered, the first action is to retain the company communication concerning one's position and follow the schedule that will be indicated. The source does not provide a single date: it is not advisable to replace it with a prediction.

2. Read the approved social plan. The plan concerns those affected, but the press release does not describe its measures, criteria or duration. It is therefore necessary to rely on its content and distinguish the measures actually provided for from the general information about the closure.

3. Clarify the project handover. Management states that its objective is to ensure careful management of the transfer to external partners. Anyone working on those activities can ask how the handover of their project will be organized, without assuming that the transfer of the activity coincides with retaining the job.

4. Prepare to look for a new job. Supporting employees in their search is among the stated objectives. You can prepare your profile and consult annunci di lavoro, while the social plan remains the document to consult for the measures provided for.
