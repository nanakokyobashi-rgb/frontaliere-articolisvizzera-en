Alcon, a group specialised in the field of ophthalmological products, has announced its results for the second quarter of 2026. The company recorded a total turnover of 2.78 billion dollars, a figure that reflects a growth of 8% on an annual basis. Considering local currencies, the increase stands at 7%. The official statement, issued after the close of trading on the Stock Exchange according to the ATS agency, highlights a significant improvement in the main economic performance indicators.

Operational Analysis and Profitability

The operating margin, referred to as EBIT, reached 20.6% in the reference period, exceeding the 19.1% recorded in the same quarter of the previous year. At the same time, earnings per share increased to 84 cents compared to 76 cents in the second quarter of 2025. 2026 Financial Outlook and Scenarios

For the full 2026 fiscal year, Alcon's management has revised its earnings per share growth forecast upward, now estimating an increase between 12% and 15%, an improvement over the initial range of 10% to 13%. Expectations regarding the operating margin have also been adjusted upward: the company now anticipates an increase of 90-190 basis points, exceeding the previous guidance of 70-170 basis points. These updates provide investors and stakeholders with a more optimistic view of the group's ability to generate value, even within a competitive market environment. The confirmation of the revenue growth forecast between 5% and 7%, calculated at constant currencies, underscores the intention to maintain rigorous operational stability.
