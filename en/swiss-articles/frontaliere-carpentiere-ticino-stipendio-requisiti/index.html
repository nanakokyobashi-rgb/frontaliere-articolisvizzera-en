Context

In a nutshell

  • The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and entered into force on 1 January 2024.
  • Old frontier workers have an exemption of €7,500 until 2033, while new frontier workers have a deductible of €10,000.
  • The average salary for carpenters in Ticino is not specified in the source.

Concrete examples

Consider two examples of border carpenters in Ticino:

  • Example 1: A border carpenter with 10 years of experience has an annual income of €60,000. Thanks to the exemption of €7,500, his taxable income is €52,500. If you were a new frontier worker, your taxable income would be €50,000.
  • Example 2: A border carpenter with 5 years of experience has an annual income of €40,000. Also in this case, thanks to the exemption of €7,500, his taxable income is €32,500. If you were a new frontier worker, your taxable income would be €30,000.

Requirements for Border Carpenters

To be considered border workers, carpenters must meet the following requirements:

  • Residence: they must prove that they have a physical residence in Switzerland.
  • Work: they must be employed in an economic activity in Switzerland.
  • Receipt: they must receive an income from work in Switzerland.

Operational details

Key facts

  • The New Frontier Agreement provides for the creation of a transitional regime for old frontier workers until 2033.
  • New frontier workers must meet the deductible requirements of €10,000.
  • G permit is required to work as a carpenter in Ticino.

Requirements to work as a carpenter in Ticino

To work as a carpenter in Ticino, you must meet the legal requirements in force. The G permit is necessary to practice the profession of carpenter in the canton of Ticino. This permit is issued by the Immigration Office of the Swiss Confederation.

Salary of carpenters in Ticino

The salary of carpenters in Ticino varies depending on the city and the type of contract. However, it can be estimated that the average salary of a carpenter in Ticino is about CHF 6,500 per month. This salary is subject to change depending on the city and the type of contract.
