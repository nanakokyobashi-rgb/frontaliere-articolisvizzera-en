LPP pension provision: interest rate of 2% from Jan. 1, 2027

By Laura Bianchi (editorial byline of the newsroom, AI-generated text) · Published on 6 November 2026

From January 2027, the minimum interest rate for the second pillar will rise from 1,25% to 2%, according to the decision of the Federal Council.

From January 2027, the minimum interest rate for the second pillar will rise from 1.25% to 2%, as decided by the Federal Council. The measure is part of the occupational pension system regulated by the BVG, the federal law dedicated to retirement, survivors' and disability pensions.

Prior to the decision, the BVG committee had recommended a rate of 1.75%, while the social partners had proposed values of between 1.25% and 3%. The 2% approved by the Federal Council thus exceeds the committee's indication, but remains within the range supported by the social partners.

The criterion used to set the minimum

To determine the minimum rate, the yields on federal bonds, equities and real estate are assessed. The government reported in a statement on Wednesday that the overall development of these three components was positive in both 2025 and 2026.

The Federal Social Insurance Office (FSIO) estimates that the average return on pension funds in 2025 is 6.1% and that of 2026 at 5.4% until the end of August. According to the government, this allows for a significant increase in the minimum interest rate and compensates for the relatively high inflation observed since 2022. The source therefore reports both the overall yield figure and the average of the institutions as elements of the picture.

Operational details

The comparison between the rate and returns

Arithmetically, the change from 1,25% to 2% amounts to 0,75 percentage points. Compared with the 1,75% recommended by the commission, the difference is 0,25 percentage points. The 2% also lies within the range between 1,25% and 3% indicated by the social partners. These are comparisons calculated from the source values, not additional rates decided by the Federal Council.

Key points

The coverage of institutions and the limits of the framework

UFAS estimates indicated, at the end of August 2026, an average coverage ratio of 122.5% for pension institutions without state guarantee. The data indicates that, on average, these institutions had value fluctuation reserves and means greater than those necessary to fulfill their obligations.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the new minimum rate of the second pillar and when will it come into force?
The Federal Council has decided that, as of 1 January 2027, the minimum rate of the second pillar (BVG) will rise from the current 1.25% to 2%. This amendment concerns occupational pensions and the Federal Act on Occupational Retirement, Survivors' and Disability Pensions.
How does the approved rate compare to the BVG commission recommendation?
The commission in charge of the BVG recommended a minimum rate of 1.75%. The Federal Council set the rate at 2%, 0.25 percentage points above the recommendation, but still within the range proposed by the social partners (1.25%-3%).
What is the average coverage ratio of pension funds at the end of August 2026?
According to estimates by the Federal Social Insurance Office (FSIO), the average coverage ratio of pension funds without a state guarantee was 122.5% at the end of August 2026. This indicates that, on average, the reserves are 22.5% higher than the level required to meet the obligations.
