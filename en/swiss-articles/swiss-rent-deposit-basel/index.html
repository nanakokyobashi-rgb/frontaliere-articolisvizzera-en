Swiss Rentals 2026: Deposit and Termination in Basel

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 October 2026

In the canton of Basel, federal rules apply: a maximum security deposit of three months' rent, an escrow account, an official form and 30 days to contest.

Context

In brief

  • Security deposit: maximum three months' rent
  • Blocked account in the tenant's name
  • Landlord's termination on the official cantonal form
  • Challenge within 30 days

Key facts

  • Scope → CO art. 253 et seq.; federal law is the same in every canton
  • Deposit → maximum three months' rent
  • Account → blocked and in the tenant's name
  • Landlord's termination → official cantonal form
  • Challenge → within 30 days to the conciliation authority

The security deposit in Swiss tenancy cannot exceed three months' rent. The sum must be placed in a blocked account in the tenant's name. For those looking for a home in the canton of Basel, these are the two essential checks regarding the financial conditions of the tenancy: the guarantee limit and account ownership.

The reference point is federal tenancy law, referred to in CO art. 253 et seq. The rule is the same in every canton. The framework applicable to Basel must therefore be read within the same national framework, without replacing the federal deposit limit with a local rule on the deposit.

The procedure for termination

A second point concerns the landlord's notice. The termination is valid only if it is made using the official cantonal form. The form is not a drafting detail: the rule directly links it to the validity of the termination. For the reader, the practical check is to verify that the document received corresponds to the canton’s official model.

The challenge must be submitted within 30 days to the conciliation authority. The deadline must be distinguished from the check on the deposit: these are two different requirements, with different subject matters. On one hand there is the guarantee linked to the tenancy; on the other, any objection to the landlord's termination. The two issues do not form a single procedure.

In the focus on the canton of Basel, the national comparison therefore concentrates on verifiable and common elements: three months' rent as the maximum limit, a blocked account in the tenant's name, the official cantonal form for the termination, and 30 days to challenge it. The cantonal part concerns the form; the framework of tenant rights remains federal and the same in every canton.

This provides a concrete basis even when the topic is presented as the 2026 real estate market. Before assessing the weight of rent in the budget, the tenant can check the structure of the tenancy and distinguish national rules from the elements that require the canton’s form.

Operational details

From rent to cost of living

A comparison of rents in the canton of Basel with the rest of Switzerland is not, on its own, enough to describe the cost of living. The budget of those residing in Switzerland also includes LAMal/KVG, taxes and salary contributions. Separating these items avoids attributing to rent costs that follow different rules.

LAMal/KVG is mandatory for residents and must be taken out within three months of arrival. Premiums are private and per capita: they vary according to canton and region, while premium reductions are a cantonal subsidy. They are not a tax and are not a salary contribution. For the housing budget, this item should therefore be kept separate from rent and payroll deductions. An in-depth look at LAMal e cassa malati helps to understand this component without confusing it with rent.

The items that change the comparison

Taxation also follows a three-level structure: direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each canton has its own law and its own multiplier; the municipality applies a multiplier to the cantonal tax. Responsibilities are distinct: AFC/ESTV administers direct federal tax and VAT, while cantonal administrations manage the cantonal and municipal levels.

Among social security deductions, AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG items follow parameters different from those of rent; for calculating a budget, they should be kept separate.

| Item | Rule to include in the budget | | LAMal/KVG | Mandatory for residents; taken out within three months of arrival | | AVS/AI/IPG | 5,3% borne by the employee; 10,6% total with the employer | | AD/AC | 1,1% up to the annual maximum | | LAINF/LAA | 0,7–1,5% according to the sector | | LPP/BVG | 7% (25–34), 10% (35–44), 15% (45–54), 18% (55+ until the reference age), on the coordinated salary |

These data do not produce a ranking of rents, but show why the comparison must be comprehensive. The minimum wage, moreover, is not set at federal level: some cantons have one of their own. To interpret costo della vita in Svizzera around Basel, it is therefore advisable to separate rent from mandatory items and local taxation, without using a single national threshold.

Key points

Tenant checklist

The practical procedure can be organized into a short sequence, without mixing up the deposit, termination notice, and budget. The objective is to check the guarantee first, then the form of the landlord's communication, and finally the deadline for taking action.

First: check the guarantee

1. Start with the rent stated in the lease and calculate the limit of three months' rent. The deposit cannot exceed it. 2. Check the account holder and the nature of the account: it must be restricted and held in the tenant's name. If either element is missing, the condition does not correspond to the federal rule indicated for the tenancy. 3. Keep this check separate from premiums and taxes. The guarantee concerns the tenancy; LAMal/KVG, IFD, cantonal and municipal taxes belong to different budget items.

Then: read the termination notice

4. If the landlord communicates the end of the tenancy, check the form. The termination notice is valid only on the official cantonal form. In the canton of Basel, the check to carry out is therefore formal and concerns the cantonal form used. 5. If you intend to contest the termination notice, consider the 30-day deadline and address the challenge to the conciliation authority. The deadline must be treated as a separate step: it does not coincide with the deposit limit and must not be replaced by an informal communication.

Finally: complete the budget

After checking the tenancy, add the items that follow their own rules to the rent. LAMal/KVG is mandatory for residents and must be taken out within three months of arrival; premiums are per capita and depend on the canton and region. For taxes, separate IFD, cantonal level, and municipal level, remembering that the municipality applies a multiplier to the cantonal one. Do not use the minimum wage as a single federal threshold: some cantons have their own.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the limit of the security deposit in Switzerland?
The security deposit may not exceed three months' rent. It must also be placed in a escrow account in the tenant's name. The rule derives from the federal lease law, referred to in CO art. 253 et seq., and applies in every canton, including the canton of Basel. The comparison with the lease must therefore start from these two controls: maximum amount and account header.
Which form is required for the landlord's termination notice?
The cancellation of the landlord is valid only if communicated on the official cantonal form. The rule does not depend on a different regulation of rentals between cantons: the lease law is federal and equal in each canton, while the document to be used is defined at the cantonal level. For Basel, the practical control consists in verifying that the communication received is on the form provided by the canton.
When is the cancellation disputed?
The deadline indicated is 30 days and the dispute must be brought to the conciliation authority. The deadline concerns the cancellation of the landlord; it should not be confused with the control on the security deposit. Those who evaluate the opposition must therefore identify the useful date and contact the conciliation authority within the window provided.
What items should be added to the rent in the budget?
For a national budget, rent is not the only item. LAMal/KVG is mandatory for residents and must be stipulated within three months of arrival; premiums are per capita, vary by canton and region and the reduction in premiums is cantonal. The three tax levels must then be distinguished: IFD, cantonal and municipal. LAMal is neither a tax nor a wage contribution.
