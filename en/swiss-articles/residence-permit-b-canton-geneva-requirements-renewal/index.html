All about how to obtain and renew the residence permit B in the Canton of Geneva: requirements, documents and procedures.

Context

In brief

  • The Type B residence permit in the Canton of Geneva is renewable every five years
  • Requirements include legal residence, stable employment, and proof of sufficient income
  • The procedure takes place at the cantonal migration office in Geneva
  • The maximum duration is 10 years for UE/AELS residents, with the possibility of extension for other citizens
  • The renewal fee is CHF 250 for each year of the permit

Key facts

  • What: Type B residence permit
  • When: Renewable every 5 years
  • Where: Cantonal migration office in Geneva
  • Who: UE/AELS residents and other citizens with specific requirements
  • Amount: CHF 250 for each year of the permit

The Type B residence permit in the Canton of Geneva is an essential document for residents who wish to settle in Switzerland. This permit allows you to live and work in the canton for a determined period, usually up to 10 years for residents of the European Union and the Free Trade Association (UE/AELS). The renewal procedure is important for maintaining the legality of your residence. Requirements for obtaining the permit include proof of legal residence, stable employment, and sufficient income. The cantonal migration office is competent to handle the renewal application. The maximum duration of the permit is 10 years for UE/AELS residents, while for other citizens it may vary depending on local regulations. The renewal fee is CHF 250 for each year of the permit.

📊 Comparisons of practical scenarios:

  • UE/AELS residents: Can renew the permit every 5 years up to a maximum of 10 years.
  • Other citizens: The maximum duration may vary, for example, in the Canton of Zurich, non-UE/AELS residents can renew the permit for a maximum of 5 years.

💡 Concrete examples:

  • Legal residence: Prove that you have lived in Switzerland for at least 5 consecutive years before applying for renewal.
  • Stable employment: Prove that you have a valid employment contract for at least 12 months.
  • Sufficient income: Prove that you have a net annual income of at least CHF 40,000 for an individual.

⚠️ Attention:

  • Renewing the Type B residence permit does not automatically grant access to Swiss citizenship.
  • It is necessary to submit all required documents and pay the renewal fee.
