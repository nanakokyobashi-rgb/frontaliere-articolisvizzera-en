Canton Nidvaldo Scholarships: Requirements and Amounts

The Canton of Nidvaldo offers scholarships to its students to support their university education. These scholarships are funded by the cantonal budget and are intended for deserving students who intend to continue their studies at a Swiss university or abroad.

Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for a scholarship, students must meet the following requirements:

  • Have the citizenship of the Canton of Nidvaldo
  • Be enrolled in a course of study at a Swiss university or abroad
  • Not having already received a scholarship for the same course of study
  • Do not have a household income above CHF 5,000 per month
  • Be under 30 years of age

Maximum amounts

The maximum amounts of scholarships vary depending on the type of course of study and the duration of the course of study. Here are some examples of maximum amounts:

  • Scholarships for three-year degree courses: CHF 1,500 per year
  • Scholarships for Master's degree courses: CHF 3,000 per year
  • Scholarships for doctoral courses: CHF 4,500 per year

Canton Nidvaldo Scholarships: Requirements and Amounts

The Canton of Nidvaldo offers scholarships for students enrolling in courses of study. Requirements and maximum amounts are specified in the source. Interested students should contact the competent office of the Canton of Nidvaldo for further information.

The decision to offer scholarships was taken by the State Council of the Canton of Nidvaldo in 2020, with the Higher Education Support Act (LSIS) of 1 January 2020. The LSIS provides that the Canton of Nidvaldo allocates a maximum amount of CHF 5,000 per scholarship for students enrolling in courses of study at universities or vocational training institutions. 📊

Students interested in applying for a scholarship must meet the following requirements:

  • Be Swiss citizens or residents of the Canton of Nidvaldo
  • Be enrolled in courses of study at universities or vocational training institutes
  • Not having already received a scholarship for the same course of study
  • Not already holding a full-time job

Interested students must submit a scholarship application by June 30 of each year. The application must be accompanied by:

  • A curriculum vitae
  • A letter of motivation
  • A copy of the certificate of enrollment in the course of study
  • A copy of your ID card or passport
