A professional growth opportunity for border workers

The G permit, issued by the Federal Tax Administration, allows border workers to work in Switzerland in several unregulated professions, including hairdresser and beautician. In Ticino as a border worker, in particular, it is possible to work in this sector and benefit from an adequate salary and legal recognition.

Salary: an opportunity to earn for border workers

According to the most recent data, the average annual salary for a hairdresser or beautician in Ticino as a border worker is about CHF 38,000. However, actual income can vary greatly depending on expertise, specialization, and geographic location. For example, an experienced hairdresser or beautician and a loyal clientele could earn more in a city like Lugano or Bellinzona, compared to a more rural or less touristy location.

Recognition and permission G: a legal guarantee

In order to work in Switzerland as a hairdresser or beautician, border workers must submit a request to the Federal Service for Border Workers (CSF), an independent Swiss authority responsible for managing relations with border workers and the Member States of the European Union.
