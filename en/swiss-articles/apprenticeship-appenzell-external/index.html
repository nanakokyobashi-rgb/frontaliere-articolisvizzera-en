Why Apprenticeship and Vocational Training is important

Apprenticeship and Vocational Training are fundamental tools for the development of our economy. According to the study "Vocational training in the Outer Appenzell Canton" conducted by the Statistics Office of the Outer Appenzell Canton, 150 apprentices were enrolled in the Outer Appenzell Canton in 2020. This number represents an increase of 20% compared to 2019.

Opportunities for apprentices in the Outer Appenzell Canton

The Outer Appenzell Canton offers a number of opportunities for apprentices. The region is characterised by a strong presence of companies in the agri-food, textile and mechanical sectors. Apprentices can find work at local companies or at service companies operating in the Outer Appenzell Canton.
