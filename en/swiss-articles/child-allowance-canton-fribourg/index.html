Family allowances in the canton of Fribourg: amounts and demand

The Canton of Fribourg offers a childbirth allowance to families with children, with the aim of supporting living expenses and encouraging the growth of children. This benefit is granted within 3 months of the birth of the child and concerns families residing in the Canton of Fribourg.

Who is eligible?

To be eligible for the birth allowance, families must reside in the Canton of Fribourg and have a family income below a certain limit. This limit is established annually and varies according to the economic situation of the Canton. For example, in 2022, the income limit was CHF 120,000 for a family with two children.

Amounts and duration

The birth allowance is granted for a period of 24 months from the birth of the child. The monthly amount varies according to the number of children:

  • CHF 150 per month for the first child
  • CHF 100 per month for the second child
  • CHF 50 per month for each subsequent child

For example, a family with two children may receive CHF 250 per month (CHF 150 + CHF 100) for 24 months.

Operational details

The birth allowance is a benefit that families with children can receive in the Canton of Fribourg. The amount of the allowance is CHF 150 per month for the first child, CHF 100 per month for the second child and CHF 50 per month for each subsequent child. This benefit is granted by the compensation funds of the Canton of Fribourg and is intended to help families cover expenses related to the education and care of children.

The application for the birth allowance must be submitted within 3 months of the birth of the child. To do this, you need to fill out a specific form that can be downloaded from the website of the clearing houses of the Canton of Fribourg. It is important to note that the application must be accompanied by certain documents, including the parent's identity card, the child's birth card, and a parent's tax return.

For example, if a parent has an annual income of CHF 80,000 and had a child in June, the application for the birth benefit must be submitted by 30 September of the same year. The amount of the allowance will be CHF 150 per month for the first child, for a total of CHF 1,800 per year.

Birth allowance amounts

The amount of the birth allowance varies depending on the number of children and the economic situation of the family. According to the Federal Birth Benefits Act (LPN) of 25 March 2005, the amount of the birth benefit is CHF 4,400 for the first child and CHF2,200 for each subsequent child. However, the Canton of Fribourg may grant amounts higher than those provided for by federal law.
