Cantonal Tax Rates

Cantonal tax rates in Geneva are calculated on 3 levels: federal, cantonal and municipal. Each canton has its own laws and multipliers for rates. Federal rates are set by the Federal Council, while cantonal and municipal rates are determined by local laws.

Practical Analysis

The new cantonal tax rates in Geneva reflect changes in federal and cantonal laws. Federal tax rates are set by the Federal Council, while cantonal and municipal tax rates are determined by local laws. Deductions allowed for cantonal taxes in Geneva vary depending on personal and professional circumstances. Deductions can include medical, educational, and home-related expenses. Deductions for workers may be higher than those for retirees. Deductions for families with children may be more extensive.

Comparison with Other Regions

Cantonal tax rates in Geneva are generally higher than those in other regions of Switzerland. Deductions allowed in Geneva are more extensive than those in other regions. Deadlines for submitting tax declarations in Geneva are set for March 31st of each year, while they may vary in other regions.

Concrete Scenarios

A worker in Geneva may benefit from higher deductions compared to a retiree. A family with children may benefit from more extensive deductions compared to a childless family. A worker who moves to Geneva may need to submit a cantonal tax declaration by March 31st of the following year.

Submission Procedure

The procedure for submitting cantonal tax declarations in Geneva is simple and online. Declarations can be submitted through the cantonal tax administration portal. Deadlines for submitting declarations are set for March 31st of each year. Early submissions can be made by January 31st of each year.
