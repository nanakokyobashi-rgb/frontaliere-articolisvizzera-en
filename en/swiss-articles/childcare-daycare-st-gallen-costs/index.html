Child custody in the Canton of St. Gallen is a relevant issue for resident families and workers moving to the canton. The system is divided between nurseries (Kitas), day families and after-school facilities, with a regulatory framework that attributes distinct but complementary competences to the Municipalities and the Canton.

Operational details

Analysis: how funding works in practice

The model of financing extra-family custody in the Canton of St. Gallen is based on a three-stakeholder flow: the Canton provides subsidies to the Municipalities, the Municipalities transfer them (totally or partially) to the structures, and the structures apply tariffs to the families. This mechanism is not equivalent to a single tariff scale based on gross family income: each municipality determines how to structure its own tariff, and the level of municipal subsidy can vary significantly.

Cantonal subsidies and the role of municipalities

The Canton pays contributions to the Municipalities for extra-family custody, calculated on the basis of parameters defined in the cantonal law. These subsidies are intended to cover part of the operating costs of the facilities, thus reducing the final fee borne by the parents. The amount of the actual subsidy for a given family depends on the Municipality of residence and the structure visited: there is no standardized amount valid for the entire canton.

This municipal fragmentation is a relevant aspect for families who move or change Municipality within the canton: the same service may have a different cost depending on the address of residence. For families with lower incomes, many municipalities offer additional reductions in fees, but conditions and thresholds vary.

Key points

What to do: operational guide for families

Families who need a place in a nursery or a childcare facility in the Canton of St. Gallen can follow a structured path to navigate the system.

Step 1 — Identify facilities in your area

The first step is to check which facilities operate in your municipality of residence or in neighbouring municipalities. Authorised nurseries, certified day families, and after-school programmes are listed in the cantonal databases and on municipal websites. It is helpful to distinguish between facilities with cantonal authorisation and unregulated offerings, since only the former guarantee verified pedagogical and structural standards.

Step 2 — Check the applicable fees

Each facility publishes its own fees, which may be daily, monthly, or calculated based on hourly brackets. Since there is no single cantonal fee scale, it is necessary to request a detailed quote from the chosen facility, including all additional costs (meals, materials, annual registration). The municipality of residence can indicate whether reductions are available for families with incomes below certain thresholds.

Step 3 — Find out about municipal and cantonal subsidies

The municipalities of the Canton of St. Gallen offer fee reductions to families with low or medium-low incomes, partly funded by cantonal subsidies. To access them, families must submit an application to the relevant municipal office, attaching income documentation (payslips, tax returns, certificates of any social benefits received). The competent cantonal office for social matters related to childcare is the responsible cantonal department, but the first point of contact remains the municipality.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who sets kindergarten rates in the Canton of St. Gallen?
In the Canton of St. Gallen, the Canton does not set single tariffs: the tariffs are defined by the individual municipalities or by the managing bodies. The Canton intervenes with subsidies for municipalities to reduce the burden on families. To find out the actual cost, you must contact the Municipality of residence or the chosen structure.
What types of storage are available in the Canton of St. Gallen?
In the canton there are kindergartens (Kitas) for early childhood up to compulsory school entry, day families with reception at certified individuals and small groups, and after-school (Horte) for school-age children with reception before and after school hours, sometimes with canteen service and homework support.
How do I access childcare benefits in St. Gallen?
The Municipalities of the Canton of St. Gallen offer tariff reductions to families with low or medium-low incomes, partly financed by cantonal subsidies. To access it, families must submit an application to the competent office of the Municipality, attaching the income documentation. The first point of contact is always the Municipality of residence.
How long is paternity leave in Switzerland and what does it mean for custody?
Federal legislation provides for a 14-week maternity leave at 80% of salary (IPG) and a 2-week paternity leave. The relatively short duration of paternity leave often makes timely access to custodial facilities necessary, with waiting lists that in some areas of the canton can be long.
Is it useful to pre-register for kindergarten in the Canton of St. Gallen?
Yes, because in many areas of the canton demand exceeds supply. Families are invited to register their child well in advance of the desired date of entry, by contacting the facilities directly to find out the average waiting times. Some municipalities manage centralised waiting lists, others refer to individual structures.
