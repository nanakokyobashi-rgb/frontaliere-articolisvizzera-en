How Naspi works

Naspi, or Unemployment Allowance for Frontier Workers, is a benefit that INPS grants to those who have lost their jobs involuntarily. This may include layoffs, sick leave, or other reasons not dependent on the job. Naspi is designed to help workers maintain a certain level of income during the period of unemployment.

Disbursement period

Naspi's disbursement period is two years. This means that workers can benefit from the allowance for up to two years, starting from the moment they lose their employment. 📊

Procedure for Requesting Naspi

Naspi (Nation of Switzerland) is an economic assistance system that protects Swiss workers in the event of dismissal. The procedure to apply for the Naspi is quite detailed and requires a series of specific steps.

1. Telematic Application : Workers must submit an electronic application within 68 days of dismissal. This can be done through the Centra per l 'employment website or through an authorized point of sale.

2. Documents Required: Among the documents required, the declaration of the end of the contract, the identity card and the tax code of the spouse and/or minor children. These documents are essential to complete the request.

3. Enroll in Employment Centers: You must enroll in Employment Centers within 15 days of submitting your application. This step is crucial to receiving the information and support needed to find a new job.
