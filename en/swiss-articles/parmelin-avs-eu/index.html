Parmelin leaves the Federal Council on December 31, 2026

## TL;DR - Guy Parmelin will leave the Federal Council on December 31, 2026 - OASI/DI pensions will rise by around 1,6% - The Switzerland-EU package is before Parliament

Context

  • The Switzerland-EU package has been before Parliament since March 13, 2026

The President of the Swiss Confederation, Guy Parmelin, announced his resignation from the Federal Council, effective December 31, 2026; Parliament will elect his successor at its next session.

The announcement and the federal calendar

The announcement places the president's departure on a precise date and identifies Parliament as the next step. The Swiss Government's page brings together the announcement with other federal dossiers, each with a different timeframe.

Starting in January, the adjustment of OASI/DI pensions will take account of price and wage developments. The expected increase is around 1,6%. The maximum permitted contribution to the third pillar will also increase in the same update. The portal thus presents a change linked to pension provision and price and wage developments.

The financial section concerns Switzerland's international role. The Government believes that the country should be able to continue contributing to the stabilization of the global financial system. For this reason, it wants to renew the commitment credit, so as to make up to 10 billion available in the event of a crisis.

The Federal Council also intends to strengthen cybersecurity through a law regulating software and hardware products, data, and digital infrastructure. The proposal must be drawn up by June 2027. The text thus describes legislative work with a specified deadline.

Finally, Switzerland and the EU have negotiated a new package of agreements to stabilize and develop bilateral relations. The Federal Council submitted the package to Parliament on March 13, 2026. The official Swiss Government page brings together this information with the decisions, activities, and functioning of the Federal Council as a collegial body, as well as popular votes.

Operational details

The practical reading for families and savers

The announcements have different timelines and should be separated into effects that are already scheduled and decisions that are still at a later stage. With regard to AVS/AHV pension provision, the closest reference to the cost of living is the evolution of prices and wages. The figure communicated is a change of approximately 1,6%, not an individual amount for each pension. Anyone reading the announcement can therefore use the percentage as a general indication, without turning it into a personal figure.

The third pillar also calls for caution. The Government announces an increase in the maximum permitted contribution, but the announcement does not state the new numerical limit. The direction of the change is clear; to decide on a specific amount, however, it is necessary to wait for the applicable figure.

The commitment credit follows a different logic. The Government wants to renew a facility of up to 10 billion so that it can help stabilize the global financial system in the event of a crisis. It is not presented as an automatic payment to families or as an immediate change in income. For the reader, the point to follow is the renewal of the credit and its stated purpose.

The other dossiers also have distinct stages. The Switzerland-EU package has been submitted to Parliament, while cybersecurity is still described as an intention accompanied by a proposal to be developed by June 2027. These steps do not amount to a law that is already applicable or to a final outcome.

Key points

Next steps to follow

The reader should not have to reconstruct a single procedure: the dossiers have different stakeholders and deadlines. A checklist based on the portal's guidance makes it possible to follow developments without anticipating decisions that have not yet been announced.

Operational checklist

1. Social security. Use the adjustment of OASI/DI pensions starting in January and the increase of approximately 1.6% as a reference. If you contribute to the third pillar, check the new maximum contribution when the figure becomes available: the announcement states that it will increase but does not give the amount.

2. Parliament. Mark the next session as the stage for the election of Guy Parmelin's successor. Until then, consider December 31, 2026 the announced date for the President of the Confederation's resignation, not the date of the election.

3. Switzerland-EU. Check updates relating to the package submitted on March 13, 2026. The practical criterion is to distinguish transmission to Parliament from subsequent developments, while keeping the stated objective of stabilizing and developing bilateral relations unchanged.

4. Cybersecurity. Mark June 2027 as the deadline indicated for drawing up the proposal. Until that deadline, consider the text a draft regulation of software, hardware, data and digital infrastructure, not an operational rule already described.

5. Global finance. Read the renewal of the commitment credit together with the stated condition, namely a crisis, and the limit of up to 10 billion. Do not use it as a parameter for estimating a personal pension or income.
