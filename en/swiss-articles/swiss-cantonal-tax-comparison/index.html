LEAD: Facts from source

Switzerland has a complex tax system, with tax rates that vary greatly between cantons. Zug and Svitto have lower rates than Geneva or Vaud, while France and Italy have higher rates.

Concrete procedures

To calculate cantonal tax in Switzerland, you need to consider the federal and cantonal rates. The federal rates are set by the Federal Council, while the cantonal rates are set by the cantonal tax administrations.

Concrete scenarios

Suppose you have a working income of €50,000 per year in Switzerland. Our tax rate would be 5.3% for AVS and 1.1% for AD/AC. If we worked in Italy, our tax rate would be 23% for personal income tax up to €28,000.
