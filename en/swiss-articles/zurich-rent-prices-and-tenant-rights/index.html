Average rents in Zurich

Average rents in Zurich are among the highest in Switzerland. According to a 2022 report published by the Federal Office for Statistics (FOS), the average monthly rent for a 3.5-room apartment in the center of Zurich is around CHF 4,300. This value is higher than that of other Swiss cities such as Bern or Geneva.

A concrete example is the following: a 3.5-room apartment in the center of Zurich can have a monthly rent of 4,300 CHF. If we consider a 12 month rent, the total would be 51'600 CHF per year.

Security deposit

The security deposit cannot exceed three months' rent. This is a rule provided for by the Federal Law on Tenancy Law (LDF) of 13 June 1951. The security deposit is an amount that the landlord requests from the tenant upon entry into the apartment. This amount is to cover any damage or loss caused by the tenant.

A concrete example is the following: if the monthly rent is 4'300 CHF, the security deposit cannot exceed 3 months of rent, i.e. 12'900 CHF.

Cancellation by the landlord

The lessor's cancellation must be made on an official cantonal form. This is a rule provided for by the cantonal law on tenancy law of 18 April 1986. The landlord's termination serves to communicate to the tenant his intention not to continue the tenancy contract.

A concrete example is the following: if a landlord wants to leave an apartment in Zurich, he must fill out the official cantonal form and deliver it to the tenant within a certain deadline.

Rules and Regulations

Swiss legislation provides some rules for the rental market. For example, Article 265 of the Swiss Civil Code states that the rent cannot exceed 10% of the value of the apartment. Furthermore, the Federal Tenancy Law Act of 20 June 1981 (LS 1981 215) provides that landlords must provide tenants with a copy of the tenancy agreement within 8 days of signing it.

Lease law

Tenancy law in Switzerland is regulated by the Federal Tenancy Law Act (LDP) of 20 March 1985. According to the LDP, the landlord has the right to request a security deposit, which cannot exceed three months' rent. The tenant, however, has the right to receive a written rental agreement and not be obliged to pay additional rent.
