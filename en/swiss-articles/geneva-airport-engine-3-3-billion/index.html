Geneva Airport is an engine for the region, worth 3.3 billion (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 September 2026

Study Infras quantifies CHF 3.3 billion in added value, 22,000 jobs and impact on the entire Swiss economy. 17.8 million passengers were handled in 2025.

Context

In brief

  • Infras study estimates 3.3 billion CHF in added value for Geneva-Cointrin Airport
  • In 2025, 17.8 million passengers handled, triple the original capacity of 5 million
  • Generates approximately 22,000 direct and indirect jobs
  • New flights to Singapore and São Paulo under study

Key facts

  • What: Economic study on the impact of Geneva-Cointrin Airport
  • Who: Infras (Zurich-based consultancy) and Jean-François de Saussure (director of Genève Aéroport)
  • Amount: 3.3 billion CHF total added value; 1.9 billion for Canton Geneva
  • When: Study published today; 2025 passenger traffic data
  • Where: Geneva-Cointrin Airport and the Swiss national economy

Today's published study by Zurich-based consultancy Infras precisely quantifies the role of Geneva-Cointrin Airport as a pillar of the national economy. The annual added value generated reaches 3.3 billion Swiss francs, with nearly 2 billion directly falling within Canton Geneva.

Jean-François de Saussure, director of Genève Aéroport, emphasized in a press conference the strategic importance of air connectivity for businesses and international Geneva. The city hosts numerous international organizations, and the airport remains the essential gateway.

The numbers are impressive. In 2025, the airport handled 17.8 million travelers — a figure that triples the original capacity of 5 million for which it was designed. The traffic supports approximately 22,000 jobs within the airport ecosystem and related sectors.

Operational details

Expansion Strategy Towards Emerging Markets

Geneva Airport is not content with the status quo. De Saussure has outlined a growth strategy focused on emerging markets, particularly Asia. Currently, the hub operates 147 routes served by 57 airlines. Two new direct connections are under study: one to Singapore and one to São Paulo, Brazil.

The most evident signal of the geopolitical shift in air traffic flows is the enhancement of capacity towards China. Weekly flights to the Dragon have been increased to seven frequencies. The result is tangible: passengers generated from China now exceed those directed towards the United States. This imbalance reflects the evolution of global economic geopolitics and the increasing importance of Asian markets for Switzerland.

For those living or working in Switzerland, this expansion has a direct practical relevance. More routes to Asia mean shorter travel times for Swiss entrepreneurs operating in that continent, and better connection opportunities for multinational corporations based in the country. The new job announcements in the airport and logistics sector related to the opening of Singapore and São Paulo will offer opportunities for specialized profiles.

The Paradox of Capacity: Overloaded Infrastructure and Continuous Growth

One of the paradoxes faced by Genève Aéroport is the discrepancy between theoretical capacity and actual traffic. The facility was designed for 5 million annual passengers. In 2025, the volume reached 17.8 million, exceeding the original projections by over 250%. This structural stress has not hindered growth — on the contrary, the airport continues to expand its services and geographical coverage.

Key points

What does it mean for Swiss employment and development

The 22,000 jobs supported directly and indirectly by Geneva Airport are spread across a wide range of professional profiles: ground staff (ground handlers, security, maintenance), commercial staff (ticket, reception), and specialists (technical maintenance, cargo management). The expected growth of new international connections — Singapore, São Paulo, and consolidation towards China — suggests that demand for employment in the sector will remain robust.

For those living in the Geneva region, Ticino or the rest of Switzerland, the airport remains an important source of employment opportunities. Profiles sought include aircraft maintenance technicians, logistics specialists, loading/unloading operators, multilingual reception staff, and international conference managers. The job opportunities in the transport and logistics sector related to the airport's expansion are expected to grow in the coming years.

The airport as a multiplier of national connectivity

The relevance of Geneva Airport to Switzerland is not merely local. Air connectivity is a critical factor for the country's international competitiveness. When international organizations, multinational companies, and business travelers can count on seven weekly flights to China, or when new corridors to Singapore and Brazil emerge, the entire Switzerland benefits in terms of commercial and diplomatic attractiveness.

Frequently Asked Questions
What economic value does Geneva Airport generate according to the Infras study?
According to the study by the Zurich-based consultancy Infras published today, the airport generates an added value of 3.3 billion Swiss francs annually. Of these, almost 2 billion go directly to the canton of Geneva, while 550 million go to the neighbouring French region and 770 million to the rest of Switzerland. The overall impact supports around 22,000 direct and indirect jobs.
How many passengers did Geneva Airport handle in 2025?
In 2025, Geneva-Cointrin Airport handled 17.8 million travellers. This volume is extraordinary when compared to the original capacity of 5 million passengers for which the structure was designed, demonstrating that real traffic exceeded the initial forecasts by more than 250%.
What are the new air links being developed?
According to Jean-François de Saussure, director of Genève Aéroport, two new direct connections are being studied: one to Singapore and one to São Paulo, Brazil. Meanwhile, traffic to China has been boosted to seven weekly flights, and passengers from this market have now surpassed those to the United States.
Why is Geneva Airport strategic for Switzerland?
The airport is the main gateway for the many international organisations in Geneva, hosting 4,600 protocol delegations and 6,000 conferences a year. It provides critical connectivity to 147 routes served by 57 airlines, giving Swiss businesses access to global markets. The 770 million in added value generated in the rest of Switzerland demonstrates the national importance of infrastructure.
How many jobs does Geneva Airport create?
The airport supports approximately 22,000 direct and indirect jobs in various sectors: ground handling, aircraft maintenance, cargo logistics, reception, security, ticketing and operational management. The growth of new international connections to Singapore, São Paulo and the strengthening towards China suggests that the demand for employment in the sector will continue to grow.
