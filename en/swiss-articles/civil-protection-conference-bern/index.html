Bern, September 24, 2026. On September 23, the Conference of Heads of Civil Protection Offices met in Bern. The central topics were strengthening capacities in the current geopolitical context and transmitting alerts to the population. The meeting provided a forum for partners to exchange views and served as a platform to discuss measures to be adopted at the cantonal and federal levels.

The conference is a platform for discussing measures to be adopted at the cantonal and federal levels. Among the main topics were the guidelines on civil protection and their interim review. The guidelines were one of the key discussion topics among partners. The work remains focused on developing capacities and strengthening resilience in the Confederation, cantons, and municipalities.

The project on armed conflict

A workshop delved into the key project on 'civil protection in the event of armed conflict.' The discussion covered conduct in the event of armed conflict and the concrete measures that the Confederation and cantons must implement. The project requires close collaboration between the two levels and is proceeding on schedule. The analysis therefore addressed both the conduct and the measures necessary to strengthen civil protection.

The conference also examined the multichannel strategy, which aims to modernize the transmission of information, alerts, and warnings in the event of incidents or imminent dangers. The report on political files and implementation work highlighted the Federal Council's decision to accelerate the introduction of cell broadcast, i.e., the alert via radio cell to mobile phones in the affected area.

Marc Siegenthaler's first participation

Having taken office in August, the new Director of the Federal Office for Civil Protection, Marc Siegenthaler, participated in the conference for the first time. On the topic of the guidelines, he outlined his objectives, general orientation, tasks, and expectations. His participation provided the opportunity to explain in detail the direction he intends to follow. The exchange finally underscored the importance of the cantons' opinions and expectations, which are considered fundamental for good collaboration in civil protection.

How to read cell broadcast

The multichannel strategy distinguishes between information, warning, and alarm, with the aim of modernizing their transmission in case of incidents or imminent dangers. Cell broadcast is presented as an alert method via radio cell to mobile phones in the affected area. The decision of the Federal Council indicates that the introduction is being treated as a step to be accelerated, but the communication does not turn it into an operational date.

This distinction matters for the reader. The source speaks of the population as the recipient of information and alarms; it does not assign new individual rules. When did the Conference of Heads of the Office of Population Protection take place?
