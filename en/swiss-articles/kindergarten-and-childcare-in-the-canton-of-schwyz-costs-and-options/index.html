Kindergarten costs in the Canton of Schwyz

The costs of nurseries in the Canton of Schwyz can vary greatly depending on the age group and the needs of the parents. According to data from the Canton of Switzerland's Office of Health and Benefits, the average costs for a child aged 0-2 years are around CHF 1,200-1,500 per month, while for a child aged 3-5 years they are around CHF 1,000-1,200 per month.

It is important to note that these costs may vary depending on the choice of nursery and the services offered. For example, a kindergarten that offers additional care services such as nutrition and cleaning may require a higher cost than a kindergarten that only offers basic services.

Alternative care options

In some cases, families may opt for alternative care options such as day families or kindergarten. Day families are an option that allows families to entrust their child to a day family who will care for them at home. This option may be cheaper than a traditional kindergarten, but it still requires a subscription and monthly payment.
