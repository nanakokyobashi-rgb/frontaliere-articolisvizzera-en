Operational details

Account Opening Requirements To open a bank account in Switzerland as a border agent, the following are generally required: - Valid ID (passport or ID card) - Proof of domicile (utility bill or lease) - Employment certificate or employment contract. For example, if you work in Geneva, you may need to provide a certificate of employment issued by your employer, while if you reside in the Canton of Basel, you may need to prove that you have an effective domicile in the region.

Management costs

The costs of managing bank accounts in Switzerland vary considerably between different banks. For example, UBS charges around CHF 150-300 per year for the management of an ordinary account, while Raiffeisen could charge around CHF 100-200. It is important to compare costs before opening an account. > “Cost transparency is critical for customers,” according to a 2022 Swiss National Bank report.

# Online services Most Swiss banks offer online services for account management.

These services usually include displaying the balance, sending bank transfers and managing credit cards. For example, the Swiss Post Bank offers an online banking service that allows you to manage your account easily and securely, with a mobile application available for iOS and Android.
