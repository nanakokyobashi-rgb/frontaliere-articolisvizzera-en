Residence Permit B Basel Countryside: Requirements and Renewal (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 August 2026

Guide to the requirements and procedure for renewing the B permit in the Basel-Country Canton. Duration, documents and useful contacts.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Permit B is a renewable residence permit for foreigners in Switzerland
  • Managed by the SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) at the federal level
  • Other than L (short duration up to 1 year), C (domicile) and G (border) permits

Key facts

  • What: Residence permit for foreigners living and working in Switzerland
  • Type: B (renewable)
  • Who manages it: SEM (State Secretariat for Migration)
  • Competent office: Migration Office of the Canton of Basel-Country
  • Variable duration: Depends on nationality and individual situation
  • Difference from permit C: B is renewable, C (domicile) requires 10 years residence

The B permit is the residence permit for foreigners who intend to reside in Switzerland on a continuous basis. Unlike the L permit, which has a maximum validity of 1 year and is intended for short stays, the B permit is explicitly renewable and offers a stable legal basis for those who decide to live and work in the country on a medium-long horizon.

The management of residence permits is entrusted to the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) at the federal level, but each canton has its own migration office that processes applications and renewals in the territory. In the canton of Basel-Country, this function is carried out by the cantonal migration office, which operates in coordination with the SEM.

Operational details

Renewal of permit B: duration and deadlines

The B permit has a limited duration, after which it must be renewed. The exact duration of the permit and the methods of renewal depend on a number of factors, including the nationality of the applicant (EU/EFTA or non-EU citizen), the employment situation and the canton of residence.

For EU/EFTA citizens, the B permit is usually easier to renew than non-EU citizens, as they benefit from the free movement guaranteed by the agreement on the free movement of persons. Non-EU citizens, on the other hand, must demonstrate that they continue to meet the admission requirements (valid employment contract, sufficient funds, etc.) at each renewal.

Differences with the C permit and path to naturalization

It is essential to clarify that permit B is not automatically a step towards permit C (domicile). However, the C permit remains the long-term goal for those who intend to settle permanently in Switzerland. According to federal rules, ordinary naturalization requires the possession of a C permit and at least 10 years of residence in Switzerland (the period can be shortened to 8–18 years according to cantonal law, but for the purposes of renewal these years count double for some purposes).

Key points

How to renew permit B: step-by-step procedure

The procedure for renewing the B permit in the Canton of Basel-Country follows a standard route at Swiss level, although the precise times and methods may vary slightly from canton to canton.

Contact the cantonal migration office. The first step is to contact the migration office of the Canton of Basel-Country directly well in advance of the expiry date of the permit. It is recommended to start at least 2–3 months before the deadline. The cantonal offices can provide the renewal form and specify exactly what documents are needed in your particular case.

Prepare the documentation. Typically, you will need to provide the renewal form (available at the migration office), passport or identity document, certificate of non-injury (issued by the authorities of the place of residence), statement on employment status and updated employment contract (or proof of self-employment), documentation of LAMal coverage, and any other documents specifically required by the canton.

Submit or submit the application. The application can be submitted in person at the migration office, by post or, in some cases, via online platform (availability depends on the canton).

Frequently Asked Questions
How long is the B permit valid in the canton of Basel-Country?
The duration of the B permit depends on nationality and individual situation. For EU/EFTA citizens, the validity is usually 5 years; for non-EU citizens, it can vary from 1 to 5 years depending on the circumstances. It is advisable to check the expiry date indicated in your permit and start the renewal in advance.
Can I renew the B permit if I have changed jobs?
Yes, it is possible to renew the B permit even after a job change, but the new job must meet federal admission criteria. The new employer must file the application with you at the migration office. Communicate the change as soon as possible to avoid interruptions in the validity of the permit.
How do you convert permit B to permit C?
The C permit (residence permit) is not an automatic extension of the B permit, but a separate procedure. Generally requires 10 years of uninterrupted residence in Switzerland (5 years for EU/EFTA citizens). After accruing the required time and demonstrating continued maintenance of admission requirements, you can apply for conversion at the cantonal migration office.
What if my B permit expires while I wait for renewal?
If you have submitted your renewal application in advance to the migration office, the validity of your permit is normally extended automatically until the decision of the authority. However, it is essential not to procrastinate: consult the office regularly to check the status of your application and avoid irregularities.
What documents are required for renewal in the Canton of Basel-Country?
Essential documents are: official renewal form, valid passport or ID, certificate of non-injury, proof of valid employment contract (or self-employment attestation), and documentation of LAMal coverage. However, each canton can add specific requests: contact the migration office directly before preparing your documents.
