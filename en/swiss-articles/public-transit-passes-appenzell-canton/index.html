The fare community system in Switzerland

Swiss public transport operates according to a federal model, where each canton establishes its own fare organization through regional fare communities. The canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden adheres to this system, dividing the territory into fare zones that determine the cost of tickets and passes.

The fare community is an association between transport companies (regional railways, buses, trams) and cantonal and municipal administrations. The goal is to simplify access to public transport by allowing passengers to purchase a single ticket or pass to travel on multiple lines and carriers within the same fare zone. Discover how public transport works in the Swiss territory. Without this system, those traveling between different transport companies would have to purchase separate tickets for each operator, complicating daily commutes.

Operational details

Annual and monthly subscriptions in the canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden

The annual subscription is the most economical option for those who use public transport daily. The cost is divided into 12 monthly installments, making it easier to manage your personal budget. The annual subscription is valid on all lines of the fare community for the entire calendar year, from 00:00 on January 1st to 23:59 on December 31st.

The monthly subscription offers greater flexibility: it allows you to start and end the subscription at any time of the month, without being tied to the calendar year. This option is ideal for those who do not have stable travel needs throughout the year (e.g., seasonal workers, students for part of the academic year).

Both subscriptions cover all lines of the fare community of the chosen zone. The price varies depending on the number of zones included. Those living in a peripheral zone but working in the cantonal capital will need to choose a subscription that covers all zones of their route.

Half-fare (Halbtax) and GA (Generalabonnement)

The Half-fare card is a national subscription that offers a 50% discount on all tickets and subscriptions for Swiss public transport, including those of the Appenzell Innerrhoden fare community. It is particularly convenient for those who do not use public transport on a daily basis but want to save on each trip.

Key points

How to choose the most suitable subscription

The choice of subscription depends on three main factors: travel frequency, average distance, and available monthly budget.

Step 1: Calculate current monthly costs. If you use public transport daily (5 days a week, 48 weeks a year), estimate how many single tickets you would buy monthly and the total cost. Compare this with the price of the monthly subscription. Generally, a monthly subscription is convenient if the cost of single tickets exceeds 60% of the subscription price.

Step 2: Evaluate the stability of the route. If your home-work route does not change during the year, an annual subscription is convenient. If the route varies (e.g., during vacation periods, remote work), a monthly subscription offers greater flexibility.

Step 3: Check if you fall into discount categories. If you are a student or apprentice, you automatically access reduced rates. If you are a worker from another canton, check if your employer in the canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden offers subsidies or corporate subscriptions integrated with the fare community.

Step 4: Consider Half-fare or GA if necessary. If you make numerous irregular trips beyond your main route, the Half-fare might be convenient. If you work in multiple cantons or travel frequently in Switzerland, the GA can offer better overall value.

Where to buy and purchase methods

Subscriptions can be purchased at:

  • Ticket offices managed by the cantonal fare community and transport companies
  • Automatic ticket machines (self-service) at main stations
  • Official online platforms of the transport company or fare community
  • Dedicated mobile apps for smartphones and tablets

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between monthly and annual subscription in the Inner Appenzell canton?
The monthly subscription is valid for one calendar month (from the 1st to the end of the month) and offers flexibility for those who do not have stable travel patterns all year round. The annual pass is valid for the entire calendar year (1 January - 31 December) and generally offers a lower monthly unit price, making it cheaper for those who use public transport on a daily basis. Choose according to your travel frequency.
How do tariff zones work in the Inner Appenzell canton?
The territory is divided into concentric zones. The price of the subscription or ticket depends on the number of zones crossed on the trip. Those who move within a single zone pay less; those who cross multiple zones pay a proportional amount. Extended season tickets cover all areas of the canton, ideal for those who travel frequently over long distances.
When should you choose Half-price instead of a monthly subscription?
The Half-price is convenient if you make less than 20-25 trips per month on public transport. If you use transport on a daily basis (at least 20 times a month), a standard monthly subscription is cheaper. Half-price remains affordable for those who travel irregularly on different lines and areas.
Which categories access discounts on Appenzello Interno subscriptions?
Students and apprentices benefit from dedicated reductions on annual and monthly subscriptions, provided they present school certificates or apprenticeship contracts at the time of purchase. Some cantons also offer discounts to pensioners and citizens over the age of 65. Check with your local transport company for the exact categories that are suitable for you.
Where and how to buy the subscription in the Inner Appenzell canton?
Subscriptions available at physical ticket offices in stations, ATMs, official online platforms of the tariff community, and dedicated mobile apps. Online shopping is instant and convenient; the subscription is delivered digitally directly to your device. Go to the main station or visit the official website of the managing body for the complete options.
