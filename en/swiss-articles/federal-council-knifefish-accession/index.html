Bern, October 14, 2026. The Federal Council gave its assent to Switzerland's accession to the memorandum of understanding on cooperation for electromagnetic combat capabilities intended for electronic attack, known as the Knifefish MoU.

The measure is part of the modernization of the Swiss Armed Forces' electronic warfare assets. The armed forces need systems capable of detecting, influencing or jamming electromagnetic signals. Switzerland is thus participating in a European program aimed at acquiring electronic attack systems.

A program built with European partners

With Knifefish, the Netherlands is implementing a program to acquire these systems and facilitate exchanges with European partner states. Germany and Denmark are already participating in the initiative. Sweden intends to join through an amending agreement.

Switzerland's accession to the MoU stems from the Sawfish program, which Switzerland joined in November 2025. The latter concerns tactical electronic warfare systems, while Knifefish concerns electromagnetic combat capabilities for electronic attack.

The Federal Council links this new step to the armaments policy strategy of June 20, 2025. Among other objectives, the strategy provides for a targeted expansion of cooperation with neighboring states and other European partners. The decision of October 14, 2026, therefore brings together three elements already identified in the source: the modernization of the Swiss Armed Forces, European cooperation on procurement, and the connection with the Sawfish program.

Operational details

What changes operationally

The most concrete effect described in the press release concerns how military procurement is organized. Cooperation makes it possible to better coordinate materials and data, as well as to jointly use testing equipment and spare parts. For Switzerland, this model may translate into economies of scale and more advantageous purchase prices.

The advantage is not solely economic. Joint procurement strengthens interoperability between Switzerland and the European states participating in the cooperation. The source therefore indicates an operational benefit, linked to the possibility of making materials, data and equipment work together, and a logistical benefit, linked to the shared management of testing and spare parts.

The three functions of electronic warfare

Electronic warfare uses the electromagnetic spectrum, which includes radio systems, radar and other electromagnetic signals. Its functions are distinct:

AreaFunction
Electronic attackReduces the ability of opposing forces to use the electromagnetic spectrum.
Electronic protectionProtects its own systems from interference.

Knifefish concerns electronic attack; the Sawfish program, on the other hand, concerns tactical systems for electronic warfare. This distinction makes it possible to view the accession as an expansion of Swiss cooperation in a sector composed of multiple capabilities, rather than as a single type of equipment.

A precise political clause also remains in place. The MoU allows Switzerland to decide on a case-by-case basis which projects to participate in. Neutrality is guaranteed, and Switzerland may withdraw from the cooperation at any time if one of the states participating in the program becomes involved in an armed conflict.
