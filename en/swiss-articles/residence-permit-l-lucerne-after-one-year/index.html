Licence L Lucerne: renewal after one year (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 22 August 2026

The short-term L permit is valid for up to 12 months and can be renewed. Find out how it works in Lucerne, the extension options and the transition to a residence permit.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The L permit allows you to work for up to 12 months in Switzerland
  • Is bound to the employer and professional activity
  • The extension must be requested before the SEM expires
  • Possible transition to permit B or C

Key facts

  • What: Short-term L permit (foreign SEM category)
  • Duration: Up to 12 months maximum from release
  • Who manages: State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) + cantonal administrations
  • Constraints: Linked to the employer and the job assignment
  • Transition: Possible passage to permit B (residence) or C (domicile)

The short-term L permit is a federal category of residence in Switzerland that allows foreign workers to carry out a temporary activity. The maximum duration is set at 12 months from release. The issuance and management of the permit is the responsibility of the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) at the federal level, in coordination with the cantonal administrations.

In the Canton of Lucerne, as in all Swiss cantons, the L permit is bound to the specific employer and professional activity indicated at the time of application. This means that the worker cannot change employer during the validity period without authorisation. Similarly, the activity is limited to that agreed with the SEM.

Operational details

What happens during the first year

During the period of validity of the L permit, the worker must respect the established constraints and maintain residence in the Canton of Lucerne (or in the specific address registered with the authorities). The employer must immediately notify the SEM of any significant change: change of address, business interruption, office transfer.

The L permit does not automatically give access to identical social benefits to Swiss residents. The social security coverage (AVS/AI, LPP, LAMal) remains linked to the employer. Check with the employer that the correct contributions have been paid from the first day of activity, and consult the nostro calcolatore della previdenza sociale to verify your accrued rights.

Expiry options: extension, transition, revocation

A few months after the expiration of the 12 months, the worker and the employer must decide on the future path.

Extension of L leave : If both parties (worker and employer) wish to continue the collaboration and the initial conditions are met, the SEM may extend the L leave for a further 12 months. There is no theoretical maximum limit for extensions, but the SEM assesses on a case-by-case basis whether the need is still temporary. After one or two renewals, the SEM may ask the employer to apply for a B permit (residence) if the employment appears stable.

Key points

How to renew or transition the L permit: a practical procedure

If you are about to expire your L permit and want to continue in Switzerland, here are the concrete steps to follow.

Step 1: Evaluate the path with the employer

At least 2-3 months before the expiration date, discuss with the employer whether they intend to request an extension or convert the permit to a stable category (B or C). The employer is the official applicant, not the employee. If the employer is not willing to continue, you need to look for a new job that requires a new L permit or plan your return.

Step 2: Prepare the documentation

For an L permit extension, the employer collects the updated employment contract (or a letter of intent to extend), certificates of salary and contributions paid in the last 12 months, a statement from the employer justifying the continuation of the temporary activity, and a copy of the current L permit.

For a transition to B, add a document attesting to the stability of the employment (a letter from the employer with an analysis of the long-term role) and employment contracts showing continuity over time.

Step 3: Submit the application to the cantonal SEM office in Lucerne

The employer (preferably through an HR consultant or lawyer) submits the application to the competent SEM office in Lucerne. The application must be submitted before the permit expires. A late application can lead to denial, even if the procedure was already underway. Check the opening times of the counters and the accepted transmission channels (paper, digital, via post).

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the maximum duration of the short-term L permit in Lucerne?
The L permit allows you to stay and work in Switzerland for up to 12 months from the date of issue. In the Canton of Lucerne, as in the other cantons, this duration is standardized at the federal level by the SEM. The period may be shorter if the work requirement is less than 12 months. Once the 12 months have expired, the permit automatically expires and an extension must be requested if you wish to continue the activity.
What happens if I lose my job while I have the L permit?
If the employment relationship with the employer is terminated before the expiry of the L permit, the permit remains formally valid for the original activity. However, because the permit is tied to the employer, you cannot work for another employer without a new permit. You must contact the SEM and your employer immediately to communicate the discontinuation. If you can find a new employer within a few days, the latter may apply for a new L permit. Is it possible to switch from L permit to B or C permit?
Yes, you can transition to a B (residence) or C (domicile) permit. Permit B is required when the employment becomes stable and lasting, generally after 2-3 years of stay with permit L. Permit C requires instead 10 years of uninterrupted residence in Switzerland (5 years if EU/EFTA citizen). The passage is at the request of the employer and must be authorized by the SEM. Please consult the cantonal office in Lucerne for the specific details of your case.
How long before the deadline do I need to request the extension?
It is advisable to start the extension procedure 60-90 days before the expiry of the permit L. The SEM usually takes 4-8 weeks to process the request. If you apply after the permit expires, the risk of refusal increases significantly. Anticipate the deadline by coordinating with your employer at least 90 days before the deadline.
Who should request the extension, me or my employer?
The request for extension or transition of the L permit must be submitted by the employer, not by the worker. The employer directly contacts the territorial SEM or the cantonal office of Lucerne with the necessary documentation (updated contract, salary certificates, justification of continuation). You as a worker support the employer by providing the required documents and informing yourself about the timing of the procedure.
