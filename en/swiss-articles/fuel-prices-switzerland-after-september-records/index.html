Fuel prices in Switzerland: petrol and diesel fall

According to the TCS, 95 gasoline falls to 2.11 francs per liter, 98 to 2.22 and diesel to 2.40, after September’s records.

The price of fuel in Switzerland has begun to retreat after reaching highs in September. This is indicated by data collected by the Touring Club Svizzero, which report average values for unleaded 95 gasoline, unleaded 98 gasoline and diesel.

For unleaded 95, the TCS reports an average of 2,11 francs per liter. Compared with three weeks ago, this represents a decline of 3 centimes. Unleaded 98 is following the same trend: 2,22 francs per liter, also 3 centimes lower than the previous reference point.

The three averages recorded

The sharpest decline concerns diesel. The fuel is priced at 2,40 francs per liter, down 6 centimes from three weeks ago. The difference compared with the two gasoline types therefore lies in the change reported by the TCS: for both types of unleaded gasoline, the reduction is 3 centimes, while for diesel it is 6.

The picture offers limited but tangible relief for Swiss motorists after weeks marked by the highs reached in September. The reference remains an average, as specified in the data, and makes it possible to compare the three products mentioned in the article.

For those following cost of living in Switzerland, the relevant value is the one expressed in francs per liter. Among the amounts reported, 95 gasoline has the lowest average price, diesel the highest, and 98 gasoline falls between the two. The data describe the decline observed, without indicating what the next movement will be.
