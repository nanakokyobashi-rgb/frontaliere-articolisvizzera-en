Scholarships Canton Fribourg: requirements and amounts

The Canton of Fribourg offers several scholarships for students who wish to continue their studies at higher education institutions in Switzerland or abroad. These scholarships are funded by the government of the Canton of Fribourg and are intended to help students support their studies.

Requirements

In order to be admitted to a scholarship from the Canton of Fribourg, students must meet certain requirements. In general, these requirements include:

  • Have the citizenship of the Canton of Fribourg or be a resident of the Canton of Fribourg
  • Enroll in a course of study at a recognized higher education institution
  • Demonstrate good motivation and commitment to studies
  • Have not previously benefited from a scholarship from the Canton of Fribourg

  • Maximum amounts: CHF 10,000 for the academic year.

  • Requirements: minimum age of 18, citizenship of Switzerland or another EU or EFTA Member State, enrolment in a recognised course of study.

Requirements for access to scholarships Canton Fribourg

To access the Canton of Fribourg scholarships, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Be at least 18 years of age.
  • Be a citizen of Switzerland or another EU or EFTA Member State.
  • Enroll in a recognized course of study at a university or vocational school in the Canton of Fribourg.

# Scholarship Types

The Canton of Fribourg offers different types of scholarships, including:

  • Study funds: intended for students who need financial aid to continue their studies.
  • Scholarships for research: intended for students who intend to conduct research at a university or vocational school in the Canton of Fribourg.
  • Scholarships for sports activities: intended for students who are engaged in sports activities nationally or internationally.

Maximum amounts and duration

The maximum amount for each scholarship is CHF 10,000 for the academic year. The scholarship is granted for a maximum of 4 years.

Key points

Scholarships Canton Fribourg: requirements and amounts

The Canton of Fribourg offers scholarships to deserving students to help them finance their university education. These scholarships are intended for students enrolling in degree courses at Swiss or foreign universities, and residing in the Canton of Fribourg.

How to apply for the scholarship

To apply for the scholarship, you should consult the website of the Canton of Fribourg, where you can find all the information on the application procedure and the requirements necessary to access this form of financial support. The application procedure involves filling out an online form, which must be submitted within the established deadline.

"Our priority is to help the most deserving students finance their university education," says the Director of the Department of Education of the Canton of Fribourg.

The deadline for applying for the scholarship is June 30 of each year. It is important to note that the deadline is fixed and cannot be extended.

Tools and resources

The Canton of Fribourg's website offers detailed guidance on the application process and the requirements to access this form of financial support. In addition, you can download a request form in PDF format and send it by mail or email.
