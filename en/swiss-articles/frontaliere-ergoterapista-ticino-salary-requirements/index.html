Ergotherapy in Ticino: salary and requirements

Ergotherapy is a health profession that consists of helping people with physical or mental disabilities to improve their skills and functionality. In Ticino, Ergotherapy is a profession recognised and regulated by the State.

Salary of the Ergotherapist in Ticino

The Therapist in Ticino can earn between 60,000 and 90,000 Swiss francsper year, depending on his/her experience and the type of employment. For example, an Ergotherapist with 5 years of experience can earn around 75,000 Swiss francs per year.

Requirements to become an Ergotherapist in Ticino

To become an Ergotherapist in Ticino, you need to have a degree in Ergotherapy or a related discipline. In addition, a qualification certification issued by the competent body is required. In Ticino, the competent body is the Institute of Vocational Training (IFP).

Permit Requirements G

To obtain the G permit, the cross-border ergotherapist must meet the following requirements:

  • Educational qualification: You must have a recognised educational qualification in Italy, such as a degree in ergotherapy.
  • Work experience: Must have at least one year of work experience in the field of ergotherapy.
  • Language skills: You must have sufficient knowledge of the German language and another Swiss language (e.g. French or Italian).

Application for permit G

To submit the application for permit G, the ergotherapist border worker must fill out a specific form and submit it to the competent authority in Ticino. It is advisable to consult the website of the Immigration Office of the Canton of Ticino for the necessary requirements and procedures.
