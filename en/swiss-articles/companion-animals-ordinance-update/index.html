The decision adopted in Bern

During the meeting of October 14, 2026, the Federal Council adopted the update to the ordinance governing the import, transit and export of pets. The press release, dated Bern, October 14, 2026, was published on the same day.

The measure addresses a regulatory framework identified precisely by its title. The operations referred to are three: import, transit and export. The scope of the news therefore remains pets and the provisions relating to these three stages.

The Federal Council identifies improved structure and readability of the provisions as the main group of amendments. The press release therefore presents an intervention concerning how the text is organized and can be consulted.

The second focus is alignment with the new EU requirements. The source directly links this alignment to ensuring equivalence in accordance with the Agricultural Agreement, with reference to the Veterinary Annex. The relationship between the amendment of the ordinance, EU requirements and the Agricultural Agreement is therefore made explicit in the same communication.

The news item includes the PDF of the ordinance, indicated as having a size of 254.29 kB and dated October 14, 2026. The document accompanies the publication notice and represents the text referred to in the decision.

The federal account can thus be summarized in two points: the Federal Council adopted the amendment, and the intervention aims to make the regulatory framework more orderly and readable, aligning it with the new EU requirements. The stated purpose remains equivalence within the scope of the Agricultural Agreement and its Veterinary Annex.
