USES and SUPSI: semester fees for students will increase (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 24 August 2026

Ticino universities will increase semester fees for students starting from the academic year 2027/2028, due to the reduction in public support by the Confederation.

Context

USI and SUPSI: semester fees for students will increase

The University of Italian Switzerland (USI) and the Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) have announced that they will increase semester fees for students starting from the academic year 2027/2028. The increase is linked to the reduction in public support from the Confederation.

In a nutshell

  • USI and SUPSI will increase semester fees for students. - The new rates will come into effect from the academic year 2027/2028. - The increase is linked to the reduction in public support from the Confederation.

Key facts

  • What: Increase in half-yearly taxes. - When: From 2027/2028. - Where: USI and SUPSI. - Who: Students. - Amount: CHF 5,000 per semester for USI and CHF 1,000 for students residing in Switzerland and CHF 2,000 for non-residents for SUPSI.

The USI will increase the fee by CHF 5,000 per semester for new enrollments, while the reduced fee of CHF 2,000 per semester will continue to apply to Swiss students and certain categories of foreign students. This means that new students enrolled in USI will have to pay CHF 5,000 per semester, while Swiss students and those who meet certain conditions will be able to continue to pay the reduced fee of CHF 2,000 per semester.

SUPSI will increase semester fees for the first time in 15 years and the fee will be CHF 1,000 for students

Operational details

The increase in semester fees for the University of Italian Switzerland (USI) and the Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) will have a significant impact on students. Students who are already enrolled in the two universities will not be affected by the increase, but those who enrol from 2027/2028 will have to pay the new fees.

The increase is linked to the reduction in public support from the Confederation, which responds to the political will to increase the contribution of students to the costs of studies and to increase fees for foreign students. The decision was taken to ensure the financial sustainability of the two universities.

According to current legislation, the contribution of students to the costs of studies will increase by CHF 200 for USI and CHF 250 for SUPSI starting from 2027/2028. This means that students will have to pay a total of CHF 2,500 for USI and CHF3,000 for SUPSI for the semester.

This increase will be applied to all students who will enroll in the two universities starting from 2027/2028, regardless of their place of origin. Students who are already enrolled in the two universities will not be affected by the increase, but will still have to pay the current fees.
