Wind energy: Federal hearing for new plants (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 September 2026

On 1 September, the ARE is launching the consultation of the cantons and the public on adapting the wind energy concept and strengthening planning security.

Context

In brief

  • ARE hearing for adaptation of the wind energy concept from 1 September 2026
  • Procedure open to cantons and public participation nationwide
  • Objective: to strengthen planning and increase the construction of wind farms
  • Required to align with new legal provisions and regulatory bases

Key Facts

  • What: Adaptation of Switzerland's Wind Energy Concept
  • When: 1 September 2026 (start date of the hearing)
  • Where: Switzerland (federal procedure involving cantons)
  • Who: Federal Office for Spatial Development (ARE)
  • Type of procedure: Hearing of the cantons + public participation
  • Purpose: To adapt the Conception to new legal provisions and new regulatory bases
  • Expected result: Strengthen planning security for authorities and managers; increase the probability of new wind farms being built

On 1 September 2026, the Federal Office for Spatial Development (ARE) initiated the hearing of the cantons and public participation to adapt Switzerland's Wind Energy Concept. The procedure is necessary to incorporate the new legal provisions and new foundations that have characterized recent regulatory developments in the energy sector. The adaptation is not just a technical revision: it aims to strengthen the security of spatial planning for local authorities and wind project managers, and at the same time to concretely increase the chances of new wind farms being built on Swiss territory.

Operational details

Why Adjustment Is Necessary

The adaptation of the Wind Energy Concept stems from the need to align the instrument with the new legal provisions recently introduced in the Swiss regulatory framework. The ARE communication points out that the change was made necessary by the change in the legal bases governing wind energy. Although the source does not specify the individual amended laws, it is clear that the federal legislator considered it essential to update the procedural and substantive framework to ensure regulatory consistency.

The hearing procedure is the lever through which the federal government involves local institutions and civil society in the decision-making process. The cantons, which have significant autonomy in spatial planning, will be able to provide feedback on the adaptation based on their specific energy and spatial needs. Public participation, simultaneous with the cantonal hearing, allows citizens, interested bodies and project managers to express opinions and observations that will be evaluated by the ARE before the final version of the Conception.

Key points

How to participate in the audition

Public participation is open to anyone wishing to submit an opinion on the adaptation of the Wind Energy Concept. This includes individual citizens, environmental associations, local authorities, companies, industry organisations and any other stakeholders interested in wind planning in Switzerland. The consultation is a real opportunity to influence the federal decision-making process and to raise concerns, suggestions or support for the adaptation of the planning tool.

To participate, you must follow the procedures indicated by the ARE in the official documentation of the public consultation. Although the source does not specify the exact procedural details (forms, email address, digital platforms, precise deadlines), you can contact the Federal Office for Spatial Development directly to obtain the necessary information and access the participation tools made available. The ARE is the body responsible for the procedure and provides all the instructions for submitting comments and comments.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Wind Energy Concept?
The Wind Energy Conception is a federal planning tool that defines the criteria and principles for setting up wind farms in Switzerland. It provides the framework for cantons, municipalities and developers to identify areas suitable for the construction of wind turbines, balancing energy needs, landscape protection and environmental impacts. It is subject to periodic updates to remain consistent with the evolution of legislation.
Why is it necessary to adapt the Conception in 2026?
According to the Federal Office for Spatial Development, the adaptation is required by the new legal provisions and regulatory bases introduced in the Swiss energy sector. The update aims to strengthen planning security for authorities and project managers, and to increase the likelihood of new wind farms being built in Switzerland.
How can I participate in the public hearing?
Participation is open to citizens, associations, local authorities and companies. You should contact the Federal Office for Territorial Development (are) to receive the official procedural instructions, participation forms and the expiry of the consultation, which started on 1 September 2026. The are provides full details on the participation channels.
What is the role of the cantons in this procedure?
The cantons are invited to participate in the federal hearing, providing feedback on the adaptation of the Concept based on their specific spatial planning and energy strategy needs. Their input is crucial to ensure coherence between federal planning and cantonal and municipal policies.
When does the consultation expire?
The ARE communication announces that the hearing was launched on 1 September 2026, but does not specify the closing date of the consultation. It is advisable to contact the Federal Office for Spatial Development as soon as possible to find out the deadline for submitting comments and comments.
