Canton Vaud school system: enrolments and cycles (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 19 September 2026

Complete guide to compulsory education in the Canton of Vaud: study cycles, age of enrollment, languages and transition to high school.

Context

In brief

  • Compulsory schooling structured in specific cycles
  • Enrollment procedures based on children's age
  • Defined pathways for the transition to upper secondary schools
  • Languages of instruction regulated at the cantonal level

Key facts

Scope: Compulsory schooling Location: Canton of Vaud Elements: Cycles, enrollment, calendar, languages Objective: Transition to upper secondary schools

The school system of the Canton of Vaud is organized to guarantee compulsory education through a cycle-based structure, designed to accompany the student from childhood until the completion of compulsory studies. The organization is based on precise age criteria for enrollment and follows a school calendar coordinated at the cantonal level, which defines lesson periods and holidays.

The organization of school cycles

Compulsory schooling in the Canton of Vaud is not a single block, but is divided into cycles that allow for gradual pedagogical progression. Each cycle has specific learning objectives and requires age requirements for access. Enrollment usually takes place based on the child's date of birth, ensuring that every student is placed in the class corresponding to their age group.

Languages of instruction represent a fundamental pillar of the system. Since Vaud is a French-speaking canton, French is the main medium of instruction, but the system provides for the introduction of other languages to prepare students for a national and international context. The management of these linguistic skills is integrated into the programs of each cycle, facilitating the integration of students coming from different linguistic regions of Switzerland or from abroad.

The transition between the various cycles is monitored to ensure that the student has acquired the necessary skills. This path culminates with the transition to upper secondary schools, where students are oriented toward academic or professional pathways based on their aptitudes and the results obtained during compulsory schooling.

Operational details

Practical implications for families

For those settling in the Canton of Vaud, understanding how school cycles work is essential for planning their children's integration. Enrollment is not a single formal act, but a process that follows the child's growth stages. A parent arriving in the canton must verify which cycle their child falls into based on age, as access is strictly linked to this parameter.

The impact of the school calendar directly affects family and work organization. Since holiday and festive dates are established at the cantonal level, residents must coordinate their commitments with school closing periods. This is particularly relevant for those working in other cantons or having partners with different calendars.

Transition and orientation scenarios

Consider the hypothetical scenario of a student completing the compulsory education cycle. At this stage, the Canton of Vaud system provides a skills analysis for the transition to upper secondary schools. Depending on the results, the student may access general education paths or vocational paths. This orientation moment is critical, as it determines the young person's future professional or academic career.

Another scenario concerns linguistic integration. If a child is not fluent in French, the cantonal school system must manage their integration through linguistic support, given that teaching takes place predominantly in this language. The system's ability to adapt to different linguistic levels is a determining factor for initial academic success.

Organization into cycles also allows for greater pedagogical flexibility, enabling teachers to adapt teaching methods to the needs of the class group, while maintaining the standards required by the cantonal curriculum. This balance between regulatory rigor and didactic adaptation is what characterizes the educational approach in Vaud.

Key points

Step-by-step registration procedure

To proceed with school registration in the Canton of Vaud, families must follow a precise administrative process. Although specific details may vary depending on the municipality of residence, the general procedure follows these steps:

1. Age verification: Check the child's date of birth to determine the corresponding school cycle. 2. Contact with the municipal administration: Visit the competent office of the municipality of residence to report the presence of the minor. 3. Submission of documents: Provide the child's identity documents and, if available, the vaccination record or previous school certificates. 4. School assignment: Receive notification of the reference school based on the residential area. 5. Class placement: Participate in preliminary meetings with teachers to define the starting level, especially regarding languages.

Frequently Asked Questions
How does school enrolment work in the Canton of Vaud?
Registration is based on the child's age and area of residence. Families must contact the administration of their municipality of domicile, presenting the child's identity documents to be assigned to the correct school year and school of reference.
What are the compulsory school cycles in Vaud?
Compulsory schooling is structured in progressive cycles that accompany the student from childhood until the end of compulsory education. Each cycle has specific pedagogical objectives and ends with orientation towards secondary schools.
What languages are taught in the Vaud school system?
French is the main language of instruction, being the official language of the canton. The school program also includes the learning of other languages to ensure complete training and integration into the Swiss context.
