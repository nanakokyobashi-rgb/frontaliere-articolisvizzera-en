Swiss company robbed in Rome: instrumentation found (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 13 September 2026

The medical equipment of a Swiss company stolen on Thursday morning was found in Rome. The equipment was recovered by the local police.

Context

In brief

  • Stolen medical equipment recovered from a Swiss company
  • The theft took place on Thursday morning in Rome
  • The instruments were discovered in a green area in Rome
  • The Swiss company was able to complete the university presentation

Key facts

  • What: Recovery of stolen medical equipment
  • When: Thursday, September 12, 2026
  • Where: Rome, via Luigi Candoni
  • Who: Local police of Roma Capitale
  • Amount: High-value instrumentation

Recovery of the equipment

The high-value medical equipment stolen from a Swiss company's van was found abandoned in a green area located near the Roma nomad camp in via Luigi Candoni, in the Italian capital. The discovery was made thanks to the intervention of a patrol from the XI Marconi group of the local police of Roma Capitale, engaged in normal surveillance duty in the area. The local police officers immediately checked the serial numbers on the instruments, successfully establishing with certainty that the entire equipment belonged to a Swiss company. The equipment was found to have been stolen from a van that had been parked near a hotel located in the Aurelio district. The representatives of the Swiss company were in the city of Rome to take part in a practical demonstration scheduled at the headquarters of the Link Campus University. After noticing the theft, the employees of the Swiss company had filed a formal complaint with the competent authorities. The timely recovery of the medical equipment allowed the Swiss company to regularly complete the initiative that had been planned inside the Roman university. Currently, investigations are underway to reconstruct in detail the route taken by the stolen goods before the recovery and to identify those responsible for the theft.

Operational details

Ongoing investigations and the context of the theft

The competent authorities are continuing their investigations to clarify every aspect related to the theft suffered by the Swiss company during its trip to the capital. The episode directly involved the Swiss corporate representatives who arrived in Rome for an academic and demonstration commitment. The theft took place on Thursday morning when the company van, parked near a hotel in the Aurelio district, was targeted by criminals. The medical equipment being transported, characterized by a high economic and technical value, was stolen even before the official presentation scheduled inside the university campus could begin. The promptness of the investigations carried out by the local police officers of the XI Marconi group prevented the goods from being definitively lost or dispersed on the black market. Thanks to the precise verification of the serial numbers engraved on the devices, the local police quickly traced the Swiss company that owns the instruments. The goods were located in a green area near via Luigi Candoni, a peripheral area of the city near a nomad camp. This recovery avoided huge commercial and operational damages to the Swiss company, which was able to proceed without further delays with the practical demonstration planned at the Link Campus University. Law enforcement continues to work to identify the subjects responsible for the robbery and to understand the methods by which the van was broken into in the Aurelio district.

Post-Find Developments and Procedures

The recovery of the stolen equipment ended an affair that risked compromising the institutional and promotional mission of the Swiss company in Rome. The employees of the Swiss company, after verifying the disappearance of the devices on Thursday morning, had immediately formalized the theft report. This move proved critical to activating control channels and enabling law enforcement to quickly identify assets once intercepted during territory patrol operations. The local police of Roma Capitale were thus able to return the medical machinery to its rightful owners in time for the academic event to take place. The investigation continues to identify any reception channels or the individual perpetrators of the theft that took place in the Aurelio district.
