Procedure for converting a foreign driver's license in the Canton of Freiburg: compulsory courses, theoretical and practical exams, required documents and step-by-step roadmaps.

The driving license is an essential document for those residing or working in Switzerland. If you arrive in the Canton of Fribourg with a driving license issued by a foreign country — whether it is a country of the European Union, a third country, or a different Swiss canton — you will quickly discover that it is not automatically recognized for driving in the Fribourg territory.

The reason is simple: in Switzerland, the competencies on road traffic, driving licenses, exams, and conversions of foreign licenses fall under cantonal jurisdiction. There is no "national" Swiss license; each canton manages its own license system according to specific cantonal ordinances. Fribourg is no exception. The Canton has developed its own process for the conversion of foreign licenses, which includes a series of preparatory courses, a theoretical exam on road signs and Swiss regulations, and a practical driving exam. These are not optional.

Operational details

The three pillars of conversion: courses, theoretical exam, practical exam

The procedure for converting a foreign licence in the Canton of Freiburg is divided into three distinct and mandatory phases, each with a specific purpose.

Compulsory preparatory courses Before taking the exams, the applicant must attend preparation courses organised by bodies authorised by the canton. The courses address specific topics of the Swiss and Freiburg reality: Swiss road signs, speed limits, rules of conduct on mountain roads, vehicle equipment obligations (winter chains, danger triangles, high-visibility vests), and road safety regulations. These courses are not optional; their attendance is a necessary condition to access the official exams.

Theoretical examination The theoretical examination tests knowledge of Swiss and cantonal road traffic regulations. It is generally conducted in written or digital form, with multiple-choice questions. It covers the international signs adopted by Switzerland, the road code, correct behaviour in critical situations (fog, snowfall, aquaplaning), specific driving rules on alpine passes, and other aspects of Swiss roads. Passing this exam is a prerequisite to access the practical exam.

Practical exam After passing the theoretical examination, the applicant takes a practical driving test with an official examiner from the canton.

Key points

How to proceed: the practical roadmap in 7 steps

Step 1: Contact the traffic office The first step is to submit an official conversion request to the traffic office of the Canton of Fribourg. You can contact them in person at their headquarters, by letter, or through the canton's online portal (if available). The office will provide you with the complete list of required documents, the administrative fees to be paid, and an indicative calendar of available courses. Do not hesitate to ask specific questions about timing and costs.

Step 2: Gather documents and pay fees Prepare all required documents: foreign driver's license, identity document, proof of residence. Pay the administrative fees set by the canton to the traffic office (amount not yet specified; verify with the office). This step is a prerequisite for enrolling in courses. Keep payment receipts and document submission certifications for future reference.

Step 3: Enroll in preparation courses The traffic office provides a list of authorized entities offering preparation courses. Choose the provider and date that suits you best, and formally enroll. Courses may be offered in Italian, French, or German, depending on the provider. Confirm the start date, duration, and documentation required for attendance.

Step 4: Attend courses and obtain the certificate Participate in all mandatory courses and obtain the official attendance certificate. This certificate is required to book the theoretical exam. Do not miss classes; absence may require repeating the entire course.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why can't I use my foreign license directly in the Canton of Freiburg?
Because in Switzerland, responsibility for road traffic falls under cantonal jurisdiction. Each canton manages its own licensing system according to specific ordinances. Freiburg requires the conversion of the foreign license through compulsory courses, theoretical examination and practical examination. The procedure verifies that you are actually familiar with Swiss and Freiburg regulations, ensuring safety on roads with different characteristics compared to many countries of origin.
What documents do I need to start the conversion process?
You will need to provide the Freiburg traffic office with: the original foreign driving licence, a valid identity document (passport or identity card), proof of residence in the canton (rental contract, municipal certificate of domicile, utility bill), the completed application form, and any medical certificates if required. The exact list is provided by the traffic office at the time of the initial request. Keep a copy of all submitted documents.
Can I start driving before completing the conversion process?
Not yet specified by the cantonal ordinances in force. Generally, driving a vehicle in Switzerland is only allowed with a valid driver's license. Please contact the Freiburg Traffic Office directly to check if there is a temporary driving licence or a driving licence during the conversion process. In some Swiss cantons this option exists, in others it does not; Freiburg will have its own specific rules.
How much does it cost to convert your driver's license in the Canton of Freiburg?
The costs vary depending on the administrative fees established by the Canton of Freiburg and the costs of the preparation courses at the authorized providers. The exact amount has not yet been specified in the sources consulted. The circulation office will provide you with a detailed and transparent quote when you submit your formal conversion request, allowing you to plan your budget in advance.
If I fail the practical exam, do I have to repeat everything from scratch?
No, you don't have to repeat everything from scratch. If you pass the theoretical exam but fail the practical exam, you can retry the practical exam without having to repeat the preparation courses. However, if you run out of the maximum number of attempts allowed by the canton, you will have to repeat the courses before you can try again. The circulation office will clearly explain to you the number of attempts allowed and the procedure for a new attempt.
