The requirements to work as a medical office assistant in Ticino

To work as a medical study assistant in Ticino, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Have a degree in medicine or a related discipline;
  • Have at least 1 year of work experience in a hospital or health facility;
  • Be able to speak at least two official languages of Switzerland, including German, Italian and French;
  • Know how to use at least two healthcare IT systems.

Requirements of the New Frontier Agreement

The new Frontier Agreement came into effect on January 1, 2023, and has introduced new rules for medical study assistants in Ticino. To be eligible to benefit from the allowance or exemption, frontier workers must meet the following requirements:

  • Be citizens of one of the countries that are party to the Frontier Agreement
  • Have a work contract with an employer in Ticino
  • Perform medical study assistance activities in a hospital or clinic in Ticino
  • Have an annual income of at least CHF 30,000
  • Not have other income or benefits in Switzerland or abroad that exceed 20% of the annual income

Concrete Examples

Let's assume that a medical study assistant in Ticino has an annual income of CHF 35,000 and was a frontier worker before July 17, 2023. In this case, they will be subject to a CHF 7,500 allowance.

On the other hand, if a new medical study assistant in Ticino has an annual income of CHF 25,000 and entered Switzerland after July 17, 2023, they will be subject to a CHF 10,000 exemption.
