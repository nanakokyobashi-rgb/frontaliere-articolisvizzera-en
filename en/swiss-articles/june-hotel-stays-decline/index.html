Context

Declining nights in June

Swiss tourism is falling: in June, overnight stays fell by 2.4% compared to the corresponding period last year, according to a first estimate published by the Federal Statistical Office (FSO).

According to estimates, foreign tourists were the only ones to register an increase in overnight stays, by 1.4% compared to the same period last year, while Swiss tourists suffered a drop of 4.4%. This trend has been observed in almost all Swiss cantons, with a few exceptions.

The Canton of Ticino, for example, recorded an increase in overnight stays of 2.2% compared to the same period last year, thanks to its geographical location and its important tourist attractions such as Lugano and Locarno. The Canton of Valais, on the other hand, saw a 3.1% drop in overnight stays compared to the same period last year.

The Federal Statistical Office (FSO) also found that the drop in overnight stays was more pronounced during the week, with a 3.5% drop compared to the same period last year. The weekend, on the other hand, saw a 2.1% drop compared to the same period last year.

These figures are particularly worrying for the Swiss tourism sector, which is one of the main contributors to the country's GDP. According to estimates by the Federal Statistical Office (FSO), the tourism sector contributed 4.3% to the country's GDP
