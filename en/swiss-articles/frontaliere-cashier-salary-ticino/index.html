The salary of a frontalier cashier in Ticino is regulated by collective labor agreements (CLAs) in the trade sector, with gross monthly salaries ranging from CHF 3'800 to 4'500. However, the net salary depends on various fiscal and social factors, significantly changed with the New Frontalier Agreement effective from 1st January 2024. Here's how it works.

Gross salary components

The gross salary includes:

  • Contractual salary: Defined by the CLA (e.g., CHF 4'200/month)
  • Indemnities: 13th month (mandatory in Switzerland), transport indemnity
  • Social contributions: 5.3% AVS/AI/IPG, 1.1% AD/AC (up to CHF 148'200), 0.7–1.5% LAINF, 7–18% LPP (professional pension, from 25th year)

Fiscal allowances

Frontaliers are subject exclusively to the Swiss source tax, calculated on a progressive cantonal scale. The effective rate depends on the gross income and family situation. Italy applies a tax credit to avoid double taxation (CE form 730).

Example:

  • Gross monthly: CHF 4'200
  • Social contributions: CHF 420 (10%)
  • Tax at source: CHF 450 (10.7%)
  • Net: CHF 3'330

Note: Simplified calculation. Actual rates depend on annual income and deductions.

Operational details

How much does a border cashier earn in Ticino

In Italy, a similar salary in commerce (e.g. €1,500-1,800 net/month) suffers withholding tax (23-35%) and INPS contributions (9.19% employee). However, the purchasing power in Switzerland is 30-40% higher thanks to:

  • Relativized prices : More expensive essential goods (e.g. rents), but higher nominal wages
  • CHF/EUR parity: Exchange rate stability (currently ~1.08 CHF/€)

# Net reducing factors

1. Health Insurance (LAMal): Compulsory in Switzerland. Border workers can choose a Swiss cashier (average cost CHF 350–500/month) or keep the Italian one with a specific request. The minimum deductible is CHF 300. 2. Occupational pension (LPP): Compulsory contribution from 25th year of age (7–18% of salary). This withholding funds the supplementary pension. 3. Deductions not applicable: Medical expenses or interest expenses not deductible from tax at source (deductible only in Italy via 730).

Tax exemptions in comparison

  • Old frontier workers (active before 17/7/2023) : Deductible €7,500 until 2033
  • New frontier workers: Deductible €10,000

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the average deduction for a border cashier?
Total withholdings (taxes + contributions) are between 15% and 22% of gross, depending on income and age. Excluding health insurance (CHF 350–500/month).
Can I keep Italian health insurance?
Yes, frontier workers have the right of option. The choice must be communicated annually by 30 November. The Italian option requires the 'Choice of insurance scheme' form from INPS.
What changes with the new 2024 border agreement?
For new frontier workers (≥2024) the tax exemption rises to €10,000 (before €7,500). The old frontier workers maintain the transitional regime until 2033.
