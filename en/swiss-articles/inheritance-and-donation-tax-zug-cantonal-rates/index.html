Inheritance and gift tax rates vary according to the degree of kinship. For example, if the property is transferred to the spouse or descendants, it is not subject to inheritance tax. The law also sets different rates for donations, based on the degree of kinship between the donor and the beneficiary. For example, donations between spouses are exempt from tax, while donations to children or grandchildren are subject to a 20% tax rate.
