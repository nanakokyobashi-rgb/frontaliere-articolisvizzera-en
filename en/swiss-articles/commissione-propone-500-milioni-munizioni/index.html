500 million for ammunition, strengthens defense

The Commission is proposing 500 million francs for ammunition to strengthen defence. The sum is in addition to the 3.4 billion of the armament program. The Commission also proposes the purchase of real estate and F-35As.

This increase in budget is necessary to ensure the security of the country, especially after the latest international events. Switzerland is a neutral country, but it can no longer afford to be defenseless. The Commission proposes to use the funds to purchase new ammunition, including missiles and grenades, as well as to modernise military infrastructure.

According to estimates, the Commission proposes to use the funds in order to meet the needs of the Swiss Armed Forces by 2025. Switzerland has already invested heavily in its defence system, but the Commission believes that additional resources are needed to ensure the country's security.

Switzerland has already received approval for the armament programme from the Federal Council in 2020, with a budget of CHF 3.4 billion. This program involves the purchase of new weapons and the upgrading of military infrastructure.

The Commission proposes to use the CHF 500 million to purchase new ammunition, including:

  • 1,000 missiles for each of the 3 mechanized brigades
  • 5,000 grenades for each of the 5 assault brigades
  • 1,000 rockets for each of the 3 artillery brigades
