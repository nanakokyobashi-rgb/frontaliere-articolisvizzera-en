Childcare options

The Canton of Solothurn offers a variety of childcare options, including daycare, day families, and after-school care. These facilities are designed to meet the needs of working families, ensuring that children receive quality care while parents are at work.

Fees and subsidies

Fees for childcare in the Canton of Solothurn are calculated based on family income. This progressive fee system ensures that families with lower incomes pay less, making assistance accessible to all. Additionally, cantonal subsidies are available to help families cover costs. These subsidies can cover a significant portion of childcare expenses, reducing the financial burden on families.

Enrollment procedure

The enrollment procedure for childcare in the Canton of Solothurn is simple and transparent. Families must submit an enrollment request to the competent authority, providing information on family income and other relevant details. Once the request is approved, families will receive communication with details of the fees and available subsidies.
