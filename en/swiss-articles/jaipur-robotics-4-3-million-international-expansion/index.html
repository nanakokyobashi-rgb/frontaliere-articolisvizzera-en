Jaipur Robotics, a Swiss startup based at the Ticino Technopolo in Manno, announced the closing of an investment of EUR 4.3 million. The new capital will allow the company to accelerate its international expansion, enter new markets and further develop its technology platform. The company, which promotes the use of AI in waste, has completed a funding round that marks a new phase of growth for the company.

The story of Jaipur Robotics

The story of Jaipur Robotics begins in 2023, when founders Ermes Zamboni and Nikhil Prakash participate in the Boldbrain Startup Challenge with the WiSort project, an artificial intelligence-based solution to automate the separation of waste in bins. The team finished the program in fifth place, also winning a desk at the Tecnopolo Ticino coworking space and the prize made available by Hemargroup to perfect their prototype.

Operational details

Practical implications for Ticino and Switzerland

The expansion of Jaipur Robotics has significant practical implications for Ticino and Switzerland. The company, based at the Tecnopolo Ticino in Manno, has already demonstrated the value of its technology through collaboration with the Azienda Cantonale dei Rifiuti (ACR) for the Giubiasco waste-to-energy plant. This type of partnership can lead to greater efficiency in waste treatment, reducing operating costs and improving plant safety.

Innovation and sustainability

Jaipur Robotics' technology, based on computer vision and artificial intelligence, allows for real-time monitoring of materials entering waste treatment plants. This not only optimizes waste mixing but also identifies dangerous or non-compliant objects, helping to prevent unplanned plant shutdowns. The ability to analyze over 5 million tons of waste per year demonstrates the effectiveness and reliability of the solution.

Job opportunities and economic growth

The international expansion of Jaipur Robotics also includes further hiring in Ticino. This represents a significant opportunity for the local job market, offering new positions in a rapidly growing sector. The company's growth also contributes to the economic vitality of the canton, attracting investments and promoting innovation.

Collaboration with local institutions

The collaboration between Jaipur Robotics and local institutions, such as the Azienda Cantonale dei Rifiuti (ACR), is an example of how technological innovation can be integrated with the needs of the territory. This type of synergy is crucial for the development of sustainable and efficient solutions that address the environmental and operational challenges of the waste sector.
