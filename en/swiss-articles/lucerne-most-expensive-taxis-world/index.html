Switzerland's taxis: world's most expensive (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 1 September 2026

A 10km ride in Lucerne costs 58.89 francs, making the Swiss city the most expensive in the world for taxis.

Context

In brief

  • Lucerne has the most expensive taxis in the world
  • A 10 km ride costs 58.89 francs
  • Waiting fees increase the price

Key facts

  • What: Analysis of taxi fares
  • When: Summer 2024
  • Where: Lucerne, Switzerland
  • Who: British insurance company Zego
  • Amount: 58.89 francs for 10 km

Switzerland remains the country with the most expensive taxis in the world, despite Iceland overtaking Switzerland as the most expensive country in the world. This is highlighted by an analysis from the British insurance company Zego, which examined locations where taxi prices were highest this summer. The analysis, conducted across over 1,000 cities worldwide, aimed to show what tourists can expect when traveling from the airport to the city center. The result: five of the ten cities with the highest taxi costs are in Switzerland, with Lucerne at the top. The Principality of Liechtenstein also features in the ranking.

High Fares

In Lucerne, a ten-kilometer ride with a ten-minute waiting fee costs 58.89 francs. Converted, this is ten cents more than Palm Springs, the California desert city known as a holiday destination for Hollywood stars like Leonardo Di Caprio. The authors of the price comparison emphasize the high average starting price in Lucerne, at 6.63 francs. In Zurich, it is even higher, while in Lausanne and Basel, it is only slightly lower. In Swiss cities, waiting costs are particularly high.

Operational details

Implications for Residents and Tourists

High taxi fares in Switzerland, and particularly in Lucerne, have significant implications for both residents and tourists. For residents, these fares can influence everyday transportation decisions, prompting many to opt for public transportation or car-sharing solutions. For tourists, planning becomes crucial to avoid unexpected costs. Knowledge of local fares and the ability to use fixed-price services can make all the difference.

Comparison with Other Swiss Cities

Lucerne is not the only Swiss city with high fares. Zurich, Lausanne and Basel have particularly high waiting fares, making taxi rides expensive even for short distances. This comparison highlights how Switzerland is unique in the world, with fares that even exceed those of luxurious cities such as Palm Springs.

Strategies to Save Money

For residents and tourists, there are several strategies to save money on taxi costs. Using public transportation, which is efficient and well-connected in Switzerland, can be a cost-effective solution. Additionally, comparing the fares of different taxi services and opting for those that offer fixed prices can help avoid surprises. Planning ahead and knowing local fares are crucial for better managing transportation costs.

Key points

Procedure for Planning Transport

To better plan your transportation in Switzerland, it is important to follow a few key steps. First of all, it is advisable to check the local taxi fares and compare them with those of public transport. Using car sharing services or airport transfers at a fixed price can be a cost-effective solution. In addition, it is useful to know peak times and periods of high demand, so as to avoid additional costs due to waiting fees and traffic.

Use of Public Transport

Public transportation in Switzerland is efficient and well-connected, offering a cost-effective solution for daily commutes. Using trains, trams, and buses can be a cost-effective choice, especially for short distances. Additionally, many public transportation services offer monthly or annual passes, which can be a significant savings.

Final Tips

In conclusion, planning ahead and knowing local fares are crucial for better managing transportation costs in Switzerland. Using public transportation, comparing taxi fares, and opting for fixed-price services can help you save money and avoid surprises.
