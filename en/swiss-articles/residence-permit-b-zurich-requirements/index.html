Key facts

  • What: Residence permit B (residence permit).
  • When: Valid for a fixed period, usually renewable.
  • Where: Canton Zurich, competent Migration Office.
  • Who: State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) and cantonal authorities.
  • Amount: Variable administration fees according to the procedure.

Residence permit B is one of the most common residence permits for foreign citizens who intend to settle in the Canton of Zurich for professional or private reasons. The responsibility for issuing and managing these permits lies with the cantonal authorities, which operate within the regulatory framework defined by the Federal Law on Foreigners and their Integration (LSI), under the supervision of the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM). To obtain such a permit, the applicant must demonstrate that they possess the necessary requirements, which generally include a valid employment contract, sufficient financial resources for their own and their dependants' livelihoods, and the absence of grounds for impediment. The application process usually starts before entering Switzerland, but can also be handled internally depending on nationality and situation

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the basic requirements for obtaining the B permit in Zurich?
The main requirements include a valid employment contract in Switzerland, sufficient financial resources to cover the cost of living of the applicant and his/her family members, and the absence of grounds for impediment (such as a serious criminal record). You must also have adequate accommodation and take out compulsory health insurance (LAMal) within three months of your arrival in the cantonal territory.
How does the procedure for renewing permit B take place?
The renewal usually takes place by sending a recall form from the migration office of the Canton of Zurich. The citizen must complete the form, attach documentation proving the persistence of the requirements (such as an updated employment contract or proof of income) and pay the expected administrative fees. The file must be sent before the expiry of the certificate to avoid interruptions in the right of residence.
What happens if I lose my job during the validity period of permit B?
Job loss must be reported to the migration office. Although the B permit is linked to a gainful activity, its validity does not automatically lapse on the day of termination of the employment relationship. However, the holder must demonstrate that they can support themselves or are actively looking for a new job by enrolling in the URC, as the lack of financial resources may affect the subsequent renewal of the permit.
