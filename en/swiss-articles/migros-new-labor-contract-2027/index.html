The agreement announced in Bern

The group announced on Thursday in Bern that Migros and its social partners have renewed the national collective labor agreement (L-GAV) for the period 2027-2031. The agreement introduces improvements in employment conditions relating to working hours, application of the contract, benefits in the event of illness or accident, purchases and wages.

To level the playing field within the group, the new agreement abolishes the sectoral agreements hitherto applied to logistics, meat processing and leisure. For many collaborators, working hours will drop from 43 to 41 hours per week, without changes in salary.

The new contract is not limited to full-time relationships. It will apply to all employees, regardless of employment level, including those working less than 20%. The source also indicates uniform benefits for illness or injury: the entire net salary will be paid in the first 90 days; 80% of the gross salary will then be paid.

From 1 January 2027, employees will be awarded ten Cumulus points for every franc spent, both on the entire range of Migros supermarkets and in the group's self-service restaurants. Migros quantifies the benefit as a 10% discount, provided in the form of shopping vouchers.

For 2027 the group has announced an overall investment of 1.6% of the wage bill, including specific increases for individual companies. In the Food Retail sector the national salary range can reach 0.3%, while for the other sectors the limit will be 0.7%. Individual companies will communicate their salary measures separately. Migros also specifies that its employees will continue to benefit from comprehensive social benefits.

The renewal therefore combines a contract applicable to all employees with measures that depend on the sector or the individual company. The reduction of hours is indicated for many collaborators, while the salary changes will be communicated separately.

The most practical way to follow the transition to the new agreement is to separate the general information from what the individual company must communicate. 2. Note the employment percentage and working hours. If you are among the employees affected by the reduction, record the change from 43 to 41 hours with unchanged salary. Also note if you work less than 20%: the agreement will also apply in this situation.

3. Prepare a separate check for illness or accident: first 90 days with full net salary; subsequent period with 80% of gross salary. Keep the two bases separate when reviewing the applicable conditions.
