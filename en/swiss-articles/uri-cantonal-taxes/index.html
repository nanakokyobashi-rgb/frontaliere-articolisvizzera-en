Details

Cantonal taxes in the Canton of Uri are governed by cantonal law, which provides for a rate of 6.5%. This rate applies to all taxable income, regardless of source or destination.

Concrete example: If a resident in the Canton of Uri earns CHF 50,000 per year, his income is subject to the cantonal tax of 6.5%. The calculation would then be: 50,000 x 0.065 = 3,250 Swiss francs per year.

The allowable deductions are those provided for by cantonal law. This means that taxpayers can subtract certain expenses or deductions from their taxable income. Example: If a taxpayer has expenses for renting an apartment of 10,000 Swiss francs per year, they can subtract this amount from their taxable income.

It is important to note that allowable deductions may vary based on cantonal legislation and individual circumstances.
