Vaud canton transport subscriptions: zones and rates (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 September 2026

Monthly, annual, half-price and General Subscriptions in Swiss public transport. How fare zones and student discounts work.

Context

In brief

  • Tariff zones organize Swiss public transport for geographical areas
  • Monthly and annual subscriptions reduce costs for commuters and regular users
  • Half-fare (ARZ) and General Abonnement (AG) offer integrated national solutions
  • Special discounts available for students, apprentices, and protected categories

Key facts

  • What: Subscription systems and tariff zones in Swiss public transport
  • Where: Canton Vaud and Swiss national network (trains, buses, trams, boats)
  • Who: Public transport operators, tariff communities, cantonal agencies
  • Tools: Zonal subscriptions, ARZ, AG, categorized offers
  • Categories: Students, apprentices, workers, retirees, people with disabilities

Swiss public transport, including Canton Vaud, is organized around tariff zones that delimit geographical areas of validity for tickets and subscriptions. This system allows passengers to plan their trips with tariff transparency and operators to structure revenues sustainably. A tariff community is an association of public entities and transport companies that coordinates fares, schedules, and offers across multiple zones, often covering several cantons.

How tariff zones work

The principle is simple: the ticket price depends on the number of zones crossed. Those traveling within a single zone pay less than those crossing three or five zones. This model encourages the purchase of commuter subscriptions, as the per-trip cost is significantly reduced compared to single tickets. Zones are geographically delimited and marked on public maps; each major city or region has its own zonal structure.

Operational details

Half-price (ARZ) and General Abonnement (AG): integrated solutions

Two Swiss national tools expand the savings options beyond the zonal subscriptions. The Half-price (ARZ – Abonnement Réduction Zéro in French) is a permanent 50% discount applied to single tickets and subscriptions of all participating networks (national railways, regional buses, trams, funiculars, boats). It is not an independent subscription but a tariff reduction tool. Those who possess ARZ purchase discounted versions of the same products. The General Abonnement (AG) represents the highest level of tariff integration. It is a single card that allows unlimited travel on the entire Swiss public transport network (trains, buses, trams, funiculars, boats) for 365 consecutive days, regardless of zones. The AG completely eliminates the geographical constraint and is convenient for those who make long, variable, or frequent trips between different cantons.

Discounts for students and apprentices

Students regularly enrolled in schools, high schools, universities, and apprentices with a valid contract fall into privileged categories in the Swiss public transport system. The reductions typically vary between 20% and 50% compared to regular fares, depending on the network and the managing entity. Access requires the presentation of an updated school enrollment document or apprenticeship contract. The cantonal public transport administrations publish the annual tariffs and access conditions for these categories.

Key points

Procedure to choose the right subscription

The selection process is pragmatic and requires a few steps:

1. Map your routes — Identify the geographical areas where you travel regularly (work, school, recurring activities).

2. Count the days of use — How many days do you actually use public transport each month? Those who travel 20+ days/month benefit more from a monthly or annual subscription compared to single tickets.

3. Compare official price lists — Access the local fare community website (e.g., TL for Lausanne/Vaud) and consult the current prices for zonal subscriptions, half-price, and General Abonnement.

4. Evaluate ARZ and AG — If your travels include multiple cantons or long distances, calculate whether the annual cost of ARZ or AG is lower than the sum of the zonal subscriptions you would have purchased separately.

5. Check for subsidized categories — If you are a student, apprentice, retiree, disabled, or fall into a subsidized category, check the applicable discounts and required documents.

6. Purchase and set up renewal — Many networks allow online purchase, via mobile app, or at physical sales points. Automatic renewal is available for annual subscriptions.

Timing and deadlines

Monthly subscriptions follow the natural calendar (from the first to the last day of the month). Annual subscriptions start from the activation date and expire exactly 12 months later. Renew a few days in advance to avoid service interruptions. Many networks offer a tolerance window (2–5 days) for renewal without loss of coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between a Zone Subscription and Half Price (ARZ)?
A zone subscription covers all specified areas and allows unlimited travel; the Half-price is a permanent 50% discount applied to the prices of individual tickets and subscriptions. The zone pass is ideal for commuters with fixed and regular routes; the Half-price suits those who travel distances and variable zones.
Is General Abonnement (AG) convenient for those who work in one canton and reside in another?
The AG covers the Swiss national network for 365 consecutive days and eliminates the geographical limit. For inter-cantonal commuters with frequent commutes, the annual cost of the GA is often lower than that of separately purchased zonal subscriptions. Make a timely comparison between the amount of the GA and the annual total of the subscriptions you would use.
How do I access student and apprentice discounts?
Present the updated school enrollment document (student card, enrollment certificate) or the apprenticeship contract when purchasing the subscription. Typical reductions range from 20% to 50% depending on the operator. Please contact your local transportation office to verify the required documents and exact percentages.
Can I buy ARZ and a zonal subscription together?
No. The ARZ is a stand-alone discount tool. If you have ARZ, you will buy the discounted version of 50% of any product (zonal, monthly, annual subscription). ARZ and subscription are two alternative, non-cumulative choices.
How and when do I renew an annual subscription?
Many networks offer auto-renewal that can be configured online or via app. Otherwise, purchase a new subscription before the previous one expires. A tolerance window of 2–5 days allows renewal without service interruptions. Check deadlines on your subscription or network platform.
