The Cantonal Hospital Organization (EOC) has opened a search for specialist and assistant physicians in the disciplines of Internal Medicine and Intensive Care Medicine. The job opportunity is focused at the Bellinzona Regional Hospital, a key facility in the Ticino healthcare system. This initiative aims to strengthen the medical teams of the two divisions, thereby ensuring the continuity and excellence of care provided to the population of the Canton of Ticino. The positions are open to both professionals who already hold specialist qualifications and physicians in training who aspire to complete their specialization in a dynamic and educational hospital setting.

Operational details

The Role of the Specialist Doctor in Ticino

The search for specialist doctors in Internal and Intensive Medicine by the Cantonal Hospital Corporation (EOC) for the Bellinzona Regional Hospital highlights the growing need for qualified healthcare professionals in the Canton of Ticino. This recruitment is part of a broader framework for strengthening cantonal healthcare services, aimed at ensuring adequate responses to the needs of a growing population with increasingly complex health needs. Internal Medicine deals with the diagnosis, treatment, and management of a wide range of pathologies affecting internal organs, while Intensive Medicine is dedicated to patients in critical condition who require constant monitoring and advanced treatment in specialized departments.

Key points

Application and Professional Path in Switzerland

To apply for specialist doctor or assistant positions at the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona, interested professionals typically follow an online application procedure via the official portal of the Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale (EOC). Required documentation usually includes a detailed CV, reference letters, copies of academic qualifications and specialization certificates (if applicable), as well as a motivation letter outlining reasons for interest in the position and the EOC. It is crucial for foreign candidates to verify the recognition of their academic and professional qualifications in Switzerland, a process that can take time and is managed by the competent authority, usually the Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH) or delegated cantonal bodies.
