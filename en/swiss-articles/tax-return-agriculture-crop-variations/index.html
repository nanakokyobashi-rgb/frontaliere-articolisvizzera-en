Key points

Procedures and support tools for managing agricultural income. To correctly declare income in 2026, agricultural entrepreneurs must follow a structured process that starts with an analysis of the crops carried out. The first phase consists of an accurate census of all cultivated surfaces, clearly distinguishing between different types of production. This data must be cross-checked with warehouse records and invoices, ensuring consistency between what is produced and what is declared. It is advisable to use specific applications for the control of agricultural business management, which allow the automatic calculation of income and the constant monitoring of margins. Tools such as the calculation of the net margin income from milk (IOFC) represent examples of how technology can support daily management, offering ready-to-use data for insertion into the tax declaration. Once the data is consolidated, the next step is to verify the available 2026 incentives. There are specific guides, including eBooks, that illustrate the new measures for young people and women in agriculture, which can significantly affect the final tax calculation. Frequently Asked Questions
Why are crop variations important for the 2026 statement?
Crop variations modify agricultural income, the taxable base for taxation. Inaccurate traceability can lead to non-compliant returns, necessitating rigorous monitoring through dedicated management software to avoid tax discrepancies.
What tools are recommended to manage farm income?
For efficient management, Excel applications are suggested for farm management control and calculation of marginal net milk gain (IOFC). For indoor crops, there are cloud-based software such as ReddiPlant that simplifies the calculation of income.
Are there specific benefits for 2026 in the agricultural sector?
Yes, the 2026 regulatory framework provides specific benefits, including incentives for young people and women in agriculture. It is essential to consult the dedicated in-depth guides to verify the applicability of these measures to your specific company.
