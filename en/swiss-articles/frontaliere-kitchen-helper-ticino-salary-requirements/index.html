Requirements to work as a kitchen assistant in Ticino

To work as a kitchen assistant in Ticino, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Have a valid residence permit
  • Have a qualification recognised by Switzerland
  • Have valid health insurance
  • Have a certificate of good conduct
  • Have at least 6 months' work experience as a kitchen assistant or in a similar field

Selection process

The selection process to work as a kitchen assistant in Ticino is as follows:
