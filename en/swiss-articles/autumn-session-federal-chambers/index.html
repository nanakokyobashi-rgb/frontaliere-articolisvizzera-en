Federal Chambers: The fall session ends today

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 October 2026

The fall session ends today in Bern. Final votes on 17 items are on the agenda for the Federal Assembly; the winter session will run from November 30 to December 18.

Context

At a Glance

  • The fall session of the Federal Chambers ends today
  • Only final votes are scheduled in the Council of States
  • There are 17 items on the Council of States’ agenda
  • The winter session will run from November 30 to December 18

Key Facts

  • Location: Bern
  • Closing date: today
  • Council of States: final votes only
  • Items on the Council of States’ agenda: 17
  • Winter session: November 30 through December 18

In Bern, the fall session of the Federal Chambers concludes today, but the final day’s agenda differs between the two chambers. In the National Council, the plenary session will address several motions and parliamentary initiatives before the traditional final votes. It will then move on to petitions and non-controversial parliamentary statements.

The sequence is therefore structured. The National Council is not merely called upon to vote on final resolutions: the agenda first includes the aforementioned parliamentary acts, followed by the session’s closing phase. The agenda thus strings together different parts of the same day, from the discussion of motions and initiatives to the subsequent phase covering petitions and non-controversial parliamentary statements.

Two Agendas for the Final Day

In the Council of States, the agenda is more focused. Only final votes are scheduled, covering 17 items that have completed the parliamentary process. The key number for the day is therefore 17: these items are in the final stage of the parliamentary process.

The difference between the two agendas provides a clear picture of the closing of the fall session. For the National Council, the day includes even more steps before the final vote. For the Council of States, the agenda is already limited to the final vote on the 17 items that have reached the end of the parliamentary process.

The calendar is already looking ahead to the next session. The winter session will be held from November 30 to December 18. November 30 marks the start of the next session, while December 18 indicates its scheduled conclusion. These dates serve as the reference points for the transition from the fall session to the winter session.

For those following the work of the Federal Chambers, this news thus establishes three key points: today’s conclusion of the fall session, the final vote on the 17 items on the Council of States’ agenda, and the timeframe from November 30 to December 18. The National Council’s schedule remains separate, as the additional debates listed are scheduled to take place before the final votes.

Operational details

For the reader, the practical implication of this is, above all, the ability to distinguish the various stages of the parliamentary process, without attributing any consequences to the 17 items beyond those associated with their respective stages. For items before the Council of States, the source links the final votes to the fact that they have completed the parliamentary process. The correct interpretation, therefore, is limited to the verifiable facts: 17 items in the Council of States and a still-pending agenda in the National Council.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does the fall session of the Federal Assembly end?
The autumn session ends today in Bern. In the Council of States, on the other hand, only the final votes are scheduled. The source therefore distinguishes the agenda of the two branches on the last day of the session.
What is on the agenda for the Council of States?
The agenda of the states includes only the final votes. There are 17 items and they have completed the parliamentary process. When will the winter session take place?
The winter session will be held from 30 November to 18 December. 30 November is the starting date indicated for the next session and 18 December is the closing date. These are the two time extremes of the next meeting of the Federal Assembly, after today's closing of the autumn session.
