The regulation provides that the Swiss tax authorities will receive the information on the income of cross-border workers from Italy. This information will be used to calculate the tax liability of cross-border workers in Switzerland. The Federal Council has stated that this agreement will help prevent tax evasion and ensure that cross-border workers pay their taxes in Switzerland.
