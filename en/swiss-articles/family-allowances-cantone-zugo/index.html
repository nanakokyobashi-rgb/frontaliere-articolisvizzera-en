In the Canton of Zug, family allowances are an important benefit for families with low or medium incomes. These checks are intended to partly replace the expense of accommodation and meals, allowing parents to spend more time with their children.

Who can claim family allowances?

Family allowances are available to citizens residing in the Canton of Zug who meet certain income and family conditions. Beneficiaries must have a household income of less than a certain amount, which varies depending on the category of beneficiaries.

Family allowance amounts

The amounts of family allowances vary depending on the category of beneficiaries and the family situation. Here are some examples of family allowance amounts in the Canton of Zug:

  • Family allowance for children: CHF 200-300 per month for a child
  • Family allowance for single parents: CHF 300-500 per month
  • Family allowance for families with more than two children: CHF 500-700 per month

Family allowances in the Canton of Zug: amounts and application

The family allowance is an economic benefit granted by the Swiss State to families with dependent children, aimed at supporting their quality of life and reducing child poverty. In the Canton of Zug, the family allowance is granted according to the rules established by cantonal law, which provides for a maximum amount of CHF 2,500.00 per family, with a ceiling of CHF1,000.00 per child.

Question and Requirements

To apply for the family allowance, families must complete the application and provide the following documents:

  • Identity document of all family members
  • Certificate of residence in the Canton of Zug
  • Income tax return of family members
  • Birth certificate of dependent children

Families must also meet the residence and income requirements established by cantonal law. In the Canton of Zug, households must have a gross household income of less than CHF 120,000.00 per year.

Key points

Application Procedure

  • What to do: Apply for a family allowance to the Canton of Zug, which applies the Federal Law on Assistance and Support for Families (LASSF) of 6 October 2000.
  • Where to go: The Social Services Office of the Canton of Zug, located in one of the most populous cities in the canton, Zug, is the reference point for applications for family allowance.
  • Required documents: identity card, residence certificate, tax return, employment and contribution certificate, marriage certificate, birth certificate of children.

Family allowance amounts

  • Basic amount: The family allowance consists of a basic amount and a contribution amount.
  • Base amount: the Canton of Zug applies a base amount of CHF 3,900.00 per year for 2023.
  • Contribution amount: The contribution amount is calculated based on the parents' income and ranges from CHF 1,800.00 to CHF 3,600.00 per year.
  • Total amount: the total amount of the family allowance is CHF 5,700.00 per year for 2023.
