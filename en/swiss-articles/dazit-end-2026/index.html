The DaziT program comes to an end, the digital transformation continues (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 September 2026

The UDSC's DaziT program will end on December 31, 2026, digitizing customs and border security processes.

Context

In brief

  • The FOCBS's DaziT programme will end on 31 December 2026.
  • DaziT has digitized customs and border security processes.
  • Digital transformation continues with new priorities.

Key Facts

  • What: End of the DaziT program.
  • When: December 31, 2026.
  • Where: Switzerland.
  • Who: Federal Office for Customs and Border Security (FOCBS).
  • Amount: Not yet specified.

The DaziT transformation programme of the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security (FOCBS) will come to an end on 31 December 2026 as planned. DaziT makes it possible to fully simplify, optimise and digitise processes relating to customs formalities, tax collection and border security. The FOCBS Executive Board took stock of the conclusion of the programme and the next priorities for digital transformation at a press conference attended by Federal Councillor Karin Keller-Sutter and economiesuisse President Christoph Mäder.

The digital transformation within the FOCBS therefore goes beyond the mere DaziT framework and also includes adjustments to the organisation, processes, job profiles, working tools, infrastructure and legal bases.

Operational details

Practical implications

The conclusion of the DaziT programme will have a significant impact on the daily lives of Swiss citizens and businesses. The digitalisation of customs and border security processes will simplify procedures and reduce waiting times. This will result in greater efficiency for companies operating in the cross-border trade sector, saving them time and resources.

As regards the performance-related heavy vehicle charge (HVF), the complete renewal of the system and the liberalisation of the market are a step towards a fairer and more transparent management of road infrastructure. This could have a positive impact on the competitiveness of transport companies, which will benefit from reduced operating costs.

The modernisation of border control systems and adaptation to Schengen developments will ensure greater security for Swiss citizens. New digital solutions for carrying out mobile checks will make it possible to respond more effectively to extraordinary situations and improve border management.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the introduction of DaziT, customs and border security processes were still largely based on manual and paper-based procedures. This resulted in longer waiting times and increased complexity for companies and citizens. Digitalisation has made it possible to simplify and optimise these processes, making them more efficient and transparent.

The digital transformation has also led to greater flexibility for the FOCBS, which can now gain more flexibility to strengthen controls and improve handling in the event of extraordinary situations. This is particularly important in a context of increasing cross-border mobility and security challenges.

Key points

Actions to take

To benefit from the new digital solutions introduced by DaziT, companies and citizens need to be informed and prepared. Here are some concrete actions that can be taken:

For companies

1. Inform yourself about new digital solutions: Companies should be aware of the new digital applications and services available, such as "Passar" for cross-border traffic of commercial goods and "QuickZoll" for customs clearance of private purchases.

2. Train staff: It is crucial that staff are properly trained in the use of new digital solutions. This will ensure a smooth transition and reduce adaptation time.

3. Update internal processes: Businesses need to review and update their internal processes to make the most of new digital solutions. This could include reviewing their merchandise and document management procedures.

For citizens

1. Use new digital services: Citizens can benefit from new digital services, such as electronic cash on delivery and solutions for tax collection and refund. These services simplify procedures and reduce waiting times.

2. Educate yourself about new regulations: It is important for citizens to be informed about new customs regulations and laws. This will ensure that they are in compliance with the new regulations and can benefit from the new digital solutions.

Useful tools

For more information and support, citizens and companies can use the following tools:

  • ePortal: A digital platform that offers access to various services and information.
  • "QuickZoll" app: An application for customs clearance of private purchases.
  • Electronic Cash on Delivery: A digital service for the collection and refund of taxes.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will the DaziT program end?
The DaziT programme will end on 31 December 2026.
What are the main objectives of DaziT?
The main objectives of DaziT include reducing regulatory costs for the economy, reducing full-time posts within the UDSC and stabilizing operating costs in the IT sector.
What new digital services have been introduced by DaziT?
Among the new digital services introduced by DaziT are "Passar" for cross-border commercial goods traffic, the "QuickZoll" app for customs clearance of private purchases and electronic marking.
