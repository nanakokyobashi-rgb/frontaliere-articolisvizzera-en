In 2025, Suva saved 123 million francs by auditing approximately 2.9 million invoices. The accident insurer announced the results on Tuesday. Treatment expenditures amounted to 1.3 billion francs, 6% higher than the previous year.

Auditing medical invoices

The audits focused on invoices for medical and therapeutic services. According to data released by Suva, 297,000 documents contained errors or inaccuracies, representing 10.4% of the total reviewed. Suva therefore returned these invoices for correction.

The press release notes, among other issues, duplicate charges, errors in billing, services without insurance coverage, and invoices for cases already closed. In some cases, there were also charges for services never performed. Overall, the examples concern billing, insurance coverage, and the actual provision of services.

Invoices for medical costs are digitally verified, and Suva automatically processes three-quarters of them. The press release therefore describes a check applied to medical and therapeutic service documents, with invoices being returned when errors or inaccuracies are discovered.

The Link to Company Premiums

Michael Widmer, head of healthcare cost management at Suva, linked the audits to the premium levels of insured companies:

"In the absence of such audits, the average premiums of insured companies would increase by approximately three percent."

The estimate refers to the average number of insured companies. Suva is headquartered in Lucerne and protects more than 2.2 million workers from the consequences of accidents and occupational diseases.

The numbers in the report use different units: 2.9 million refers to audited invoices, 297,000 to those with errors or inaccuracies, and over 2.2 million refers to insured workers. The savings are expressed in francs, the increase in healthcare costs as a percentage, and the effect on premiums as an estimated average. The comparison with the previous year concerns healthcare costs; it is not presented as an explanation for the anomalies found.
