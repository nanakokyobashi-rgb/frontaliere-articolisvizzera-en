The September figure

In September 2026, Swiss watch exports abroad stood at 2,2 billion francs. According to data released by the Federation of the Swiss Watch Industry (FH), the value increased by 11,7% compared with the same period in 2025. From Bienne (BE), the source therefore reports growth of over 11%, while the number of timepieces sold rose by 19,9%, reaching 1,4 million.

The arithmetic comparison between 19,9% and 11,7% yields 8,2 percentage points: quantity grew more than value in the monthly data. Both measures are therefore increasing, but the difference between the rates should be interpreted as the gap between growth in the number and growth in value, not as a second export figure.

September's growth follows that of the previous three months, already identified by the source as a confirmed trend: August recorded +9,1%, July +9,6% and June +11,2%. The monthly trajectory also includes May's mini-rebound, +0,4%, after a very difficult April, at -16,6%. Between August and September, the rate rose from +9,1% to +11,7%, an increase of 2,6 percentage points calculated from the stated rates.

Over the January–September period, the balance remains positive: Swiss watch exports rose by 2,7% in value year on year, reaching 19,5 billion francs. The cumulative figure covers the first nine months, while the 2,2 billion concerns September: the two figures therefore refer to different time windows.
