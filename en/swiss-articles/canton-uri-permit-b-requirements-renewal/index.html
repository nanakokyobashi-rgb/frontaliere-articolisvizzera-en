How to apply for and renew residence permit B in Canton Uri: documents, duration, cantonal migration office and full procedure.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Permit B issued by Canton Uri, normally for 5 years and renewable
  • National regulatory reference: Federal Law on Foreigners (LStr)
  • Cantonal competence: Migration Office of Canton Uri
  • Typical documents: passport, employment contract, accommodation certificate

Key facts

  • What: Residence permit B (Aufenthaltsbewilligung B / permis de séjour B) for foreign nationals in Canton Uri
  • When: Initial duration up to 5 years, then periodic renewals; renewal terms not yet specified
  • Where: Canton Uri (Altdorf), cantonal migration office
  • Who: SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) at federal level; cantonal migration services for implementation
  • Amount: Cantonal issuance/renewal fees not specified in the source

Residence permit B is governed by the Federal Law on Foreigners (LStr) and is issued in Switzerland by each Canton. In the Uri Canton, operational responsibility lies with the Cantonal Migration Office, which works in agreement with the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) in Bern. The B permit is intended for foreign nationals residing in Switzerland with a long-term residence intention, usually linked to a work activity or family reunification, and is granted for an initial duration that can reach up to five years, with subsequent possibility of renewal.

Operational details

What changes for residents in Canton Uri

Obtaining and maintaining a B permit involves a series of recurring obligations that affect everyday life. The holder is required to notify the Migration Office of any relevant change: change of employer, change of domicile, changes in marital status, birth of children. Failure to update this information may jeopardise renewal. For non-EU/EFTA citizens, the issuance of the B permit is often tied to a specific employment relationship: job loss must be reported promptly and may affect the residence title.

Cost of living and cantonal integration

Living in Canton Uri means dealing with a more contained housing market than in the major Swiss urban centres, but it is still governed by federal law on tenancy (art. 253 ff. CO). The deposit may amount to up to three months' rent and must be paid into a blocked account in the tenant's name. On the healthcare front, mandatory health insurance (LAMal/KVG) must be taken out within three months of arrival, with a per-capita premium that varies by canton and region; for adults, deductibles ranging from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500 are available, and the Canton grants premium reductions based on income.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure for the B permit in Canton Uri

Here is an indicative procedure, based on the federal framework of the FNA (Federal Act on Foreign Nationals) and the cantonal practice of Uri. The actual steps and exact documents must always be confirmed with the Migration Office of Canton Uri.

1. Collection of documents: valid passport, biometrics, employment contract or binding offer, proof of accommodation, civil status certificates if necessary. 2. Submission of the application to the Migration Office of Canton Uri (Altdorf), with the cantonal form duly completed. 3. Verification of the requirements by the cantonal service, in agreement with the SEM (State Secretariat for Migration) regarding quotas and allocations. 4. Possible interview and collection of additional documents (extracts, references, proof of integration). 5. Decision and issuance of the B permit with an initial duration of up to five years. 6. Notification of any change (employment, address, civil status) for the entire period of validity. 7. Renewal request before expiry, with updated documentation.
