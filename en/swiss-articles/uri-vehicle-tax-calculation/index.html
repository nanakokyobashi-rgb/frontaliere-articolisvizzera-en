Uri motor vehicle tax: calculation and payment

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 10 October 2026

In the Canton of Uri, the calculation, deadlines and amounts of the motor vehicle tax follow cantonal rules: what to check for each vehicle and address.

Context

TL;DR

  • In Uri, the motor vehicle tax follows cantonal rules.
  • The calculation, deadlines and amounts must be checked against the official source.
  • Changes of vehicle and address require checking the status.

In the Canton of Uri, motor vehicle tax is among the services governed by cantonal rules and offices. The calculation and payment procedure must therefore be understood through the Canton's official guidance: requirements, deadlines and amounts are elements to be verified against the cantonal source.

The operational aspect comprises four connected steps: understanding the calculation criteria, identifying the deadline, contacting the road traffic office and updating the record when the vehicle or address changes. The guide does not replace the Canton's guidance with a standard amount valid for every motorist. Therefore, reading the communication received and the official page is part of the procedure.

The boundary between areas of responsibility

The distinction from federal taxes helps avoid procedural errors. AFC/ESTV is the authority indicated for direct federal tax and VAT. Motor vehicle tax in Uri instead follows the cantonal channel, with the competent cantonal offices. UST/BFS has a statistical role and does not set rates; UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG, and does not determine this tax.

The distinction matters when a family or a worker checks multiple items in the Swiss budget. A payment request relating to a vehicle should not be confused with direct federal tax, VAT or social contributions. dichiarazione delle imposte also belongs to a separate administrative process. For the vehicle levy, the road traffic office and the official source of the Canton of Uri remain the references to follow.

What the verification covers

The correct reading begins with the vehicle and the administrative record associated with the request. The criteria used for the calculation, the stated amount, the payment deadline and the office's instructions are then checked. If the vehicle has changed in the meantime or the address is no longer up to date, the matter must be reconsidered in light of that change. The prudent rule is to use the official cantonal guidance, without replacing it with formulas taken from another Canton. The criterion applies to both calculation and payment.

Operational details

From the document to the concrete situation

The most delicate part is not looking for a generic formula, but understanding which administrative situation the request describes. In Uri, the reference remains cantonal, so a comparison with another canton cannot replace verification of the applicable rule. This applies especially when a change occurs: a different vehicle, an updated address or a new communication from the road traffic office.

| Situation | Practical check | What to avoid | | Unchanged status | reread the criteria, amount and deadline indicated | applying a rule from another canton | | Change of vehicle | verify how the new situation is handled by the office | assuming the previous reference is valid without checking | | Change of address | update the data according to cantonal instructions | leaving correspondence linked to outdated data | | Doubts about the authority | distinguish between the cantonal office, AFC/ESTV and other bodies | sending the file to the wrong body |

The table does not introduce an alternative formula: it serves to separate the steps. The source does not indicate a standard amount, a formula or a general date to apply to every vehicle. Consequently, the useful question is not what amount applies throughout Switzerland, but which criterion and deadline apply to the status registered in Uri.

Change of vehicle and change of address

A change of vehicle opens a specific check because the matter falls among the circumstances to be verified with the road traffic office. The owner must compare the new situation with the official instructions, without taking for granted that the previous document still describes the current case. If the vehicle does not change, the check focuses instead on the data and deadline reported in the available communication.

Updating the address has a distinct administrative function. It serves to keep the data used for communications from the canton consistent; the method and channel to use depend on the official instructions. Here too, the rule is not to derive the procedure from another territory.

For the household budget, checking the tax can be supplemented by the guide on costo della vita in Svizzera, but the two tools have different purposes: a cost guide does not replace the cantonal calculation. The correct amount remains the one determined according to the rules and indications of the competent authority in Uri.

Key points

Practical procedure for Uri

Whoever has to handle the case can proceed step by step, keeping the vehicle data separate from the other Swiss taxes.

1. Identify the case. Verify that the request concerns the motor vehicle tax of the Canton of Uri and not direct federal tax, VAT or social security contributions. This distinction immediately points to the correct cantonal channel. 2. Open the Canton's official source. Look for the section dedicated to motor vehicle tax and read the calculation criteria, requirements, deadline and amount applicable to the case in question. Do not use a table from another Canton as a substitute. 3. Check the vehicle's status. If there has been a change of vehicle, follow the specific instructions of the road traffic office and verify how the case is updated. If the vehicle is unchanged, compare the available communication with the data already on file. 4. Update the address. When the address has changed, use the procedure indicated by the competent office and check subsequent communications. The source does not establish a single channel: the reference is the cantonal one. 5. Make the payment. After verifying the criteria, amount and deadline, follow the official payment instructions and keep the communication linked to the case.

How to avoid the most common mistakes

The first mistake is looking for the answer from AFC/ESTV simply because it concerns a tax. AFC/ESTV deals with direct federal tax and VAT; for Uri's motor vehicles, verification is based on cantonal rules and offices. The second is treating a change of address as a detail separate from the case: the update should instead be considered together with the communications from the road traffic office. The third is paying based on previous data without checking whether the vehicle has changed.

If the matter also involves other charges, it is useful to keep the cantonal levy, tax return and social security contributions separate. dichiarazione delle imposte can help with the general tax process, but it does not replace Uri's instructions. To compare the effect of the main items on disposable income, use calcolatore stipendio/imposte.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who manages the motor vehicle tax in the Canton of Uri?
For this matter, the reference is cantonal: motor vehicle taxes follow the rules and offices of the Canton. In practical terms, this includes the road traffic office. AFC/ESTV is instead responsible for direct federal tax and VAT; UST/BFS produces statistics, and UFAS/BSV deals with social security. These bodies do not replace Uri's official source for criteria, deadlines and amounts.
Is there a single deadline for all vehicles in Switzerland?
A national deadline derived from another canton's situation must not be applied. For Uri, deadlines, requirements and amounts must be verified against the Canton’s official source and the information provided by the road traffic office. The guide does not provide a single date: payment must be arranged according to the deadline associated with the relevant matter.
What should be checked if the vehicle changes?
Changing vehicles is one of the situations to be checked in cantonal practice. It is necessary to reread the calculation criteria and the instructions of the road traffic office to understand how to update the matter and which amount or deadline to follow. It is not advisable to automatically transfer the previous reference: verification must be carried out according to the official rules of the Canton of Uri.
How is the address updated?
When the address changes, the update must be carried out according to the instructions of the competent office of the Canton of Uri. The procedure must follow the channel indicated at cantonal level and not the instructions of another territory. After the update, it is advisable to check subsequent communications relating to the motor vehicle tax and verify the payment deadline associated with the matter.
