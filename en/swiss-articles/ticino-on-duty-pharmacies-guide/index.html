Operational details

What “on duty” means here

The duty data comes from the official pages of the Ticino pharmacists’ association (OFCT). The snapshot used for this guide covers only Mendrisiotto, Luganese, Bellinzonese and Biasca e Valli. Locarnese is not yet included in the duty parser, so no coverage is inferred for that area.

If you need a pharmacy open now, use the duty page for your area and check the stated interval. The address directory and ordinary opening hours are different surfaces from verified duty.

Directory, opening hours and duty: three different data points

  • directory: A directory record means that the official catalogue contains the entry; it does not mean that the pharmacy is open now.
  • opening hours: Opening hours are shown only when the page or source publishes them; they are not inferred from the directory or its timestamp.
  • verified duty: Verified duty means a regional interval published by OFCT; it is not the same as continuous opening. Check by phone before travelling.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which OFCT areas are covered by the duty data?
The duty snapshot covers Mendrisiotto, Luganese, Bellinzonese and Biasca e Valli. Locarnese is not yet included in the duty parser.
Does duty mean that a pharmacy is open continuously?
No. Duty is a regional interval published by OFCT; check the page and confirm by phone.
Does the site show Italian pharmacy duty services?
No. The Italian catalogue is an address directory and this guide makes no Italian on-duty claim.
