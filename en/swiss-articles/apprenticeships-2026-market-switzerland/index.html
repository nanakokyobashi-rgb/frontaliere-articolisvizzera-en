The apprenticeship market in Switzerland is proving stable throughout 2026, with around 76,000 signed contracts recorded at the end of August. This figure confirms the strength of Swiss vocational education and training, demonstrating how companies continue to invest concretely in the new generation, offering young people important opportunities to enter the job world and significant career prospects. The observed stability continues in line with the volumes of previous years, highlighting a solid educational structure rooted in the country's economic fabric.

…

Operational details

The relevance of Swiss vocational education and training is not limited to the domestic market, but also finds resonance and recognition on the international scene thanks to top-tier competitions. From September 22 to 27, 2026, the city of Shanghai will host the 48th edition of the WorldSkills professional championships. In this important global showcase, approximately 1,400 talents from all over the world will compete in a wide range of professional disciplines, offering a concrete demonstration of their technical and operational skills on a premier international stage.

The Swiss Delegation and Global Competition

Switzerland is participating in the international event with the SwissSkills 2026 national team, composed of 42 highly qualified young professionals. Accompanying and supporting the Swiss delegation in this adventure in Shanghai are 38 industry experts. Events of this international magnitude make it possible to highlight the structural strengths of the Swiss educational model, characterized by the close link between classroom theory and daily practice in the workplace, the high quality of the teaching provided, and constant cooperation between the Confederation, the Cantons, and organizations in the world of work. For the young professionals in competition, direct comparison with peers from other nations represents not only a rigorous test of their technical capabilities, but also an experience of personal and professional growth of inestimable value.

What is the Transition Barometer and when is it published?
The Transition Barometer is a specific survey conducted every year at two different times, specifically in April and August. The tool detects the latest developments in the transition phase from compulsory school to secondary level II. The results of the August survey, carried out by the gfs.bern institute on behalf of SEFRI, will be made public at the end of October 2026.
How does Switzerland participate in the 2026 WorldSkills World Championships?
Switzerland participates in the international event with the SwissSkills 2026 national team, made up of 42 highly qualified young professionals. Accompanying and supporting the Swiss delegation in Shanghai are 38 industry experts. The event takes place from 22 to 27 September 2026 and sees the participation of around 1,400 talents from all over the world.
