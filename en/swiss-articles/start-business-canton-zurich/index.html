Operational details

Opening a business in the Canton of Zurich requires registration in the commercial register. The cost of registration in the trade register varies from CHF 500 to CHF 5,000, depending on the type of business and the size of the company. You must submit an application for registration in the commercial register of the Canton of Zurich, which can be done in person or through a legal representative.

The trade register registration requirement is required for business activities in the Canton of Zurich, including limited liability companies (SRL) and unlimited liability companies (SRI). Sole proprietorships and partnerships are not subject to registration.

Concrete example: If you want to open a restaurant business in Zurich, the cost of registration in the commercial register could be CHF 2,000, plus the costs of publishing the name and address of the company (CHF 500). In addition, additional expenses may be required for the registration of workers and for the purchase of occupational accident insurance (CHF 1,000).

Regulations: Article 17 of the Commercial Law of the Canton of Zurich stipulates that every business must be registered with the commercial register within 30 days from the date of commencement of the business. The law was amended in 2019 to include new provisions on the registration of online activities (Law 16,635 of 31 July 2019).

The process of registration in the commercial register is managed by the Commercial Register of the Canton of Zurich, which is responsible for registering and keeping up-to-date information relating to commercial companies.
