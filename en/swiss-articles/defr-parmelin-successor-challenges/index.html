Parmelin's successor: US tariffs, agriculture and housing

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 October 2026

The new head of the EAER will tackle US tariffs, PA30+ and the housing plan with more than 30 measures, between the vote on November 29 and the 2028 deadline.

A handover with open files

On 29 November, Guy Parmelin will once again be called upon to defend the revised War Material Act before the people. In the meantime, his successor at the helm of the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research, the EAER, will have to take charge of strategic dossiers for the future of Switzerland.

Among the challenges are the US tariffs. Switzerland has been negotiating with the United States on tariffs for exports to the American continent for months. Parmelin had taken it up again after the complicated start between Karin Keller-Sutter and President Trump. The declaration of intent signed in November 2025 aims to reduce the uncertainty of rates changed according to the mood of the US administration.

In free trade, Parmelin concluded agreements with India, renewed the agreement with China and continued the one with Mercosur. Parliament approved the South American treaty, but agricultural circles and those linked to the protection of the environment and human rights promised a referendum. The successor will have to defend it before the people.

The Federal Economic Machine

The succession also concerns the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs. Helene Budliger Artieda, who will resign next March, played a crucial role in the trade negotiations. The Federal Council will have to appoint her successor; it is unclear whether Parmelin will be able to propose a candidate before leaving.

Agriculture, arms and relations with the EU

Parmelin, a winegrower by profession, presented the agricultural policy for the coming years. The PA30+ provides for cuts in direct payments for small farms in the lowlands. Farmers and environmentalists are contesting the project, which the next person responsible will have to defend before parliament in 2028.

War material will be the focus of the vote on November 29. If the changes are rejected, the right will lobby for the Swiss arms industry; if they pass, the left will criticize the weight of economic interests on humanitarian law.

The Swiss-EU package is being examined by parliament. After discussions in the Council of States on wage protection, state aid and European research and education programmes, the National Council will address the issue in December: Parmelin will be able to defend the government's position, or it will be up to his successor.

Houses and rentals

At the beginning of 2024, after two round tables with representatives from the cantons, cities, municipalities, construction and real estate, a national action plan with more than 30 measures was adopted. A follow-up is planned: the new head of the EAER is to find solutions to the housing shortage and rising rents and to deal with the Asloca initiative against high rents. Operational details

What is at stake for families, businesses and territories

The handover should be read as a series of decisions with different timings. A negotiation on tariffs, a trade agreement with a promised referendum, a popular vote and parliamentary scrutiny are not the same path. For those who live or work in Switzerland, distinguishing these steps helps to understand when a dossier remains in political negotiation and when it comes to a public choice.

Exports and free trade

For companies exporting to the American continent, the concrete issue is the uncertainty of US tariffs. The rates are changed according to the mood of the American administration, while the declaration of intent of November 2025 aims to limit it. The Mercosur dossier, on the other hand, follows a parliamentary trajectory: the treaty has been approved and the opposing circles have announced a referendum. For companies and citizens, the distinction separates a negotiation with the United States from a possible decision before the people.

Agriculture and housing

The PA30+ focuses the discussion on small farms in the lowlands and on direct payments. The source indicates the opposition of farmers and environmentalists and sets the parliamentary defense for 2028. The point to follow is therefore the political choice on support for the sector and its defense before Parliament.

In the housing market, the reported problem is the housing shortage together with rising rents. The national plan, adopted at the beginning of 2024 after round tables, contains more than 30 measures and links the federal dossier to the realities of cantons, cities and municipalities, as well as the construction and real estate sectors. The Asloca initiative against high rents adds a separate step. To follow this dimension, you can consult the costo della vita in Svizzera.

Key points

How to follow the next steps

To turn the news into a practical control of the calendar, it is advisable to separate the deadlines and note the decision-making level for each one. The following sequence distinguishes the popular vote, Parliament, negotiations and action plan without confusing their effects.

Five checks on the calendar

1. November 29 — War material. It marks the date of the popular vote and considers the two outcomes indicated: the rejection would open pressure from the right for the Swiss arms industry; the approval would fuel the criticism of the left on the relationship between economic interests and humanitarian law.

2. December — Switzerland-EU. Follow the passage to the National Council after the debates in the Council of States. The topics that have already emerged are wage protection, state aid and European research and training programmes. The defence of the government's position will be up to Parmelin or his successor.

3. 2028 — agricultural policy. It retains an entry dedicated to PA30+. The point to follow is the parliamentary defense of agricultural policy, with attention to direct payments of small lowland farms and the positions of farmers and environmentalists.

4. Tariffs and Mercosur — two distinct paths. For US tariffs, the reference is to the Swiss-US declaration of intent signed in November 2025 and the negotiations still ongoing. For Mercosur, the approval of parliament and the promise of a referendum by the opposing circles must be separated.

5. Housing — national follow-up. It monitors the planned follow-up to the action plan adopted at the beginning of 2024 after the two round tables. The more than 30 measures and the Asloca initiative against high rents are two distinct elements; the cantons, cities, municipalities, construction and real estate are involved in the dossier.

On a separate sheet, he notes the succession of the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs. Helene Budliger Artieda has resigned in March and the Federal Council must appoint a successor. The source leaves open the possibility that Parmelin will or will not propose a candidate before departure: it is a different step from the leadership of the EAER.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main challenges for Guy Parmelin's successor?
The successor will have to manage US tariffs, defend the PA30+ agricultural policy in 2028 and continue the national housing plan. In addition, he will have to manage the Swiss-EU package in parliament and the possible referendum on the Mercosur treaty, as well as oversee the succession of the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs.
When will the vote on the law on war material be taken?
The popular vote is set for November 29. Guy Parmelin will still be in office to defend the law. If passed, the left will criticize the weight of economic interests; if rejected, the right will lobby for the Swiss arms industry.
What does the national housing plan provide?
Adopted at the beginning of 2024 after round tables with the cantons, cities and municipalities, the plan contains more than 30 measures. The new head of the EAER will be responsible for dealing with housing shortages, rising rents and the Asloca initiative against high rents.
What is the state of trade negotiations with the US and Mercosur?
A declaration of intent was signed with the US in November 2025 to reduce tariff uncertainty. The Mercosur treaty was approved by Parliament, but agricultural and environmental circles promised a referendum, which the successor will eventually have to face.
