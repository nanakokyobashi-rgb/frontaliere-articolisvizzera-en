The security deposit for a tenancy in Switzerland may not exceed three months' rent. The amount must be held in a blocked account in the tenant's name. These are the two operational elements of the guarantee indicated for 2026: a limit expressed in months' rent and an account in the tenant's name.

The reference is CO Art. 253 et seq. The source characterizes tenancy law as federal and the same in every canton. The maximum security deposit is therefore not defined by a different rule for Zurich than for other cantons.

Another rule concerns the landlord's termination notice. To be valid, it must be drawn up on the official cantonal form. If the tenant intends to challenge it, the stated deadline is 30 days and the application must be addressed to the conciliation authority. The cantonal reference therefore concerns the form and the channel for the challenge, not the federal limit on the security deposit.

Within the framework of the rental market in Switzerland in 2026, the comparison of average prices by canton is paired with protections, with a focus on the canton of Zurich. The market data helps interpret territorial differences; legal protection, on the other hand, follows the federal rules referred to in the source. For those looking for a home in Zurich, the national comparison does not replace verification of the cantonal procedure.

Zurich in the national comparison

The canton of Zurich is therefore the specific point of observation, not an exception to federal tenancy law. Anyone measuring the housing burden in costo della vita in Svizzera must therefore look at average prices and, separately, the rules on security deposits and termination notices.

The comparison between a tenancy in the canton of Zurich and the rest of Switzerland does not stop at the rent. Premiums and deductions also form part of the cost of living, but each item follows different rules. Separating them makes the budget easier to read and prevents rent from being confused with a social or insurance deduction.

Costs beyond rent

For a resident, LAMal e cassa malati is mandatory and must be taken out within three months of arrival. It is private insurance with a per-capita premium, not a tax and not a payroll contribution. The deductibles for adults are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 or 2500; premiums vary by canton and region, while premium reductions are cantonal. Healthcare costs must therefore be kept separate from rent, even when measuring the cost of living.

Salary also has its own deductions. AVS/AI/IPG accounts for 5.3% for the employee and 10.6% overall with the employer; AD/AC is 1.1% up to the annual maximum. For LPP/BVG, on the coordinated salary, the indicated rates are 7% between the ages of 25 and 34, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54, and 18% from age 55 up to the reference age. These percentages do not change the security deposit limit: they serve to assess disposable income without mixing different rules.

In Zurich, this sequence makes it possible to distinguish the market price from the procedure prescribed for the tenant.

Practical procedure for the tenant

Five checks before and after the tenancy

To manage a tenancy in 2026 without confusing the market with rights, proceed in this sequence:

1. Start with the market comparison. If the reference is the canton of Zurich, use the comparison of average prices by canton as the national framework. Do not turn the price data into a rule on the security deposit or termination.

2. Verify the guarantee. Before accepting the conditions, check that the deposit does not exceed three months' rent. The money must be placed in a blocked account in the tenant's name.

3. Examine the form of the termination. If the landlord communicates the termination, check the official cantonal form. The source links the validity of the landlord's termination precisely to this form.

4. If you intend to contest it, use the correct deadline. The challenge must be submitted within 30 days to the conciliation authority. This step must not be replaced with a different procedure: it is the channel indicated for the challenge.

5. Complete the assessment. For a resident, LAMal must be taken out within three months of arrival. Bear in mind that premiums vary by canton or region and that premium reductions are cantonal; to assess deductions and disposable salary, you can use busta paga svizzera alongside it.

The sequence separates the checks concerning the contract from those concerning the personal budget. In the canton of Zurich, the market can be assessed through the cantonal comparison, but the operational verification must stop at the rules indicated: security deposit, blocked account, official form and conciliation authority.

The salary-related part should also be considered separately. AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC and LPP/BVG follow their own percentages; they are not items to be added to the security deposit, but entries to distinguish when assessing disposable income. The comparison remains clearer if the rent, LAMal premiums and deductions are considered as different components of the cost of living.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much can the security deposit be in Switzerland?
The maximum security deposit is equal to three months' rent. The sum must be paid into a escrow account in the tenant's name. The rule is part of the federal lease law referred to in CO art. 253 et seq. and applies in the same way in every canton, including the canton of Zurich.
When is the landlord's cancellation valid?
The lessor's cancellation is valid only if it is communicated on the official cantonal form. If the tenant wants to dispute it, he must contact the conciliation authority within 30 days. The term and channel indicated by the source are valid within the framework of the federal regulations of the lease, while the form has cantonal reference.
Does the canton of Zurich have different bail rules?
The canton of Zurich is the specific focus of the 2026 comparison, but the federal lease law is the same in every canton. So the bail limit doesn't change. The official form used for the cancellation of the lessor and the operational reference to the conciliation authority remain cantonal.
How do LAMal and premiums affect the tenant's budget?
For residents, the LAMal/KVG is mandatory and must be stipulated within three months of arrival. Premiums are per capita and vary by canton or region; the reduction in premiums is cantonal. Adult deductibles are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500. This item must be distinguished from rent and wage contributions.
