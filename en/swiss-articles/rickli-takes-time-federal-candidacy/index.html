Natalie Rickli is taking time to consider a federal candidacy

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

Rickli, a Zurich cantonal councillor from the UDC, does not confirm her candidacy for the Federal Council and postpones her answer until after the autumn holidays; the deadline for applications is October 23.

Natalie Rickli, a Zurich State Councillor from the Swiss People’s Party (SVP) and one of the favorites for the position that Guy Parmelin will leave vacant, has not confirmed a candidacy for the Federal Council. The position she has made public remains noncommittal: Rickli is not committing herself on a possible move to the federal government and is taking the time necessary to reflect.

A dual interest

In an Instagram post, the Swiss People’s Party politician wrote that she feels comfortable in the Council of State. In the same message, she declared herself ready to run for a third term. This willingness signals that she is ready to seek re-election to her Zurich post, but it does not resolve the question of Parmelin’s succession. The text indeed separates the two issues: on the one hand, the positive relationship with her current role and her willingness to run again; on the other, a federal candidacy that, according to the reported statement, requires further reflection.

The news therefore records neither a yes nor a no. It records a pause in decision-making. The holiday period also helps define the timeline of the matter: the councillor is spending a holiday devoted to scuba diving.

The date given

The postponement was expressed in clear terms:

“Now I’m disappearing underwater, literally, and I’ll speak out after the autumn holidays.”

The next statement is thus scheduled for after the autumn holidays, while the deadline for candidacies to succeed Parmelin is next October 23. The source does not give a more precise date for Rickli’s return to the issue and does not anticipate what her choice will be.

For the national public, the key point to watch is the gap between a willingness to seek a third term on the Council of State and a candidacy for the Federal Council. Until Rickli speaks, both pieces of information must be considered separately.
