The popular initiative "Yes to protection against illegal rents" has reached the minimum number of signatures needed to be presented to the referendum.

Context

Abusive prisons, the initiative succeeded with over 108,000 signatures

The popular initiative "Yes to protection against illegal rentals" was presented by the promoters of the Swiss Tenants' Association (ASI). The initiative calls for curbing the rise in rents and making rentals more accessible. According to developers, tenants pay an average of CHF 360 per month too much.

Key facts

  • What: protection against abusive rents
  • When: 23 June 2026
  • Where: Federal Chancellery
  • Who: Swiss Tenants' Association (ASI)
  • Amount: over 108,000 signatures

The initiative aims to introduce a maximum ceiling for rentals, which cannot exceed 70% of the market value of a property. In addition, it is planned to introduce a system of stricter controls for abusive rentals.

According to data provided by ASI, Swiss tenants pay an average of CHF 360 per month too much for their rentals. In some cantons, such as the Canton of Zurich, tenants pay as much as 500 francs per month more than the market value.

The initiative has already been successful, with over 108,000 signatures collected. The next move will be to present the initiative to the Federal Chancellery for evaluation.
