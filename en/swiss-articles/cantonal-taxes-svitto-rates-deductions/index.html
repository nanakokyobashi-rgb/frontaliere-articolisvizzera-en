Cantonal taxes in Svitto: rates and deductions

The Canton of Schwyz is one of the Swiss cantons that levies a cantonal tax on residents' income. From 1 January 2024, cantonal tax in Svitto will be subject to new rates and deduction rules.

Cantonal Tax Rates

Cantonal tax in Svitto is calculated on the basis of residents' income. The cantonal tax rates in Svitto are as follows:

  • 1.2% on income up to CHF 8'000
  • 1.5% on income between CHF 8'001 and CHF 20'000
  • 2% on income between CHF 20'001 and CHF 50'000
  • 2.5% on income between CHF 50'001 and CHF 100'000
  • 3% on income above CHF 100,000

Cantonal Tax Deductions

Residents of the Canton of Svitto may benefit from certain cantonal tax deductions. The main deductions are:

  • CHF 2,000 for each dependent child
  • CHF 1,000 for each dependent spouse
  • CHF 5,000 for each taxpayer supporting a relative or dependent spouse
  • CHF 10,000 for each taxpayer who supports a relative or dependent spouse and has an income of less than CHF20,000
