Geneva: 1,500 march for Palestine, urge Israel sanctions

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 October 2026

Around 1,500 demonstrators in Geneva call for relations with Israel to be blocked and for Netanyahu to be arrested. Bern: no detention for negotiating purposes.

In Geneva, around 1,500 people took part in a pro-Palestine demonstration organized by the Collectif Urgence Palestine. The Geneva police, quoted by Keystone-ATS, reported the number of participants and a spokeswoman for the police described the mobilization as completely calm. The protest thus returns to the city three years after 7 October and Israel's offensive in Gaza.

The message to Switzerland

The march aimed primarily at the Swiss government. The protesters are asking for a strong signal: the immediate blocking of all military, energy and commercial relations with Israel. The request also extends to commercial, academic and cultural relations and military cooperation. In support of the stop, they recalled the agreement stipulated by Switzerland for the purchase of Israeli war drones.

One of the most frequent chants was «Switzerland accomplice». The participants accused Israel of carrying out a real ethnic cleansing in the Palestinian territories, recalling repeated warnings from the UN. They also denounced the use of hunger as a weapon of war and the toll of deaths and injuries in the Gaza Strip.

The conduct of the settlers in the West Bank has also come under fire, formally condemned by Switzerland only a few days ago. According to the protesters, Bern has stood by or even favored Israel over the last three years.

The request on mandates

The Collectif Urgence Palestine calls for the immediate arrest of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant if they enter Switzerland. The request is linked to the arrest warrant issued by the International Criminal Court. Bern's reported position is different: Israeli leaders will not be stopped if they travel to the country for negotiating purposes.

The collective also urges federal authorities to implement the directives of the International Court of Justice, which require states to do everything possible to prevent genocide. The source recalls that the term was used primarily by the UN special rapporteur and other independent entities of the United Nations. Since last year, the International Commission of Inquiry on the Palestinian Territories believes that in Gaza Israel committed four of the five acts that the 1948 Convention defines as genocide. The same commission underlines that military operations continue with strong intensity despite the ceasefire agreed in October 2025.
