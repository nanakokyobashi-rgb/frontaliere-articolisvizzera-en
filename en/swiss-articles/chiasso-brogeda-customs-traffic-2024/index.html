Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main developments in Chiasso Brogeda customs procedures in 2024?
The updated procedures include the use of digital technologies for the control of goods, an electronic booking system to reduce waiting times and stricter controls to ensure compliance with tax and health regulations.
How do changes to Chiasso Brogeda customs affect border workers?
The changes facilitate the passage of border crossers by reducing queues and waiting times, thus improving punctuality and reducing costs related to transport and time lost in queues.
What documents are needed to transit through Chiasso Brogeda customs?
You must have up-to-date documentation including transport documents, invoices for the goods, customs and tax certifications compliant with Swiss regulations. It is recommended to use the electronic booking system to facilitate the transition.
