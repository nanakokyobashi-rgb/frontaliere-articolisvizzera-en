False alarm for alleged shootings during a school party (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 September 2026

About 300 people evacuated in Goldach, in the canton of St. Gallen, after reports of alleged gunfire.

Context

In a nutshell

  • About 300 people evacuated in Goldach, in the canton of St. Gallen.
  • Reports of alleged shootings during a school party.
  • Precautionary evacuation and return home.

Key facts

  • What: Evacuation of about 300 people.
  • When: Friday night.
  • Where: Wartegg school building in Goldach (SG).
  • Who: St. Gallen Cantonal Police.
  • Amount: Not applicable.

On Friday evening, during a school party at the Wartegg school building in Goldach (SG), several reports of alleged shootings came in. The St. Gallen Cantonal Police reported that, as a precautionary measure, students and teachers were initially secured inside the building. Shortly after one o'clock in the morning, officers evacuated the facility. The approximately 300 people were able to return home and reunite with their waiting parents.

Police Intervention

The police report that they ended the operation in the early hours of the morning. No hint of gunfire was found. The perceived noises presumably came from pyrotechnic objects. Several patrols and special units of the Sangalla Cantonal Police and neighbouring police forces intervened. An investigation has been launched.

Operational details

The evacuation of about 300 people during a school party in Goldach, in the canton of St. Gallen, raised concerns among parents and the local community. The rapid response of law enforcement has made it possible to manage the situation effectively, avoiding panic and ensuring the safety of everyone present.

Implications for School Safety

The Goldach episode underscores the importance of having well-defined safety protocols in schools. School authorities and law enforcement must work closely together to ensure that such protocols are strictly followed in the event of an emergency. This includes training school staff and students on how to react in crisis situations.

Prevention and Preparation

Prevention is essential to avoid similar situations in the future. Schools must invest in security measures such as alarm systems, surveillance cameras and continuous training for staff. In addition, it is important that local communities are informed about safety protocols and know how to react in an emergency.

Conclusion

The Goldach episode is a reminder of the importance of school safety and the need for close collaboration between schools and law enforcement. With proper preventive measures and good preparation, a safe environment for students and school staff can be ensured.

Key points

In case of emergency, it is essential to follow the instructions of the competent authorities and remain calm. Schools must have clear and well communicated evacuation plans for students and staff. In addition, it is important that parents are informed about school safety protocols and know how to contact the authorities in case of need.

# Emergency Steps

1. Stay Calm: Stay calm and follow the instructions of the authorities. 2. Evacuation: Follow the evacuation instructions and head to the designated collection points. 3. Communication: Inform parents and authorities of your situation. 4. Safety: Make sure you are in a safe place and do not return to the building until it has been declared safe by the authorities.
