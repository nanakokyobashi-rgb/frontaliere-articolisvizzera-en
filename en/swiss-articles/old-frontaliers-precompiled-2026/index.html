The Common Reporting Standard (CRS) obliges the tax authorities of the acceding States to automatically transmit the financial and income data of entities residing in other Contracting States every year. Switzerland has joined the CRS and applies automatic exchange with Italy on the basis of existing bilateral agreements.

How automatic data exchange works

The automatic exchange of data between Switzerland and Italy takes place through the Revenue Agency (ADE) and Switzerland. Switzerland transmits the salary data to the Revenue Agency, which verifies them and integrates them with the data already in its database.
