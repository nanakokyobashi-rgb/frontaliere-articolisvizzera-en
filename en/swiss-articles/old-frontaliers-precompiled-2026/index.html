The Common Reporting Standard (CRS) requires the tax authorities of participating States to automatically transmit each year the financial and income data of individuals resident in other contracting States. Switzerland has joined the CRS and applies automatic exchange with Italy on the basis of the bilateral agreements in force.

The pre-filled tax return is an aid for filing a tax return, not an official assessment of the tax due. It contains data that the Revenue Agency has received from third parties and that the taxpayer is required to verify, correct or supplement.

How automatic data exchange works

Automatic data exchange between Switzerland and Italy takes place through the Revenue Agency (ADE) and Switzerland. Switzerland transmits salary data to the Revenue Agency, which verifies it and supplements it with the data already present in its database.
