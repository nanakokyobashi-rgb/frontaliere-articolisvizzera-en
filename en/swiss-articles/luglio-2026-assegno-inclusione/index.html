How does the processing of the inclusion check work?

The processing of the inclusion cheque is an automated process that is performed by the Swiss Bank and other Swiss banks. The process starts when the Swiss Bank receives the payment order from the federal government. The payment order contains the information necessary to make the payment, such as account number and amount.

When do payments arrive?

Mid-month payments will be arranged around July 15. However, it is important to note that the actual credit date may vary depending on the type of account and the bank used. For example, if you use an online checking account, the payment may be credited immediately after processing, while if you use a traditional checking account, the payment may be credited after a few days.

The Inclusion Allowance is a social benefit provided by the Swiss State to citizens in economic difficulties. To avoid performance blockages, it is advisable to check in time the expiration of your ISEE (Equivalent Economic Situation Indicator) and, if necessary, contact a CAF (Centre for Employment and Training) or submit a new Single Substitute Declaration (DSU).

The expiration of the ISEE: an attention not to be overlooked

The expiration of the ISEE is an important time for Inclusion Check recipients. In Switzerland, the ISEE must be updated annually by 31 March. If it is not submitted, the service may be suspended or even cancelled. It is therefore essential to check the deadline and update your ISEE on time.

Exemption cases: when it is not necessary to update the ISEE

There are cases where there is no need to update the ISEE. For example, if the household income does not exceed a certain amount (CHF 80'000 for a household of four people in 2023, as established by the Federal Act of 16 December 2005 on the Inclusion Allowance), the update of the ISEE is not required. However, it is always best to check your situation with the CAF or the public welfare service in your canton.
