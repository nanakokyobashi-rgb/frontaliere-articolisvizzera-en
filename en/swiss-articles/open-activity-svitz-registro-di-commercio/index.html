The Commercial Register in the Canton of Schwyz

In the canton of Schwyz, registration in the commercial register is compulsory for all commercial activities. According to cantonal law, the trade register is managed by the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of the Canton of Schwyz. Registration in the commercial register is necessary for:

  • Issuance of the operating permit
  • Registration in the Register of Companies
  • Payment of registration fees

Costs of registration in the commercial register

The costs of registering with the commercial register in the Canton of Schwyz vary depending on the legal form chosen and the sector of activity. Here are some examples of registration fees:

  • Registration with a sole proprietorship: CHF 500 (article 17 of the cantonal law on trade)
  • Registration with a limited liability company: CHF 1,000 (article 18 of the cantonal trade law)
  • Registration with a limited company: CHF 2,000 (article 19 of the cantonal law on trade)

The trade register is a public register that keeps track of companies active in the Canton of Schwyz. To register a business with the trade register, you must submit a written application to the trade register and pay the registration fees, which vary depending on the legal form you choose. For example, for a sole proprietorship, the registration fee is CHF 500, while for a limited liability company, the registration fee is CHF 1,000.

On 1 January 2020, the Federal Law on the Participation of Women in Business came into force, which requires companies to have at least one female member on the board of directors. This law applies to all companies active in the Canton of Schwyz and requires companies to submit a declaration of conformity by 31 December 2020.
