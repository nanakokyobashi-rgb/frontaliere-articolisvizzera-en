The Post Office cuts jobs: up to 110 redundancies are expected (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 September 2026

The Post Office plans to cut up to 110 jobs to reduce internal costs and finance the public service in the long term without public money.

Context

In brief

  • Up to 110 jobs at risk
  • Cuts in management and support areas
  • No impact on post offices and delivery
  • Objective: to safeguard public service

Key facts

  • What: Cut of internal jobs
  • When: Announced in a press release issued today, Monday
  • Where: In Switzerland, in the management and support sectors
  • Who: Swiss Post
  • Amount: Up to 110 positions affected

…

Operational details

The in-depth analysis of the announced maneuver highlights how the corporate reorganization will specifically and exclusively target certain administrative and support sectors. The staff cuts planned by company management will specifically affect management and internal logistic-administrative support areas. The company wanted to reassure users and operational staff by specifying that the measures currently under review do not in any way affect services dedicated directly to customers. Consequently, operational staff directly in contact with the public, such as those assigned to daily mail delivery or those employed in counter activities within branches scattered across the national territory, will not be affected by these staff reduction measures. This strategic choice makes it possible to safeguard the widespread presence of traditional postal service, focusing savings solely on top-level and management support structures that do not have a direct and daily impact on the use of services by citizens residing in Switzerland. ### The four-part strategy for financial autonomy and public service To fully understand the scope of the restructuring announced by Swiss Post, it is necessary to examine the four fundamental pillars on which the entire company strategic maneuver rests. The first pillar concerns the adoption of tariff measures that are defined as proportionate to the actual costs of the services offered. The second pillar focuses decisively on the continuous improvement of internal efficiency through the optimization of work processes. The third pillar provides for targeted growth focused exclusively on the company core business, trying to develop new revenue channels where the market offers favorable opportunities. The fourth and final pillar consists in the modernization of regulatory framework conditions, a fundamental aspect to allow the company to move agilely in a communication and postal services market that is increasingly digitized and competitive at a national level.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many jobs does the Post Office risk cutting?
The Post Office plans to cut up to a maximum of 110 jobs as part of its new strategy to reduce internal costs.
Which staff sectors will be affected by the cuts?
The planned staff cuts will only affect the areas of management and internal support, without touching the operational staff or the branches.
What are the main reasons for this decision?
The decrease in the sending of paper letters and the growing preference of citizens for online payments over physical counters.
