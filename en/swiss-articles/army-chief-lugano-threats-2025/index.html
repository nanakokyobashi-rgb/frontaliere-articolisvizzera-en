Safety as a prerequisite for other federal priorities

Roos rejects the idea of pitting military spending against the Confederacy's other priorities, from social security (AVS, LPP) to climate policy. “Safety is the foundation for many things. Without security there is no thriving economy and no social and cultural coexistence. " It is a position that has a direct political weight: the distribution of federal resources touches on items that concretely affect the residents' portfolio, from sickness insurance premiums (LAMal) to first and second pillar annuities, up to energy costs and the cost of living.

In this scheme, defence spending is presented not as a competing item, but as a prerequisite. A useful framework to read the next messages of the Federal Council on the Budget and financial plans, as well as the role of Parliament in defining budgets.

What to watch in the coming months

Federal dossiers to follow

Roos's speech in Lugano comes at a time when the Confederation must fund several priorities: social security (OASI, DI, supplementary benefits), climate policy, healthcare, and defence. Some practical elements to keep an eye on:

  • Confederation budget and financial plan: the 'defence' line is growing; the other priorities are not set in opposition but 'in sequence', according to the logic expressed by the army chief.
  • Messages from the Federal Council on the army: following Roos's statements, a request for concrete results on the open internal dossiers (recruitment, equipment, cyber-defence) is likely.
  • Semi-annual reports from the FIS and the security agencies: useful to understand the real scale of the hybrid attacks mentioned by Roos, beyond political statements.
  • Parliamentary debate on military and civilian service: any legislative changes may have direct implications for permits, careers, and taxes.

Frequently Asked Questions
What did the army chief say in Lugano?
On Friday in Lugano, Benedikt Roos met the Ticino political and economic world, defining the geopolitical framework as gloomy. He cited hybrid threats, cyber, espionage and drone overflights, adding that the military suffers attacks every day. He asked for a change of pace: the time for pamphlets is over, now action must be taken.
What is the risk of military escalation cited by Roos?
The army chief spoke of strong unrest in the Baltics and cited rumours that the first attack attempts could occur by the end of this decade. He added that, according to some media, Russia is having new drone ramps built along the Finnish borders.
How does defense tie in with other federal priorities?
Roos rejects the idea of juxtaposing military spending with AVS, LPP, and climate policy. Security is presented as a basis for economy and social cohesion, a prerequisite on which the other confederal expenditure items depend, from the Budget to the financial plan.
What internal problems in the army did Roos recognize?
Roos acknowledged internal problems that have emerged in recent years and called for transparency and concrete countermeasures to regain trust. He said that the things that don't work have not been hidden and that several countermeasures have already been taken.
Where to find reliable information about these threats?
The primary source remains the Federal Department of Defence, Population Protection and Sport (DDPS) and the Swiss Army. For macroeconomic and contextual data, the institutional references are SECO and the Federal Statistical Office (FSO/BFS).
