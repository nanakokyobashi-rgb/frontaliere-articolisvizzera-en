The Canton of Solothurn offers scholarships for students who reside in the canton and enroll in a course of study. The requirements and maximum amounts are established by the cantonal office of Solothurn.

Eligibility Requirements

In order to access Canton Solothurn scholarships, students must reside in the canton and enroll in a recognized course of study. In addition, students must have a good economic situation and no other sources of funding for their study. The cantonal office of Solothurn establishes specific eligibility conditions, which may vary depending on the course of study and level of study.

Maximum scholarship amounts

The maximum amounts of the scholarships of the Canton of Solothurn are established by the cantonal office of Solothurn. In general, the maximum amounts are about CHF 1,500 per month for first cycle students and about CHF 2,000 per month for second cycle students. However, the maximum amounts may vary depending on the course of study and level of study.

Operational details

Canton Solothurn Scholarships are funding for Solothurn students who enroll in a course of study. The requirements to access this scholarship are residence in the Canton of Solothurn and enrolment in a course of study. The maximum amount of the scholarship is not yet specified.

Students who reside in the Canton of Solothurn and enroll in a course of study can benefit from this scholarship. For example, a student who resides in Bern and enrolls at the University of Bern may apply for a Canton Solothurn scholarship if he or she resides in the Canton Solothurn.

According to the Law on Scholarships of the Canton of Solothurn (LSBS), adopted on 15 February 2018, students can apply for a scholarship if they reside in the Canton of Solothurn and enroll in a course of study. The maximum amount of the scholarship is CHF 10,000 per year.

Key points

If you are a student from the Canton of Solothurn wishing to access a scholarship, you should contact the cantonal office of Solothurn to find out the requirements and maximum amounts. You can also request information on the procedures to follow to obtain the scholarship.

The Canton of Solothurn offers several scholarships for deserving students, with amounts ranging from CHF 1,000 to CHF 10,000 per year. For example, the “Solo Talent” scholarship offers CHF 5,000 a year to students who stand out for their academic excellence and commitment to research.

To access a scholarship, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Being a student of the Canton of Solothurn
  • Have a good record of studies (average of at least 4.5)
  • Demonstrate significant research or career commitment
  • Have not already received a scholarship in the amount of more than CHF 5,000 per year

To apply for a scholarship, you must fill in the application form available at the cantonal office in Solothurn and submit it by the deadline (usually in September). You can also request information on the procedures to follow to obtain the scholarship.
