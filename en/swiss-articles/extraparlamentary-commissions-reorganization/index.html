Federal Council reorganises extra-parliamentary committees (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 September 2026

The Federal Council adopts the message on the reorganization of extra-parliamentary committees: 3 dissolved, 7 merged into 2 structures; savings ~ CHF 100,000 per year from 2028.

Context

In brief

  • Federal Council adopts message on reorganization of extraparlimentary commissions on 9 September 2026
  • Dissolution of 3 commissions, merger of 7 into 2 new structures; savings of approximately 100,000 CHF per year
  • Commissions reduced by 16% from 1 January 2028; amendment to federal law on government and administration

Key facts

  • What: Message on reorganization of extraparlimentary commissions (review 2025)
  • When: Adopted 9 September 2026; entry into force 1 January 2028
  • Who: Swiss Federal Council
  • Where: Federal Administration level
  • Amount: Approximately 100,000 CHF savings per year
  • Impact: 16% reduction in commissions from 2028

On 9 September 2026, the Federal Council adopted the message on the reorganization of extraparlimentary commissions within the framework of the 2025 review. The decision provides for the dissolution of three commissions and the merger of seven others, which will become two unified structures. The measure will generate savings of almost 100,000 francs per year starting from 1 January 2028.

The dissolved commissions

Three extraparlimentary commissions will be dissolved: the Commission for the Dissemination of Swiss Education Abroad, the Federal Consumer Commission and the Federal Housing Commission. The Federal Council has furthermore decided to dissolve two additional commissions and to reorganize the Federal Accreditation Commission. The tasks of the dissolved commissions will be reallocated to other competent federal offices or eliminated if superseded.

Operational details

The four-year verification cycle of commissions

The purpose, responsibilities and composition of extra-parliamentary commissions are reviewed globally every four years, on the occasion of their complete renewal. The Federal Council has completed the consultation on the matter and has noted the results before adopting the message on reorganization. This regular cycle allows the Confederation to continuously adjust the administrative structure to current operational needs and to eliminate overlaps of competencies that have accumulated over time.

Administrative rationalization and impact on the federal budget

Savings of approximately 100,000 francs annually reflect a logic of administrative efficiency and control of public spending. Fewer organizations mean lower management costs, meetings, secretariats and personnel. The overall reduction of 16 percent of commissions from 2028 represents a step towards broader rationalization of the federal apparatus, although the amounts involved remain limited on a national scale. The reform is part of a more general strategy to streamline federal bureaucracy.

Consolidation of medical professions and labor policy

The two most significant mergers reflect an integrated vision of the federal government. The reunification of the five examination commissions into a single commission simplifies oversight of chiropractic, veterinary medicine, dentistry, human medicine and pharmacy, creating consistency in protocols and examination procedures. Similarly, the new tripartite Labor Commission brings together the Federal Labor Commission and the former tripartite commission on freedom of movement, creating a leaner structure to address labor issues at the federal level.

Key points

Legislative timeline and crucial deadlines

The message was formally adopted by the Federal Council on September 9, 2026. From this point on, the text will follow the ordinary parliamentary procedure: examination in the commissions of the National Council and the States, discussions in plenary sessions, and possible exchanges between the two chambers. Entry into force is scheduled for January 1, 2028, allowing sufficient time for organizational transition within the Federal Administration and enabling the involved commissions to adapt their internal structures.

Operational impact on involved commissions

Dissolved commissions will cease their regular activities. Their tasks will be reallocated to other competent federal offices or eliminated if superseded by new administrative dynamics. Merged commissions will have to carry out a significant internal reorganization: the new Commission for the Examination of University Medical Professions will have to coordinate the examination protocols of the five medical professions, while the Tripartite Labor Commission will have to integrate the two previous bodies with common decision-making structures and processes. During the transition period until January 1, 2028, current members will remain in office according to the procedures established by the Federal Council.

Future review of the Swiss Science Council

The Federal Council has postponed the examination of a possible reorganization of the Swiss Science Council by the end of 2027. This signals that the administrative review is not yet concluded and that other government bodies could undergo similar changes in the coming months. The review of the Swiss Science Council will follow its own timeline and will be communicated later.

Frequently Asked Questions
What exactly are the three commissions that will be dissolved?
The three extra-parliamentary committees that will be dissolved are: the Commission for the Dissemination of Swiss Education Abroad, the Federal Consumer Commission and the Federal Housing Commission. The Federal Council has also decided to dissolve two additional commissions. The tasks of the dissolved commissions will be reallocated to other federal offices or eliminated if exceeded.
In which structures will the seven commissions be merged?
Of the seven commissions to be merged, five will become the University Medical Professions Examination Commission (merger of the five Examination Commissions in Chiropractic, Veterinary Medicine, Dentistry, Human Medicine and Pharmacy). The other two will become the Confederation's Tripartite Labour Commission (merger of the Federal Labour Commission and the Federal Tripartite Commission for Free Movement).
When does the commission reorganization take effect?
It is expected to enter into force on 1 January 2028. The message was adopted by the Federal Council on 9 September 2026 and will be submitted to Parliament for approval of the regulatory change. The transition period until 2028 allows for an orderly transition between old and new facilities.
How much do you save overall with this reorganization?
The reorganisation will generate savings of around CHF 100,000 per year from 1 January 2028. In addition, the total number of extra-parliamentary committees will decrease by 16 percent, reducing the costs of management, meetings, secretariats and staff of the federal administrative apparatus.
How often does the Federal Council review extra-parliamentary committees?
The verification of the raison d 'être, the tasks and the composition of the extra-parliamentary committees takes place every four years on the occasion of the full renewal. The 2025 audit now underway has led to this reorganization. The next verification cycle will take place in 2029.
