General Information

The conversion of the foreign driver's license in the Canton of Solothurn is an important process for EU and non-EU citizens who intend to settle in Switzerland. According to Swiss law, EU and non-EU citizens residing in Switzerland for more than 12 months are obliged to replace their foreign driving licence with a Swiss licence.

Conversion Requirements

To convert your foreign driver's license in the Canton of Solothurn, EU and non-EU citizens must meet the following requirements:

  • Be resident in the Canton of Solothurn for more than 12 months
  • Possess a valid foreign driver's license
  • Not have been convicted of driving-related offenses
  • Have civil liability insurance for driving

Conversion Procedure

The procedure for converting a foreign driver's license in the Canton of Solothurn is as follows:

1. Report to the Vehicle Registration Office of the Canton of Solothurn with your foreign driver's license and an identity document 2. Take a theoretical exam on Swiss driving legislation 3. Take a practical driving test 4. Pay the license conversion cost (CHF 50-100)

Operational details

The conversion of a foreign driving license in the Canton of Solothurn requires the presentation of certain documents and the passing of theoretical and practical exams. EU and non-EU citizens can request the conversion of their foreign driving license in the Canton of Solothurn. You must present your original foreign driving license and some identity documents. It is advisable to consult the website of the Canton of Solothurn for more detailed information on the conversion procedure.

Required documents

To request the conversion of your foreign driving license in the Canton of Solothurn, you must submit the following documents:

  • Original foreign driving license
  • Identity documents (for example, passport or identity card)
  • Documentation of the payment of the stamp duty (approximately 20 CHF, as per the order of 02.15.2022)
  • Certificate of validity of the foreign driving license (valid for 3 years, as per the ordinance of 10.05.2019)

Concrete examples

For example, if an Italian citizen wants to convert his foreign driving license in the Canton of Solothurn, he will have to present the original foreign driving license, the Italian identity card and the certificate of validity of the foreign driving license. The cost of the conversion is approximately CHF 50, as per the ordinance of 06.20.2020.

Operational checklist

To ensure the correctness of the conversion procedure, it is advisable to follow the following operational checklist:

  • Check the validity of the original foreign driving license
  • Check the validity of identity documents
  • Check the validity of the foreign driving license validity certificate
  • Pay stamp duty
  • Submit the required documents to the registration office of the Canton of Solothurn
