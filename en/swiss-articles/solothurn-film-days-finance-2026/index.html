The Solothurn film festival recorded 65,000 spectators, but is facing financial difficulties with an operating result of 39,000 francs in the red.

Context

In brief - The number of spectators at the Solothurn Film Days in 2026 remained stable at 65,000. - The festival recorded an operating result of 39,000 francs in the red. - The main sponsor Swisscom withdrew at the end of the 60th edition in January 2025. ## Key facts - What: Financial difficulties for the Solothurn Film Festival. - When: 2026. - Where: Solothurn, Switzerland. - Who: Organisers of the Solothurn Film Festival. - Amount: 39,000 francs in the red. The Solothurn Film Days recorded a stable number of spectators in 2026, with 65,000 admissions. However, the festival continues to face financial difficulties, with an operating result of 39,000 francs in the red, as announced today by the organizers. Despite the absence of a main sponsor, the accounts are better than expected thanks to additional revenue from ticket sales, additional support from foundations and public authorities, as well as constant cost optimization efforts. ### Economic Environment and Sponsorship The economic environment and the sponsorship situation remain difficult for the new financial year. The Solothurn Film Festival hopes to find funding that will allow them to cover the costs. The main sponsor, Swisscom, withdrew at the end of the 60th edition in January 2025. ### Film Presentation Last January, the festival presented 170 fiction films, documentaries and animated films from all linguistic regions of Switzerland, offering 1,300 film professionals a platform for meeting, exchanging and networking. ### Future prospects For President Andreas Spillmann, quoted in the press release, 'the Solothurn Film Festival is an unmissable event for Swiss cinema. The current situation presents us with a challenge, but the goal is clear: to strengthen the quality and importance of the festival in the long term."

Operational details

Practical implications The financial difficulties of the Solothurn Film Festival could have repercussions on the Swiss film industry. The search for new sponsors and funding is crucial to ensure the continuity of the festival and support for national cinema. ### Public Support and Foundations Additional support from foundations and public authorities is crucial to compensate for the absence of a main sponsor. This funding model could become a trend for other cultural events in Switzerland. ### Cost Optimization Cost optimization efforts are essential to maintaining the financial balance of the festival. This could include reducing operating expenses and adopting more efficient practices in event management. ### Impact on Swiss Cinema The Solothurn Film Festival is an important platform for Swiss cinema, offering visibility to films from all linguistic regions of the country. The financial stability of the festival is crucial for the national film industry.
