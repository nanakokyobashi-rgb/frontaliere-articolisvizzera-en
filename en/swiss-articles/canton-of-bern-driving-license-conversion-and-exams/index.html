Context

In a nutshell

The conversion of the foreign driver's license in the Canton of Bern requires theoretical and practical examination, according to the cantonal regulations. Compulsory courses must be attended before taking the exam. The relevant circulation office is responsible for processing the conversion application.

Key facts

  • What: Foreign driver's license conversion. When: within 3 months from the date of submission of the conversion application. Where: Canton of Bern. Who: competent traffic office. Amount: CHF 100 for the theory exam and CHF 150 for the practical exam.

The process of converting a foreign driver's license in the Canton of Bern is governed by the cantonal rules, published on the website of the traffic office. Art. 10 of the cantonal law on road traffic states that the foreign driver's license must be converted within 3 months from the date of submission of the conversion application.

# Conversion Requirements

To convert your foreign driver's license, you need to:

  • be a resident of the Canton of Bern;
  • have a valid foreign driver's license;
  • have attended a compulsory driving course;
  • take the theoretical and practical exam.
