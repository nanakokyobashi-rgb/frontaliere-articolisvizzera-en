Driving licence in Bern: conversion and requirements

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 13 August 2026

The conversion of the foreign driver's license in the Canton of Bern requires the theoretical and practical examination, as well as the attendance of compulsory courses. The relevant circulation office is responsible for processing the conversion application.

Context

In brief

The conversion of a foreign driving licence in the Canton of Bern requires the theoretical and practical examinations, in accordance with cantonal regulations. Mandatory courses must be attended before taking the examination. The competent road traffic office is responsible for processing the conversion application.

Key facts

  • What: conversion of a foreign driving licence. When: within 3 months of the date of submission of the conversion application. Where: Canton of Bern. Who: competent road traffic office. Amount: CHF 100 for the theoretical examination and CHF 150 for the practical examination.

The process for converting a foreign driving licence in the Canton of Bern is governed by cantonal regulations, published on the website of the road traffic office. Art. 10 of the cantonal road traffic act stipulates that the foreign driving licence must be converted within 3 months of the date of submission of the conversion application.

Requirements for conversion

To convert a foreign driving licence, it is necessary to:

  • be resident in the Canton of Bern;
  • hold a valid foreign driving licence;
  • have attended a mandatory driving course;
  • take the theoretical and practical examinations.

Conversion procedure

The procedure for converting a foreign driving licence is as follows:

1. Submit the conversion application to the competent road traffic office. 2. Pay the conversion fee (CHF 100 for the theoretical examination and CHF 150 for the practical examination). 3. Attend a mandatory driving course. 4. Take the theoretical and practical examinations.

Concrete examples

Example 1: A foreign national residing in Bern wants to convert their foreign driving licence. After submitting the conversion application and paying the fee, they must attend a mandatory driving course and take the theoretical and practical examinations.

Example 2: A Swiss citizen residing in Lausanne wants to convert their foreign driving licence issued in another canton.

Regulations and deadlines

  • Cantonal Road Traffic Act (LCSS), Art. 10: "The foreign driving licence must be converted within 3 months of the date of submission of the conversion application". * Regulation on the theoretical and practical examination (REST), Art. 5: "The theoretical and practical examination must be taken within 6 months of the date of submission of the conversion application".

Theory and practical examination

The theory and practical examination consists of two parts:

  • The theoretical part consists of a written test of 30 questions, with a maximum time of 1 hour
  • The practical part consists of a 30-minute driving test, with an examination of 10 questions

Conversion procedure

1. Submit the conversion application: you must submit the conversion application to the competent road traffic office of the Canton of Bern. You can download the application form from the road traffic office's website or request a copy from the office itself. 2. Processing of the application: the road traffic office processes your application and checks your foreign driving licence to ensure that it is valid and has not been suspended or revoked. 3. Theory test: you must take a theory test on Swiss road law and road safety. The theory test can be taken at the road traffic office or at an approved training centre. 4. Mandatory courses: you must attend mandatory training courses on road safety and Swiss road law. The courses are organised by approved training centres and last approximately 12 hours. 5. Practical test: after attending the mandatory courses, you must take a practical test to demonstrate your ability to drive in different conditions.
