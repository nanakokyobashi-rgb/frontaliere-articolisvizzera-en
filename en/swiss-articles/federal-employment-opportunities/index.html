The Swiss Federal Administration positions itself as an attractive employer for those intending to start a career in the public sector. The official portal stelle.admin.ch centralizes the offer of internships for newly graduated students, 50 structured professional training programs and flexible employment conditions. The fundamental value of the federal entity is to put its talent at the service of Swiss public interest, working in multidisciplinary teams on innovative solutions. The official communication emphasizes how working at the Federal Administration is meaningful and allows one to take on concrete responsibilities for the well-being of the population.

Operational details

The Federal Administration's positioning in the national labor market

The Federal Administration is one of Switzerland's major employers, managing a workforce distributed throughout the national territory and operating in very diverse fields: central administration, research, security, social and economic policies. The ability to attract young talents through structured internship programs is crucial for the generational renewal of the federal public sector. The offer of flexible work arrangements responds to a global trend that characterizes the contemporary labor market: new generations of graduates increasingly value the balance between professional life and personal commitments. In the Swiss context, where public salaries are historically competitive, flexibility has become a differentiating element to attract the best candidates. You can compare federal opportunities with the private sector by consulting job postings.

Compensation and career progression

The source explicitly mentions compensation based on performance, an element that distinguishes the federal offer from other public administrations that follow rigid salary grids based on seniority. For those entering as recent graduates, this means that the possibility of increasing your income does not depend solely on automatic advancement through seniority steps, but also on effective contribution to projects and achievement of objectives. Continuous training represents a direct investment by the institution in the professional growth of its staff. In a context of digital transformation of public administration and growing national challenges (environmental sustainability, data governance, advanced social policies), the ability to update skills is a fundamental added value for those working in federal institutions.

Key points

How to Apply to Federal Administration: Basic Procedure

The first step to explore opportunities with the Federal Administration is to visit the official portal stelle.admin.ch, where all information about apprenticeships, professional training programs, and job postings is centralized. Here you can filter opportunities by sector of interest.

The application procedure follows these essential steps:

1. Access the stelle.admin.ch portal and navigate to the apprenticeship or training section of interest 2. Verify specific requirements: university degree, required certifications, language skills, specific experience 3. Prepare a complete application: curriculum vitae, cover letter, academic or professional references 4. Submit your application online respecting the deadlines indicated in the selection notice 5. Wait for contact from the entity for any selection interviews or subsequent evaluations

What to Expect from a Federal Apprenticeship

If selected for an apprenticeship, the recent graduate gains access to a structured experience that combines operational duties with integration into multidisciplinary teams. During the apprenticeship, the candidate has access to complementary training directly during the performance of the assignment, thus accelerating the acquisition of practical skills. The duration and specific compensation of apprenticeships are not detailed in the official portal communication, but federal apprenticeships historically represent an entry point to the federal public sector, often a precursor to stable employment positions. The 50 professional training programs mentioned follow paths for acquiring specialized skills, potentially over multiple years, with the issuance of certifications recognized at the federal level.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the specific requirements to access an internship at the Federal Administration?
The source indicates that trainees are intended for recent graduate students. However, the detailed requirements (university degree, languages required, specific skills, areas of specialization) are not specified in the official communication. You should consult the stelle.admin.ch portal directly and check the criteria required for the sector of interest.
What are the 50 professional trainings offered by the Federal Administration?
The source mentions 50 professional trainings available at the Federal Administration, underlining the commitment to growing talents. However, the specific details of each training, including sectors, duration, career paths and certifications issued, are not provided in the communication. For a complete list and detailed description, you should consult the portal stelle.admin.ch.
How does remuneration work in the Federal Administration?
The Federal Administration offers remuneration based on performance, rather than relying solely on seniority steps. This means that the compensation depends on the employee's actual contribution to the projects and the achievement of the established objectives. Specific details on salary brackets, performance rewards and valuation-related increases are available on the recruitment portal.
What forms of flexible working are available in the Federal Administration?
The source cites flexible forms of work as one of the benefits offered by the Federal Administration, without specifying whether it is smart working, flexible hours, part-time, teleworking or other modalities. To know the specific flexibility available for each position, it is advisable to consult the job listings on stelle.admin.ch.
How to contact the Federal Administration for career information?
The official portal stelle.admin.ch is the main channel for information on internships, vocational training and job opportunities at the Federal Administration. Each job advertisement or training call includes the contact details of the federal department responsible for specific questions and clarifications.
