Swiss AI job market: +32% in one year

Between July 2025 and June 2026, almost 5'000 job postings related to artificial intelligence were published in Switzerland. Compared with the same period of the previous year, the volume increased by 32%. This figure is reported in the Swiss AI Jobs Report 2026 by Hochschule Lucerne, dedicated to the demand for AI professionals in the country.

The result is not confined to IT. Demand grew in all ten main areas of activity analyzed. Outside IT, the report also considers the finance and insurance sector, education and research, the chemical-pharmaceutical sector, trade and logistics, public institutions, engineering, industry, legal and business consulting, and healthcare. IT retains first place with 1'211 listings, while the sharpest increase was recorded among public institutions, where postings rose by 67%.

Curdin Derungs, author of the study, notes that the increase in AI-related positions in the public sector signals growing confidence among politicians and administrators in this technology.

Small and medium-sized enterprises generated 76% of the overall increase and account for 62% of all published positions. For Derungs, the technology is no longer limited to large tech groups: it is entering the processes and services of established SMEs.

Profiles and regions

Almost 40% of postings concern Data Engineering and Machine Learning Engineering profiles, involved in developing data infrastructure and the operational functioning of AI models. The report also identifies opportunities for Product Owner, Manager and AI Consultant roles: these professionals are responsible for identifying uses of AI within companies and turning them into operational solutions.

The market favors experienced profiles capable of combining technical and strategic skills. Senior positions increased by 42%, while permanent junior jobs account for only 5% of the total. At regional level, Zurich accounts for 2'181 postings, equal to 45% of the national total. In Central Switzerland, there are between 100 and 140 offers per 100'000 inhabitants, compared with a Swiss average of 96; in the Canton of Lucerne, postings grew by 55%.

Operational details

The total number of job postings describes an expanding market, but it does not offer the same indication to everyone looking for work. A practical interpretation involves different questions: which sector is demand concentrated in, which role is being sought, what experience is valued, and where are the positions posted.

From quantity to choice

For a technical profile, IT remains the main numerical reference. The increase in public institutions' listings, however, adds a second perspective: 67% measures the growth in listings, not their absolute volume. SMEs offer another way of interpreting the data: with 62% of positions and 76% of the increase, demand is not confined to large technology groups.

The mix of roles helps distinguish two directions. Data Engineering and Machine Learning Engineering concern data infrastructure and model operations. Product Owner, Manager and AI Consultant are linked to identifying business applications and implementing them. For those at the beginning of their careers there are opportunities, but the share of permanent junior jobs remains at 5%; for those with experience, senior positions have increased by 42% and the search is described as more challenging.

Geographic distribution also changes the picture. Zurich accounts for 2'181 listings, representing 45% nationally, while in the cantons of Central Switzerland the rate is between 100 and 140 offers per 100'000 inhabitants, compared with 96 nationwide. The Canton of Lucerne records an increase of 55%. Anyone consulting annunci di lavoro can therefore use sector, profile and area as distinct criteria, without reducing the phenomenon to the national figure alone.

The report combines figures and professional voices: the Applied AI center at Hochschule Lucerne examined 8'439 listings published between July 2024 and June 2026, provided by x28 AG, processed and analyzed with the support of AI. The quantitative section is complemented by interviews with professionals active in different sectors and roles.
