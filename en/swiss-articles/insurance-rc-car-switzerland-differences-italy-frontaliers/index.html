How does car liability insurance work in Switzerland for cross-border commuters?

Car liability insurance in Switzerland for cross-border commuters is subject to withholding taxes. This means that insurance amounts are considered taxable income and subject to tax. For cross-border commuters, the wealth tax is applied to insurance premiums, which may vary depending on the canton of residence.

Comparison with Italy

Italy, however, avoids double taxation with the tax credit (EC framework of 730). This means that insurance amounts are not considered taxable income and are not subject to tax. The tax credit is calculated based on your insurance premium and can be used to reduce your wealth tax.

The New Cross-Border Agreement

The New Cross-border Workers Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and will be applied from 1 January 2024. This agreement provides for the simplification of taxation procedures and the reduction of taxes for cross-border workers. The agreement was signed between Switzerland and Italy to reduce bureaucratic barriers and promote the mobility of cross-border workers.
