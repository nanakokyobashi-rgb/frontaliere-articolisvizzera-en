Frequently Asked Questions
What happened to the nights in August?
In August, overnight stays in Switzerland fell by 1.9% year-on-year. The text specifies that a decrease of -2.1% had been indicated a week earlier. The figure is therefore presented in a more attenuated form than in the previous communication. The source also points out that, if confirmed, the result would be in addition to the negative months from March to June.
Which guests had the most impact on the outcome?
Guests from abroad weighed most heavily, with -2.3%. Domestic demand also fell by -1.4%. Among foreign markets, Asia recorded -9.2%, with the Gulf States at -15.6%, China at -10.7% and India at -7.6%. America is at -2.5%, while Europe is almost stable at -0.2%.
How was Europe in August?
Europe is almost stable at -0.2%, but the individual markets have different trends: Holland -6.8%, Germany -0.8%, France +0.4%, United Kingdom +1.1% and Italy +2%. The overall European figure therefore brings together different declines and increases in August, all reported in the source.
When will the absolute and regional data arrive?
For absolute numbers, regional disaggregated data and all other information indicated by the FSO, we will have to wait until 5 October. The source recalls that 2025, with 43.7 million nights, marked a new record. January, February and July had grown, while from March to June the results were negative.
