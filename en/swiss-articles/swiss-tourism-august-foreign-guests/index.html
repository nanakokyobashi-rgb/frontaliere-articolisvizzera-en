The national data

In August, tourist overnight stays in Switzerland decreased by 1.9% year-on-year. This is the figure communicated by the Federal Statistical Office, also indicated in the text with the acronym UST, in a more attenuated form compared to the -2.1% indicated a week ago. The guests coming from abroad above all affect the overall result. Domestic demand, however, also falls, with -1.4%. The change therefore concerns both the foreign and domestic components, but with a greater weight of guests from abroad on the overall result. The comparison used is annual: August is compared with August of the previous year.

Foreign markets

The foreign component recorded -2.3%. Among the international markets, Asia stands out, at -9.2%. Within this group, the Gulf States score -15.6%, China -10.7% and India -7.6%. America is also in decline, with -2.5%. Europe, however, is almost stable at -0.2%. The European detail remains structured: Holland -6.8%, Germany -0.8%, France +0.4%, United Kingdom +1.1% and Italy +2%. The European result, therefore, does not erase the differences between individual markets. The source reports together countries in decline and countries in growth, keeping the continental data and that of individual states separate.
