Sika: turnover at 5.59 billion in the first half (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 July 2026

The Zughese group Sika closed the half-year with a turnover of CHF 5.59 billion. Positive organic growth and high forecasts for the current year.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Half-yearly turnover of CHF 5.59 billion.
  • Organic growth of 2.9% in the semester, 5.7% in the second quarter.
  • Net profit of CHF 552.1 million.
  • Upward revision of the turnover forecast for the year.

Key facts

  • What: Sika's semi-annual financial results.
  • When: January-June.
  • Where: Headquarters in Baar, Zug.
  • Who: Sika Group.
  • Amount: 5.59 billion francs of turnover.

The Sika industrial group, based in Baar in the canton of Zug, published its results for the first half of the year, outlining a framework of resilience despite a complex macroeconomic context and the weight of the Swiss franc. From January to June, the company recorded a turnover of CHF 5.59 billion. Although this figure represents a nominal decrease of 1.5% compared to the corresponding period of the previous year, the analysis net of exchange rates reveals a different dynamic, showing a progression of 4.0%.

The company pointed out that recent acquisitions contributed 1.1% to growth. Particularly significant is the figure for organic growth, which reached 2.9% for the entire semester, with a marked acceleration in the second quarter, where it reached 5.7%. This positive signal interrupts a period of stagnation, given that the group did not register an organic growth of this entity since the third quarter of the previous year.

Operational details

Cost Impact and Profitability Analysis

Managing profitability in the first half required careful monitoring of operating expenses. Despite external pressures, the company has limited declines, benefiting decisively from cost savings from a business efficiency program. Operating profit, measured by EBITDA, stood at CHF 1.06 billion, a modest decline of 0.7%. The EBITDA margin, a key indicator for assessing operational soundness, showed a slight improvement, rising to 19.0%.

With regard to net profit, the supplier of building materials reported a value of CHF 552.1 million, a reduction of 0.4% compared to the previous year. These data confirm a substantial resilience of the business model. It is interesting to note that the comparison with the initial forecasts shows a constant exceeding of analysts' expectations. The company has demonstrated its ability to navigate the complexities of the Swiss market, where labour costs and franc dynamics directly affect profit margins.

The impact of foreign exchange remains a constant variable for Swiss exporting companies. Sika's strategy, focused on organic growth and the integration of new acquisitions, makes it possible to partially mitigate foreign exchange risk. Key points

Perspectives and Financial Management In light of the semi-annual results, Sika has updated its forecasts for the entire fiscal year. The company has revised upward its revenue growth target, now aiming for an increase between 3 and 6 percent in local currency. This target represents a significant improvement over the previous range, which was set between 1 and 4 percent. This revision reflects greater visibility into future operations and a solid pipeline of ongoing projects.

Regarding profitability, estimates have been slightly adjusted prudently. Sika now anticipates an EBITDA margin between 19.0 and 19.5 percent, compared to the previous estimate of 19.5-20.0 percent. This change accounts for market variables and expected operational costs for the second half of the year. For workers and sector partners, these data provide a clear indication of the group's strength, which continues to invest in its expansion and internal efficiency.
