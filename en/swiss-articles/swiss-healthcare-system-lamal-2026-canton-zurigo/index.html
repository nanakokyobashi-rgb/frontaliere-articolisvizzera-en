Operational details

LAMal health insurance is an obligation for residents in Switzerland, as established by the Federal Act of 1996. The canton of Zurich, for example, offers adult deductibles from CHF 300 to CHF 2,500, with a maximum of CHF100,000 per year for medical benefits.

Residents of Switzerland can choose from several sick cases, each with its own premiums and deductibles. The main sick fund is the Kassenärztliche Vereinigung, which covers 75% of medical expenses. Other sickness funds include the Confederal Disease, the Canton Zurich Sickness Fund and the Canton Bern Sickness Fund.

The canton of Zurich also offers a premium reduction for residents who already have health insurance. This means they can save up to 20% on their annual premiums. In addition, the canton of Zurich has introduced a deductible for medical examinations by a general practitioner, which is covered up to CHF 50 per visit.

Key points

To obtain LAMal health insurance, residents of Switzerland must enter into a contract with one of the authorized health insurance funds. The canton of Zurich offers adult deductibles from CHF 300 to CHF 2500.

Residents of Switzerland can also opt for a premium reduction if they already have health insurance. For example, if a Swiss citizen already has health insurance with a monthly premium of CHF 500, they could get a 20% reduction for a total of CHF 400 per month.

It is important to check the premiums and deductibles of your sick fund before entering into a contract. The Federal Act on Illness (LAMal) states that maximum premiums for an adult may not exceed 7.5% of gross annual income.

The canton of Geneva, for example, offers adult deductibles from CHF 200 to CHF 2000. The Geneva sickness fund also offers a 15% reduction for Swiss citizens who already have health insurance.

Residents of Switzerland can also opt for health insurance with a lower deductible. For example, the Cassa di malato in Bern offers a CHF 100 allowance for adults, while the Cassa di malato in Lausanne offers a CHF 150 allowance.

It is important to note that premiums and deductibles may vary by region and canton. Residents of Switzerland must check the premiums and deductibles of their sick fund before entering into a contract.
