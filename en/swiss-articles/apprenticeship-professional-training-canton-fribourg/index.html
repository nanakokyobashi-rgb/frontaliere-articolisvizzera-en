Apprenticeship and vocational training in the Canton of Fribourg represent a fundamental path for many young people who wish to enter the world of work with solid practical preparation. This article explores how to find an internship, the details of the internship contract, the salary of apprentices, and the opportunities offered by professional maturity.

How to find an internship

Finding an internship in the Canton of Fribourg can be a challenging process, but there are several resources available to facilitate the search. Apprentices can consult internship offers published by vocational schools, training centers, and local companies. It is also possible to participate in job fairs and networking events to meet potential employers.

Internship contract and apprentice salary

The internship contract is a legal document that defines the rights and duties of both the apprentice and the employer. In the Canton of Fribourg, the internship contract must be signed by both parties and registered with the competent cantonal office. The salary of apprentices varies depending on the sector and level of training, but it is generally regulated by collective agreements or individual contracts.

Practical analysis: concrete implications at national/cantonal level

Apprenticeships and vocational training in the Canton of Freiburg have concrete implications for the Swiss labour market. Apprentices who successfully complete their internship acquire practical and theoretical skills that are highly demanded by companies. This contributes to reducing the youth unemployment rate and ensuring a skilled workforce.

Comparison with the previous situation

Before the introduction of structured apprenticeship programmes, many young people struggled to find work after school. With the advent of apprenticeship, apprentices now have a clear path to employment, with the support of employers and educational institutions. This has led to an increase in the number of young people choosing vocational training as a viable option for their future.

Concrete scenarios

Consider the case of an apprentice who wishes to become a mechanic. After completing their internship in a local company, the apprentice may choose to continue working for the same company or look for opportunities in other companies. With professional maturity, the apprentice also has the opportunity to continue their studies in a technical or university institute, opening further doors for their career.

Action: what to do concretely in Switzerland

For apprentices and employers in the Canton of Freiburg, it is important to follow a series of steps to ensure a successful apprenticeship experience. Here's a step-by-step guide:

Steps to find an internship

1. Consult the internship offers published by vocational schools and training centres. 2. Attend job fairs and networking events to meet potential employers. 3. Prepare a resume and motivation letter highlighting your skills and interests. 4. Apply to selected companies and prepare for interviews.

# Internship Contract Procedure

1. Sign the internship contract with the employer. 2. Register the contract with the competent cantonal office. 3. Ensure that the contract includes all details relating to pay, working hours and responsibilities.

Opportunities for professional maturity

1. Successfully complete the internship and pass the final exams. 2. Enroll in a technical institute or university to continue your studies. 3. Use the professional baccalaureate to access advanced career opportunities.
