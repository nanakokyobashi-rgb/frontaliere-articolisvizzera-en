Employment Rate and Job Demand

The Canton of Uri is considered one of the Swiss cantons with the highest employment rate, with a constant demand for labour and strong economic growth. According to data from the Statistical Service of the Federal Statistical Office (SFSO), in 2022 the employment rate in the Canton of Uri was about 83.4%, higher than the national average of 78.1%. This means that almost 8 out of 10 inhabitants of the Canton of Uri are employed.

Industries that hire more

The sectors that employ the most in the Canton of Uri are industry, construction and services. Industry is the sector that absorbs the most workers, with a share of about 35% of the total workforce. Construction occupies about 25% of the workforce, while services occupy about 40%.

Average salary

The average salary in the Canton of Uri is about CHF 5,500 per month, according to Statistics Service data. This means that workers in the Canton of Uri have an average income level above the national average.

Wages and labour market in URIs: hiring and collective agreements

The Canton of Uri is known for its strong economy and dynamic labour market. The region is characterized by a high employment rate, which represents a strong point for entrepreneurs and investors.

The main area of recruitment in the Canton of Uri is industry, which accounts for the majority of occupations. Construction and services are the other sectors that hire the most. Entrepreneurs in the industrial sector are active in the production of manufactured goods and in the provision of technological services.

Average salary

The average salary in the Canton of Uri is about CHF 5,500 per month. This level is influenced by economic conditions and labour demand in the region. Entrepreneurs operating in the Canton of Uri can count on a skilled and productive workforce.

Collective Agreements

Collective agreements are in force for employees in the Canton of Uri. These contracts are negotiated between trade unions and business associations to ensure balanced working conditions and protection of workers' rights.
