The maximum security deposit is 3 months' rent, as established by the Federal Law on Leases (Lloc) of 20 March 2009. This means that the landlord can request a maximum guarantee deposit of 3 months' rent, which can be used to cover any damage or shortcomings to the lease.

The dispute of the initial fee is possible within 30 days to the conciliation authority. According to the Federal Law on Leases (Lloc), the tenant has the right to dispute the initial rent within 30 days of receipt of the lease letter. The conciliation authority will be responsible for resolving the dispute between the landlord and the tenant.
