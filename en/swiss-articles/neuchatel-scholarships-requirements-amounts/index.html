Neuchâtel Scholarships: Requirements and Amounts (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

Complete guide to scholarships in the Canton of Neuchâtel: award criteria, residency and academic merit requirements, application procedure and where to find updated calls.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Scholarships for deserving students in financial need in the Canton of Neuchâtel
  • Requirements: cantonal residence, academic merit and economic situation
  • Maximum amounts and deadlines vary annually: consult official notices
  • To apply: registration, financial documents, application by deadline

Key facts

  • What: Scholarships for deserving and economically needy students
  • Where: Canton of Neuchâtel
  • Who: Cantonal Office of Education and Training
  • Main requirements: Cantonal residence, academic merit, analysis of the family economic situation
  • Frequency: Calls and deadlines vary annually

The Canton of Neuchâtel offers scholarships to students who accumulate academic merit with economic difficulties. Unlike other social aid, training grants are not universal: each Swiss canton decides on its own amounts, criteria and allocation procedure, in compliance with federal principles of equity.

Access to scholarships in the Canton of Neuchâtel passes through an analysis of the economic situation of the student's family. There is no published fixed maximum income: the canton uses an assessment system that considers gross income, deductions for fixed expenses, the number of dependent employees and the cost of living in the cantonal territory.

Who is entitled?

Residents of the Canton of Neuchâtel attending first level studies (bachelor), second level (master)

Operational details

Differences between cantons and impact on student mobility

Scholarships in the Canton of Neuchâtel, as in all Swiss cantons, create particular effects on the geographical mobility of students. A student residing in Neuchâtel who moves to Zurich to study might lose access to the Neuchâtel scholarship, as some cantons extend support even if the student changes canton for study reasons, others do not. A student from another canton who moves to Neuchâtel as a resident must wait to be registered in the population register before qualifying for cantonal scholarships.

Second element: the incentive to enroll in local institutions. Since each canton mainly funds studies at its own universities and training institutes, a student residing in Neuchâtel often receives higher amounts if they attend the University of Neuchâtel (UNINE) compared to enrollments in other Swiss cantons. This reflects the policy of encouraging local education, typical of Swiss cantonal governance.

Concrete scenarios

Imagine a student whose family has an annual gross income that falls into the moderate need bracket of the canton: not poor, but not middle class either. Calculate your potential gross salary to assess if your household might qualify. If they attend a low-cost bachelor's degree, the scholarship could cover 20-30% of the training costs. If they attend an expensive master's degree, it could rise to 40-50%. If they have excellent merit (high grades), some cantons award bonus amounts.

Another scenario: a student with a serious need situation (single-parent family, unemployment of a parent). Even with average academic merit, they might have priority over candidates with high merit but less need. The canton of Neuchâtel balances these two factors in the evaluation.

Key points

How to apply

The procedure varies from year to year in Neuchâtel, but it follows a standard pattern.

First, check your residence. Make sure you are registered in the population register of the Canton of Neuchâtel. If you have just arrived from another canton or from abroad, you may have a waiting period (usually a few weeks from the first registration in the register before you qualify).

Second, download the form and the notice. Visit the website of the cantonal office of education and training (or Neuchâtel scholarship service) to find the annual notice. This document contains the deadlines, the application form, and the complete list of required documents.

Third, gather the documents: residence certificate, last bank statements or asset declarations, parents' pay slips for the last three months, copy of the family tax return (if available), enrollment certificate in the educational institution, academic merit document (official report card, academic transcripts).

Fourth, fill out the form carefully. Sections dedicated to personal data, family situation, declared income, current academic commitments. Be precise: errors or incomplete information can cause the application to be rejected.

Fifth, submit the application before the cantonal deadline. Deadlines are usually in the spring (for studies starting in September) or early autumn (for studies already started). There are no flexible deadlines: after the deadline, the application is not considered.

Sixth, wait for the evaluation by the canton. It usually communicates decisions within two or three months. If the application is rejected, you can appeal through the cantonal administrative procedure (appeal times vary depending on the canton).

Frequently Asked Questions
Can a non-resident student in Neuchâtel access the canton's scholarships?
Not automatically. The bags are mainly reserved for residents of the canton. A student from another Swiss canton transferring to Neuchâtel as a new resident must wait to be entered in the population register (generally 3-6 months) before qualifying. A foreign student residing in Neuchâtel could qualify if he/she holds a residence permit (permit C or B), but he/she must check the annual notice for specific details.
What is the maximum amount of a scholarship in Neuchâtel?
There is no fixed amount published on a stable level: the canton determines maximum amounts each year based on the available budget, the number of candidates, and the availability. Amounts generally range from 10-15% to 50-70% of training costs, depending on the level of studies (bachelor's or master's) and the degree of need ascertained by the family assessment. Consult the annual call of the competent office for details of the current academic year.
If I receive a scholarship from Neuchâtel and change my residence, what happens?
It depends on the terms of the bag at the time of assignment. Generally, scholarships are conditional on maintaining residence in the canton. If you move out of Neuchâtel to another canton, the bag ceases or you have to return the amounts already received. If you change your residence within the canton (from one municipality to another in Neuchâtel), you are not interested. Always check the conditions in the bag allocation contract.
What are the application deadlines for 2026?
Deadlines change every year and depend on the academic cycle (autumn vs. spring semester). Visit the official website of the cantonal office of education and training in Neuchâtel for specific dates 2026. Usually, deadlines for the fall semester fall in the spring, while deadlines for the spring semester fall in the fall. Do not postpone beyond the established deadlines: late applications are not considered.
What happens if my bag application is rejected?
The canton will communicate the reason for the rejection (for example: income above the entry threshold, insufficient academic merit, incomplete documentation). If you believe that the decision is unfair or incorrect, you can appeal through the cantonal administrative procedure within the time limits specified in the rejection letter. An appeal requires a detailed written reasoning and, in some cases, may involve legal fees.
