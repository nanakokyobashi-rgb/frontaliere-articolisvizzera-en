Apprenticeship and vocational training opportunities in the Canton of Freiburg

Apprenticeship and vocational training are two job opportunities that allow young people to gain skills and work experience. In the Canton of Freiburg, apprenticeship and vocational training are managed by the Department of Education and Vocational Training.

Types of Apprenticeships and Vocational Training The Canton of Freiburg offers different types of apprenticeships and vocational training, including:

  • Basic apprenticeship: a 2-year basic apprenticeship that allows young people to gain skills and work experience in a specific sector.
  • Specialization apprenticeship: a 3-year specialization apprenticeship that allows young people to acquire skills and work experience in a specific sector and to deepen their knowledge.

Key points

Apprenticeship in the Canton of Freiburg: find an internship

  • To find an internship position and an internship contract, interested young people can consult the website of the Canton of Freiburg or contact the Department of Vocational Education and Training. 📊
  • Young people must have a high school certificate or vocational training diploma to be eligible for an apprenticeship or vocational training. Internship contracts must be entered into with an employer who is qualified to provide an apprenticeship or vocational training.
  • Need for an internship contract :
  • An internship contract must be concluded for a period of at least 6 months and no more than 2 years.
  • The internship must be remunerated with a minimum salary of 2,400 Swiss francs per year.
  • The employer must provide an internship contract specifying the conditions of the internship, including the trainee's working hours, duties and responsibilities.
