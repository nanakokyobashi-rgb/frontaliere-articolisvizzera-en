Parmelin resigns: the business community thanks him

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 October 2026

The 66-year-old Federal Councillor Guy Parmelin announced his resignation today; Economiesuisse and the FSVO praise its agreements with India, Vietnam and renewal with China, as well as the Corporate Relief Act in force from 2024.

Context

In brief

  • Guy Parmelin announced his resignation today.
  • Economiesuisse and the FSV praised his economic achievement.
  • Agreements mentioned: India, Vietnam and the start of renewal with China.
  • The Business Relief Act has been in force since 2024.

  • Decision → resignation announced today

  • Business Relief Act, in force → 2024

Federal Councillor Guy Parmelin's decision to resign, announced today, has also provoked reactions from the business world. Monika Rühl, Director of Economiesuisse, expressed regret for the choice and paid tribute to the 66-year-old's merits in the business world.

Parmelin has worked tirelessly for a strong Switzerland as a business location.

According to Rühl, numerous free trade agreements of great importance to the exporting Confederation have been negotiated under her leadership. The director thanked Parmelin for the good cooperation.

Economiesuisse considers the commitment of the successor of the People's Party leader at the head of the EAER to be decisive for a competitive Swiss business location. From an economic point of view, Rühl stressed, the department must remain in bourgeois hands.

Trade, business and training

The FSVO also expressed its appreciation for the departing Federal Councillor. In its financial statements, the association described Parmelin's work in economic policy as exceptional, with particular reference to the free trade agreements with India and Vietnam. It also attributed to Parmelin the initiation of the renewal of the free trade agreement with China.

Among the internal policy measures, the FSVO cited the law on the relief of companies, which came into force in 2024. It then referred to the revision of the vocational education and training legislation and the enhancement of tertiary level professional education and training through the additional titles Professional Bachelor and Professional Master, which came into force on 1 October 2026.

For the FSVO, Parmelin was a valuable partner in social partnership and took employers' demands seriously. As President of the Confederation, the association adds, he represented Switzerland successfully and with dignity.

The USC Assessment

Markus Ritter, president of the Swiss Farmers' Union, praised Parmelin's leadership and ability to maintain calm and overview. He recalled the extraordinary challenges of the mandate: the coronavirus crisis, the war in Ukraine, the unpredictability of American trade policy over the past two years, the fire in Crans-Montana and the landslide in Blatten (VS). The Vaud politician, he added, was a crisis manager: with the Americans, he contributed to the search for solutions thanks to his French-speaking charm and convincing arguments, also choosing qualified personnel for his entourage.

Operational details

The point of operation for those who work

The clearest practical consequence concerns the succession at the helm of the EAER. In its reported reactions, Economiesuisse points to the competitiveness of Switzerland as a business location as a criterion for the successor. The source does not give the name of the future manager or describe an immediate measure for companies, employees or families: the step to be taken is therefore political and institutional, not a new individual procedure.

Foreign trade as an indicator

The free trade agreements referred to by Economiesuisse and the FSVO define the foreign dossiers associated with Parmelin's budget. India and Vietnam are mentioned directly; for China, the source speaks of the start of the renewal, not of an agreement that has already been renewed. For exporting companies, this signals a political priority. However, it does not allow for the indication of sectors, costs, revenues, hires or deadlines: these data do not appear in the text.

Internally, the Business Relief Act and Vocational Education and Training have a distinct relevance. The former is referred to as a measure that came into force in 2024. The Professional Bachelor's and Professional Master's degrees, on the other hand, are presented as additional qualifications to tertiary level vocational education and training, which have been in force since 1 October 2026. The source does not specify requirements, costs, procedure or effects on employment contracts.

Positions and decisions

The demand to keep the EAER in bourgeois hands is a position of Economiesuisse, not the name of a successor. Similarly, the FSVO's assessment of the social partnership describes the relationship it had with Parmelin and its focus on employers' demands; it does not announce new rules.

Markus Ritter's assessment adds the perspective of agriculture and crisis management: calm, oversight and the ability to deal with the Americans. Here, too, the source offers a judgment on the mandate, not a specific measure for farmers or the labor market.

Key points

How to check the next steps

To use this news without overlapping different plans, it is worth proceeding by verification. The announcement of the resignation, the assessments of Economiesuisse, FSO and USC and the dossiers mentioned in Parmelin's financial statements do not have the same weight: the first concerns the announced decision, the second are judgments, the last are economic policy and education issues.

A four-step checklist

1. Separate the dossiers. Classify the issue between free trade, business relief, vocational training, social partnership or crisis management. 2. Check the dates. The law is indicated as coming into force in 2024; for Professional Bachelor and Professional Master, the source indicates October 1, 2026. The announcement remains dated only as today. 3. Distinguish between positions and decisions. The Economiesuisse request on the EAER and the praise of the FSVO and FSO do not indicate the name of the successor or a new measure. 4. Limit conclusions. Don't add requirements, costs, industries, wage effects, or deadlines that the source doesn't specify.

An employer can thus understand whether his application concerns a business file, a measure for companies, higher education or social partnership. Those who study or work can mark the date of the additional qualifications, without attributing them conditions or advantages that are not described.

The source does not indicate forms, offices, applications to be submitted or documents to be prepared after the announcement. For this reason, the concrete step is to follow the evolution of the EAER and compare each subsequent communication with the facts already explained, without anticipating the appointment or the effects.

Those who follow agriculture can read the USC's judgment as an assessment of Parmelin's leadership, not as a new decision for the sector. The same criterion applies to the statements of the associations: check who is speaking, on which dossier and whether the text describes an event that has already happened or a request.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why did Economiesuisse praise Parmelin?
Monika Rühl, director of Economiesuisse, expressed regret over the resignation of the 66-year-old and highlighted his commitment to a strong Switzerland as a business location. She emphasized the numerous free-trade agreements negotiated under his leadership and thanked Parmelin for the good cooperation, considering it crucial that the DEFR remain in bourgeois hands.
Which trade agreements were mentioned in the USAM assessment?
USAM praised Parmelin for the free-trade agreements with India and Vietnam and acknowledged the launch of the renewal of the free-trade agreement with China. It also mentioned the law on business relief, which entered into force in 2024, and the revision of vocational training regulations, with the Professional Bachelor and Professional Master qualifications from 1 ottobre 2026.
When did the law on business relief and the new training qualifications enter into force?
The law on business tax relief has been in force since 2024. The additional titles Professional Bachelor and Professional Master, introduced to enhance higher vocational education, entered into force on October 1, 2026, as reported in USAM’s statement of appreciation.
