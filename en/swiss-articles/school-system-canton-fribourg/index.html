School system Canton Fribourg: enrolment and cycles

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 17 August 2026

The age of enrolment, curricula and school calendar in the Canton of Freiburg.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The age of registration is 6 years. The cycles of studies are: primary cycle (6 years), secondary cycle I (5 years), secondary cycle II (4 years). The school calendar starts in September and ends in June.

Key facts

  • What: School system Canton Fribourg
  • When: From the age of 6
  • Where: In the Canton of Freiburg
  • Who: All children and adolescents
  • Amount: None

The Canton Fribourg school system is based on three cycles of studies: primary cycle, secondary cycle I and secondary cycle II. The enrolment age is 6 years and the school calendar starts in September and ends in June.

Study cycles

The primary cycle consists of 6 years of school and focuses on the formation of the fundamental bases of knowledge, including basic subjects such as reading, writing and arithmetic. In Freiburg, there are about 60 primary schools offering this course of study.

Secondary cycle I consists of 5 years of school and focuses on the formation of advanced skills, including core subjects and specialized subjects such as science, technology and sociology. In Freiburg, there are about 20 I secondary schools offering this cycle of study.

Operational details

The school obligation in the Canton of Freiburg lasts 9 years and includes the three cycles of studies mentioned above: primary cycle (1-3 years), secondary cycle I (4-6 years) and secondary cycle II (7-9 years). Children and adolescents must be enrolled at the age of 6 and continue their studies until completing secondary cycle II.

The school calendar is 9 months and ends in June. The total duration of a course of study is 3 years, with a total of 9 hours of lessons per day. Children may enroll at the age of 6, but only if they have reached the age of 6 years and 1 month during the current school year. Parents must apply by January 15 of the current school year.

The Canton of Freiburg has set a ceiling of 12 pupils per class, to ensure a personalized and quality education. Public school is free for all Swiss students, while foreign students must pay a tuition fee of CHF 500 per year.

Children must attend school for at least 180 days per year, with a total of 900 hours of tuition. Parents must present a medical certification if their child is unable to attend school due to illness or disorder. The school must be closed for at least 5 days a year to allow teachers to take assessment exams and plan educational activities.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is compulsory schooling for?
School obligation serves to ensure that all children and adolescents have access to education and can develop their skills and knowledge.
How does enrollment work?
Enrollment in the curriculum takes place through the local school office and requires the presentation of certain documents, such as an identity card and birth certificate.
What are the requirements to enroll in the study cycles?
The requirements to enroll in the study cycles are to be 6 years of age and to have good physical and mental health.
