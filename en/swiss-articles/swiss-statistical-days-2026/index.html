The Swiss Statistics Days 2026 feature a rich and varied programme, with 4 plenary conferences, 2 round tables, 21 workshops and 1 poster session. Artificial intelligence (AI) is revolutionising the way we collect, analyse and interpret data. According to a report by the Federal Statistical Office (FSO), in 2020, 75% of Swiss companies used data analysis tools to make informed decisions. However, only 12% of them used AI technologies. This means that there is still a lot to do to make the most of AI capabilities.

One of the main challenges is data management. According to a report by the Swiss National Bank (SNB), in 2022, Switzerland had an amount of accumulated data that exceeded 1,000 petabytes (1 petabyte = 1,000 terabytes). This means we need to find more efficient ways to collect, store, and analyze data. AI can help us achieve this, for example, by using machine learning techniques to identify patterns and trends in data.

Key points

For more information, you can consult the complete program on the website of the Federal Statistical Office (FSO) and use the calculator to calculate the most important statistics. 📊

The programme provides for the collection of data on different aspects of the economic and social life of Switzerland. Among these, the collection of data on economic activities, such as GDP (Gross Domestic Product), which measures the production of goods and services of a country. According to 2022 data, Switzerland's GDP amounted to CHF 725.4 billion.

In addition, the program includes the collection of population data, such as the working population, which refers to those who are engaged in a work activity. According to 2022 data, the active population in Switzerland amounted to about 4.5 million people.

You can use the calculator to calculate the most important statistics, such as GDP growth or unemployment. For example, the calculator can provide unemployment data for each canton in Switzerland. According to 2022 data, the highest unemployment had been recorded in the Canton of Ticino, with an unemployment rate of 3.2%.
