Serious injuries: almost 90% return to work (cross-border guide)

Suva: After serious accidents, 90% of policyholders return to work. 78% return within 3 months thanks to medical care and personalised case management.

Key Facts

  • Who: Suva (Swiss Federal Accident Insurance)
  • What: Professional reintegration rates following serious workplace accidents
  • When: Past year; 2,400 people in case management in 2025
  • Where: Switzerland; specialized clinics in Bellikon and Sion

Suva recognized almost 480,000 workplace accidents over the past year. Of these, approximately 29,000 were so serious that they caused work incapacity lasting more than three months. Despite the severity of many cases, nearly 90% of insured persons involved in serious accidents managed to return to work, thanks to effective medical care, professional support, and the backing of those close to them.

The recovery path is long, but timeliness is crucial

A workplace accident can happen in an instant: a moment of distraction, a fall, or an unforeseen event can change one's daily life. However, the recovery and reintegration path into professional life is often long and demanding. Studies show that after six months of absence from work, the chances of returning are cut in half, making timely intervention by Suva and all the figures involved in the process essential.

Operational details

The fundamental role of Suva case management

For the most complex cases, Suva activates its own case management: case managers coordinate care, rehabilitation and return to work, collaborating with doctors, employers and other social insurances. In 2025, 2,400 people were followed through this service, a significant number of professionals who benefit from a structured and personalized accompaniment during the healing and reintegration process.

A key role is played by the clinics of Bellikon and Sion, which specialise in post-traumatic rehabilitation and professional reintegration. These facilities bring together medical, rehabilitation and professional skills to address the most complex cases, offering integrated programs that go beyond simple medical care and involve both physical healing and preparation for return to work.

What is meant by successful reinstatement

The rate of professional reintegration of Suva policyholders after a serious accident stands at 88%, a figure that has remained stable over the years, confirming the effectiveness of the integrated approach. Reintegration is considered successful when the person returns to their job or finds a new one without the need for a disability pension, or with a pension of less than 20%. This 20% threshold represents the boundary between a functional return and a situation that still requires social security support.

Key points

Personal network support is as important as medical care

Fundamental to the recovery process, alongside care and professional support from Suva, is the support of one's personal network: family, friends, colleagues, and employers play a crucial role in the healing process, offering strength and confidence during difficult times. Many injured individuals report that the emotional and practical support from those around them was decisive in overcoming the most critical phases of rehabilitation and return to work.

Sandro Michel, former professional athlete and bobsledder, suffered a serious accident in 2024 and was able to count on the support of his personal network and Suva, demonstrating how the combination of institutional and personal resources can produce concrete results. "I am incredibly grateful for the support I received. Without all these people, I wouldn't be where I am today," Michel explains, illustrating how reintegration is not purely a technical process, but also an emotional and relational one.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Suva case management?
Case management is a personalised service for complex accident cases. In 2025, Suva followed 2,400 people through this service. A dedicated case manager coordinates medical care, rehabilitation and return to work, working with doctors, employers and other social insurances. The aim is to ensure a rapid and successful professional reintegration.
How long is considered normal to return to work after a serious injury?
78% of the injured return within three months, with an average of 44 days of inability to work. However, 6% face longer periods, especially in the case of injuries from falls involving the knees, shoulders, ankles or back. Suva's studies show that after six months of absence, the chances of returning are halved, which is why timely intervention is crucial.
What does "successful reinstatement" mean according to Suva?
Reintegration is considered successful when the person returns to their job or finds a new one without the need for a disability pension, or with a pension of less than 20%. The overall rate of professional reintegration of Suva policyholders is 88%, a figure that has remained stable over the years, confirming the effectiveness of the integrated approach.
What are the most difficult types of injury to recover from?
Injuries caused by falls are among the most complex, especially when they affect the knees, shoulders, ankles or back. These injuries involve longer periods of rehabilitation and often require the intervention of specialized clinics such as those of Bellikon and Sion, where medical, rehabilitation and professional skills are combined.
How does Suva support job reintegration?
Suva uses an integrated approach that combines effective medical care, customized case management for complex cases, collaboration with employers and specialized clinics. In 2025, 2,400 people benefited from case management. The support of the personal network is also fundamental: family, friends, colleagues and employers play a crucial role in the healing process.
