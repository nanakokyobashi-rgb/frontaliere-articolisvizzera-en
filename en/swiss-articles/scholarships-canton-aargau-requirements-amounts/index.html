Scholarships canton Aargau: requirements and amounts (cross-border guide)

Complete guide to scholarships in the Canton of Aargau: admission criteria, maximum amounts, application procedure and competent cantonal office.

Context

In brief

  • Scholarships in Aargau are managed by the canton with its own criteria and amounts
  • Requirements include cantonal residence and family income conditions
  • The application must be submitted by deadlines set annually by the canton
  • The competent office of the canton provides details and updated forms

Key facts

  • What: Scholarships and loans for higher education
  • Where: Canton of Aargau (northeastern Switzerland)
  • Who: Students residing in the canton
  • Basic requirement: Cantonal residence and income conditions
  • When: Variable deadlines, set each year by the canton
  • Amount: Annual maximums set by the Canton of Aargau

In the Swiss education system, the funding of post-compulsory education is the responsibility of the cantons. Like other cantons, the Canton of Aargau provides scholarships and study loans to support young people pursuing higher education paths (university, specialized schools, advanced apprenticeships). Cantonal scholarships are not universal: each canton sets its own eligibility criteria, maximum amounts, and application times. This decentralization allows cantons to adapt their support offerings to local needs and their budgetary capacities.

For the Canton of Aargau, as for other Swiss cantons, the funding of scholarships is managed by a competent cantonal office, usually called the Higher Education Office, Department of Education, or equivalent. In the Canton of Aargau, the requirements follow a general structure: family application, income verification, determination of the scholarship amount. However, the specific criteria and maximum amounts are set locally. To know the exact details — income thresholds, scholarship amounts for types of studies, deadlines — it is necessary to contact the competent cantonal office directly.

Operational details

Admission criteria: how income calculation works

The scholarship application requires an assessment of the family's economic situation. The canton applies a means test: it calculates the gross income and assets of the parents, deducts necessary expenses (rent, taxes, insurance, other living expenses), and determines whether the 'remaining margin' is sufficient to finance studies. Each canton has its own thresholds. A family net income within a certain range might qualify for a full scholarship, while a higher income might reduce the scholarship amount proportionally, up to the point where no scholarship is awarded.

In the Canton of Aargau, these thresholds are set by the competent office and updated annually. They also vary depending on: the number of children in the family undergoing education simultaneously; the type of education (university, specialized school, advanced apprenticeship); the distance between the family's residence and the place of study (if outside the canton); any potential contribution from the applicant (part-time work during studies).

Hypothetical scenario: a student applying for a scholarship

Imagine a student living in the Canton of Aargau, with a family in the middle net income bracket, and enrolling in a university degree course. When filling out the cantonal scholarship application, they will need to provide: family income documentation (tax certificates, pay slips); bank statements or asset declarations; university enrollment letter; identification of the declared income bracket. The Aargau cantonal office will verify these documents and, based on its criteria, determine whether the student is eligible and for what amount.

Key points

Procedure to apply for a scholarship in Aargau

The application for a cantonal scholarship follows a standard procedure across all Swiss cantons, with specific variations for Aargau.

Step 1: Gather documentation

Before submitting your application, prepare: proof of residence (certificate of domicile); family tax documentation (last 2-3 fiscal years); bank statements or asset declarations; letter of admission or confirmation of enrollment at the educational institution; official application form (available from the cantonal office).

Step 2: Consult Aargau-specific criteria and deadlines

Visit the website of the relevant office of the Canton of Aargau (Department of Education/Office of Higher Education, local nomenclature). Download the current regulation and application form. Check the deadlines: they vary between cantons and from year to year, so it is crucial to check the official Aargau website for the exact dates.

Step 3: Complete the application

Fill out the application form with the required details. Attach all the documentation listed above. Errors or incomplete documentation can cause the application to be rejected, so proceed with care.

Step 4: Submit by the deadline

Send the application by registered mail, deposit it at the office's registry, or use the canton's online system (if available). Always keep a copy of the receipt.

Step 5: Wait for the decision

The canton usually takes 4-8 weeks to notify the result. If approved, the scholarship will be disbursed in installments (monthly or semi-annually) according to the school calendar.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who can apply for a scholarship in the Canton of Aargau?
Generally, students residing in the Canton of Aargau who attend post-compulsory training (universities, specialized schools, advanced apprenticeships), provided that the economic situation of the family does not exceed the income thresholds set by the canton. Specific criteria vary; consult the competent office of Aargau for exact details on residence, family income and type of eligible studies.
What are the maximum scholarship amounts in Aargau?
The maximum amounts are established annually by the Canton of Aargau based on the available budget and the type of studies. There is no national ceiling: each canton decides independently. To find out the current amounts and variations by type of training, visit the official website of the competent office of the canton of Aargau.
What is the application deadline?
Deadlines vary by canton and by academic year. Check the exact dates on the official website of the Canton of Aargau at the competent office. Contacting the office directly is the most reliable way to learn about current deadlines and filing deadlines.
What is the difference between a scholarship and a student loan?
The grant is a contribution that does not have to be returned (grant title). The loan is a sum that must be repaid after studies, with favorable interest rates, grace periods before starting the installments, and sometimes the possibility of forgiveness in case of economic difficulties. Many cantons, including Aargau, offer both instruments.
What do I need to prepare to apply for a scholarship?
Prepare with: certificate of domicile (proof of residence), multi-year tax documentation, financial statements, letter of registration with the educational institution, and completed application form. Check the full list of required documentation on the official website of the canton of Aargau, as the requirements may vary from year to year.
