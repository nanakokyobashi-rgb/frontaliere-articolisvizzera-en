Permit L Basel Countryside: duration and renewal (cross-border guide)

Short-term permit L in Basel Countryside: validity up to 1 year, renewal, employer restrictions and transition to residence permit B.

Context

In brief

  • Short-term L permit valid up to 1 year in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft
  • Renewable at expiry if employment continuity is maintained
  • Tied to the specific employment contract and employer
  • Transition to B residence permit (renewable) expected after employment stability

Key facts

  • What: Short-term L permit for foreign workers in Switzerland
  • Maximum duration: Up to 1 year
  • Location: Canton of Basel-Landschaft (competent cantonal authority)
  • Federal authority: State Secretariat for Migration (SEM)
  • Renewability: Yes, according to employment continuity and documentation

The short-term L permit is the authorizing document for foreigners working in Switzerland with limited-term contracts in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. Managed by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) at the federal level and by local cantonal authorities, the L permit allows working for a specific employer for a maximum period of up to 1 year.

Maximum duration and expiry dates

The maximum duration of the short-term L permit is set at up to 1 year from the start date of the employment contract. This limit is determined by federal migration regulations and is uniform across all Swiss cantons. In the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, the SEM applies the same regulatory standards as in any other canton.

The validity of the permit starts from the date indicated in the application form and typically coincides with the end date of the employment contract declared at the time of application. If the contract ends before the maximum expiry of 1 year, the permit automatically loses validity on the date of the end of the relationship. Operational details

What changes concretely for the worker with a permit L

The permit L introduces a series of specific operational constraints compared to longer-term permits. The first concerns professional mobility: the permit is strictly linked to the employer indicated in the application. A change of employment, even within the same sector and canton, requires a new permit L application to the cantonal office and approval from the SEM.

This constraint has concrete consequences for job searching. Someone who enters Switzerland with a permit L for a specific company cannot transfer to another company without a bureaucratic procedure. The processing time for a new application involves a transition period during which the worker cannot legally perform work activities.

Taxation and pension contributions

Regardless of the type of permit (L, B, or C), anyone working in Switzerland is subject to three levels of taxation: federal (federal direct tax), cantonal, and municipal. In the canton of Basel-Landschaft, the cantonal and municipal rates are determined locally; however, the deductions and calculation mechanisms follow uniform federal rules.

Even with a permit L, pension contributions are due: AVS/AI/IPG (5.3% of the employee's salary), and if applicable, LPP (the mandatory pension fund from January 1 of the year the worker turns 17). Mandatory health insurance (LAMal) must be taken out within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland. Compare health insurance premiums in the canton of Basel-Landschaft: this is a private insurance with individual premiums per canton, not a tax or salary contribution.

Key points

How to obtain and renew the L permit: practical procedure

The procedure to obtain the short-term L permit begins with the employer. In the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, the company intending to hire a foreigner with a limited-term contract submits a job declaration to the cantonal office for business registration and migration.

Subsequently, the foreign worker (non-resident in Switzerland) applies for the L permit at the office of the Migration Office of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. If the worker is already in Switzerland for other reasons, the application must still be submitted to the same office within the timeframes set by cantonal regulations.

The required documentation typically includes:

  • Employment contract with the employer, specifying duties and duration
  • Valid and non-expired passport or identity document
  • Proof of legal accommodation within the canton
  • Official application form, available at the cantonal office
  • Work fitness certificate (required in some specific sectors)

Renewal times and critical deadlines

The renewal of the L permit follows the same procedure if the employment relationship continues or the worker finds new employment. It is crucial to submit the renewal application before the expiry of the current permit to avoid illegal interruptions of employment. The interval between the expiry of the old permit and the approval of the new one can involve working days during which the worker cannot legally work.

It is advisable to start the renewal procedures at least 4-6 weeks before the expiry date indicated on the permit itself. This interval allows the cantonal offices to process the documentation and the employer to complete all the administrative communications required by the SEM.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long is the short-term L permit in the Canton of Basel-Country?
The short-term L leave is valid for a maximum of 1 year from the start date of the employment contract indicated in the application. The deadline typically coincides with the term of the declared contract. If the contract ends before 1 year, the permit automatically loses validity on the end of employment date. The exact expiry date is clearly stated in the permit document itself.
Can I change employer before the expiry of the L permit?
No, not directly with the same permission. The L permit is closely linked to the specific employer indicated in the original application. A change of employer requires a new formal application for permission L with the cantonal authorities of Basel Campagna and approval of the SEM. This procedure involves administrative time; it is advisable to start the process at least 4-6 weeks before the expiry of the current permit.
How do I move from permit L to permit B?
The transition to permit B is possible after a period of permit L and demonstration of continuous employment. You must submit a new application to the cantonal office, providing proof of stable income, economic autonomy and tax compliance with federal and cantonal rules. Permit B is not tied to a single employer and is renewable. The path to residence permit C requires 10 years of residence in Switzerland (5 years for EU/EFTA citizens).
What fees and taxes do I have to pay with the L permit in the canton of Basel-Country?
Even with an L permit, you are subject to the three levels of Swiss taxation: federal (direct federal tax), cantonal and municipal. AVS/AI/IPG social security contributions (5.3% of salary), LPP if applicable (pension fund), and LAMal (health insurance, to be taken out within 3 months) are mandatory. Health insurance is private at individual premiums per canton, with standard deductibles (CHF 300-2500 for adults).
