G-Bewilligung Antrag 2026: Complete Guide, Costs and Renewal

Detailed procedure for permit G from 2026: documents, Migrationsamt, cost 65 CHF, validity 5 years and renewal.

Context

In short

  • G permit valid for 5 years for cross-border commuters
  • Application cost: 65 CHF
  • Request from the cantonal Migrationsamt
  • Possibility of renewal upon expiration

Key facts

  • What: Application for G permit to work in Switzerland
  • When: From 2026
  • Where: Migrationsamt of the working canton
  • Who: Cross-border workers residing abroad
  • Amount: 65 CHF for the application
  • Validity: 5 years
  • Renewal: Possible upon expiration

From 2026, those who live abroad and work in Switzerland must apply for the G-Bewilligung (G permit), following a standardized procedure. The request is forwarded to the Migrationsamt of the Canton where the work activity takes place. The administrative cost of the application is set at 65 CHF, a single amount for each application.

The G-Bewilligung is valid for 5 years from issue, thus offering cross-border workers a stable planning horizon. At the end of the 5 years, there is the possibility of renewing the same authorization, subject to verification of the required conditions. The procedure involves the presentation of specific documents, which must be presented in a complete manner and in accordance with the provisions of the competent Migrationsamt.

What the question entails

Obtaining the G-Bewilligung allows you to work legally in Switzerland while maintaining residency abroad. This status particularly concerns workers who regularly travel between their country of residence (such as Italy) and their workplace in Switzerland. Access to the procedure, costs and duration of validity are regulated and equal aspects throughout the national territory, with management entrusted to dedicated cantonal services.

For those who intend to work or continue to work in Switzerland from 2026, it is essential to know these steps and prepare the required documentation, given that failure to comply with the rules can prevent the start or continuation of work activity. For further details on tax implications and cantonal rules, consult the practical guide dedicated to work permits [tax declaration] (nav:tax-return).

Operational details

The procedure for requesting the G-Bewilligung from 2026 represents a central step for all cross-border workers who intend to maintain or start an activity in Switzerland. The fixed amount of 65 CHF for the application applies nationwide, without differences between Cantons, thus ensuring transparency and uniformity for all concerned.

Implications for workers and comparison with previous procedures

Compared to previous methods, the five-year validity of the G permit offers greater stability, allowing easier planning for workers and companies. The obligation to submit the required documents directly to the cantonal Migrationsamt centralizes and simplifies the process, reducing possible administrative discrepancies between the different regions of Switzerland. The cost of 65 CHF, being unique, makes the initial expenses related to access to the Swiss labor market predictable.

From a tax perspective, possession of the G-Bewilligung does not imply automatic changes to taxation, but allows interested parties to access the rights and duties provided for cross-border workers. The source does not specify any further news regarding direct federal tax, AVS/AHV, LPP/BVG or LAMal/KVG related to the issuance of the G-Bewilligung, but the regulatory framework remains governed at federal and cantonal level.

For those who already have a G permit expiring by 2026, the possibility of renewal allows the employment relationship to continue without interruption. A typical scenario concerns the worker who, upon expiry, regularly submits updated documents and obtains the extension of the permit without further financial burdens except for the payment of 65 CHF.

Key points

To start the G-Bewilligung application in 2026, a precise procedure must be followed. Below is a step-by-step operational guide: 1. Collection of documents required by the cantonal Migrationsamt. 2. Submission of the application to the competent office of the canton of employment. 3. Payment of the administrative fee of CHF 65. 4. Waiting for the verification and issuance of the permit, valid for 5 years. 5. On expiry, submit the renewal application with the updated documents. Failure to comply with the deadlines or incompleteness of the documentation may result in the rejection of the application or delays in its release. To better prepare, it is useful to consult the checklist provided by the Migrationsamt of the Canton where you intend to work. The procedure may only vary in operational details, but the national structure remains unchanged. Those who have to renew the permit due to expire in 2026 will have to go through the same steps, making sure to comply with the new provisions and check for any regulatory updates issued at the federal or cantonal level. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the validity of the G-Bewilligung required from 2026?
The G-Bewilligung issued from 2026 will be valid for 5 years from the time of issue. On expiry, renewal is possible by submitting updated documents to the cantonal Migrationsamt.
How much does it cost to apply for the G permit?
The cost of the application for the G permit is set at CHF 65. The amount is unique and applies nationally for each application submitted to the Migrationsamt of the working Canton.
Where is the application for the G-Bewilligung?
The application must be submitted directly to the Migrationsamt of the Canton where you intend to work in Switzerland. It is necessary to attach all the required documents according to the instructions of the cantonal office.
