Context

Driving license conversion in the Canton of Lucerne is a mandatory procedure for holders of foreign driving licenses. The requirements and procedures for conversion are established by the cantonal law of the Canton of Lucerne.

Driving license conversion is necessary for holders of foreign driving licenses who intend to reside or work in the Canton of Lucerne. This is particularly important for foreigners who intend to move to Switzerland and work or study in the Canton of Lucerne.

Here are some concrete examples:

  • A German citizen who intends to move to Lucerne for work must convert his German driving license within 6 months of the date of arrival in Switzerland.
  • An Italian citizen who intends to study in Lucerne must convert his Italian driving license within 3 months of the date of arrival in Switzerland.

To convert your driving license, you must submit the following documents:

  • The original driving license
  • A valid identity document
  • A certificate of residence or work
  • A payment of the conversion fee (CHF 50.-)

The conversion fee may vary depending on the type of driving license and the nationality of the holder. It is therefore important to check the specific requirements and procedures for your situation.

Conversion requirements

In order to convert your driving license, you must meet some requirements. Among these:

  • Have a valid driving license in another country
  • Be at least 18 years of age (or 16 for motorcycle licenses)
  • Have resided in Lucerne or another Swiss canton for at least 3 months
  • Have civil liability insurance for driving a vehicle

Declaration of acceptance

Furthermore, it is necessary to sign a declaration declaring that you accept the conditions established by the cantonal law of the Canton of Lucerne for the conversion of your driving license. This declaration must be signed in the presence of an official of the Canton of Lucerne.
