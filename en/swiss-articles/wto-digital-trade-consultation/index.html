The decision and the consultation

Switzerland will open a consultation on the plurilateral agreement on electronic commerce on 14 October 2026. The text concerns a negotiation conducted within the WTO and already completed in 2024. The opening is communicated by the Federal Department of Economy, Education and Research, indicated in the official communication.

On 28 March 2026, at the 14th (regular) session of the WTO Ministerial Conference, 67 members, including Switzerland, opted for the application of the Agreement through an Annex on provisional provisions. The initiative does not yet equate to the formal inclusion of the agreement in the WTO legal framework: for now, the provisional regime adopted by the members remains distinct from the legal milestone envisaged for the agreement.

An agreement still in the integration phase

The objective remains the formal inclusion of the agreement in Annex 4 of the WTO Agreement. According to the communication, this step is currently blocked by some WTO members. For this reason, the text describes a provisional solution already chosen by the 67 participants and, in parallel, a broader legal objective that has not yet been achieved. Switzerland appears among the members who have decided on implementation via the annex on provisional provisions, while the formalization is indicated as still blocked.

The calendar allows us to distinguish three moments. Negotiations within the WTO concluded in 2024; on 28 March 2026 the 67 members decided on implementation through the Annex on Interim Provisions; the consultation starts on 14 October 2026. They are different passages, with different functions in the text: the first concerns the end of the negotiations, the second the provisional arrangement, the third the time window communicated for the Swiss consultation. The consultation ends on 28 January 2027.

For those observing the Swiss economy, the concrete point indicated by the source is the objective of simplifying digital trade between members and, at the same time, strengthening legal certainty. The communication does not announce new operational rules, amounts or obligations for businesses and consumers. Furthermore, it does not provide a timetable after 28 January 2027.
