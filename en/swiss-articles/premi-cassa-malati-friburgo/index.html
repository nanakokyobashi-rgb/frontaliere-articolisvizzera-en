In addition, it is important to check whether you are already enrolled in compulsory health insurance in the Canton of Freiburg and whether you are already beneficiaries of the premium reduction. To do this, simply visit the insurance website and consult your situation.

For example, if you are a resident of Freiburg and are already enrolled in compulsory health insurance, you could benefit from the 10% premium reduction on medical expenses. According to insurance data, for the year 2022, the premium reduction was CHF 500 for each family of 4 people.
