Frequently Asked Questions
What taxation applies to the chauffeur border crossing in Ticino from 2024?
From 1 January 2024, under the new Agreement, taxation takes place in Switzerland (for new cross-border workers also in Italy, with a tax credit). The worker must declare income in Italy, benefiting from the tax credit to avoid double taxation, as required by current legislation.
What licenses are required to work as a driver in Switzerland?
You must be in possession of a recognised Category C or CE licence. In addition, a valid driver qualification card (CQC) is essential, attesting to the professionalism required for road transport on Swiss soil.
Which income does the €10,000 limit apply to?
Article 9’s transitional regime applies only to workers who meet the Agreement’s definition and worked in Graubünden, Ticino or Valais between 31 December 2018 and 17 July 2023; covered employment income is taxable exclusively in Switzerland. The ordinary regime applies in other cases, with Italian taxation and credit for Swiss tax where applicable.
