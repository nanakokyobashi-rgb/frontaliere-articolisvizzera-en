Tour of the Three Countries 2026: road closures in the Engadine and Val Müstair (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 June 2026

On Sunday, June 28, 2026, the Umbrail Pass will be closed and there will be slowdowns on the main roads between Val Müstair and Lower Engadine.

Context

In Brief

  • Umbrail Pass closed on June 28, 2026
  • Road restrictions and blocks in Val Müstair and Lower Engadin
  • Traffic disruptions between 8:00 AM and 12:30 PM
  • Cantonal Police urge to follow instructions

Key Facts

  • What: Road closure for the Giro dei Tre Paesi cycling race
  • When: Sunday, June 28, 2026
  • Where: Val Müstair, Lower Engadin, Umbrail Pass
  • Who: Graubünden Cantonal Police, Giro dei Tre Paesi organizers
  • Cost: not yet specified

The 33rd edition of the Giro dei Tre Paesi is scheduled for Sunday, June 28, 2026, directly affecting road traffic between Val Müstair and Lower Engadin. The cycling route, starting and ending in Nauders, Austria, will impact several strategic sections also on Swiss territory, with a particular focus on the Umbrail Pass. According to the announcement, the pass will be completely closed to vehicular traffic in both directions between Sta. Maria Val Müstair and the summit from 8:00 AM to 12:30 PM. Motorists will need to carefully plan their morning trips, as it will not be possible to transit on this important Alpine connection.

Alternative Routes and Expected Disruptions

In addition to the total closure of the Umbrail Pass, slowdowns and possible disruptions are expected on other routes affected by the event. The course includes points such as the Stelvio Pass, Sta. Maria VM, Ofen Pass, Zernez, Scuol, Martina, and Müstair. The Graubünden Cantonal Police recommend all road users to strictly follow the instructions provided by officers and traffic management personnel. The event, involving three countries – Switzerland, Italy, and Austria – entails a temporary change in road accessibility, particularly in the early hours of the day, with repercussions for those traveling between Graubünden and neighboring regions.

Implications for Travelers and Residents

The closure of the Umbrail Pass and the traffic restrictions planned for the Giro dei Tre Paesi have a tangible impact on local and cross-border mobility. Specifically, on the morning of June 28, 2026, anyone needing to cross the Alps between Switzerland, Italy, and Austria must consider the total closure of the Umbrail from 8:00 to 12:30. This is one of the rare occasions when a nationally significant alpine pass is completely blocked, affecting connections between cantons and local economic activities.

How Traffic Will Change

During the event, traffic flows will be diverted onto alternative routes, but the source does not specify which roads are recommended. Those planning work, tourist, or freight movements during this time window should anticipate possible delays. These disruptions could impact the cost of living for businesses and commuters, especially in regions with a strong tourist vocation such as Lower Engadine and Val Müstair.

The Canton Police’s recommendation to follow instructions from personnel on site is essential to ensure safety and smooth traffic flow, particularly in an area where international traffic is frequent. No special exemptions or facilitations for emergency vehicles or specific vehicles are reported; any additional information will be communicated by the competent authorities. For those who work or reside in Switzerland, the race day is a concrete example of how sporting events can affect daily logistics and mobility habits, even at a national level.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will the Umbrail Pass for the Tour of the Three Countries be closed?
The Umbrail Pass will be completely closed to traffic in both directions between Sta. Maria VM and the summit from 8:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. on Sunday, June 28, 2026, on the occasion of the 33rd edition of the Tour of the Three Countries.
What other routes will be subject to discomfort during the race?
In addition to the Umbrail Pass, slowdowns and inconveniences may occur on the Sta routes. Maria VM – Passo dell 'Ofen – Zernez – Scuol – Martina and on the Sta. Maria VM – Müstair, as indicated by the source.
What does the Cantonal Police recommend during the event?
The Cantonal Police of Graubünden invites all road users to follow the instructions of the agents and traffic management personnel, to ensure the safety and fluidity of traffic during the event.
