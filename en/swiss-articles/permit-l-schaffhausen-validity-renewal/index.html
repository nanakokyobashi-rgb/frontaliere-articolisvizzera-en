Permit L Schaffhausen: validity and extension (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 August 2026

Permit L up to 1 year in Switzerland. In the canton of Schaffhausen, it is renewable and can lead to a residence permit. Conditions, procedures and constraints.

Context

Brief Overview

  • Swiss L permit: valid for up to 1 year, renewable
  • Administered by local cantonal authorities in the Canton of Schaffhausen
  • Renewal possible; transition to B permit requires established employment
  • Restrictions tied to declared employer in the request

Key Facts

  • What: Short-term work permit for foreigners
  • When: Valid for up to 1 year, renewable upon request
  • Where: Canton of Schaffhausen, Switzerland
  • Who: Foreigners primarily from non-EU/EFTA countries
  • Duration: Maximum 12 months for a single L permit
  • Renewal: Discretionary, upon request of the employer
  • Transition: To B permit (residence 5 years) after 24 months
  • Employer: Worker bound to declared employer

The L permit is a temporary work permit valid for up to 1 year under Swiss legislation on foreign nationals. In the Canton of Schaffhausen, as in all cantons, it is managed by the local migration office according to federal criteria set by the SEM (State Secretariat for Migration).

The L permit allows a foreigner to work for a specific employer for a maximum period of 12 months. It is not renewable automatically for the same position, but can be extended if the employer requests it and the cantonal authorities approve based on local economic conditions and labor market conditions.

In the Canton of Schaffhausen, the procedures for the request and extension follow the federal framework. The Swiss employer must formally submit the request to the cantonal authorities, declaring the expected duration of the employment relationship (up to 1 year).

Operational details

Extension and Conditions in the Canton of Schaffhausen

The extension of the L permit in the Canton of Schaffhausen is subject to specific conditions that vary depending on the labor market situation and the economic needs of the canton. Unlike what is commonly believed, the extension is NOT automatic, even if the employer formally requests it. The cantonal authorities must evaluate various factors before granting the extension.

Firstly, the employer must submit the extension request before the expiration of the existing L permit. In the Canton of Schaffhausen, as in the entire Switzerland, a request submitted after the expiration date may be considered inadmissible, resulting in rejection and the need for a new authorization procedure.

Secondly, the cantonal authorities verify the local labor market situation. If the canton has registered high unemployment rates during the period, the extension may be denied, giving priority to Swiss workers and authorized residents. This is a federal criterion applied uniformly in all cantons.

Furthermore, if the foreign worker has committed administrative or penal violations during the first L permit (irregular work, labor law violations, safety infractions), the extension may be denied. Respect for the law is a fundamental condition for remaining in the canton.

The Employer's Binding in Practice

During the L permit, the foreign worker remains legally bound to the employer declared in the original request. If the employer closes the business, ceases the activity for which the permit was granted, or terminates the worker, the L permit generally becomes ineffective. The foreign worker may receive a departure notice from the canton.

Key points

To extend the L permit in the Canton of Schaffhausen, the employer must follow a formal procedure at the cantonal migration office, the competent authority for residence and work permits. Step 1: Check the expiry date The employer checks the exact expiry date of the L permit by consulting the original document or the cantonal administration portal. The extension request MUST be submitted at least 30-60 days before the expiry date. The timeframes vary slightly between cantons, but this is the general practice in Switzerland. A late request risks rejection. Step 2: Prepare the required documentation The employer collects the documentation according to the criteria of the cantonal office: - Extension request form (available at the Schaffhausen cantonal migration office) - Updated employment contract or letter of extension - Documentation of the employer's financial solvency and ability to pay the salary - If required: Certificate of integration of the foreigner in the canton (good conduct, regular payment of taxes, absence of administrative sanctions) Step 3: Submit the request The request is submitted to the cantonal migration office in writing or through the online portal (if available in the Canton of Schaffhausen). It is advisable to keep a copy of the request and the receipt of submission for tracking purposes. Step 4: Wait for the administrative decision The cantonal administration generally has 2-4 weeks to decide on the extension, although the timeframes may extend if additional verifications are required (labor market control, security checks, contacts with the employer). Step 5: Receive the decision If the extension is approved, the employer receives the new L permit (usually by regular mail or for pickup at the office). They cannot work in the Canton of Schaffhausen or remain as a resident. Continuing to work without a valid permit is a criminal offense and involves penalties for both the worker and the employer. This expiry date is categorical: there are no tacit or automatic extensions. If the L permit expires, the foreigner's situation in the canton becomes irregular. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the maximum duration of the L permit in the Canton of Schaffhausen?
The L permit is valid for up to 1 year (12 months) under Swiss law. It is not automatically renewable; the extension requires the discretionary approval of the cantonal authorities. If extended, the new permit is again valid for a maximum of 1 year. After a total of 24 months (two consecutive cycles of L leave), it is possible to request the transition to residence permit B if the conditions of occupational stability and integration are met.
Can I change employer with the L permit?
No. The L permit is linked to a specific employer indicated in the original application. Changing employers requires a new authorisation and a new permit L. The new employer must submit a request to the cantonal authorities before you can start working for them. Working without this authorization is illegal and carries criminal penalties for both you and the employer.
What happens if the L permit expires without being extended?
If the permit expires and is not extended, you generally have 30 days to leave Switzerland. You can no longer work in the Canton of Schaffhausen or stay as a resident. Staying or working without permission is a criminal offence and carries penalties for both you and the employer. Contact the cantonal migration office in Schaffhausen immediately for information on the extension before the deadline.
When do I have to submit the request for an extension of the L permit?
The application MUST be submitted before the expiry of the L permit, ideally 30-60 days before. It is not possible to submit an application after the expiry date - the permit will have already expired and the late application may be considered inadmissible. If you do not submit the request in time, you risk the obligation to leave the canton.
Is it possible to switch from permit L to permit B in Canton Schaffhausen?
Yes, it is possible after about 24 months of L leave (two consecutive periods). The employer may request the change to a B permit (residence, valid for 5 years) if the conditions of employment stability and integration of the foreigner in the canton are met. The transition to B and discretionary permits - the cantonal authorities assess each application individually on the basis of the local employment and economic situation.
