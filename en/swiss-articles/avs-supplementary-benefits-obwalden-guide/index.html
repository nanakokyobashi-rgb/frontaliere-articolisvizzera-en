The Swiss social security system rests on three pillars, and the first — old-age and survivors' insurance (AVS/AHV) — is managed at the federal level but enforced through cantonal compensation funds. In the Canton of Obvaldo, as in the rest of Switzerland, each resident is required to pay AVS contributions from 1 January following the age of 17, continuing until the reference age. Operational details

The practical implications of the first pillar affect all residents in Switzerland, regardless of their canton of domicile. Understanding how the AHV/AVS pension mechanism and supplementary benefits work means knowing what levers each insured person has to avoid reductions or, conversely, to access support when the pension is not enough.

Concrete implications at the national and cantonal level

The AVS/AI/IPG contribution is deducted from every salary paid in Switzerland, and the principle of the place of work determines to which cantonal fund the contributions are directed. This means that an employee working in the Canton of Obwalden pays their contributions to the Obwalden fund, even if they reside in another canton. The system is uniform at the federal level, but each cantonal fund has its own operational margins in managing accounts, payment timing, and assistance to insured persons. For those who change cantons during their career, the contributions paid remain valid for the purpose of calculating the pension: what matters is the sum of the years and incomes recorded at the federal level, not the canton that collected them.

From a pension standpoint, the first pillar is designed to cover the minimum subsistence level in combination with the LPP/BVG (second pillar) and the third pillar 3a. When this coverage is not achieved — for example, due to careers with modest incomes, periods of inactivity, or contribution gaps — supplementary benefits come into play. Supplementary benefits (PC in Italian, EL in German, PC in French) are not an automatic right: the Obwalden fund assesses the applicant's overall economic situation, taking into account income, assets, and recognized needs.

Key points

Checking your AVS situation is a concrete action that every resident in Switzerland can take in a few steps. The cantonal compensation office of Obwalden is the operational point of contact for those who work or reside in the canton.

Step-by-step procedure: how to find your way

1. Request the individual account (CI): the Obwalden compensation office sends the CI periodically, but it can be requested at any time. It is the document showing all registered income and credited years of contributions. 2. Check for any gaps: if years or months are missing, it is possible to ask the office how to fill the gap, for example through recovery contributions or by counting equivalent periods (education, caregiving). 3. Estimate the future pension: upon request, the office provides a projection of the AVS pension based on the registered data. For more detailed simulations, an external pension planning tool is also useful. 4. Plan the 3a pillar: if the projected AVS pension is modest, the 3a pillar allows you to accumulate supplementary capital with a tax advantage. The maximum payable amount is indexed every year. 5. Submit a PC application if needed: when the AVS pension plus other income does not cover the vital needs, it is possible to apply for supplementary benefits to the Obwalden office, attaching the required income and asset documentation.

Documents typically required

For the request of the individual account, an identity document is sufficient. For the PC application, instead: the statement from the compensation office with the assigned AVS pension, the statements from the LPP/BVG pension fund, the tax certificate, the land registry extract, bank and postal statements, and any other proof of income or assets. The cantonal office provides the updated list and forms.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who manages the AVS in the Obvaldo Canton?
The Obvaldo cantonal compensation fund manages the collection of contributions and the payment of benefits. The Federal Office of Social Insurance (UFAS/BSV) defines the federal legislation: reference age, rates and rules for calculating pensions are the same in all cantons.
When are you affiliated with Obvaldo's compensation fund?
The attribution follows the place of work for employees and self-employed with activities in the canton, while for people without gainful activity domicile is valid. Those who work in Obvaldo pay contributions to the Obvaldo cantonal fund, even if they reside in another canton.
What if there are contribution gaps?
The AVS annuity is calculated on the average of the determining incomes and on the years of contribution: any missing years or months — for stays abroad, modest incomes as an independent or years of study before the age of 21 — proportionally reduce the annuity. Recovery contributions can be paid within the timeframes provided for by federal legislation.
When do ancillary (PC) services take effect?
PCs take over when the AVS annuity, alone or with other income, does not cover the minimum living allowance recognised by law. They are a subsidiary service, mainly financed by public funds, and the Obvaldo fund assesses the income, substance and needs of the applicant after a formal application.
How do I apply for an individual AVS account?
An identity document is enough: the Obvaldo clearing house sends the individual account periodically and can issue it at any time. It is the key tool to verify years of credited contribution, recorded income and any gaps to be filled.
