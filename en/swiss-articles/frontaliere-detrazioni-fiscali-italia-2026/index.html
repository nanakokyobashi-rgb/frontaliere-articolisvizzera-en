Operational details

Italian law provides for tax deductions for frontier workers, but it is not yet specified what expenses and in how many amounts are allowed. Border workers, workers who reside in Italy but work in Switzerland, can benefit from a series of deductions that could lighten the tax burden. However, it is critical to verify tax documents to determine eligible expenses. ### Concrete examples A border worker who works in Lugano, in the Canton of Ticino, and resides in Varese, could claim deductions for travel expenses. Considering an average cost of 10 euros per day for the journey, over a year of work (about 220 days), the total expense would be 2,200 euros. However, without clear indications, it is difficult to know if this expense will be fully deductible. ### Legislation Law 232/2016, which regulates tax deductions, provides that expenses are documented and necessary for work.
