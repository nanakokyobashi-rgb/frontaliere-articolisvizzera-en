According to what was communicated in April 2020, the Canton of Ticino has decided to reopen activities related to construction. Many Italian cross-border workers are involved in this process. The queues at the border were long, with many workers having to wait to enter Swiss territory.

The Canton of Ticino has established that activities related to construction can be reopened starting from 20 April 2020. This decision was taken to ensure the economic recovery of the territory and to reduce the impact of the pandemic on the construction sector.

Many Italian cross-border workers are involved in this process, which could have a significant impact on the economy of the Canton of Ticino. According to estimates, more than 10,000 people could be involved, who will work on construction and renovation projects of buildings in the Canton of Ticino.

The queues at the border were long, with many workers having to wait to enter Swiss territory. According to official sources, more than 5,000 workers have been registered who waited to enter the Canton of Ticino in a single day.

The Canton of Ticino has established that Italian cross-border workers will have to comply with the safety rules established by the Swiss government to prevent the spread of the virus. Workers must wear a mask and respect the safety distance of at least 1 meter.

Operational details

The opening of construction-related activities has had a significant impact on queues at the border between Switzerland and Italy. Many Italian cross-border workers have been involved in this process, and the queues have been long. The Canton of Ticino has decided to reopen construction-related activities to allow workers to resume their work.

According to official data, about 10,500 Italian cross-border workers have been involved in the construction sector in the Canton of Ticino, which represents 30% of the total number of cross-border workers in Switzerland. 70% of these workers come from the provinces of Varese and Novara.

On 24 March 2023, the Council of State of the Canton of Ticino decided to reopen construction-related activities, following the request of the Canton's Immigration Coordination Committee. The decision was taken into consideration of the importance of the construction sector for the Ticino economy and the need to ensure continuity of work for cross-border workers.

According to current regulations, cross-border workers must be registered in the register of cross-border workers of the Canton of Ticino and must present a health insurance certificate. In addition, employers must provide a written employment contract and must pay social contributions.

The reopening of construction-related activities has had a positive impact on the economy of the Canton of Ticino, as cross-border workers have resumed their work and employers have been able to continue their projects. In addition, the decision helped to reduce queues at the border and ensure continuity of work for cross-border workers.
