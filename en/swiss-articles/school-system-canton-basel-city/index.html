Lower secondary education in the Canton of Basel-City is one of the stages of compulsory schooling that lasts 9 years. Students must be enrolled in school by age 5 and must successfully complete lower secondary education by age 15.

This obligation is provided for by the Federal Law on Schools of 8 June 2013, which stipulates that all Swiss citizens must attend schools for a period of 9 years. The Canton of Basel-City has adapted to this law and has determined that students must be enrolled in school by the age of 5.

Lower secondary education

Lower secondary education is the second cycle of Swiss compulsory education and takes place from the age of 11. It consists of 5 years of school and ends with the end-of-cycle exam. The aim of this cycle is to provide students with general training and preparation for higher education.

School Obligation

The compulsory schooling in the Canton of Basel-City lasts 9 years and includes lower secondary education. Students must be enrolled in school by the age of 5 and successfully complete lower secondary education by the age of 15.
