Traffic in Switzerland generated costs of about 117 billion francs. The car is the dominant means of transport. Passenger traffic is responsible for 77% of these costs, while freight transport is responsible for the remaining 23%. Between 2019 and 2023, overall expenses increased by 8%.

Practical Implications

Traffic in Switzerland is a serious problem that requires attention and concrete solutions. The increase in passenger traffic and overall expenses is a warning sign that requires a change in perspective and greater attention to road safety and the environment.

Root cause of traffic

The main cause of traffic in Switzerland is the high demand for mobility. According to data from the Federal Statistical Office (FSO), 71% of Swiss households own at least one car. This means that around 2.5 million vehicles are on the road in Switzerland. This number is set to grow, considering that the Swiss population is on the rise and that the demand for mobility is increasingly high.

According to a report by the Federal Office of Transport (FOT), traffic costs in Switzerland were estimated at around 117 billion francs in 2020. This cost includes not only direct expenses for road infrastructure, but also economic losses caused by traffic, such as time lost in queues and lack of productivity.
