The public transport subscription system in the Canton of Uri is operated by the Office of Public Transport (UBA) and is based on a network of buses and trains that connect the canton's main cities, such as Altdorf, Attinghausen and Flüelen.

Tariff Zones

The subscription system is divided into 3 main areas:

  • Zone A: includes the cities of Altdorf and Attinghausen, with a service area of about 10 km radius.
  • Zone B: includes the cities of Flüelen and Isorno, with a service area of about 15 km radius.
  • Zone C: includes the rural areas of the canton, with a service area of about 20 km radius.

Annual Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are available for residents and non-residents. Residents can purchase an annual subscription starting at CHF 1,200, while non-residents can purchase an annual subscription starting at CHF2,000.

Combination with half-price and AG

Joint subscriptions for families and groups are available for those who purchase a half-price subscription and an AG subscription. This subscription allows you to travel throughout the public transport network of the Canton of Uri.

Operational details

The public transport subscription in the Canton of Uri is available in three main areas: A, B and C. Residents can purchase an annual subscription, while non-residents can opt for a monthly subscription. The combination with half-price and AG is available for families and groups, while those under 25 can enjoy special discounts.

Service areas

Zone A covers the area of Altdorf, Alpnach and Attinghausen, while zone B includes the area of Bürglen, Flüeli-Ranft and Isenthal. Zone C, on the other hand, covers the entire Canton of Uri. It is important to note that zone boundaries may vary based on the needs of local communities.

Rates & Prices

The annual fee for residents of the Canton of Uri is CHF 420 for zone A, CHF 540 for zone B and CHF 720 for zone C. Non-residents, on the other hand, can opt for a monthly subscription of CHF 120 for zone A, CHF 150 for zone B and CHF 220 for zone C.

Combinations and discounts

The combination with half-price and AG is available for families and groups of at least four people. Individuals under 25 can enjoy special discounts, with an annual fee of CHF 210 for zone A, CHF 270 for zone B and CHF 360 for zone C.

Key points

To purchase a public transport pass in the Canton of Uri, you can visit the Canton's official website or go to one of the Canton offices. You can pay by credit card or bank account. In addition, it is advisable to check the regulations in force regarding the benefits for residents and students.

The pricing of public transport in the Canton of Uri is divided into several zones. Zone A includes the town of Altdorf, while zone B covers the surrounding areas. The basic fare for a single ride is CHF 2.50, while a day ticket costs CHF 6.50. You can also buy a monthly ticket for CHF 85.50.

For students, a special subscription is available which costs CHF 34.50 per month. You can also purchase an annual subscription for CHF 1,050.00. It is important to note that these prices are subject to change and it is always advisable to check the updated information before making a purchase.

The combination of subscriptions and tickets is also possible. For example, you can buy a season ticket for zone A and a ticket for zone B. In this case, the base fee is CHF 4.50. You can also purchase a season ticket for zone A and a monthly ticket for zone B for CHF 44.50.
