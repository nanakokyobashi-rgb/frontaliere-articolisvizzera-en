The change communicated by DEFR

On October 13, 2026, in Bern, the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research, referred to by the acronym DEFR, adapted the form used to authorize transfers of assets governed by Art. 21 of the Ordinance on measures against the Islamic Republic of Iran. The department's external communication was published on the same date.

The consequence is clearly defined. From now on, only the updated form will be accepted. The change therefore concerns the document to be submitted as part of the procedure referred to in Art. 21: the version used must be the one subsequent to the adaptation communicated by DEFR.

An effective date with no alternatives indicated

The date has a dual significance: the form was adapted on October 13, 2026, and the communication was published on October 13, 2026. The text does not defer application to a future date. The phrase from now on identifies the point from which the updated form becomes the only one accepted under the notice.

The note does not describe the individual changes made to the form. It does not report the contents of the fields, nor does it indicate other alternative models. However, it provides the essential operational reference: the updated form is available at the link indicated by the communication. For anyone who must prepare a request, that document is the starting point to retrieve.

The scope of the news remains the one indicated by the department: it concerns asset-transfer operations regulated by Art. 21 of the Ordinance on measures against the Islamic Republic of Iran. The communication does not present any other areas of application. For this reason, the form should not be confused with different administrative instruments, such as dichiarazione delle imposte, which concerns a separate topic.

The update is therefore limited in scope but operational. Anyone faced with the procedure referred to by the ordinance must start from the version identified as updated, because the notice excludes the acceptance of forms other than the updated one. The DEFR note does not add, in the text reproduced, any other conditions to the documentary rule.

Operational details

What changes in practice

For practical handling, the point to focus on is the version of the form. The text focuses on the acceptance condition: the document to be used must be the updated one. The check therefore concerns the correspondence between the procedure provided for by Art. 21 and the form made available with the notice.

Before and after October 13

The source does not describe the previous version and does not allow its fields to be compared. However, the before-and-after discernible from the text is clear: after the adaptation of October 13, 2026, acceptance is limited to the updated form. A different document does not meet the condition communicated by DEFR. This is the practical change that can be stated without adding requirements not present.

If a request is still to be prepared, the most straightforward step is to open the form indicated by the communication and use it as a reference. If a different template has already been completed, the published rule requires checking the version before proceeding. It is not possible, however, to infer from the text whether an application already submitted must be resubmitted, supplemented, or left unchanged: the notice does not regulate this scenario.

A second case concerns the scope of the request. The update is linked to asset transfers pursuant to Art. 21 of the ordinance against the Islamic Republic of Iran. The announcement does not expressly extend the new version to different transfers. Therefore, the correct reading remains anchored to the cited provision, without turning the notice into a general rule on every movement of assets.

One document, not a broader procedure

The fact that the announcement indicates an updated form does not allow adding submission channels, signatures, attachments, costs, or additional deadlines. These elements are not reported in the text provided. Anyone looking for general financial information can also consult the guide to conti bancari in Svizzera, but that in-depth resource does not replace the form referred to by the ordinance. For the DEFR news item, the applicable point remains the updated version of the form.

Key points

Steps to follow

The operational procedure that can be derived from the communication can be kept concise. There is no need to add instructions that the text does not contain; it is sufficient to comply with the version change announced by DEFR.

Request checklist

1. Identify the reference: verify that the transfer of assets in question falls within the scope of Article 21 of the Ordinance on Measures against the Islamic Republic of Iran. 2. Retrieve the document: open the form identified as updated in the external communication published on October 13, 2026. 3. Check the version: compare the template you intend to use with the updated one linked to the notice, without relying on a different copy. 4. Use the updated form: from now on, according to the text, only this version will be accepted.

These steps distinguish identifying the case from checking the document. The first prevents automatically extending the information to cases not mentioned by the source; the second leads to the form indicated by DEFR; the last two serve to comply with the acceptance condition. The communication does not describe a transmission channel, a receiving office or a signing method. It is therefore not possible to add a specific destination or formality without going beyond the available information.

If the form has not yet been chosen

The simplest rule is to use the updated version linked to the notice directly. If a different form has instead been prepared, the version must be checked before proceeding, because the notice does not recognize templates other than the updated one as acceptable. The text does not establish how to handle a case that has already been accepted and does not introduce transitional rules: these cases remain outside the published guidance.

The date to retain as a reference is October 13, 2026, when DEFR adapted the form and published the external communication. The operational point, however, does not change: for the procedure provided for by Article 21, the updated form indicated in the communication must be used.
