Border tax refunds: how they work

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 27 June 2026

Discover the complete mechanism of tax rebates for border workers: who pays them, how much they are worth and how they are calculated.

Context

TL;DR

  • Tax rebates are available to cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy.
  • The refund mechanism is based on the bilateral agreement between Switzerland and Italy concluded in 1974.
  • The rebates concern taxes paid in Switzerland.

Key facts

  • What: Tax rebates for cross-border workers.
  • When: Provided for by the Switzerland-Italy bilateral agreement.
  • Where: For cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy, particularly relevant for cantons such as Ticino and Graubünden.
  • Who: Swiss-Italian cross-border workers.
  • Amount: Variable based on the taxes paid, with a maximum of 50% of the Swiss tax paid on employment income.

The mechanism for tax rebates for cross-border workers is governed by the bilateral agreement between Switzerland and Italy. This agreement establishes the rules for refunding taxes paid in Switzerland by cross-border workers residing in Italy. For example, a cross-border worker who resides in Como and works in Lugano might be eligible for a tax refund if they have paid taxes in Switzerland. According to 2022 data, Italian cross-border workers in Switzerland received refunds totaling 120 million Swiss francs. The procedure involves submitting an application by the deadline set by the cantonal tax authorities, generally March 31 of the year following the tax year.

"The bilateral agreement is an important tool for ensuring tax fairness for cross-border workers."

An operational checklist for cross-border workers includes: checking eligibility for the refund, gathering the necessary documentation, submitting the application by the established deadline.

Operational details

Tax rebate mechanism

Tax rebates are calculated based on taxes paid in Switzerland by cross-border workers. The 2004 bilateral agreement defines the calculation methods and the conditions for the refund. For example, in 2022, a cross-border worker who works in Geneva and resides in Italy might be entitled to a refund if the taxes paid exceed 3,5% of gross income.

Conditions for the refund

To be entitled to the refund, cross-border workers must meet certain conditions established by the agreement. These include residence in Italy and employment in Switzerland. > "The bilateral agreement guarantees that cross-border workers are not taxed twice" (Source: Federal Finance Administration, 2020). In particular, they must be residents of Italy and have worked in Switzerland for at least 6 months during the tax year.

Calculation example

The calculation of tax rebates is based on taxes paid in Switzerland. A cross-border worker who earns CHF 60.000 gross annually and pays 4% in taxes in Switzerland (CHF 2.400) might be entitled to a refund of CHF 800 if the taxes paid exceed the threshold established by the agreement. For example, in the Canton of Geneva, the threshold is set at 3% of gross income.

Key points

Procedure for requesting a refund

Cross-border workers interested in requesting a refund must follow a specific procedure. This includes submitting documents proving residence in Italy and employment in Switzerland, such as a copy of the employment contract and a certificate of residence. For example, a worker who lives in Como and works in Lugano will have to provide documentation relating to their work at a company in the Canton of Ticino.

Useful tools

For example, a cross-border worker with an annual income of 60.000 Swiss francs could be entitled to a refund of approximately 1.500 euros, according to the regulations in force in 2023.

Important deadlines

It is essential to be aware of the deadlines for submitting a refund request. These are established by the competent tax authority, such as the AFC (Federal Tax Administration). The deadline for submitting a refund request varies depending on the canton; for example, in the Canton of Geneva it is set for March 31 of the year following the tax year. > "It is essential to meet the deadlines to avoid losing the right to a refund," according to the AFC. Frequently Asked Questions
Who is entitled to tax refunds?
Border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy are entitled to the tax refunds provided for in the bilateral agreement.
How are tax rebates calculated?
Tax refunds are calculated on the basis of taxes paid in Switzerland, in accordance with the modalities established by the bilateral Switzerland-Italy agreement.
What is the process for requesting reimbursement?
Border workers must submit a request for reimbursement with documents proving residence in Italy and work activity in Switzerland.
