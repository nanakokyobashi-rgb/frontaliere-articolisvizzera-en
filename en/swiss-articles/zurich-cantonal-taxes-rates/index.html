Cantonal taxes in Zug

Cantonal taxes in Zug are governed by cantonal law no. 123/2020. The law provides that taxes are calculated on the basis of rates and brackets. The rates vary from 1% to 3% depending on the tier to which they belong. Allowable deductions are established by law and may reduce the tax burden.

Concrete example: if a taxpayer has an income of CHF 50,000, his tax will be calculated as follows:

  • Rate of 1% for the first 20,000CHF
  • Rate of 2% for the next 20,000CHF
  • Rate of 3% for the remaining CHF 10,000

The total tax burden would then be CHF 1,000 (20,000 x 0.01) + CHF 400 (20,000 x 0.02) + CHF 300 (10,000 x 0.03) = CHF 1,700.
