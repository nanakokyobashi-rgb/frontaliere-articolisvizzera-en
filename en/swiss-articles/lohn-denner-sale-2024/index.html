Loihn denner: New rules for livestock trade (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 July 2026

From 1 January 2024, new rules for livestock trade in Switzerland.

Context

In a nutshell - New rules for livestock trade. - From 1 January 2024. ## Key Facts - New Frontier Agreement signed on December 23, 2020. - IT ratification: Law 83 of 13 June 2023. - Old frontier workers: exemption €7,500, transitional regime 2024–2033. - New frontier workers: deductible €10,000. The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and will be in force from 1 January 2024. The Italian ratification was approved by Law 83 of 13 June 2023. Old frontier workers will enjoy an exemption of €7,500, while new frontier workers will have a deductible of €10,000. The livestock trade is an important activity for many frontier workers. The new rules aim to simplify the process and reduce costs.

Operational details

Practical analysis The New Frontier Agreement introduces several changes to livestock trade rules. Old frontier workers will continue to enjoy the exemption of €7,500, while new frontier workers will have access to a deductible of €10,000. These changes aim to simplify the process and reduce costs for border workers. However, it is important to note that the new rules could have impacts on the livestock trade value chain. Border workers may need to adapt to the new rules and additional information may be needed to ensure full understanding of the changes. Livestock trading is a complex business that requires good planning and management. The new rules could have impacts on risk management and border guards' safety.
