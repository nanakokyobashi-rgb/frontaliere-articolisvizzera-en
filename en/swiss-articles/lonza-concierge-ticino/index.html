Responsibilities and Required Skills

The position of installation and concierge staff at Lonza in Ticino is crucial for the smooth operation of daily activities. Responsibilities include supervising and maintaining technical installations, managing access and security, welcoming visitors and suppliers, and responding to emergencies or sudden breakdowns. The ideal candidate should have solid knowledge of Swiss safety regulations, particularly those related to industrial building management and incident prevention. Good manual skills and the ability to perform minor repairs or routine maintenance interventions are also required. Familiarity with alarm systems, access control, and correspondence management is a preferred skill. The company values previous experience in similar roles, especially in production or research contexts. The ability to work independently but also effectively collaborate with other departments is essential. Discretion and professionalism are equally important, given the handling of sensitive information and interaction with diverse personnel. Knowledge of German or English, in addition to Italian, could represent a significant competitive advantage, given the international nature of the company and potential interaction with colleagues or partners from different geographical areas. Lonza's working environment is characterized by a strong focus on quality and safety, with rigorous procedures to ensure compliance with current regulations, both at the Swiss and international levels. The company offers continuous training and professional development opportunities, encouraging the development of employee skills. The position requires flexibility in working hours, including potential availability for shifts or standby, in line with the operational needs of the service.
