Costs and subsidies for daycare and childcare in Canton Glarus

The Canton of Glarus offers a kindergarten and childcare system for low-income families. According to the regulations in force, kindergarten is compulsory for children under the age of 3. Low-income families can benefit from subsidies to pay for daycare and childcare fees.

How the subsidy system works

The subsidy system for low-income families is managed by the Office of Nursery and Child Custody of the Canton of Glarus. Families wishing to access this system must submit an application and provide documents related to their income. The Office evaluates the application and decides if the family is eligible to receive the subsidy.

Key points

Nursery and childcare Canton Glarus costs

To access the subsidies, families must submit an application to the Canton of Glarus and provide documents relating to their income and financial situation. Families can also apply for financial help to cover the costs of daycare and childcare. The Canton of Glarus also offers support services to help families manage their costs and access subsidies.

The Canton of Glarus has set a ceiling of CHF 2,500.00 per month for families who require financial assistance for daycare and childcare. This amount is set by the Family Support Act of 1 January 2019 (RA 1 of 2019). In addition, families can request a contribution to cover transport and feeding costs for children.

To access the subsidies, families must submit an application to the Canton of Glarus by 30 April of the year following the year in which the child reached the age of 3. The application must be accompanied by documents demonstrating the family's income and financial situation. In particular, households must provide:

  • A certificate of residence of the Canton of Glarus
  • A child's birth certificate
  • A marriage certificate (if applicable)
  • A family bank statement
  • A family income tax return
