Cantonal taxes

Cantonal taxes in the Canton of Zug are established by the Federal Tax Act (LIF) of 1992, which provides for a progressive tax system. Rates vary depending on income category and residency. For example, residents of the Canton of Zug who have an annual income of up to CHF 20,000 are subject to a tax of 0.9%, while those with an annual income of over CHF100,000 are subject to a tax of 1.8%.

Income taxes

In the Canton of Zug, income taxes are calculated on the basis of the amount of the annual income. The maximum amount subject to taxation is CHF 20,000.00 for natural persons. For income above CHF 20,000.00, the tax is 10% on the first CHF5,000.00, 15% on the subsequent CHF 15,000.00 and 20% on the remaining sums.

For example, if a taxpayer has an annual income of CHF 80,000.00, their income tax will be CHF 18,000.00, calculated as follows:

  • CHF 5,000.00 (10% = CHF 500.00)
  • CHF 15,000.00 (15% = CHF2,250.00)
  • CHF 60,000.00 (20% = CHF12,000.00)

Real Estate Taxes

Property taxes in the Canton of Zug are calculated on the basis of the value of the property. The tax rate varies depending on the intended use of the asset and its location.

For example, a property located in the city of Zug, used as a dwelling, is subject to a tax rate of 0.8% on its value. If the value of the asset is CHF 500,000.00, the tax will be CHF4,000.00.
