Reordering incentives for companies: what changes with the new decree

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 June 2026

The Council of Ministers approved the decree streamlining business incentives, focusing them on five main instruments.

Context

In short

  • The decree rationalizes incentives for businesses.
  • Concentration on five main tools.
  • Excluding the agricultural, forestry, fisheries and aquaculture sectors.

Key Facts

  • What: Rationalization of business incentives.
  • When: Definitely approved.
  • Where: Italy.
  • Who: Ministry of Business.
  • Amount: Not specified. The Council of Ministers has approved, under final review, the legislative decree revising the system of incentives for companies, adopted in implementation of Article 3, paragraphs 1 and 2, letter a), of Law 27 October 2023, no. 160. The measure pursues a stated objective: to make the public support system more organic and effective, reducing the fragmentation of existing measures. In concrete terms, the offer of incentives managed by central administrations is rationalised and concentrated on the instruments deemed most suitable to cover specific areas of intervention, also through the repeal or modification of pre-existing disciplines. The agricultural, forestry, fisheries and aquaculture sectors are excluded from the application of the decree.

Operational details

The rationalisation of business incentives represents a significant step towards greater efficiency and coherence in the public support system. This change could have concrete implications for Swiss companies operating in Italy, offering them more targeted and accessible tools for growth and innovation.

Implications for Swiss Companies Swiss companies operating in Italy could benefit from these new tools, which have been designed to be more effective and less fragmented than in the past. Comparison with the Previous Situation Before this reform, the incentive system was characterized by a fragmentation that made it difficult for companies to access the necessary financing. The new focus on five main tools aims to simplify this process, making it easier for businesses to understand what options are available and how to access them.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the five main instruments of the new decree?
The five main instruments are the Sustainable Growth Fund, the Guarantee Fund for Small and Medium Enterprises, the Venture Capital Support Fund, the Instrumental Goods – Nuova Sabatini measure and the incentives for the aerospace sector.
Which sectors are excluded from the new decree?
The agricultural, forestry, fisheries and aquaculture sectors are excluded from the application of the new decree.
How can Swiss companies benefit from these new incentives?
Swiss companies can benefit from these new incentives by accessing the funding and guarantees offered by the five main instruments, which have been designed to be more effective and less fragmented than in the past.
