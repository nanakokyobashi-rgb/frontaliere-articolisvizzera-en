The Antara Tantra Festival, formerly known as Tantra Festival Switzerland, is taking place these days in the canton of Bern, precisely in Lenk, with dates between 19 and 23 August 2026. This year's edition comes after allegations of sexual abuse made in 2023 against one of the organizers, guru Eugene Hedlund, accused of having taken sexual advantage of various participants by exploiting his position of authority. Faced with this critical context, the event decided to change course, adopting a renewed name and an approach focused on new rules of conduct and the safety of participants.

The new program and activities

The official program of the event includes several activities and workshops, including ecstatic dance, sound healing and orgasmic yoga.

Operational details

# New safety measures and manuals

To prevent any kind of drift and mentor participants, the organization introduced specific operating procedures before and during the event in the canton of Bern. Each participant has previously received a seven-page manual dedicated to consent, a document that has been presented again on the spot and posted in the common areas of the structure. The text clearly specifies that consent must be given without any pressure, force, persuasion or manipulation, and reiterates that a yes can become a no at any time.

Webinars and workshops on limits

In addition to paper-based material, preparatory webinars were organized, including meetings focused on managing and understanding personal limits. Organizer Ananda Inglin emphasizes that each participant enjoys complete freedom to not take part in a specific activity or to leave the group at any time. Among the reported introductory workshops, participants follow sessions in which they learn to listen to their own body and observe their own physical reactions, for example through exercises in which two people slowly approach each other by monitoring their own reactions.

The perplexities of the Intercantonal Belief Center

Despite the introduction of strict protocols and a QR code reporting system for inappropriate behaviour, the approach continues to raise questions.

Key points

Management of reports and future prospects

To ensure an immediate response to any critical issues raised during the festival in Lenk, the event provided a digital alert system. In the event of inappropriate behaviour or ambiguous situations, participants have the right to submit a report in real time simply by scanning a QR code made available on the premises. This measure is combined with information sessions and preparation webinars to strengthen transparency and protection of the individual within the seminar context.

The role of the former organizer

The Zurich newspaper that followed the event also interviewed former organiser Eugene Hedlund, who became the focus of controversy in 2023. Hedlund himself stated that he had contributed to the definition of the current security measures adopted for the current edition of the Antara Tantra Festival, trying to respond to the problems that emerged in previous editions.
