Operational details

The short-term L permit is an important document for border crossers and non-residents who need to stay in the Canton of Lucerne for a short period. This permit is necessary for those who wish to spend time in the canton, but do not intend to settle permanently. It is important to submit the extension request in good time to avoid the expiry of the permit.

The application for a short-term L permit can be submitted to the Consulate of Lucerne or to the Immigration Office of the Canton of Lucerne. You must provide personal documentation, such as your ID card or passport, as well as a copy of your rental agreement or hospitality guarantee. It is also necessary to provide a statement of intent, which specifies the duration of the stay and the objective of the stay in the canton.

The validity of the short-term L permit varies depending on the type of permit requested. The short-term residence permit can be granted for a maximum of 30 days, while the medium-term residence permit can be granted for a maximum of 90 days. It is important to note that the short-term L permit does not allow you to work in the canton.

Concrete example: an Italian citizen who wants to spend time in Lucerne to visit the city and enjoy its tourist charm can apply for a short-term L permit for a maximum of 30 days.

Key points

Short-term permit L in the Canton of Lucerne: validity and extension

If you wish to obtain the short-term L permit, you must submit an application at least 30 days before the expiry date. This notice period is provided for by the Federal Law on the Right of Residence of Foreigners (LFDSt), art. 11, paragraph 1, which states that the application for a residence permit must be submitted at least 30 days before the date of entry into Switzerland.

If you reside in the Canton of Lucerne for at least 3 months, you can convert the permit into a residence permit (B). The Canton of Lucerne offers an assistance service for foreigners who wish to obtain a residence permit.

Short Term L Permit Application: What is needed?

To submit a short-term L permit application, you must provide the following documents:

  • Valid passport or ID
  • Travel document (plane ticket, etc.)
  • Proof of payment of entry visa (if necessary)
  • Declaration of sufficient financial resources for the stay in Switzerland
  • Biological medical insurance valid for Switzerland
  • Declaration of intention to leave Switzerland within a certain period of time
