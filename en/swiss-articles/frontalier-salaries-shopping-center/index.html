A shopping centre hires staff offering high salaries, especially for border workers. Ticinese seem less likely to work in the industry.

Context

A recent job announcement at the "Balexert" mall in Mendrisio sparked interest in the high salaries offered. According to reports, salaries can reach up to CHF 5,000 per month for specific roles such as department managers or sales specialists.
