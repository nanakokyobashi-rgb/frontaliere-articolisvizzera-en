Operational details

Finali's speech does not introduce figures or specific measures: it is a contribution of opinion that sets a framework of priorities. However, the keywords used — organic, time, inclusion, working conditions — touch levers that have a direct reflection on the organization of public school in Ticino, and by extension on the lives of families that entrust their children to the cantonal school system.

What does "time" mean in the Ticino school

The reference to "time" as a resource to be protected is the analytical heart of the text. Finali lists a series of activities that have multiplied around teaching: meetings, documentation, coordination, interviews, training, administrative tasks. The point is not to judge these activities — some are mandatory, others required by school regulations — but to recognize that they subtract hours from classroom activity. For a teacher, this means less preparation for lessons, less availability for individual difficulties, less room for the educational relationship. For families, it means more pressured teachers and, potentially, a less personalised training offer.

Key points

Finali's article is not an operational guide: it is an opinion contribution that identifies priorities. For this reason, the concrete actions that follow from it must be built starting from the parties mentioned in the text.

What the union can do

VPOD is presented as the body that collectively organises school staff. For a teacher who shares the concerns expressed in the article, the logical step is to approach the union, take part in assemblies, and contribute to defining the bargaining platforms. The union is indicated as the privileged channel for negotiation, dialogue with the authorities and, ultimately, mobilisation.

What teachers can do

Finali explicitly mentions the risk that the time devoted to the relationship with students may be compressed. The union response proposed is to bring 'time' back to the centre of the debate. In practice, this translates into the demand to reduce the weight of ancillary activities — meetings, documentation, administrative tasks — in order to return hours to teaching and classroom work. It is a demand that directly concerns the organisation of the teaching profession.

What families and citizens can do

The article points out that the quality of the school offer should not depend on the municipality. For parents, the very issue is this: the educational offer can vary at a local level. The text does not indicate operational tools, but the principle that emerges is that oversight of the municipal school offer and dialogue with the local school authorities are levers available to citizens.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who relaunched the appeal on the conditions of teachers in Ticino?
Matteo Finali, co-chairman of the VPOD Ticino teachers' committee and teacher of Music Education at the Municipal Schools of Massagno and Bellinzona (Piazza Grande area), has published a contribution of opinion at the opening of the new school year in which he asks for adequate staff, coordination times and services provided for the Ticino school staff.
What are the union priorities of the VPOD for the Ticino school?
The VPOD Ticino indicates as appropriate organic priorities, coordination times, sufficiently equipped services, training, collaboration and reconciliation between work and private life. The key word evoked is "time", to be brought back to the centre of the debate as a resource for teaching and educational relations.
Why can't school inclusion rest only on the goodwill of teachers?
According to Finali, inclusion "cannot be entrusted solely to the goodwill of teachers": we need adequate staff, dedicated coordination times and sufficiently equipped support services. Without these elements, the burden falls on the teaching staff and, indirectly, on the students.
What is the reported risk for municipal schools?
The union representative warns that the quality of the school offer should not depend excessively on the Municipality in which you work or live. The municipal school is described as a place of belonging and community, but with the same path, a child's experience can vary depending on local resources.
What is the role of the VPOD according to the article?
The VPOD is presented as the union that collectively organizes the staff of the school in Ticino. The aforementioned levers are bargaining, confrontation with the authorities, defence of rights and, when necessary, mobilisation. Finali does not announce strikes or specific disputes, but clarifies that the union is the privileged channel to assert the requests of the teaching staff.
