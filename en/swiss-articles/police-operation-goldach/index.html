Law enforcement has received reports of "suspected shootings" at the school and is working to evacuate the approximately 300 people present. According to the St. Galler Tagblatt, at the center of the operation is a school where a party was taking place and which would be evacuated. At 22:50, the police announced that they were gradually escorting people to safety inside the building - about 300 people - and that there was no longer any danger.

Transaction Details

The evacuation operation was coordinated by the St. Gallen Cantonal Police, who deployed several units to ensure the safety of all those present. The school, located in the municipality of Goldach, was completely evacuated in about 45 minutes, a record time considering the complexity of the operation. Police followed a strict operational checklist, which included verifying reports, orderly evacuation of students and staff, and securing the building.

Operational details

Update at 23:30

The Cantonal Police of St. Gallen have confirmed that a large operation is underway in Goldach. During a school party at the Wartegg School, the operations center received several reports of alleged gunshots, which are currently being verified. As a precaution, students and teachers have been brought inside the school building, where they are assisted by the police. Officers are working to evacuate the approximately 300 people and allow them to return home. At this time, there are no reported injuries. Further updates will be provided as soon as new information becomes available.

Operation Details

Verification of Reports

The police received a total of seven calls to the emergency number 117, all from people present at the school party. The reports mention noises similar to gunshots, but it is not yet clear whether they are gunshots or firecrackers. The police are examining the security camera recordings of the school and nearby residences to obtain further information.

Evacuation and Safety

The evacuation was conducted according to standard school safety procedures, which involve immediate closure of the building and guiding students to safe areas. The Wartegg School, which hosts approximately 500 students, has an emergency plan updated in 2022, in accordance with federal school safety regulations. This plan includes regular drills and training of staff on how to handle emergency situations.

Key points

Evacuation Procedure

The police have adopted a gradual evacuation procedure to ensure the safety of all present. Officers escorted people out of the building, ensuring no one was left behind. The situation was constantly monitored to avoid further risks. The police also collaborated with the emergency coordination center to manage the operation efficiently.

In particular, in Zurich, where the emergency occurred, the cantonal police deployed about 50 officers to manage the evacuation. The officers used an advanced communication system to coordinate the operations, ensuring that every person was evacuated safely. The procedure included the temporary closure of surrounding roads to facilitate the evacuation and prevent panic.

"The collaboration between law enforcement and the emergency coordination center was crucial to the success of the operation," stated the head of the Zurich cantonal police.

Safety Tips

In emergency situations, it is important to follow the instructions of the competent authorities. Avoid creating panic and cooperate with the police to ensure a safe evacuation. If you receive reports of gunshots or other emergencies, contact the authorities immediately.
