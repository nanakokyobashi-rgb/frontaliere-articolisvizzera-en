This annual event marks the return to the school routine for over 600,000 Swiss students.

The resumption of lessons is accompanied by safety measures to prevent the spread of the SARS-CoV-2 virus. In this sense, the cantons have established a common protocol to manage any cases of infection. For example, in the Canton of Zurich, schools must have an emergency plan in the event of a student or teacher testing positive. 📊

In the meantime, parents are asked to provide information on the health status of their children before the start of lessons. This obligation is provided for by the federal law of 30 September 2021, which requires the communication of any cases of infection to school managers within 24 hours of the start of lessons. ⚠️

In the canton of Vaud, parents are also asked to provide information on the vaccination of their children. According to the cantonal law of 18 February 2022, children must be vaccinated against infectious diseases before starting school.

In the canton of Geneva, schools must have an emergency plan to manage any cases of infection. This plan must be shared with parents and must be activated within 24 hours of the start of lessons.

In general, parents need to be aware of the safety measures in place in schools and that they need to provide information on the health status of their children.
