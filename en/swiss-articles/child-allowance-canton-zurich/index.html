The Canton of Zurich offers a family allowance to parents who have minor children, with the aim of supporting families and fostering their economic stability. The family allowance is an important benefit for families with children, which can help them cover education, living and health expenses.

Types of Family Allowance

The Canton of Zurich offers two types of family allowance:

  • Basic family allowance: this is a fixed amount that is granted to all families with children, regardless of the age and income of the parents.
  • Supplementary family allowance: it is an additional amount that is granted to families with low or medium income, according to their economic needs.

Amounts and demand

The amount of the basic family allowance is CHF 2'200 per year for each minor child, up to a maximum of CHF 8'800 per family. The amount of the supplementary family allowance varies according to the income of the parents and the economic needs of the family.

To apply for the family allowance, parents must submit an application to the Canton of Zurich by 31 December of the previous year. The application must be accompanied by identity documents and a tax return.

Key points

Practical Implications

The family allowance is an important social benefit for families with children in the Canton of Zurich. The application procedure is straightforward and can be submitted online or at the premises of the relevant clearing house. It is important to check the legal conditions and the exact rates for the family allowance.

Eligibility Conditions

To be eligible for the family allowance, parents must reside in the Canton of Zurich and have a total family income of less than a certain amount, currently set at CHF 2,500 per month for a family with two children. In addition, parents must have an employment contract or a pension and not have an independent income of more than CHF 1,500 per month.

Family Allowance Amounts

The amount of the family allowance varies according to the number of children and family income. For a family with two children, the maximum amount of the family allowance is CHF 250 per month. For a family with three children, the maximum amount is CHF 375 per month.

Family allowance application

The application for a family allowance can be submitted online or at the premises of the competent compensation fund. Identity and income documents must be provided, as well as a tax return. The application must be submitted by 31 December of the year following that in which the benefit was received.
