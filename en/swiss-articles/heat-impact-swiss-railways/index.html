Context

In short - Heat stroke causes track deformations and air conditioner failures - SBB, BLS and sob guarantee a stable service - Slight delays and immediate interventions to solve problems ## Key facts - What: Track deformations and air conditioner failures - When: During the summer heat stroke - Where: All over Switzerland - Who: SBB, BLS, sob - Amount: Not specified The great heat of these days also has repercussions on rail traffic. When the air conditioning is running at full capacity, faults occur more often. The tracks may also deform or contact line problems may occur. However, Swiss railway companies have so far managed to guarantee a stable service even in these extreme conditions. In recent days, SBB has encountered some disruptions to railway infrastructure and rolling stock caused by the heat, such as overheating of traction engines or failures of air conditioning systems. According to what the company announced today, the affected trains were immediately replaced, so that the defective equipment could be changed. With the heat, the steel of the rails also expands and there is a risk of deformations of the tracks and exchanges. On Monday afternoon at the central station in Zurich it was necessary to arrange an exchange again precisely for this reason, the SBB announced. Some trains ran with slight delays. ###

Operational details

Impact of heat on trains Extreme heat not only affects passenger comfort, but also the safety and efficiency of rail traffic. Swiss railway companies have implemented several measures to mitigate these effects. ### Monitoring and maintenance SBB, BLS and SOB have adopted an advanced monitoring system to detect any heat-related issues early. This allows you to intervene quickly and minimise disruption. For example, during the 2022 heat wave, SBB used thermal sensors along the Gotthard line to monitor track temperature. When the temperature exceeded 50°C, cooling procedures were activated to avoid deformation. ### Emergency procedures In the event of air conditioner failures or track deformations, railway companies have well-defined emergency procedures. The affected trains are immediately replaced and defective appliances are repaired or replaced. During the summer of 2021, BLS had to replace 15 trains due to air conditioning failures, with an estimated cost of CHF 500,000 for repairs. ### Comparison with other nations Other European countries, such as France, are facing similar problems due to heatwave. Key points

What to do in case of delays

If you find yourself facing delays or disruptions due to heat, here are some guidelines to follow:

Check for updates

Use the apps of the railway companies or their websites to check real-time updates on delays and cancellations. For example, the Swiss Federal Railways (SBB) app offers push notifications for immediate updates.

Plan ahead

If you know there will be a heatwave, plan your trips with extra time to account for potential delays. During the 2022 heatwave, average delays at major stations like Zurich and Geneva were around 30 minutes. Planning an extra hour can make a difference.

Use alternative means

In case of significant delays, consider using alternative means such as buses or taxis to reach your destination. For example, during the 2021 heatwave, substitute bus services between Bern and Lucerne reduced waiting times by up to 50%.

Contact customer service

If you need assistance, do not hesitate to contact the customer service of the railway companies for information and support. The SBB helpline is 0800 112 112, available 24/7.

What causes train delays during the dogfight?
Delays in trains during the heat stroke are mainly caused by deformation of the tracks and failures of the air conditioners. Extreme heat can overheat traction motors and cause contact line problems.
How do rail companies handle heat disruption?
Swiss railway companies have implemented an advanced monitoring system to detect any heat-related problems in a timely manner. In the event of failure, the affected trains are immediately replaced and defective appliances are repaired or replaced.
What measures can passengers take to deal with delays caused by the heat?
