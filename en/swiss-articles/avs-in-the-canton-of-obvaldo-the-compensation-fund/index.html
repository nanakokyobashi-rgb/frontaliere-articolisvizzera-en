Context

The cantonal compensation fund is a crucial aspect of the Swiss social security system, and in the Canton of Obvaldo it is managed with care and precision to ensure a living minimum for citizens.

The complementary benefits of the AVS (Old Age Insurance and Assistance) are essential to ensure that all Swiss citizens have a decent standard of living. These benefits include unemployment benefits, care benefits and allowances for workers retiring from the labour market.

In the Canton of Obvaldo, the cantonal compensation fund is managed by the Department of Finance and Social Security. This department is responsible for administering the funds of the compensation fund, ensuring that benefits are delivered on time and providing assistance to citizens who request it.

According to the federal law of 20 March 2003, the minimum amounts of care benefits are set at CHF 1,350 per month for a single person and CHF 2,100 per month for a couple. These amounts are updated annually based on inflation.

The Obvaldo Cantonal Compensation Fund also manages a solidarity fund to help citizens who are in financial difficulties. This fund is funded with contributions from employers and workers, and the amounts are set based on workers' income.
