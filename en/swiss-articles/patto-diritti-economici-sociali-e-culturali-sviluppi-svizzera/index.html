The report describes measures taken in the areas of anti-discrimination, gender equality and labour market policy. Switzerland has ratified several international conventions, especially in the areas of worker protection, intellectual property and artificial intelligence. For example, Switzerland ratified the International Convention on Labour Licences (CIL) on 15 February 2018, which sets standards for the protection of migrant workers.

The Federal Council adopted the fifth Swiss report on the implementation of Pact I, which presents a detailed assessment of the measures taken by Switzerland to implement the Pact. The report highlights that Switzerland has made significant progress in protecting economic, social and cultural rights, but that there are still challenges to overcome.

In 2019, Switzerland submitted a report on the implementation of Pact I, which highlighted the progress made and areas for improvement. The report also proposed a series of recommendations for government and local authorities to accelerate the implementation of the Pact.

One of the concrete examples of the implementation of Pact I is the promotion of basic education and vocational education.
