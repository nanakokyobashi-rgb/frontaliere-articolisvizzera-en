Pension and finance

From January, AVS/AI pensions will grow by approximately 1.6%, in line with the trend in prices and wages. At the same time, the maximum amount payable into the third pillar will also increase. To follow the topic of annuities, guida alle rendite AVS is available.

The Government wants to renew the commitment credit which would allow Switzerland to provide up to 10 billion in the event of a crisis. The indicated objective is to allow the country to continue contributing to the stabilization of the global financial system.

Cybersecurity and relations with the EU

The Federal Council also intends to strengthen cybersecurity with a law dedicated to software and hardware products, data and digital infrastructures. A proposal will be drawn up by June 2027: the source then sets a deadline for its preparation.

Switzerland and the EU have negotiated a new package of agreements to stabilize and develop bilateral relations. The Federal Council forwarded it to Parliament on 13 March 2026.

The democratic calendar finally indicates November 29, 2026. On that date, Swiss citizens will express their opinion on the additional financing of the AVS, on the fireworks initiative, on the taxation of spouses and on the law on war material.
