The framework to read before the contract

In Switzerland, there is no federal minimum wage: some Cantons have their own. For anyone seeking an apprenticeship in the Canton of Uri, this is the first distinction to bear in mind: remuneration is not compared with a single national figure, but with what is provided for in the offer and the applicable rules.

The search for an apprenticeship position is the first concrete step. annunci di lavoro helps gather opportunities, but the assessment continues with the apprenticeship contract. Before accepting, it is worth comparing the duties, working hours, vacation and remuneration stated in the offer. These items clarify what one is accepting and prevent confusing a gross amount with the money actually available.

Maximum working hours are 45 hours per week in industry, offices and retail; in other sectors the limit is 50 hours. The minimum vacation entitlement is four weeks, rising to five for those under 20. These are useful reference points for understanding how work is organized, but they do not replace the specific terms of the contract.

The role of training

The vocational baccalaureate must be considered together with the chosen pathway, not just the pay. In the Canton of Uri, anyone considering this option must separate questions about the apprenticeship position from those concerning the school pathway and verify the conditions with the official cantonal source. A predefined national deadline should not be applied: for requirements and the timetable, the relevant official information is what counts.

The same criterion applies when comparing multiple offers. The presence of a cantonal reference does not automatically mean that every job has the same conditions as an apprenticeship: the choice must be based on the written content and the competent canton. SECO guidance can help interpret the national framework governing work; for details governed by cantonal rules, the official source of the Canton remains the reference.

For the reader, the point is to keep three levels separate: the opportunity to seek, the contract to check and the educational pathway to verify. Only after this separation does the comparison between offers become clear, even when the remuneration seems similar.

Operational details

From gross salary to actual budget

An apprentice's salary should be considered together with deductions, without turning general rates into standard net pay. The contribution percentages describe the deductions; the comparison should start from the gross amount stated in the contract and the items that actually apply.

Deductions to separate

ItemStated rule
AVS/AI/IPG5,3% paid by the employee; 10,6% total with the employer
AD/AC1,1% up to the annual maximum
LAINF/LAA0,7–1,5% depending on the sector
LPP/BVG7%, 10%, 15% or 18% depending on the age bracket

AVS/AHV, together with AI and IPG, accounts for 5,3% paid by the employee; the total with the employer is 10,6%. AD/AC is 1,1% up to the annual maximum. For LAINF/LAA, the stated percentage is between 0,7% and 1,5%, depending on the sector. LPP/BVG is calculated on the coordinated salary and provides for 7% between 25 and 34 years, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54 and 18% from age 55 until the reference age. The calculation does not consist of automatically adding all the percentages: LPP concerns the coordinated salary and the indicated age brackets.

The busta paga svizzera must then be distinguished from the LAMal premium. For residents, health insurance is mandatory and must be taken out within three months of arrival. The premium is per capita: it is not a tax and it is not a salary contribution. It varies by canton and region, while the reduction in premiums is a cantonal subsidy. In calculating the cost of living, this item should therefore be kept separate from net salary. For more details on healthcare, the confronto LAMal follows this distinction.

Taxes and offices

Taxation also follows a different logic: in Switzerland there are three levels, direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each Canton has its own law and multiplier; the municipality applies a multiplier to the cantonal tax. The AFC/ESTV handles IFD and IVA, while the cantonal administrations handle cantonal and municipal taxes. UFAS/BSV deals with social security and does not set taxes, while UST/BFS produces statistics and not tax rates. Separating these responsibilities avoids confusing a salary deduction, a LAMal premium and a tax.

Key points

From research to decision

The choice in the Canton of Uri can be managed through a short sequence, without confusing what belongs to the contract with what depends on the canton or the personal situation.

Four checks before signing

1. Finding an offer. Start with job advertisements and select positions that clearly indicate an apprenticeship. For each offer, keep the information on activities, working hours, vacation, pay and educational path. This makes different proposals comparable without adding unwritten conditions.

2. Reading the contract. Check that pay is clearly stated as a gross amount and ask how the salary items are presented. Compare the working hours with the limit of 45 hours in industry, offices and retail, or 50 in other sectors. If you are under 20, also check the minimum of five weeks of vacation; for everyone else, the stated minimum is four.

3. Calculating the budget. Apply only the relevant percentages: AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC, LAINF/LAA and, if relevant to coordinated salary and age, LPP/BVG. Keep LAMal separate. A resident must take out insurance within three months of arrival; premiums and premium reductions depend on the canton or region and the cantonal subsidy.

4. Verifying the training. For the vocational baccalaureate, consult the official source of the Canton of Uri before making commitments. The verification must clarify requirements, schedule and connection with the chosen path, without automatically importing an unspecified national procedure.

After the four checks, keep a simple sheet with three blocks: offer content, contractual conditions and separate budget costs. The sheet helps avoid confusing the minimum wage, which is not federal, with a cantonal rule, or LAMal with a tax.

Taxes in fact follow three levels: federal, cantonal and municipal. If evaluating the apprenticeship also leads to tax questions, the competent office must be sought according to the subject: AFC/ESTV for direct federal tax and VAT, and the cantonal administration for cantonal and municipal taxes. For conditions specific to Uri, deadlines and amounts must be checked against the official source of the Canton.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is there a federal minimum wage for an apprentice in the Canton of Uri?
No. The source establishes that there is no federal minimum wage and that some Cantons have their own. For a position in the Canton of Uri, remuneration must therefore be read in the contract and compared with the applicable cantonal rules. It is not correct to use a single national amount; the comparison must distinguish the gross figure from deductions from the salary.
Which contributions affect the apprentice's salary?
The percentages indicated for the salary are 5.3% paid by the employee for AVS/AI/IPG, with 10.6% in total together with the employer; 1.1% for AD/AC up to the annual ceiling; 0.7–1.5% for LAINF/LAA depending on the sector. LPP/BVG uses the coordinated salary: 7% between 25 and 34 years, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54, and 18% from age 55 up to the reference age.
Is the LAMal premium a payroll deduction?
No. For residents, LAMal is mandatory and must be taken out within three months of arrival. The premium is per capita, not a tax and not a salary contribution. The amount varies according to canton and region; the premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy. In an apprentice's budget, the premium should therefore be kept separate from net remuneration.
What hours and holidays should I check in the contract?
The maximum working time is 45 hours per week in industry, offices and retail, or 50 hours in other sectors. The minimum vacation entitlement is four weeks and becomes five for anyone under 20. The contract must nevertheless be checked to verify how the conditions applicable to the individual apprenticeship are set out.
How is professional maturity verified in Uri?
For the vocational baccalaureate, the salary data alone is not enough: in the Canton of Uri, the requirements, timetable and connection with the pathway must be verified using the official cantonal source. The choice must keep the educational pathway, contract and remuneration separate, so as not to automatically apply an irrelevant national procedure.
