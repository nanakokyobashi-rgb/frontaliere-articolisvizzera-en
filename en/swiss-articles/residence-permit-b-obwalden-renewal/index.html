Residence permit B in Obvaldo: requirements and renewal (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 30 August 2026

Permit B allows you to live and work in Switzerland on a renewable basis. Learn about the requirements, documents, and renewal procedure in the Obvaldo Canton.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Permit B: renewable document to live and work in Switzerland
  • Duration: from 1 to 5 years, with the possibility of renewal
  • Release: at the cantonal migration office
  • Requirements: employment contract, health insurance, good conduct

Key facts

  • What: Residence permit (category B) managed by SEM
  • When: Renewable before expiration (30-60 days in advance)
  • Where: Cantone Obvaldo
  • Who: Cantonal Office of Migration
  • Validity: Varies between 1-5 years, automatically renewable

The B residence permit is one of the main Swiss residence documents for foreigners who intend to reside and work in the federal territory. Unlike the C (domicile) permit, the B permit is renewable and is intended for those who have not yet accumulated enough years of residence to access a definitive status. In Canton Obvaldo, as in all Swiss cantons, the cantonal migration office is the authority responsible for the issuance, renewal and administrative management of the B permit.

Key features of permit B

Permit B represents an intermediate category in the Swiss system of residence permits managed by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM). This document allows the foreigner to live, work and settle in the territory of Switzerland for the duration of the permit. Its renewable nature distinguishes it from the L permit (of short duration, up to 1 year) and makes it a less definitive title than the C permit. The duration of the B permit is not standardized at the federal level, but varies based on cantonal decisions and individual circumstances. Usually, the first renewal or the initial issuance covers a period of 1 to 5 years. The permit is automatically renewable if the underlying conditions (employment, means of subsistence) remain met and there are no grounds for refusal.

Operational details

# Residence Permit Requirements B

The B permit in the Obvaldo Canton follows federal criteria established by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM), although the cantonal authority retains discretion in evaluating each application. The main requirements are:

1. Legitimate employment contract : The foreigner must have an offer of employment confirmed by a Swiss employer. The contract must comply with Swiss working standards (maximum weekly duration, minimum holidays, etc.) and be compatible with the person's level of qualification. 2. Certificate of Good Criminal Conduct: Issued by the country of origin, this document attests to the absence of serious criminal convictions. It must be recent (usually issued in the last 3-12 months).

3. Livelihood check: The salary agreed in the contract must be sufficient to cover the cost of living in the destination canton. There is no federal minimum wage, but each canton may have its own thresholds for wage acceptability.

4. Compulsory health insurance (LAMal/KVG): Within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland, the foreigner must take out a assicurazione malattia with an authorized private provider. This insurance is required by law and premiums vary depending on the canton, region and deductible level chosen. Adult deductibles start from CHF 300 up to CHF 2,500 per year.

Key points

B Permit Renewal Procedure

The renewal of the B permit is generally a simpler procedure compared to the initial application, as the cantonal office already has the applicant's personal, employment, and administrative data on file. However, it is essential to start the process well in advance to avoid any interruption in validity.

Step 1 — Check the expiration date: Verify the expiration date printed on your B permit. It is usually clearly highlighted.

Step 2 — Contact the cantonal office: Reach out to the cantonal migration office of the Canton of Obwalden at least 30-60 days before the expiration date. You can contact them by email, phone, or visit in person during their office hours.

Step 3 — Fill in the renewal form: The cantonal office will provide the specific renewal form. This form is generally also available online on the cantonal administration website. It is important to complete it fully and accurately.

Step 4 — Submit the documentation: Even though the documentation required for renewal is usually less burdensome than for the initial application, you will still need to submit a valid passport, an updated certificate of good conduct (if required), proof of valid health insurance (LAMal/KVG), and proof of your current employment situation or means of subsistence.

Step 5 — Await approval: The cantonal office will process the application. Processing times vary, but renewal is usually completed within 4-8 weeks. In case of delays, you can contact the office to check the status of your file.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does the residence permit B last?
The B permit is renewable and its duration depends on the specific circumstances and cantonal needs. It is generally valid from 1 to 5 years, but is always renewable. Unlike the C (domicile) permit, the B permit is not a definitive title and must be renewed periodically before it expires.
What are the main requirements to obtain a B permit?
The main requirements include a legitimate employment contract, certificate of good criminal conduct, proven means of subsistence, and compulsory sickness insurance (LAMal/KVG) taken out within 3 months of arrival. For EU/EFTA nationals, the requirements may be less stringent. The exact details depend on the individual situation and the decision of the cantonal authority.
How do I renew my B permit?
Please contact the cantonal migration office at least 30-60 days before the permit expires. Fill out the renewal form and submit the required documents: valid passport, updated certificate of good conduct (if required), and proof of valid health insurance. Renewal is usually completed within 4-8 weeks.
What is the difference between permit B and permit C?
The B permit (residence) is renewable and of average duration (1-5 years); the C permit (domicile) is a more stable title, generally obtainable after 10 years of residence (5 for EU/EFTA) and does not require regular renewals. The transition from B to C takes place automatically when the residency and integration requirements are met.
Who should I contact for the application or renewal of the B permit?
You must contact the cantonal migration office of the Obvaldo Canton. For the exact address, reception times, presentation methods (online, by mail, at the counter) and administrative costs, consult the official website of the Obvaldo Canton or contact them directly. Processing times for renewal are generally 4-8 weeks.
