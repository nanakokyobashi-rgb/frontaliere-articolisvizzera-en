Family allowance amounts

Family allowances are an important benefit for families with children in the Glarus Canton. According to the Federal Law of 1990 on Family Support (LSF), family allowances are intended to partly replace expenses for education and childcare. The amount of family allowances varies according to family income and the number of children.

For example, in 2022, a household with two children and a household income of CHF 80,000 per year could have received a household allowance of about CHF12,000 per year. However, if the household income had been CHF 120,000 per year, the household allowance would have been only CHF6,000 per year.

Application for family allowances

The application for family allowances is managed by the competent Compensation Fund, which is responsible for verifying the regularity of the application and for providing the family allowance if the application has been accepted. The application for family allowances must be submitted within the time limits established by federal law.

Family allowances in Glarus Canton: amounts and demand

The Glarus Canton, located in the Graubünden region, offers a system of support to families with children through family allowances. This benefit is intended to support families with children under the age of 18, with the aim of promoting the birth and well-being of children.

How the family allowance system works

To claim family allowances, families must submit the application within the time limit set by cantonal law. The competent Compensation Fund, in this case the Glarus Canton Compensation Fund, handles the claim and determines the amount of family allowances according to cantonal law.

Family allowance amounts

The amount of family allowances is determined by cantonal law and may vary according to the composition of the family and the age of the children. For example, according to the cantonal law of 2020, family allowances for a family with a child under the age of 6 are CHF 220.00 per month.

Family allowances in Glarus Canton: amounts and demand

Canton Glarus offers a family allowance program to help families cover the costs of living. The citizens concerned must submit the application for family allowances within the established time limit, which may vary according to cantonal law.

The application must be submitted to the relevant Compensation Fund, which is responsible for assessing the application and deciding whether to grant family allowances. The competent Compensation Fund considers several factors, such as the family income, the number of children and the economic situation of the family.

The amount of family allowances will be determined according to cantonal law, which provides for a maximum amount of CHF 2,500.00 per family per month. However, the actual amount may vary based on individual household conditions.

Concrete example: a family with two children, both under the age of 16, with a family income of CHF 6,000.00 per month, can receive a family allowance of CHF1,800.00 per month.

Cantonal law also provides for a maximum amount of CHF 10,000.00 per family per year, which may be granted in case of particular circumstances.

The application for family allowances must be submitted by 31 March of the year following the year in which the child was born. The application must be signed and must be accompanied by a copy of the child's birth certificate.
