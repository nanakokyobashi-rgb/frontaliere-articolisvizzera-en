Child care in Switzerland is organized under a cantonal jurisdiction model. In the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, as in all Swiss cantons, the cantonal administration and municipalities manage child care services, ensuring access to various types of facilities with fees calculated based on family income.

Why Responsibility Lies with the Cantons

Unlike other European countries where childcare is regulated at the national level, in Switzerland each canton has complete autonomy over regulations, quality standards, fee structures, and available subsidies. This means that the childcare system in Basel-Landschaft has its own unique characteristics, even though it follows principles common throughout Switzerland: professional quality, fair fees based on income, and equal access for all families.

Operational details

Cantonal Costs and Subsidies

In the canton of Basel-Landschaft, the cost structure for child care combines three main elements: the basic hourly rate, the income-based calculation, and any cantonal subsidy.

The base hourly rate varies depending on the type of facility (daycare center vs. family daycare) and the location within the canton. However, this standard rate is not applied uniformly to all families: each family pays a percentage of the total cost based on its net income. Families with lower incomes may pay 10–30% of the standard rate, while those with higher incomes pay a higher percentage, up to 100%. This progressive structure ensures that the cost of childcare remains affordable regardless of a family’s financial situation.

Cantonal Subsidies and Additional Assistance

Cantonal subsidies form the second pillar of the system. The canton of Basel-Landschaft provides public funds to further reduce fees for families facing financial hardship. These subsidies are often automatically applied when calculating the final fee, without requiring separate applications from the family. Some municipalities in Basel-Landschaft also offer additional reductions for the second and third child, or increased subsidies for single-parent families.

Key points

How to Access Services

Access to child care services in Basel-Landschaft follows a standardized procedure at the cantonal level, although there may be variations depending on your municipality of residence.

Step 1: Get information from your municipality Contact the Social Services Office (Sozialamt) in your municipality of residence. There, you will receive information on: a list of public and private childcare facilities available in the area (daycare centers, family daycare providers, after-school programs), enrollment procedures, the fee calculation model specific to your municipality, and details on available subsidies based on your financial situation.

Step 2: Required Documentation To qualify for income-based fees and financial assistance, you’ll need: a copy of the previous year’s tax return, a certificate of residence issued by the municipality, personal information for all family members, and (if applicable for separated families) a custody agreement signed with the other parent.

Step 3: Enrollment in the Childcare Facility Once you’ve identified an available facility, you’ll submit an enrollment application. The facility will coordinate with the Social Services Office to calculate your personalized fee based on your documents. Waiting times range from a few months to 1–2 years, especially for full-time daycare centers during periods of high demand. It is advisable to enroll at least 6–12 months in advance to increase your chances of securing a spot.

Frequently Asked Questions
How exactly are child care rates calculated in Basel-Landschaft?
Fees are calculated based on the household's net income. Each municipality applies a formula that determines the percentage of the hourly rate to be paid: families with lower income pay lower percentages (10–30%), while families with high income pay higher percentages, up to 100% of the standard rate. The cantonal subsidy is automatically deducted from the final rate.
What types of custody are available in Basel-Landschaft?
In the canton there are kindergartens (Kindertagesstätten) for children from 0 to 4–5 years old, day families (Tagesfamilien) with flexible hours, and after-school services (Hort) for school-age children. All facilities must comply with cantonal regulations on quality, staff training and safety.
How can I apply for child care assistance?
Contact the Social Office (Sozialamt) of your municipality of residence with a copy of the tax return and certificate of residence. In many municipalities, the subsidy is calculated automatically at the time of enrollment and discounted from the fee without requiring a separate application.
How much does a full-time daycare center cost, approximately, in Basel-Landschaft?
The cost varies significantly based on household income and municipality. A middle-income family could pay CHF 400–700 per month, while lower-income families could pay CHF 200–400. The exact rates must be verified directly with the municipality of residence.
How long does it take to get a spot in daycare?
Waiting times vary from a few months to 1–2 years, depending on local availability, registration period and capacity of the facilities. It is advisable to register well in advance (6–12 months before the desired start) to secure a seat.
