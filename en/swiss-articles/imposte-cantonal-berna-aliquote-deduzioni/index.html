Operational details

The rates for cantonal taxes in the Canton of Bern are different depending on the income bracket. Allowable deductions include household expenses and family contributions. It is important to note that cantonal taxes may vary by region and city.

# Cantonal Tax Rates

The rates for cantonal taxes in the Canton of Bern are as follows:

  • For income brackets up to 7,500 Swiss francs (CHF) the rate is 0.4%.
  • For income brackets between CHF 7,501 and CHF 20,000 the rate is 1.2%.
  • For income brackets between CHF 20,001 and CHF 35,000 the rate is 1.4%.
  • For income brackets between CHF 35,001 and CHF 50,000 the rate is 1.6%.
  • For income brackets above CHF 50,000 the rate is 1.8%.

Allowed Deductions

Allowable deductions for cantonal taxes in the Canton of Bern include:

  • House expenses, such as mortgage or rent.
  • Family contributions, such as pension fund contributions or family allowances.
  • Expenditure on education, such as private schools or universities.
  • Health expenses, such as medical care or personal assistants.

Key points

To find out what deductions are allowed and how to calculate cantonal taxes in the Canton of Bern, you can consult the online portal of the cantonal tax administration. You can also use the cantonal tax calculator to determine the taxes due.

The cantonal tax in the Canton of Bern is established by the “Tax Circular” of 2022, which provides for a tax of 13.2% on taxable income. However, it is important to note that there are different rates for different types of income. For example, investments in government bonds are subject to a 4.5% tax, while investments in companies are subject to a 6.5% tax.

To calculate cantonal taxes, you need to determine taxable income, which is gross income minus allowable deductions. Allowable deductions are set out in the 2022 “Tax Circular” and include:

  • The deduction for the family, which is CHF 10,000 per family with two parents and two children
  • The deduction for the dwelling, which is CHF 8,000 for a 100 m² apartment
  • The transport deduction, which is CHF 2,000 for a car

  • Taxable income: CHF 80,000
