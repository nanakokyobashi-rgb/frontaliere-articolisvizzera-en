How School Enrollment Works

School enrolment in the Canton of Lucerne is a simple and quick process. Parents must submit an application for enrollment by the end of the previous school year, along with a copy of the child's ID card and a statement of residence. It is important to note that registration is open to all Swiss and foreign citizens residing in the Canton of Lucerne.

School Calendar

The school calendar consists of two semesters, one winter and one summer. Each semester lasts about 20 weeks and includes a mix of didactic and extracurricular activities. The school calendar is established by cantonal law and is published annually.

Operational details

School enrolment in the Canton of Lucerne is a simple and quick process. Parents can register their children for compulsory schooling via the Canton's website or in person at the local school office. Compulsory schooling begins at age 6 and lasts for 9 years. The school calendar consists of two semesters, with a 2-week break between summer and winter.

The compulsory school in the Canton of Lucerne offers a wide range of courses and extracurricular activities, such as music, art and sports. Students may choose to pursue a course of study that suits their needs and interests.

Example of registration

A parent can enroll their child in the compulsory school of the Canton of Lucerne by following these steps:

1. Go to the website of the Canton of Lucerne and click on the "School registration" section. 2. Select the desired compulsory school and the class your child belongs to. 3. Enter the personal and contact details of the parent and child. 4. Confirm your registration and receive an email confirmation.

Registration fees

School enrolment in the Canton of Lucerne is free for Swiss citizens. However, foreign parents may be required to pay a tuition fee, which varies by canton and school. For example, in the Canton of Zurich, the tuition fee for foreign students is around CHF 500 per year.

Key points

To enroll their children in compulsory school in the Canton of Lucerne, parents must submit the application for enrollment by August 15 of each year. The application for registration must be accompanied by all the necessary documents, such as the child's identity card and that of the parents, as well as the tax return. The Lucerne compulsory school also offers a support service for students with disabilities.

For example, in 2022, about 14,500 new classes were enrolled in the compulsory school of the Canton of Lucerne, an increase of 3.5% compared to the previous year. This increase was largely due to the arrival of new families in the region.

The Lucerne compulsory school follows the model of balanced education, which combines theoretical aspects with practice. Students can choose from different training options, such as foreign language learning, music and art. Lucerne's compulsory school is also equipped with the best technologies to ensure a quality education.

However, it is important to note that the Lucerne compulsory school is subject to federal and cantonal rules on education.
