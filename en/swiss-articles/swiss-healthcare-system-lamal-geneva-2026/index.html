Within three months of arrival, anyone residing in Switzerland must take out the compulsory health insurance provided for by LAMal/KVG. This is the operational deadline from which to begin the 2026 guide to the Swiss healthcare system, including for those living in the canton of Geneva. LAMal is private insurance with per-capita premiums: it is not a tax and it is not a payroll contribution.

One common rule, cantonal variables

The rule requiring insurance is national. Premiums, however, are structured by canton and region, while premium reductions are a cantonal subsidy. LAMal is not a payroll deduction

The premium must be kept separate from contributions linked to salary. AVS/AHV, AI, IPG, AD/AC, LAINF/LAA and LPP/BVG belong to the payroll contribution system; LAMal instead follows the logic of the per-capita premium. Direct federal tax and VAT are also different items: AFC/ESTV is responsible for these two taxes, while cantonal administrations oversee the cantonal and municipal levels. UFAS/BSV concerns social security and UST/BFS statistics. Distinguishing these levels prevents healthcare spending from being treated as a rate on salary.
