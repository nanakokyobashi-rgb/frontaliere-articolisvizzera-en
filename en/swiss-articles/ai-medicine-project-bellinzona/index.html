AI and drugs: Bellinzona in the SWIT-DREAMS project

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

SWIT-DREAMS gets underway: 1,37 million euros from Interreg for research between Novara and Bellinzona on nanobodies and artificial intelligence applied to medicine.

The SWIT-DREAMS project officially gets underway in Novara. The presentation was held yesterday at the premises of Fondazione Novara Sviluppo, during the kick-off meeting between the scientific partners and the representatives of the European Interreg Italy-Switzerland programme. The programme funded the initiative with a budget of 1,37 million euros.

The objective is to create an integrated Italian-Swiss platform for the development of next-generation antibodies and bioconjugates. The project brings together artificial intelligence and molecular biology, with a pathway dedicated to molecules intended for biopharmaceutical research.

The biological drugs sector includes antibodies, nanobodies and antibody-drug conjugates, also known as ADCs. Globally, it is currently worth approximately 256 billion euros. The estimate for 2035 stands at 852 billion, with an annual increase of 13%.

The roles assigned to the four partners

The work will last 30 months and involve four research and innovation organizations. On the Italian side, the University of Eastern Piedmont is leading the project through the Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences. The teams of Professors Davide Ferraris and Tracey Pirali will work on the biostructural study of antibodies and the development of bioconjugates, with the support of the academic spin-off IXTAL.

The Swiss partner is Choose Life Biotech SA of Bellinzona, led by Luca Varani and specializing in nanobody screening. The University of Milan, with Professor Alessandro Contini's group, will handle AI-based molecular modelling. The Ibis Consortium of Novara, under the leadership of President Luigi Panza and Director Barbara Tosi, will handle communication, oversight and dissemination of the scientific results.

The structure therefore brings together diverse expertise: antibody selection and study, nanobody screening, computational modelling and research communication.

Operational details

The Swiss dimension of the project

For those reading the news from the Swiss side, the concrete point is the role assigned to Bellinzona within the platform. Choose Life Biotech SA is not indicated solely as the Swiss site, but as the partner specializing in nanobody screening. Swiss participation is therefore included in a specific phase of the scientific work.

The project should be viewed at an equally precise scale. The 256 billion euros referring to the current value of the sector and the estimate of 852 billion in 2035 are global data on the biological drugs sector. They do not represent a specific forecast for Switzerland. The 13% indicated in the source likewise describes the annual growth of the global sector, not an expected result for a single company or a single area.

The same criterion applies to the funding. The source indicates a total budget of 1,37 million euros for the Italy-Switzerland Interreg initiative, without assigning separate shares to the four partners. For this reason, the soundest interpretation concerns cross-border collaboration and the positioning of the research, rather than an economic impact already measured for Bellinzona.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the SWIT-DREAMS project and who is involved in Switzerland?
SWIT-DREAMS is a research initiative that combines artificial intelligence and molecular biology to develop next-generation antibodies and bioconjugates. The main Swiss partner is Choose Life Biotech SA, based in Bellinzona, which specialises in nanoantibody screening. The project is funded by the Interreg Italy-Switzerland program with a budget of 1.37 million euros and also involves the University of Eastern Piedmont, the University of Milan and the Ibis Consortium.
What is the specific role of the Bellinzona office?
Within the integrated Italian-Swiss platform, Choose Life Biotech SA of Bellinzona, led by Luca Varani, has the specific task of dealing with the screening of nanoantibodies. This phase is crucial in the research path that also includes molecular modeling through AI, entrusted to Italian partners, and the subsequent chemical synthesis of molecules intended for the global biopharmaceutical sector.
What are the timing and economic outlook for the sector?
The project has an expected duration of 30 months starting from the official kick-off in Novara. At the market level, the biological drugs sector (antibodies and ADCs) is now worth around €256 billion globally. Estimates for 2035 predict a growth of up to 852 billion euros, with an annual increase of 13%. These data reflect the potential of the sector in which cross-border collaboration is inserted.
