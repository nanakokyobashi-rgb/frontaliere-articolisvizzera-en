The Canton of Aargau has ordered an external investigation into sexual offences in Untersiggenthal involving a former UDC MP. The purpose is in particular to clarify to what extent the protection of the two foreign victims has been guaranteed.

The investigation, conducted by a team of external experts, aims to verify whether the authorities involved have acted correctly and whether the rules protecting the rights of foreign victims have been respected. In particular, the investigation will focus on case management and communication between the authorities involved and the victims.

The Canton of Aargau has already launched an internal investigation, but has decided to arrange an external investigation to ensure the objectivity and impartiality of the process. The external investigation will be conducted by a team of experts in the field of criminal law and protection of victims' rights.

The Prosecutor of the Canton of Aargau has applied the Federal Law on Criminal Law (LDP) of 1987, which provides for life imprisonment for sexual abuse.
