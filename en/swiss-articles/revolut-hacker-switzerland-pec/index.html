Revolut hacker: data from 700 Swiss customers (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 20 September 2026

Hacker attack on Revolut: almost 700 Swiss customers hit. Criminals are asking for $3 million not to publish the data.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Revolut, an online bank, suffered a hack that led to the delivery of data of almost 700 customers to cybercriminals.
  • Hackers posed as Italian authorities, putting pressure on Revolut.
  • No money was stolen, but sensitive data such as passport information and account numbers.
  • The incident mainly involves Swiss customers.

Key facts

  • Hacker attack: Revolut provided data to cybercriminals.
  • Method: Hackers exploited an official Italian communication channel (PEC).
  • Compromised data: Passports, accounts and addresses of almost 700 customers.
  • Victims: especially Swiss customers.
  • Ransom: Hackers demand $3 million from Revolut not to publish the data.

The hacker attack on the online bank Revolut also affected Switzerland, involving a significant number of Swiss customers. The hackers acted in an unusual way, publishing their ransom demand on a website and addressing public opinion instead of negotiating directly with the company. This has raised questions about the real motivations of cybercriminals.

Operational details

The hacker attack on Revolut raises concerns about the security of Swiss customers' personal data. The incident highlights the vulnerability of official communication channels, such as the Italian PEC, which can be exploited by cybercriminals to obtain sensitive information. This case highlights the need to strengthen security measures and raise users' awareness of the importance of protecting their personal data.

"For this reason there is great nervousness in Italy," says Pascal Lago. "Hackers could also access the data of many other companies. For this reason, the Italian authorities are also investigating the case. "

The impact on Revolut's image in Switzerland could be significant, especially considering the recent banking license application. Swiss customers, who often use Revolut as an additional account for holidays abroad, may be discouraged by the incident and prefer other safer options. In addition, the incident could have repercussions on the trust of customers towards online banks in general, pushing them to prefer traditional institutions with physical branches.

The hacker attack also highlights the need for greater collaboration between Swiss and Italian authorities to counter cross-border cybercrime. Ongoing investigations in Italy could provide valuable information to prevent future similar attacks in Switzerland.

Key points

Following the hacker attack on Revolut, it is important for Swiss customers to take some precautions to protect their personal data. Here are some steps to follow:

Data Security Verification

Regularly check your Revolut account to verify any suspicious activity. If you notice anything unusual, immediately contact Revolut's customer service to report the issue.

Personal Data Protection

Ensure you have secure and unique passwords for your Revolut account and other financial services. Avoid sharing sensitive information, such as account numbers or passport details, unless absolutely necessary.

Activity Monitoring

Keep an eye on the transactions on your account and report any unauthorized activity. Revolut offers tools to monitor your spending and receive real-time notifications.

Information and Updates

Stay informed about the latest news regarding the Revolut hacking incident. The company should provide regular updates on the situation and the measures adopted to protect customer data.

Changing Banks

If you feel insecure or have doubts about the security of your data with Revolut, consider the possibility of changing banks. In Switzerland, there are many options to choose from, both traditional and online. Make sure to compare the different offers and choose an institution that meets your needs and guarantees the security of your data.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many Swiss customers are involved in the attack on Revolut?
According to the source, the incident mainly involved Swiss customers, with almost 700 people whose data was compromised. Cybercriminals obtained sensitive information such as passport data, account numbers, and addresses. It does not appear that money was taken directly from the accounts.
How did the hackers get Revolut's data?
Hackers posed as Italian authorities using an official communication channel, PEC (Certified Electronic Mail). This method put pressure on Revolut, leading her to hand over the data to criminals. The vulnerability of official channels was highlighted as a critical point.
What do hackers ask for in exchange for silence?
Cybercriminals posted their ransom demand on a website, addressing the public. They are asking Revolut for $3 million not to publish the stolen data. This mode of attack, public rather than private, raised questions about the real motivations behind the action.
What precautions should Swiss customers take?
It is recommended to regularly check the account for suspicious activity, use strong and unique passwords, and monitor transactions. If in doubt, it is advisable to contact customer service or consider changing banks, comparing the offers available in Switzerland through the bank cost calculator.
