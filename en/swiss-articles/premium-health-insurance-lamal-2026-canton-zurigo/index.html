Operational details

LAMal health insurance premiums in the Canton of Zurich for 2026

LAMal health insurance is private insurance at per capita premiums, not a tax or salary contribution. Premiums are fixed by canton and award region. The premium reduction is applied to all cantons.

In the Canton of Zurich, the maximum premium for LAMal health insurance for 2026 is CHF 4,570. The minimum premium is CHF 2,560. The premium reduction is CHF 1,010, which means that citizens of the Canton of Zurich will be able to save up to CHF1,010 depending on their premium.

The premium reduction is applied to all cantons, but the maximum and minimum premiums vary depending on the canton. In the Canton of Bern, for example, the maximum premium for LAMal health insurance for 2026 is CHF 4,380, while in the Canton of Ticino it is CHF4,740.
