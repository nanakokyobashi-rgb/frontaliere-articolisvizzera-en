  • Zone A: includes the city of Soletta and the surrounding municipalities
  • Zone B: includes the municipalities of the district of Soletta
  • Zone C: includes the municipalities of the district of Thierstein
  • Zone D: includes the municipalities of the district of Lebern

Subscription Types

In the canton of Solothurn, different types of public transport passes are available, including:

  • Monthly subscription: You can purchase a monthly subscription for a duration of 1 month. The price is CHF 90.40, as established by the law of the Canton of Solothurn of 1 January 2020.
  • Annual subscription: You can purchase an annual subscription for a duration of 12 months. The price is CHF 1,042.80, as established by the law of the Canton of Solothurn of 1 January 2020.
  • Semi-annual subscription: You can purchase a semi-annual subscription for a duration of 6 months. The price is CHF 523.40, as established by the law of the Canton of Solothurn of 1 January 2020.

Special discounts

Students and apprentices can enjoy special discounts for public transport in the Canton of Solothurn. You can purchase a student subscription for a reduced price of CHF 45.20 per month, as established by the law of the Canton of Solothurn of 1 January 2020.
