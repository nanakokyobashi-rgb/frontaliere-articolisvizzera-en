Bern, 18 August 2026. The University of St. Gallen has published a new periodic analysis of the EasyGov platform commissioned by the Secretariat of State for Economic Affairs (SECO). The results confirm that EasyGov significantly lightens the burden on Swiss small and medium-sized enterprises, both in terms of time and administrative costs.

The study reveals the importance of digital systems to simplify bureaucratic requirements and reduce the operating costs of Swiss SMEs.

What the EasyGov study confirms

The analysis conducted by the University of Graubünden represents a systematic periodic evaluation of the platform. SECO chose to entrust this work to an accredited university to gain an independent and rigorous view of EasyGov's effectiveness in achieving its administrative simplification goals.

The data emerging from the study confirms a positive trend: EasyGov reduces both the time and costs required to comply

Operational details

The importance of a platform like EasyGov lies not only in the measurable results, but also in the underlying logic it represents: the simplification of the relationship between public administration and companies.

How administrative lightening works

Swiss SMEs operate in a complex regulatory environment, where they have to comply with administrative obligations at the federal, cantonal and municipal levels. EasyGov centralizes access to many of these digital services, reducing fragmentation and the time it takes to compile and transmit documents.

A company no longer has to physically move to different offices or wait for long response times by mail. The platform allows you to manage many procedures directly from a single portal, 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

This impact manifests itself in two directions: on the one hand, the time saved by the owners and administrative employees of the company; on the other, the costs associated with errors, delays or the need for external advice to navigate the bureaucracy. A solid and recognized as effective platform facilitates the adoption of digital solutions by those SMEs that still hesitate to invest in administrative transformation.

Key points

SMEs wishing to take advantage of EasyGov can access the platform through the channels made available by SECO. The tool is designed to be accessible even to those without advanced technical skills.

Getting started with EasyGov: first practical steps

For an SME looking to reduce administrative burdens, EasyGov represents a real opportunity. The first step is to find out about the services available on the platform and what administrative procedures can be managed digitally at federal, cantonal and municipal offices.

Once the relevant procedures for its business have been identified, the company can proceed with the registration and start using the services. The platform has been designed with an intuitive interface, precisely to facilitate access even to small businesses operating in different sectors.

The added value of EasyGov is not only technical, but also procedural simplification: it is not necessary to learn different systems for each federal or cantonal administrative fulfillment. Everything is centralized in a single portal.

What concrete benefits does EasyGov bring to Swiss SMEs?
The study confirms that EasyGov significantly reduces the burden on Swiss SMEs, both in terms of time and administrative costs. A company saves hours dedicated to bureaucracy, reduces the risks of errors or delays, and frees up resources to be reinvested in innovation, product and employment.
