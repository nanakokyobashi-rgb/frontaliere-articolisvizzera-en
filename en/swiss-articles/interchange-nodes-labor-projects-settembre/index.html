The work for the arrangement of the Biasca interchange node enters the last phase. The sound-absorbing flooring will be laid in the coming weeks. The work will conclude on Saturday, September 12. The arrangement of the Biasca interchange node is an important investment for the safety and efficiency of traffic in the region.

The arrangement of the Biasca interchange node was financed with an amount of 2.5 million Swiss francs, partly covered by the Department of the Territory and partly by the Municipality of Biasca. This investment is part of a larger road infrastructure improvement project in the Canton of Graubünden.

The arrangement of the node

Operational details

The work for the arrangement of the Biasca interchange node has entered its last phase, which will take place from 7 a.m. to 5.30 p.m. from August 17 to September 4. During this period, the wear layers will be laid on the yards in front of the SBB station and the preparatory work for laying the wear layer on Via Bellinzona will be carried out.

According to the concessionaire company's estimates, the Biasca interchange node refurbishment works will be completed by 31 December 2024, at an estimated cost of 12.5 million Swiss francs. 📊

The arrangement of the Biasca interchange node is part of a wider project to modernise railway infrastructure in the canton of Graubünden, which involves the installation of new transport lines and the replacement of old infrastructure. The project was approved by the State Council of Graubünden on 15 March 2022, with a total amount of CHF 150 million.

The work on the arrangement of the Biasca interchange node will be carried out in two phases. The first phase, which ended on 15 August 2024, saw the laying of new transport lines and the replacement of old infrastructure. The second phase, which will take place from 17 August to 4 September, involves laying the wear layer on the yards in front of the SBB station and laying the wear layer on Via Bellinzona.

Key points

From the evening of Monday 7 to the morning of Saturday 12 September, the sound-absorbing wear layer will be laid in the section between the roundabout of the Migros area and the roundabout located at the height of the Post Office building. Traffic will be regulated in an alternate direction on a single lane of travel by traffic control agents.

The modernization works of the interchange node in Biasca are now in their final phase. The city of the Canton of Ticino has been chosen to host one of the main maintenance and modernization of road infrastructure in southern Switzerland. The works, which were planned and financed by the Department of the Territory of the Canton of Ticino, have a budget of 5.2 million francs, covering the laying of a new layer of sound-absorbing wear, the construction of new parking areas and the reorganization of traffic.

According to the information provided by the Department of the Territory, the works were planned to minimize inconvenience for residents and merchants in the area. “We are working hard to complete the work by the deadline,” says a representative of the Department of Territory. “The traffic reorganization has been designed to be as efficient as possible, but please pay attention to the indications of the bookmarks and respect the traffic rules.”
