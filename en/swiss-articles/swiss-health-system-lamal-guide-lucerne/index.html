LAMal Obligation

Health insurance is compulsory for all Swiss citizens, including border workers residing in the Canton of Lucerne. According to the Federal Act of 1982 (LAMal), every person residing in Switzerland must be insured against medical expenses. This obligation applies to all Swiss citizens, regardless of their nationality or residence status.

Deductibles

The deductible is the maximum spending limit that the health insurance covers for each person. The deductible varies depending on the canton and the health insurance fund chosen. For example, in the canton of Zurich, the allowance for an adult is CHF 2,500 per year, while in the canton of Bern it is CHF 2,200. 💡

The health insurance obligation is provided for by the Federal Act of 1982, which states that all Swiss citizens must be insured against health expenses. In particular, adults residing in the Canton of Lucerne are obliged to choose one of the 60 health insurance funds (Kassen) recognized by the State.

The allowance for adults varies according to age and economic situation. For example, adults between the ages of 26 and 59 have a deductible of CHF 300-2500. It is important to note that the deductible may vary depending on the Canton of residence and the individual economic situation.

Deductibles and coverage

Switzerland has a deductible system, which means policyholders have to pay a portion of medical costs before they can access services. Deductibles vary depending on the health insurance fund chosen and the type of insurance.
