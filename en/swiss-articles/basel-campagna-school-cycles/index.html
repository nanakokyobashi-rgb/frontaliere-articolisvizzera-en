In the canton of Basel-Landschaft, compulsory schooling is the starting point for reading the cantonal school system. The source focuses on a precise sequence: enrolment, cycles, age of enrolment, school calendar, languages of instruction and transition to secondary school. These are the six elements that define the angle of the guide and allow you to follow the path without confusing entry, organisation of the year and continuation of studies.

The map of the route

Enrolment is the first junction. It must be considered together with the age of enrolment, because the two pieces of information concern the start of the path. The cycles add the structure: they indicate the stages through which compulsory schooling is presented. The school calendar, on the other hand, brings the time factor into the reading of the system. For a family, this means keeping the moment in which you enter, the phase in which you are and the rhythm of the year separate.

The languages of instruction constitute a further axis of the overview. The source places them next to enrolment, cycles and calendar, not as a detail extraneous to the path. The picture closes with the transition to secondary school: the guide therefore does not stop at compulsory schooling, but also considers the connection with the next level.

The distinction makes the analysis more concrete. Asking only when enrolment takes place leaves out the cycles; looking only at the calendar does not clarify the language of instruction; describing compulsory schooling without the next step interrupts the path before it continues. Operational details

The practical value of the map emerges when a family has to organize the route without mixing different plans. The age of enrolment does not perform the same function as the cycles; the calendar does not answer the question of language; the transition to secondary school concerns a different phase from entering compulsory schooling. Distinguishing these passages reduces ambiguities and makes any cantonal information more readable.

Key points

To turn the reading into an operational plan, it pays to work in five steps. The sequence holds the themes of the source together and prevents the inscription from being confused with the next step.

Operational checklist

  • Define the goal: enrolment in compulsory school or transition to secondary school.
  • Write down the age of enrollment as the first criterion, separating it from the school calendar.
  • Place the path in the relevant loop before evaluating the next step.
  • Enter the languages of instruction as a standalone test item.
  • Reorder the calendar and transition to high school in a single sheet.

The first item clarifies the starting point. The second avoids using the calendar as a substitute for the age of enrolment. The third brings the question about cycles to the center of the sheet. The fourth keeps the dimension of the languages of instruction visible. The fifth connects compulsory school to upper secondary schools without skipping the steps indicated by the source.

The procedure can be used whenever the objective of the consultation changes. For a new enrolment, the card opens on the age and cycle; for the continuation, the calendar and languages are used to arrive at the next step. In both cases, the reference remains the school system of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft and the five items should be read together, not as isolated answers.

Before closing the planning, reread the sheet in the order in which the path was constructed: entrance, cycle, calendar, language and continuation to secondary school. In this way, the consultation remains adherent to the available topics and does not turn a single piece of information into a general rule.
