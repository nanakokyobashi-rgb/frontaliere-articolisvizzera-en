What is the subject of the opinion published by the Federal Council?
The Federal Council expressed its official opinion in response to the report of the Management Commission of the Council of States (CoG-S), dated 18 May 2026. The formal act, adopted during the session of 19 August 2026 and made public on 21 August 2026, defines the government's determinations regarding the strategic planning of national railway infrastructure, a crucial sector for the maintenance of transport efficiency and connectivity between the Cantons.
What are the key dates of this institutional process?
The process saw the presentation of the report by the Management Commission of the Council of States (CoG-S) on 18 May 2026. Subsequently, the Federal Council discussed and formalized its opinion during the session of 19 August 2026.
