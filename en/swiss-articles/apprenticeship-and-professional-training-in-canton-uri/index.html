Apprenticeship in Canton Uri: how to find an internship

  • If you are a frontier worker interested in a vocational apprenticeship, please contact your workers' representative or employer for information on the selection procedure. - Make sure you have an internship contract that sets out the apprentice's salary and vocational training requirements. - Follow the procedures and requirements established by law for vocational training and apprenticeship.

Canton Uri offers several apprenticeship opportunities for border workers interested in developing their professional skills. The Canton Uri Ministry of Labour and Health is responsible for the regulation of apprenticeships in the canton and provides information and support to employers and apprentices.

To begin your search for an internship position, you should contact your worker representative or employer for information on the selection process. These can provide you with information on the availability of traineeships and the selection procedure.

In addition, it is important to ensure that you have an internship contract that sets out the apprentice's salary and vocational training requirements. The contract must be signed by the employer and the apprentice and must be kept for at least 10 years.
