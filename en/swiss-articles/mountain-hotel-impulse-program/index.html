Mountain hotels: 100 million for sanitation (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 September 2026

The Federal Council allocates 100 million francs for the energy modernization of mountain hotel facilities in the period 2028-2035.

Context

In brief

  • The Federal Council has adopted the additional message for the hotel sector
  • 100 million francs allocated for mountain regions over eight years
  • The program aims to encourage energy renovation of hotels
  • Implementation will be carried out through the Swiss Hotel Credit Society (SCA)

Key facts

  • What: Impulse program for hotel modernization
  • When: September 2, 2026 (adoption of message); period 2028-2035
  • Where: Mountain regions in Switzerland
  • Who: Federal Council and Swiss Hotel Credit Society (SCA)
  • Amount: Total of 100 million francs

On September 2, 2026, the Federal Council adopted the additional message related to the total revision of the federal law on the promotion of the hotel sector. This decision aims to introduce a limited-duration impulse program specifically dedicated to the modernization of hotel businesses located in mountain regions. The initiative is part of a broader regulatory path that saw, already on April 16, 2025, the government adopting a message for the relaunch of the Swiss Hotel Credit Society (SCA), the central entity in the process of financing and promoting the sector.

…

Operational details

The introduction of this boost program represents a significant change in the federal strategy to support alpine tourism. While the hypothesis of implementing motion 19.3234 Stöckli had previously been shelved by the Federal Council for financial and organizational reasons, the current post-consultation scenario has required a new analysis. The choice to link public support to investments in energy renovation reflects the sector's need to align with modern efficiency requirements. For hotel businesses operating in mountain regions, this availability of capital does not only represent an incentive for ordinary maintenance but a catalyst for deep renovation projects that would otherwise be economically unsustainable. The Swiss Hotel Credit Society (SCA), in this process, assumes the role of guarantor and facilitator, ensuring that resources are channeled towards projects with a real qualitative and environmental impact.

Key points

For hotel businesses intending to access these resources, the procedure follows the regulatory framework provided for the Swiss Hotel Credit Society (SCA). Although the operational details for submitting applications will be defined during the implementation of the federal decree, companies must demonstrate that their projects comply with the exemplary energy renovation criteria required by law. The administrative process will require rigorous technical documentation that not only proves the need for the intervention but also the added value in terms of energy efficiency. It is advisable for companies to monitor the official communications of the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research to understand the opening times of the calls and the minimum access requirements, thus ensuring they are ready as soon as the 2028-2035 funding period enters the operational phase.
