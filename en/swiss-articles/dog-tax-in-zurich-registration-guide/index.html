The tax framework to keep distinct

The Swiss framework provides for three tax levels: direct federal tax, cantonal taxes and municipal taxes. The Cantons do not automatically share the same rules: each has its own law and its own multiplier; at the municipal level, the multiplier is applied to the cantonal one. For dog tax in the Canton of Zurich, the operational reference is therefore the cantonal and municipal levels.

Distinguishing between offices helps avoid an incorrect search. AFC/ESTV is associated with direct federal tax and VAT, while the cantonal administrations handle cantonal and municipal matters. UST/BFS performs statistical tasks and does not set tax rates. These roles do not, by themselves, provide the amount of dog tax, but they clarify which institutional level to use as a starting point.

For those who live or work in Switzerland, the practical result is to separate national data from local rules. A reference to direct federal tax or VAT does not answer the question about registering an animal in the Canton of Zurich. Similarly, an amount reported without indicating the cantonal or municipal rule is not enough to describe the obligation.

The guide must consider amounts together with the conditions accompanying them. First, verify whether the tax is due; then identify the authority; next, check registration and any exemptions; finally, follow the prescribed payment method. This sequence keeps the obligation, registration, exemptions and payment distinct and prevents a local rule from being treated as general tax information.
