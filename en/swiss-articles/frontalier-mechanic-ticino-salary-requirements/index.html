Requirements to work as an assembler in Ticino

To work as an assembler in Ticino, you need to have a qualification certificate in window and door assembly, issued by a recognized training organization. This certificate must be valid for the assembly of metal, wood and PVC windows and doors.

In addition, you must have working experience of at least 3 years as a fitter in an EU country or in a country with which Switzerland has an agreement on the free movement of workers. It is also necessary to have a knowledge of the German or French language, as window and door assembly is an industry that requires good communication with customers and colleagues.

To work as an assembler in Ticino, frontier workers must obtain a G permit. The G permit allows border workers to work in Switzerland without having to obtain a residence permit.

Border workers can obtain the G permit by presenting the required documentation, including their passport, health card and certificate of registration with the labour office. It is important to note that the G permit is only valid for a certain period of time and can be renewed before the expiry date.
