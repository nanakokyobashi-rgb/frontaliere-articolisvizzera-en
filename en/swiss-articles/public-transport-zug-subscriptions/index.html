Public transport passes Canton Zug zones and fares

The public transport subscription system in the Canton of Zurich is complex and requires good planning to avoid high costs. The main public transport company is Verkehrs- und Tarifverbund Zürich (VZ), which operates the public transport system in the city of Zurich and the Canton of Zurich.

Different zones and rates

The Canton of Zurich is divided into several zones, each with its own tariff. There are six main zones, numbered 1 to 6, with different rates for each zone. The lowest fare applies to Zone 1, which includes the city of Zurich, while fares increase as you move to the outermost areas.

Annual and monthly subscriptions

There are two types of subscriptions: annual and monthly. Annual subscriptions are cheaper than monthly subscriptions, but require an annual payment. Monthly subscriptions are more flexible, but require a monthly payment.
