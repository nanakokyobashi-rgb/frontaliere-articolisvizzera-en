Musical training: reduced fees for young people (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 August 2026

From 2026, fees up to 90% for fragile families and extended access up to 25 years. Ticino invests CHF 2.5 million per year in extracurricular music training.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Musical fees reduced by up to 90% for families with cantonal subsidies
  • Age threshold extended from 20 to 25 years
  • Cantonal investment grows to CHF 2.5 million per year (2026-2028)

Key facts

  • What: New accessibility measures for out-of-school musical training
  • When: From the new school year 2026
  • Where: Canton of Ticino
  • Who: Department of Education, Culture and Sport (DECs)
  • Amount: Over CHF 2.5 million per year for the three-year period 2026-2028
  • Beneficiaries: Economically fragile families; young people up to 25 years old
  • Origin: Counterproject to the popular initiative '100 days for music'

The decision of the Cantonal Council of Ticino

From the new school year, extracurricular musical training in Ticino becomes more accessible. The Cantonal Council approved a package of measures contained in a counter-project to the popular initiative '100 days for music', with two objectives: to expand access to music training for children and young people, and to adapt the cantonal legal bases to federal regulations.

The first tangible effect concerns school fees. For economically fragile families that benefit from specific cantonal subsidies, a further reduction in the fee is foreseen. The reduction can reach up to 90%, making musical training concretely accessible even for families with less economic capacity.

Operational details

Who benefits from the new reductions

The new measures target economically fragile families as a priority. The access criterion is clear: only households that already benefit from specific Ticino cantonal economic subsidies will be able to take advantage of the reduction of the tuition up to 90%. This approach ensures that public aid is focused on the most vulnerable population groups, avoiding the dispersion of resources towards families with sufficient economic capacity.

The reduction of the fee represents a concrete lever of social inclusion. For a family that is in a situation of economic fragility, the cost of music lessons can represent an insurmountable obstacle to access to training. With a reduction of up to 90%, the economic barrier drops significantly. Those who want to check in detail how costs impact the family budget can consult il calcolatore del costo della vita for a preliminary assessment of the general situation.

The Meaning of Age Widening

The extension of the age limit from 20 to 25 years has two important practical meanings. First, it allows young adults to pursue musical studies without suddenly interrupting them in their 20s. For those who pursue a more demanding musical training or want to develop musical skills at a semi-professional level, these additional five years can make a difference.

Key points

How to Access Tuition Reductions

Access to reduced contributions is not automatic. Interested families must first meet the basic requirements: the young person must be between zero and 25 years old (the new limit), and the family must already benefit from Ticino cantonal economic subsidies. This means that applications must be submitted to music schools recognized by the Canton, not directly to the administration.

The exact procedures for submitting requests and verifying requirements will be communicated by the individual music schools recognized by the Ticino Canton. It is important that interested families contact directly the music facility where they wish to enroll their child, verifying the necessary documentation (income statements, certification of cantonal subsidies received). To assess the economic impact of the family situation, the tax and subsidies calculator provides a useful reference base.

The New School Year Deadline

The measures come into effect from the new school year 2026. This means that the first enrollments benefiting from fee reduction can be signed starting from the opening of the new school year. It is advisable to check with individual music schools what the enrollment deadlines and reduction application submission deadlines are.

Next Steps for Families

Those residing in Ticino with children interested in music education should: verify if their family already benefits from cantonal subsidies; gather the necessary documentation (income certificates, declarations of subsidies received); contact the chosen music school to learn about application procedures and enrollment deadlines; verify the updated pricing plan that reflects the new reduction up to 90%.

Frequently Asked Questions
When do the new music tuition reduction measures come into effect?
The new measures come into force from the new school year 2026. Families will be able to apply for a tariff reduction at the music schools recognised by the Canton from that moment on. It is advisable to contact the chosen facility directly to check the registration deadlines.
What is the maximum discount on the music tuition?
For economically fragile families who benefit from Ticino cantonal subsidies, the reduction in tuition can reach up to 90%. This is one of the main effects of the new cantonal policy, aimed at making musical training accessible even to families with less economic capacity.
Up to what age can you benefit from musical training subsidies?
The maximum age limit rises from 20 to 25 years. This means that young people will be able to access cantonal subsidies until they reach the age of 25, significantly extending the period of access to tariff reductions compared to the previous rules.
Which families are entitled to a reduction in their tuition?
Economically fragile families already benefiting from specific Ticino cantonal economic subsidies are entitled to the new reductions. The reduction is not universal but is concentrated on nuclei with limited economic capacity, as indicated by the Cantonal Council.
Where does funding for these measures come from?
The Canton has earmarked a significant additional financial contribution through the Swisslos Fund. The total contributions to Federations and recognized music schools will increase from about one million francs per year to over 2.5 million per year for the three-year period 2026-2028.
