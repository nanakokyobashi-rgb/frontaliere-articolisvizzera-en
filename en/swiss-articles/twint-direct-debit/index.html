How does Twint Direct Debit work?

Twint Direct Debit is a service that allows users to authorize, manage, and view direct debit permissions. Users will be able to grant authorization for recurring payments, such as sick pay premiums, subscriptions, or bills. The offer will replace LSV+ direct debits from 30 September 2028.

Operational details

Direct debit also benefits issuers, who will be able to collect recurring payments more easily and reduce the burden required for reminders and manual procedures. The offer will replace LSV+ direct debits from 30 September 2028.

This technological innovation will be particularly useful for national traders and professionals, such as small entrepreneurs in Lugano, who will be able to handle recurring payments more efficiently and reduce costs. In addition, direct debit will also be a beneficial solution for customers, who will benefit from greater ease and speed in payments.

Twint's offer provides that direct debits will be handled automatically, without the need for reminders or manual procedures. This means that recurring payments will be executed in real time, without the need to wait for payment confirmation. In addition, direct debit will also be safer than manual payments, as there will be no errors in writing or reading data.

The regulation governing direct debit is the federal law of 15 December 2017 on payment cards, which provides for direct debits to be handled securely and transparently.
