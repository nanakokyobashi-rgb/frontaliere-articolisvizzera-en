Family allowances Canton Schaffhausen: amounts and demand

The Canton of Schaffhausen offers a training allowance for front-men who intend to continue their studies or acquire new skills. The amount of this allowance is determined by cantonal law/ordinance and varies depending on the age and family situation of the beneficiary. 📊

Training Check Amounts

  • For frontboards under the age of 20, the maximum amount is CHF 2'000.00 per year of study, according to Article 9 of the Law on Training Benefits of 1 January 2020.
  • For frontboards between the ages of 20 and 25, the maximum amount is CHF 1,500.00 per year of study, according to Article 10 of the Law on Training Benefits of 1 January 2020.
  • For frontboards over the age of 25, the maximum amount is CHF 1'000.00 per year of study, according to Article 11 of the Training Benefits Act of 1 January 2020.

Application and documents required

To obtain the training allowance, the border worker must submit an application to the competent office of the Canton of Schaffhausen. The documents required for the application include:

  • A copy of your ID card
  • A copy of the birth certificate

Operational details

Key facts

  • The national system guarantees a minimum federal base amount, but allows the cantons to increase it independently.
  • The Canton of Schaffhausen has established one or more territorial funds responsible for examining applications, verifying requirements and providing the service.
  • The benefit is provided to border workers residing in the Canton of Schaffhausen.

Benefit Amounts

The family allowance is an economic benefit provided by the Canton of Schaffhausen to border workers residing in the territory. The federal base amount is CHF 350.00 for each member of the household, but the cantons can increase it independently. In the canton of Schaffhausen, the basic amount is CHF 420.00 for each member of the household.

According to the federal law of 27 September 2012, the Canton of Schaffhausen has decided to increase the basic amount from CHF 420.00 to CHF 600.00 for each member of the household. This means that a household of 4 people will receive a total amount of CHF 2,400.00.

Question and Requirements

To apply for the family allowance, border workers residing in the Canton of Schaffhausen must submit an application to the responsible territorial funds. The requirements for the request are:

  • Be a border worker resident in the Canton of Schaffhausen
  • Have a household with at least one member
  • Reside in the Canton of Schaffhausen for at least 12 months in the previous year
  • Have no other economic benefits provided by other authorities

Start with the facts: Canton Schaffhausen has a family allowance system that guarantees a minimum federal base amount.

Canton Schaffhausen is one of 26 Swiss cantons that offer a system of family allowances to support families with children. This system is regulated by the Swiss Federal Constitution, which states that every Swiss citizen is entitled to financial support for his or her family. The system of family allowances is managed by the Federal State and the cantons, which can establish their own amounts and access criteria.

Practical implications: Border workers residing in the Canton of Schaffhausen can benefit from a minimum federal base amount.

Border workers residing in the Canton of Schaffhausen can benefit from the minimum federal base amount provided for by law. This means that they can receive financial support for their family, regardless of their nationality. However, it is important to note that frontier workers must have resided in the Canton of Schaffhausen for at least one year to be eligible for this support.
