Costs and prices of nurseries in the Canton of Basel-City

Kindergartens in the Canton of Basel-City offer a variety of services for children from 0 to 12 years old. Prices are calculated based on the parents' income and may vary depending on the type of service and age group of the child. For example, a kindergarten for children under the age of 3 can cost around 1,200-1,500 Swiss francs per month, while a kindergarten for children between the ages of 3 and 6 can cost around 1,000-1,200 Swiss francs per month.

Types of services and prices

Kindergartens in the Canton of Basel-City offer different types of services, including:

  • Full-time nursery: 1,200-1,500 Swiss francs per month
  • Part-time nursery: 800-1,200 Swiss francs per month
  • Nursery for children with disabilities: 1,500-2,000 Swiss francs per month

Cantonal subsidies

Cantonal subsidies are available to help cover kindergarten costs. For example, the Canton of Basel-City offers a subsidy of 500 Swiss francs per month for each child enrolled in a time-based kindergarten

The Canton of Basel-City offers a wide range of services for families with children, including nurseries and day care. These services are available for children from 0 to 12 years old and are offered by cantonal and municipal administrations.

Types of services

*Nurseries: Nursery services are available for children aged 0-3 years. Kindergartens are open 5 days a week and offer personalised assistance to children. Prices for kindergartens range from CHF 800 to CHF 1,200 per month, depending on the parents' income.

  • Day Families: Day care services are available for children ages 3-12. Day families offer childcare during daylight hours while parents are at work. Prices for day families range from CHF 500 to CHF 1,000 per month, depending on the income of the parents.

Costs and Fees

  • Prices: Prices for daycare centers and families vary depending on the parents' income and type of service. Prices are calculated according to a tariff that has been established by the cantonal and municipal administrations.

Kindergarten prices in the Canton of Basel-City

Prices for kindergarten in the Canton of Basel-City vary according to the age group of the child and the type of service chosen. Here are some examples of real prices:

  • Nursery for children from 0 to 1 year: CHF 1'200-1'800 per month
  • Nursery for children from 1 to 3 years: CHF 1'000-1'600 per month
  • Nursery for children from 3 to 6 years: CHF 900-1,400 per month

The subsidies available to your child

The Canton of Basel-City offers several subsidies to help families cover the costs of kindergarten. Here are some examples:

  • Cantonal kindergarten allowance: up to CHF 1,200 per month
  • Municipal kindergarten allowance: up to CHF 800 per month
  • Subsidy for low-income families: up to CHF 1,000 per month

Operational checklist to apply for cantonal subsidy

To apply for cantonal subsidy, you must have the following documents:

  • Identity document
  • Income tax return
  • Birth certificate of the child
  • Asylum contract
