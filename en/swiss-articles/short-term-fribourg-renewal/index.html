L Fribourg permit: validity, extension and requirements (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 September 2026

The short-term L permit in the Canton of Fribourg is valid for a maximum of one year and can be renewed under specific conditions. Find out about duration, extension and constraints.

Context

In brief

  • L permit: maximum validity 1 year, renewable
  • Application at the Cantonal Office of Fribourg
  • Constraints related to the specific employer
  • Extension: conditions verified by the authorities

Key Facts

  • Type: Short-term permit for foreigners
  • Validity: Maximum 1 year for a single issue
  • Canton: Fribourg (local management and renewal)
  • Who: Foreign workers on temporary contracts
  • Action: Request before the start of work
  • Extension: Renewable if conditions persist
  • Limit: Cannot be extended indefinitely

The short-term L permit is an authorization document for foreigners who intend to stay and work in Switzerland for limited periods. In the Canton of Fribourg, as in the other Swiss cantons, this permit is regulated by the Federal Act on Foreigners and managed locally by the competent cantonal office.

The maximum duration of the L permit is one year. This period is set at federal level by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) and applies uniformly in all cantons, including Fribourg. In contrast to the B permit, which can be renewed under general conditions, the L permit is specifically designed for temporary stays and involves stricter constraints: in particular, the close link with the employer who initiated the application.

When L permission is needed

The L permit becomes mandatory if a foreigner wants to work in Switzerland on a fixed-term contract or for a temporary assignment. In the canton of Fribourg, the application must be submitted before the start of work. The cantonal authorities check whether the conditions laid down by federal law are met: availability of a job, adequacy of the salary to local standards, and the employer's ability to guarantee the expected employment conditions.

Operational details

Extension conditions in the canton of Fribourg

An extension of the L permit in the canton of Fribourg is possible, but it is not automatic. The cantonal authorities assess whether the original reasons for the application persist. If the foreign national continues to work for the same employer and the contract has been renewed, the extension may be granted for a further annual period.

However, the L permit cannot be extended indefinitely. After a few years of continuous residence (the precise limit varies according to cantonal and federal legislation), the permit must be converted into a B permit or the foreigner must return to the country of origin. This mechanism ensures that the L permit effectively remains an instrument for temporary stays, and does not become a hidden form of permanent residence.

Employer constraints

A key aspect of the L permit is the mandatory connection with the employer who initiated the application. The foreigner cannot freely change employment during the period of validity of the permit. If the employment relationship is terminated, the L permit holder loses the legal basis for residence and must leave Switzerland.

This constraint reflects the temporary and instrumental nature of the permit: it is not an independent residence, but an authorization conditional on the continuation of a specific employment relationship. In the Canton of Fribourg, as in all cantons, the authorities may order the revocation of the permit if a violation of this fundamental condition is found. During the stay with an L permit, the worker remains subject to the AVS/LPP contributions like any Swiss worker, guaranteeing social security coverage.

Key points

How to apply for an L permit in the Canton of Fribourg

The procedure for obtaining an L permit in the Canton of Fribourg follows a standardized federal process, with local management by the Cantonal Immigration Office. The main steps are:

1. Submission of the application: The employer or his/her advisor submits the application to the Cantonal Office of Fribourg before the foreigner enters Switzerland. The application must include a preliminary employment contract, the applicant's identity documents and educational qualifications, and an application form completed according to the instructions provided by the office.

2. Administrative verification: The cantonal authorities check that the employer is in good standing, that the workplace has not been published on the Swiss market on time, and that the proposed salary complies with local standards.

3. Decision: If the application is complete and compliant, the cantonal office issues an entry and residence permit for the specified duration (up to a maximum of one year).

4. Issuance of the permit: The foreigner receives the L permit at the federal level (managed by the SEM) and can start work.

Documents Required

Check with the Cantonal Office of Fribourg for the complete and up-to-date list. Generally, the following are required: signed employment contract, valid passport or identity document, certificates of competence and diplomas (if required by the employer), any criminal records from the country of origin, declaration from the employer on economic capacity.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does the L permit apply to all foreigners?
No. The L permit is intended for non-EU foreigners on temporary contracts and, in some cases, for EU citizens with short-term assignments. EU/EFTA citizens usually have access to more favourable conditions according to the Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons (AFMP). The precise rules depend on your nationality and the intended duration of your stay. Check your status with the Fribourg Cantonal Office.
How long can I extend my L permit in the canton of Fribourg?
The L permit is valid for a maximum of one year per issue and is renewable under conditions. However, it cannot be extended indefinitely. After 2-3 consecutive renewals (the exact limit varies), the authorities may require you to switch to another type of permit (B) or leave Switzerland. Consult the cantonal authorities for the applicable cumulative limit.
What happens if I change employer before my L permit expires?
Changing employers during the validity period of the L permit is normally prohibited. If the employment relationship is terminated, the permit loses its legal basis and must be revoked. If you wish to change jobs, you must apply for a new L permit or consider switching to a B permit if conditions allow. Notify the cantonal office immediately of any changes.
What is the difference between L and B permits in the canton of Fribourg?
The L permit is temporary (up to 1 year) and tied to the specific employer. The B permit is a residence, renewable and is not tied to a single employer: it allows for a certain job mobility. To obtain the B permit, you generally need a permanent employment contract or a proven stable situation. The B permit is the first step towards permanent rooting in Switzerland.
Can I go straight from L permit to C permit?
No, not directly. To obtain the C permit, you must accumulate at least 10 years of continuous legal residence in Switzerland (5 years if an EU/EFTA citizen). During this period, foreigners typically transition from L permit to B permit, then to C. The C permit cannot be applied for before completing the minimum period of residence and demonstrating stable integration.
