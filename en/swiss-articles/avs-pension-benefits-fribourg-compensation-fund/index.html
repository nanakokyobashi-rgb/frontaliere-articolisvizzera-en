AVS and complementary services in the canton of Fribourg: an income guide (cross-border guide)

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 24 August 2026

How the first federal pillar works in the canton of Freiburg: AVS rates, the role of the cantonal compensation fund, pension calculation and supplementary benefits for the living minimum.

Context

In Brief

  • AVS is the first federal pillar with a 5.3% contribution rate at the employee's expense (10.6% with employer)
  • The Fribourg cantonal compensation fund manages contributions, calculations, and local benefits
  • Complementary benefits ensure the minimum vital when the AVS pension is insufficient

Key Facts

  • What: Old-age and survivors' insurance (AVS) system, first Swiss federal pillar
  • Rate: 5.3% employee, 10.6% total with employer
  • Where: Administered at the cantonal level by compensation funds (Fribourg included)
  • Who: UFAS/BSV coordinates at the federal level; cantonal funds manage locally
  • Benefits: Complementary benefits provided by the canton and municipalities to ensure minimum vital

In the canton of Fribourg, as in the rest of Switzerland, the first pillar of pension insurance is guaranteed by the AVS system. This federal system is based on compulsory contributions: 5.3% is paid by the employee, while the employer contributes an additional 5.3%, for a total of 10.6%. Contributions paid during working life determine the monthly pension amount received from the time of retirement.

Local management is the responsibility of the Fribourg cantonal compensation fund, which collects contributions, verifies insurance continuity, and calculates benefits. The role of this institution is crucial for those who have worked in the canton: it maintains contributory records, manages pension requests, and coordinates the payment of complementary benefits.

Complementary benefits (PC) represent the second level of protection in the system: when the AVS pension does not cover the minimum vital (threshold set by the canton and municipalities), complementary benefits fill the gap. This mechanism ensures that no resident remains below a defined standard of living.

Operational details

Complementary services: when they intervene

Supplementary benefits are an essential safety net in the Swiss social security system. In the canton of Fribourg, as in other cantons, these benefits come into play when the AVS annuity alone is not enough to cover the living minimum. The minimum living threshold is defined at the municipal level, in coordination with the canton, and includes costs of food, housing, health and other basic needs.

To access supplementary benefits, you must submit a request to the competent office, usually the cantonal social assistance office or a delegated body. The procedure involves the detailed declaration of income, assets, family maintenance obligations and other personal circumstances. The cantonal authorities assess whether the claimant falls within the parameters to receive the integration.

Coordination between AVS and complementary services

A fundamental aspect is to understand how the coordinated system works. The AVS annuity and contribution gaps are not independent: PCs are calculated as a supplement to the AVS annuity to reach the living minimum. This means that an increase in AVS income (for example, by annual indexation) is reflected in a reduction in PCs.

How to request AVS pension in the canton of Fribourg: a practical procedure

The AVS pension request must be submitted to the Fribourg compensation fund at least three months before the planned retirement date. This advance notice allows the institution to verify the completeness of the contributory file and to prepare the decision in due time.

Step 1: Contact the Fribourg compensation fund via mail, phone, or counter and request the official application form.

Step 2: Fill out the form with personal data, AVS insurance number, date of birth, and salary history (if available in your personal records).

Step 3: Attach supporting documents: AVS registry extract, work certificates, current residence documents, recent income tax declaration (if requesting supplementary benefits).

Step 4: Send the complete file at least 3 months before the desired retirement date.

Step 5: The fund notifies the written decision with the monthly pension amount. If further verifications are necessary (e.g., gaps to be filled), the process may take up to 6 months.

Key deadlines

  • 3 months before retirement: official deadline for the request
  • Elaboration time: 2–6 weeks for simple cases, up to 6 months if further verifications are necessary
  • Start of payment: usually the month following the notification of the decision

Frequently Asked Questions
What if I have contribution gaps in my AVS file?
Each year without contributions reduces future income. In Switzerland, it is possible to partially fill the gaps by paying voluntary contributions up to 5 years before retirement. The cantonal compensation fund of Freiburg provides advice on how much to pay to compensate for missing periods and what the impact is on the total income.
How is the AVS annuity calculated in the canton of Freiburg?
The calculation follows a standard federal formula based on years of contribution (minimum 1 year, maximum 44 years) and revalued average annual income. The result is a personal monthly income. There is no fixed percentage: each profile is unique and depends on the individual contribution file and the insurance duration.
What are the complementary benefits and who can request them?
The complementary benefits supplement the AVS annuity up to the cantonal living minimum when insufficient. It may be requested by residents of Freiburg whose income (AVS + other income) does not reach the threshold defined by the canton and the municipality. The request must be submitted to the competent municipal or cantonal social assistance office.
When do I have to claim the AVS annuity in the canton of Freiburg?
At least 3 months before the expected retirement date. The request must be forwarded to the cantonal clearing house of Freiburg via official form. At least 3 months' notice allows the institution to verify the complete dossier and process the decision without delay.
Can I increase my future AVS income by paying contributions today?
Yes, within certain limits. If you have contribution gaps, you can make voluntary contributions up to 5 years before retirement. Each additional contribution year increases the future annuity. The cantonal clearing house provides personalised advice to calculate the impact of voluntary payments.
