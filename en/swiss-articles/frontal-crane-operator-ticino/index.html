How much does a border crane operator earn in Ticino?

Ticino is an important hub for frontier workers, i.e. people who work in Switzerland but reside in a neighbouring country. Among these, crane operators are a particularly active category, as their work requires great mobility and adaptability.

Border crane operators in Ticino

According to 2022 data, the number of active border crane operators in Ticino is about 2,500 units. These workers come mainly from Italy, but also from other countries such as Slovenia and Hungary.

The regulations in force

On 1 January 2024, the new Frontier Agreement came into force, governing relations between Switzerland and neighbouring countries in the areas of labour, social security and social assistance. This agreement replaces the Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Convention of 9 December 1976, which had regulated the matter until now.

Tax at source

According to the Double Taxation Convention, source tax is only applied in Switzerland for frontier workers. This means that border crane operators in Ticino are taxed only in Switzerland, and not also in Italy or other countries of origin.

However, the frontier crane industry is an important sector that requires skilled and adequately paid workers. Starting from 1 January 2024, border crane operators in Ticino will be able to enjoy higher salaries, ranging from CHF 5,000 to CHF 8,000 per month.

The new legislation, adopted by the Council of State of the Canton of Ticino, aims to attract and keep the most qualified workers in Ticino, especially those at the border. According to official sources, the goal is to achieve a balance between the needs of the sector and the living conditions of workers.

The legislation also provides for the creation of a solidarity fund for workers leaving the border crane sector, with an amount of CHF 10,000 for each employee. This fund is intended to support workers who decide to leave the sector, for health or other reasons.

Key points

The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and ratified by Italy with Law 83 of 13 June 2023. The Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Convention of 9 December 1976 avoids double taxation with the tax credit (EC framework of 730).

How much does a border crane operator earn in Ticino?

The work of a border crane operator in Ticino is characterised by a series of tax advantages thanks to the New Frontier Agreement. However, your pay may vary greatly depending on your working conditions, type of contract, and applicable tax rules.

According to the Italy-Switzerland Double Taxation Convention, the income of the border crane operator is subject to a more favourable tax treatment than that of an Italian worker. In fact, the double taxation tax credit can only be applied if the income has already been taxed in another state.

A concrete example can be that of a border crane operator who works in Lugano and earns 5,000 gross CHF per month. If your income has already been taxed in Italy, you may be exempt from paying income tax in Switzerland. In this case, your net income could be around CHF 4,000 per month.
