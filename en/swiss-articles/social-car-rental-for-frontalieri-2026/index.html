Social rent for border workers: 100 euros per month?

The Italian Government is ready to launch an innovative project to offer new cars to frontier workers at a monthly fee of 100 euros. This initiative is promoted by the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy (MIMIT) and the ACI, with the aim of replacing more polluting cars with more modern and safer vehicles.

In a nutshell

  • The project plans to offer new cars to frontier workers at a monthly fee of 100 euros.
  • The initiative is promoted by the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy (MIMIT) and the ACI.
  • The project is aimed at border workers who use cars to transport people or goods between Italy and Switzerland.

Key facts

  • What: social rental for border workers
  • When: 2026-2030

  • Amount: 100 euros per month

How the project works

The project plans to offer new cars to frontier workers at a monthly fee of 100 euros. This means that border crossers will be able to use new and modern cars to transport people or goods between Italy and Switzerland, without having to face the costs of buying or maintaining the car.

The project is funded by the Ministry of Business and Made in Italy (MIMIT) and the ACI, which have allocated an amount of 10 million euros for the project. This amount will be used to purchase new cars and to cover maintenance and maintenance costs

Operational details

The project plans to finance the experimental phase until 30 June 2030 with 50 million euros. The project was born with the aim of favouring the replacement of the most polluting cars and ensuring accessible mobility for families with fewer economic possibilities.

According to estimates, 70% of border families use a vehicle to get around, while 30% use public transport. This behaviour contributes to pollutant emissions and noise pollution. Therefore, the project aims to encourage the use of electric or hybrid vehicles, which guarantee more sustainable mobility.

The project aims to create a social rental system for border crossers, which allows them to use an electric or hybrid vehicle at an affordable cost. The rental price would be 100 euros per month, which includes technical assistance, insurance and vehicle maintenance.

The project plans to collaborate with the cantonal and municipal authorities to ensure the implementation of the project. The Canton of Ticino, for example, has already allocated 10 million francs to support the transition to sustainable mobility. The project also aims to involve private companies to support the implementation of the project.

Key points

The project plans to select one or a few car models that will be made available to the beneficiaries. Cars must meet certain technical requirements, such as belonging to at least the Euro 6 category, have CO₂ emissions not exceeding 135 g/km and have a maximum price of 14,800 euros plus VAT. The project plans to manage requests through an online procedure with the allocation of resources until the available funds are exhausted.

The measure was launched in 2022, in response to growing concerns about access to public transport for border crossers. According to data from the Federal Department of the Interior (DDI), in 2020, around 20,000 border crossers used public transport services in Switzerland, an increase of 15% compared to the previous year.

The cost of the social charter has been set at 100 euros per month, an amount that is considered reasonable for border workers working in Basel, Lausanne or Geneva. However, it is important to note that the cost may vary depending on the car model chosen and the time of use.

The project involves selecting cars that meet the technical requirements and are available for lease. Here are some examples of cars that could be selected:

  • Volkswagen Golf (Euro 6, 115 g/km, 13,500 euros plus VAT)
  • Renault Clio (Euro 6, 105 g/km, 12,000 euros plus VAT)
  • Peugeot 308 (Euro 6, 120 g/km, 14,000 euros plus VAT)
