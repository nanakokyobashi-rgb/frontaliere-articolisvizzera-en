Frequently Asked Questions
Where can I find CSR job offers?
Job vacancies can be consulted directly on the official portal of RSI Radiotelevisione svizzera di lingua italiana, accessible through the section dedicated to open positions and work.
Are there specific sections for those who are at the beginning of their career?
Yes, the portal includes a section called "Youth & Careers", specifically designed to offer support, guidance and information on professional opportunities reserved for those who are entering the world of work or wish to grow professionally.
How can I apply for an open position?
To apply, you must consult the “Application Process” section within the CSR site. Here are described the operating procedures, the necessary requirements and the steps to follow to correctly submit your professional profile to the attention of the institution.
