Changing health insurance provider can save up to 633 francs

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

According to Axa, the first switch saves an average of 563 francs per year, the next 383 francs; in Ticino, the initial saving reaches 633 francs.

Context

In brief

  • First switch: 563 francs per year on average
  • Subsequent switch: 383 francs per year on average
  • Ticino: 633 francs per year for the first switch

A few hours before the publication of the new premiums for 2027, whose announcement is expected tomorrow, Axa released an analysis this morning of the potential savings. According to the study, regularly switching insurer for mandatory basic insurance is more worthwhile in the long term than remaining with the same health insurer for a long time.

The research comes as the study forecasts a substantial increase for next year as well. The issue highlighted by Axa is the relationship between the first switch and the changes that follow, not only the initial choice of insurer.

With the first switch, policyholders save an average of 563 francs per year. In the event of a further switch, the indicated average saving is 383 francs per year. The figure is lower than that of the first switch, but the study still considers regularly switching worthwhile in the long term.

«Many policyholders believe that after a first switch, the potential for savings is practically exhausted», said Alexandra Gmür, head of health insurance provision at Axa, quoted in the statement released this morning.

Scope of the survey

The Keystone-ATS report situates the analysis in the hours preceding the publication of the 2027 premiums. The document, released by Axa, accompanies the wait for tomorrow's expected announcement with an estimate of the potential of repeated switches.

To conduct the study, Axa examined all switches in basic insurance made over the past four years by customers who also had supplementary insurance. The stated scope therefore includes both the first switch and subsequent ones, according to the distinction adopted to calculate the two average savings.

The group also specifies that it does not directly offer basic insurance. The analysis is thus presented as an assessment of the savings potential observed in the switches considered, and not as a proposal for an Axa basic insurance product.

For those who keep the same insurer for a long time, the source therefore points to possible savings left on the table; for those who have already switched, the review is not described as concluded. These are the two situations that Axa compares on the eve of the new announcement.

Operational details

Cantons, premiums and cost of living

The savings potential on the first change is particularly high in the cantons with the highest premiums. The values reported by Axa show how much the margin changes from one area to another:

For practical reading, the basic compulsory coverage is the KVG/KVG: the premiums are per capita, vary by canton or region and are not a tax or a salary contribution. For this reason the comparison touches on an item of the cost of living in Switzerland, while it remains separate from salary deductions.

Those who want to combine territorial values ​​with a dedicated tool can open the health insurance comparison and also consult the cost of living in Switzerland. The cantonal data offer a concrete reference to understand where the study finds the highest potential on the first pass.

First change and subsequent steps

However, the gap between the first change and the subsequent ones varies from canton to canton. In Zurich, people who change insurers for the first time save 61% more than those who make subsequent changes. In Ticino the difference between the first and subsequent exchange rates is 31%, while in Geneva it is 35%.

These percentages do not describe the same gap across Switzerland. Instead, they show how the first-step advantage, measured by AXA, can be more pronounced in some cantons than in others.

Age range shifts the result

The same variability emerges between age groups. People between 26 and 30 years old are those who benefit most from the exchange, with an average saving of 451 francs per year. Between the ages of 19 and 25 the savings are 436 francs, while for people over 51 the potential amounts to 417 francs.

For children and young people up to 18 years of age, the average savings indicated is 235 francs. Canton and age group thus become two practical coordinates for reading the results of the study together with one's insurance cost.

Key points

Put the comparison in your calendar

Axa recommends that policyholders note the comparison of premiums in their calendar every year. The immediate reference is the publication of the premiums for 2027, scheduled for tomorrow: the appointment can become the moment to reopen the comparison, without considering the savings exhausted after a single choice.

The operational sequence

1. Mark an annual time in your calendar dedicated to comparing premiums, as suggested by Axa. 2. When the new 2027 premiums are published, check the compulsory basic insurance again and the possibility of changing insurer. 3. Include in the same comparison the savings-oriented insurance models indicated in the source: family doctor, telemedicine and HMO. 4. Also assess alternative deductibles, without stopping at the name of the health insurer alone.

The first step should not be treated as a conclusive check. The same analysis also takes subsequent changes into consideration and links regular checking to long-term cost-effectiveness. The procedure indicated is therefore an annual habit, to be reopened when the new premium data arrive.

The scope of the study should be kept in mind when reading the results: Axa examined the changes made over the last four years by customers who also had supplementary insurance. The group also clarifies that it does not directly offer basic insurance. The reported figures therefore describe the analysis of the changes considered by Axa.

In the comparison, basic coverage remains the starting point. The alternatives mentioned concern the insurance model and the deductible; these are the items that the recommendation invites one to reconsider every year together with the premium. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the average savings at the first change of basic insurer according to the Axa study?
The first change of the basic compulsory insurer saves an average of CHF 563 per year. This value is a national average; in Ticino the average saving is even higher, equal to 633 francs per year, while in Geneva it reaches 697 francs. These data derive from Axa's analysis of the changes made in the last four years by customers with complementary insurance.
How much do you save on average by making one more change after the first one?
After the first step, a further change of the basic insurer generates an average saving of CHF 383 per year. Although this figure is lower than the first change, the study still considers it advantageous to change regularly over the long term, as savings accumulate year after year.
When will the 2027 premium announcement be published and what does Axa recommend to policyholders?
The announcement of the awards for 2027 is scheduled for tomorrow. Axa recommends that policyholders bookmark an annual premium comparison and use this time to recheck the basic compulsory insurance, also evaluating model alternatives (family doctor, telemedicine, HMO) and different deductibles, without considering the savings potential exhausted after a single choice.
