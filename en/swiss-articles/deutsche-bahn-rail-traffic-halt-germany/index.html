Context

In short - Deutsche Bahn rail traffic interrupted - All trains currently stopped in Germany - Problem with the digital radio system - DB technicians at work to solve ## Key facts - What: Total suspension of DB rail traffic - When: 23 June, confirmed in the evening - Where: Germany, at federal level - Who: Deutsche Bahn, DB spokesperson, DB technicians - Amount: not yet specified - Reason: Disturbance in the digital radio system - Status: All trains stopped at stations - Solution: DB technicians committed to restoring the situation On the evening of 23 June Deutsche Bahn (DB) confirmed a total suspension of rail traffic in Germany. According to the company's spokesperson to the DPA agency, all trains are currently stopped at the stations due to a disruption in the digital system for radio communications. The outage takes effect on a federal scale, blocking the movement of DB convoys throughout Germany. The exact cause of the blackout has not yet been specified. Deutsche Bahn technicians have been mobilized and are working intensively to resolve the issue. At the moment, it is not possible to predict when the situation will be normalized and rail traffic will be back in operation.
