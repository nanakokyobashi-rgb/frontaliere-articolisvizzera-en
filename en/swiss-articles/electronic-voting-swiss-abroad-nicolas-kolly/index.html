Frequently Asked Questions
Why does Nicolas Kolly support e-voting for Swiss Abroad?
Nicolas Kolly supports electronic voting for Swiss Abroad because he believes it is essential that Swiss Abroad participate more in voting and elections. However, it clarified that priority must be given initially to the population residing in the Confederation.
What is Kolly's position on Resident Priority?
Nicolas Kolly made it clear that the population residing in the Confederation must have priority in the electronic voting process. This approach reflects an explicit recognition of Switzerland's centrality as a nation for its resident citizens.
What is the role of the parliamentary intergroup for Swiss Abroad?
The parliamentary intergroup for Swiss Abroad, which has more than 60 parliamentarians, represents a support and coordination network for the interests of the diaspora, allowing a stronger voice in federal politics.
