Switzerland is a country known for its high quality of life and high costs of living. However, the cost of living varies greatly between the different Swiss cantons. Some cantons, such as Geneva and Zurich, are considered among the most expensive, while others, such as Ticino, are considered cheaper.

Rental expenditure is an important factor in determining the cost of living in each canton. For example, in Geneva the average cost of an apartment is about CHF 2,500 per month, while in Bern it is about CHF 1,800 per month.

In addition, transport and insurance are other important factors contributing to the cost of living in each canton.
