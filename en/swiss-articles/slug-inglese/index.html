What is the Swiss Third Pillar 3a?

Switzerland's Third Pillar 3a is a single payment system that allows Swiss citizens to reduce their direct federal tax. This system was introduced in the Canton of Basel and offers unique tax and deposit advantages.

How the Swiss Third Pillar 3a single deposit system works

The Swiss Third Pillar 3a single-payment system was introduced with the 22 June 2020 amendment of the Federal Law on Mass Investments (LIF) and the Federal Mass Insurance Ordinance (OAM). The system provides that contributions paid for supplementary pension are deductible from direct federal tax (FDI) up to a maximum of CHF 35,000 per year.

This means that Swiss citizens who pay supplementary pension contributions can reduce their direct federal tax by up to CHF 3,500 per year. It is important to note that this benefit is subject to specific conditions and limits.
