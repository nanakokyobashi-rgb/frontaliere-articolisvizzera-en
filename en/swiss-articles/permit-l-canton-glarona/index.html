Operational details

Short-term permit L in the Canton of Glarus: validity and extension

The short-term L permit is an important measure adopted by the Canton of Glarus to allow frontier workers to work in the cantonal territory for a limited period of time. This permit is granted if the undertaking or organisation applying for the permit is established in the Glarus Canton and if the frontier worker is able to demonstrate that he has a stable and regular employment in the Glarus Canton.

According to Article 12 of the Immigration Regulation of the Canton of Glarus (RICG), published on 15 June 2022, the short-term L permit is not renewable as it is a short-term permit. However, it is possible to obtain a long-term work permit if the frontier worker is able to prove that he has a stable and regular employment in the Glarus Canton for at least 6 months.

In order to obtain the short-term L permit, frontier workers must submit an application to the Immigration Office of the Canton of Glarus. The application must be submitted at least 3 months before the expiry date of the permit. Border workers must also provide documents proving their stable and regular employment in the Glarus Canton.

Documentation required

For the application for the short-term L permit, frontier workers must submit the following documents:

  • Employment contract signed by the employer
  • Certificate of registration with the Registry Office of the Canton of Glarus
  • Documentation relating to remuneration and contributions
  • Certificate of insurance against accidents at work
  • Documentation relating to the stay in the Canton of Glarus
