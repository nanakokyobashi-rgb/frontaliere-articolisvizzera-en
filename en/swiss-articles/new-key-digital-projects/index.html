New key projects for federal digital sovereignty

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 October 2026

On October 1, 2026, the Chancellor designated the digital sovereignty workstation and EasyGov 2.0 as key projects; NEPRO leaves the category. Currently, 22 key projects account for more than half of civilian IT spending.

On October 1, 2026, the Federal Chancellor decided, after consulting the Conference of Secretaries General, to manage the program for a workstation based on digital sovereignty and the EasyGov 2.0 project as key projects. The announcement was published on October 6, 2026, in Bern.

Initiatives concerned

The workstation program was launched at the beginning of September by the Federal Chancellery. Its stated aim is to reduce dependence on foreign providers. The project concerns all departments and is of great importance for strengthening the digital sovereignty of the Federal Administration.

EasyGov 2.0 is the project of the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs. The source attributes great strategic importance to it for the economy and the Confederation. Companies use EasyGov to easily access the services offered by numerous administrative units in all departments. The project creates the technical and organizational foundations needed for the platform's further stable development.

The change also concerns NEPRO, swisstopo's new production systems program. Following the scaling-down it underwent in March 2026 and the marked improvement in the risk situation, NEPRO will no longer be managed as a key project.

The announcement recalls the scope of the category. These are projects or programs of the Federal Administration in the area of digital transformation and IT that, because of their scope, strategic importance, complexity or associated risks, are of particular significance. For this reason, they require intensified management, coordination and oversight.

The Swiss Federal Audit Office conducts systematic audits of key projects, while responsibility for individual projects remains with the competent departments and administrative units. In accordance with the Digitalization Ordinance, it is the Federal Chancellor, after consulting the Conference of Secretaries General, who determines which initiatives fall within the category. There are currently 22 key projects, and their total volume exceeds half of the expenditure of the entire portfolio of ongoing IT projects in the civilian sector.

Operational details

What changes operationally

The transition to key-project status brings the two new projects under the intensified management, coordination and oversight regime provided for this category. The Swiss Federal Audit Office conducts systematic reviews. Responsibility, however, remains with the competent departments and administrative units: the classification strengthens oversight without replacing the project owners.

For a company using EasyGov, the focus is the platform’s technical and organizational framework. The source says that EasyGov provides access to the services of numerous administrative units across all departments and that the project is preparing further stable development. No new functions, new access methods, costs or deadlines for companies are announced. The governance decision must therefore be distinguished from any potential changes in use, which the communiqué does not describe.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the two new key projects designated on October 1, 2026?
The digital sovereignty-based workplace and SECO’s EasyGov 2.0 project have been included in the category of key projects. This classification entails an intensification of management, coordination and oversight by the Swiss Federal Audit Office, while operational responsibility remains assigned to the competent departments and administrative units.
What happens to the NEPRO program after the decision?
Following the downsizing it underwent in March 2026 and the marked improvement in the risk situation, NEPRO will no longer be managed as a key project. It is therefore leaving the regime of intensified controls, while remaining under the responsibility of swisstopo, without the additional oversight provided for key projects.
What is the weight of the key projects in the Confederation's civilian IT portfolio?
There are currently 22 key projects, and their combined volume exceeds half of the expenditure of the entire portfolio of ongoing IT projects in the civilian sector. This figure was stated in the chancellor’s press release published on 6 October 2026 in Bern.
