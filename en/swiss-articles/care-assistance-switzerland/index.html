Find out how care for the elderly and those in need of care works in Switzerland

Context

In short - Home care is fundamental in Switzerland - Cantons and Municipalities have key roles in the organisation - There are different forms of support for those in need of care ## Key facts - What: Home and institutional care - When: Required at any time - Where: Throughout the Swiss territory - Who: Elderly and people in need of care - Amount: Varies by canton and income Assistance to the elderly and people in need of care is a fundamental issue in Swiss society. Switzerland offers different forms of support, both at home and institutionally. The Cantons and Municipalities play a key role in the organisation and management of these services, such as in the Canton of Zurich, where an integrated home care system has been implemented. Swiss legislation provides for several options to ensure the quality of care and support for families caring for them, such as the Federal Act on Care for the Elderly of 2011. ### Forms of support Support can be provided at home or in dedicated facilities. Home care is often preferred as it allows people to stay in their family environment. For example, in Basel, home care organisations offer services ranging from the simple company to specialist medical care, at an average cost of CHF 40 per hour. Families can also benefit from subsidies

Operational details

Practical analysis Home care in Switzerland is mainly managed by private and public organisations. Costs can vary greatly depending on the Canton and the type of service requested. For example, in the Canton of Zurich, the hourly cost for home care can vary from 40 to 80 francs, while in the Canton of Bern it can vary from 35 to 70 francs. Some cantons offer financial contributions to families who care for their loved ones at home. It is essential to inquire at the competent cantonal offices to know the specific support possibilities. For example, the Canton of Geneva offers a monthly contribution of up to CHF 1,500 for families who care for their loved ones at home. ### Comparison between Cantons The Swiss Cantons have different approaches in organising care. For example, the Canton of Lucerne offers vouchers for care services, while the Canton of St. Gallen provides direct funding to families. This diversity reflects the specific needs of different regions. > “The diversity of care models reflects the complexity of regional needs” (Report of the Federal Office of Public Health, 2022). ### Concrete scenarios A family in need of home care can choose from several options. Action To request assistance in Switzerland, you need to contact the relevant cantonal office. The procedure may vary depending on the Canton of residence. For example, in the Canton of Zurich, you can apply online on the website of the Office for Health and Senior Citizens. Frequently Asked Questions
How do I request home care?
By contacting the competent cantonal office and providing the necessary information about your situation.
What are the costs of home care?
Costs vary depending on the canton and the type of service requested. It is advisable to inquire at the cantonal offices.
Can I get financial contributions for assistance?
Yes, some cantons offer financial contributions to families who care for their loved ones at home.
