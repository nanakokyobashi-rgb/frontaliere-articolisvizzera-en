SSR Survey on Federal Voting on September 27 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 September 2026

The SRG launched a new survey on the initiatives on the ballot on 27 September. Online participation open until 23.00 next Wednesday.

Context

In brief

  • Participate in the 2nd SSR survey on the votes of September 27.
  • Online survey open until 11:00 PM next Wednesday.
  • Data collection managed by the polling institute gfs.bern.

Key facts

  • What: Second SSR survey on federal initiatives.
  • When: Deadline next Wednesday at 11:00 PM.
  • Where: Online via SSR link.
  • Who: Polling institute gfs.bern.
  • Topic: Initiative on neutrality and initiative on food.

The Swiss electorate is preparing to vote on the two initiatives submitted to the popular vote on September 27. Less than a month before the polling day, envelopes containing the informative material are reaching households throughout the country. The SSR, through its channels, has launched a new survey to delve into the citizens' orientations on these two specific topics. Following a first survey conducted about two weeks ago, the new inquiry aims to measure the evolution of opinions and to better understand the reasons driving voters to support or oppose the proposals under examination. Participation is open to all eligible citizens. To contribute, it is necessary to access the dedicated link published by the SSR and respond to the questionnaire prepared by the polling institute gfs.bern. The entire procedure is designed to take only a few minutes and guarantees maximum anonymity for anyone who decides to take part in the survey. The final results will be subsequently presented and analyzed in detail by the various SSR channels, thus providing an updated overview of national political trends ahead of the consultation. Participating in this type of survey represents a way to contribute to the public debate, especially at a time when the election campaign is heating up. It is essential to emphasize that the data collection is aimed at providing a clear picture of voting intentions on issues of federal relevance, without directly influencing the final outcome. Transparency and anonymity are central pillars of this process, aimed at ensuring that public opinion is accurately represented by public service media. We invite citizens to consult the available information to better understand the voting objects before expressing their preference, thus ensuring an informed and conscious participation in the next national referendum.

Operational details

Analysis of Electoral Trends

The approaching date of September 27 marks a crucial moment for Swiss politics. The dynamics of electoral campaigns often reflect in opinion polls, which serve as a thermometer to measure the pulse of the country. Analyzing the motivations behind voters' choices allows us to understand the real concerns of the population, ranging from foreign policy to food security. The comparison between the first survey, conducted about fifteen days ago, and this second inquiry will allow us to observe any shifts in consensus. Often, the final phase of the campaign sees an increase in interest from voters as the voting material is examined and the positions of the various parties become clearer. It is precisely during this period that individual reflection translates into a considered decision, capable of influencing the future structure of the country. In a context where Swiss neutrality remains a central theme in institutional debate, the consultation of September 27 assumes a symbolic and practical importance of primary importance. Similarly, issues related to food touch on daily aspects that impact the life of every resident. The poll promoted by SSR does not limit itself to collecting simple 'yes' or 'no' answers but seeks to capture the nuances of thought that characterize the different linguistic and social regions of Switzerland. For those who live or work in the country, understanding these trends means having a broader vision of the directions in which federal politics is moving. The implications of such votes can extend far beyond the single electoral act, influencing future decisions of the Federal Council and the priorities of Parliament. Participation in such polls, managed by professional entities like gfs.bern, ensures that the data collected is scientifically sound and representative. Comparing one's opinions with general trends allows citizens to feel part of a living and dynamic democratic process, where the voice of each contributes to defining the national political agenda for the years to come.

Key points

Guide to participating in the survey

To actively participate in the statistical survey proposed by SSR, you need to follow a simple and linear procedure. The first step is to connect to the portal indicated on the RSI website, where there is a direct link to the online questionnaire. Once logged in, the user will be guided through the questions formulated by the demoscopic institute gfs.bern. It is essential to remember that the deadline for completion is set for 11:00 PM next Wednesday. Responses sent after this time will not be accepted, as the data processing process must start promptly to allow the publication of the results. Make sure you have at hand, if necessary, the information related to your voting material, although the survey focuses mainly on your judgment and personal motivations. Participation requires only a few minutes and, as reiterated, is completely anonymous. No sensitive data that could lead to the identity of the participant is required. Once the survey is completed, there is nothing else to do: the data will be centrally processed and the results will be disseminated by SSR in the coming weeks. For those who wish to deepen the topics covered, the advice is to regularly monitor the pages dedicated to national political information, where updates and necessary insights will be published. We remind you that, beyond the outcome of the survey, the fundamental act remains that of September 27, when each citizen can express their final vote in the ways provided by law. For those who wish to monitor the cost of living or the economic impact of political decisions on their paycheck, various online tools are available, such as the salary calculator, which allows you to analyze your personal finances in detail in relation to the Swiss economic context. Conscious participation is the best exercise of active citizenship possible.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the topics covered by the SSR survey?
The survey promoted by the SSR focuses on the two federal initiatives submitted to the popular vote on September 27: the neutrality initiative and the food initiative. The survey aims to measure the evolution of citizens' opinions compared to an initial survey carried out two weeks ago, trying to understand the reasons that drive voters to support or oppose such proposals in view of the official consultation.
How can I participate in the survey?
Participation is open to all citizens with voting rights. Simply access the dedicated link published by the SRH and answer the questionnaire prepared by the demoscopic institute gfs.bern. The procedure is designed to take a few minutes and guarantees the total anonymity of the participants, as no sensitive data is requested that can be traced back to the identity of the individual user.
What is the deadline for completing the survey?
The deadline for completing the online questionnaire is 23.00 next Wednesday. After this time it will no longer be possible to send their responses, as the gfs.bern institute will start the timely processing of the data to allow the subsequent presentation of the results by the SSR in the weeks before the election appointment on September 27.
