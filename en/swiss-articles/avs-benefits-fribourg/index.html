In the Swiss pension landscape, the first pillar represents the fundamental basis for financial security in old age and survivors. Within the Canton of Freiburg, the operational management of insurance positions and contributions is the responsibility of the cantonal compensation fund. This entity interfaces directly with workers, employers and residents to ensure the correct application of the federal regulations established regarding AVS, AI and IPG. The local administrative structure also coordinates claims and payments, ensuring that every citizen or resident can rely on a territorial reference point for their social security situation.

Operational details

The calculation of the AVS annuity is based on the years of contribution accumulated and the average income achieved during working life. In the Canton of Freiburg, as in the rest of Switzerland, the presence of contribution gaps deriving from periods not covered by payments can significantly reduce the amount of the monthly income to which one is entitled once the reference age has been reached. Regularly checking your individual statement at the clearinghouse allows you to promptly identify any missing periods and evaluate retroactive payments allowed by federal law within the established deadlines.

Complementary services and minimum life insurance coverage

When the AVS or AI pension is not sufficient to cover the essential expenses for daily life, the Swiss system provides access to supplementary benefits. In the Canton of Freiburg, this support instrument is administered in close cooperation with the competent cantonal bodies to ensure that every indigent or income-limited resident reaches the minimum living standards guaranteed by law. The supplementary benefits cover the difference between the expenses recognized and the insured's computable revenues, also including the costs related to sickness and disability expenses recognized by current legislation. Key points

To proceed with the claim for benefits or to regularize your contributory position in the Canton of Freiburg, you must follow a structured and strict procedure. The first step is to contact the cantonal clearing house of Freiburg directly to request the statement of the individual AVS account, verifying the absence of contribution gaps. In the presence of periods not covered, it is necessary to collect the documentation certifying the work activity or the periods of study and residence in Switzerland to evaluate the recovery of the contributions within the time limits provided for by federal legislation.

In the event that the monthly income does not cover primary needs, the application for supplementary benefits must be submitted using the official forms made available by the cantonal administration. The request must be accompanied by all tax receipts, income certificates, rental expenses and LAMal compulsory health insurance policies. After forwarding, the competent office proceeds to verify the income and capital requirements, issuing a formal decision that establishes the monthly amount of the supplementary subsidy. Frequently Asked Questions
Who manages the AVS in the Canton of Freiburg?
The operational management is entrusted to the compensation fund of the Canton of Freiburg, which applies the federal provisions established on social security by the UFAS.
What are complementary services in the Canton of Freiburg?
These services are intended to cover the difference between recognised expenses and computable revenues, guaranteeing the minimum life for those who receive an insufficient AVS or AI income.
How are AVS contribution gaps addressed?
Gaps are addressed by requesting the individual account statement from the compensation fund and verifying the possibility of making retroactive payments within the terms established by federal law.
