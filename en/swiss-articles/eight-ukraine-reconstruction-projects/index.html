Ukraine: eight Swiss projects worth 135 million

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 October 2026

The Federal Council allocates 135 million francs to eight projects by Swiss companies in Ukraine, spanning energy, public transport and water resources.

On 2 October 2026, the Federal Council approved eight projects by Swiss companies in Bern to support reconstruction in Ukraine. The decision is part of the national programme for Ukraine and provides for funding of CHF 135 million for initiatives in the areas of energy, public transport and water.

The measure is part of a program that already finances projects by Swiss companies to strengthen the resilience of Ukraine, described in the press release as a war-torn country. The logic indicated is operational: to make Swiss products and technical know-how available to meet urgent needs.

A selection for Swiss companies

The projects were selected through a competitive procedure. Swiss companies with a branch in Ukraine were invited to submit proposals with a budget of between CHF 3 million and CHF 30 million. The initiatives had to respond to an urgent need for support from Ukraine and be implemented by local companies.

The evaluation followed criteria established in advance by both parties: relevance, impact on development, technical feasibility, risk, resource efficiency and Swissness. For the selected projects, the companies will also have to cover at least 10 percent of the costs from their own resources.

The final selection took place in collaboration with the Ukrainian government, based on the country's needs. The press release refers to an attached table with the list of the eight projects, without indicating their names in the text.

Operational details

The practical significance for the Swiss economy focuses on the relationship between the federal decision, Swiss companies and activities carried out on site. The scope indicated by the press release is precise: the procedure concerned Swiss companies with a branch in Ukraine, committed to submitting projects for urgent needs.

The before and after are just as clear. The national programme for Ukraine already financed initiatives by Swiss companies; the decision of 2 October 2026 approved eight further projects. The funding thus links Swiss products and technical know-how to interventions in the energy, public transport and water sectors.

Key points

In order to read the decision from the operational side, the procedure described in the press release can be transformed into a verification grid for companies. This is not a procedure aimed at private individuals: the recipients of the selection were Swiss companies with a branch in Ukraine.

The Operational Grid

1. Check the business requirement: The proposal had to come from a Swiss company with a branch in Ukraine. 2. Link the project to an urgent need for support from Ukraine, in the areas of energy, public transport or water resources. 3. Budget between CHF 3 million and CHF 30 million and indicate that the company will implement it locally. 4. Consider the equity ratio from the outset: For selected projects, companies will have to cover at least 10 percent of the costs.

In addition to this initial review, the qualitative grid used in the selection process is used. The proposals were evaluated according to relevance, impact on development, technical feasibility, risk, resource efficiency and Swissness. These are therefore the points that distinguish the structure of the procedure described by the source.

The final decision was made in collaboration with the Ukrainian government and on the basis of the country's needs. For a company interested in the topic, this means comparing the proposed activity with local needs and the ability to carry it out directly in Ukraine. For those who observe the Swiss labor market, the operational reference remains the section dedicated to aziende che assumono, without attributing employment numbers to the projects not indicated in the press release.
