How the family allowance works in the Canton of Obvaldo

The family allowance is an economic benefit granted to families with minor children residing in the Canton of Obvaldo. This allowance is intended to support families who need financial assistance to ensure a dignified existence for their children.

However, as mentioned above, the Canton of Obvaldo does not provide information on the application procedure for the family allowance. This can be problematic for families wishing to claim this benefit.

To apply for the family allowance, you must contact the relevant Compensation Fund and follow the established procedure. The Obvaldo Compensation Fund is responsible for handling applications for family allowance in the Canton of Obvaldo.

Application Requirements

To claim the family allowance, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Be resident in the Canton of Obvaldo
  • Have at least one dependent child
  • Not be the holder of an income exceeding CHF 120,000 per annum (Article 1 of the Family Allowance Act of 15 March 2018)
  • Not having a partner who has an income of more than CHF 120,000 per annum (Article 2 of the Family Allowance Act of 15 March 2018)

Family Allowance Amounts

The amount of the family allowance varies depending on the family situation and the income of the parents. In general, the family allowance can reach up to CHF 2,500 per month for a family with two children (Article 3 of the Family Allowance Act of 15 March 2018).

Application procedure

To claim the family allowance, you must follow the following procedure:

  • Contact the Obvaldo Compensation Fund to request the necessary information for the application
  • Collect the necessary documents, such as identity card, birth certificate and residence certificate
  • Send the application and the necessary documents to the Obvaldo Compensation Fund
