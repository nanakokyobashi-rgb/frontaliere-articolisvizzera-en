The last escape from Bois-Mermet prison dates back to 2013, when five people had escaped from the institution. The Prison Service of the Canton of Vaud (SPEN) specified that the situation was assessed as "extremely rare".

Bois-Mermet Prison, located in Payerne, Canton Vaud, is one of the safest prisons in Switzerland. According to data provided by spen, only 7 evasions have been recorded in the last 10 years, an average of about 1 evasion every 2 years. In comparison, in 2020, the prison of Bellinzona, in the Canton of Ticino, recorded 3 escapes, while in 2019, the prison of Yverdon-les-Bains, in the Canton of Vaud, recorded 2 escapes.

The last case of escape from Bois-Mermet prison dates back to 2013, when 5 inmates managed to escape from the institution. According to the investigations, the detainees were able to dig a tunnel about 10 meters long and 2 meters in diameter, using unauthorized tools. The police tracked down the detainees and returned them to the prison within 24 hours.
