Freiburg Transport Subscriptions: Zones and Rates (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 August 2026

How fare zones, half-price, AG and student discounts work in the transport subscriptions of the Canton of Fribourg.

Context

In brief

  • Fribourg integrates the national system (Half-Fare, GA) with local fare zones
  • Monthly and annual subscriptions are available, combinable with federal discounts
  • Students and apprentices have access to specific discounts

Key facts

  • What: Transport subscriptions with fare zone systems
  • Where: Canton of Fribourg, Switzerland
  • Who: Cantonal transport operators; Half-Fare and GA managed federally
  • When: Subscriptions renewable annually
  • Availability: Monthly and annual, with discounts for categories

The Canton of Fribourg organizes public transport through a fare zone system integrated with federal options: the Half-Fare travelcard, which offers a 50% discount on rail travel throughout Switzerland, and the GA (General Abonnement 365-day pass for unlimited national transit). This system makes it easy to choose between annual, monthly, and daily subscriptions for those commuting daily within the cantonal territory.

Fare zones operate on a simple principle: the subscription price increases with the number of zones covered. In Switzerland, each canton structures its own zone scheme — a standardized national mechanism that facilitates inter-cantonal mobility. In the Canton of Fribourg, as in other Swiss cantons, zones allow passengers to choose the geographic coverage suited to their needs, from urban to regional travel.

Half-Fare and GA: the federal options

The Half-Fare travelcard is a federal tool managed by the national railway system and accepted by numerous regional operators: it allows for a fixed 50% discount on tickets and subscriptions for the whole of Switzerland. The GA, complementarily, covers access to public transport throughout the nation for 365 consecutive days — a solution for those who travel frequently beyond cantonal borders. Operational details

Monthly and annual subscriptions

The structural difference between monthly and annual subscriptions reflects different mobility choices. An annual subscription is cost-effective for those who use public transport daily and predictably; a monthly one offers flexibility to those who alternate between office and remote work, or to those who frequently change workplace or residence.

In Switzerland, many cantons — including Fribourg — allow you to subscribe to passes locked for specific periods (12 months, 6 months, or single months), often with progressive rates: the longer the commitment, the lower the average daily cost. Some residents calculate the break-even point by choosing to purchase monthly subscriptions only for months of intense use (e.g., winter with more travel), alternating with single tickets during intermediate seasons.

Discounts for students and apprentices

A discount verified at the Swiss level concerns students and apprentices enrolled in recognized programs (upper secondary schools, vocational training, universities). The reduction varies by canton but is generally significant — sometimes 25-50% off the cost of an annual subscription. In the Canton of Fribourg, as in other cantons, this discount is designed to support the mobility of young people towards schools and internship locations, recognizing the importance of education for society.

To access the discount, simply present a valid enrollment document (student card, apprenticeship certificate) at an authorized point of sale or during the online subscription process. Documentation varies slightly by canton, but local operators provide updated checklists on their websites.

Key points

How to purchase and where to find information

Purchasing a transport subscription in the Canton of Fribourg follows three main channels, each with specific advantages:

1. Physical points of sale: Major train stations and authorized shops sell subscriptions; staff can provide advice on choosing the optimal zone and duration. This channel is ideal for those who want immediate clarification or prefer to sign up in person.

2. Online: Many cantonal operators offer digital platforms where you can compare prices and sign up remotely. An identity document and a payment method are sufficient to complete the registration. The digital subscription is activated within a few hours, reducing waiting times.

3. Mobile App: Various Swiss operators, including some active in Fribourg, provide apps to purchase subscriptions directly on your smartphone, with optional automatic renewal. Apps also allow you to monitor usage, deadlines, and tariff changes in real time.

Practical checklist before purchasing

To choose the most cost-effective subscription:

  • Determine how many zones you cover in your regular commutes (ask at the point of sale or count the stops)
  • Check if you are eligible for discounts for students, apprentices, or special categories (over 65, disabled, young families)
  • Compare the cost of an annual subscription with that of a monthly one multiplied by 12
  • Calculate if a Half-Fare travelcard or an integrated GA travelcard suits your travel profile (days of use × average price per trip)
  • Ask for information on seasonal promotions or trial periods (some cantons offer discounted trial subscriptions)

Frequently Asked Questions
What are zones in Swiss transport?
Zones are geographic areas (typically groups of stops around urban centers) that determine the price of a ticket or subscription. The more areas you cover, the higher the final cost. In the Canton of Fribourg, as in other Swiss cantons, the passer-by can choose to cover only his own city (1 zone), the district (2-3 zones), or the entire province (multiple zones). This system encourages targeted purchasing and significantly reduces costs for short-haul users, promoting sustainable mobility.
How does half-price work?
Half-price is a federal discount of 50% applied to individual train tickets and subscriptions throughout Switzerland. It is operated by the Swiss Railway Company (SBB/CFF/FFS) and accepted on numerous regional and municipal networks. In the Canton of Fribourg, the half-price reduces both individual tickets and local subscriptions, if combined. The annual cost is modest and recovers quickly with regular, even sporadic use.
Who is eligible for apprenticeship discounts?
Apprentices formally enrolled in a recognised vocational training course are entitled to reductions on transport passes in the canton where they work and study. An apprenticeship certificate or identification card issued by the training institution must be presented at an authorized point of sale or during online enrollment. The reduction varies by canton, but is generally 25-50% off the standard rate.
Can I combine multiple discounts at the same time?
Generally, discounts can be combined, but not always unlimited. For example, you can often combine half-price with a regional subscription and a student discount, but the exact rules vary by canton and operator. For clarification on the specific compatibility, please contact the physical point of sale or the official online platform of the Canton of Fribourg, where you will find up-to-date information on the cumulative allowances.
When is the right time to renew your subscription?
An annual subscription renews automatically (if subscribed with continuous renewal) or manually on the expiry day. A monthly subscription starts on the 1st of each month and expires on the last day. It is advisable to renew a few days before the deadline to avoid service interruptions; most online platforms send reminders by email.
