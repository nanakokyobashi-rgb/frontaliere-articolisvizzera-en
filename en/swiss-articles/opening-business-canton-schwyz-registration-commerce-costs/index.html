Practical Implications

Opening a business in the Canton of Svitto has several practical implications. The choice of the right legal form depends on the needs of the company and the laws of the Canton of Schwyz.

SRL is a form of company in which the shareholders are responsible for the company's debts up to a specific amount. The SA, on the other hand, is a form of company in which the shareholders are not responsible for the company's debts.

Registration in the commercial register is required for all commercial activities. Registration in the Trade Register requires the submission of a number of documents, including the Certificate of Incorporation, the Articles of Association and the Share Register.

The minimum capital required varies depending on the legal form chosen. The SRL requires a minimum capital of CHF 10,000, while the SA requires a minimum capital of CHF 50,000.

Registration fees vary by canton. In the Canton of Schwyz, the registration fee for business activities is 2% of the share capital.

The insurance obligations are different depending on the legal form chosen. The SRL requires insurance against commercial risks, while the SA requires insurance against financial risks.
