Donald Trump again advocates the permanent green light then legal in the United States. The US president wrote directly on the Truth platform to urge a timely intervention by the Federal Senate. In his public statements spread online, the political leader expressed a firm stance against the custom of moving the hands of the clock back and forth throughout the year. The issue directly concerns the functioning of federal legislative institutions in Washington and the ongoing debate about the future of time management in the country. The bill at the center of the discussion is known as the No More Changing the Clocks Act, a legislative measure aimed at definitively eliminating the practice of double annual time variation. According to the President's official communications, the primary goal is to avoid all the disruption and operational chaos that inevitably results from the semi-annual switch between daylight saving time and daylight saving time.

Frequently Asked Questions
What exactly does the Sunshine Protection Act provide for?
The Sunshine Protection Act aims to end the semi-annual shift between Daylight Saving Time and Daylight Saving Time, making Daylight Saving Time permanent throughout the United States.
What are the motivations expressed by Donald Trump?
The president wrote in Truth that changing clocks and hands twice a year brings discomfort and chaos, while having more sunlight in the afternoon hours also offers advantages for fighting crime.
At what stage is the legislative process of the measure?
The legislative measure has already been voted and approved by the House of Representatives and is currently awaiting final approval by the Federal Senate.
