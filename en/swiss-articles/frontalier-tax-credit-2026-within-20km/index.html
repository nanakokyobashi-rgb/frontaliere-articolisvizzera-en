For Article 9 of the Agreement, “old frontier workers” are people who met the tax requirements and worked in Graubünden, Ticino or Valais on 17 July 2023 or during the period from 31 December 2018 through 17 July 2023. Frequently Asked Questions
Where is the tax credit required for frontier workers?
The tax credit to avoid double taxation between Switzerland and Italy is requested by filling in the EC framework of the tax return (form 730) in Italy. The tax at source is withheld in Switzerland, while Italy grants the credit to offset the IRPEF due.
Which income does the €10,000 limit apply to?
Article 9’s transitional regime applies only to workers who meet the Agreement’s definition and worked in Graubünden, Ticino or Valais between 31 December 2018 and 17 July 2023; covered employment income is taxable exclusively in Switzerland. The ordinary regime applies in other cases, with Italian taxation and credit for Swiss tax where applicable. The €10,000 in Article 4 of Law 83/2023 is only the limit for income referred to in Article 1(175) of Law 147/2013, not a general allowance. What are the IRPEF rates applicable to border workers in 2026?
The Italian personal income tax rates applied to income (net of deductibles) are: 23% for income up to 28,000 euros, 33% for the range between 28,001 and 50,000 euros, and 43% for the part of income that exceeds 50,000 euros.
