In August, the Municipality of Como issued 1.259 fines in 31 days, for a total of 102.738 euros. The figure is accompanied by the daily amount of 3.322 euros. The penalties were recorded through the work of municipal police officers and auxiliary personnel.

The figure recorded by the Municipality

The text presents the figure within Como's tourism context. Hotel stays are one of the indicators mentioned; the other is the number and amount of fines issued to vehicles with foreign license plates, which usually go unpaid. The reports concern cars left in unacceptable spots and positions. The phenomenon is not limited to summer: cases have also been reported outside the summer season.

Among the foreign license plates, the source specifically mentions Swiss ones. For Swiss motorists in Como, the connection is therefore tangible: the issue is the parking of a vehicle with a foreign license plate and the collection of the municipal fine. The issue is described as longstanding and linked to the difficulty of cross-border collection, as well as the high costs of pursuing offenders.

Over time, many fines issued to vehicles with foreign license plates, especially Swiss ones, have remained unpaid within the prescribed deadlines. Available estimates indicate that the share of ignored notices exceeds 80%. This results in a difference between the amount of fines issued and the amount actually collected, according to the framework outlined by the source.

This difficulty is also reflected in the municipal resolution:

The resolution provides for an allocation to the allowance for doubtful debts for receivables of doubtful and difficult collection.

Operational details

The August figure should be read on two distinct levels: the ability to issue tickets and the ability to collect them when the vehicle has a foreign license plate. The first level concerns the issuance of fines; the second concerns their payment. The gap between the two is the most concrete point for anyone driving with a Swiss license plate in Como: the number recorded by the Municipality measures enforcement activity, while estimates of non-collection show the subsequent problem.
