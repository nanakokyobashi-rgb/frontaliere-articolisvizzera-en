Key points

The drought that is affecting the country is creating significant problems for Swiss farmers, who are facing economic and environmental difficulties. To help these struggling companies, the food distribution company Migros has decided to allocate two million francs to support farmers.

The aid will be managed through the Bucher Gossweiler-Stiftung, close to the Swiss Union of Peasants (USC). The allocation of funds will be decided by the Board of Trustees. The farms concerned may submit applications according to the criteria and procedure that will be published on 31 August.

The drought is particularly affecting the Canton of Ticino, where summer temperatures have been higher than normal, causing a sharp reduction in rainfall. According to data from the Swiss Meteorological Service, the Canton of Ticino recorded a 30% reduction in rainfall compared to the same period last year.

The situation is also critical in the Canton of Graubünden, where drought is causing significant problems for farmers. According to data from the Federal Office of Agriculture, the Canton of Graubünden recorded a reduction in rainfall of 25% compared to the same period last year.
