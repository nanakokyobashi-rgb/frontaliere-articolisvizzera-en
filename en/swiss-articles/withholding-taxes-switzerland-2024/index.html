Frequently Asked Questions
Who is subject to source tax in Switzerland?
Tax at source applies to workers resident abroad who carry out a gainful activity in Switzerland and to foreign workers resident in Switzerland who do not have a residence permit C, according to the criteria established by federal and cantonal tax laws.
How are the rates applied?
Tax rates and tariffs at source are established by federal and cantonal statutory provisions and are applied directly by the employer at the time of payment of the monthly salary.
Is it possible to request a correction of the taxation applied?
Yes, in the presence of particular conditions or changes in personal and financial data, taxpayers can submit a request for rectification or evaluate the subsequent ordinary taxation procedure within the established terms.
