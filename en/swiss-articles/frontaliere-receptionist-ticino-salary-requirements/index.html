Requirements for working as a receptionist in Ticino

To work as a receptionist in Ticino, it is necessary to meet the following requirements:

  • Be a cross-border worker with a valid residence permit.
  • Have a high school diploma or equivalent training course.
  • Have at least one year of work experience in the services sector.
  • Know Italian and, possibly, German or French.
  • Be able to work in a multicultural environment and communicate effectively with customers.
