Context

The situation in Ukraine had a significant impact on the Swiss economy. The DEFR decided to amend Annexes 2 and 14a of the ordinance to reflect the new economic and political realities. These measures have been taken to ensure the stability and security of the Swiss economy.

Operational details

Ukraine: Measures Ordinance

Amendments to Annexes 2 and 14a of the Ordinance could have practical implications for residents and border guards in Switzerland. It is important to check the new measures and understand how they apply to your circumstances.

The protection measures for Ukrainian citizens in Switzerland have been modified to adapt to the new needs. The new Ordinance, which entered into force on 1 January 2024, introduces amendments to Annexes 2 and 14a of the Ordinance on Protection Measures for Ukrainian Citizens.

The changes mainly concern the definition of “Ukrainian citizen” and the conditions for access to the right of residence. Ukrainian citizens who have been forced to leave the country due to the war are considered “Ukrainian citizens” and can benefit from the protection measures.

According to Article 3 of the ordinance, Ukrainian citizens who have been forced to leave the country due to the war can benefit from the right of residence if they have a minimum income of 4,000 Swiss francs per month. This means that Ukrainian citizens who have been forced to leave the country due to the war and have a minimum income of 4,000 Swiss francs per month can benefit from the right of residence and can stay in Switzerland for a period of three years.
