Coop reaches 1'000 supermarkets in Switzerland

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 30 September 2026

Tomorrow, Coop’s 1,000th store will open in Emmen: 2,500 m². CEO Philipp Wyss announces new store openings and highlights the group’s leadership position in Ticino.

Context

In brief

  • Coop will open its thousandth supermarket in Emmen tomorrow
  • The new store measures 2'500 square meters
  • The network covers about 1,1 million square meters
  • In Ticino, Coop describes itself as the market leader

Key facts

  • Milestone → 1'000 supermarkets
  • Opening → tomorrow
  • Location → Emmen, canton of Lucerne
  • Area → 2'500 square meters
  • Investments over ten years → about 3,5 billion francs

Tomorrow, in Emmen in the canton of Lucerne, Coop will open the thousandth store in its supermarket network. The new store will have an area of 2'500 square meters and will bring the cooperative to the milestone of 1'000 supermarkets in Switzerland.

An expansion measured in stores and floor space

The milestone comes after ten years during which Coop opened 124 new branches and invested about 3,5 billion francs in renovating its stores. Overall, the stores occupy about 1,1 million square meters of retail space. The figure therefore combines new openings and work on existing stores.

The network is spread across very different settings. It starts in city centers, passes through villages and reaches mountain locations. Coop cites Bettmeralp, in Valais, at an altitude of 1'948 meters, as an example of its presence at high altitude. This example shows that the network includes urban locations, small centers and Alpine destinations.

The next chapter of growth

For CEO Philipp Wyss, the thousandth store does not mark the end of the group's growth. In his assessment, the potential remains mainly in Central and Eastern Switzerland, as well as in Geneva and Lausanne. Wyss also described Coop's position in some of Switzerland's main cities:

«In Zurich and Basel we are extremely strong, and in Ticino we are the market leader».

After reaching the Emmen milestone, the group will continue opening new stores, the CEO announced. The announcement therefore links tomorrow's opening to the intention to continue opening stores in the locations indicated by Wyss. Coop is also focusing on integrating traditional and online retail, with the option of ordering groceries on Coop.ch and having them delivered to a store in their holiday destination.

Operational details

A Comparison with Migros

The size of the chain becomes clearer when considering both the number of stores and the retail space. At the end of 2025, Migros had 659 standalone supermarkets, with just under 1.06 million square meters of retail space. Coop now has 1,000 supermarkets and approximately 1.1 million square meters.

The key aspect of this news is therefore the link between digital ordering and the physical store network.

The steps described by the source

1. Use Coop.ch to order groceries. 2. Have the groceries delivered to a store located in your vacation destination.

This sequence connects the online channel to stores located throughout the region. The network mentioned by Coop includes city centers, villages, and mountain resorts; the online service adds a delivery option tied to vacation destinations to this physical presence.
