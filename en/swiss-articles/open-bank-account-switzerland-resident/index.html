To open a bank account in Switzerland as a resident, it is important to present the necessary identity and residence documents to the bank. In particular, you must provide a passport or civil identity for proof of identity and a rental agreement or declaration of residence for proof of residence. Some banks may also require income or equity documents.

Documents required to open a bank account in Switzerland

To open a bank account in Switzerland, you need to present the following documents:

  • Passport or civil identity
  • Rental agreement or declaration of residence
  • Income or equity documents, if required by the bank

Main banks in Switzerland for residents

In Switzerland, the following banks are known to offer bank account services to residents:

  • UBS
  • Credit Suisse
  • Julius Baer
  • Banknote Money Services International (BMSI)
  • Ergast Bank

Cost of bank account in Switzerland

The cost of the bank account in Switzerland depends on the bank and its policies. In general, current accounts based in Switzerland have a lower annual cost than current accounts based abroad.
