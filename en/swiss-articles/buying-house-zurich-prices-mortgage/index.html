Average Prices and Mortgage

Own Funds and Additional Costs

In addition to the mortgage, buyers must have own funds to cover part of the purchase cost. Additional costs include transfer tax, which varies depending on the municipality, and notary fees. It is important to plan these costs in advance to avoid financial surprises.

Transfer Tax and Notary Fees

Transfer tax is a tax applied at the time of property transfer. The rate varies depending on the municipality and can significantly affect the total cost of the purchase. Notary fees are another expense to consider, covering the legal and administrative costs associated with the transaction.
