The Federal Health Insurance Act (LAMal) is private insurance at per capita premiums, not a tax and not a wage contribution. The deductible is CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000, 2500. Premiums are fixed by canton and award region.

In the Canton of Basel, the premiums for LAMal were established according to the needs of the population and the economic conditions of the region. Citizens of the Canton of Basel will have to pay an annual premium of CHF 3,500 for the deductible of CHF2,500, as established by the federal law of 18 March 2022.

How does LAMal work in the Canton of Basel?

LAMal is compulsory insurance for all Swiss citizens, including those from the Canton of Basel. The deductible is the maximum amount that the insured must pay for each medical visit or for each hospitalization. Premiums are set based on the region of residence and the insured's income.

Suppose a citizen of the Canton of Basel has an income of CHF 60,000 per year and lives in Basel city. Swiss social insurance, also known as LAMal, is a compulsory health insurance system for all Swiss citizens and foreigners residing in the country. The system is run by the cantons, which are responsible for ensuring health coverage for all their citizens.

In the canton of Basel, the health insurance system is managed by the cantonal tax administration. This entity is responsible for collecting contributions from citizens and managing the health insurance system.

LAMal 2026 health insurance premiums in the canton of Basel were determined by the cantonal tax administration according to the regulations in force. According to the Federal Law on Social Insurance of 22 March 1984, the amounts of premiums must be determined on the basis of the health expenses incurred by citizens during the previous year.

In 2025, LAMal health insurance premiums in the canton of Basel amounted to approximately CHF 4,500 for citizens who incurred health expenses of less than CHF10,000. For citizens who have incurred healthcare expenses in excess of CHF 10,000, the premium amounts were approximately CHF7,000.
