The Swiss tax map

Each canton has its own law and its own multiplier. Municipalities apply a multiplier to the cantonal tax. This arrangement yields a simple relationship: taxation relating to Switzerland is not described as a single, uniform levy, but as a sum of levels with their own territorial rules.

The AFC/ESTV has the stated responsibility for direct federal tax, or DFT, and for VAT. The cantonal administrations, by contrast, administer cantonal and municipal taxes. Responsibilities are therefore not interchangeable: the federal and cantonal authorities have different remits, while the municipality acts through its own multiplier at the cantonal level.

Operational details

Taxation, social security and health insurance: three different areas

The most useful comparison allowed by the source is not between different forms of digital evidence, which are not listed, but between taxation and the other systems that affect economic life in Switzerland. The distinction prevents every administrative item from being treated as a tax or as a responsibility of AFC/ESTV.

Taxes, contributions and LAMal are not the same item

For taxes, the structure can be viewed through three precise associations:

  • IFD and VAT: under the responsibility of AFC/ESTV.
  • Cantonal and municipal taxes: under the responsibility of the cantonal administrations.
  • AVS/AHV, AI and LPP/BVG: social security, within the scope of UFAS/BSV, not taxation.

The third distinction is decisive in preventing a deduction from pay from being confused with a tax. On salaries, AVS/AI/IPG amount to 5,3% for the employee and 10,6% in total including the employer. AD/AC is 1,1% up to the annual ceiling, while LAINF ranges from 0,7% to 1,5% depending on the sector. For LPP, on the coordinated salary, the stated rates are 7% between ages 25 and 34, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54 and 18% from age 55 up to the reference age.

These data describe social security or insurance contributions linked to salary. The material does not present them as ADE's digital evidence and does not authorize treating a contribution item as an element of direct federal, cantonal or municipal taxation. The verifiable relationship is different: a payslip may contain components of different types, which fall under different areas of responsibility.

LAMal/KVG also follows a separate logic. Health insurance is mandatory for residents and must be taken out within three months of arrival. It is private insurance with per-capita premiums, not a tax or a salary contribution. Premiums vary by canton and region, while the premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy.

This nevertheless helps avoid an attribution error: social security, health insurance and Swiss taxation are distinct areas, even when they may appear in the same personal or employment situation.
