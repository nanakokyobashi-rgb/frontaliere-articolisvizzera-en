How apprenticeships work in the Canton of Zug

Apprenticeship in the Canton of Zug is an employment contract that combines vocational training with work experience. The apprentice works for a company and, at the same time, attends a vocational training course. The apprenticeship contract must be concluded between the company and the apprentice, and must be registered with the Chamber of Commerce

Key points

To hire an apprentice in the Canton of Zug, it is necessary to follow the procedures established by the cantonal labour law. The company must ensure that the apprentice is enrolled in a vocational training course and receives a minimum salary. The apprentice is entitled to quality vocational training.

The cantonal labour law stipulates that the apprentice must be at least 15 years old, but not more than 21 years old. In addition, the apprentice must be in good physical and mental health and have no impairment that would impair his or her ability to learn or work. 📊

The company must ensure that the apprentice is enrolled in a vocational training course recognized by the Canton of Zug. The vocational training course must be at least 2 years and must include theoretical and practical training. The apprentice must receive a minimum salary of CHF 3,500 per month, starting in 2024.

The apprentice has the right to quality professional training that allows him to acquire the knowledge and skills necessary to practice the profession. Vocational training shall be conducted by a qualified instructor and shall include an ongoing assessment of the trainee's progress.
