Operational details

Choosing the right mortgage loan is crucial for sustainable purchasing. In Switzerland, the mortgage market offers various options: fixed rate mortgages (duration 5, 10, 15 years), variable rate mortgages and indexed Libor/SOFR mortgages. The Swiss mortgage loan is secured by a lien on the property. The bank records its pledge in the land register, which becomes the guarantee of the credit. If the borrower does not pay, the bank can proceed with foreclosure and forced sale of the property.

Mortgage structure and mortgage rates

Mortgage rates in Switzerland are quoted by banks and vary based on: the level of the reference interest rate (decided by the SNB/SNB), the creditworthiness of the borrower (credit history, stable employment), the LTV ratio (Loan-to-Value: percentage of the mortgage compared to the price), the type of rate (fixed costs more than variable initially), and the duration of the rate (5 years fixed vs. 10 years fixed have different rates). Key points

Once you have verified your equity and obtained a mortgage pre-approval, it is time to move on to the operational purchase procedure in the Canton of Schaffhausen.

Step 1: Find the property and negotiate

Search for properties on Swiss real estate platforms (Immobiliare.ch, Homegate, Newhome, etc.). Filter by Canton Schaffhausen, city, square footage, price. Contact your local real estate agent or private seller. Negotiate the price based on local market assessments. Once you reach an agreement, sign a promise to purchase contract. This document is optional but recommended: it binds both parties to the price and conditions.

Step 2: Bank appraisal and mortgage confirmation

The bank orders a professional appraisal of the property to verify that the declared value is realistic. If the appraisal is lower, the mortgage is reduced proportionally. With the appraisal confirmed, the bank sends the mortgage offer letter, which specifies: amount, rate, duration, monthly installments, preliminary costs.

Step 3: Involve the notary and land registry registration

A notary is mandatory for all real estate purchases in Switzerland. In the Canton of Schaffhausen, no real tax is levied on real estate transfers: fees apply for the transfer and/or registration in the land register. Notary fees vary between 0.5% and 1.5% of the purchase price, depending on the canton and complexity. In the canton of Schaffhausen, check the rates at the cantonal notary chamber.

Step 4: Closing and Transfer

Once the property is registered in the land register, the transfer is complete. The bank registers the mortgage as a pledge. The cadastral registration costs are borne by the buyer and vary by canton.
