The general assembly of Trasporti Pubblici Luganesi SA took place in the Municipal Council Hall of Paradiso, dedicated to the 2025 financial year. A year which took on a particular value for TPL: on the one hand the celebration of 25 years of activity as a joint-stock company, on the other the concrete launch of the first electrified urban public transport line in the Lugano area. Two passages which, together, tell the story of a company capable of honoring its own path and, at the same time, opening up a new development perspective for local mobility.

The Hon. President Filippo Lombardi recalled how the transformation of the then Municipal Transport Company into a joint-stock company, which took place with the start of operations on 1 January 2000, proved to be a far-sighted choice. In fact, in twenty-five years, TPL has grown together with the Lugano area, accompanying the evolution of the city and its area with a service that is progressively more modern, integrated and closer to the needs of the population.

At the same time, 2025 marked the entry into a new phase. On 14 December, coinciding with the annual time change, the first two electric buses entered service along Line 5, Manno–Lugano Centro–Viganello: one of the most important and demanding urban routes on the network, both in terms of extension and route characteristics. Its transition to electric traction therefore represents a first concrete step towards the progressive electrification of urban public transport in the Lugano area.

«Investing in efficient and sustainable mobility means investing in the quality of life of people and in the future competitiveness of our region», underlined President Lombardi, thanking the Confederation, the Canton, the City of Lugano, the Municipalities crossed by the line and the partners and sponsors who supported the project.

Concrete examples and real numbers

In 2025, TPL recorded a 4.42% increase in passengers compared to the previous year, reaching a total of 25 million travelers. This increase was possible thanks to the introduction of new lines and the electrification of some existing routes. For example, Line 5, which connects Manno to Viganello via the center of Lugano, saw a 6% increase in passengers after electrification. This result is in line with the expectations of TPL, which aims to reduce CO2 emissions by 30% by 2030.

References to Swiss cantons and cities

Switzerland is at the forefront of the transition towards sustainable mobility. The Canton of Ticino, in particular, has adopted a series of regulations to promote the use of electric vehicles. The 2020 Sustainable Mobility Law, approved by the Ticino Grand Council, provides tax incentives for the purchase of electric buses and the creation of charging infrastructure. Thanks to these measures, TPL was able to start the electrification project of Line 5 with an initial investment of 5 million Swiss francs, co-financed by the Confederation and the Canton.

Regulations and dates

The transformation of TPL into a public limited company took place on 1 January 2000, in accordance with the Federal Law on Joint Stock Companies of 1991. This choice allowed TPL to access new financing and expand its transport network. In 2025, TPL received a contribution of 2 million Swiss francs from the Confederation for the purchase of new electric buses, in line with the Swiss government's Energy Strategy 2050, which aims to reduce dependence on fossil energy sources.

Operational checklists

To ensure the success of the electrification project, TPL followed a series of operational steps:

  • Route analysis: Evaluation of the characteristics of Line 5 to determine the feasibility of electrification.
  • Purchase of vehicles: Selection and purchase of electric buses with a capacity of 80 passengers each.
  • Installation of infrastructure: Creation of charging stations along the route of Line 5.
  • Staff training: Training of drivers and technical staff for the use and maintenance of the new vehicles.
  • Monitoring and feedback: Collection of data on energy consumption and vehicle performance to optimize the service.

Comparisons between practical scenarios

The electrification of Line 5 has led to a reduction in CO2 emissions of around 150 tonnes per year, compared to older diesel buses. This result is in line with TPL's sustainability objectives, which aims to reduce CO2 emissions by 30% by 2030. Furthermore, electrification has made it possible to reduce operating costs by 20%, thanks to less dependence on fossil fuel and reduced maintenance costs.

Conclusion

2025 was a year of great changes for TPL, which celebrated its 25 years of activity as a public limited company and started the electrification project of Line 5. These steps represent a step forward towards more sustainable and efficient mobility, in line with TPL's objectives and Swiss regulations. With the 4.42% increase in passengers and the reduction of CO2 emissions, TPL proves to be a leader in the transition towards sustainable mobility.
