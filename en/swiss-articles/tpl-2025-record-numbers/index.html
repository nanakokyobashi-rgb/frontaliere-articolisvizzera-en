In short - TPL celebrates 25 years of activity as a public limited company - Start of the first urban electrified line in Lugano - 4.42% increase in passengers in 2025 ## Key facts - What: Celebration of 25 years of TPL and start of the first electrified line - When: 2025 - Where: Lugano - Who: Trasporti Pubblici Luganesi SA - Amount: 4.42% increase in passengers The general assembly of Trasporti Pubblici Luganesi SA took place in the Hall of the Municipal Council of Paradiso, dedicated to the 2025 financial year. A year that for TPL has taken on a particular value: on the one hand, the celebration of 25 years of activity as a Public Limited Company, on the other hand, the concrete start of the first electrified urban public transport line in Lugano. Two passages that, together, tell the story of a company capable of honouring its own path and, at the same time, opening a new perspective of development for local mobility. President Filippo Lombardi recalled how the transformation of the then Municipal Transport Company into a limited company, which took place with the start of the financial year on 1 January 2000, proved to be a forward-looking choice. In fact, in twenty-five years, TPL has grown together with Lugano, accompanying the evolution of the city and its area with a progressively more modern service, integrated and close to the needs of the population. 2025 has at the same time marked the entry into a new phase.
