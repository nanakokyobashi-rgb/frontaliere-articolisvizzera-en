# Nursery and childcare options

The Child Care and Nursery Service of the Canton of Freiburg offers several child care and nursery options for families with young children. Some of the options available include:

  • Full-time nursery: offered for children aged 0-3 years
  • Part-time nursery: offered for children aged 0-6 years
  • Child care: offer for children from 0 to 12 years old
  • Home care: offer for children with disabilities or special needs

Key points

If you are a family with young children and are looking for a daycare or childcare in the Canton of Freiburg, there are several options available.

Some of the options available include:

  • Nursery: a service that offers daily care to children under the age of 3. This type of service is particularly suitable for families with young children who require constant attention and care. - Child care: a service that offers daily care to children over the age of 3. This type of service is ideal for families who have children who require more structured and socialized activities. - Farms: a service that offers daily care to children in a rural environment. This type of service is particularly suitable for families looking for a more natural environment and full of outdoor activities.

For example, the Kindergarten and Child Custody Service of the Canton of Freiburg offers a fee of CHF 25.50 per hour for a child under the age of 3, while for a child over the age of 3 the fee is CHF 20.50 per hour. It is important to note that these rates may vary depending on the availability and demand for services.
