Wolfurt: stop to import customs clearance from 1 October

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 October 2026

From 1 October 2026, the procedures for the commercial traffic of goods in the Rhine Valley will change: import customs clearance will no longer be carried out in Wolfurt.

Context

In brief

  • From 1 October 2026, the procedures in the Rhine Valley will change.
  • The turning point concerns the commercial traffic of goods.
  • Import customs clearance is completed in Wolfurt.
  • The exchange rate is linked to Smart Border Austria and Passar 2.0.

Key Facts

  • Date → October 1, 2026
  • Area → several border posts in the Rhine Valley
  • Smart Border Austria and Passar 2.0 → programs
  • Wolfurt → no import customs clearance
  • Leadership → the most important digital and automated processes

On 1 October 2026, the procedures for the commercial traffic of goods at various border posts in the Rhine Valley will change. The external communication published in Bern links the change to two steps: the introduction of Smart Border Austria and the transition to Passar 2.0.

The most concrete point concerns Wolfurt. As of the date indicated, import customs clearance operations will no longer be carried out in this place. The novelty is part of a broader change, which involves several places in the Rhine Valley and not just one border point.

More space for digital processes

The communication also indicates the direction of the new set-up: digital and automated processes will become more important. This is the link between the two programmes mentioned and the change in operations at Wolfurt. For commercial freight traffic, the date to be taken into account is therefore 1 October 2026, while the place expressly indicated is Wolfurt.

The verifiable fact remains the change in procedures: in Wolfurt there is no import customs clearance and, in the places concerned, the role of digital and automated processes is growing. As a separate in-depth study on currency exchange, comparatore CHF/EUR is available.

What changes for shipments

The before-after comparison is defined above all by the Wolfurt case. With the announced change, that post will no longer carry out import customs clearance operations. At the same time, the procedures at the various border posts in the Rhine Valley will be affected by an increased importance of digital and automated processes.

For those who manage commercial traffic of goods, the practical consequence is a verification of the procedural path: it is not enough to keep the reference to Wolfurt as the place of import customs clearance, because the communication indicates that this activity will no longer be carried out there from 1 October 2026. The source focuses on the change in the procedure and the increased relevance of digital.

The role of Smart Border Austria and Passar 2.0

The two names are not ancillary details. Smart Border Austria is linked to the introduction of the new structure, while Passar 2.0 is linked to the step indicated in the communication. The explicitly stated result is a greater role for digital and automated processes.
