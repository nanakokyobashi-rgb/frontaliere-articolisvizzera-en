The Swiss public transport system

In Switzerland, regional public transport operates through fare communities organised by geographical area. Each canton has its own tariff structure, with zones extending from the urban centre to the suburbs. The more areas the passenger travels, the higher the price of the ticket or subscription.

The system is designed to be fair: those who live near the center pay less than those who come from peripheral areas, but can count on regular transport frequencies. Annual, monthly and weekly subscriptions allow residents and commuters to choose the type of coverage that best suits their use.

For the canton of Fribourg, like other cantons, the tariff structure is defined by the tariff community

Operational details

Discounts and combinations: half-price, AG, students and apprentices

In addition to standard subscriptions, Switzerland offers several tools to reduce the cost of mobility.

The federal half-price

Half-price is a federal national pass that reduces the cost of single tickets on public transport throughout Switzerland by 50%. It is available to Swiss residents of all ages on an annual basis (variable cost, subject to annual indexation). Learn how to reduce mobility costs by combining half-price with regional subscriptions. Half-price is compatible with most cantonal tariff systems, including the canton of Freiburg, and can be combined with regional subscriptions to get additional discounts.

Those who own a half-price and live in Freiburg can purchase a regional subscription at a reduced rate, further increasing overall savings.

The AG subscription (National Network)

AG is a subscription product that covers public transport throughout Switzerland. Conceptually similar to half-price, but fully paid, the GA allows unlimited access to all regional, rail and urban transport in the country. It is ideal for those who travel frequently between different cantons or for those who want maximum flexibility.

Key points

How to Inform Yourself and Purchase: Step-by-Step Procedure

If you reside in the canton of Fribourg and wish to subscribe to a public transportation pass, follow these practical steps:

1. Identify Your Usage Zones

Determine how many zones you regularly cross (e.g., home-work, home-school). This number is crucial for choosing the right type of pass and calculating its cost. If you live in the city center and work in a peripheral area, count the zones between the two points.

2. Consult the Local Tariff Community Website

Each canton has a tariff community with an official website that publishes:

  • Zone Tariff Map
  • Complete Price List for Annual, Monthly, Weekly Passes
  • Procedure for Subscribing to a Pass

In the case of the canton of Fribourg, the regional tariff community website provides all this information in real-time. Check the Fribourg cantonal government website for direct links to local transportation authorities.

3. Evaluate Combining with Half-Price, AG, or Student Discounts

If you already possess a half-price pass or are eligible for a discount (student, apprentice), check how to combine it with the regional pass. Some tariff systems allow applying the discount to the pass tariff, while others offer inclusive prices. Contact the managing authority directly for precise information.

4. Purchase the Pass

Passes can be purchased through:

  • Online: Tariff community website or dedicated mobile app
  • At the Counter: Railway stations, municipal offices, or authorized retailers
  • By Mail: Direct contact with the managing authority

5. Activate and Use

Once received, the pass is valid from the specified date. If purchased online, it is often available in digital format (QR code or app) for immediate use.

Frequently Asked Questions
How are fare zones calculated in Swiss public transport?
Tariff zones depend on the distance between the starting point and the destination. Each canton divides the territory into geographical zones (e.g. zones 1-5, 1-8, 1-10). When you purchase a subscription, choose the number of zones that cover your usual route. The more areas you travel, the higher the cost. Check your regional community's fare map to see the exact number of zones.
What are the discounts for students and apprentices in public transport?
Students and apprentices get significant reductions (typically 20-50%) on regional subscriptions. To access the discount, simply show a school affiliation document (school record, student card) or the apprenticeship contract. Discounts vary by canton and by managing body. In the canton of Fribourg, check directly with the local tariff community which reductions apply to your situation.
Is half-price compatible with cantonal subscriptions?
Yes. The federal half-price reduces individual tickets by 50% and can be combined with regional subscriptions in many cantons for additional discounts. Those who own a half-price and live in the canton of Freiburg can purchase a regional subscription at a reduced rate, increasing overall savings. Contact the Freiburg tariff community to find out how to apply both discounts to your subscription.
Can I use the AG subscription in the canton of Freiburg?
Yes. The AG is a national subscription that covers all public transport in Switzerland (railways, regional buses, city trams). Residents of the canton of Freiburg can subscribe to a GA; some cantons offer reduced rates to their residents. Check with the Freiburg tariff community if residential discounts are available for the AG subscription.
Where to buy a public transport pass in the canton of Freiburg?
Subscriptions can be purchased online (fare community website or mobile app), at the counter at train stations and municipal offices, or by mail by contacting the managing body directly. Online shopping often offers instant access in digital format (QR code or app). For information on where to find the nearest counter, please consult the website of the cantonal government of Fribourg.
