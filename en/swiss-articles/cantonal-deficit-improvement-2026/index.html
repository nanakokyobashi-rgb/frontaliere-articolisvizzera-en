Cantonal deficit 2026: down to -49.7 million francs (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 5 August 2026

The State Council updated the June forecast, reducing the forecast deficit to -49.7 million CHF, thanks to higher revenues and controlled expenses.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Deficit 2026 estimated at -49.7 million CHF
  • Improvement of 8.3 million compared to the previous budget
  • BNS/SNB revenues increased by CHF 106.1 million

Key facts

  • What : Update of the preliminary budget for the end of June 2026
  • When: June 2026 (interim statement)
  • Where: Bellinzona, Council of State of the Canton of Ticino
  • Who: State Council of the Canton of Ticino
  • Amount: Estimated deficit -49.7 million CHF (improved by 8.3 million)

The State Council of Bellinzona became aware of the cantonal finances based on the interim report at the end of June 2026. The update of the main items of expenditure and revenue brings the estimated deficit to -49.7 million francs, an improvement of 8.3 million compared to the previous budget. The estimated operating deficit was -109 million francs, so the new figure represents a significant reduction.

Expenses increased by CHF 46 million, while revenues grew by CHF 105.3 million. Among the most significant expenses are AVS/AI supplementary benefits (+12 million), participation in the reduction of the health insurance premium (+12 million) and welfare benefits (+4.7 million). Other expenditure items include asylum claims (+3.3 million), people with S status (+3 million) and refugees (+2.5 million). In addition, the expenditure of teaching staff has increased

In brief

  • Increased expenses but higher revenues

  • Potential impact on cantonal tax policy

Key facts

  • What: Analysis of expenditure and revenue variations
  • When: June 2026 update
  • Where: Canton Ticino
  • Who: Canton's financial authorities
  • Amount: +105.3 million CHF in revenue, +46 million CHF in expenses

The increase in revenues, driven mainly by the BNS/SNB profit share, has a direct impact on the ability to cover social expenses. A more contained deficit can reduce pressure on possible adjustments to the cantonal tax, although the Council of State has not yet announced concrete changes. The deficit reduction, amounting to 8.3 million CHF compared to the pre-budget forecast, is an indicator of greater room for maneuver for the cantonal authorities.

From a practical point of view, border residents and Swiss residents who benefit from AVS/AHV, LAMal/KVG, or possible LPP/BVG contributions should monitor any future adjustments to premiums or contribution thresholds. A more balanced budget can translate into a lower need to raise health insurance premiums or increase AVS/AHV contributions.

Possible scenarios

  • Scenario 1 – If BNS/SNB revenues remain stable, the canton can maintain current spending without resorting to new debt.
  • Scenario 2 – If AVS/AHV expenses were to grow beyond expectations, it may be necessary to review the cantonal participation quota.
  • Scenario 3 – A potential deterioration in national economic conditions (inflation, VAT changes) could affect cantonal tax revenues, increasing the risk of new deficits.

These scenarios show how the current situation, although improved, remains sensitive to macroeconomic changes and federal and cantonal financial policy decisions.

Key facts

For those who wish to personally verify the data, the first step is to go to the official portal of the Canton of Ticino, in the "Finance" section. There you can download the attached report that shows the comparison between budget and pre-consumer data. The document is available in PDF format and does not require any registration.

# Step-by-step procedure

1. Access the site: type the address of the Canton of Ticino (e.g. www.ticino.ch) and select the "Finance" item. 2. Look for the preliminary balance sheet: identify the publication of "Interim statement at the end of June 2026". 3. Download the file: click on the attached report link to open the PDF. 4. Analyze the items: compare the "Expenses" and "Revenues" sections with the reference data of the quote. 5. Use the tools: to assess the impact on your budget, you can use the calcolatore stipendio of the Frontier Ticino website, entering your gross income and any deductions.
