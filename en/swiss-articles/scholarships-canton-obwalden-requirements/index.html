Who can apply for scholarships and loans in the Obvaldo Canton, how the needs are calculated and how to apply to the Department of Education.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The Canton of Obvaldo provides scholarships and loans for studies for post-compulsory training
  • The subsidies are intended for those who cannot finance the training with their own means
  • The application is submitted before the start of the training course at the cantonal office
  • The amount is calculated based on the applicant's financial needs

  • Who: Cantonal Department of Education and Culture of Canton Obvaldo (office designated by cantonal ordinance)

  • When: questions normally before the start of training, annual deadlines published by the Canton
  • To whom: residents of the Canton who undergo recognized post-compulsory training
  • How: calculation of the requirement based on training costs, income and substance of the applicant and his/her family
  • Tools: cantonal form + updated income and assets documentation

Operational details

Practical analysis for those studying in Switzerland

The system of training subsidies for the Obvaldo Canton is part of a national context in which, in the absence of a federal minimum wage (only some Cantons, including non-Obvaldo, have introduced a cantonal minimum wage), the cost of living remains high and the financing of studies represents a significant burden for families. For those who start a training path — for example, an apprenticeship, a secondary school or a tertiary education — cantonal scholarships are often the variable that makes the choice sustainable.

Difference between bags and loans

From a practical point of view, the difference between exchanges and loans directly affects long-term financial planning. The grants are grants, calculated on the basis of the established need: they do not have to be returned, and their amount varies from year to year depending on the economic situation of the applicant. Loans for studies, on the other hand, are sums that the Canton advances to the applicant and that must be repaid — normally starting a number of years after the end of training, at rates and with deadlines provided for by cantonal law. Often the overall package is mixed: a non-repayable portion and a reimbursable portion, in proportion decided on a case-by-case basis by the cantonal office.

Key points

How to apply in Canton Obwalden: step-by-step procedure

Obtaining a scholarship or study loan in Canton Obwalden requires a precise process. Here are the practical steps to follow, based on the cantonal practice for training subsidies.

Step 1: verify residence and training requirements

First of all, you must be a resident of Canton Obwalden and have obtained a place in a recognized post-compulsory education (apprenticeship, upper secondary school, specialized upper school, professional university school, university, or equivalent training). The cantonal office verifies that the training falls among those eligible for the subsidy.

Step 2: gather the documentation

The application must be submitted using an official form provided by the Canton and with the following documents normally required:

  • confirmation of the training place or enrolment;
  • tax return or equivalent certificate of the applicant and of parents/spouse;
  • bank statements and documentation on the family unit's assets;
  • evidence of any other income (e.g. secondary employment, previous scholarships, third-party contributions);
  • plan of expected training and living costs.

Step 3: submit the application by the deadline

The application is normally submitted before the start of the training year or semester, according to the deadlines published annually by the Department of Education and Culture (or by the office designated by it). It is important to meet the deadlines: late applications may result in the loss of instalments or the application of reduced criteria.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who can apply for a scholarship in Canton Obvaldo?
Residents of the Canton of Obvaldo who intend to undertake or continue recognised post-compulsory training (apprenticeship, upper secondary school, tertiary education) may apply when their own and family financial resources are not sufficient to cover the costs of training and subsistence. The assessment takes into account the income and substance of the applicant and their parents or spouses.
How do I calculate my scholarship requirements?
The calculation follows cantonal practice inspired by the harmonising inter-cantonal agreement on training subsidies. Training costs (taxes, material, transport), living costs (food, accommodation, minimum requirements), the income and substance of the household and any third-party contributions are considered. If the ascertained need exceeds the financial capacity of the family, the difference is covered by a bag, loan or a combination.
What is the difference between scholarships and student loans?
The grants are non-repayable, calculated on the basis of established needs and must not be returned. Loans for studies are advanced by the Canton and must be repaid at the end of the tolerance period, at rates and conditions governed by the cantonal law of Ovaldo. Often the overall package is mixed, with a non-refundable portion and a refundable portion decided on a case-by-case basis.
When and how is the application filed in the Obvaldo Canton?
The application must normally be submitted before the beginning of the training year or semester, according to the terms published annually by the Cantonal Department of Education and Culture (or the office designated by it). You need the official form, the confirmation of the training place, the tax declaration of the applicant and the parents/spouse, the bank statements and the plan of the expected costs.
What to do in case of rejection of the application?
If the application is rejected or the amount is deemed insufficient, it is normally possible to appeal to the competent cantonal authority within the time limits indicated in the decision. It is advisable to contact the office before sending to verify the necessary documentation.
