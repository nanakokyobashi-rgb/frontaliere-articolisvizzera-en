Comparison with Other Permits

The C residence permit is the highest level of residence permit in Switzerland. Step-by-step procedure

Following the correct procedure is essential to obtain the residence permit C in the Canton of Valais. Here's a step-by-step guide to submitting your application:

1. Verification of requirements: Make sure you meet all requirements for residence permit C, including period of residence, integration criteria and language skills. 2. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the requirements to obtain a residence permit C in the Canton of Valais?
To obtain residence permit C in the Canton of Valais, you must meet the following requirements: a period of residence of at least 10 years in Switzerland (5 years for EU/EFTA citizens), demonstrate integration into Swiss society and pass a French language exam.
What documents are required to apply for residence permit C?
The documents necessary to submit the application for residence permit C include the completed application form, a valid identity document, proof of residence, proof of integration and the certificate of passing the French language exam.
What are the advantages of residence permit C compared to residence permit B?
Residence permit C offers numerous advantages over residence permit B, including permanent residence without time limits, the possibility to work freely in any sector and access to all public services and to the social and political participation of the canton.
