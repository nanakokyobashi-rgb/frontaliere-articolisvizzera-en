Practical implications and scenarios Time changes and the suppression of some connections have a significant impact on the daily mobility of border workers and Ticino residents. The reduction of some trains and the use of replacement buses can lead to longer travel times and inconveniences, especially on the busiest routes such as Biasca-Locarno and Bellinzona-Locarno. For border crossers, one possible scenario is the increase in journey time, affecting the cost of the trip and punctuality. Those who work or study in the affected areas may have to leave first or rely on alternative means such as cars or carpooling. On the other hand, the concentration of maintenance work in the summer makes it possible to limit inconvenience during the school and working year. The choice to carry out the work during the holidays aims to reduce the risks of congestion and improve the quality of the rail network in the long term. From an operational point of view, users will have to update themselves daily on changes through the official website and stations. It's a good idea to plan trips with extra time and check listings at the station or on monitors. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main dates of train changes in Ticino in 2026?
The changes are planned between July 27 and August 30, with some deletions and time changes on the main routes.
How do I check for updates in real time?
It is advisable to consult the official website ([ffs.ch/cantieri-ticino](https://ffs.ch/cantieri-ticino)) and use the dedicated mobile applications, which provide real-time updates.
