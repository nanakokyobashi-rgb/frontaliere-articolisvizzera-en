Context

In brief - Mortgage in Italy with Swiss salary possible - Necessary documents: pay slip, employment contract - Guarantees required: stable income and insurance - Banks specializing in mortgages for cross-border commuters ## Key facts - What: Guide to obtaining a mortgage in Italy with a Swiss salary - When: Valid from January 1, 2024 - Where: Italy and Switzerland - Who: Cross-border commuters with Swiss salary - Amount: Not specified Obtaining a mortgage in Italy with a Swiss salary is a real possibility for cross-border commuters. The new legislation, which came into force on January 1, 2024, simplifies the process for those who work in Switzerland but want to buy a house in Italy. ### Required documents To apply for a mortgage, cross-border commuters must submit a number of documents, including their pay slip and employment contract. These documents are used to demonstrate income stability and the ability to repay the mortgage. In addition, you must provide a copy of your residence permit and a valid ID. ### Guarantees required Banks require specific guarantees to grant a mortgage to cross-border commuters. These include a stable income and insurance that covers any risks. It is important to have a permanent employment contract and a clean credit history. Banks may also require a mortgage guarantee on the property to be purchased.

Operational details

Practical analysis ### Tax implications One of the main tax implications concerns income tax. Cross-border commuters pay income tax only in Switzerland, thanks to the agreement between Italy and Switzerland of 9 December 1976. This means that there will be no double taxation, but it is important to consider Swiss rates and contributions such as AHV/IV/EO, ALV/CA, UVG and BVG. ### Comparison with the previous situation Before the new agreement, cross-border commuters faced a number of obstacles in obtaining a mortgage in Italy. The lack of clear legislation made the process more complicated and less transparent. With the new agreement, the process has become simpler and more accessible, thanks to greater collaboration between Italian and Swiss banks. ### Concrete scenarios A cross-border commuter who wants to buy a house in Italy can benefit from lower interest rates than those offered in Switzerland. However, it is also important to consider additional costs, such as notification fees and registration fees. In addition, it is crucial to assess the stability of the CHF/EUR exchange rate to avoid financial risks.
