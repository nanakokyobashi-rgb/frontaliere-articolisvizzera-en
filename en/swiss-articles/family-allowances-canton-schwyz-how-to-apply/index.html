Family allowances in Svitto: how to claim them (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 September 2026

In the Canton of Svitto, family allowances are a public contribution for parents with children. Find out who is entitled, how to apply and what documents they need.

Context

In brief

  • Schaffhausen pays monthly family allowances for each child based on federal law
  • Amounts and conditions vary by canton according to local legislation
  • Application with the local compensation fund

Key facts

  • What: Monthly public contribution for parents with children
  • Where: Canton of Schaffhausen
  • Who: Parents resident and employed in the canton
  • Right: Subject to income and number of children
  • Procedure: Application to the local compensation fund

In the Canton of Schaffhausen, as in every Swiss canton, family allowances are paid monthly to parents who meet specific income and residence requirements. The system is based on a federal law that defines general principles, but leaves to the cantons the autonomy to set local amounts and payment procedures.

Every parent who resides or works in Schaffhausen can claim a contribution for each child, up to an age limit established by cantonal legislation. Allowances are divided into two forms: family allowances for children up to approximately sixteen years old, and training allowances for children in apprenticeships or continuing studies.

Cantonal variability is a structural characteristic of the Swiss system. In the Canton of Schaffhausen, monthly amounts, income thresholds and access procedures may differ significantly from other cantons. For this reason, applicants must verify specific details with the competent local fund.

How the national system works

The Swiss family allowance system is based on a principle of universality: every parent has the right to support, provided they meet income and residence requirements. The territorial compensation fund manages requests, assesses eligibility and pays monthly amounts to beneficiaries.

The application requires submission of a complete tax return and the birth certificate of each child. For a guide on preparing the documentation, consult the tax return service. The Schaffhausen cantonal fund processes requests and communicates the outcome within the timeframes established by local legislation.

Operational details

Who is Entitled to Family Allowances

To access family allowances in the Canton of Schwyz, the applicant must meet some fundamental requirements. First, they must reside in the canton (or work there, if residing in another Swiss canton); secondly, they must have income determined by dependent or independent employment. Swiss cantonal legislation establishes a minimum and maximum income threshold: below a certain gross annual amount, the parent is not eligible; above another amount, the entitlement expires or is reduced.

The system takes into account the family situation: the number of children, the age of each one and, in some cases, the marital status (married, divorced, widowed, single) affect the determination of the total amount.

Family Allowances and Training Allowances

Swiss legislation distinguishes between two forms of aid:

  • Family allowances: paid monthly for children who have not yet reached the age limit established by the canton (generally sixteen years). The amount is identical for all children.
  • Training allowances: a form of support for children who are following a professional apprenticeship, school education or study course up to a maximum age that varies by canton.

In the Canton of Schwyz, training allowances have an amount higher than standard family allowances, as they aim to support the cost of professional or academic training.

Non-Cumulative Character and Annual Variability

Swiss federal law provides that the entitlement to family allowances cannot be cumulative for the same child with two employers or in two different cantons. If a parent changes canton during the year, the application must be updated with the new competent fund.

In case of divorced or separated parents, cantonal legislation defines which parent receives the allowance, usually the parent with custody. The amounts of allowances may undergo annual variations for reasons of indexation or revision of income thresholds; it is important to verify the updated details each year at the local fund.

Key points

How to submit an application in Canton Schwyz

To obtain family allowances in Canton Schwyz, the first step is to identify the competent compensation fund, usually located in the parent's place of residence.

The application must be submitted on an official form provided by the compensation fund itself. In Canton Schwyz, the form is available at the compensation fund's offices, on the official cantonal website, or at the municipal offices of your residence. You must complete the form in all its parts, providing the applicant's personal data, data for each child (name, date of birth, AVS insurance number if available), and determining income (gross annual, as declared in your last tax return).

Required documentation

The competent compensation fund typically requires:

  • Previous year's tax return (extract from the tax declaration)
  • Birth certificate for each child
  • Copy of the employment contract or income declaration for self-employed workers
  • Possible custody certificate (if parents are divorced or separated)

The exact documentation may vary by canton; it is advisable to verify in advance with the competent compensation fund.

Deadlines and timelines

There are no strict deadlines for submitting the initial application; however, the right to family allowances generally begins in the month following the submission of a complete request. Cantonal compensation funds generally require renewal of documentation every year or every two years, according to their internal rules.

How to find the compensation fund and updated information

Updated information on amounts, deadlines, and exact procedures in Canton Schwyz are available from the local compensation fund, the canton's municipalities, and the official websites of the cantonal administration. Consulting directly with the compensation fund ensures you receive the most up-to-date and specific information for your personal situation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are family allowances in the Canton of Schwyz?
Family allowances are a monthly public contribution paid to parents residing or employed in the Canton of Schwyz, subject to income conditions. Swiss federal law establishes the general principles; the canton fixes the local amounts. The allowances are divided into actual family allowances (for children up to about sixteen years old) and training allowances (for children in apprenticeship or higher education).
Who is entitled to family allowances in Schwyz?
Parents who reside in the Canton of Switzerland (or work there, if they reside elsewhere in Switzerland) and have a specific income are entitled. The entitlement is subject to a minimum and maximum income threshold set by cantonal legislation. The number of children and their age affect the determination of the total amount.
What documents are needed to submit the application?
Typically, the following are required: tax return for the previous year, birth certificate of each child, copy of the employment contract (or income statement if self-employed), and possibly certificate of custody if divorced or separated parents. The exact documentation may vary; always check with the competent cashier.
How do I find the competent clearing house in the Canton of Schwyz?
The competent fund is the one territorially responsible for your place of residence in the Canton of Schwyz. You can find the contact details at the municipality of residence, on the website of the Swiss cantonal administration or by contacting the cantonal office for social insurance and benefits directly.
Do family allowances vary between Swiss cantons?
Yes, the monthly amounts of family allowances vary significantly between Swiss cantons. Although the federal law establishes the general principles, each canton independently sets the amounts, conditions of access and methods of disbursement. In order to know the specific amounts of the Canton of Schwyz, you need to contact the local cashier.
