Permit validity L

Extension of permit L

The short-term L permit may be extended if the foreign worker meets certain conditions. These conditions include:

  • The presence of an employment contract with an employer of the Canton of Basel-City;
  • Termination of the previous permit L;
  • Submission of an extension application to the SEM (Immigration Service of the Canton of Basel-City);
