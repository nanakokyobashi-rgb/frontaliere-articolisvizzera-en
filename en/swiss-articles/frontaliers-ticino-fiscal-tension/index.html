Frontier workers, fiscal tension with Ticino: trade unions call for intervention

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 28 July 2026

The trade unions are calling for the Government to intervene to resolve the situation of border workers with Ticino.

Context

TL;DR

CSIR Ticino, Lombardy and Piedmont has reported a new interpretation by the Ticino cantonal withholding tax authority, which could have serious consequences for cross-border workers. The new interpretation applies full taxation to certain categories of cross-border workers, disregarding recent Italian legislation. This situation could penalize cross-border workers and create tensions between Switzerland and Italy.

Key facts

  • Report: CSIR Ticino, Lombardy and Piedmont has reported a new interpretation by the withholding tax authority of the Canton of Ticino.
  • Issue: The new interpretation could apply full taxation to certain categories of cross-border workers.
  • Request: CSIR has called for intervention by the Italian Government and the Lombardy Region.
  • Impact: Cross-border workers could face higher taxation than that provided for under Italian legislation.

CSIR has called for intervention by the Italian Government and the Lombardy Region to resolve the situation of cross-border workers. CSIR has also requested that Italian law be respected and that Switzerland comply with international tax rules.

The situation is particularly critical for cross-border workers who reside in the Canton of Ticino and are employed by companies located in the Lombardy Region. According to CSIR estimates, approximately 10.000 cross-border workers could be affected by this new interpretation.
