Contributions and withdrawals: how the second LPP pillar works

The second pillar of the Swiss pension system, known as "Work, Pension, Productivity" (LPP), was introduced in 2020 to provide greater pension security for Swiss workers. This pillar is made up of two fundamental elements: contributions and withdrawals.

Contributions

Contributions are an important part of the second BVG pillar. The contribution rate varies based on the age of the worker, as follows:

  • 7% of income for workers between 25 and 34 years old
  • 10% of income for workers between 35 and 44 years old
  • 15% of income for workers between 45 and 54 years old
  • 18% of income for workers aged 55 and over

These contributions are deducted from the worker's gross income and paid to the second pillar pension fund.

Withdrawal

The withdrawal is another important component of the second BVG pillar. This levy is applied to the worker's gross income and has a rate of 25%. The withdrawal is paid to the second pillar pension fund, together with the contributions.

Redemption of gaps

You can only redeem gaps in contribution payments if you have paid at least one year of contributions. This means that if a worker has not paid contributions for a year or more, he or she will only be able to redeem the gap after having paid at least one year of contributions.
