More est: one in four defective electrical products (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 17 August 2026

One in four electrical products was found to be defective during inspections by the Federal Inspectorate of High Current Installations (ESTI).

Context

In a nutshell

  • One in four electrical products was found to be defective.
  • The checks were carried out by the Federal Inspectorate of High Current Systems (esti).
  • 1001 electrical products were checked.

Key facts

  • What: One in four electrical products is defective.
  • When: in 2025.
  • Where: in Switzerland.
  • Who: the Federal Inspectorate of High Current Systems (ESTI).
  • Amount: 1001 electrical products have been checked.

28% of the products checked had defects. Of these, 172 products have defects affecting electrical safety.

ESTI issued 65 sales bans and ordered recalls of 23 defective products. These included power banks, cordless vacuum cleaners, and air fryers.

The ESTI warns against products purchased on foreign online platforms, especially when prices are unusually low.

In particular, the ESTI recommends that you buy electrical products from reliable sellers and pay attention to the presence of a reachable contact address and clear instructions.
