Difference between bags and loans

The source indicates that both scholarships and loans for studies (Studiendarlehen) are available in the Canton of St. Gallen. These are two distinct instruments: grants are grants, calculated on the basis of the applicant's economic and personal situation; loans are instead sums to be repaid after completion of the training course, generally at a subsidised rate. How to apply for a scholarship in St. Gallen

Procedural steps

The standard procedure to apply for a scholarship in the Canton of St. Gallen involves the following steps, based on established Swiss practice and on what the source indicates regarding the structure of the system:

1. Check the requirements on the official website of the Canton of St. Gallen (sg.ch), in the section dedicated to scholarships and study loans. 2. Gather the required documentation: proof of income and assets of the family unit, confirmation of enrolment or admission to the school, and any other income of the applicant. 3. Fill in the cantonal application form, available online or at the competent office, within the deadlines set for the relevant academic year. 4. Submit the complete application to the cantonal office by the published deadline. The source does not indicate the exact deadline dates. 5. Wait for the decision, which is communicated in writing and indicates the amount awarded and the duration of the support. 6. In the case of a study loan, sign the loan agreement and comply with the repayment conditions set by the canton.

Deadlines and terms

Application deadlines vary depending on the level of education and the academic year. The source does not provide specific dates for St. Gallen. Deadlines are published annually on the cantonal website. In general, applications for the autumn semester close in spring, while those for the spring semester close in autumn. To avoid mistakes, it is advisable to consult the updated calendar.
