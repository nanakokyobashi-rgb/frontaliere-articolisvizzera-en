Operational details

Average rents by area

The average rents in the Canton of Aargau vary considerably depending on the area. For example, in urban areas such as Aarau, rents are generally higher than in rural areas. This is due to the higher demand for housing in cities and the limited availability of properties. According to 2023 data, the average rent for a three-room apartment in Aarau is around 1,800 CHF per month, while in rural areas such as Lenzburg, the average rent for the same type of apartment is about 1,500 CHF per month.

Security deposit

The security deposit is an amount of money that the tenant pays to the landlord as a guarantee for any damages or unpaid rent. In the Canton of Aargau, the security deposit may not exceed three months' rent and must be paid into a blocked account in the tenant's name. This account is only accessible to the landlord in case of damages or unpaid rent, and the balance must be returned to the tenant at the end of the lease. For example, for an apartment with a monthly rent of 1,800 CHF, the maximum allowed security deposit would be 5,400 CHF.

Contesting the initial rent

Tenants have the right to contest the initial rent within 30 days of the start of the lease. If the rent is considered excessive, the tenant can contact the rental conciliation authority to obtain a reduction. It is important that the tenant provides documentary evidence to support their request, such as the rents of similar properties in the same area. For example, if a tenant believes that the rent of 1,800 CHF for a three-room apartment in Aarau is excessive, they can submit a request for a reduction by providing documentation showing that other similar apartments in the same area are rented for 1,600 CHF.

Key points

Termination of the lease agreement

The landlord can only terminate the lease agreement using the official cantonal form. The termination must be communicated to the tenant with adequate notice, which varies depending on the duration of the contract. For example, in the Canton of Aargau, for an indefinite contract, the notice period is three months. Tenants have the right to contest the termination if they believe it is not justified. It is important that the landlord provides a valid reason, such as the sale of the property or the need to use the apartment for personal use. In case of a dispute, the tenant can turn to the rental mediation authority.

Rental mediation authority

In case of disputes between tenant and landlord, it is possible to turn to the rental mediation authority. This authority has the task of mediating between the parties and finding a fair solution for both. It is important that both parties present their evidence and arguments clearly and in detail. For example, if a tenant in Zurich contests a rent increase, they must provide documentation that shows the increase is not justified by the actual costs. The mediation authority will examine the evidence and decide based on the current regulations.
