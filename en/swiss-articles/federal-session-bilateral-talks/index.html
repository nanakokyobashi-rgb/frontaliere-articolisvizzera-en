Olympics, navigation, asylum and bilateral meetings in Parliament

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 October 2026

The National Council examines 8,24 million for sporting events; the Council of States addresses Erasmus+, navigation under the Swiss flag and five asylum motions.

BERN - Parliament resumes its debates today with an afternoon session in both chambers. In the National Council, the first item concerns a credit of CHF 8.24 million to support international sporting events that will take place in Switzerland in the period 2027-2029.

Subsequently, the Chamber will have to express its opinion on the federal contribution to the costs of the 2038 Winter Olympics. The figure indicated in the program is 200 million francs, but the wording used for this passage speaks of a contribution that should amount to this amount.

The program of the National

The National Council's dossiers also include a draft law prohibiting the payment of severance pay to executives in the Federal Administration and semi-public companies. The ban is accompanied by an exception: it does not apply to terminations that are not attributable to the employee. The source cites reorganisation as an example.

In the Council of States, on the other hand, the examination of the so-called "Bilateral III", which began on Monday, is coming to an end. In particular, the senators must vote on the commitment credit for Switzerland's participation in the Erasmus+ programme. The Chamber of Cantons then moves on to the amendment of the Federal Act on Maritime Navigation under the Swiss Flag.

Navigation and asylum

The amendment contains one of the main innovations indicated in the project: a shipowner can fly the Swiss flag even when the owner company is based abroad. The stated objective is to facilitate access to modern financing models, in particular leasing and bank financing by foreign partners.

Finally, the states will hold an extraordinary session dedicated to asylum, with five motions under discussion. Among these is that of Marco Chiesa, UDC/TI, who calls for the rejection of the EU's amendment to the rules on compensation for unemployed cross-border workers. The calendar thus puts on the same day support for international events, a possible financial commitment for 2038, a discipline for public summits and three strands followed by the states: agreements with the EU, navigation and asylum. For each item, the text indicates the expected examination or vote, not the final result of the session. Operational details

For those who live or work in Switzerland, the most concrete reading of the day lies in the distinction between what Parliament examines and what is already a rule. The source places all the dossiers within a session: it talks about examination, scheduled vote and extraordinary session. In the text, therefore, the sports credit, the 200 million for 2038, the modification of navigation and the five motions on asylum are not presented as measures already applicable.

What it means for families

The credit of 8.24 million concerns international sporting events in Switzerland in the period 2027-2029. The figure makes it possible to identify the time horizon of the expenditure, but the source does not indicate which events will receive support or how the amount will be distributed. For the same reason, the 200 million linked to the 2038 Winter Olympics should be read as a federal contribution submitted to the National Championship's examination: the text says that the figure should amount to that amount and does not describe the outcome of the discussion.

The project on severance pay, on the other hand, has a precise perimeter. If the described ban is approved, it concerns cadres of the Federal Administration and parastatal companies, but the source keeps out terminations not attributable to the employee, citing reorganizations. The useful comparison, therefore, is between the general prohibition provided for and the explicit exception, not between professional sectors that the text does not mention.

The dossiers of the States

For Erasmus+, the source reports a commitment credit, without indicating the amount. For navigation, it indicates a possible opening to the Swiss flag for shipowners with an owner company abroad and explains the link with leasing and bank loans from foreign partners, but does not provide a timeframe for application. On the asylum dossier, Marco Chiesa's motion concerns EU rules on compensation for unemployed cross-border workers; the outcome of the vote is not indicated.

Key points

To follow the work without turning an item on the agenda into an already active rule, it is advisable to use a monitoring sheet divided by Chamber and by type of act. It is a simple step, but it avoids mixing a credit, a bill, a legislative amendment and a motion.

A three-step tab

First step: indicate the Chamber. In the box of the National Council, the financial files and the project on departure allowances must be placed; in the box of States, the acts relating to the Bilateral III, maritime navigation and the extraordinary session on asylum. There is no need to add recipients or deadlines that the text does not indicate.

Second step: report the figure without expanding it. For sporting events, the amount and the period 2027-2029 can be recorded; for the Winter Olympics, the figure of the federal contribution is the year 2038. On the project for middle managers, the form must also retain the exception for terminations not attributable to the employee, including those related to a reorganization.

Third step: mark the status. The source speaks of examination, vote, continuation of the session and extraordinary session. These labels are more precise than a judgment on the outcome. For Erasmus+, the commitment credit must be distinguished from the amount, which is not indicated. For navigation, the foreign office of the owner company, the possibility for the shipowner to fly the Swiss flag and the objective of facilitating access to leasing and bank financing by foreign partners must be noted.
