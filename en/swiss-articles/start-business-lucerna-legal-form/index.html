Operational details

Trade register and registration procedure

To legally start a business in the canton of Lucerne, it is mandatory to register with the cantonal Trade Register (Handelsregister). This registration gives the company a recognized legal personality and allows it to operate legally. The procedure varies slightly depending on the form chosen, but follows uniform federal principles throughout Switzerland.

As a rule, registration requires the presentation of documents such as the deed of incorporation (in the case of companies), the indication of the shareholders or the person responsible, the business address and the registration of the authorized signatories. For a sole proprietorship, the cantonal administration requires the completed official registration form and proof of business address in Lucerne.

Once registered, the company appears in a public register accessible online: this is the recognized legal status of the company before suppliers, customers and authorities. Registration in the Trade Register is the first legal step towards operation.

Federal social security and insurance obligations

Immediately after registration, a central obligation arises for every entrepreneur: those who are self-employed or employ employees must join the three pillars of Swiss social security. These obligations are set at the federal level and apply throughout the country, including the canton of Lucerne.

Key points

Practical checklist: steps to start a business

Here is a simplified operational sequence for those who want to establish a company in the canton of Lucerne:

1. Choose the legal form based on your project and consult a professional (accountant or lawyer) if the activity has a certain complexity.

2. Prepare the required documentation: deed of incorporation, articles of association if it is a company, official registration forms, and identity documents.

3. Contact the Commercial Register of the canton of Lucerne online or at the competent local office and submit the registration application with the attached documents.

4. Wait for registration and obtain the official certificate, which attests to the legal status of the company.

5. Register with federal bodies: once registered, communicate the data to the federal managers (BSV for AHV/IV, SUVA or private accident insurer, and the health insurance fund for KVG). Many cantons offer unified online forms.

6. Manage tax accounting: each canton has its own deadlines for tax returns. In the canton of Lucerne, taxation follows the three-tier federal system: direct federal tax, cantonal tax, and municipal rates (each municipality applies its own multiplier). Keep accurate accounting documentation.

7. Take out insurance: choose a KVG provider, notify the start of the activity to SUVA or the accident insurer, and check the UVG coverage if you are self-employed.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the simplest legal form for a small business in the canton of Lucerne?
The sole proprietorship is the simplest form: a single person manages the business, with unlimited liability. It requires fewer administrative requirements and fewer registration costs than a company. It is ideal if you do not have partners and your project is small. However, in the case of debts, your liability is personal and unlimited. Consult an accountant to see if it's right for you.
How much does it cost to register with the Trade Register in Lucerne?
Registration fees vary depending on the legal form chosen (sole proprietorship, SA, Sagl). The canton of Lucerne has its own official tariffs. For exact amounts, please contact the cantonal trade register or the website of the cantonal administration (Kanton Luzern) directly. Remember that any security deposits and notary fees for certain documents can also affect the total.
If I open a business, do I need to take out health insurance (LAMal) immediately?
Yes. Health insurance (LAMal) is mandatory for all residents of Switzerland, including those starting a business. You have 3 months from the official start of the activity to subscribe to it. Choose from various deductible options (from CHF 300 to CHF 2500). The canton of Lucerne offers municipal subsidies for those with a low income, reducing the costs of monthly premiums.
What are the social security contributions I have to pay if I hire an employee?
As an employer, you pay 10.6% of the employee's salary for AVS/AI/IPG (the employee pays 5.3%, you pay 10.6%). For the second pillar (LPP), contribute by age: 7% (25–34 years), 10% (35–44 years), 15% (45–54 years), 18% (55+ up to the reference age) of the coordinated salary. For accidents (SUVA/LAINF), contribute 0.7%-1.5% depending on the sector.
How does taxation work for an activity in the canton of Lucerne?
Taxation is at three levels: direct federal tax (federal), cantonal tax (canton Lucerne) and municipal taxes (the municipality where the business is located). The municipality applies a multiplier to the cantonal tax base. Each municipality in Lucerne has a different multiplier, which influences the effective rate. For specific details about your situation, please contact the cantonal tax office (Steuerverwaltung Luzern).
