Zurich renews with HCL: Gold Sponsor until 2030

LUGANO - Significant renewal in the sponsorship sector for Hockey Club Lugano. The white and black club has signed a new four-year agreement with Zurich Assicurazioni, valid until the end of the 2029/2030 season. Zurich Assicurazioni, an HCL partner for over 20 years, will continue to be part of the HCL sponsor pyramid in the prestigious role of Gold Sponsor.

With a history dating back to 1872, Zurich Assicurazioni is today one of the leading international insurance groups, present in over 200 countries. The solidity of a large global entity is combined with a strong presence in the Swiss market and in our Canton, where Zurich Assicurazioni supports individuals, companies, and institutions with dedicated solutions and services for risk management and prevention.

It is in the sharing of values such as reliability, commitment, innovation, and attention to people that Zurich Assicurazioni and Hockey Club Lugano find significant harmony, laying the foundation for a collaboration based on a common vision of the territory and the community.
