Context

In a nutshell

  • The residence permit C in the Canton of Uri is valid for 10 years of residence.
  • Integration criteria require language skills in German, French or Italian.
  • The application must be submitted within 6 months of arrival in Switzerland.
  • Residence permit C is granted for a maximum period of 10 years.

Key facts

  • What: Residence permit C in the Canton of Uri.
  • When: 10 years of residence.
  • Where: Canton of Uri.
  • Who: Persons with integration requirements.
  • Amount: Not applicable.

The Federal Council has approved a new regulation for residence permit C that comes into force on 1 January 2023.

Integration Requirements

  • Knowledge of at least one of the official Swiss languages (German, French or Italian).
  • Ability to integrate into Swiss society.
  • Sufficient economic resources to support their family.
  • Health insurance.
  • Ability to support their children.

Question and Procedure

  • The application must be submitted within 6 months of arrival in Switzerland.
  • The application must be submitted to one of the Swiss immigration offices.
  • The application must be accompanied by a series of documents, including:
  • Passport.
  • Identity document.
  • Residence document.
  • Proof of knowledge of one of the official Swiss languages.
  • Evidence of ability to integrate into Swiss society.
  • Evidence of sufficient economic resources.
  • Evidence of insurance

Key points

To obtain residence permit C in the Canton of Uri, the subject must submit the application within the time limits established by law. The application procedure is described in the regulations of the Federal Council.

The subject must demonstrate an advanced knowledge of the German, French or Italian language. In particular, knowledge of level B2 or higher according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CFR) is required. This means that the subject must be able to express themselves easily and clearly in one of the three languages.

In addition, the subject must demonstrate a good knowledge of the culture and history of the Canton of Uri. This can be demonstrated through the acquisition of a language or culture certificate, issued by a recognised institution.

The subject must submit the application within the time limits established by law. The terms are generally 3-6 months, depending on the complexity of the application. The subject may request to submit the application in advance, but must ensure that they have complied with the established deadlines.
