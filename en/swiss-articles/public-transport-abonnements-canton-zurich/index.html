New subscription rules and zones

Starting from 1 January 2023, the Canton of Zurich has introduced new rules for public transport subscriptions. Subscription zones have been redesigned to offer more flexibility and convenience to users. Users can choose to pay for a specific area or for the entire public transport network.

Subscription Zones

The Canton of Zurich has established 5 different zones for public transport passes:

  • Zone A: Zurich district, including the historic centre
  • Zone B: Zurich region, including the districts of Altstetten, Albisrieden and Oberengstringen
  • Zone C: Winterthur region, including the neighbourhoods of Winterthur and Kloten
  • Zone D: St. Gallen, including the districts of St. Gallen and Rorschach
  • Zone E: Lucerne region, including the districts of Lucerne and Emmetten

Operational details

Public transport passes in the Canton of Zurich have recently been modified to offer greater flexibility and adaptability to users' needs. The subscription zones have been redesigned to allow users to choose to pay for a specific zone or for the entire public transport network.

According to the new rules, public transport passes in the Canton of Zurich have been divided into 3 main zones: A B and C. The A zone covers the city centre and surrounding areas, the B zone covers the outermost areas of the city centre and surrounding cities, while the C zone covers the entire public transport network of the Canton.

Users can choose to pay for a specific area or for the entire public transport network. For example, a user who lives and works in the city center could purchase a membership for zone A, while a user who works in a neighboring city could purchase a membership for zone B.

According to the Federal Traffic Law (LTF) of 7 December 1987, public transport subscriptions must be adapted to the needs of users and must offer fair and transparent pricing. The law establishes that public transport subscriptions must be divided into different zones, according to the needs of users.
