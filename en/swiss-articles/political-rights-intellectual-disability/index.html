People with intellectual disabilities, consultation on political rights

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 14 October 2026

The Federal Council launches the consultation on October 14, 2026 Consultation deadline: January 28 Proposed: federal vote and signing of initiatives

On October 14, 2026, the Federal Council launched the consultation procedure on the participation of people with intellectual disabilities in Switzerland’s political life. Like some cantons, it opened a process that will remain open until January 28. The dossier concerns a constitutional amendment and an amendment to the Political Rights Act.

The current framework at federal level provides for the exclusion of people with intellectual disabilities from exercising political rights, as a government note recalls. The proposal, by contrast, aims to enable adult Swiss citizens in the future to contribute to shaping the country’s policies, defining a federal-level framework different from today’s.

According to the objective outlined by the Federal Council, in the future all adult Swiss citizens should be able to participate in political life. The source concretely lists the forms of participation indicated: National Council elections, federal votes, the signing of popular initiatives and the signing of referendums. The list therefore includes both voting and signing the instruments specified.

From the federal level to the cantons

The federal process runs alongside initiatives already mentioned in some cantons. In Geneva, in 2020, people with intellectual disabilities were granted the right to vote and stand for election at cantonal level; the text also cites Appenzell Innerrhoden in 2024. In other cantons, the source adds, similar efforts are under way. Institutionally, the Federal Council’s consultation is therefore presented alongside the cantonal processes already mentioned or still under way.

The report, broadcast in the 12.30 Radiogiornale on October 14, 2026, presents the prospect as a future one. The announced deadline for the consultation is January 28.

Operational details

The practical point to keep separate is the level at which the right is exercised. The source distinguishes the current federal situation, the consultation launched by the Federal Council, and the developments mentioned in some cantons. To read the news correctly, the cantonal recognition mentioned for Geneva should not be confused with the proposal concerning the Confederation.

What the federal perspective includes

The proposal described is not limited to a single election. The reference to elections to the National Council concerns participation in that federal vote; the reference to federal votes broadens the scope to the consultations indicated by the source. Signing popular initiatives and referendums adds the participation tools listed alongside voting.

The specified target group is precise: all adult Swiss citizens. For a practical reading, it is useful to associate the subject, level, and right: adult Swiss citizens, the Confederation, elections to the National Council, federal votes, popular initiatives or referendums.

The chronological comparison is equally useful. Geneva is placed in 2020, Appenzell Innerrhoden in 2024, while for the federal level the reference is October 14, 2026, with consultation until January 28. The other cantons are described as engaged in similar efforts. The overall picture therefore varies by territory and phase: some references concern the cantons, while others concern a federal consultation open until January 28.
