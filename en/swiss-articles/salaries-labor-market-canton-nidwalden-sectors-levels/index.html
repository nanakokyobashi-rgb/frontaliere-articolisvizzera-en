Minimum wage in the Canton of Nidvaldo

The minimum wage in the Canton of Nidvaldo is CHF 4,260 per month, as established by the cantonal law of 1 January 2020. This means that all employees in the Canton of Nidvaldo must be paid at least at this level. However, it is important to note that some industries and businesses may offer higher wages based on their ability to pay.
