The residence permit C represents recognition of your stable integration in Switzerland. In the Canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden, as in the other Swiss cantons, this permit follows the federal procedure established by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM), with the addition of specific cantonal requirements.

Federal legislation sets the minimum term of residence: 10 years for non-EU citizens, reduced to 5 years for citizens of the European Union and the European Free Trade Association (EFTA). The C permit is not automatic after the period: it depends on the integration assessment, linguistic knowledge and the absence of reasons for refusal.

Federal requirements vs. cantonal requirements of Appenzell Innerrhoden

The C permit obeys a two-level structure: the Federal Migration Act (LEMI) defines the general criteria, while the canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden applies additional cantonal criteria.

At the federal level:

  • Continuous and stable residence (10 years, 5 for EU/EFTA)
  • Socio-economic integration: legitimate work, contributions to the Swiss social security system (AVS/LPP) verifiable in your individual account
  • Absence of significant criminal convictions
  • Positive contribution to Swiss society

The Canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden adds specific assessments:

  • Linguistic knowledge in the cantonal language (German)
  • Compliance with local laws
  • Evaluation of cultural and social integration

The procedure begins at the cantonal immigration office in Appenzell, where you submit your application with the required documents. There is no fixed processing deadline: it depends on the complexity of the dossier and the cantonal deadlines.

Integration criteria: linguistic knowledge and social involvement

Language skills are assessed according to the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR). For the Canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden, the cantonal authorities verify the level of German language (official language) through recognized certificates, interviews or evaluation of the dossier.

There is no federal minimum: each canton sets its own standard. Appenzell Innerrhoden normally requires a level sufficient to maintain employment and participate in local life, typically equivalent to CEFR level A2-B1.

In addition to language, the authorities evaluate:

  • Duration of stable employment in Switzerland
  • Contributions paid to the AVS/LPP system over the years
  • Absence of dependence on social assistance
  • Family relationships and community ties
  • Respect for legality and public order

The early issuance of the C permit is possible in exceptional cases (e.g. naturalisation, marriage to a Swiss citizen, extraordinary public service), but remains a discretionary cantonal decision.

Operational details

Difference between Permit B (residence) and Permit C (domicile)

Many applicants confuse the two permits. Both are permitted in the long term, but with different legal implications.

Permit B (Residence):

  • Renewable annually (not automatic, but usual renewal)
  • Linked to occupation or initial reason for stay
  • Revocable if you lose your job or change your circumstances
  • Average duration: 5 years of consecutive renewals

Permit C (Address):

  • Indefinite (renewable on request every 10 years, formally)
  • Independent of employment (you do not lose your permit if you are unemployed)
  • Right to stay almost acquired
  • Path to Swiss naturalisation

For those who want to move to a permanent residence in Switzerland (not just temporary employment), the C permit is the crucial step.

In the Inner Appenzell Canton, the transition from B to C takes place upon explicit request. It is not automatic: you must prove 10 years of residence and meet the integration criteria.

Download tax returns from the cantonal or federal portal. Sign up for an official language skills test (e.g. GOETHE, Telc) if required — Appenzell Innerrhoden usually asks for a certificate or assessment interview.

Step 3: Apply (appropriate time) You can apply at the Immigration Office of the Canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden (in Appenzell, the capital). Step 4: Processing by the canton (3-6 months) The cantonal office examines the dossier. Step 5: Decision and release If approved, you receive written notification. The C permit is issued to you in the form of official documents (replacement identity card or legitimation sheet). Register with the municipality to update your residency status.

Deadlines and waiting times

Tip: Apply during a period of employment stability, not in the event of an imminent layoff or ongoing job change. Economic stability strengthens your demand.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between 10 years of residence for non-EU nationals and 5 years for EU/EFTA nationals?
The Federal Migration Act (LEMI) sets different terms for nationality. EU and European Free Trade Association (EFTA) citizens enjoy free movement agreements, so the requirement is reduced to 5 years of residence. Non-EU citizens must respect 10 years. The Inner Appenzell Canton applies these federal standards without exception.
Can I apply for the C permit before the expected 10 years or 5 years?
In exceptional cases (marriage to a Swiss citizen, extraordinary public service, naturalization on merit), the canton may grant early release. However, it is not guaranteed — it depends on Appenzell Innerrhoden's discretionary assessment and the specific circumstance. Tip: Contact the cantonal immigration office to evaluate your case.
What level of German do I need for the C permit in Inner Appenzell?
There is no minimum prescribed federal standard. Appenzell Innerrhoden normally requires functional capacity (A2-B1 according to the European CEFR framework) — sufficient to maintain work and converse locally. You can prove it with a recognized certificate (GOETHE, Telc), self-declaration + interview, or direct evaluation by the cantonal office.
If I am unemployed, can I apply for C leave?
Short-term unemployment does not automatically exclude C. However, if you are dependent on cantonal social aid, it is very difficult to obtain permission (almost impossible). You must demonstrate your own economic capacity or family benefits. Tip: Wait for economic stability, or consult the immigration office about your specific situation for a preliminary assessment.
How much does it cost to apply for a C permit in the Inner Appenzell?
The cost is not fixed by federal law. Each canton sets its own administrative fees (usually CHF 50-300 depending on complexity and presentation channel). Contact the Immigration Office of Appenzell Innerrhoden directly to find out the current exact quotas and payment methods.
