Health insurance premiums in the Canton of Obvaldo: reduction and deductibles

The cantonal law on health insurance in the Canton of Obvaldo provides for age groups and deductibles to reduce premiums. This means that some categories of people will be able to pay less for health insurance.

According to cantonal law, age groups are divided into three categories:

  • Families with children: Families with children up to the age of 18 can enjoy a premium reduction of up to 20% compared to families without children.
  • Elderly people: Older people over 65 can enjoy a reduction in premiums of up to 15% compared to people between the ages of 18 and 65.
  • Families with disabilities: Families with disabilities can enjoy a premium reduction of up to 25% compared to families without disabilities.

Deductibles are another important aspect of cantonal law. Deductibles are the cost that the patient must pay before they can access health services. According to cantonal law, deductibles are set at:

  • CHF 300 for medical examinations
  • CHF 500 for hospital services
  • CHF 200 for specialist visits
