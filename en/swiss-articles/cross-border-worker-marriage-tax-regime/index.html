Frequently Asked Questions
Does marriage change border status?
No, marriage does not automatically change border status. The worker maintains his G permit as long as he resides in Italy and returns daily or regularly to Switzerland for professional reasons. The tax regime remains based on double taxation conventions, with withholding tax in Switzerland and tax credit in Italy.
What happens if the border worker moves to Switzerland after the wedding?
If the border worker transfers his residence to Switzerland, he loses the status of border worker. Become a Swiss tax resident and will be subject to ordinary taxation in the municipality of residence, ceasing to be subject to the taxation regime at source provided for border workers. This procedure requires notification to the local authorities and the SEM.
How is double income taxation avoided?
Double taxation is avoided through the tax credit mechanism. Taxes paid at source in Switzerland are deducted from taxes due in Italy, in accordance with the 1976 Double Taxation Convention. This operation must be correctly indicated in the CE framework of the Italian model 730.
