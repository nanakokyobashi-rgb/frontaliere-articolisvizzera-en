The LAMal is mandatory for residents of Switzerland and must be taken out within three months of arrival. This is the starting point for understanding health insurance premiums in the Canton of Basel-Stadt: there is no single price that applies uniformly across the entire country, as premiums are set by canton and region.

The LAMal premium is per person. It is not a tax, nor is it a payroll deduction. This distinction matters in your monthly budget: the LAMal line item should be kept separate from taxes and payroll deductions. For those living in Basel-Stadt, the comparison must therefore start with the geographic area specified for the premium and the coverage tier associated with the chosen plan.

Available Deductibles for Adults

The deductible is a key factor to consider before reviewing the total cost. For adults, the options listed are CHF 300, CHF 500, CHF 1,000, CHF 1,500, CHF 2,000, and CHF 2,500. These are six distinct levels, which should be listed as is without substituting them with an average or a value from another canton.

This list does not authorize the interpretation of any figure as a guaranteed savings amount: a deductible, on its own, does not determine the applicable premium. The correct interpretation involves considering the deductible in conjunction with the premium for the Basel-Stadt region and the specific insurance situation in question.

A second element is the premium reduction. This is a cantonal subsidy, not an item defined as a federal tax or payroll tax. For the Canton of Basel-Stadt, the issue must therefore be addressed at the cantonal level, while the LAMal remains the mandatory insurance for residents.

The LAMal e cassa malati guide serves to keep three questions separate: which coverage is mandatory, which adult deductible is being considered, and how to account for the cantonal subsidy. Without an official amount for the year in question, it is incorrect to use a generic figure to describe the premium in Basel-Stadt.

Operational details

The comparison that matters in the budget

The practical comparison in Basel-Stadt starts with separating four elements: area, deductible, premium and subsidy. If they are mixed together, the result does not make clear how much health insurance really affects the cost of living. The practical rule is simple: use only the relevant canton and region for the calculation, then note the selected adult deductible and treat the premium reduction separately.

The table does not replace an official amount, but it avoids two common errors. The first is to use the premium of a different region or canton as a reference for Basel-Stadt. The second is to add or subtract the subsidy as if it were a salary deduction. LAMal is not a salary contribution: the comparison should be based on the per-capita premium and the cantonal reduction, keeping the two aspects separate.

This separation also helps avoid confusing insurance, taxation and social security. AFC/ESTV concerns direct federal tax and VAT; cantonal administrations manage the cantonal and municipal levels of taxation. UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS/AI/LPP, and does not set taxes; UST/BFS produces statistics and does not set tax rates. These are distinct domains from that of choosing LAMal.

In the overall budget, the premium should therefore be considered alongside the other expenses, without converting it into a percentage of the salary. AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG also belong to the language of social security and salary contributions, whereas the LAMal premium remains per capita. To track the effect on costo della vita in Svizzera, it is advisable to record the item separately; for social security, one can consult the previdenza AVS e LPP guide.

The result is a more cautious comparison: it does not promise savings in the absence of verified amounts, but makes the difference between premium, deductible and subsidy in the Canton of Basel-Stadt clear.

Key points

Procedure for Basel-Stadt

The operational process can be structured without automatically transferring rules from other cantons. For those who need to arrange coverage in Basel-Stadt, five steps bring together the deadline, territory and budget.

1. Set the arrival date. LAMal must be taken out within three months of arrival for those resident in Switzerland. The deadline should be noted before comparing options, because the insurance obligation comes before choosing the deductible.

2. Identify the premium scope. The calculation must refer to the applicable canton and region. Do not use as a basis a figure referring to another territory: premiums are not a uniform national value.

3. Compare adult deductibles. Record separately CHF 300, CHF 500, CHF 1'000, CHF 1'500, CHF 2'000 and CHF 2'500. The comparison remains clear only if each amount is linked to the offer and region considered.

4. Keep the premium reduction separate. It is a cantonal subsidy. In the verification for Basel-Stadt, it must be considered as a separate item from the per-capita premium, taxes and salary contributions. The available information does not allow this check to be replaced with a threshold or amount valid for everyone.

5. Close the monthly budget. Add LAMal expenditure to the overall cost-of-living item, but do not include it among IFD, VAT, AVS/AHV or LPP/BVG: the nature of the premium is different. If alternative models are presented, record for each the premium, region, deductible and any indicated reduction, without adding conditions not reported.

This framework makes it possible to understand where to stop when a data point is missing: figures are not invented, criteria from other cantons are not imported and the cantonal reduction is not confused with a salary deduction. Frequently Asked Questions
Is LAMal mandatory for those residing in Switzerland?
Yes. The source considers LAMal a mandatory health insurance for residents in Switzerland. Coverage must be taken out within three months of arrival. The deadline concerns the insurance obligation and should not be confused with the choice of the adult deductible or with premium reduction, which is a cantonal subsidy.
What deductibles are available for adults?
The deductibles indicated are CHF 300, CHF 500, CHF 1'000, CHF 1'500, CHF 2'000 and CHF 2'500. These are levels to compare with the premium applicable to the canton and region. The deductible should not be replaced with a national average or with a value pertaining to another canton.
What is the premium reduction in the Canton of Basel-Stadt?
It is a cantonal subsidy. It should be treated separately from the per-capita premium, taxes and wage contributions. It is not a tax and not a wage contribution; moreover, it should not be confused with direct federal tax or VAT. For Basel-Stadt, the reference remains the cantonal level.
Are LAMal premiums the same in all Cantons?
No. Premiums are defined by canton and region, so a figure referring to another area cannot automatically be used to assess the situation in Basel-Stadt. In the comparison, the adult deductible and the cantonal subsidy must also be kept separate, because they are distinct elements in the assessment of the premium.
