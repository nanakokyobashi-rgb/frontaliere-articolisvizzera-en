Three Swiss Cantonal Banks: Three Times Growing Profits

In the context of Switzerland, where financial stability is synonymous with security, cantonal banks have always played a key role in the country's economy. In this sense, the latest news regarding the half-yearly accounts of these three institutions can only be welcomed with enthusiasm. Indeed, rising profits represent an important signal for the Swiss economy, which continues to show signs of recovery.

The cantonal bank of Bern, for example, made a profit of 83 million francs, 8% more than the same period in 2025. This result is particularly significant considering that the bank has faced a number of challenges, including the crisis in the real estate sector and increasing competition from private banks. Operational details

The three Swiss cantonal banks presented half-yearly accounts with growing profits. The cantonal bank of Bern made a profit of 83 million francs, 8% more than in the same period of 2025. The St. Gallen company analogy reached 120 million francs, 5% more than the same period in 2025. The cantonal bank of Basel-Country made a profit of 97 million francs, 5% more than in the same period of 2025.

According to official data, the cantonal bank of Bern recorded a 10% increase in revenue compared to the same period in 2025, thanks to the growth in deposits and investment operations. The bank also announced plans to increase share capital by CHF 500 million by the end of the year, to finance growth and improve the stability of the banking system.

The cantonal bank of St. Gallen, on the other hand, recorded a 15% increase in revenue compared to the same period in 2025, thanks to the growth in investment transactions and the management of real estate assets. The bank also announced plans to expand its presence in the canton of St. Gallen, with the opening of new branches and the hiring of new employees.

Key points

The news was released by the St. Gallen company, which also announced that it had reached the threshold of 10 billion francs administered in the business sector in Germany. The online bank Radicant, which has ceased operations, is proceeding according to plan. Next week the news of the similar accounts of Banca Cantonale Grigione (BCG) and, in Ticino, of BancaStati will be released.

According to internal sources, Banca Cantonale Grigione (BCG) reported an increase of 12.5% compared to the previous year, reaching a total of 850 million francs in net profits. The banking group also announced that it had reached the threshold of 15 billion francs administered in Switzerland. In particular, BCG highlighted the growth of the small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) sector in eastern Switzerland, with a 25% increase in loans granted.

Meanwhile, BancaStati, the public bank of the Canton of Ticino, reported a 10% increase over the previous year, reaching a total of 650 million francs in net profits. The banking group also announced that it had reached the threshold of 8 billion francs administered in Switzerland. In particular, BancaStati highlighted the growth of the textile business sector and the agri-food sector in central Switzerland.
