Salaries and the labor market in Vaud: sectors and levels

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 30 September 2026

No federal minimum wage: in the Canton of Vaud, the comparison involves hourly wages, AVS/LPP contributions, LAMal, taxes, and cost of living.

The starting point for Vaud

In Switzerland there is no federal minimum wage: some cantons do, however, have their own threshold. For anyone looking for or changing jobs in the Canton of Vaud, this is the first check to make, before assigning a salary level applicable to everyone to a training course or a sector. There is no single national figure: the comparison starts with the potentially applicable cantonal rule, the contract and any relevant collective agreement.

Looking beyond the figure in the offer

The second concrete piece of information is working hours. The maximum limit is 45 hours per week in industry, offices and retail; in other sectors it reaches 50 hours. The difference affects how an offer is read because the same gross amount must be linked to the working time specified in the contract. The minimum vacation entitlement is four weeks, rising to five for anyone under 20. Notice periods are one, two or three months: one month in the first year, two from the second to the ninth and three from the tenth.

Contributions then come into play in relation to salary. AVS/AI/IPG amounts to 5.3% for the employee, while the total with the employer reaches 10.6%. AD/AC is 1.1% up to the annual maximum; LAINF/LAA ranges from 0.7% to 1.5% depending on the sector. For LPP/BVG on the coordinated salary, the rates indicated are 7% between 25 and 34, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54 and 18% from age 55 up to reference age.

These items do not include LAMal/KVG. It is mandatory for residents and must be taken out within three months of arrival; it is private insurance with per-capita premiums, not a tax or a salary contribution. Premiums vary by canton and region, while premium reductions are a cantonal subsidy. To check the transition from gross to net, you can consult busta paga svizzera, without turning a health insurance premium into a salary deduction.

Operational details

Net pay also depends on taxes and housing

A salary comparison in Canton Vaud does not end with gross pay and payroll deductions. The cost of living also depends on the municipality, the LAMal premium and rent. Swiss taxation has three levels: direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each canton has its own law and multiplier; the municipality applies a multiplier to the cantonal tax.

Do not confuse the offices

Distinguishing responsibilities avoids using the wrong data to estimate net pay. The AFC/ESTV handles the IFD and IVA. Cantonal administrations manage cantonal and municipal taxation. UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS/AI/LPP, but does not set taxes. UST/BFS produces statistics and does not set tax rates. Therefore, a salary statistic is not a tax table, and a reference to social security does not replace the calculation of direct federal tax.

Housing and residence status

Tenancy law is federal and the same in every canton. If the landlord gives notice, the termination is valid only on the official cantonal form; the challenge must be submitted within 30 days to the conciliation authority. For those who do not have Swiss citizenship, SEM distinguishes permit L, short-term and valid for up to one year, B, a renewable residence permit, C, a settlement permit generally after ten years of residence or five for UE/AELS citizens, and G for cross-border commuters. These are checks separate from salary, but they affect the overall assessment of an offer in Canton Vaud.

Key points

The checklist before accepting

Practical verification can be carried out in five steps, using only documentable items and referring to annual indexed figures from official sources.

1. Identify the salary rule

Check if the position is covered by a cantonal minimum wage or a collective agreement. There is no single federal threshold. If the comparison concerns the level of education, do not replace the contract with an average: use the salary stated in the offer and verify the sector and working hours.

2. Read the working conditions

Compare the gross salary, weekly hours, holidays, and notice period. The reference is 45 hours for industry, offices, and sales, and 50 in other sectors; minimum holidays are four weeks, five for those under 20. Also note the 14 weeks of maternity leave at 80% via IPG and the two weeks of paternity leave where applicable.

3. Separate payslip and insurance

Check the indicated items: AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC, LAINF/LAA, and LPP on the coordinated salary, with the brackets provided for age. Keep the LAMal on a separate line: it is mandatory for residents, private, with a per capita premium, and must be stipulated within three months of arrival. If applicable, check for the cantonal premium reduction.

4. Link taxes and housing

Remember the three tax levels and do not attribute functions belonging to other offices to the UFAS/BSV or UST/BFS: AFC/ESTV concerns IFD and VAT, while cantonal administrations handle cantonal and municipal taxes. For housing, verify the deposit, blocked account, and official form if a notice of termination arrives; the contestation period is 30 days.

5. Verify status and updates

If you are a foreign national, identify the SEM category: L, B, C, or G. For indexed amounts such as the 3a third pillar maximum, LPP coordination threshold or deduction, AD/AC maximum, average LAMal premiums, and median salary, do not use an outdated figure: it must be checked in the official source. To search for opportunities, you can consult job advertisements and companies that are hiring. Frequently Asked Questions
Is there a federal minimum wage for a job in the Canton of Vaud?
No, there is no federal minimum wage in Switzerland. Some cantons have their own threshold. For a position in the Canton of Vaud, it is therefore necessary to verify the applicable cantonal rule and any collective agreement, without treating an average salary as the legal minimum. The contract must be read together with the sector and the time scheduled for the position.
What deductions affect the Swiss salary?
The main items indicated are AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3% for the employee, with 10.6% overall together with the employer, AD/AC at 1.1% up to the annual ceiling and LAINF/LAA between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the sector. The LPP on the coordinated salary provides for 7%, 10%, 15% and 18% in the indicated age groups.
Is LAMal a deduction on salary?
For residents, LAMal is mandatory and must be stipulated within three months of arrival. It is private insurance at premiums per capita: it is not a tax and it is not a salary contribution. Premiums vary by Canton and region; the premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy, so it must be kept separate from the net salary calculation.
What are the tax levels in Switzerland?
Taxes have three levels: federal, cantonal and municipal DFIs. Each Canton has its own law and multiplier; the municipality applies a multiplier on the cantonal tax. The AFC/ESTV deals with DFIs and VAT, while cantonal and municipal administrations manage. UFAS/BSV and UST/BFS do not set tax rates.
What rules apply to the rental deposit?
The security deposit may not exceed three months and must be held in an escrow account in the tenant's name. The landlord's cancellation is valid only on the official cantonal form. The dispute must be submitted within 30 days to the conciliation authority. These rules derive from the federal lease law, which is the same in each Canton.
