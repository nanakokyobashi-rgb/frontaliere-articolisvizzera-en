AVS and complementary services in Canton Bern

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 7 September 2026

How the calculation of the AVS pension works in the Canton of Bern: mandatory contributions, contribution gaps, supplementary benefits and coverage of the minimum living allowance.

Context

In brief

  • AVS contributions 5.3% for employees (total 10.6% with employer)
  • Contribution gaps reduce retirement pension amount
  • Supplementary benefits supplement the minimum living standard for retirees
  • Compensation fund of the Canton of Bern manages payment administration

The first pillar in the Canton of Bern

In the Canton of Bern, as in the rest of Switzerland, the Old-Age and Survivors' Insurance (AVS) represents the first pillar of the national pension system. The mandatory contribution for employees is set at 5.3% on the salary, while the employer pays another 5.3% share, bringing the total to 10.6% of the coordinated salary. These payments feed into the calculation of the future AVS pension at the time of retirement and form the basis for access to insurance benefits.

The cantonal compensation fund of the Canton of Bern manages the administration of these payments and maintains the contribution accounting of each insured person. A crucial role to ensure that the contributions paid during the professional career are correctly recorded, accounted for, and subsequently used to calculate the pension. Without this precise administration at the cantonal level, it would not be possible to determine with certainty the amount due to each retiree.

Operational details

How to calculate the AVS pension in the Canton of Bern

The AVS pension is determined based on the total contributions paid throughout one's working career. The more contributions accumulated and the longer the career, the higher the pension amount at the ordinary retirement age. In the Canton of Bern, as in other cantons, the calculation is made according to uniform federal formulas; you can consult the guide on AVS, LPP and pension for more details on the calculation mechanism. The local administration of the compensation fund ensures that the contribution data is accurate and up-to-date throughout one's professional life.

A continuous working career without gaps allows for the accumulation of the maximum annual contributions, thus optimizing the future pension. However, many people face interruptions in their work career: periods of unemployment, geographical transfers, sector changes, leaves for family or personal reasons, or years in which the income is below the minimum contribution threshold. Each of these situations generates a contribution gap, i.e., a year or period in which no AVS contribution is paid or the payments are below the required minimum.

Concrete Impact of Gaps on Pension Calculation

One of the critical points in the Swiss pension system is how contribution gaps are handled in the pension calculation. Unlike other systems that apply forms of automatic recovery, Switzerland considers gaps as empty periods in the computation of accumulated contributions; these remain in the accounting and reduce the total number of years with full contributions. A worker who accumulates, for example, 5 years of gaps during a 45-year career will see their AVS pension calculated on 40 years of contributions instead of 45, with a proportional reduction in the amount due.

Key points

How to check your AVS contributions with the Bern compensation fund

To plan your retirement in the Canton of Bern, the first step is to check your contribution statement with the cantonal compensation fund. The Bern compensation fund provides contribution statements upon request, detailing the years of contributions paid and any gaps in your work history. This document is essential for calculating the estimated amount of your AVS pension at retirement.

The check can be done directly at the compensation fund offices or, in many cases, through the online portals provided by the entity itself. Insured individuals have the right to receive a statement of their contribution data regularly; consulting these statements is essential to identify any errors or omissions and to request timely corrections before retirement. It is advisable to check your salary calculator annually to ensure that your contribution data is correct and up-to-date.

Procedure to request supplementary benefits in the Canton of Bern

If the analysis of the statement shows that the future AVS pension will not be sufficient for the guaranteed minimum living standard, it is advisable to inform yourself in advance about the procedures to access supplementary benefits in the Canton of Bern. Supplementary benefits are not automatically granted but require a formal application in which you document your income, assets, and overall financial situation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are AVS contributions in 2026?
In 2026, the AVS contribution for employees is 5.3% of the gross salary. The employer pays another share of 5.3%, for a total of 10.6%. These amounts are revalued annually according to the evolution of the economic indices. Complementary contributions (AD/AC) and accident insurance (LAINF) also have specific rates managed by UFAS.
What are contribution gaps and how do they affect income?
Contribution gaps are periods during which AVS contributions are not paid: unemployment, break from work, non-employment, income below the minimum threshold. Each gap reduces the total accumulated contributions and, consequently, the amount of the AVS pension calculated at retirement. A gap of one year means a missing year in the career count.
How can I verify my tax return in the Canton of Bern?
The Canton of Bern Compensation Fund provides extracts from the report upon written request or via the available online services. The excerpt shows the years with contributions paid and any gaps present during the course of the career. It is advisable to check the report at least once a year to identify errors or omissions and request corrections promptly.
Who is entitled to supplementary benefits?
Supplementary benefits are due to pensioners in the Canton of Bern with income and assets below the legal threshold. They supplement the AVS annuity until the guaranteed minimum life expectancy is reached. You must submit a formal application to the clearing house or to the competent cantonal office with documentation of your economic situation (tax returns, bank statements, asset declarations).
What are the requirements to access the AVS annuity?
The AVS pension is due upon reaching the ordinary retirement age. Contributions must be paid regularly for at least 1 year during the career. Those with contribution gaps will receive an annuity reduced proportionally to the number of years of contributions accumulated compared to a career without interruptions. The supplementary services compensate for the gap up to the vital minimum.
