Residence permit B canton Vaud: requirements and renewal (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 September 2026

How to renew the B permit in the Vaud? What are the requirements? Practical guide to the procedures, necessary documents and deadlines for the renewal of the residence.

Context

In brief

  • The B permit is a renewable residence permit for foreign workers in Switzerland
  • Managed by the federal SEM and the Vaud canton migration office
  • Renewal should be initiated 1–2 months before expiration
  • Requirements: employment contract, social security contributions, mandatory LAMal insurance

Key facts

  • What: Renewable residence permit B for foreigners working in Switzerland
  • Who: State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) and cantonal migration office
  • Duration: Renewable, usually annual or biennial depending on the canton
  • Procedure: Submit application 1–2 months before expiration
  • Requirements: Employment contract, social security contributions (AVS/AI/IPG 5.3% employee), active LAMal

In the Swiss system of foreign permits, the residence permit B represents the most common category for those working in the country. It is a renewable authorization that allows foreign citizens to reside and work in Switzerland permanently, while still being subject to periodic verification.

Management is divided between the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) at the federal level and local cantonal authorities. In the canton of Vaud, as in other cantons, the cantonal migration office processes applications, issues, and renews permits according to federal and cantonal laws.

The B permit: basic features

The B permit allows foreign citizens to reside continuously in Switzerland, provided they maintain regular employment and meet social security requirements. Unlike the L permit (short duration, up to 1 year), the B permit is renewable and is intended for those who wish to settle in the country for a medium to long period.

Operational details

Renewal procedure and general requirements

The renewal of the B permit must generally be initiated 1–2 months before the expiry date. There is no single national date; each cantonal office sets its own processing times. Those who forget to renew and continue to work illegally expose themselves and their employer to administrative sanctions and potential revocation of authorizations.

The fundamental requirements for maintaining an active B permit include:

  • A valid employment contract with an employer resident in Switzerland
  • Regular payment of social security contributions (AVS/AI/IPG, unemployment contributions, accident insurance)
  • Subscription and maintenance of health insurance (LAMal) — mandatory within 3 months of arrival and to be maintained continuously for all residents
  • No legal impediments (relevant criminal records, tax debts)

The role of social security and insurance

Those working in Switzerland automatically pay 5.3% of AVS/AI/IPG contributions on the employee part (the employer pays a total of 10.6%). These contributions are tracked by employers and communicated to migration offices during permit renewal. A gap in social security contributions results in the rejection of the renewal application.

The LAMal health insurance is private and based on per capita premiums: the premium depends on the canton, the tariff region, age, and the chosen deductible. It is neither a tax nor a salary contribution — it is a policy taken out directly with an insurer. The migration office verifies that all residents have active coverage. Those without LAMal at the time of the renewal request will see their application rejected. If you wish to consult the LAMal health insurance options for the canton of Vaud, dedicated tools are available for your canton of residence.

Key points

How to start the renewal process in Vaud

Step 1: Check the expiry of your permit The number and expiry date are visible on the physical document of the permit. Do not wait until the last day: renewal takes time and involves administrative procedures.

Step 2: Gather the documents Prepare:

  • Official application form of the canton of Vaud (available on the website of the cantonal migration office)
  • Copy of the current employment contract
  • Latest pay slips (last 3–6 months, as requested)
  • Tax regularity certificate (if requested by the canton)
  • Proof of active LAMal coverage (insurer's document)
  • Valid passport or identity document

Step 3: Submit the application The application can generally be submitted in two ways:

  • In person at the migration office of the canton of Vaud
  • By post, if the canton allows it (check the official website)

The complete form and the payment of any administrative fees follow the instructions of the local office.

Step 4: Wait and renew Processing the application generally takes 4–8 weeks. The office will notify the outcome by letter or email. If approved, the new permit will be delivered or collected at the office.

Where to go in the canton of Vaud

The canton of Vaud has a Service de la population — Office de la migration with offices in Lausanne and other locations. The exact coordinates, opening hours, and submission methods are available on the canton's official website (Vaud canton domain, Migration and Population section).

It is not advisable to rely on private agencies for renewal: the procedure is managed directly and free of charge by the cantonal office. External agencies may charge unnecessary fees.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long before the expiry do I have to apply for the renewal of the B permit in the canton of Vaud?
The standard practice is to start the application 1–2 months before the deadline. The exact times are communicated by the cantonal migration office. If the application is submitted too late, the process will not have time to complete, exposing you to the risk of expiry and illegitimacy of residence.
If I lose my job while I have a B permit, what should I do?
You must notify the migration change within 14 days. If you remain registered as unemployed at the Swiss employment office and keep your social security contributions, the B permit may continue; otherwise, it may not be renewed or may be revoked.
Is health insurance (LAMal) included in permit B or do I have to take it out separately?
LAMal is a private policy taken out separately with a Swiss insurer. It is mandatory within 3 months of arrival and must be maintained continuously. The migration office checks that you have active cover during your permit renewal.
What is the difference between permit B and permit C in the canton of Vaud?
The B permit is renewable annually or biennially. The C permit is a permanent residence, generally granted after 10 years of legal residence in Switzerland (5 years for EU/EFTA citizens). Permit C does not require periodic renewal.
Can I apply for renewal online in the canton of Vaud?
The mode depends on the policies of the canton. Some cantons accept online applications through dedicated portals; others require paper or in-person submission. Check the current procedures on the official website of the Vaud migration office.
