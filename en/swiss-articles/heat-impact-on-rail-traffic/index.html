High temperatures affect the operation of trains in Switzerland. Learn about the practical implications and how railway companies are handling the situation.

Context

In short - Intense heat causes air conditioning system failures and engine overheating. - The rails may deform due to thermal expansion. - Swiss railway companies are taking measures to ensure a stable service. ## Key facts - What : Heat affects rail traffic. - When: In the last few days. - Where: All over Switzerland. - Who: Swiss railway companies. - Amount: Not specified. The great heat of these days also has repercussions on rail traffic. When the air conditioning is running at full capacity, faults occur more often. The tracks may also deform or contact line problems may occur. However, Swiss railway companies have so far managed to guarantee a stable service even in these extreme conditions. In recent days, SBB has encountered some disruptions to railway infrastructure and rolling stock caused by the heat, such as overheating of traction engines or failures of air conditioning systems. According to what the company announced today, the affected trains were immediately replaced, so that the defective equipment could be changed. ### Practical implications The heat also dilates the steel of the rails and there is a risk of deformations of the tracks and exchanges. Operational details

Practical analysis High temperatures represent a significant challenge for Swiss railway companies. Intense heat can cause overheating of traction motors and failures of air conditioning systems, as reported by SBB. These issues can lead to service delays and outages, although so far companies have been able to manage the situation effectively. Another critical aspect is the deformation of the rails and exchanges due to the thermal expansion of the steel. This phenomenon was observed at the central station in Zurich, where it was necessary to regulate an exchange. Although delays have been slight, these episodes underscore the importance of constantly monitoring rail infrastructure during heatwaves. The BLS reported that the heat represents a huge load for vehicles, especially for electronics and air conditioning systems. Although the air conditioning systems may stop working in isolated cases, the company has ensured that they will be repaired immediately. This commitment is critical to ensuring passenger comfort and service safety. Südostbahn (SOB) reported that all its trains are equipped with air conditioners and that, so far, no significant disruptions have been reported. The sob is able to monitor the systems and the temperature in the trains through remote diagnosis, allowing you to intervene quickly
