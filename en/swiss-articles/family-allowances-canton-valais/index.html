The Canton of Valais offers monthly family allowances for each dependent child. These allowances are intended to support families with children under the age of 16 or up to the age of 20 if they attend school. The application procedure is managed by the competent clearing house, which evaluates the legal conditions and calculates the monthly amounts.

Legal Conditions

The legal conditions for receiving family allowances in the Canton of Valais include residence in the canton and possession of a valid residence permit. In addition, the parents must be insured at a Swiss compensation fund and the children must be dependent on the parents.

Application Procedure

The application procedure for family allowances in the Canton of Valais provides for the submission of an application to the competent compensation fund. The application must be accompanied by documents attesting residence, residence permit and family situation. The clearinghouse evaluates the demand and, if approved, calculates the monthly amounts to be paid.

Action

To benefit from family allowances in the Canton of Valais, families must follow a specific application procedure. The first step is to verify the eligibility conditions and gather the necessary documents, such as the residence permit and certificates of residence.

Application Procedure

The application procedure for family allowances in the Canton of Valais involves submitting a request to the competent compensation fund. Competent Compensation Fund

The competent compensation fund for managing family allowances in the Canton of Valais is responsible for evaluating applications and calculating monthly amounts. Families should contact the compensation fund for detailed information on the application procedure and eligibility conditions.

Monthly Amounts

The monthly amounts of family allowances in the Canton of Valais vary depending on the number of dependent children and the family's economic situation. The amounts are calculated by the competent compensation fund and can be consulted at the fund itself or on the official website of the canton.
