Operational details

Apprenticeship in the Canton of Obvaldo is a vocational training program that lasts from 1 to 3 years. This program is aimed at providing young generations with the necessary skills to enter the world of work and to develop the professional skills of existing employees.

The Obvaldo Canton is an important economic and financial centre of Switzerland, with a strong presence of companies from sectors such as the textile industry, crafts and technology. Apprenticeship is therefore an opportunity for young generations to enter this dynamic and competitive job market.

Apprenticeship in the Canton Obvaldo is regulated by the Employment Relations Act of 12 June 1943 (LRL), which establishes the general principles for employment relations in the private sector. The law provides that the apprenticeship is a professional training contract that lasts from 1 to 3 years and that it is stipulated between the apprentice and the company.

The apprenticeship contract must contain the following elements:

  • the duration of the apprenticeship
  • the content of vocational training
  • working conditions and remuneration
  • the methods of evaluation and certification of the training course

The apprentice is entitled to receive a monthly salary that cannot be less than one third of the minimum wage established by law. The company is required to provide the apprentice with a written and signed apprenticeship contract.
