Hyper-depreciation: via confirmation communications (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 21 July 2026

The executive decree for the confirmation of the investments of the Transition Plan 5.0 has been published. Here's how to proceed through the GSE portal.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Directorial decree published by MIMIT for the Transition Plan 5.0.
  • Communications confirming operational investments from 21 July.
  • Submission procedure managed entirely through the official GSE portal.

Key facts

  • What: Communications confirming investments Transition Plan 5.0
  • When: From 21 July
  • Where: GSE web portal
  • Who: MIMIT and GSE
  • Document: Directorial Decree of 20 July

The Ministry of Business and Made in Italy (MIMIT) has officially launched the procedures for confirmation communications relating to the investments envisaged by the New Transition Plan 5.0. Through a recent press release, the dicastery announced that the executive decree necessary to start the operations has been duly signed. This regulatory act, which incorporates the provisions contained in the Ministerial Decree of 7 May last, allows companies to proceed with the required obligations from the date of 21 July. The process is part of the broader framework of national policies to encourage technological investment and energy efficiency.

Practical Implications for Investment Management

The transition to the confirmation phase represents a crucial moment for the financial planning of companies. The correct execution of this fulfillment allows to consolidate the position of the company with respect to the tax benefits associated with the Transition Plan 5.0. Compared to ordinary procedures, the current confirmation system requires a precise synchronization between the time schedule of the investments made and the documentation uploaded to the GSE platform. Companies must ensure that each individual item of expenditure is consistent with what was declared in the preliminary stages of access to the incentive. A discrepancy between what was planned and what was confirmed could result in delays in the delivery of benefits or the need for costly documentary corrections in terms of time and administrative resources.

The digitization of the process, managed through the GSE portal, aims to drastically reduce the investigation time, allowing a faster cross-check between the data provided by the company and the ministerial directives. For companies operating in a competitive market, optimizing these tax processes is not only a bureaucratic obligation, but a strategic lever to maintain corporate liquidity and plan future investments in innovation. Operational Procedure and Deadlines

To proceed correctly with the confirmation of investments, companies must follow a structured process on the GSE portal. The first step is to access the reserved area using the enabled corporate credentials. Once authenticated, select the specific section dedicated to the New Transition 5.0 Plan. Here, the user must upload the documentation required by the decree, paying close attention to the requested formats and the completeness of the data. It is recommended to prepare all the necessary documentation in advance to complete the submission in a single session of work, reducing the risk of errors or expired sessions that could compromise the submission of data. Each stage of the compilation must be internally validated before submitting definitively to the central system.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which companies can access the confirmation of investments?
Access is reserved for companies that have made investments under the New Transition Plan 5.0 and have already completed the preliminary phases. The confirmation procedure is mandatory to formalise the investment and benefit from the benefits provided, following the detailed instructions provided by the GSE on the dedicated portal.
What happens if mistakes are made during the procedure on the GSE portal?
Errors in the compilation or submission of data can cause significant delays in the investigation of the case or, in the most serious cases, exclusion from tax benefits. It is essential to verify the correctness of any data entered before final confirmation. In case of doubt, the operating instructions provided by the GSE represent the official guide for each administrative step.
What is the deadline for submitting communications?
The directorial decree, in implementation of the ministerial decree of 7 May, established the start of confirmation communications from 21 July. Companies are invited not to postpone the fulfillment to avoid the congestion of the portal and ensure the correct handling of the demand by the competent offices of the GSE.
