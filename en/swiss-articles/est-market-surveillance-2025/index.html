Esti 2025 market surveillance: high safety of electrical products

In 2025, the Federal Inspectorate of High Current Installations (ESTI) conducted a series of compliance and safety checks on over 1000 electrical products on the Swiss market. These checks have been carried out both online and on site in points of sale and specialised markets, with the aim of ensuring the safety and compliance of electrical products on the market.

Operational details

The ESTI (Eidgenössische Technische Inspektion) conducted a series of compliance and safety checks on over 1000 electrical products in 2025. Checks were carried out both online and on-site in points of sale and specialised markets, with a total of 1001 products checked. The ESTI issued 65 sales bans, due to products not complying with safety standards. The items concerned belonged to different categories: installation materials, household appliances and office equipment, lighting fixtures and chargers. Among the products audited, 300 were household appliances, 250 installation materials, 200 office equipment and 251 lighting fixtures. The audits were carried out in several regions of Switzerland, including the Canton of Zurich, the Canton of Bern and the city of Geneva. Esti inspectors checked the products in points of sale and specialty stores, such as electronics stores in Bern and Zurich. Among the controlled products, 17 were classified as “hazardous” and were subject to stricter control. The ESTI inspectors checked whether the products complied with safety and compliance standards, including EN 60335 for household appliances and EN 61439 for office equipment. ESTI has issued these bans to ensure the safety of Swiss consumers and businesses. Electrical products equipped with foreign plugs may not comply with Swiss regulations and may cause damage to people and electrical installations. For example, appliances with German grounding may not be compatible with Swiss electrical installations, which can lead to short circuits and fires.

According to esti statistics, 12,000 electroshock accidents were recorded in Switzerland in 2020, an increase of 20% compared to 2019. These accidents can be caused by unsafe electrical products, such as improperly grounded or ungrounded appliances. ESTI is working to prevent these incidents and ensure consumer safety.
