Switzerland Bets on Europe

Switzerland has sent a signal in favour of Europe and the economy according to the international press, following the rejection of the 'No to a Switzerland of 10 million' initiative. The decision was seen as a choice of stability and openness.

The 'No to a Switzerland of 10 million' initiative was launched in 2022 and proposed reducing the number of foreign residents in Switzerland to 10% of the total population. The proposal was controversial and had raised concerns among Swiss exporters and European authorities.

The vote took place on 26 February, and official data showed that the majority of voters (55.6%) expressed themselves against the initiative. According to data from the Swiss Federal Statistical Office (FSO), Switzerland has 8.7 million inhabitants, of whom approximately 2.4 million are foreign residents.

The decision to reject the initiative was seen as a choice of stability and openness by the international press. "Switzerland is an open and stable country, and this decision shows that we are ready to work with other European countries to promote economic growth and cooperation," stated the President of the Confederation, Alain Berset.

Switzerland has a long history of interaction with Europe, and has already negotiated several trade and free trade agreements with EU member states. Switzerland is a member of the Coordinated Agricultural Policy (ACP) and the European Free Trade Association (EFTA).
