Key points

Procedure for verifying an opening

To obtain reliable information about store opening hours in the Canton of Zurich, it is advisable to follow a simple sequence and always refer to the official source. The procedure does not require inventing a calendar or automatically applying rules valid elsewhere.

Operational checklist

1. Define the case. Establish whether the request concerns ordinary business hours, a Sunday, a public holiday or an exception.

2. Identify the competent level. The verification may concern the Canton, the municipality or another competent institution. The territorial reference alone is not enough.

3. Consult the Canton's official source. Look for the rule applicable to business hours and verify whether the situation in question is directly regulated.

4. Check the conditions. If the case is special, verify the requirements, deadlines and any amounts indicated in the official source. These data must not be replaced with values derived from other Cantons.

5. Identify the competent office. When the opening falls within an exception, the request must be referred to the office indicated for that case and to the relevant special authorizations.

This sequence is useful both for those organizing a store's operations and for those who need to plan an opening on a Sunday or public holiday. If the official source does not provide the information sought, it is not correct to turn the absence of information into hours valid for everyone. The answer must remain anchored to the specified authority and the requirements actually available.
