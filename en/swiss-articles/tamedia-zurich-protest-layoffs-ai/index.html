On Tuesday at noon, more than 100 Tamedia employees gathered outside the publishing house's headquarters on Werdstrasse in Zurich. The demonstration protested the layoffs announced in recent weeks. The workers called for an immediate halt to the job cuts, also receiving support from Raphael Golta, president of the city of Zurich and a member of the SP.

Speaking in front of the workers, Golta recalled his connection to the Zurich daily newspaper. He explained that he reads it every morning to follow events around the world. The former journalist said he was concerned about the future of local journalism and declared that he wanted to continue reading articles written by professionals rather than produced by artificial intelligence systems.

The newsrooms' demands

The company management came under fire during the mobilization. According to the newsrooms, the consequences of wrong decisions and ineffective strategies systematically fell on employees. Another point criticized was the progressive reduction in resources available for editorial work. Among the signs displayed was the slogan «Journalism instead of AI», which sums up the demand to invest in the work of information professionals.

Staff from Tamedia's French-speaking Switzerland newsrooms also joined the mobilization. On Tuesday, the French-speaking colleagues sent the Zurich employees affected by the cuts a letter expressing solidarity.

About two weeks ago, the company announced the elimination of 34 full-time positions in the newsrooms of German-speaking Switzerland and French-speaking Switzerland; the measure will lead to 41 employees being laid off. Staff are now asking that the consultation procedure, already planned in French-speaking Switzerland, also be extended to German-speaking Switzerland.
