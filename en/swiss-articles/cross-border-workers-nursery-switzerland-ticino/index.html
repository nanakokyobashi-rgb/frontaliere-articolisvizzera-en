Border workers with children: nurseries in Switzerland and Ticino (cross-border guide)

Practical guide for border workers with children: costs, waiting lists, subsidies and alternatives for nurseries in Ticino

Context

In short - Border workers can register their children at Ticino kindergartens - Waiting lists vary depending on the municipality - Subsidies are provided for low-income families ## Key facts - What: Registration at Ticino kindergartens for border workers - When: Waiting lists open all year - Where: Municipalities of Ticino - Who: Border workers with children - Amount: Variable subsidies depending on income In the Canton of Ticino, border workers can register their children at municipal kindergartens. For example, in Lugano, the monthly fee can vary from 800 to 1,500 francs depending on family income. Registration procedures and waiting lists vary from municipality to municipality. It is possible to inquire directly at the competent municipal offices. According to the cantonal Ticino law on early childhood education of 2018, low-income families can benefit from subsidies. In Bellinzona, for example, the subsidy can cover up to 70% of the fee. Border workers can inquire at the municipal offices to find out the specific conditions and documents necessary for registration. > "Border workers are welcome in Ticino kindergartens, provided they comply with the registration procedures and provide the required documentation," says the director of the Education Office of the Republic and Canton of Ticino.

Operational details

Waiting lists and registrations Waiting lists for Ticino kindergartens are generally open all year round. Parents can apply directly online or at the municipal offices. The priority of inclusion varies depending on municipal policies. For example, in Lugano, applications are accepted online all year round, while in Bellinzona you need to contact the municipal office directly. ### Costs and subsidies The costs of nurseries vary depending on the municipality and family income. Subsidies are provided for low-income families. For example, in 2022, the Canton of Ticino allocated around 14 million francs for kindergarten subsidies. Border families can apply for these benefits by submitting the necessary documentation, such as a work certificate and tax return. > "Subsidies are key to ensuring access to childcare services for even the most vulnerable families" In some cantons, such as Zurich, subsidies can cover up to 90% of the total costs for very low-income families. To apply, you must submit the required documents within the deadlines set by the municipality or canton.

Key points

Contact directly the kindergarten office of the municipality of interest, such as in Zurich where the average monthly cost for a kindergarten place is about CHF 1,200, but it can vary greatly depending on the family income and allowances received. 2. Consult the municipality's website for information regarding waiting lists and registration requirements; for example, the Canton of Ticino offers a maximum contribution of CHF 550 per month for border workers with children registered in nurseries, provided that the family income does not exceed certain limits set annually. 3. Use the subsidy calculator for an estimate of the subsidies you might be entitled to, considering factors such as income and family composition. > “Advance planning is critical to securing a place at kindergarten, given the high demand and waiting lists that can range from a few months to over a year in cities like Geneva.” It is important to plan in advance for enrollment, given the variability of waiting lists; an operational checklist may include: verification of enrollment requirements, calculation of available subsidies, contact with the competent nursery office.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I register my child for kindergarten if I work in Switzerland but reside in Italy?
Yes, border workers can register their children at Ticino kindergartens. It is necessary to inquire about the specific procedures of the municipality of interest.
How do I apply for kindergarten benefits?
Subsidies can be requested by submitting the necessary documentation to the nursery office of the municipality. Requirements vary by municipality.
How long does it take to get a kindergarten seat?
The waiting time varies depending on the municipality and the availability of seats. It is advisable to inquire directly at the municipal office.
