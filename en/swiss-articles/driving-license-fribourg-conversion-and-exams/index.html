Conversion Requirements

To convert your foreign driving license in the Canton of Freiburg, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Have a valid driver's license issued by another country
  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Have a good knowledge of German (or French, depending on the area of residence)
  • Have a residence address in the Canton of Freiburg
