September 2026 promises to be the month of the environment in Collina d 'Oro, with a rich programme of initiatives dedicated to sustainability and quality of life. The Municipality invites citizens and families to actively participate, promoting responsible and environmentally friendly behaviour.

The central event will be the eighth edition of Environment Day, scheduled for Saturday 12 September from 2 pm to 6 pm at the Recreation Centre, surrounded by nature. The Village of the Environment will host numerous thematic stands dedicated to renewable energy, sustainable mobility and energy renovation of buildings. Participants include Pedibus/Agisco, Pro Velo Ticino, Svizzera Energia, Ticino Energia, Polizia Ceresio Sud, Publibike, TCS, IGSU, ConProBio, ACR, Afior di Gusto and an information stand of the Municipality of Collina d 'Oro.

During the day it will be possible to taste the local water at the Bar dell 'Acqua and participate in the "zero waste transformations – garden in brine" atelier at the Afior di Gusto gazebo, with tastings of Ticino products and wines.

Operational details

September? It's the month of the environment

Environment Day represents the core of the program, but the calendar also includes other initiatives dedicated to sustainable mobility, reuse, waste management, biodiversity, and the fight against food waste.

Cyclomania returns, the national cycling challenge between municipalities promoted by Pro Velo Switzerland. Participating is simple: by cycling, points are collected, and you can compete for prize draws. The municipality proposes three tours starting from the recreational center. The first is the Family Bike Tour on Saturday, September 12, at 9:30 AM, with a route suitable for families; the second, on Sunday, September 20, at 9:30 AM, will be the Endurance Tour, with 32 km through forests, dirt roads, and secondary roads. Finally, the Enduro Experience on Sunday, September 27, also at 9:30 AM: a route dedicated to experienced bikers on the trails.

These initiatives not only promote sustainability but also offer opportunities for the Collina d'Oro community to actively participate in a greener and more responsible future.

Initiatives for Sustainable Mobility

Switzerland has always been at the forefront of promoting sustainable mobility. According to the Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN), in 2022, 27% of trips in Switzerland were made by bicycle, with a 15% increase compared to 2021. Initiatives like Cyclomania contribute to this positive trend. The Canton of Zurich, for example, invested 100 million francs in 2023 to improve cycling infrastructure, with the goal of reaching 30% of trips by bicycle by 2030.

Key points

September? It's Environment Month

To participate in the initiatives of the month of the environment, citizens can consult the detailed program on the website of the Municipality of Collina d 'Oro. You can sign up for bike tours and workshops directly online or at the Recreation Centre. Participation is open to all, and the community is encouraged to take an active part in these initiatives to contribute to sustainability and quality of life.

For more information and to register for the activities, visit the website of the Municipality of Collina d 'Oro or contact the Recreation Centre directly. Participation in these initiatives is an important step towards a more sustainable and responsible future for all the citizens of Collina d 'Oro.

Initiatives and Activities

The month of the environment in Collina d 'Oro offers a variety of activities that involve the whole community. Among the most popular initiatives are cycling tours, which thisyear include routes of 10, 20 and 30 kilometres, with departures from the Recreation Centre. These tours not only promote physical activity, but also the discovery of the natural landscapes of the canton of Ticino.

Environmental ateliers are another main attraction. Thisyear, the Municipality has organised workshops on topics such as domestic composting, plastic waste reduction and the creation of vertical gardens. Each atelier can accommodate up to 30 participants, at a cost of CHF 10 per person.
