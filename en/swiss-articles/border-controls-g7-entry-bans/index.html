Context

In short - Reinforced border controls between France and Switzerland - 11 entry bans on Switzerland - Hundreds of dangerous items seized ## Key facts - What: Reinforced border controls - When: During the Evian G7 - Where: France-Switzerland border - Who: Geneva State Council - Amount: 11 entry bans, hundreds of items seized Reinforced border controls between France and Switzerland, carried out as part of the recent Evian G7, led to 11 entry bans on the Confederation. This was announced today by the State Council of Geneva, according to which almost 120 reports of people wanted by the authorities have been collected, of which about a hundred by the French. With regard to hazardous materials, more than 160 seizures have been carried out, for a total of hundreds of objects.
