The network remains central

The St. Gallen Cantonal Bank (SGKB) confirms the central role of its network of 37 physical branches. This is the position expressed by CEO Christian Schmid in an interview published by Finanz und Wirtschaft, while the Swiss banking landscape is described as evolving.

Schmid, 57, linked the role of branches to proximity to the local area, networks of contacts and roots in the region. For the bank, this presence is a strength, and no downsizing of the network is planned.

The bank considers proximity to the local area, relationships built locally and regional roots to be its strength and categorically rules out any downsizing of its branch network.

The main account as a goal

Schmid approaches competition from neobanks, including Revolut, with a certain calm. The manager identifies defending its status as the main bank as the goal. The reasoning concerns the number-one current account: whoever holds it, according to Schmid, remains the main financial point of reference in the customer’s life.

The executive’s profile includes a doctorate earned at HSG, the University of St. Gallen. In the interview, this element accompanies the assessments of competition and the bank’s position in the region.

The next strategic update

An update to the corporate strategy is scheduled for February. The source does not preview any other details of the plan, but reports that the operating model aimed at the domestic market in German-speaking Switzerland, Germany, institutional investors and external asset managers is producing excellent results, according to management.

The physical network, the main account and the scope of the business model are therefore the points highlighted in the interview. The February update is the step indicated for the strategy’s continuation, with no advance content in the source.
