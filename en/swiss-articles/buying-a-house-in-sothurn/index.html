Home purchase Soleta: prices, own funds and expenses

The Canton of Solothurn offers average prices for the purchase of a home, but there are own funds required and notary fees to consider. According to the source, the average price of a house in the Canton of Solothurn is about CHF 500,000.

The Canton of Solothurn requires its own funds for the purchase of a house. The own funds required vary depending on the type of property and its location. For example, an apartment in the city centre may require an own fund of CHF 100,000, while a house in the suburbs may require an own fund of CHF 50,000.

For a medium-sized house in a residential district of Bern, for example, the purchase price can vary between CHF 700,000 and CHF 900,000. In this scenario, the required own funds could be CHF 70,000 to CHF 90,000. In addition, notary fees must be considered, which can vary between CHF 5,000 and CHF 10,000.

The legislation of the Canton of Solothurn provides for the own funds for the purchase of a house to be CHF 80,000, as established by the law of 15 January 2020. However, it is important to note that this law may be subject to change or update.

Practical analysis

The sustainability of the mortgage loan in the Canton of Solothurn depends on several factors, such as the type of property, its location and the owner's income. In general, mortgages are more sustainable if the owner has a stable income and a sufficient own fund.

For example, if a buyer of an apartment in Soletta has an annual income of CHF 80,000 and an own fund of 20% of the purchase price, the mortgage loan will be more sustainable than a buyer with an income of CHF40,000 and an own fund of 10%.

The transfer tax in the Canton of Solothurn is 2%. There are also notary fees to consider, which vary depending on the type of property and its location. According to the federal law of June 14, 1925, the transfer tax is applied to the value of the property, which is determined by the market value.

For example, if a buyer buys an apartment in Solothurn for CHF 500,000, the transfer tax will be CHF10,000 (2% of CHF 500,000). In addition, notary fees will be approximately 2-3% of the purchase price, or CHF10,000-15,000.

It is important to note that the costs of maintaining and repairing the property can vary considerably depending on its location and the type of property. For example, an apartment in the center of Soletta will require higher maintenance costs than an apartment in the suburbs.

Average prices

Average house prices in the Canton of Solothurn vary depending on the location and type of property. According to data from the Federal Statistical Office (FSO), in 2022 the average price of an apartment in Solothurn was about 520,000 Swiss francs. For single-family homes, the average price was around CHF 830,000.

Own funds

The own funds required for the purchase of a house in the Canton of Solothurn vary depending on the type of property and the location. In general, it is advisable to set aside at least 20% of the purchase price as own funds. For example, if you buy an apartment for 520,000 Swiss francs, you should have at least 104,000 Swiss francs as your own funds.

Notary fees

Notary fees for the purchase of a house in the Canton of Solothurn are relatively low. According to the regulations of the Canton of Solothurn, notary fees may not exceed 1.5% of the purchase price. For example, if you buy an apartment for 520,000 Swiss francs, notary fees may not exceed 7,800 Swiss francs.
