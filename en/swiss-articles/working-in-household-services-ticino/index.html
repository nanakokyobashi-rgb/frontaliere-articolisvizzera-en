Working as a household service worker in Ticino (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 17 June 2026

Discover how to work as a household service worker in Ticino, Switzerland. Requirements, tasks and practical advice.

Context

Working in domestic service in Ticino can be an interesting option for those looking for domestic employment. Domestic service workers are responsible for performing various tasks within homes, such as cleaning, meal preparation, and resident assistance. To work in this sector, it's essential to have a good knowledge of the Italian or German language, depending on the region where the home is located. Tasks may vary depending on the needs of the family or individual resident. This job requires attention to detail and the ability to work independently.

Operational details

Practical implications

Working as a household service worker in Ticino can have various practical implications. First, it’s important to consider the distance between the workplace and your residence. For example, if you live in Lugano and work in Bellinzona, you may need to use public transport, such as the train, which takes about 30 minutes, or a personal vehicle to reach the workplace.

  • The average distance between Ticino cities can range from 20 to 50 km, so it’s essential to plan travel times.
  • Additionally, it’s crucial to be flexible and available to work at different times, including weekends and bank holidays.

Knowledge of local languages can be a significant advantage, as it facilitates communication with employers and other family members. In Switzerland, especially in the canton of Ticino, it’s useful to know Italian, but a bit of German or French can also be helpful.

"Flexibility is the key to successfully working in household services in Ticino."

📊 As of 1 January 2023, the minimum wage in Switzerland is 4'020 CHF per month. However, for domestic workers, the wage may vary depending on experience and duties.

⚠️ It’s important to verify local and national regulations regarding domestic work, such as the Domestic Work Law (L‑LD) of 20 March 2019.

Operational checklist:

  • Verify the distance between the workplace and your residence
  • Plan travel times
  • Be flexible and available to work at different times
  • Know the local languages
  • Verify local and national regulations regarding domestic work
  • Establish a clear and detailed employment contract with the employer.

Key points

What to do concretely

To work as a household service employee in Ticino, it is advisable: 1. To have previous experience in household service or a similar sector, such as in the case of a home care assistant. 2. To have a good knowledge of the Italian or German language, particularly in the Ticino region where Italian is the predominant language. 3. To be available to work flexible hours, considering that families may require services at different times, such as in Lugano or Bellinzona. 4. To have a positive attitude and be willing to learn, especially with regard to the specific needs of families. 5. To look for job postings on online platforms such as Indeed or at local employment agencies like the Regional Employment Office (URL) of the Canton of Ticino.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main duties of a domestic service worker?
The main duties include cleaning, meal preparation, resident assistance, and other domestic activities.
Is a work permit required to work in domestic services in Ticino?
Yes, a work permit is required to work in Switzerland. It's essential to inquire about the procedures to obtain it.
How can I find a job as a domestic service worker in Ticino?
You can search for job postings on online platforms, at local employment agencies, or through personal referrals.
