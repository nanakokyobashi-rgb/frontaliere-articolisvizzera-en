Starting an economic activity in the Canton of Svitto requires precise planning that starts from the choice of the legal form that best suits the needs of the business. The Swiss landscape offers several options, including sole proprietorship, limited liability company (Sagl) and public limited company (SA). Each choice has different implications in terms of responsibility, minimum capital required and obligations towards the trade register. In the Canton of Svitto, as throughout the national territory, registration is not a mere bureaucratic act, but the legal basis for operating in the Swiss market, guaranteeing transparency and security in relations with third parties.

Operational details

A practical analysis of starting a business in the Canton of Schwyz must necessarily consider the tax burden and social security obligations weighing on the structure. The Swiss tax system, structured across three levels (federal, cantonal, and municipal), requires accurate planning of financial flows. Taxes are determined by the multiplier applied at the municipal level to the cantonal burden, making the choice of the operational headquarters a significant factor for the overall cost of the activity. It is necessary to clearly distinguish the competencies of the authorities: the Federal Tax Administration (FTA) manages direct federal tax and VAT, while cantonal administrations manage local direct taxes. The FSIO does not deal with taxation, but is the reference body for social security.

Key points

To concretely start a business in the Canton of Schwyz, the first operational step is to draft the articles of incorporation or the deed of foundation. At this stage, it is essential to precisely define the corporate purpose and internal organization. Subsequently, it is necessary to open a transit bank account for the deposit of the share capital. Once the payment is completed, the bank issues the certificate required for registration in the commercial register. In parallel, it is fundamental to define the registered office and sign commercial lease agreements, keeping in mind that, in accordance with federal law (CO), the security deposit cannot exceed three months' rent and must be paid into a blocked account in the tenant's name. The termination of premises follows current regulations, with the mandatory use of the official cantonal form in case of withdrawal by the landlord.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main steps to start a business in Svitto?
The process starts with the choice of legal form, such as sole proprietorship, SAGL or SA. It is necessary to draw up the articles of association, define the corporate purpose and the internal organisation. It is then necessary to open a transit bank account for the deposit of the share capital, obtain the bank certificate and proceed with the mandatory registration at the Cantonal Trade Register Office, subject to verification of the availability of the company name.
What are the contributory obligations for the employer?
The employer must pay the social contributions AVS/AI/IPG, equal to 10.6% of the gross salary, distributed equally (5.3% to be paid by the employer and 5.3% to be paid by the worker). Added to these are unemployment insurance (AD) of 1.1% and accident insurance (LAINF), varying between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the sector. For employees above a certain income threshold, it is also mandatory to join the occupational pension scheme (LPP/BVG) with increasing rates according to age.
When is registration in the trade register mandatory?
Registration is always mandatory for joint-stock companies such as SA and Sagl. With regard to the sole proprietorship, the obligation to register in the commercial register is triggered when certain annual turnovers are exceeded. Registration gives the company the legal personality or commercial identity necessary to contract obligations and regularly invoice customers.
