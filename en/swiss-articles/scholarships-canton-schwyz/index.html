Canton Svitto offers several scholarships for students wishing to continue their studies in Switzerland. The scholarships are funded by the State and are available to students from all Swiss cantons. Scholarships are awarded based on criteria such as meritocracy, poverty and disability.

Scholarship Requirements

To obtain a scholarship in the canton of Schwyz, students must meet the following requirements:

  • Be Swiss citizens or have the citizenship of an EU member country
  • Have at least 15 college credits completed
  • Enroll in a course of study at a Swiss university or high school
  • Have no previous criminal convictions
  • Do not have a monthly income greater than CHF 2,500

Scholarship Amounts

Scholarships in the canton of Schwyz vary depending on the type of course of study and the financial condition of the student. Here are some examples of scholarship amounts:

  • Scholarships for secondary school students: CHF 500-1,500 per month
  • Scholarships for university students: CHF 1,500-3,000 per month
  • Scholarships for students with disabilities: CHF2,000-4,000 per month

To be eligible for scholarships, students must meet the following requirements:

  • Be Italian citizens
  • Have a good level of knowledge of German (or French, depending on the university)
  • Be admitted to a bachelor's or master's degree program at a university in the canton of Switzerland

Concrete examples of scholarships offered by the canton of Switzerland include:
