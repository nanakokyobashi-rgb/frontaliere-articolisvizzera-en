Residence permit C: permanent right of residence

Residence permit C represents the highest level of legal protection for foreigners residing in Switzerland. Unlike the B permit (residence, renewable every 5 years), the C permit is normally granted after 10 years of continuous residence in the country and is permanent, not subject to periodic renewal. For citizens of the European Union and the European Free Trade Association (EFTA, including Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway), the term is reduced to 5 years of uninterrupted residence.

Operational details

Integration and language skills

Canton Schaffhausen, like all cantons in German-speaking Switzerland, requires proven proficiency in the German language as an essential criterion for the issuance of the C permit. There is no single federal standard for languages: each canton calibrates its criteria according to the official cantonal language. The Schaffhausen authorities assess the language through direct conversations with officials or, in some cases, through certificates of completed courses (level A2-B1 of the European Framework of Reference for Languages).

Integration is not limited to language proficiency alone. The authorities also examine active participation in community and cultural activities, positive dialogue with neighbours, stable and continuous work involvement, and strong family ties in the cantonal territory. A person who resides 10 years without interruption, but socially isolated and without future plans in the canton, might encounter difficulties in issuing the C permit.

Differences from permit B

Permit B (residence) is renewable every 5 years and remains subject to the economic situation of the holder, i.e. stable employment or proven financial independence. The C permit, once granted by the federal authority, is not subject to periodic renewal: it is permanent and transferable between cantons. Practical procedure: how to apply for a C permit in Schaffhausen

1. Eligibility check: Verify that at least 10 years (or 5 for EU/EFTA) of uninterrupted residence have passed. The counting date starts from the day of official registration in the municipality of domicile.

2. Document collection: Prepare permanent residence certificates (from the municipality), employment and salary statements (last 2-3 years), reference letters from employers or the community, proof of language proficiency (official conversation or completed course certificate), and a criminal record certificate (requested from the cantonal judicial office).

3. Written request: Address the formal application to the Migrationsamt (Migration Office) of the Canton of Schaffhausen. The request must contain the reason for the application, a personal integration statement, and include all documentation.

4. Cantonal evaluation: The cantonal office reviews the file within 2-4 weeks. It may request additional information or summon the applicant for an in-depth interview.

5. Federal transmission: If the cantonal evaluation is positive, the file is forwarded to the SEM for federal verification and formal issuance.

6. Notification and collection: The C permit is issued and notified by mail. The original document is collected at the cantonal office within 30 days of communication.

Indicative timelines

From the submission of the application to the issuance of the C permit, approximately 2-3 months are required. However, exact timelines are not yet officially specified at the cantonal level and may vary based on the complexity of the file.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the main difference between permit B and permit C?
The B permit (residence) is renewable every 5 years and is conditional on maintaining employment or economic independence. The C (domicile) permit is permanent, not subject to renewal, and is granted after 10 years of continuous residence (or 5 for EU/EFTA). Once obtained, the holder enjoys an unlimited right of residence, regardless of the economic situation.
How many years of residence are required to obtain the C permit in the Schaffhausen Canton?
As a rule, 10 years of uninterrupted residence in Swiss territory. For citizens of the European Union and EFTA (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway), the period is reduced to 5 years, thanks to the bilateral agreements between Switzerland and the EU on the free movement of persons. Residence must be documented and continuous.
What language skills are needed for the C permit in Schaffhausen?
Canton Schaffhausen, being in German-speaking Switzerland, requires sufficient knowledge of the German language. There is no strict federal standard: the cantonal authorities assess competence through direct interview or course certificates (level A2-B1). Social inclusion and community participation are equally important.
What happens if I commit a crime during the period of residence?
Any entry in the criminal record may constitute an obstacle to the issuance of the permit C. The authorities examine the type of offence, the circumstances, and the time elapsed since the event. Serious crimes related to public order and security result in the refusal of the application.
Does the C permit guarantee the right to a pension in Switzerland after retirement from work?
The C permit guarantees the right of permanent residence regardless of employment. However, access to social security benefits (AVS/AHV, LPP/BVG) depends on contributions paid during working life, not directly on the type of leave. It is advisable to check your social security situation with the AVS cantonal office.
