The Swiss tax system is distinguished by decentralization: while direct federal tax is managed by the Federal Tax Administration (AFC/ESTV) with uniform federal rates, cantonal and municipal taxes vary significantly from canton to canton. In the Inner Appenzell Canton, as in all 26 Swiss cantons, the cantonal tax administration is the entity responsible for the collection and management of local taxes.

Operational details

Allowable deductions and rate structure

The deductions that can be made in the cantonal tax return of Appenzello Interno are subject to cantonal law. However, some categories of deductions are common to most Swiss cantons and reflect the principles of net income taxation.

Deductions generally allowed in the Swiss cantons include:

  • Professional expenses and labour costs (professional clothing, tools, travel for employees)
  • Interest on mortgage loans (for own homes)
  • Health insurance premiums (LAMal/KVG, the mandatory coverage for Swiss residents)
  • Contributions to previdenza professionale (LPP/BVG, the mandatory second pillar)
  • Payments to the third pillar (social security savings accounts 3a), within annual indexed ceilings every year
  • Previous cantonal taxes (in some cantons), union and associative contributions

Each canton then defines its own rate brackets: the system is progressive, so taxpayers with higher taxable income pay a higher percentage rate. In the Inner Appenzell Canton, as in the other cantons, the brackets are fixed by law and may vary every few years with parliamentary adjustments.

Key points

How to present the tax return in Appenzell Innerrhoden

Taxpayers residing in the Canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden must submit a cantonal tax return every year, by the deadline set by the cantonal administration (usually between May and July of the following year after the tax period). The exact deadline and delivery methods (paper or electronic) are communicated by the cantonal tax office.

For 2026 and subsequent years, most Swiss cantons accept digital tax returns through an online portal. The Canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden provides a portal for the cantonal tax office where you can:

  • Download the official tax return form
  • Consult the applicable tax rates and brackets
  • Find instructions for calculating deductions
  • Electronically submit the tax return (if available)
  • View communications and notices from the administration

Step-by-step procedure

Step 1: Gather documentation. You will need a copy of the federal income tax return (if submitted), pay slips for the year, bank statements for interest and dividends, insurance premium receipts (LAMal, accident insurance LAINF/LAA if self-employed), and a copy of the mortgage account statement (if property owner).

Step 2: Contact the cantonal tax office of Appenzell Innerrhoden to receive the official tax return form and instructions. This can be done via email, phone, or through the cantonal online portal.

Step 3: Complete the tax return, indicating the gross income, allowed deductions in the canton (following official instructions), and calculating the taxable income.

Step 4: Submit by the deadline set. If the form is available in digital format, use the cantonal online portal. Otherwise, send via registered mail to the cantonal tax office address.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between federal, cantonal and municipal tax in Inner Appenzell?
Switzerland has 3 tax levels: the direct federal tax (uniform throughout Switzerland, managed by the AFC/ESTV), the cantonal tax (managed by the Inner Appenzell Canton with its own rates) and the municipal tax (managed by the individual municipality, which applies a percentage multiplier to the cantonal tax). The total tax burden therefore depends on the canton and municipality of residence.
What are the main deductions allowed in the cantonal taxes of Appenzello Interno?
Generally accepted deductions include: professional expenses, interest on mortgage loans, health insurance premiums (LAMal/KVG), occupational pension contributions (LPP/BVG), payments to the third pillar (3a) within indexed annual ceilings. Each canton can add its own deductions; for the complete and specific list of Inner Appenzell, consult the cantonal tax administration.
When does the cantonal tax return in Inner Appenzell expire?
The deadline is generally between May and July of the year following the tax period, but varies each year. The exact date is communicated by the cantonal tax administration of Appenzello Interno through the online portal, mailing and public notices. It is advisable to check the specific deadline by contacting the cantonal office or by consulting the official website.
Can I file the Appenzello Interno tax return online?
Yes, most Swiss cantons (including Inner Appenzell) offer an online portal for filing tax returns electronically. The portal is accessible via the site of the cantonal tax administration of Appenzello Interno. Instructions and technical support are provided by the cantonal office.
What happens if I don't submit my cantonal tax return by the deadline?
If the declaration is not submitted by the deadline, the cantonal administration may issue a flat-rate taxation (estimation of the amount due without detailed assessment). In addition, administrative sanctions may apply. It is important to submit the declaration on time; if you have difficulties, contact the administration to obtain an extension (assessed on a case-by-case basis).
