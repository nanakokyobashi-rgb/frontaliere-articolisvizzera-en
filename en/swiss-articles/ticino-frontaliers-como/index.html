Ticino, border crossing from Como focuses on two areas

Ticino, one of the most industrialized and populated Swiss cantons, registers every year a large number of border workers and students who enter the territory for study, work or tourism. According to the investigations of the Department of the territory, in 2022 over 250,000 border crossers and students were registered who entered Ticino through the 21 most important crossings in the canton.

Of these, 65% come from Como and Varese, with percentages of 46% and 41% respectively. This trend is also confirmed by the 2021 statistics, which reported an increase of 10% compared to the previous year. Mendrisiotto and Lugano

Operational details

Ticino, border crossing from Como points to two areas

Ticino, as a border with Italy, continues to be an important reference point for the Swiss economy. Recently, there has been a significant increase in the degree of vehicle occupancy, from 1.21 to 1.29 compared to the previous survey. This suggests that the demand for transport is growing, especially at the level of commuting.

According to a recent study, 67% of Ticino commuters have free parking at their destination. This is particularly evident in the cities of Lugano and Bellinzona, which represent the main economic centres of the canton. In addition, 50% of commuters say they have no alternative to using a car to reach their workplace.

This trend is particularly interesting considering that Ticino is one of the Swiss cantons with the highest population density. This means that the demand for transport is already high, and the increase in the degree of vehicle employment can be considered a sign of economic growth.

The Law on Parking at Destination

In 2015, the federal law on destination parking was amended to incentivize the use of destination parking. Under the law, municipalities with more than 5,000 inhabitants are required to provide free on-demand parking for commuters. This has led to a significant increase in the supply of parking spaces at destination

Ticino is one of the Swiss cantons closest to the Italian border, and the inhabitants of Como who work or study in Switzerland are numerous. According to official data from 2022, around 30,000 border crossers from Como travel daily to Switzerland for work or study, most of whom use the train to achieve their goals. 📊

The Varese-Stabio axis is one of the busiest routes for border crossers from Como, with over 10,000 travellers using the train every day. The regularity of use is such that 90% of travellers use the train several times a week, with an average delay of 5 minutes than expected. > "The train is the best choice for us, it is fast, comfortable and reliable" says a border worker from Como.
