Valais family allowances: amounts and application procedure (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 6 September 2026

In the Canton of Valais, family allowances amount to CHF 300 per month per child, rising to CHF 440 for the training allowance after the age of 16.

Context

In brief

  • The child allowance in Valais amounts to CHF 300 per month.
  • The training allowance in Valais amounts to CHF 440 per month.
  • The right is regulated at the cantonal level for residents and workers.
  • The procedure requires the involvement of a compensation fund.

Key facts

  • What: Family and training allowances in the Canton of Valais
  • When: In force according to the current cantonal legislation
  • Where: Canton of Valais
  • Who: Competent cantonal compensation fund
  • Child amount: CHF 300 per month
  • Training amount: CHF 440 per month

In the Canton of Valais, the family support system is structured through the payment of family and training allowances, regulated by specific cantonal regulations that define the amounts due for each dependent child. These benefits represent a fundamental component of local welfare, aimed at supporting families in meeting the costs related to the maintenance and education of children. The Valais normative framework establishes that for each child under the age of 16, the entitled parent receives a monthly allowance of CHF 300. Once this age threshold is exceeded, if the child continues a professional or academic training path, the economic support is transformed into a training allowance. In this specific case, the monthly amount paid is CHF 440, provided that the requirements for recognizing the right are met, which covers the study period until the age of twenty-five. The disbursement of these sums is not automatic but requires coordinated management between the employer, the applicant, and the relevant pension institution. Competence in this matter is entrusted to the compensation funds, which operate in the Valais territory to ensure that the benefits are correctly paid based on the applicant's work and family situation. It is essential that beneficiaries understand the transition from the child allowance phase to the training allowance phase, as the age thresholds and amounts vary significantly. The structure of the allowances is designed to provide continuous support that accompanies the minor throughout the growth and education process, respecting the provisions in force in the Canton. The structure of the Valais system

The Valais normative framework is distinguished by the clarity of the defined amounts, which offer constant support to resident workers. Exceeding the age of 16 marks the turning point between the two types of allowances, requiring a timely documentary verification to avoid interruptions in disbursements. The compensation funds, as guarantors of the system, require that any change in the child's training status be communicated promptly, allowing the update of the file and the correct calculation of the monthly entitlements according to the legal parameters established by the Canton.

Unlike other benefits that may depend on subjective variables, Valais family allowances are characterized by fixed amounts that allow families to plan their household finances with greater security. The distinction between the child allowance, set at CHF 300, and the training allowance, set at CHF 440, reflects the increased costs associated with maintaining a child who undertakes post-compulsory studies or professional training. Key points

The procedure to request family allowances in the Canton of Valais begins with the completion of an official form provided by the competent compensation fund. The worker must submit the application through their employer, who will forward the necessary documentation to the relevant authority. It is essential to attach all documentation proving the family relationship or custody, as well as, in the case of children over 16 years old, the training certificate attesting enrollment in a school or recognized professional path. The compensation fund then verifies the requirements and, once the application is approved, proceeds with the payment. In case of interruption of studies, the beneficiary has the obligation to report the change immediately to avoid undue payments, which should be subsequently returned. The regularity of payments depends on the accuracy with which the information requested at the beginning of the employment relationship or at the birth of a child is provided. It is advisable to always keep a copy of the correspondence with the compensation fund, including the forms sent and the receipt notifications. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the amounts of checks in the Canton of Valais?
In the Canton of Valais, the system provides two types of benefits: the child allowance, equal to CHF 300 per month, paid until the age of 16, and the training allowance, which amounts to CHF 440 per month. Thelatter is granted for children who continue a course of professional or academic training after the age of 16 and until they reach the age of twenty-five, provided that the requirements are met.
How do I apply for the Valais family allowance?
The procedure must be initiated through your employer. The worker must complete the official form provided by the competent compensation fund and attach the necessary documentation, such as the kinship certificate or custody. For children over the age of 16, it is essential to attach a training certificate attesting enrollment in a school or a recognized professional path to activate the provision of the training allowance.
What happens if your child stops studying?
If the child stops the training course, the beneficiary is obliged to immediately report the change to the relevant compensation fund. Failure to communicate may cause undue payments, which must be subsequently returned. It is essential to maintain constant communication with the social security institution and keep a copy of all correspondence and forms sent to ensure the correctness of your social security position.
