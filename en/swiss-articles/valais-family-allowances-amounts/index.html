Valais family allowances: amounts and application procedure (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 6 September 2026

In the Canton of Valais, family allowances amount to CHF 300 per month per child, rising to CHF 440 for the training allowance after the age of 16.

Context

In brief

  • The child allowance in Valais amounts to CHF 300 per month.
  • The training allowance in Valais amounts to CHF 440 per month.
  • The right is regulated at the cantonal level for residents and workers.
  • The procedure requires the involvement of a compensation fund.

Key facts

  • What: Family and training allowances in the Canton of Valais
  • When: In force according to the current cantonal legislation
  • Where: Canton of Valais
  • Who: Competent cantonal compensation fund
  • Child amount: CHF 300 per month
  • Training amount: CHF 440 per month

Operational details

The analysis of the family allowance system in the Canton of Valais reveals a mechanism integrated with Swiss social security. Unlike other benefits that may depend on subjective variables, Valais family allowances are characterized by fixed amounts that allow families to plan their household finances with greater security. The distinction between the child allowance, set at CHF 300, and the training allowance, set at CHF 440, reflects the increased costs associated with maintaining a child who undertakes post-compulsory studies or professional training. This setup is consistent with the need to mitigate the impact of the cost of living in Switzerland, ensuring that families do not face excessive economic pressures during the years of education. The Valais system requires that the applicant be active in the local job market, as the funding of the allowances occurs primarily through contributions paid by employers. This link between employment and welfare is a pillar of the Swiss model, where social benefits are often tied to the contributory position of the worker. It is necessary to consider that, in case of a change of employment or canton of residence, the applicable legislation may vary, making it crucial to verify the competent compensation fund. The management of such procedures requires attention, especially when comparing the situation with other cantonal realities that may have different thresholds or amounts. For those evaluating their family budget, it is useful to remember how such income adds to the net income, influencing the ability to save and the possibility to invest in instruments such as the third pillar 3a, which allows for optimizing long-term pension planning in parallel with the support received for children.

Key points

The procedure to request family allowances in the Canton of Valais begins with the completion of an official form provided by the competent compensation fund. The worker must submit the application through their employer, who will forward the necessary documentation to the relevant authority. It is essential to attach all documentation proving the family relationship or custody, as well as, in the case of children over 16 years old, the training certificate attesting enrollment in a school or recognized professional path. The compensation fund then verifies the requirements and, once the application is approved, proceeds with the payment. In case of interruption of studies, the beneficiary has the obligation to report the change immediately to avoid undue payments, which should be subsequently returned. The regularity of payments depends on the accuracy with which the information requested at the beginning of the employment relationship or at the birth of a child is provided. It is advisable to always keep a copy of the correspondence with the compensation fund, including the forms sent and the receipt notifications. Transparency in the application procedure ensures smooth access to benefits, allowing parents to focus on their children's educational path without the worries of bureaucratic delays or misunderstandings about requirements. Remember that any change in civil status or the study situation of the child must be reported promptly to maintain the right to the allowance in the correct amount. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the amounts of checks in the Canton of Valais?
In the Canton of Valais, the system provides two types of benefits: the child allowance, equal to CHF 300 per month, paid until the age of 16, and the training allowance, which amounts to CHF 440 per month. Thelatter is granted for children who continue a course of professional or academic training after the age of 16 and until they reach the age of twenty-five, provided that the requirements are met.
How do I apply for the Valais family allowance?
The procedure must be initiated through your employer. The worker must complete the official form provided by the competent compensation fund and attach the necessary documentation, such as the kinship certificate or custody. For children over the age of 16, it is essential to attach a training certificate attesting enrollment in a school or a recognized professional path to activate the provision of the training allowance.
What happens if your child stops studying?
If the child stops the training course, the beneficiary is obliged to immediately report the change to the relevant compensation fund. Failure to communicate may cause undue payments, which must be subsequently returned. It is essential to maintain constant communication with the social security institution and keep a copy of all correspondence and forms sent to ensure the correctness of your social security position.
