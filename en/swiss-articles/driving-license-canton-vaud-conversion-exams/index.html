Driver's license in the Canton of Vaud: conversion, exams and compulsory courses (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 September 2026

Practical guide to the conversion of the foreign driver's license in the Canton of Vaud, including theoretical and practical exams, compulsory courses and the competent traffic office.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Conversion of foreign licence in the Canton of Vaud
  • Compulsory theoretical and practical exams

Key facts

  • What: Foreign driver's license conversion
  • When: Within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland
  • Where: Circulation Office of the Canton of Vaud
  • Who: All residents with a foreign license
  • Amount: CHF 100 for the theory exam, CHF 150 for the practical exam, CHF 200 for the compulsory courses

The conversion of the foreign driver's license in the Canton of Vaud is a mandatory process for all residents who hold a foreign driver's license. This process includes theoretical and practical exams, as well as mandatory road safety courses. The competent traffic office is the body responsible for managing these procedures.

Conversion Procedure

The conversion procedure begins with the presentation of the foreign license at the traffic office of the Canton of Vaud. It is necessary to pass a theoretical and a practical exam, as well as to attend compulsory courses for road safety. These courses are designed to ensure that drivers are aware of Swiss road regulations and best practices for safety.

Operational details

Practical Implications

The conversion of a foreign driver's license in the Canton of Vaud has significant practical implications for residents. It is important to note that the procedure must be completed within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland. This means that new residents must plan their time adequately to pass the exams and attend the mandatory courses.

Time Planning

Time planning is crucial to complete the conversion procedure. New residents must book theoretical and practical exams in advance and ensure they have the necessary time to attend the mandatory courses. This requires good organization and the ability to manage time effectively.

For example, a new resident arriving in Lausanne must book the theoretical exam at the Vaud Canton's traffic office. The theoretical exam costs CHF 60 and must be passed within 12 months of the arrival date. Subsequently, it is necessary to attend a mandatory 6-hour training course, which costs CHF 250. After the course, you can book the practical exam, which costs CHF 120.

Costs and Resources

The costs associated with license conversion can vary. It is important to inform yourself about the costs of exams and mandatory courses at the Vaud Canton's traffic office. Additionally, new residents must consider transportation costs and any additional expenses related to the conversion procedure.

For example, a resident living in Montreux who needs to attend a course in Lausanne will have to consider transportation costs. A round-trip train ticket costs about CHF 20, and if you decide to use a car, fuel and parking costs can add another CHF 30.

Key points

Action: Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Submission of Foreign License: Submit the foreign license at the Vaud canton's traffic office. It is necessary to book an appointment through the office's website or by calling the dedicated number. During the appointment, you will be required to fill out an application form and pay an administrative fee of CHF 45. 2. Booking of Exams: Book the theoretical and practical exams in advance. The theoretical exams cost CHF 60 and can be booked online or through the traffic office. The practical exams, which cost CHF 120, must be booked with an authorized driving school. 3. Attendance of Mandatory Courses: Attend the mandatory road safety courses. These courses, which last approximately 12 hours, are offered by authorized driving schools and generally cost between CHF 200 and CHF 300. The courses cover topics such as safe driving, emergency management, and Swiss road regulations. 4. Passing the Exams: Pass the theoretical and practical exams. The theoretical exams consist of a multiple-choice test of 50 questions, of which at least 42 must be answered correctly. The practical exams last approximately 45 minutes and assess driving skills in various conditions. 5. Collection of Swiss License: Collect the Swiss license once the procedure is completed. After passing the exams and completing the mandatory courses, you can collect the license at the Vaud canton's traffic office. The Swiss license is valid for 10 years and costs CHF 60.

Tools and Resources

For further information and support, new residents can consult the website of the Vaud canton's traffic office. Additionally, you can use the salary calculator to plan the costs associated with the conversion procedure.
