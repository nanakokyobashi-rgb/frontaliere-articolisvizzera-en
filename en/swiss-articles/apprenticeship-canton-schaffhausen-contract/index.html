Apprenticeship and vocational training Schaffhausen (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 August 2026

Canton Schaffhausen offers federally contracted apprenticeships. Learn how to find an internship, the rights in a contract, and pathways to professional maturity.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Federal apprenticeship protected by contract: minimum 12 months before the start (CO art. 344-346)
  • Salaries vary by canton and sector; negotiated between apprentice and employer
  • Two final certifications: AFC (Professional Training Certificate) and MPC (Professional Maturity)
  • Cantonal Vocational Training Office manages posts and registrations

Key facts

  • What: Internship with employment contract + theoretical training in cantonal vocational school
  • Where: Companies in the Canton of Schaffhausen + vocational school
  • When: Average duration 3-4 years, usually beginning of September
  • Who: Apprentice, employer, school, cantonal office
  • Requirements: Completion of compulsory schooling, minimum 15 years
  • Certifications: AFC or MPC, often both
  • Hours: Maximum 45 hours/week (industry/offices), minimum 4 weeks annual holiday

The Swiss apprenticeship system in the Canton of Schaffhausen

Apprenticeships in Switzerland are regulated by the Federal Law on Vocational Training (LFPr 2002) and protected by the Code of Obligations (CO art. 344-346). In the canton of Schaffhausen, as in the other cantons, the system follows uniform federal rules, with cantonal management of the training offer and the available places.

Operational details

How to find an internship in Schaffhausen

It is essential to start the research at least 18-24 months before the end of compulsory schooling. In the canton of Schaffhausen, the cantonal vocational training office maintains a public database of authorised companies and regularly publishes lists of available posts, which can also be consulted through national platforms such as Annunci di lavoro.

The main steps of the research:

1. Check the available sectors: metalworking, construction, sales, crafts and logistics are traditionally strong in the canton 2. Contact the cantonal office: free advice and referral to companies in line with interests and skills 3. Consult the company database: identify active employers and their training profiles 4. Apply directly: Submit CV, motivation letter, and school results (there is no federal minimum grade requirement, but many companies evaluate math, languages, and behavior) 5. Participate in cantonal orientation days: the canton organizes annual meetings between candidates and companies

Required skills and application timeline

Companies evaluate not only school grades, but also practical aptitude, concentration, precision, motivation expressed in the letter, personal hygiene and willingness to attend vocational school 1-2 days/week.

Key points

Compensation, apprentice rights and professional maturity

Salary: formation and variability

In Switzerland, there is no federal minimum wage for apprentices; the compensation is negotiated between the employer and the apprentice, often consulting trade union tariffs or local sectoral standards. In the canton of Schaffhausen, the level varies significantly by sector, company size and applicable collective agreements.

First year: contained compensation, linked to the initial learning phase. Second and third year: gradual increases according to acquired skills. Fourth year (if provided): approach to the salary of a qualified worker.

The compensation is paid monthly by the employer; the apprentice pays social security contributions (AVS/AI 5.3%, unemployment insurance 1.1%) on the gross salary, like every worker. The canton monitors that levels do not fall below reasonable thresholds, but does not impose uniform minimums.

Apprentice rights and duties

In addition to maximum working hours and minimum holidays, the apprentice has the right to:

  • Protection of health and safety at work (equipment, training on risks)
  • Theoretical education in a vocational school without loss of salary for attendance days
  • Didactic material provided by the school (books, handouts)
  • Periodic evaluations by the school and the employer to monitor the progress

The duties include: regular attendance at school, work loyalty, respect for company procedures, company confidentiality.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the minimum wage for an apprentice in Schaffhausen Canton?
There is no federal minimum wage for apprentices in Switzerland. Remuneration is negotiated between employer and apprentice, often following collective agreements or industry standards. In the Canton of Schaffhausen, it varies by sector and year of internship. The canton monitors that the levels are reasonable, but does not impose uniform minimums. Consult the Cantonal Vocational Training Office or trade associations for indicative rates in your industry.
How long does an apprenticeship last and when does it start?
The average duration is 3-4 years, according to the professional profile. The start is usually in September. The contract must be signed at least 12 months before the effective start date. After the first month (trial period), the contract is binding. The exact duration is specified in the contract itself and depends on the chosen professional sector.
Can I attend the professional maturity during the internship?
Yes. In the Canton of Schaffhausen, integrated professional maturity (1-2 additional afternoons/week during the internship) or subsequent professional maturity (1-2 years after the AFC) is available. The MP is federally recognized and allows direct access to University Professional Schools (SUPs) without additional exams. Speak to the Cantonal Office and your employer to assess which modality is most suitable.
What are the main rights and duties in an internship contract?
The apprentice is entitled to: maximum 45 hours/week (industry/offices) or 50 hours in other sectors, at least 4 weeks of annual holidays (5 if under 20), theoretical training in vocational school without loss of salary, health and safety protection. Duties include: regular school attendance, job loyalty, adherence to company procedures. Cancellation before the end of the trial period (3 months) is free; afterwards, it requires valid reason.
How do I actually find an internship in Canton Schaffhausen?
Consult [Annunci di lavoro in Svizzera](nav:jobs) and the public database of the Cantonal Office of Vocational Training. Contact the canton to receive the list of authorized companies. Prepare a CV and clear motivation letter. Apply directly to the companies that interest you. Participate in cantonal orientation days. If initial research doesn't yield results, the canton offers bridging courses and support programs to hone your skills.
