Traffic in Switzerland: what it means for the Swiss

Traffic in Switzerland is a serious problem for the Swiss. According to a report by the Federal Statistical Office, traffic generated costs of about 117 billion francs in 2023. 77% of these costs derive from passenger traffic, while 23% from freight. 68% of passenger-km journeys were made by car.

This means that every year the Swiss spend about 85 billion francs on passenger traffic and about 27 billion francs on freight traffic. Passenger traffic costs are so high because every year Swiss people travel around 140 billion kilometres by car, which represents an increase of 10% compared to 2022.

Traffic in Switzerland is a problem that affects all cantons and cities in the country. For example, in the Canton of Ticino, traffic is a particularly serious problem, especially during peak hours. According to data from the Cantonal Administration of Ticino, there are about 50,000 vehicles passing through the city of Lugano every day, which represents an increase of 15% compared to 2022.

In addition, traffic in Switzerland is also a problem for the environment. According to data from the Federal Office for the Environment, traffic in Switzerland emits about 12 million tons of CO2 every year, which represents 20% of the country's total emissions.
