# Scholarship requirements in the Obvaldo Canton

To be eligible to receive a scholarship in the Obvaldo Canton, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a border worker resident in the Obvaldo Canton or in another neighbouring canton
  • Have a household income of less than a certain amount (CHF 50,000 for the family of four)
  • Be enrolled in a course of study or have a degree certificate
  • Not having previously received a scholarship in the Obvaldo Canton
