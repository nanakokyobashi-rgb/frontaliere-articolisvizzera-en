The cantonal compensation fund of Solothurn plays a crucial role in managing the first pillar AVS. This system is essential for ensuring the social security of the canton's residents. The compensation fund is responsible for calculating the AVS pension and supplementary benefits, which are essential for covering the minimum living standard.

Contribution Gaps

Contribution gaps represent a significant problem for many insured individuals. These gaps can arise from periods of unemployment, maternity, or other interruptions in AVS contributions. The cantonal compensation fund of Solothurn offers support to fill these gaps, ensuring that insured individuals can benefit from an adequate pension once they reach retirement age.

Supplementary Benefits

Supplementary benefits are designed to supplement the AVS pension and ensure that insured individuals can maintain a dignified standard of living. These benefits are particularly important for those with low contributions or contribution gaps. The cantonal compensation fund of Solothurn works to ensure that these benefits are accessible and adequate to the needs of the insured.

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The management of the first pillar AVS by the cantonal compensation fund of Solothurn has concrete implications for the residents of the canton. The AVS pension is calculated based on the contributions paid during the working life. This means that those who have contributed regularly can expect a higher pension compared to those with contribution gaps.

Implications for Cross-Border Workers

Cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and reside in Italy must pay particular attention to their AVS contributions. It is important to ensure that contributions are paid correctly and that there are no gaps. The cantonal compensation fund of Solothurn can provide assistance to verify contributions and fill any gaps.

Hypothetical Scenarios

Consider the case of a cross-border worker who has worked in Switzerland for 20 years but has a contribution gap of 5 years. The cantonal compensation fund of Solothurn can help fill this gap, ensuring that the cross-border worker can benefit from an adequate AVS pension. This is an example of how the compensation fund can make a difference in the lives of insured individuals.

Concrete Examples

A worker who has contributed for 40 years with an average salary of CHF 6,000 per month can expect an AVS pension of approximately CHF 2,300 per month. However, if the same worker has a contribution gap of 10 years, their pension could be reduced to CHF 1,800 per month. This example shows the importance of regular contributions.

Regulations and Amounts

According to the Federal Law on Old-Age and Survivors' Insurance (LAPV), AVS contributions are calculated on the annual salary subject to contribution, with a maximum limit of CHF 86,040 for 2023. The contributions are divided between the employer and the employee, with a rate of 5.312% each.

Key points

Action

For residents of the Canton of Solothurn, it is essential to be informed about their AVS contributions and supplementary benefits. The cantonal compensation fund offers support and assistance to ensure that policyholders can benefit from an adequate pension.

Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Contact the cantonal clearing house in Solothurn to verify your AVS contributions. You can do this through their website or by going directly to the headquarters in Soletta. 2. Request assistance to fill any contribution gaps. For example, if you worked abroad for a period, you may not have contributed to the Swiss AVS during that time. The clearinghouse can help you supplement these periods. 3. Learn about supplementary services and how to claim them. The supplementary benefits are intended for those with an insufficient AVS income. In 2023, the maximum amount of supplementary benefit for a single person is CHF 1,200 per month.

Useful Tools

The site of the cantonal clearing house of Solothurn offers a number of useful tools for residents. You can access detailed information about AVS contributions, supplementary services and how to request them. In addition, the site offers an AVS annuity calculator that can help you estimate your future annuity.
