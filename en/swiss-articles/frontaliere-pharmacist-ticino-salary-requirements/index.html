Enablement Iter

The G permit is issued after passing a rigorous selection and completing a mandatory training course organized by the Confederation. During the course, candidates learn the basics of the pharmaceutical profession, Swiss law on the storage of medicines and safety regulations.

Pharmacists authorized to work in Ticino with a G permit may practice their profession in any public or private pharmacy in the Canton of Ticino. Recognition is guaranteed by a special health license plate and an authorization receipt issued by the Ticino Federation of Pharmacists.
