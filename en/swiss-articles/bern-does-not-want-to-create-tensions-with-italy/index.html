Implications for cross-border workers

The statement by the Swiss Federal Council could have implications for cross-border workers who work in Switzerland and live in Italy. In particular, cross-border workers may see an improvement in relations between the two countries, which could have a positive impact on their daily lives.

Scenario: A future with more friction between Switzerland and Italy Conversely, if relations between Switzerland and Italy deteriorate, cross-border commuters could find it more difficult to work across borders.
