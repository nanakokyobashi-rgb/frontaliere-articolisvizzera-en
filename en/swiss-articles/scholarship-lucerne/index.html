Requirements and maximum amounts

The Canton of Lucerne offers scholarships to deserving students who intend to continue their studies at Swiss universities and vocational schools. The requirements and maximum amounts for scholarships are set by the cantonal law on student support, which came into force on 1 January 2020 (Cantonal Law on Student Support, LSA 2020). Under this regulation, students who intend to benefit from a scholarship must meet the following requirements:

  • Have a maximum age of 30 years
  • Be resident in the Canton of Lucerne
  • Regularly attends a vocational school or university
  • Not be in full-time employment
  • Not have already benefited from a scholarship for a total period of more than 12 months

The maximum amounts of scholarships are established according to the level of study and the duration of the support. For example, for students attending a vocational school, the maximum amount is CHF 1,800 per month, while for students attending a university, the maximum amount is CHF2,500 per month.

# Scholarship Access Requirements

To access the scholarships offered by the Canton of Lucerne, students must meet the following requirements:

  • Have Swiss citizenship or be resident in the Canton of Lucerne.
  • Be enrolled in a course of study at a Swiss university.
  • Having a good financial situation.
  • Have not already benefited from a similar scholarship in the past.

Maximum amounts established by cantonal law

The maximum amounts for scholarships are set by cantonal law and may vary depending on the type of scholarship and level of studies. For example, in 2022, the Canton of Lucerne set maximum amounts for scholarships as follows:

Key points

Lucerne Cantonal Scholarships: Requirements and Amounts

The scholarship is offered by the Canton of Lucerne for deserving students who intend to continue their studies at a higher education institution. The requirements and maximum amounts are set by cantonal law, in particular by the Student Financial Assistance Regulations of 2020, which provides for a maximum amount of CHF 10,000 for the academic year.

To obtain the scholarship, students must submit the application within the deadline set by the competent office, which is the Department of Education and Culture of the Canton of Lucerne. The application must be completed correctly and accompanied by the required documents, such as financial documentation and a letter of justification.

Students wishing to continue their studies at a higher education institution may request further information from the relevant office. You can also consult the web portal of the Canton of Lucerne, where the requirements and procedures for applying for the scholarship are published.
