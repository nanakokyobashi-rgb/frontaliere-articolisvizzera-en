Key points

The final operational phase for starting a business in Basel-Landschaft requires strict compliance with a well-defined temporal and procedural sequence, ranging from the preparation of incorporation documents to the actual opening of doors to the public or clients. ### Operational phases for completing the startup. The procedure first requires the signing of the incorporation deeds, often in the presence of a notary in cases required for corporations, and then proceeding with the payment of the share capital into a blocked escrow account in the name of the future company. Once the bank certification of the deposit is obtained, the formal application for registration in the cantonal commercial register is submitted, accompanied by all notarized signatures and declarations required by current legislation. Upon receiving confirmation of registration and the assignment of the company identification number, the entrepreneur must proceed with registration at the cantonal and municipal tax administration to determine tax obligations, including potential VAT management if the turnover exceeds the thresholds established by federal law. At the same time, it is necessary to finalize social security positions with the competent OASI compensation fund and take out mandatory insurance coverages for employees, such as occupational pension plans (BVG/LPP) and accident insurance (UVG/LAINF).
