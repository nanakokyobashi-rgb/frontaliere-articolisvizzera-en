Ascend Sport Technology, a Lucerne company based in Kriens (LU), is preparing to land on the Swiss stock exchange. The path passes through a merger with Evonext, an already listed holding company. The parties have signed a binding union agreement; the merger is expected to close by the end of the year, but shareholders of both companies have yet to give their consent.

Ascend operates in the sector of new technologies applied to professional sports. The company focuses its activity on digital advertising, artificial intelligence and the commercial valorisation of sporting events. His interlocutor is the holders of sporting rights: clubs, leagues and competition organisers. The indicated objective is to increase commercial revenues.

The service is explained with a case related to football. During a match, Ascend can virtually replace pitchside advertising in broadcasts, allowing you to display different advertising messages in different countries. The described mechanism allows messages to be differentiated between one country and another.

The role of Evonext

Evonext is based in Reinach (BL) and is what remains of the previous biotech group Evolva. The holding is already listed and, through the merger, Ascend can access the stock exchange without a classic initial placement. The listing is therefore not described as a separate operation, but as the result of the union between the two companies.

The operation gives Ascend a value of 500 million francs and sets the price of Evonext shares at 2.50 francs; a share issue is also planned to raise new capital for the further growth of the company. In this way, access to the grant is linked to finding resources for the future development of Ascend.

The calendar has already gone through two steps. In August Evonext confirmed talks with Ascend. It also warned at the end of September that if the merger failed and no alternatives emerged, it could be liquidated. Now the binding agreement brings the decision to the shareholders of both companies.
