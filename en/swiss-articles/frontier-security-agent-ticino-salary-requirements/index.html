The New Cross-Border Commuter Agreement provides that cross-border security guards in Ticino may work as such starting January 1, 2024. This agreement was signed on December 23, 2020 and ratified by Italy with Law 83 of June 13, 2023.

The new agreement opens up new prospects for cross-border commuters who wish to work as security guards in Ticino. Cross-border security guards in Ticino will be able to work in the border areas with Italy, such as Chiasso, Bellinzona and Lugano. 📊

Requirements for working as a security guard in Ticino as a cross-border commuter

To work as a security guard in Ticino as a cross-border commuter, it is necessary to meet the following requirements:

  • Be a citizen of one of the member countries of the Schengen Area or Schengen Agreement.
  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Have a certificate of suitability issued by a competent body.
  • Have a good knowledge of the Italian language and a Swiss language (German, French or Italian).
  • Have a good knowledge of security rules and procedures.
  • Have good conduct and no criminal record.
  • Be in good physical and mental health.

The procedure for obtaining the certificate of suitability to work as a security guard in Ticino as a cross-border commuter is as follows:

Undergo a medical check to demonstrate good physical and mental health. 4. Undergo a conduct check to demonstrate good conduct and no criminal record. 5. Receive the certificate of suitability.
