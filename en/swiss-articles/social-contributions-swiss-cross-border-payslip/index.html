Guide to Swiss social contributions for frontier workers: payroll detail (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 23 June 2026

Find out how social contributions are divided into paychecks for border workers working in Switzerland.

Context

In short - Social contributions in Switzerland are divided between worker and employer - AVS, AI, IPG, AD, LPP and LAINF are the main contributions - Each contribution has a specific percentage ## Key facts - What: Social contributions in paychecks for frontier workers - Where: Switzerland - Who: Frontier workers and employers - AVS: 10.60% - AI: 1.40% - IPG: 0.50% Social contributions in Switzerland represent a significant part of the paycheck for frontier workers. These contributions are split between the worker and the employer and cover various aspects of social security. ### Breakdown of contributions Social contributions in Switzerland are divided into different categories, each with a specific percentage. Here is an overview of the main contributions: - AVS (Old Age and Survivors Insurance): 10.60% - AI (Disability Insurance): 1.40% - IPG (Income Loss Allowance): 0.50% - AD (Unemployment Insurance): 2.20% - LPP (Occupational Insurance): Varies depending on the employer - LAINF (Accident Insurance): 0.20% These contributions are mandatory and are deducted directly from the worker's paycheck.

Operational details

Practical analysis of social contributions Understanding social contributions is crucial for frontier workers operating in Switzerland. Each contribution has a significant impact on net payroll and offers specific coverages in case of need. ### Impact of contributions on net payroll Social contributions have a significant impact on net payroll. For example, a worker with a gross salary of CHF 5000 will see a significant deduction due to social contributions. Here is a rough estimate: - AVS: 530 CHF - AI: 70 CHF - IPG: 25 CHF - AD: 110 CHF - LAINF: 10 CHF These amounts are approximate and may vary depending on the employer and canton where you work. ### Coverages offered by social contributions Each social contribution offers specific coverage: - AVS: Covers old age and survivors - AI: Covers disability - IPG: Covers loss of earnings - AD: Covers unemployment - LPP: Covers occupational insurance - LAINF: Covers accidents These coverages are essential to ensure financial security in the event of unforeseen events.
