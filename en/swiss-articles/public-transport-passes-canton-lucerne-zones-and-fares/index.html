Zones and Rates

The Canton of Lucerne is divided into several zones, each with its own tariff. For example, zone A includes the city of Lucerne and neighboring municipalities, while zone B includes the more distant municipalities. The fee for zone A is 120 CHF per month, while the fee for zone B is 180 CHF per month.

Regulations and rules

The legislation governing public transport subscriptions in the Canton of Lucerne is the Law on Public Transport of 15 May 2018. The law states that subscriptions must be purchased by the 15th of the previous month and that the fee must be paid by the 15th of the following month. The law also stipulates that residents and students of the Canton of Lucerne must be treated equally.
