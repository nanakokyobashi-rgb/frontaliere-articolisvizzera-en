L Fribourg permit: duration and renewal (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 September 2026

L Freiburg permit: valid for up to 1 year, renewal and transition to B and C permits. Procedures, documents and employer constraints.

Context

In brief

  • The L permit is valid for up to 1 year and is intended for temporary work in Switzerland
  • It is renewable upon joint request with the employer, under the same conditions
  • From the L permit you pass to B (residence, 5 years) and then to C (residence, 10 years)

Key Facts

  • What: Residence permit and temporary work for foreigners in Switzerland
  • Duration: Up to 1 year (not automatically renewable; renewal on request)
  • Where: Canton of Fribourg (federal SEM regulations for all cantons)
  • Who: Secrétariat d'État aux migrations (SEM) — federal competence; cantonal administration by cantonal procedure
  • Local authority: Office of Employment and Migration of the Canton of Fribourg

What is the L permit and how does it work

The short-term L permit is the instrument through which Switzerland regulates the temporary entry of foreign workers. It is valid for a maximum of 12 months and allows you to live and work in the country during this period. Unlike other permits (B for residence, C for domicile), the L permit does not offer long-term stability, but is an ideal solution for temporary, seasonal or project-specific assignments.

In the canton of Fribourg, as in all other Swiss cantons, the L permit is subject to the federal regulation of the Secrétariat d'État aux migrations. There are no specific cantonal variants that differentiate the conditions in Fribourg from other cantons: the rules on duration, renewal and contractual obligations remain uniform at the national level. The cantonal administrative procedure, however, is handled by the office of the canton of Fribourg responsible for employment and immigration.

Who can get it and employer constraints

The L permit is subject to the submission of a genuine job offer. The Swiss employer (or the Swiss branch of a foreign company) must prove that it cannot find a resource available on the domestic market and that the position is actually necessary. This check is carried out by the competent authorities when submitting the application.

The employer remains the real "anchor" of the permit: the L permit is nominative, i.e. in the name of a specific company, and the worker cannot change employer without the re-registration procedure and new approval by the SEM and the canton. This is a fundamental constraint that distinguishes the L permit from the simple authorization to reside. It means that the permit is linked to the permanence in the same workplace and that any change requires timely notification to the authorities.

Operational details

Renewal of the L permit and conditions for extension

An L permit can be renewed, but renewal is not automatic. The procedure requires a new joint application by the employer and the foreigner to the Fribourg cantonal administration, preferably submitted 60-90 days before expiry. Renewal is possible if the initial conditions are still met: the employer confirms the employment need, the employment is real and continues, and there are no legal impediments such as no criminal record, tax regularity, and timely payment of social contributions (AHV/IV, unemployment insurance, health insurance).

The duration of each renewal period is still up to 1 year. There is no theoretical limit to the number of consecutive renewals, as long as the conditions remain met. However, the Swiss permit system is designed to incentivize temporariness: if the employment becomes structural and permanent, it is more convenient for everyone (employer, employee, cantonal authority) to transition to a B (residence) permit, which offers a validity of 5 years and less administrative bureaucracy.

Transition from L to B: when and how

After the first year of the L permit (or after one or more consecutive renewals), the transition to the B permit is the natural step. The B permit is valid for 5 years and can be renewed indefinitely, representing significantly greater stability. To access the B permit, there is no need to change cantons: the canton of Fribourg remains the competent party. The administrative procedure is handled by the Fribourg office, which automatically assesses the possibility of transition once the first L permit has expired and if the requirements are met.

Minimum requirements for the transition to B include: documented actual employment for at least 1 year (demonstrable with payslips, renewed contracts); absence of pending sanctions or expulsion measures; regular and full payment of social contributions; ability to economic self-sufficiency, demonstrated by the net income that allows you to live without cantonal public support.

Difference Between B and C Permit

The C permit (domicile) represents the final step in the progression. It is generally available after 10 years of continuous legal residence (or 5 years for citizens of the European Union or the European Economic Area), and offers almost absolute stability. Unlike the B permit, the C permit allows for wider job mobility: it is possible to change employer and sector with fewer administrative constraints and without the need for a new authorization for each change.

In addition, the C permit is a prerequisite for accessing ordinary Swiss naturalization, which then requires the C permit plus 10 years of actual residence, together with the requirements established by the canton and municipality of residence.

Key points

Step-by-step: how to renew your L permit in Freiburg

The renewal procedure is managed by the Fribourg cantonal administration. Here are the operational steps:

Step 1 — Coordination with the employer (60-90 days before expiration): The employer and the employee must agree on the willingness to renew. It is essential that the employer confirms in writing the stable employment and the need to continue employment beyond the expiration date of the current permit.

Step 2 — Collection of documentation: the employer prepares a reasoned declaration (explaining the reasons for continuing), the renewed or extended employment contract, the worker's last 3 pay slips, a copy of the company's tax return for the last 2 financial years, and (if the employee has resided in Switzerland for more than 2 years) a certificate of good conduct.

Step 3 — Submission of the application: joint application (employer + employee, preferably with both signatures certified) to the competent office in the canton of Fribourg (usually the employment or migration desk). Check whether the canton accepts digital applications via the SEM platform or whether it requires paper documentation.

Step 4 — Verification and timing: The canton of Fribourg takes an average of 4-8 weeks to evaluate the application. It is not mandatory for the permit to be renewed BEFORE it expires (the employee can take advantage of a short tolérance during the verification procedure), but it is advisable to submit the application in time to avoid interruptions.

Step 5 — Issuance and Communication: Once approved, the positive decision is notified to both the employer and the employee. The renewed permit is valid for an additional 12 months from the date of approval.

Documents required for renewal

  • Valid passport and photocopy of personal page
  • Renewed or extended employment contract (with signature of both parties)
  • Last 3 pay slips (payslip)
  • Company tax return (last 2 tax years)
  • Certificate of good conduct (if resident for more than 2 years)
  • Statement of account from the provident fund (AHV/IV) to check the regularity of contributions

When it is convenient to switch from permission L to B

If the renewal of the L permit becomes recurrent (more than 2 consecutive renewals, i.e. more than 3 years of stay), it is advisable to consider switching to B. Concrete advantages: less bureaucracy (renewal every 5 years instead of every year); expanded labor mobility (possibility of changing employer with a simplified procedure); recognition of stable and long-term residence, with tax and social security benefits.

Conversely, if the occupation is actually temporary (maximum 2-3 years), the L permit remains the most pragmatic choice: less administrative effort, and no obligation to transition to B.

Support and practical tools

Further information on cantonal specific procedures and application forms can be found on the official website of the administration of the canton of Fribourg (www.fr.ch, Employment and Migration section).

To check the regularity of the payments of social contributions (AHV/IV) and to calculate the actual amounts withheld from the payroll, the calcolatore di stipendio e contributi is available, which also allows you to estimate the applicable tax deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does the short-term L permit remain valid?
The L permit is valid for a maximum of 1 year (12 months) from the issue or renewal. It is not automatically renewable; renewal requires a new joint application to the canton of Freiburg. There is no theoretical limit to the number of consecutive renewals, as long as employment conditions remain stable and social contributions are paid regularly.
Can I change employers with a valid L permit?
No, not directly. The L permit is nominative (in the name of a specific company). A change of employer requires a re-registration procedure with the SEM and the canton of Fribourg, with a new employer submitting a new application. In some cases, an administrative change is possible, but it depends on the circumstances and the assessment of the authorities.
What are the advantages of switching from the L permit to the B permit?
The B permit is valid for 5 years (renewable indefinitely) and offers greater stability. It allows you to change employers with a simplified procedure, without having to request a new authorisation each time. It is the natural step if the occupation becomes permanent and is a prerequisite for later access to the C permit (domicile) and Swiss naturalization.
What happens if my L permit expires while I'm waiting for renewal?
If the renewal request was submitted before the deadline, you remain in a state of tolérance (tolerance) for a short period (1-2 weeks) pending the decision. However, it is strongly recommended to anticipate the request by 60-90 days to avoid interruptions of the authorization and administrative complications.
What social contributions do I have to pay with an L permit in Freiburg?
With L permit (as with B and C), you are obliged to pay AVS/AI contributions (5.3% as an employee, 10.6% total with the employer), AD/AC unemployment insurance (1.1% up to the annual ceiling), and compulsory sickness insurance (LAMal). These are federal payments, uniform in all cantons, including Freiburg.
