The fall in domestic rates in Graubünden

The energy group Repower, based in Poschiavo, announced a slight reduction in electricity tariffs for domestic users in its network area. From 2027, a family of four with an annual consumption of 4500 kilowatt hours will pay 1449 francs for the current, 20 francs less than the current year. The savings amount to a 1.4% decrease in the energy component of the bill.

How Optima fare works

From 1 January 2027 Repower will offer its customers the opportunity to voluntarily join the new Optima variable rate. The system requires that both the cost of energy and the cost of using the network vary according to the time of day. Each hour will be associated with one of the three price levels, identified by the colors green, yellow and red.

Step-by-step: upgrading to the Optima rate

To join Repower's new Optima variable tariff from 1 January 2027, interested customers will first need to verify that they are equipped with a smart meter. Otherwise, they can request the installation from their network manager. The replacement of the traditional meter with the smart one is generally free for the end customer and is carried out by the local distributor as part of the network modernization investments.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much will a typical family in Graubünden save by 2027?
A family of four with an annual consumption of 4500 kilowatt hours will pay 1449 francs in 2027, 20 francs less than the current year. The decrease corresponds to a 1.4% drop in the energy component of the bill. However, the actual savings will depend on real consumption and municipal taxes charged on behalf of the Grison municipalities.
How does Repower's new Optima rate work?
The Optima tariff includes three price bands identified by the colors green, yellow and red, based on the availability of renewable energy and the load of the grid. In the green hours, typically corresponding to times of high renewable production and low demand, prices are more affordable. The bands are defined in advance and remain unchanged per quarter, facilitating the scheduling of consumption. A smart meter is required to access.
How do you adhere to the Optima rate from 2027?
From January 1, 2027, Repower customers can voluntarily join the Optima rate. Those who do not yet have a smart meter can request its installation from their local distributor free of charge. Once equipped with the device, simply contact Repower to activate the option. Those who prefer more predictable conditions can continue with the standard Grischunpower product, which still maintains the seasonal structure introduced in 2027.
