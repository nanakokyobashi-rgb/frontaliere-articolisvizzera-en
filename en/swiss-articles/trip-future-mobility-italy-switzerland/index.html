The context

The TRIP project (Cross-border Intermodal Project) is an initiative that aims to improve mobility between Lombardy and Graubünden, two regions overlooking the Alps that have a history of collaboration and cultural and commercial exchange. The project was launched in 2020 and is expected to be completed by 2025.

The objectives

The main objectives of the TRIP project are:

  • Develop the rail and intermodal network between Lombardy and Graubünden, in order to improve mobility and the connection between the two regions.
  • Create a structured cross-border dialogue between regional and local authorities, to ensure collaboration and coordination between the different entities involved.
  • Promote sustainable mobility and the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions, through the use of electric vehicles and more efficient transport technologies.

The benefits

The TRIP project is expected to bring several benefits, including:

  • Improved mobility and connection between Lombardy and Graubünden.
  • Creation of new job opportunities and economic development.
  • Reduction of gas emissions

Operational details

The TRIP (Cross-border Railway Interoperability Project) project aims to define future scenarios for the development of cross-border mobility, through the analysis of opportunities for strengthening the network and possible new connections. The objective is to strengthen cross-border dialogue and collaboration to address in a coordinated manner the challenges related to rail and intermodal mobility between Lombardy and Graubünden.

The basic idea of the TRIP project is to create a more efficient and more sustainable railway network, which can meet the needs of a growing population and an increasingly integrated economy. To achieve this goal, the project is based on three fundamental pillars:

1. Strengthening the existing network : the TRIP project focuses on the enhancement and restructuring of existing railway infrastructures, to improve their efficiency and transport capacity. This includes replacing old tracks and modernising signalling and control systems. 2. New connections: the TRIP project envisages the creation of new railway connections between the cantons of Lombardy and Graubünden, to improve the connection between the two regions and increase the mobility of people and goods. Key points

The TRIP project is an interesting initiative and we recognise the importance of strengthening cross-border dialogue and collaboration. At the moment there are no concrete interventions directly on the territory, but the project aims to define a shared strategic vision and create a planning basis for possible future developments.

The TRIP project is the result of a partnership between the Swiss Confederation and the cantons of Geneva, Valais and Graubünden, with the aim of improving mobility and connection between the Alpine regions. This initiative is particularly important for Switzerland, which is one of the main transit countries for travellers between Western and Eastern Europe.

The TRIP project focuses on defining a shared strategic vision for alpine mobility, which takes into account the needs and priorities of the different actors involved. This includes setting clear and measurable objectives, as well as creating a planning base that can be used to develop concrete and bankable projects.
