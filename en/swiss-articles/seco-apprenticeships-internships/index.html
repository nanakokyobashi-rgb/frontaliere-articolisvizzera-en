Working at SECO: Apprenticeships and Internships

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 October 2026

SECO has more than 900 employees, three offices in Switzerland and around 30 secondments: apprenticeships, traineeships and translation mandates.

Context

At a Glance

  • SECO has over 900 employees and executives.
  • Three offices in Switzerland and approximately 30 staff members stationed in various countries.
  • Offers AFC apprenticeships, SMC internships, and university internships.

Key Facts

  • Workforce: over 900 employees and managers
  • Offices: three in Switzerland
  • Staff stationed abroad: approximately 30 in various countries
  • AFC apprenticeships: commerce, digital business, and information technology
  • Internships: SMC and university-level

Over 900 employees and managers work at SECO, spread across three locations in Switzerland. In addition to this workforce, there are approximately 30 staff members on assignment in various countries. The presentation thus describes an employer with an organized presence in Switzerland and a component that extends beyond national borders.

Four Entry Pathways

The opportunities are not limited to a single profile. SECO offers AFC apprenticeships for commercial clerks, digital business developers, and IT professionals. Commercial high school interns are also welcome: the commercial high school internship is presented as a distinct path from the AFC apprenticeship.

For university students, the program offers a university internship. SECO presents this as a career starter in which interns can put their knowledge into practice, take on various responsibilities, and work on a range of concrete projects. The program is thus described in terms of operational activities and responsibilities.

SECO also highlights an area aimed at external collaborators. The language services provide various specialized services and have an extensive network of freelance translators who translate into the official languages or other languages.

In terms of the work environment, SECO emphasizes responsibility, open-mindedness, and personal initiative. The presentation is aimed at employees who wish to help shape Swiss economic policy and associates the work with purpose, responsibility, and excellent working conditions. SECO also states that it opens up new opportunities for those who want to contribute to Swiss economic policy. Training, apprenticeships, university internships, and language-related positions are therefore all part of the same career opportunity.

Operational details

For those considering a career path at SECO, a practical understanding begins with distinguishing between vocational training, apprenticeships, university internships, and external collaboration. These are not interchangeable terms: the source assigns a different function to each.

The AFC is the track to consider when your interest lies in commerce, digital business development, or the IT sector. The SMC internship, on the other hand, is designed for business school interns. This distinction clarifies the first step in the selection process: it’s not enough to simply look for a generic opportunity; you need to identify the pathway that aligns with your own career trajectory.

The university internship offers an additional dimension: it connects previously acquired knowledge with responsibility and concrete projects. Those evaluating the application can therefore view the proposal as a hands-on experience, not merely as time spent in the office. The description of the work environment adds three compatibility criteria: responsibility, open-mindedness, and personal initiative. These are the qualities that SECO states it values in its day-to-day work.

The translation track follows a different logic. It is aimed at external collaborators and is linked to the language services, which work with a broad network of freelance translators in the official languages or other languages. This distinction helps prevent confusion between an internal training opportunity and an external assignment.

The common thread highlighted in the presentation is the relationship between work and growth: SECO discusses new prospects for employees, while for university interns, it emphasizes the application of knowledge and projects. The choice can therefore be evaluated on two levels: the access channel and the specific content of the experience.

The point is not to compare different offers, but to use SECO’s classification to understand what type of experience to look for: an AFC apprenticeship, an SMC internship, a university internship, or a translation assignment.

Key points

Anyone who wants to turn this description into a structured search can follow a checklist, without mixing up paths with different goals.

Steps for Choosing

1. Define your starting point. The AFC apprenticeship covers the three fields listed: commercial clerks, digital business developers, and the IT sector. The SMC internship, on the other hand, is for business school interns. 2. If you’re a college student, distinguish your college internship from the other options. The key criteria to look for in the job description are the opportunity to apply your knowledge in practice, take on various responsibilities, and work on concrete projects. 3. If you work as a freelance translator, consider opportunities for freelance assignments. SECO’s language services have an extensive network and work in the official languages as well as other languages. 4. Compare your goals with the work environment described by SECO: responsibility, open-mindedness, and personal initiative are explicitly valued. 5. Keep the type of position distinct. An AFC apprenticeship, an SMC internship, a university internship, and a translation assignment are not presented as the same type of opportunity; recognizing the category helps you avoid searching for information using keywords that are too generic.

The sequence leads from the title of the opportunity to the nature of the experience. For an apprentice, the first filter is the AFC framework; for an SMC intern, the connection to the business school; for a university intern, the combination of knowledge, responsibility, and projects. For an external contractor, however, the reference is the language service and the freelance network.

A careful reading reduces the risk of attributing AFC characteristics to a university internship or of considering a freelance assignment as an internship. The terms used by SECO help keep the pathways separate and link each choice to the experience described. The same logic allows you to highlight what the presentation emphasizes: responsibility, open-mindedness, personal initiative, and the opportunity to work on concrete projects.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many people work at SECO?
According to the description, SECO has more than 900 employees and managers at three locations in Switzerland. The same presentation shows around 30 secondments in different countries. The figure shows the size of the organisation as an employer and distinguishes between employees at Swiss sites and people posted abroad.
Which apprenticeships does SECO offer?
SECO offers apprenticeships for commercial employees, digital business developers and IT specialists. The presentation adds that interns from the business school are welcome. The EFZ and the SMC apprenticeship are therefore referred to as different opportunities within the training offer.
What does a university internship at SECO include?
The university internship is presented as a career start. Interns have the opportunity to put their knowledge into practice, take on various responsibilities and work on a number of concrete projects. The proposal thus links university education with practical experience within SECO.
Who are translation mandates for?
Translation contracts are addressed to external collaborators. SECO's language services provide specialised services and have an extensive network of freelance translators. Translations can be carried out in the official languages or in other languages. The formula is different from apprenticeships and apprenticeships presented in the same offer.
