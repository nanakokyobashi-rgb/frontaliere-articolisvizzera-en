From Fribourg, the Fribourg-based company Aevis announced the alliance between its subsidiary Swiss Medical Network and Phoenix Pharma Switzerland, based in Ticino. Aevis operates in the hotel and hospital sectors. The announcement does not disclose the financial details of the transaction.

The entry of the Benu pharmacies

The consequence indicated in the note concerns the Benu pharmacies in Ticino: they will become part of Swiss Medical Network's Rete Sant'Anna. The network has been active in Ticino since January 2025. The announcement therefore links Phoenix Pharma Switzerland's presence in the canton with a Swiss Medical Network network already active in the area.

The transition described also concerns the care pathway of Ticino-based insured persons with the Viva model. These people will be able to turn to a Benu pharmacy for an initial consultation, preventive services, vaccinations or smoking cessation programs. The note presents the four areas as services accessible through the pharmacy, coordinated with their family doctor.

The agreement leaves the family doctor as the point of reference in the care pathway, as Aevis specifies. Benu pharmacies will thus be able to offer Ticino-based insured persons with the Viva model the listed services, coordinating with the doctor.
