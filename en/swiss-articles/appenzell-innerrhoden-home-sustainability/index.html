Buying a house in Appenzello Interno: mortgage and taxes

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 3 September 2026

Procedure, own funds, mortgage sustainability, notary fees and cantonal taxes to purchase properties in Appenzell Internno.

Context

In brief

  • Buying a house requires own funds (min. 20%), mortgage sustainability and property valuation
  • Notary fees and cantonal taxes vary by canton; Appenzell Innerrhoden has its own tax system
  • Procedure: search, financing, inspection, notarial deed, land registry

Key facts

  • What: Residential property purchase with mortgage in Switzerland
  • Where: Canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden
  • Who: Buyer, bank, notary, land registry office
  • When: Multi-phase procedure (2-6 months from search to registration)
  • Competence: Cantonal/municipal taxation (transfer taxes, registration fees)

Buying a house in Appenzell Innerrhoden, like in any Swiss canton, means dealing with three levels of taxation (federal, cantonal, municipal) and standardized procedures at the national level but with specific cantonal variations.

The buyer must ensure sufficient own funds (minimum percentage required by banks), mortgage sustainability (assessment of repayment capacity according to standardized criteria), and coverage of expenses (notary fees, registration fees, property transfer taxes).

In Appenzell Innerrhoden, as in every canton, real estate taxation is governed by cantonal laws and municipal ordinances. There is no federal tax on purchased properties — the federal state DOES NOT tax property transfers — but the canton and municipalities have their own laws on transfer taxes (so-called transfer tax or registration fee) and mortgage taxes.

Operational details

The sustainability of the mortgage is the core of the bank's evaluation. Swiss banks apply standardized criteria that go beyond the simple ratio between the loan amount and the property price. The LTV ratio and own funds The mortgage CANNOT exceed a certain percentage of the property value. The national standard: mortgage up to 80% of the estimated value, while the last 20% are covered by the buyer's own funds. Some banks offer up to 85-90%, but require minimum own funds and complementary mortgage insurance. This means that a couple intending to buy without having at least 20% in own funds will encounter significant obstacles in financing. Stress test and repayment capacity The bank does not only evaluate the mortgage amount, but whether the buyer will be able to repay it in adverse scenarios. The stress test simulates an increase in interest rates (from today up to +1 or +1.5% per year), a reduction in family income (possible job loss of a spouse), increase in ordinary expenses (energy, maintenance). The buyer must demonstrate that even with higher rates, the mortgage installment plus housing expenses remains sustainable (usually <30-33% of gross income). This criterion is fundamental: a young couple who earns combined gross amounts, in a stress test scenario with higher rates, must demonstrate that they can continue to pay the mortgage without dedicating more than 33% of their income to mortgage debt and related housing expenses. Income capacity verification Banks require the last 3 pay slips, tax return, job offer (if new employee), company balance sheets (if self-employed/freelancer). The income considered is the net income, with federal/cantonal taxes deducted. In Appenzell Innerrhoden, as in every canton, banks automatically deduct the federal direct tax (FDT) and the cantonal/municipal tax based on official data — this varies by canton and municipality and directly impacts the estimated available income. For those working in another jurisdiction, the bank requires income verification from that source and will take into account the tax burden applied by the law of that jurisdiction.

Key points

From Research to Registration

Phase 1: Research and Negotiation (Weeks 1-4) The buyer identifies a property on the market through a real estate agent, cantonal online portals, or municipal advertisements. Once they find the property, they negotiate the price with the seller, request an appraisal or property inspection, and sign a purchase agreement (private or notarial document, depending on the canton).

Phase 2: Financing (Weeks 2-6) Contact at least 3 banks to compare interest rates (fixed vs. variable), opening fees, management fees, and early repayment conditions. Provide documentation (pay slips, tax return, bank statement, employer letters). The bank performs a sustainability test and property valuation according to standardized criteria.

Phase 3: Financing Commitment (Week 6) The bank issues a "mortgage commitment" (subject to final checks) for 30-60 days. The buyer has a limited time to proceed with the notary and complete the transaction.

Phase 4: Notarial Deed (Weeks 7-8) The buyer contacts a notary in the canton of Appenzell Innerrhoden. The notary drafts the deed of sale (Kaufvertrag), verifies the seller's title deeds, calculates due taxes and fees (transfer tax, registration fee), organizes the security deposit in a blocked account, and coordinates with the bank for the mortgage payment. All parties (buyer, seller, bank, notary) sign the deed in the presence of the notary. The notary keeps the original deed and deposits copies with the land registry office.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much equity do I need to buy a house in Appenzello Interno?
Minimum 20% of the purchase price is the Swiss national standard accepted by banks. Some banks require 25-30% to accept more favourable terms or to cover rate increases. If you have less than 20%, you can still get a mortgage, but you will have to take out supplemental insurance and face higher rates. The higher your equity, the more favorable the interest rate.
How do I calculate if a mortgage is sustainable for my family?
The rule of thumb: mortgage installment plus living expenses (heating, insurance, maintenance) should NOT exceed 30-33% of your combined household gross income. The bank applies a stress test by adding +1.5% to the current rates to check repayment capacity in adverse scenarios (rate increase, job loss, income reduction). Use the online calculator to simulate different scenarios.
What are the notary fees and taxes in Appenzello Interno?
Notary fees vary by canton and are regulated by cantonal tariff (percentage of the purchase price or fixed amount — consult the notary association Appenzello Interno). Appenzello Interno has a pass-through tax rate (consult the cantonal tax administration Finanzkammer). Add land registration fee (fixed amount or percentage) and any mortgage fee (some cantons require it for registration of the loan in the land register).
How long does it take from contract to notarial deed?
Generally 6-12 weeks from the signing of the purchase promise to the signing of the notarial deed. It depends on financing verification (2-4 weeks), property inspection (1-2 weeks), documentation preparation (1-2 weeks), notary availability. Some complex cases (inheritance, rights of first refusal, border disputes) take up to 4-5 months.
