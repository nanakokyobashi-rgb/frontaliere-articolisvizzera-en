These rules provide for the application of an exemption and a tax credit for frontier workers who reside in Italy but work in Switzerland. ### Deductible and tax credit The new tax regime introduces an exemption and a tax credit for frontier workers. The deductible is a sum that is not taxed, while the tax credit is a sum that is deducted from the taxes due. These measures were introduced to ease the tax burden on border workers working near the border. ## Differences between the old and the new regime The old tax regime did not provide any exemption or tax credit for frontier workers. With the new agreement, on the other hand, frontier workers can benefit from these tax breaks. This represents a significant change for border workers working within 20 km of the border.
