Healthcare and old border workers: the contribution for the staff (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 29 July 2026

Lombardy Region intends to finance the 20% increase for doctors and nurses through monthly contributions for old border workers.

Context

In a nutshell

  • 20% increase in doctors' and nurses' salaries within 20km of the border
  • Monthly contribution for old border workers between 30 and 200 euros
  • Measure based on Financial 2024 to remedy regulatory gaps
  • What: Contribution for health and increase in health salaries
  • When: Announced in April 2026
  • Where: Lombardy (Swiss border area)
  • Who: Councillor Massimo Sertori and old border guards
  • Amount: Salary increase 20%; contribution 3% - 6% of net income

According to what was communicated in April 2026, the Lombardy Region has outlined a plan to combat the flight of health professionals to Switzerland. The goal is to increase by 20% the salaries of doctors and nurses operating within a radius of twenty kilometers from the Swiss border, where the attractiveness of cross-border salaries — which can triple or quadruple compared to Italian ones — makes it difficult to hire new staff.

The details of the salary increase

The economic operation translates into concrete figures for health personnel: a doctor would receive about ten thousand euros more per year, while for a nurse the increase would be 5,400 euros. To finance this measure, the councilor for local authorities, Massimo Sertori, confirmed the intention to apply the contribution provided for by the 2024 Finance Act.

This levy would affect the so-called "old border workers", residents in Lombardy and workers in Switzerland who benefit from the binary system.

Operational details

Analysis of the binary system and acquired rights

To understand the impact of this measure, it is necessary to analyse the position of the old frontier workers. These workers paid the sick pay in Switzerland for themselves and, until 1999, a bill of about 600 thousand lire to allow family members to use the Italian National Health Service (SSN). Subsequently, with the transition of healthcare to the Regions and the Switzerland-EU agreement, the right of option was introduced.

Comparison of health options

The frontier worker, within three months, can choose between two distinct paths to healthcare:

| Option | Indicative Cost | Coverage | | :--- | :--- | :--- | | Swiss Health | Minimum 350 euros/month | Only the worker | | SSN Italian | Variable contribution (€30-200) | Worker and family members |

Councillor Sertori explained that, due to a regulatory vacuum, the old border workers have stopped paying the contribution for access to the SSN. The Finanziaria of 2024, promoted by Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti, aims to remedy this situation by introducing a contribution ranging between 30 and 200 euros per month.

Key points

Application Procedures and Controls

The Lombardy Region has already begun the operational phase for the implementation of these payments. The assessor Sertori has stated that the administration is preparing the necessary applications for the collection of contributions. A critical point is the identification of the subjects obliged: the list of old frontaliers is currently held by the Swiss authorities, making direct access to the data for the Italian part difficult.

Despite the lack of an official list provided by Switzerland, the Region will proceed through the following operational steps:

1. Implementation of the informatic applications for the calculation of the contribution. 2. Request for self-certifications from workers residing in Lombardy and employed in Switzerland. 3. Execution of sample checks to verify the truthfulness of the declarations. 4. Issuance of payment vouchers based on the percentage of net income (3%-6%).

For those working in Switzerland, it is essential to monitor one's fiscal and insurance position. Although the payroll tax will be deducted in Switzerland, access to Italian healthcare services for Lombardy residents will now be linked to the payment of these contributions, which vary from 30 to 200 euros per month.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who are the old frontiersmen involved in this measure?
They are residents of Lombardy who work in Switzerland and who benefit from the so-called binary system, having paid in the past the sickness fund in Switzerland and a contribution for the extension of the SSN to family members until 1999.
What is the expected monthly contribution amount?
The contribution provided for by the Financial 2024 varies from a minimum of 30 euros to a maximum of 200 euros per month, generally calculated between 3% and 6% of the worker's net income.
What will the funds raised by the Lombardy Region be used for?
The funds will be used in the territory to finance a 20% salary increase for doctors and nurses working within 20 km of the border, counteracting the hemorrhaging of personnel to Switzerland.
