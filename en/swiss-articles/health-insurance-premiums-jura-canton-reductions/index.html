  • Cantonal premium reduction depends on income and assets.

Key Facts

  • What: LAMal premiums and reductions in the Canton of Jura.
  • Legal basis: Federal Health Insurance Act (LAMal).
  • Adult deductibles: CHF 300/500/1000/1500/2000/2500.
  • Premium reduction: Cantonal subsidy based on income/assets.
  • Alternative models: HMO, Telmed, healthcare networks.

In the Canton of Jura, as throughout Switzerland, basic health insurance (LAMal/KVG) is mandatory for all residents. Premiums, set annually by health insurers, vary significantly between cantons and insurance models. The Jura-sano.ch system, the cantonal portal, provides official information on available premiums and reductions.

Deductible Tiers and Premiums

Adult insured individuals can choose from six annual deductible tiers: CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000, or 2500. The choice directly influences the premium amount: higher deductibles reduce the monthly premium but increase out-of-pocket costs in case of treatment. For example, a deductible of CHF 2500 can reduce the premium by up to 30% compared to the minimum tier (CHF 300).

Alternative Models

Alternative insurance models offer reduced premiums in exchange for restrictions on access to care. The main ones are:

  • HMO: Coordinated care through a family doctor or a medical center.
  • Telmed: Preliminary telephone consultation with service doctors.
  • Healthcare network: A predetermined list of care providers.

These models can lower premiums by up to 20% compared to the standard model but limit the free choice of doctor.

Operational details

Practical implications for policyholders

The choice of deductible and insurance model requires a careful analysis of your health risk profile and financial capacity.

Savings Scenarios

Case 1: A single person without chronic diseases could opt for a CHF 2500 deductible and Telmed model, reducing the monthly premium by about CHF 150 compared to the standard model with minimum deductible. However, in the event of an unforeseen hospitalisation, it would pay up to CHF 2500 before repayment.

Case 2: A family with young children might prefer the CHF 300 deductible and HMO model for immediate access to affiliated paediatricians, accepting a higher premium but predictable costs.

Comparison with other cantons

While Swiss average LAMal premiums increase (UST/BFS), the Canton of Jura maintains relatively low premiums thanks to cantonal cost containment policies. However, cantonal reductions are less generous than cantons with greater fiscal capacity, such as Zurich or Vaud.

# Reduction Procedures

The Public Health Office jura-sano.ch verifies income and assets through: 1. Tax Return : Gross Income and Deductions 2. Extract from the Land Registry: Real Estate Properties 3. Bank statements: Accounts and investments

Subsidies are calculated automatically and reported by June. The beneficiaries receive a certificate to be presented to the sickness fund for the adjustment of the premium.

How to get the premium reduction in the Jura

Operating steps

1. Eligibility Check : Check the 2024 income/equity limits on jura-sano.ch (available from January) 2. Collect documents: Tax return, bank statements, land register 3. Apply: Online or by post to the Cantonal Health Office by 31 March 4. Wait for decision: The approved subsidies are valid from 1 January of the current year

Useful tools

  • Premium calculator: Simulate deductible/model premiums on comparis.ch or jura-sano.ch
  • Family check-up: Evaluate the annual health risk to choose the optimal deductible
  • Cantonal consultancy: Free jura-sano.ch service to optimise coverage

Critical ⚠️ deadlines: Applications submitted after 31 March do not receive retroactive reductions. New residents have 3 months from arrival to take out basic insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who is entitled to the premium reduction in the Canton of Jura?
Residents with taxable income and net worth below cantonal thresholds. The 2024 limits are published annually on jura-sano.ch. Single-parent families and recipients of social aid have priority.
How is the premium reduction calculated?
The Cantonal Office applies a formula based on income, wealth, family composition and average cantonal premiums. The subsidy covers up to 50% of the basic premium for the most needy cases.
Can I change deductibles during the year?
No, the deductible is chosen annually by November 30 for the following year. Changes during the year are only possible for exceptional events (e.g. serious illness).
