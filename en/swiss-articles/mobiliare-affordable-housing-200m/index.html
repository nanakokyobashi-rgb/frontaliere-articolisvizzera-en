The insurance company will invest 200 million to build 1000 rented apartments commensurate with real costs.

  • Rental model: Pigment commensurate with real costs

  • Where: Switzerland (based in Bern)
  • Who: La Mobiliare (cooperative society)

Furniture, the oldest Swiss insurance company, enters the social housing sector. The announcement comes from a statement issued by Bern: the company will invest up to 200 million francs of equity to build 1000 apartments for rent at a price commensurate with real costs. The initiative responds to a growing national challenge: finding affordable housing has become increasingly difficult for families, young people and the elderly.

The solution proposed by Furniture is based on a model already known in Switzerland: the rent commensurate with costs. The rent will therefore cover the actual costs of construction, financing and maintenance, without profit margins. Operational details

Because housing scarcity is a national issue

The housing access crisis is affecting Switzerland across the board. The costo della vita, including real estate, remains a major concern for Swiss residents. La Mobiliare explicitly acknowledges in the press release that the challenge concerns "families, young people and the elderly" — three demographic groups under pressure in the contemporary housing market.

The model of rent commensurate with costs represents a consolidated approach in Switzerland, already practiced by housing cooperatives and non-profit organizations. Unlike the free market, where the price reflects demand, location and profit margins, the cost rent covers only the actual costs of construction, financing and maintenance. This allows significantly lower rents than market averages, resulting in a concrete economic advantage for tenants.

The Furniture initiative is placed in a context where the contribution of the private sector to public utility construction remains limited. The cooperative stresses that it is pursuing "a long-term approach": the objective is to "make a lasting contribution to ensuring the availability of affordable housing in the Confederation".

Implications for housing availability

The investment of 200 million in 1000 apartments corresponds to an average of 200,000 francs per housing unit.

Key points

How to Access Dedicasa Housing by Mobiliare

Project research and examination have already been initiated, according to Mobiliare's announcement. However, neither the construction start date, nor the schedule for housing availability, nor the tenant selection criteria have been communicated in the initial message. For those interested in following the project's development, the first step is to monitor Mobiliare's official communications through its institutional website.

The company established Dedicasa specifically for this purpose and will entrust Mobiliare Switzerland Asset Management with operational management. Although procedural details for applying are not yet available, it is reasonable to expect that access procedures will be published in the coming months, as soon as the projects are sufficiently advanced.

Verify Your Housing Budget in Switzerland

In the meantime, those searching for housing in Switzerland should evaluate the proportion that rent represents of their income. The widely accepted empirical rule suggests that monthly rent should not exceed 30-33% of net income. For a family with average Swiss incomes, this share often remains a real challenge.

By calculating your available budget for rent, you can identify regions and cantons where market prices fall within your reach. Some cantons have lower average rents than others, and proximity to major urban centers (Zurich, Geneva, Bern, Basel, Lausanne) significantly affects prices. By consulting real estate search platforms and official statistics, you can get a realistic idea of the options available in the current market.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many apartments will Furniture build with this investment?
Furniture announced its intention to build 1000 apartments within the framework of the investment of 200 million francs. Implementation will take place gradually, with research and review of projects already underway. Details of the completion deadlines and geographic distribution of the listings have not yet been released in the official release.
How does the cost-responsive rent model work?
The rent commensurate with costs is a model where the rent covers only the actual costs of construction, financing and maintenance of the property, without profit margins. This enables rentals to be significantly lower than market prices, offering protection from rent inflation and long-term rate predictability for tenants.
Who will have access to Dedicasa's apartments?
La Mobiliare stated that the initiative aims to offer sustainable housing solutions for families, young people and the elderly. The application procedures and specific selection criteria will be communicated in the coming months, as soon as the projects are more advanced. It is advisable to monitor the official channels of the Furniture.
When will the first Dedicasa apartments be available?
Furniture pursues a long-term and gradual approach. The release specifies that the search for and examination of suitable projects has already begun, but does not provide a specific date for the start of construction or delivery of the first homes.
Why does Furniture enter the public housing sector?
According to the statement, Furniture recognizes that finding affordable housing is a growing challenge for many people. As a cooperative founded in 1826, the company says it cares about the community and wants to make a lasting contribution to ensuring the availability of affordable housing in the Confederation.
