Frequently Asked Questions
What is the correct procedure for checking Sunday openings?
To verify a Sunday or holiday opening in the Canton of Zurich, it is necessary to consult the official source of the Canton. The procedure involves defining the perimeter (shop and date), classifying the day, consulting the official regulations and, if necessary, verifying the special authorization at the competent office indicated by the source. Who is competent for exempt opening authorisations?
The competence for exceptions to ordinary business hours may depend on the Canton, the Municipality or another competent institution, depending on the specific case. The Canton's official source provides the necessary information to identify the correct authority to contact for special authorisations. It is essential not to confuse the administrative level and always check the conditions, requirements and deadlines directly on the official cantonal documentation before planning the opening or purchase.
Why is it important to distinguish between ordinary hours and exceptions?
Distinguishing between ordinary hours and exceptions is essential because Sunday and holiday openings often require special authorizations that do not fall within the standard discipline.
