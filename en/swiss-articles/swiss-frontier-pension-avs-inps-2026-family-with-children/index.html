Operational details

Border Worker Pension 2026: AVS/INPS Coordination and Totalization Without Personal Examples

To plan your pension, it’s essential to understand current regulations and basic rules. It’s also critical to grasp how coordination between AVS (Swiss Workers’ Insurance) and INPS (Italian National Social Security Institute) will impact your pension. The Swiss pension system is known for its complexity and interconnectedness with other countries, making precise planning fundamental.

Current regulations require Swiss citizens who worked abroad to request totalization of their pension with INPS. This process may take several hours and documents, so advance planning is crucial.

Coordination between AVS and INPS is vital to ensure accurate pension calculation and payment.

It’s important to note that the Swiss pension system is significantly more generous than Italy’s, with a maximum monthly pension of CHF 4,500.
