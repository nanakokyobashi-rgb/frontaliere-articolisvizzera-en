The Swiss pension system is known for its complexity and interconnectedness with other countries, making precise planning fundamental.

Current regulations require Swiss citizens who worked abroad to request totalization of their pension with INPS. This process may take several hours and documents, so advance planning is crucial. Under the Swiss Federal Law on Social Security (LPS), Swiss workers who worked abroad must request totalization within 3 years of ending their work abroad.

For example, a Swiss worker who worked in France for 10 years must request pension totalization within 3 years of ending their employment, or else lose eligibility for Swiss pension benefits. Regulations also state that Swiss citizens who worked abroad must pay Swiss taxes on part of their pension, which can reach up to CHF 80,000 per year.

Coordination between AVS and INPS is vital to ensure accurate pension calculation and payment.
