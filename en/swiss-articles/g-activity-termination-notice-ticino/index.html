The notification of end of G activity is a crucial step for cross-border workers operating in the Canton of Ticino. In the event of termination of employment, the procedure requires official communication to the SPOP (Population Section) of the Department of Institutions of the Republic and Canton of Ticino. This communication is essential for holders of a G permit, the document that allows residents abroad to work in Switzerland while returning daily or weekly to their country of residence.

Role of SPOP (DI) in the Procedure

The SPOP (DI) is the cantonal office responsible for managing residence and work permit procedures. The notification of end of activity is a necessary administrative requirement to update the cross-border worker's status in cantonal and federal databases.

The notification procedure complies with the obligation to report any changes related to the employment relationship, as required by the regulations for cross-border workers in Ticino. Even in the absence of a specific date or amounts, compliance with this procedure ensures that the administrative status is regularized with both the Ticino authorities and the competent services in Italy.

Operational details

Practical Implications of the End of Activity Notification G

The notification of the end of activity G at the SPOP (DI) has concrete impacts on the working and administrative life of cross-border workers. The proper management of this communication is indeed closely linked to tax rights and obligations, access to social services, and the regularity of one's status in Switzerland and Italy.

What Happens After the Notification

Once the notification is made to the SPOP (DI), the G permit is updated or canceled depending on the situation. This update is necessary to prevent invalid employment relationships from being recorded as active in the Swiss authorities' registers. In the absence of specific dates or amounts in the source, the procedure may vary based on individual situations, but the principle remains that of timely regularization.

The update of the status can also have repercussions on related social benefits, such as AVS/AHV, LPP/BVG, and, in certain cases, on liability for federal direct tax. It is therefore advisable to verify, following the notification, one's contribution and tax status both in Switzerland and in Italy.

Hypothetical Scenarios

In the case of a cross-border worker who voluntarily ceases work activity, the notification allows for the proper closure of the file at the SPOP (DI). In the event of the end of activity for reasons beyond the worker's control, the notification is equally mandatory to ensure administrative transparency.

Frequently Asked Questions
To whom should the notification of the end of activity G in Ticino be presented?
The notification of the end of activity for G permit holders must be submitted to the SPOP (DI), or the Population Section of the Department of the Institutions of the Republic and Canton of Ticino. This office handles the procedures related to residence and work permits.
What documents are needed for the end of activity notification G?
The source does not specify the detailed list of required documents. In general, you must fill out a notification form and attach any supporting documents such as the letter of dismissal or the employment contract. It is advisable to check directly with the SPOP (DI) which documents are required.
When should the G activity end notification be submitted to SPOP (DI)?
The source does not indicate a precise deadline for the submission of the notification. However, it is good practice to transmit the communication promptly, immediately after the termination of the employment relationship, to ensure administrative regularity.
