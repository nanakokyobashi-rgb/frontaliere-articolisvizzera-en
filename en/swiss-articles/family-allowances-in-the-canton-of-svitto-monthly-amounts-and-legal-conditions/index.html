The Canton of Switzerland is one of the few Swiss cantons to grant family allowances to Swiss citizens and resident foreigners. This economic benefit is intended to support families with minor children, enabling them to cover the costs of living and provide a quality education to their children.

Monthly amounts and legal conditions

The payment of family allowances is regulated by the Federal Law on Assistance and Social Security (LAP), which establishes the minimum and maximum amounts for each child. Under this law, the monthly amount for the first child is CHF 250, while for each subsequent child it is CHF 150.

Operational details

The Canton of Switzerland grants family allowances to Swiss citizens and resident foreigners. The monthly amount is CHF 250 for the first child and CHF 150 for each subsequent child. The family allowance is granted for a maximum period of 5 years.

The application for a family allowance may be submitted at the administrative headquarters of the Canton of Schwyz, located in Lucerne. The following documents must be submitted:

  • Birth certificate of the child
  • Parent ID
  • Parent Income Statement

The application for family allowance must be submitted in writing and must be accompanied by a copy of the identity document of the parent and the child. The request can also be submitted through the online portal of the Canton of Schwyz.

The family allowance is granted for a maximum period of 5 years, starting from the date of birth of the child. However, if the parent changes his/her residence in the Canton of Schwyz, the family allowance may be granted for a shorter period.

The law regulating the family allowance in the Canton of Schwyz is the Law on Family Allowances of 1 January 2015. Article 3 of the law states that the family allowance is granted to parents who have an income of less than CHF 120,000 per year.

To apply for the family allowance, you must submit a written application to the Social Service of the Canton of Schwyz. You can download the application from the Canton's website. The family allowance will be granted for a maximum period of 5 years.

The family allowance is a social benefit intended to support families with children. Its aim is to help families cover their children's subsistence and education costs. The extent of the benefit varies depending on household income and the number of children.

In the Canton of Svitto, the family allowance is granted according to the rules of the Social Insurance Code (CSS), approved on 21 March 1995. The law stipulates that the family allowance shall be granted for a maximum amount of 2,500 Swiss francs per month for the first daughter and 2,000 Swiss francs for each subsequent child. However, the actual amount of the allowance may vary depending on the household income and the number of children.

For example, if a father with a monthly income of 6,000 Swiss francs has a 3-year-old daughter, the family allowance will be granted to him in the amount of 1,500 Swiss francs per month, since the first daughter has already been granted the maximum benefit. If, on the other hand, he has two children, the check will be granted to him in the amount of 1,000 Swiss francs per month, since the second son receives a lower amount than the first daughter.
