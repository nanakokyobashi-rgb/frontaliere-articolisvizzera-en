Canton Uri Scholarships: Requirements and Amounts

The Uri Canton, located in the heart of Upper Switzerland, offers scholarships to cross-border students who wish to continue their studies in the Canton. These scholarships are an important support for students who wish to continue their academic training in the Uri Canton.

# Scholarship Access Requirements

To access Canton Uri scholarships, students must meet the following requirements:

  • Citizenship: beneficiaries must be citizens of one of the cantons bordering the Uri Canton.
  • Studies: students must be enrolled in courses of study recognized by Swiss universities.
  • Residence: students must reside in the Uri Canton or in one of the neighbouring cantons.
  • Income: students must have a family income of less than a certain amount, specified by cantonal law.

# Scholarship amounts

Operational details

Uri Canton Scholarships are intended for cross-border students wishing to continue their studies at a higher education institution in Switzerland. To be eligible, students must meet the specific requirements established by the Uri Canton. These requirements include residency in Canton Uri, attendance at a higher education institution, and submission of a correct and complete application.

The maximum amount of the scholarship is specified by Canton Uri and varies according to the individual needs of the students. For the 2022-2023 academic year, the maximum scholarship amount was CHF 10,000 for students attending a higher education institution in Canton Uri. 📊

To be eligible, students must meet the following requirements:

  • Have residency in Canton Uri for at least one year before the start of studies;
  • Attending a higher education institution in Canton Uri;
  • Submit a correct and complete application by the deadline set by the Uri Canton.

Example: A student living in Bellinzona, Canton Ticino, and attending the University of Lausanne, Canton Vaud, might be eligible for a Canton Uri scholarship if they meet the requirements listed above.

Key points

To access Canton Uri scholarships, students must submit a correct and complete application by the deadline specified by Canton Uri. The application must include information about residence, attendance at a higher education institution, and other specific requests. The competent cantonal office will evaluate the applications and allocate the scholarships to the recipients. The selected students will receive an official communication and will be informed about the amounts of the scholarship.

The deadline for submitting applications is set for April 15 of each year. Students must ensure that they submit their application by this date in order to be considered for the scholarship. The application must be complete and correct, otherwise it will not be considered.

Uri Canton Scholarships are intended for students residing in the canton and attending a higher education institution. Beneficiaries can be students of any age, but must be at least 18 years old to be considered. The amount of the scholarship varies depending on the demand and availability of funds. In 2022, the scholarship amount was CHF 1,500 per semester.
