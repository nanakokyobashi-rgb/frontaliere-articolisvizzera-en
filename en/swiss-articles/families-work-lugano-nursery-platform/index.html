Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 June 2026

A cross-party motion calls for regular surveys on after-school services and a unique platform for enrolling in kindergartens in Lugano.

A cross-party motion, with Céline Antonini as the first signatory, was filed with the city council to ask for new operational tools. The main objective is to launch regular surveys, which can function as a constant barometer to assess the real needs in terms of extracurricular services. According to the promoters, in recent years the offer of these services in Lugano has indeed improved, but there is still "room for manoeuvre" for further progress to the benefit of the well-being of families and the attractiveness of the city. It should be noted that current services have significant costs for both families and the Municipality, making it essential to precisely calibrate between supply and demand, to avoid imbalances. Operational details

Practical implications for families and administrations

The motion presented in Lugano highlights several practical implications for both resident families and municipal, and potentially cantonal, administrative management. If implemented, the proposal for regular surveys would allow for the collection of updated data on the concrete needs of families, ensuring a service planning that is more in line with reality. This approach could also be replicated in other Swiss municipalities, promoting a culture of continuous evaluation of public services.

The shortage of places in nurseries, recognized by the motion as a critical issue, is a widespread problem in many urban areas of the country. The creation of a single platform for registrations would facilitate transparency and fairness in access to services, avoiding multiple registrations and reducing situations of prolonged waiting. This would represent a concrete advancement in the management of public resources, with positive repercussions on family well-being and the attractiveness of the territory.

Interaction between supply and demand: a replicable model

The experience of Lugano, if it were to be applied, could become a model for other cantons and Swiss cities facing similar challenges regarding services for children and work-family reconciliation. Cantons such as Zurich, Geneva, Basel, and Bern, where the pressure on the demand for educational services is high due to the high cost of living and demographic changes, could take inspiration from the adoption of similar tools.

Key points

Procedures and Operational Tools for Families

While waiting for the motion to be discussed and potentially approved by the Lugano City Council, families must continue to follow the current procedures for enrolling in nurseries. This involves the need to apply to multiple facilities, monitor waiting lists, and independently manage the search for available spots. The proposal for a single platform, if adopted, would simplify this process by allowing a single online registration with the selection of preferred locations and transparent visibility of available spots.

For municipal administrations, collecting data through regular surveys would require the implementation of reliable digital tools and the establishment of data analysis processes that allow for a quick response to emerging needs. Families will need to get used to actively participating in these surveys, providing useful feedback to improve the quality and quantity of services offered.

What to Do While Waiting for Changes

While waiting for the situation to evolve, it is advisable for families to periodically check in with municipal offices and monitor the publication of updates on enrollment procedures, both through the official channels of the City of Lugano and through industry associations. At the same time, it is useful to maintain a comparative view with other Swiss realities, utilizing the available data on nurseries and child services.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does the cross-party motion presented in Lugano provide for?
The motion calls for the introduction of regular surveys to assess the needs of families on after-school services and the creation of a single platform for kindergarten enrollment, so as to improve the reconcilability between work and family life.
What is the main problem related to nurseries in Lugano?
According to the petitioners, there are no places available in nurseries in Lugano. Families often sign up for multiple facilities in the hope of finding a free place, but they still remain on the waiting list even after getting a place elsewhere.
How would the proposed single platform for kindergarten enrollment work?
The platform would allow centralised registration at multiple facilities, allowing you to choose locations and check seat availability in real time. In this way, double registrations would be avoided and unnecessary waiting lists would be reduced.
