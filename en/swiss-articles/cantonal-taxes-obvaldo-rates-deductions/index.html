Operational details

The cantonal tax administration manages cantonal taxes in the Canton of Obvaldo. The permitted steps and deductions are established by cantonal law and are subject to annual variations. The delivery deadlines for the cantonal tax return are set by the cantonal government.

Establishment of cantonal taxes

According to cantonal law, cantonal taxes were established to finance essential public services, such as education, health and safety. Taxes are levied on taxable income, which includes earned income, interest, and annuities.

Cantonal Tax Rates

Cantonal tax rates vary based on taxable income. For example, in 2022, the disposal of cantonal taxes was as follows:

  • 0% for incomes up to CHF 3,000
  • 10% for income from CHF 3,001 to CHF 6,000
  • 15% for incomes from CHF 6,001 to 10,000
  • 20% for income from CHF 10,001 to CHF 15,000
  • 25% for income above CHF 15,000

Allowed Deductions

The allowable deductions for cantonal taxes are established by cantonal law and vary according to the type of income and individual circumstances. For example, people who have dependent children can deduct up to CHF 2,000 per year for living expenses. In addition, people who have lost their jobs can deduct up to CHF 10,000 per year for adaptation expenses.

Rates and Taxation

The income tax in the Canton of Obvaldo is 11.5% for incomes up to CHF 20,000, while for incomes above CHF20,000, the rate is 11.5% for the first CHF 20,000 and 12.5% for higher incomes. It is important to note that income tax may vary based on income and personal situation.

Deductions and discounts

Individuals may deduct the following amounts from their taxable income: CHF 2,500 for the family, CHF1,250 for the spouse, CHF 1,250 for each child under the age of 16, and CHF 500 for each student child. In addition, natural persons may deduct an amount of CHF 2,500 for the purchase of a main home.

Property taxes

Property tax in the Canton of Obvaldo is 0.8% for real estate and 0.5% for movable property. It is important to note that property tax may vary based on the value of the property and personal situation.
