Operational details

Canton Uri school system: enrolment and cycles

The school obligation in the Canton of Uri is established by cantonal law and lasts for 9 years. The school cycles are divided into elementary and upper secondary. The age of registration is 4-6 years. The school calendar is 38 weeks. The school system is managed by the Department of Education and Vocational Training.

School cycles

The Canton of Uri offers two levels of education: primary and upper secondary. The elementary cycle lasts 6 years and ends with the third class. The upper secondary cycle lasts 3 years and ends with maturity. The aim of the school system is to provide quality education to young people in the Canton of Uri.

Schools and languages of instruction

The Canton of Uri offers several schools with different languages of instruction. Schools can be private or public and offer instruction in German, French or Italian. Private schools are run by associations or foundations and may have a different school structure and calendar than public schools. 📊

Concrete examples

Example: Altdorf Public School offers German language instruction and has a 38-week school calendar. Sarnen Private School offers French language instruction and has a 40-week school calendar. 💡

Key points

Canton Uri school system: enrolment and cycles

In order to enroll your children in high school, you must meet the requirements established by cantonal law. These requirements may include attending certain disciplines or participating in specific programs. It is important to consult the official information of the Canton of Uri for specific details.

The Canton of Uri follows the Swiss school model, which provides for an overall educational structure of 13 years. Swiss children start primary school at the age of 6, for a period of 4 years. Subsequently, they attend lower secondary school (Gymnasium) for another 5 years.

To access high school, children must pass a series of end-of-cycle exams, which assess their linguistic, mathematical and scientific skills. 📊 According to the federal law of 13 December 2013, students must achieve a proficiency level of at least 4.5 out of 6.0 in one of the official languages (German, French, Italian or Romance).

Some concrete examples of high school enrollment requirements in the Canton of Uri include:

  • Attendance of at least 3 scientific disciplines (mathematics, physics and chemistry) with a grade of at least 4.0 out of 6.0.
  • Participation in English, French or German language programs for at least one year.
  • The presentation of a research project or a literary work of at least 10 pages.
