Sergio Romaneschi guided the body for 13 years. The new commander is Paolo Baiardi. Ceremony at the Civic Palace.

Context

In brief

  • Sergio Romaneschi led the Luganesi Volunteers for 13 years
  • Official change of command at Palazzo Civico
  • New commander Paolo Baiardi takes over

Key facts

  • What: Handover of command of the Luganesi Volunteer Corps
  • When: Today
  • Where: Palazzo Civico, Lugano
  • Who: Sergio Romaneschi (outgoing commissioner), Paolo Baiardi (new commander), Mayor Michele Foletti
  • Amount: Not applicable

The handover of command of the Luganesi Volunteer Corps took place today at Palazzo Civico in Lugano. Sergio Romaneschi, who has led the corps for 13 years, officially handed over the duties to Paolo Baiardi. The ceremony was officiated by Mayor Michele Foletti, who emphasized the historical and civic role of this institution. In his farewell speech, Romaneschi proudly recalled the most significant moments of his term, including the celebrations for the 225th anniversary of the Lugano Motions. Among the most important events, the parade of historical Swiss militias strengthened the bond between the corps and the local community. Romaneschi expressed gratitude and confidence in the future of the corps, highlighting the spirit of service and commitment of its members.

Handover and values

The mayor highlighted that this transition represents an important renewal to ensure continuity and new energies. The tradition of solidarity, sense of duty, and respect for traditions are still relevant values that make the Corps a point of reference for the city and the canton.

Practical Analysis

The change of command at the Volontari Luganesi represents an example of how civic organizations can evolve while maintaining the fundamental values of solidarity, sense of duty, and belonging. After 13 years of leadership, Romaneschi has contributed to strengthening the civic role of the corps, which holds historical and symbolic significance not only for Lugano but for the entire region.

The volunteer institution, formed over two centuries ago, has played a crucial role during historical moments, such as the Moti di Lugano, and continues to be a reference point for collective memory. The presence of representatives from political institutions, such as councilor Norman Gobbi and municipal officials, confirms how deeply rooted this organization is in the civic and political fabric of the canton.

The new commander, Paolo Baiardi, faces the challenge of preserving this tradition of civic engagement while also introducing innovations that can attract new generations. The continuity of the mission, accompanied by renewed energy, is essential to keep the sense of responsibility and solidarity alive.

In analyzing the practical implications of this transition, one can observe how it may influence civic participation at the local and regional levels. The continuity ensured by the new commander will strengthen support and community service activities, even in a context of increasing social complexity.

For interested citizens and volunteers, this represents an opportunity to actively participate in civic life, contributing to strengthening the social fabric of the canton. The chance to participate in events, demonstrations, and training activities offers concrete tools to engage new recruits.

Action For those who wish to actively contribute to the Lugano Volunteer Corps, the first step is to find out about the methods of membership and training. You can consult the official website of the Corps or contact Palazzo Civico di Lugano directly. The procedure generally involves some basic steps: 1. Official registration via online form or in person at the headquarters. 2. Participation in training and refresher courses, which allow you to acquire basic and specialized skills. 3. Commitment to community service and support activities, according to their abilities and availability. Active participation may involve relief activities, civic support and participation in public events. It is important to adhere to deadlines and internal body regulations. For new volunteers, the Corps periodically organizes information meetings and training courses, which are an opportunity to learn more about roles, responsibilities and values. If you also want to contribute with financial or logistical support, you can contact the municipal authorities or support associations. How does the handover to the Lugano Volunteers take place?
The transfer of command took place today at Palazzo Civico in Lugano, where the outgoing commander Sergio Romaneschi officially handed over the functions to the new commander Paolo Baiardi, in the presence of institutional representatives and the mayor Michele Foletti.

The body represents fundamental values such as solidarity, sense of duty, discipline, respect for traditions and attachment to the city. These values were remembered during the ceremony and are the basis of the volunteers' civic engagement.
