Dertour Suisse aims to surpass one billion in revenue

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

Dertour Suisse aims to exceed 1 billion francs, summer 2026 bookings show 14% for moderate destinations and it expects over 20% in 4‑6 years; branches 124, employees 1750, 250 positions to be cut by the end of 2027.

The most important Swiss tour operator aims to exceed a billion francs in turnover thanks to the acquisition of competitor Hotelplan. Dertour Suisse sees a recovery in bookings after the collapse caused by the conflict in the Middle East and expects only a slight decline in revenues for the whole year compared to 2025.

«We will achieve this goal in the near future», says CEO Stephanie Schulze zur Wiesch in statements reported by the Awp agency.

The year had started very well. Until mid-February, the manager explained, the trend was excellent; then the war in Iran interrupted everything. For weeks, bookings have dropped markedly. Destinations in the Middle East and the Indian Ocean recorded a double-digit contraction, linked to the closure of the Persian Gulf hubs. With the summer the situation stabilized and in recent weeks bookings have returned above the previous year's level.

The destinations driving the recovery

The reopening of the important Dubai hub accompanied the recovery of the Indian Ocean destinations. Maldives shows strong growth; Seychelles and Mauritius significantly increasing demand. The Caribbean and South America also did well, with a very lively last minute.

In the short haul, Crete, Spain and the Canary Islands are strong, while Sardinia remains in high demand. Mallorca did less well than the previous year due to partly rising prices, but remains an important destination. Turkey is less in demand: in addition to the war in Iran, less convenient prices and more expensive flights from Switzerland weigh on it. Swiss customers focus on Tunisia. US-related assets are doing poorly due to President Donald Trump's policies and sharp price increases.

The scorching summer in Switzerland has changed customer habits. Approximately 14% of 2026 summer bookings are for destinations with more moderate temperatures. Interest in Scandinavia, Scotland, Iceland and Canada has grown by around a fifth in the last five years; the CEO predicts that in 4-6 years the share could exceed 20%, without equaling the volumes of the Mediterranean.

Operational details

Practical reading for those who live in Switzerland

Dertour Suisse's indications allow you to read the market on two levels. On the one hand, tourist demand reacts quickly to the reopening of hubs, prices and temperatures. On the other hand, the integration of Hotelplan changes the operational structure of the group, with observable effects on the branch network, the headquarters and the labor market.

Branches, headquarters and job market

The second front concerns company reorganization. The branches have fallen to 124, compared to 133 at the end of May, but Dertour Suisse is not announcing a wave of closures: these points of sale still generate more than half of the turnover. The network, therefore, remains central in the distribution of the offer even while the IT migration proceeds.

The transfer of the headquarters from Glattbrugg, a hamlet of Opfikon (ZH), to Zurich Altstetten is expected by the end of the year. The cut of 250 posts by the end of 2027 is indicated as in line with plans; at the end of May the workforce numbered 1750 employees. For those who observe the labor market, the data thus describes a planned reduction in the workforce together with the maintenance of a commercial network that is still decisive for revenues.

How to use data before deciding

The source does not propose a booking procedure, but offers concrete criteria for navigating through destinations, prices, and temperatures. The following steps remain limited to available information and help separate the different causes that are shifting demand.

Four practical checks.

1. Start with geographic area. For short haul, compare Crete, Spain, Canary Islands and Sardinia. For more distant destinations, consider Indian Ocean, Caribbean and South America separately, because the source describes different trends after the reopening of the Dubai hub.

2. Isolate the price factor. Mallorca remains an important destination, but has done less well after the partial price increase. In Turkey, both cheaper prices and more expensive flights from Switzerland weigh in. For the United States, the source reports a sharp increase in prices: so the comparison must be made on this item as well.

3. Include temperature among the criteria. After the scorching summer in Switzerland, more moderate destinations attracted 14 percent of 2026 summer bookings. Scandinavia, Scotland, Iceland, and Canada are the areas indicated by the source to follow this trend.

4. If you follow the company as a client or worker, note the stated deadlines. The headquarters is expected to move by year-end, and the plan is to cut 250 positions by the end of 2027. However, the branch network remains active, with 124 locations and more than half of the sales generated by these stores.
