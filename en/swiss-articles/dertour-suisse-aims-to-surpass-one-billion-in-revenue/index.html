Dertour Suisse aims to surpass one billion in revenue (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 September 2026

Dertour Suisse aims to exceed 1 billion francs, summer 2026 bookings show 14% for moderate destinations and it expects over 20% in 4‑6 years; branches 124, employees 1750, 250 positions to be cut by the end of 2027.

Operational details

Practical reading for those living in Switzerland

The indications from Dertour Suisse make it possible to read the market on two levels. On the one hand, tourism demand reacts quickly to the reopening of hubs, prices and temperatures. On the other, the integration of Hotelplan is changing the group's operating structure, with observable effects on the branch network, headquarters and labour market.

For a Swiss customer, the useful data point is the difference between destinations that recover quickly and destinations that lose appeal for reasons of price or political factors. Mallorca remains important even after a result below the previous year. Turkey, however, shows weaker demand, while for the United States the source flags both Donald Trump's policies and the drastic increase in prices.

Branches, headquarters and labour market

The second front concerns corporate reorganization. The number of branches has fallen to 124, from 133 at the end of May, but Dertour Suisse is not announcing a wave of closures: these points of sale still generate more than half of turnover. The network therefore remains central to distributing the offering even as the IT migration proceeds.

The relocation of the headquarters from Glattbrugg, a district of Opfikon (ZH), to Zurich Altstetten is expected by the end of the year. The reduction of 250 positions by the end of 2027 is described as being in line with the plans; at the end of May, the workforce numbered 1750 employees. For those observing the labour market, the data thus describe a planned reduction in headcount together with the retention of a commercial network that remains decisive for revenue.

Key points

How to use the data before deciding

The source does not propose a booking procedure, but offers concrete criteria for navigating among destinations, prices and temperatures. The following steps remain limited to the available information and help separate the different causes shifting demand.

Four practical checks

1. Start with the geographic area. For short-haul travel, compare Crete, Spain, the Canary Islands and Sardinia. For more distant destinations, consider the Indian Ocean, the Caribbean and South America separately, because the source describes different trends after the reopening of the Dubai hub.

2. Isolate the price factor. Mallorca remains an important destination, but performed less well after the partial price increase. In Turkey, both less favorable prices and more expensive flights from Switzerland weigh on demand. For the United States, the source notes a sharp increase in prices: the comparison should therefore also be made on this item.

3. Include temperature among the criteria. After the scorching summer in Switzerland, more moderate destinations attracted 14% of summer 2026 bookings. Scandinavia, Scotland, Iceland and Canada are the areas indicated by the source for following this trend.

4. If you follow the company as a customer or worker, note the stated deadlines. The headquarters should be relocated by the end of the year, and the plan calls for a cut of 250 positions by the end of 2027. The branch network nevertheless remains active, with 124 locations and more than half of the revenue generated by these stores.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Dertour Suisse's turnover target and how does it intend to achieve it?
Dertour Suisse aims to surpass one billion francs in revenue thanks to the acquisition of Hotelplan and the resumption of bookings after the decline due to the conflict in the Middle East; CEO Stephanie Schulze zur Wiesch says the goal will be achieved in the near future.
What trends are emerging in the travel preferences of Swiss customers for summer 2026?
14% of summer bookings 2026 concern destinations with more moderate temperatures (Scandinavia, Scotland, Iceland and Canada); interest in these destinations has grown by about a fifth in the last five years and the share could exceed 20% in the next 4-6 years, while remaining lower than the volumes of the Mediterranean.
What are Dertour Suisse's employment and sales network prospects by the end of 2027?
The workforce currently has 1,750 employees (end of May) and expects a reduction of 250 posts by the end of 2027; branches have fallen to 124 from 133 at the end of May, but the network still generates more than half of the turnover and remains central, while the headquarters will move from Glattbrugg to Zurich Altstetten by the end of the year.
