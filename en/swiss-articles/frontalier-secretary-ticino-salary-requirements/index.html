Border Secretary in Ticino: salary, requirements and leave

The New Frontier Agreement, signed on 23 December 2020, is an important agreement for frontier workers wishing to work in Switzerland. As of January 1, 2024, this agreement will come into force and can provide greater benefits and opportunities for frontier workers.

The Italian-Swiss Double Taxation Convention, signed on 9 March 1976, is an important treaty to avoid double taxation of income. This agreement ensures that frontier workers are not subject to double taxation of income in Italy and Switzerland.

The Ticino border is one of the most important frontiers for frontier workers. According to 2022 data, the average salary for a border secretary in Ticino is about CHF 60,000 per year. However, this salary may vary depending on the canton and city where you work.

For example, in Lugano, the average salary for a border secretary is about CHF 65,000 per year, while in Bellinzona it is about CHF 55,000 per year.

Salary and requirements to work as a secretary in Ticino as a border worker From 1 January 2024, border workers will be able to work as secretaries in Ticino with a salary of CHF 4,500 per month. In order to access this job, you must meet certain requirements. First of all, you must be a border worker, that is, have the nationality of a country other than Switzerland and reside in another region of Switzerland. In addition, it is necessary to have a good knowledge of the Italian language, which is the official language of the Canton of Ticino.

Language requirements Knowledge of the Italian language is a fundamental requirement to work as a secretary in Ticino. Border workers must have a good knowledge of the written and spoken language, at least at the A2 level of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). This means that they must be able to understand and produce written and oral texts in Italian, as well as to express themselves with precision and clarity.

Permits and authorizations To work as a secretary in Ticino, border workers must obtain a valid work permit. The work permit is issued by the Immigration Office of the Canton of Ticino, after submitting the application and meeting the necessary requirements.
