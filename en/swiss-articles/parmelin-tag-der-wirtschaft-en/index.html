On September 11, 2026, in Lucerne, the Head of the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research, Federal Councillor Guy Parmelin, delivered his speech at the Tag der Wirtschaft, the traditional gathering of the Swiss business community. The event also marked Christoph Mäder's farewell from the presidency of economiesuisse, a significant institutional transition for the national economic fabric. The speech opened by outlining the global economic framework characterized by geopolitical tensions, trade conflicts, and uncertainties in export markets, compounded by demographic transition and rapid technological evolution.

Operational details

The opening strategy and trade treaties

The prosperity of the nation depends crucially on access to international markets, a vital element for a strongly export-oriented economy. For this reason, the Federal Council is steadily pursuing the development of its network of free trade agreements. In recent years, significant progress has been made on various international negotiating fronts. The trade agreement with India officially entered into force in 2025, while new agreements were signed with Thailand, Malaysia, Kosovo, and the Mercosur member states. In July, trade negotiations with Vietnam and the United Kingdom were also successfully completed, while three weeks before the Lucerne event, negotiations for the optimization of the bilateral agreement with China concluded.
