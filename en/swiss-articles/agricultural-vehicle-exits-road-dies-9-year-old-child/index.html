The Public Prosecutor's Office has opened an investigation to determine the exact circumstances of the accident. According to the elements collected so far, the child was on board an agricultural vehicle taken off the road. It was reported that the vehicle was an agricultural tractor, a John Deere 6215R, which was used for agricultural work in the area.

Valais, like other Swiss cantons, has a long tradition of agriculture and pastoralism. Agricultural tractors such as the John Deere 6215R are common in the region and are used for jobs such as harvesting and sowing.

The accident occurred on the main road that connects Chandonne to Saint-Luc. The road is one of the main routes of communication between the Valais and the rest of Switzerland.

The Public Prosecutor's Office has opened an investigation to determine the exact causes of the accident. The investigation is still ongoing and no further information has been released.

Operational details

The accident occurred the day before, on Monday, around 4:30 pm, on the main road that connects the municipality of Sierre, in the canton of Valais, to an agricultural property located in the territory of Ravoire. The nine-year-old boy, who lives in the municipality of Sierre, was on board an agricultural vehicle with his father and a 35-year-old adult when the vehicle lost its drive and went off the road.

The vehicle, an agricultural tractor with a track, flipped over and suffered an impact with a tree, causing serious injuries to the child. Valais police said in a statement that the child was a resident of the canton and was on board the farm vehicle at the time of the accident.

The child was transported by helicopter to the University Hospital Centre of Lausanne (CHUV), where he died the next day at around 9:30 am. The child's death triggered a widespread reaction of shock and solidarity in the local and national community.
