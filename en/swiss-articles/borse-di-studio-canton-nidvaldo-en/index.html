Scholarship requirements and amounts for border students from the Canton of Nidwalden

Context

In a nutshell

The Canton of Nidwalden offers scholarships to cross-border students who wish to continue their studies in Switzerland. These scholarships are intended for students residing in another country who intend to continue their high school or university education in Switzerland.

Key facts

• What: scholarships for border students • When: not specified • Where: Canton of Nidwalden • Who: cross-border students • Amount: not specified

Requirements

To be eligible for Nidwalden Canton Scholarships, cross-border students must meet the following requirements:

  • Reside in another country and not be a Swiss citizen
  • Be at least 15 years old and not yet 26 years old
  • Have good academic conduct and demonstrate a high level of motivation for their studies
  • Have a well-defined study plan and have not already benefited from a scholarship for the same period of study

Amounts and duration

The amounts and duration of scholarships may vary depending on the specific needs of each student and the chosen study plan. In general, scholarships can cover up to 100% of tuition and study costs, as well as any transportation and food costs. The duration of the scholarships can vary from a few months to two years, depending on the specific needs of each student.

The requirements to apply for a scholarship for cross-border students are as follows:

  • Have their habitual residence in the Canton of Nidwalden
  • Be a student at a Swiss school or university
  • Have not already received a scholarship for the same academic year
  • Not having reached the age of majority

Scholarship amounts for cross-border students from the Canton of Nidwalden vary depending on the age and study status of the students. Here are some concrete examples:

  • For students of lower secondary education (ISI), the amount of the scholarship is CHF 1'200 per school year.
  • For students of upper secondary education (ISS), the amount of the scholarship is CHF 1,500 per school year.
  • For university students, the amount of the scholarship is CHF 2'000 per academic year.
