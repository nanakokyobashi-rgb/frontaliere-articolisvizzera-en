From the fire to the restoration

At 05:40 this morning, rail traffic between Yverdon-les-Bains, in Canton Vaud, and Lausanne returned to normal. SBB communicated this to Keystone-SDA, following a phase of severe disruptions that began on Friday.

An act of arson had broken out in Eclépens (VD), affecting the connection. The fire had been discovered on Friday around 03:00 inside a cable duct near the tracks. The damage had consequently interrupted traffic between Cossonay-Penthalaz and Chavornay.

The operational consequences had affected several long-distance trains, which were canceled during the disruption. Passengers had been forced to choose alternative routes. SBB had also mobilized around one hundred employees and twenty replacement buses.

The restoration announced at 05:40 concerns the route between Yverdon-les-Bains and Lausanne and marks the return to normal of the rail traffic indicated by SBB. The described sequence ranges from infrastructure damage to cancellations, up to replacement solutions for travelers.

On the judicial level, SBB condemned the criminal act 'with the utmost firmness' and announced its intention to file a complaint against unknown persons. The ongoing investigation aims to clarify the circumstances and identify the author or authors.
