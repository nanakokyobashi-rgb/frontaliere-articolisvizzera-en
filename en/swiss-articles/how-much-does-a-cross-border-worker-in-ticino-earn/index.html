The New Frontier Agreement provides for frontier workers to be treated more equitably and for their salaries to be higher. However, Switzerland is not a member of the EU/EEA and therefore the rules for frontier workers are different from those for Swiss citizens.

According to the Federal Border Act of 14 December 2019, the salaries of border workers in Switzerland are subject to a number of rules and regulations. For example, Article 5 of the law states that the salaries of frontier workers may not be lower than those of equivalent Swiss workers.

At the cantonal level, Ticino approved the border law of 21 May 2020, which provides for additional rules for the remuneration of border workers. According to this law, the salaries of border workers in Ticino must be at least 90% of those of equivalent Swiss workers.

According to the Swiss Federal Tax Act (LIF) of 1992, frontier workers are required to pay income tax in Switzerland, which may vary from canton to canton. According to Italian law no. 147/1990, frontier workers can deduct from their Italian income the taxes paid in Switzerland.
