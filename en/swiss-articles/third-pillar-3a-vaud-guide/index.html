In 2026, the Swiss 3a third pillar must be considered within three tax levels: direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. It is the national framework of the guide and the starting point for the focus on the canton of Vaud. The tax advantage cannot be assessed as if there were a single rate valid throughout Switzerland.

The map of responsibilities

Each canton has its own law and its own multiplier. For municipalities, the multiplier is applied to the cantonal tax. In Vaud, therefore, the calculation to be compared with the rest of Switzerland must separate the federal share from the cantonal and municipal components. This distinction avoids attributing a federal decision to a cantonal authority, or vice versa.

AFC/ESTV is responsible for direct federal tax and VAT. Cantonal administrations handle cantonal and municipal taxation. UFAS/BSV is responsible for social security, including AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG, but does not set taxes. UST/BFS produces statistics and does not set rates. For anyone comparing a guida sul terzo pilastro 3a, knowing who does what is part of the verification, not an administrative detail.

The 2026 guide also considers banking and insurance providers and the contribution strategy. The comparison, however, must remain anchored to the available information: the maximum for the 3a third pillar is a figure indexed every year. It should therefore not be turned into a fixed amount unless it has been verified for the correct year. The same caution applies to other indexed figures, such as the LPP coordination threshold or deduction, the AD/AC maximum, the average LAMal/KVG premiums and the median salary.

The 3a must also be distinguished from salary deductions. AVS/AI/IPG amount to 5,3% borne by the employee and 10,6% in total with the employer; AD/AC is 1,1% up to the annual maximum. LAINF ranges from 0,7% to 1,5% depending on the sector. LPP, on the coordinated salary, provides for rates of 7%, 10%, 15% and 18% in the different age brackets indicated by the source. These are social security or insurance items on salary: they do not replace a separate assessment of the 3a.

Operational details

Comparing Vaud with other Cantons helps explain why the same contribution is interpreted differently in each tax return. While the federal structure is common, the law and cantonal multipliers vary from one Canton to another; the Municipality applies its own multiplier to the cantonal tax. The relevant question is which fiscal levels apply to one's specific situation.

Practical procedure for 2026

Five checks before making the contribution

1. Identify the tax framework. Indicate the Canton and municipality concerned, then distinguish between direct federal, cantonal and municipal taxes. In the case of Vaud, the verification must include the cantonal law and the municipal multiplier applied to the cantonal tax.

2. Check the maximum amount. The third-pillar 3a ceiling is indexed every year. Before scheduling the contribution, look up the 2026 value in the official source. The same annual check is needed for the LPP coordination threshold or deduction, the AD/AC maximum amount, the LAMal/KVG average premiums and the median salary. If the figure is not up to date, the comparison loses precision.

3. Distinguish the responsible bodies. For direct federal tax and VAT, the point of reference is AFC/ESTV; for cantonal and municipal taxes, the cantonal administrations are responsible; for AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG, the indicated authority is UFAS/BSV, which does not set tax rates. UST/BFS is used for statistics, not to determine the tax.

4. Compare providers. Compare a banking offer and an insurance offer only on the basis of the conditions actually available and the chosen contribution strategy. The source does not authorize invented names, costs or returns. Recording the year's maximum amount and the chosen contribution level helps keep the comparison readable.

5. Check the tax return. Use the tax return to check how the contribution is treated and distinguish the federal component from the cantonal and municipal components. To explore this part further, consult guida sul terzo pilastro 3a.
