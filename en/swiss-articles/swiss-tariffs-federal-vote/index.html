On 24 July 2026, the United States adopted additional tariffs on Switzerland of up to 12.5%; on 29 November 2026, the vote will be held on war material.

The decisions indicated by SECO

On July 24, 2026, the United States adopted some measures in the form of new additional duties. The decision stems from the investigation into the trade in goods produced using forced labor, conducted under section 301 of the 1974 U.S. Trade Act, also referred to as the Trade Act of 1974.

A variable additional duty of up to 12.5% applies to imports from Switzerland. NPF (Most Favoured-Nation) duties that are already in force will still be taken into account. The wording indicated by SECO therefore contains both the new element and the reference to the duties already in force.

The Swiss political calendar lists November 29, 2026 as the date of the consultation on the amendment of the Federal Act on War Material. On that day, the Swiss people will give their opinion. The news item defines the subject of the vote, but does not add details on the content of the change.

Another date is 16 September 2026. On that day, President Guy Parmelin informed the Federal Council that State Secretary Helene Budliger Artieda had decided to resign with effect from the end of March 2027. A special commission will be set up to succeed her. The text does not indicate the name of a successor.

Other institutional information

The page also recalls that on 28 February 2022, the Federal Council decided to support the European Union's sanctions against Russia, thus strengthening their effectiveness. This decision is reported separately from the 2026 trade measures.

SECO is described as an organisation with more than 900 employees at three locations in Switzerland and 50 seconded staff in 32 countries. Operational details

What you can read and what you can't

The trade measure should be read as a range, not as a single percentage. The wording refers to a variable additional duty and indicates 12.5% as the maximum; it also refers to the MFN duties already in force. For those looking at imports from Switzerland, the number alone does not describe every possible application. The source does not list goods, specific rates or methods of calculation. Consequently, it does not allow the cap to be converted into a one-size-fits-all amount.

The same caution is needed for the vote on 29 November 2026. The concrete data is the consultation of the Swiss people on the amendment of the Federal Act on War Material. The source does not report the content of the amended articles or other effects. Attributing detailed consequences to the vote would go beyond the available information.

The transition to the State Secretary also requires a distinction between announcement and succession. 16 September 2026, the decision to resign with effect from the end of March 2027 and the future establishment of a special commission are indicated. The name of the successor or a timetable for the selection are not indicated.
