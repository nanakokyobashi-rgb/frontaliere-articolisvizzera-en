What are cantonal tax rates?

Cantonal tax rates are tax rates applied to the income of individuals and corporations within a certain amount of income. Income above this amount is subject to a fixed tax rate. For example, in the canton of Vaud, individuals with incomes up to CHF 3,600 are subject to a tax rate of 1.8%, while those with incomes above CHF3,600 are subject to a tax rate of 10.9%.

Concrete examples

Suppose you have an income of CHF 4,000 in the canton of Vaud. According to the cantonal tax rate table, you will be subject to a tax rate of 10.9% on income above CHF 3,600, or CHF 400. The amount of tax you have to pay will therefore be CHF 43.60 (CHF 400 x 10.9%).
