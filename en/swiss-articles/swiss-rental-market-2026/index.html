Switzerland is a country with a very competitive rental market, especially in larger cities such as Zurich, Geneva and Bern. According to 2022 data, the average price of an apartment for rent in Zurich is about 2,500 Swiss francs per month, while in Geneva it is about 3,500 Swiss francs per month.

However, it is important to note that rental prices can vary greatly depending on the area and the type of property. For example, an apartment for rent in Bern can cost only 1,800 Swiss francs per month, while an apartment for rent in Lausanne can cost up to 4,500 Swiss francs per month.

Key points

If you are a landlord or tenant in Switzerland, it is important to know the rights and responsibilities that come with the lease. The rental market in Switzerland is characterized by variable prices depending on the canton and city. For example, in Zurich, the average monthly price for a 3-bedroom apartment is about CHF 3,500, while in Bern it is about CHF2,800. 📊

The highest prices are in the Zurich region and Geneva, where the average monthly price for a 3-bedroom apartment can reach CHF 4,500. Unlike other countries, there is no national law regulating rental prices in Switzerland. However, many cantons have introduced laws and regulations to limit over-renting. For example, the canton of Zurich has introduced a law that prohibits rentals above 10% of the purchase price of the property. 📊

The lease agreement in Switzerland must be concluded in writing and must contain the following information:

  • The lease conditions, including the price and duration of the contract
  • The responsibilities of the landlord and the tenant
  • The methods of payment and collection of expenses
  • Any special clauses, such as the presence of pets or children

The landlord is obliged to provide the tenant with a lease agreement that complies with applicable laws and regulations. In addition, the landlord must keep the property in good condition
