Three months' rent is the maximum limit for the security deposit set by the federal tenancy framework. The reference is CO Art. 253 et seq., and the rule applies in every canton, including the canton of Aargau. The deposit must also be placed in a blocked account in the tenant's name.

One federal limit, one cantonal procedure

The distinction between the two levels avoids a common mistake. The deposit limit derives from federal law and does not change from one canton to another. For the landlord's termination, however, validity depends on the use of the official cantonal form. In the canton of Aargau, the tenant must therefore check the form prescribed by the canton when receiving a termination notice; the formal requirement cannot be replaced by an informal communication.

The time limit for responding is 30 days. The challenge must be submitted to the conciliation authority. The deadline turns reading the letter into a calendar-based action: it must be taken into account without waiting until the last moment.

The blocked account is not a secondary formality: the account being in the tenant's name goes hand in hand with the quantitative limit. The check must therefore cover both conditions, not just the number of months' rent stated in the contract.

The practical value of the rule is national. The same framework applies to a contract in Aargau as in any other canton; what remains cantonal is the official form used for the termination.
