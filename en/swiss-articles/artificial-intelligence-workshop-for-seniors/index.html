Free workshop on AI for the elderly in Lugano (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 September 2026

The OCST Union organises a free workshop on artificial intelligence for the elderly on 11 September 2024 in Lugano.

Context

In brief - Free AI workshop for the elderly - AI opportunities and risks explained in a practical way - Workshop organised by the OCST Trade Union in Lugano ## Key facts - What: Free AI workshop - When: 11 September 2024 - Where: Cantonal Secretariat of the OCST Trade Union, Via Serafino Balestra 19, Lugano - Who: Ticyweb, Linkfloyd, City of Lugano and GenerationMore - Amount: Free In Lugano, on Friday 11 September, a free workshop dedicated to the elderly and artificial intelligence will be held. The initiative, organised by the OCST Trade Union in collaboration with Ticyweb, Linkfloyd, the City of Lugano and GenerazionePiù, is part of the Swiss {ai} Weeks programme. The goal is to bring AI in front of an audience that feels excluded, offering a two-hour hands-on workshop where participants can get their hands on the tools, understand what they can do and, above all, learn to recognize scams. Admission is free, but registration is mandatory and subject to approval. At the end of the meeting there will be a refreshment offered to those present. # ## AI Opportunities and Risks The lab moves on two parallel tracks. On the one hand, opportunities will be explored: how to use AI to seek and understand information in daily life and how technology can support active ageing. For example, AI can help find specific medical information, - Opportunities: AI can help you find specific, up-to-date medical information, such as symptoms of a disease or best practices for managing a chronic condition. - Risk: Misinformation can lead to poor decisions and worsening health. It is important to check the sources and consult a medical professional. Scenario 2: Using AI to create fake content - Opportunity : AI can be used to create original and creative content, such as articles, videos, and music. -Risk: Creating fake content can lead to fraud, scams, and misinformation. It is important to recognize machine-generated content and verify its authenticity. In conclusion, the workshop on AI for the elderly in Lugano offers a unique opportunity to learn how to use AI safely and responsibly. With the help of local professionals and the experience of Swiss cities such as Bern and Geneva, participants will be able to acquire the necessary skills to face the challenges and opportunities of AI in everyday life.

Operational details

## Implications for the elderly The Lugano workshop is a crucial initiative for those who feel excluded from the conversation on artificial intelligence. AI is producing cloned rumors, fake videos, and scam messages that can fool those who don't have the tools to recognize this content. This workshop offers a concrete opportunity for those most exposed to the dark side of AI to learn how to defend themselves. The initiative is part of a larger project that includes over 180 appointments in 37 cities in Switzerland, with a network of 220 partners. In Ticino alone, the cantonal programme has more than thirty events, with a peak of participation recorded in Locarno, where more than 500 people participated in a workshop dedicated to digital security. # ## Insights The goal is to bring participants closer to artificial intelligence in a simple, concrete and accessible way. There will be no speakers speaking from the stage to a silent audience, but tools will be presented to be tried first-hand, concrete experiments proposed and space given to the questions of the participants. This hands-on approach is critical to ensuring that the message about AI reaches those who are most vulnerable to it.
