Salary is not just the figure

Contributions come into play in pay: AVS/AHV, AI and IPG are borne by the employee at 5,3%, while the total with the employer is 10,6%; AD/AC is 1,1% up to the annual maximum. LAINF/LAA varies from 0,7% to 1,5% depending on the sector. For LPP/BVG on the coordinated salary, the source indicates rates of 7% between 25 and 34 years, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54 and 18% from age 55 up to the reference age.

These data explain why the right question is not only how much an apprentice earns, but what amount is provided for and what items accompany it. To begin the search, you can consult the annunci di lavoro, keeping the offer, the contract and the school pathway separate.

From pay to monthly budget

The remuneration figure indicated is not the whole calculation. Social contributions are linked to salary, whereas LAMal/KVG follows a different logic: for residents, it is compulsory private health insurance with per-capita premiums. It is neither a tax nor a salary contribution. Premiums vary by canton and region, and premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy.

For an adult, the deductibles indicated are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500. Even without entering an invented amount for remuneration, these items make it possible to carry out an orderly assessment of the cost of living: first consider the amount specified in the contract, then separate out social deductions and finally add the health insurance premium. The result is not a national rate, but a personal budget linked to the specific situation.

Key points

Four operational steps

1. Define the dossier. Write separately the objective of the apprenticeship position, the contract to be examined, the remuneration to be compared and any interest in the vocational baccalaureate. This division avoids treating training as a simple salary question.

2. Search for the opportunity and keep the offer. When you find a position, note the proposed amount, working hours, vacation and every item related to pay. If an element is unclear, ask for an answer referring to the specific case instead of using a national average.

3. Verify which level is responsible. For requirements, deadlines and amounts linked to cantonal services, consult the official source of the Canton. The same criterion applies to any contributions to continuing education and to programs that depend on the canton, municipality or competent institution. For the vocational baccalaureate, the source indicates the topic but does not provide conditions or deadlines: these must be requested from the competent authority.

4. Reconstruct the budget. Start from the expected remuneration and separate social deductions from the LAMal/KVG premium. If the person is resident and arrives in Switzerland, consider the three-month deadline for taking out health insurance. Do not enter precise amounts when the year is not certain: the source notes that the average LAMal premiums, the threshold and coordination deduction under LPP, and the AD/AC maximum are indexed annually.

Finding the right office

The choice of contact person also matters. AFC/ESTV concerns direct federal tax and VAT; cantonal administrations manage cantonal and municipal taxation. UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS, AI and LPP, but does not set tax rates. UST/BFS produces statistics and does not establish rates. Separating these roles reduces the risk of using an official page for a question that belongs to another authority.

What social security contributions are deducted from the apprentice's salary?
The following are charged on salary: AVS/AI/IPG (5,3% paid by the employee, 10,6% total), AD/AC (1,1% up to the annual maximum), LAINF/LAA (0,7%-1,5% depending on the sector) and LPP/BVG on the coordinated salary, with age-based rates: 7% (25-34 years), 10% (35-44), 15% (45-54), 18% (55+ until the reference age).
How many weeks of vacation is the apprentice entitled to?
The stated minimum is four weeks, increasing to five for anyone under 20. This figure should be verified in the apprenticeship contract and compared with the applicable rules.
When must health insurance (LAMal/KVG) be taken out?
Anyone arriving in Switzerland to begin a training program must take out health insurance within three months of arrival. The deadline concerns LAMal, not the signing of the apprenticeship contract. Premiums are per capita, vary by canton and region, and the deductibles for adults are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000, 2500.
