Context

For Article 9 of the Agreement, “old frontier workers” are people who met the tax requirements and worked in Graubünden, Ticino or Valais on 17 July 2023 or during the period from 31 December 2018 through 17 July 2023. Article 9 keeps covered employment income taxable exclusively at source in Switzerland; other workers follow the ordinary regime, with ordinary taxation in Italy, a credit for Swiss tax and Swiss withholding at 80% of the ordinary rates. The €10,000 figure in Article 4 of Italian Law No. 83/2023 is only the income limit referred to in Article 1(175) of Law No. 147/2013; it is not a general allowance for new frontier workers. Rates and Contributions Swiss rates and contributions include the AHV/IV/EO at 5.3% for employees, the ALV/AC at 1.1% (capped at CHF 148,200), the UVG from 0.7% to 1.5%, and the BVG from 7% to 18% depending on the age group (from 25 years).
