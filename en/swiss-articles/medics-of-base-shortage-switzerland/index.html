Operational details

The shortage of primary care physicians is felt especially in primary care with family doctors and paediatricians. In Switzerland, for example, there are only 4.3 doctors per 1,000 inhabitants, while the Expert Commission for the Training of Physicians (KFS) recommends having at least 5.5 doctors per 1,000 inhabitants. Rural regions are more affected than cities, because many young doctors prefer to live and work in urban areas. The biggest problem, however, is the too few master's degree positions in human medicine. According to the statistics of the Expert Commission for the Training of Physicians (KFS), in 2020 there were only 290 places available for general practitioners, while at least 450 were needed to cover the country's needs. In addition, the admission procedure is considered too difficult and time-consuming. The doctors' association calls for a further development of the admission procedure, which also assesses the candidates' social, communicative and emotional skills. Family medicine is becoming more feminine, as Dr. Habib says. Many women doctors have children, relationships, commitments in associations or other projects. According to statistics from the Swiss Medical Federation (FMH), 63% of family doctors were women in 2020. However, the shortage of primary care physicians is a problem that affects everyone, regardless of sex. A quarter of doctors are already over 60, and one in three family medicine clinics no longer accept new patients. - 14,000 Swiss doctors over 60 years of age (Swiss Medical Federation, 2020) - 4,000 new doctors who have graduated in the last 10 years (Swiss Medical Federation, 2020) Checklist: - Check 1: Verification of the creation of new master's posts in human medicine - Check 2: Verification of the enhancement of candidates' social and communication skills - Check 3: Verification of the creation of continuous training programmes for doctors in service - Check 4: Verification of the improvement of the admission procedure for general practitioners - Check 5: Verification of the creation of new family medicine clinics - Check 6: Verification of the improvement of the support network for doctors in service
