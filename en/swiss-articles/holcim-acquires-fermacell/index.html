Holcim acquires Fermacell: transaction of 784 million (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 21 August 2026

The Swiss group Holcim takes over the German Fermacell for 784 million francs. The operation, completed within the first half of 2027, is aimed at the construction market.

Key facts

  • What: Holcim's acquisition of Fermacell
  • When: Transaction expected within the first half of 2027
  • Where: Fermacell headquarters in Düsseldorf, Germany
  • Who: Swiss group Holcim and James Hardie
  • Amount: €840 million (CHF 784 million)

The Swiss industrial group Holcim has formalised a strategic agreement for the acquisition of Fermacell, a German company specialising in the production of walls and floors. The transaction provides for an economic outlay of 840 million euros, corresponding to approximately 784 million Swiss francs. The selling counterparty is represented by James Hardie, an American-Irish entity that is implementing a disengagement plan from its European activities concentrated in the fiber-reinforced cement sector. ### Operational details of the acquired company The company being acquired, Fermacell, maintains its operational headquarters in Düsseldorf and employs a workforce of approximately 1,000 employees. The company's industrial structure is solid, with six strategically located production plants. Fermacell has a consolidated commercial presence, operating actively in 13 different European countries.
