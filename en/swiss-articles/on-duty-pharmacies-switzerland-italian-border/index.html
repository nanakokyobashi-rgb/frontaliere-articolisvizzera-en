Operational details

What is verified and what is not

The verified duty data published by our dataset concerns five Ticino regions: Mendrisiotto, Luganese, Bellinzonese, Biasca e Valli and Locarnese. Locarnese uses a regional association source; for other cantons we provide navigable official links where available, but publish no unified roster, active coverage or inferred hours.

Open the cantonal or provincial source before travelling, follow its published instructions and call the pharmacy. Where a source exposes no public feed or roster, the guide says so rather than filling the gap with assumptions.

Directory, opening hours and duty: three different data points

  • directory: A directory record means that the official catalogue contains the entry; it does not mean that the pharmacy is open now.
  • opening hours: Opening hours are shown only when the page or source publishes them; they are not inferred from the directory or its timestamp.
  • verified duty: Verified duty means a regional interval published by OFCT or the relevant association source; it is not the same as continuous opening. Check by phone before travelling.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does this guide show a nationwide on-duty pharmacy calendar?
No. It explains where to check the current source by canton. The published dataset has verified data for five Ticino regions: Mendrisiotto, Luganese, Bellinzonese, Biasca e Valli and Locarnese; Locarnese uses a regional association source.
Does a cantonal link mean that a pharmacy is open?
No. A link is a route for verification; it does not promise hours, duty or active coverage.
How do I check Como, Varese and Verbano-Cusio-Ossola?
Open ATS Insubria or the named local document, check its update and confirm directly with the pharmacy. This guide infers no Italian duty.
