Welders in Ticino: salary, requirements and recognition

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 20 July 2026

The New Frontier Agreement provides new rules for border welders in Ticino. In this article, we learn about the requirements, average salary, and possible exemptions.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The New Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and will be in force from 1 January 2024.
  • Border welders in Ticino must meet language and training requirements.
  • The G permit is required to work as a welder in Ticino.

The requirements to work as a welder in Ticino

Border welders wishing to work in the canton of Ticino must meet the language and training requirements established by the New Border Agreement. In particular, you need to have a good knowledge of German, Italian or Romance, depending on the area in which you want to work. In addition, specific training in welding is required, which can be obtained from a recognized training institution.

Permission G

To work as a welder in Ticino, you must obtain the G permit, which is a document that recognizes the qualification of welder. The G permit is issued by the Immigration Office of the canton of Ticino after submitting the required documentation and passing a qualification exam.

The remuneration of welders in Ticino

The remuneration of welders in Ticino is competitive and may vary depending on the area in which they work and the type of project. In general, welders can earn between 4,500 and 6,000 Swiss francs per month, in addition to additional benefits such as health care and vacation days.

Requirements and recognition

Border workers wishing to work as welders in Ticino must hold a certificate of qualification issued by a recognised institution. In addition, they must have completed at least one year of work experience as a welder in another country. It is important to note that the requirements may vary according to current legislation.

Salary and working conditions

The amount of the salary has not yet been specified, but it is assumed that it will comply with the salary regulations of the Canton of Ticino. Frontier workers will also have to comply with the working conditions established by Swiss regulations, such as the right to rest and holidays.

Action

To obtain the G permit, border welders in Ticino must submit the required documentation and take the language and training exam. The required documentation includes a curriculum vitae, a certificate of professional qualification, a certificate of German or Italian language and a certificate of social insurance.

Switzerland has a very advanced vocational training system and welders can obtain a nationally recognised professional qualification. For example, the professional qualification certificate for border welders is issued by the Swiss Confederation and requires the attendance of a training course of at least 1,500 hours.

Border welders in Ticino can also request recognition of the qualification to obtain the G permit. However, the requirements for recognition are not yet specified.
