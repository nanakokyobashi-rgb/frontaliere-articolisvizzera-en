  • What: Residence permit category B (renewable)

In the Canton of Basel-City, the category B residence permit represents the renewable residence permit for foreigners wishing to settle in Switzerland. Managed by the cantonal migration office, this permit is part of the SEM system that regulates the entry and stay of foreign nationals throughout Switzerland. Unlike other residence permits, the B permit is characterised by its renewability, allowing those who hold it to extend their residence permit at the end of its validity.

Permit B in the Swiss SEM system

In Switzerland, the stay of foreigners is regulated by the SEM permit system, which provides for four main categories. The L permit is intended for those who intend to stay for a short period, up to one year. Operational details

Permit B vs permit C: practical differences

Those who plan to move to Switzerland often ask which type of permit is more suitable for their situation. The main differences between the permit of residence (category B) and the permit of domicile (category C) are significant both on the administrative and practical planes.

The permit B is renewable for limited periods and must be requested for renewal. The permit C, on the other hand, is intended for those who have accumulated a minimum period of residence in Switzerland: generally not less than 10 years for citizens of non-EU/EEA countries, and not less than 5 years for citizens of the European Union and the European Free Trade Association. The permit C confers a more stable position and does not require periodic renewals in the same way.

Even from the point of view of geographical mobility, the two categories differ. The holder of a permit B must generally notify changes of domicile within the canton, and must renew the permit if they decide to move to another canton. The holder of a permit C has greater freedom of movement within Switzerland.

From the point of view of access to social services and healthcare, both permit B and C holders have the right to coverage of LAMal (mandatory health insurance), which is a private insurance with premiums per capita. In the Canton of Basel City, as in all cantons, LAMal is mandatory for all residents within 3 months of arrival. Cantonally subsidized premiums for reducing premiums vary by canton and individual situation.

Fiscal and pension implications

From a tax perspective, both permit B and C holders are subject to the Swiss three-tier tax system: federal direct tax (managed by AFC/ESTV), cantonal tax, and municipal tax. Each canton has its own rates and regulations, and municipalities apply a multiplier to cantonal tax. In the Canton of Basel City, specific rates must be checked with the competent cantonal tax administration.

Regarding social security, permit B holders who work in Switzerland are enrolled in the AVS/AI (old-age and disability insurance) system. Contributions are 5.3% for the employee and 10.6% in total with the employer. In addition, employees are subject to contributions for unemployment insurance (AD/AC), amounting to 1.1% up to an annual maximum.

Regarding the company pension fund (LPP/BVG), permit B holders who earn a coordinated salary have compulsory contributions that vary by age range: 7% for the 25-34 age range, 10% for 35-44 years, 15% for 45-54 years, and 18% for 55 years up to the reference age. These contributions are deducted directly from the salary.

Family allocations and social rights

An important aspect concerns access to family allocations. Permit B holders who work in Switzerland and have children can access family allocations, the amount of which varies by canton. In addition, the permit B holder has the right to 2 weeks of paid leave for paternity insurance (federal right).

As for the insurance against work-related accidents (LAINF/LAA), this is mandatory for employees with permit B and amounts to around 0.7-1.5% of the salary depending on the sector. Insurance against occupational diseases is part of the same coverage.

Key points

How to Apply for a B Permit in Basel City: Procedures and Deadlines

Those wishing to relocate to the Canton of Basel City must follow a clear administrative procedure. The first step is to complete the application form provided by the cantonal migration office of Basel City. This form must be accompanied by the required documentation (identity document, residence certificate, police certificate, and any income documentation).

Once the application is submitted, the cantonal migration office examines the request and verifies compliance with the requirements. The processing time typically ranges from a few days to several weeks, depending on the complexity of the application and the office's workload. It is recommended to maintain regular contact with the office to check the status of the application.

After approval of the application, the B permit is issued with a validity and expiration date. The permit holder receives an official copy, which must be carefully preserved, as it is the document that attests the right to stay in Switzerland.

Permit Renewal: Timelines and Documents

The renewal of the residence permit (category B) must be requested before the expiration date. Normally, the Canton of Basel City sends a reminder to the permit holder a few months before the expiration date, reminding them to renew. However, it is the permit holder's responsibility to check the expiration date on the permit.

To renew, you must:

  • Submit a renewal request within the times set by the canton
  • Provide updated documentation (residence certificate, police certificate, and any income documentation)
  • Pay any applicable administrative renewal fee
  • Verify that the original conditions persist (residence in the canton, compliance with the law)

If the conditions persist and there are no issues with the application, the permit is renewed, and a new copy is issued valid for the next period.

Contacts and Information at the Cantonal Migration Office

The cantonal migration office of Basel City is the primary point of contact for any questions related to the B permit. It is recommended to contact the office to verify specific requirements, obtain the updated list of required documents, know the submission deadlines, receive assistance in completing the application, and clarify any doubts about the renewal procedure.

Cantonal migration offices operate according to fixed opening hours and usually offer the possibility of phone, email, or in-person contact. It is recommended to check the contact details and hours before visiting the office.

Support Tools: Fiscal and Social Security Information

Once the B permit is obtained, the permit holder must fulfill obligations such as registration with the local municipality and enrollment in the tax system. At the federal level, the Federal Administration of Customs and the Federal Tax Administration (ESTV) manage the federal direct tax and VAT. At the cantonal level (at the Basel City Tax Administration), practical compliance with cantonal and municipal taxes is managed.

Regarding social security, it is essential to verify enrollment in the AVS system at the Social Insurance Office (UFAS/BSV) and enrollment in the occupational pension fund (LPP) at the employer or relevant pension fund.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between permit B and permit C?
The B permit is renewable and intended for a residence of intermediate duration; the C permit is the definitive domicile that requires at least 10 years of residence in Switzerland (5 years for EU/EFTA citizens). The B permit involves periodic renewals, while the C permit does not require renewals in the same way.
What documents are needed to apply for the B permit in Basel City?
Commonly required documents include the application form (cantonal), valid identity document (passport or ID card), certificate of registration with the municipality, police certificate (criminal record extract), and documentation of income or employment contract. For the exact list, please contact the cantonal migration office in Basel-City.
How long does it take to get the B permit?
Processing time generally ranges from a few days to a few weeks, depending on the complexity of the demand and the workload of the office. It is advisable to maintain regular contact with the office to check the status of the practice.
How do I renew my B permit in the Canton of Basel-City?
Renewal must be requested before the permit expires. You must submit a renewal application, provide updated documentation (certificate of residence, police certificate, any income documentation), pay any administrative fees, and verify that the original conditions are maintained. The office sends a notice a few months before the deadline.
Do B permit holders need to enroll in health insurance (LAMal)?
Yes, LAMal is mandatory for all residents in Switzerland, including B permit holders. Registration must take place within 3 months of arrival. In the Canton of Basel-City, the cantonal subsidies for the reduction of premiums vary according to the individual situation.
