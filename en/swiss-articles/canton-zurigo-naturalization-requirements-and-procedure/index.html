The naturalization procedure in the Canton of Zurich also requires a number of requirements, including:

  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Have sufficient knowledge of the German language (B1)
  • Have sufficient knowledge of Swiss culture
  • Have enough income to support themselves financially
  • Have no criminal convictions
