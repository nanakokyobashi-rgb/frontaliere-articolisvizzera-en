Operational details

Practical analysis

The Swiss-German Consumer Protection Foundation conducted a study to assess the protection of Swiss banks against online scams. The study found that only three out of twenty banks are reachable 24 hours a day in case of fraud related to e-banking. In addition, most banks do not offer a 24-hour telephone service in case of emergencies related to the Swiss Twint payment system.

The situation is particularly critical in some Swiss regions. In the Canton of Zurich, for example, only 10% of banks are active 24 hours a day to manage fraud related to e-banking. In the Canton of Geneva, 20% of banks offer a 24-hour support service, but only for Twint operations.

The study also found that Swiss banks are not transparent enough about their policies to protect against online scams. The Consumer Foundation has called for banks to be clearer and more transparent in their communications with customers, particularly regarding the security measures they have in place to protect customers from cyberattacks.

Key points

Action

If you are a victim of an online scam, it is important to contact your bank as soon as possible to report the fraud. In addition, it is advisable to use a secure payment system and regularly check your account to avoid falling victim to a scam.

More Consumer Foundation: more support for banks against online scams The Swiss Consumer Foundation has launched a new initiative to help citizens protect themselves from online scams. In collaboration with leading Swiss banks, the Foundation has created a web portal dedicated to online scams and methods to prevent them.

"Our mission is to help citizens protect themselves from online scams and make a difference in terms of financial security," says the director of the Swiss Consumer Foundation.

The Foundation has identified three key areas to combat online scams: prevention, reporting and support. Citizens can report online scams using an online form available on the Foundation's portal. In addition, Swiss banks offer a secure payment system and regularly check their customers' accounts to avoid falling victim to a scam.
