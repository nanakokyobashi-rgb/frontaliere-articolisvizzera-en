Key facts

  • What: Downtown Melide redevelopment
  • When: 6 September
  • Where: Melide (TI)
  • Who: Municipality of Melide, Studio JPA Antorini

The long redevelopment works of the centre of the municipality of Melide on Lake Como were formally concluded on 6 September with a ceremony. The idea was to change the face of the town centre, reviewing the flooring with precious and local raw materials, as well as the urban furniture. For the work, Iragna granite and Cuasso al Monte pink porphyry were used. The official inauguration, in the presence of the municipal and cantonal authorities, was held on 6 September.

📊 Data and numbers:

  • The works involved an area of about 5,000 square meters.
  • The project required an investment of about CHF 2 million.
  • 15 skilled workers were employed for a period of 6 months.

Operational details

Implications for Melide

The completion of the redevelopment works represents a significant investment for the municipality of Melide, aiming to improve urban infrastructure and the value of the territory. The redevelopment of the town center is an example of how public investments can transform the image of a municipality and improve the quality of life of its residents. The paving with local and high-quality materials not only enhances the aesthetic appeal of the center but also supports the local economy.

📊 Concrete data: The redevelopment project of Melide's center involved an estimated investment of 2 million Swiss francs, funded through municipal funds and state contributions. This amount represents a significant portion of the municipality's annual budget, but was justified by the potential economic and social growth generated by the project.

💡 Best practice examples: Melide followed the example of municipalities like Lausanne and Geneva, which heavily invested in the redevelopment of their historic centers. For instance, Lausanne spent 50 million Swiss francs on the "Lausanne 2020" project, which included the redevelopment of streets, squares, and public infrastructure. This led to an increase in the cadastral value of properties and an increase in tourist visits.

💡 Comparison with other cantons: The canton of Ticino, where Melide is located, has a public investment rate in the infrastructure sector of 2.5% of the GDP, which is higher than the national average of 1.8%. This has allowed the canton to maintain a high standard of living and attractiveness for investors.

What to do for residents

Melide residents can now enjoy a more modern and welcoming town centre. The redevelopment of the centre represents an important step towards the modernisation of the municipality's urban infrastructure. The works, which began in 2022 with a budget of CHF 2.5 million, saw the renovation of 1,200 square metres of public spaces, including municipal offices, meeting rooms and service areas. The new LED lighting has reduced energy consumption by 30%, while the new wear-resistant ceramic tiles have been chosen to ensure a longer life.

📊 Key facts: Budget: CHF 2.5 million

  • Renovated surface: 1,200 m²
  • Energy consumption reduction: 30%
  • Materials used: Wear-resistant ceramic tiles, LED lighting

💡 Examples of good practice: Lugano: The historic centre of Lugano has seen a 25% reduction in energy consumption thanks to the implementation of led solutions and solar panels.

  • Zurich: The municipality of Zurich has invested CHF 5 million in the redevelopment of the historic centre, completed in 2023, with results similar to those of Melide.
