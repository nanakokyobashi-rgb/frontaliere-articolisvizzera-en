The obligations that run in parallel

Taxation, staff and contributions

In tax matters, the structure has three levels: direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each canton has its own law and multiplier; the municipality applies a multiplier to the cantonal tax. The AFC/ESTV handles IFD and IVA, while the cantonal administrations cover the cantonal and municipal levels. In planning for a public establishment, the tax check must therefore be conducted alongside the application, without assigning the licence tasks that belong to the tax system.

Personnel entail further rules. There is no federal minimum wage, although some cantons have their own. The maximum working time is 45 hours per week in industry, offices and retail, or 50 hours in other sectors. Minimum annual leave is four weeks, five for those under 20. Maternity leave lasts 14 weeks at 80% through IPG; paternity leave lasts two weeks. Notice periods are 1, 2 or 3 months, respectively, during the first year, from the second to the ninth year and from the tenth year onward.

From verification to application

To turn research into action, it is best to follow a brief, orderly sequence. There is no need to build a national checklist: what is needed is to identify the competent authority and apply the official source relevant to the case.

Five operational steps

1. Define the activity. State whether the application concerns a restaurant or a venue in the Canton of Bern. This distinction is the basis for identifying the relevant requirements.

2. Verify the authority. Check whether the procedure is cantonal, municipal or entrusted to another competent institution. Use the official source for the level identified.

3. Single out the professional requirement. Look for the section dedicated to the required training or responsibility. Do not assume that only one of the two conditions applies to every activity.

4. Check timelines and amounts. Before submitting, verify the requirements, deadlines and amounts in the official source. These elements follow the rules of the competent authority.

5. Submit the application. Once the authority has been identified, submit the application according to the procedure indicated by the official source and retain the authority's reference.

If the verification points to the canton, a municipal page does not replace the cantonal source; if it points to the municipality, a generic cantonal description is not enough.
