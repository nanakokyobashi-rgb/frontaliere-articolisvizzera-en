Agroscope: keys to the new laboratories in Posieux (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 16 August 2026

The Canton of Freiburg has officially handed over to the Confederation the keys to the new Agroscope laboratories in Posieux. The ceremony took place on Friday, August 14, 2026, with the participation of the President of the Confederation Guy Parmelin and the delegations of the Fribourg State Council and the directorates of Agroscope.

Confederation President Guy Parmelin attended the key handover ceremony of the new Agroscope laboratories in Posieux, together with delegations from the Fribourg State Council and Agroscope directorates as well as from the Federal Offices of Agriculture UFAG and of Construction and Logistics UFCL. With this step, the central element of Agroscope's location strategy came into operation.

The delivery marks the conclusion of a strategic project carried out in close collaboration between the Canton of Freiburg, the UFCL and Agroscope. The Canton built the building on land owned by the Confederation. It will initially be leased to the Confederation, which will then take possession of it upon expiry of the surface contract.

Operational details

Agroscope: Keys to the New Laboratories in Posieux

The key handover represents a key milestone in the implementation of Agroscope's location strategy. The concentration of a large part of laboratory activities and research technologies in Posieux allows Agroscope to reduce the costs of managing and maintaining laboratory infrastructures. Once all the removals from the various offices in Posieux have been completed, both in the laboratories and in the administrative building (the latter still to be renovated), the campus will host about 420 Agroscope collaborators.

The choice of Posieux as the main location for Agroscope's activities was influenced by its proximity to major Swiss universities, such as the University of Bern and the University of Freiburg. This will strengthen links with academic research and enhance collaboration between institutions. In addition, Posieux has a good transport infrastructure, with the presence of a train station and a state road connecting the city to Bern and Freiburg.

Key points

Agroscope: keys to the new laboratories in Posieux

The handover of the keys to Agroscope's new laboratories in Posieux represents an important step forward for the national research campus. The Canton of Freiburg and the UFCL worked together to implement this strategic project, which also includes the construction of an administrative building and the renovation of an existing infrastructure. The laboratories have been designed to be highly specialised and efficient, with a roof largely covered with photovoltaic panels.

The project, funded with a contribution of 10 million Swiss francs from the Canton of Freiburg and 5 million Swiss francs from the UFCL, has seen the creation of over 1,000 square meters of research space, which will host a team of scientists and technicians from different disciplines. The goal is to create a high-quality research center that can contribute to the advancement of science and technology in the agricultural and food fields.

The construction of the buildings has been carried out in a sustainable way, with the choice of environmentally friendly materials and the minimisation of greenhouse gas emissions. The roof covered with photovoltaic panels makes it possible to produce electricity and reduce dependence on fossil sources.
