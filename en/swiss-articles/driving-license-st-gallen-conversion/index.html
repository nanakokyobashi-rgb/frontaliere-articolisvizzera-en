Converting a foreign driver’s license to a Swiss license is a mandatory step for anyone settling in Switzerland who intends to continue driving. The procedure is not the same for all foreign license holders: the process depends on the country where the license was issued and the type of license held. The canton of St. Gallen, like the other Swiss cantons, entrusts the handling of these matters to the Strassenverkehrsamt, the cantonal motor vehicle office, which operates in accordance with relevant federal legislation and its own implementing regulations.

Practical Implications for New Residents

Those arriving in Switzerland who wish to maintain the validity of their driver’s license must consider several practical aspects. The first is the link to the residence permit: a foreign driver’s license may be used in Switzerland for a transitional period, but conversion becomes necessary once actual residence is established. The source refers to the general requirement for residents to convert their licenses but does not specify a precise deadline in days or months: this is information that the cantonal Strassenverkehrsamt can provide directly.

The distinction between countries with reciprocal agreements and third countries has a direct impact on the applicant’s process. A resident from an EU or EFTA country faces a more streamlined administrative process, while a resident from a third country may be required to take theory and practical exams at driving schools recognized by the canton. In both cases, the final decision on the procedure to follow rests with the cantonal traffic office.

Step-by-step procedure

To start the conversion of a foreign driving licence in the canton of St. Gallen, the resident can follow a path divided into several steps, bearing in mind that the source does not provide specific details on timing and costs.

The first step consists in gathering the required documentation: original valid driving licence, official translation if necessary, identity document, residence permit issued by the SEM (State Secretariat for Migration), proof of residence in the canton of St. Gallen. The second step is submitting the application to the Strassenverkehrsamt of the canton of St. Gallen, which assesses the applicant's situation and indicates the applicable process.

The third step concerns the possible theoretical and practical exam: the source does not confirm that these exams are always required, but indicates that they may be ordered by the cantonal office depending on the country of origin of the licence. The fourth step is the issuance of the Swiss driving licence, with possible return of the original document.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the competent office to convert the foreign license in the canton of St. Gallen?
The Strassenverkehrsamt of the canton of St. Gallen is the body that receives conversion applications, verifies the documentation and indicates the next steps. It operates on the basis of federal road traffic legislation and its own cantonal executive provisions.
Is the procedure the same for all countries of origin?
No. Swiss law provides for separate regimes: holders of licences issued by EU or EFTA States benefit from facilitated recognition thanks to reciprocity agreements. For holders of licences from third countries, the procedure may require theoretical and practical examinations, with procedures established on a case-by-case basis by the cantonal office.
What documents do you need to start the conversion?
The basic documentation indicated by the source includes: valid foreign license, valid identity document, residence permit issued by the SEM (Secretariat of State for Migration) and confirmation of residence in the canton of St. Gallen. Any additional documents (photos, medical certificates, translations) must be requested directly from the Strassenverkehrsamt.
When do I need to convert my driver's license after moving to Switzerland?
The source indicates the general conversion obligation for residents of Switzerland, but does not specify a precise deadline in days or months. The foreign license can be used for a transitional period, but the conversion becomes necessary once the actual residence is acquired. Details should be requested from the Strassenverkehrsamt.
How much does it cost to convert my driver's license in St. Gallen?
The source does not specify the amount of cantonal conversion fees or the costs associated with any examinations. Costs and taxes vary from canton to canton and from case to case: for up-to-date information, please contact the Strassenverkehrsamt of the canton of St. Gallen directly.
