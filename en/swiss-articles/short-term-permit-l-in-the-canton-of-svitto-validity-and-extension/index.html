The Short-Term L Permit in the Swiss Canton is an important option for border crossers, i.e. people who reside in a country bordering Switzerland and who need to stay temporarily in Swiss territory. This permit is regulated by the Federal Law on Foreigners (LFGS) and the Cantonal Law on Foreigners (LSI) of the Canton of Switzerland.

Validity and extension

The Short Term L Permit is valid for a maximum of 1 year, but may be extended up to a maximum of 3 years, if legitimate reasons for the extension are found. For example, if a border worker needs medical treatment at a Swiss hospital or if they have to attend a professional event in the Canton of Switzerland.

Requirements and procedures

To obtain the Short Term L Permit, frontier workers must meet certain requirements. First, they must demonstrate that they have safe accommodation and sufficient financial resources to cover their living costs in the Canton of Switzerland. In addition, they must provide complete documentation, including health insurance, accident insurance and liability insurance.

The application for a short-term L permit must be submitted at least 3 working days before arrival in Switzerland. You can apply online via the "SUISSE PASS" portal or at the nearest border office. You must present a copy of your passport, a recent black and white image, a declaration of residence and a health insurance valid in Switzerland.
