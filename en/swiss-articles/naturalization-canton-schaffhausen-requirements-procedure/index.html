Naturalization in the canton of Schaffhausen: requirements and procedure

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 September 2026

Learn about the requirements and procedure for ordinary naturalization in the Canton of Schaffhausen.

Context

In brief

  • Ordinary naturalization requires a C permit and 10 years of residence.
  • The integration and language test is mandatory.
  • Fees vary by municipality.

Key facts

  • What: Ordinary naturalization in the Canton of Schaffhausen.
  • When: C permit + 10 years of residence.
  • Where: Canton of Schaffhausen.
  • Who: Cantonal naturalization office.
  • Amount: Variable fees by municipality.

Ordinary naturalization in the Canton of Schaffhausen requires possession of a C permit and at least 10 years of continuous residence in the canton. This period can be reduced to 8 years if the applicant has completed an educational path in Switzerland or has worked in a qualified occupation. Applicants must pass an integration and language test, which verifies knowledge of the German language and local customs.

Specific Requirements

In addition to the general requirements, each municipality in the Canton of Schaffhausen may have additional requirements. For example, some municipalities require a longer period of residence or additional proof of integration. It is important to check with the cantonal naturalization office for detailed information on the specific requirements of your municipality of residence.

Procedure

The naturalization procedure begins with the submission of an application to the cantonal naturalization office. The application must be accompanied by a series of documents, including a residence certificate, a certificate of good conduct, and proof of integration. Once the application is submitted, the cantonal office will evaluate the request and, if necessary, summon the applicant for an interview.

Fees and Duration

Fees for naturalization vary by municipality. It is important to inquire at the cantonal naturalization office to know the exact amount of fees to be paid. The duration of the procedure can vary, but it generally takes several months to complete.

Operational details

Practical Implications

The ordinary naturalization in the Canton of Schaffhausen offers numerous advantages to applicants. Among these, the possibility to participate in cantonal and municipal elections, as well as to obtain a Swiss passport. However, it is important to note that naturalization is not automatic and requires a series of specific requirements that must be met.

Integration and Language Test

The integration and language test is a fundamental requirement for naturalization. This test verifies knowledge of the German language and local customs. Applicants must demonstrate the ability to communicate effectively in German and to understand the social and cultural norms of the canton.

Comparison with Other Cantons

The naturalization procedure in the Canton of Schaffhausen is similar to that of other Swiss cantons, but there are some differences in specific requirements. For example, some cantons require a longer period of residence or additional proof of integration. It is important to compare the naturalization requirements of different cantons to determine which is most suitable for one's needs.

Hypothetical Scenarios

Suppose an applicant has lived in the Canton of Schaffhausen for 9 years and has completed an educational path in Switzerland. In this case, the applicant may be eligible for ordinary naturalization, as the period of residence can be reduced to 8 years for those who have completed an educational path in Switzerland.
