LAMal 2026 in the Canton of Vaud: operational guide

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 30 August 2026

Obligation within 3 months of arrival, six tiers of deductible from CHF 300 to 2500 and cantonal subsidy: how to orient yourself.

Context

In brief

  • Mandatory LAMal coverage for all residents in Switzerland within 3 months of arrival
  • Adult deductibles: CHF 300/500/1000/1500/2000/2500, premiums by canton/region
  • Premium reduction (subsidy) administered at cantonal level, varying criteria
  • Vaud: no federal minimum wage, own cantonal system to be verified

Key facts

  • What: obligation to take out health insurance under LAMal (KVG)
  • When: within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland or birth
  • Where: all cantons, premiums differentiated by region (Vaud included)
  • Who: residents in Switzerland, including people with B, C, G permits and family members
  • How: recognized health insurers, per-capita premium (not a tax, not a salary contribution)
  • Adult deductibles: CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000, 2500 (six tiers)
  • Cantonal subsidy: premium reduction for low- and middle-income households, rules and thresholds set by Canton Vaud

LAMal (KVG in German, Federal Health Insurance Act) requires every person residing in Switzerland to take out basic health insurance within three months of arrival or birth. It is neither a tax nor a salary contribution: it is a per-capita premium collected by recognized health insurers. The obligation applies equally to the Canton of Vaud and any other canton: what changes are the premiums, calculated on a regional basis, and the relief available to low- and middle-income households.

Premiums are not uniform nationwide. Insurers publish separate rates by region, and the Canton of Vaud has its own territorial structure. The monthly premium must be paid regardless of the use of healthcare services: basic insurance provides for no exclusion based on lifestyle, age (over 18) or pre-existing health status.

Who must get insured and when

The obligation applies to residents, including employees, holders of B (residence) and C (domicile) permits, cross-border commuters with G permits—for whom specific coverage rules apply—and family members without gainful employment. Newborns must be insured within three months of birth. Anyone who does not meet the deadline is assigned ex officio to an insurer, with retroactive application of premiums and a possible surcharge.

At federal level, the UFSP/BAG (Federal Office of Public Health) oversees the regulatory framework, while operational supervision of insurers falls to the cantonal authorities. In Vaud, the relevant body for subsidies and information to citizens is the cantonal administration responsible for health matters, which publishes the income thresholds for premium reductions every year. The exact criteria and subsidy amounts in the Canton of Vaud for 2026 are not specified in this guide and should be checked on the Canton’s official website.

Operational details

Choosing the deductible is the first real lever for optimizing the LAMal premium. Adult policyholders can choose from six levels: CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 or 2500. The higher the deductible, the lower the monthly premium, but the greater the share of healthcare costs paid out of pocket before the insurer steps in. The deductible is annual and is renewed every calendar year. The decision should be based on expected healthcare use: those who rarely visit the doctor can save with high deductibles, while people with chronic conditions or families with children often prefer low deductibles.

In addition to the deductible, there is a 10% cost-sharing rate: once the deductible has been exceeded, the policyholder continues to pay 10% of the excess costs up to an annual statutory maximum, after which the insurer reimburses the full amount. This mechanism — distinct from the deductible system — is an integral part of LAMal and must be taken into account when planning annual healthcare expenses.

Insurance model and freedom of choice

Switzerland has a compulsory insurance system managed privately: policyholders are free to choose their health insurer and change provider. Insurers must accept any applicant, without risk selection. Competition is based on service quality, the network of affiliated doctors and, to a lesser extent, alternative insurance models (family doctor, HMO, telemedicine), which can reduce the premium in exchange for restrictions on the free choice of doctor. Several recognized insurers operate in the Canton of Vaud, with networks of doctors and hospitals distributed throughout the canton, including the university centers of CHUV and affiliated facilities.

Coverage: what LAMal actually reimburses

LAMal basic coverage is broad but not comprehensive. It includes medical visits, inpatient stays in the general ward of hospitals, outpatient care, medicines on the specific list, maternity care and most diagnostic services. Dental care, except for the specific exceptions provided by law for particular medical situations, is not included in compulsory coverage: for dental health, it is common practice to take out private supplementary insurance, which is not regulated by LAMal. Lenses, cosmetic procedures and many alternative therapies are also not covered under basic insurance.

Premium reductions in the Canton of Vaud

Each canton manages its own premium reduction system for low- and middle-income households. In Vaud, the eligibility conditions (income threshold, household composition, subsidy percentage) are defined by the cantonal authority. For 2026, the updated parameters — the reduction amount and the income bracket eligible for assistance — should be verified on the Canton of Vaud website, since the precise figures are not provided here. The subsidy application generally must be submitted each year, and failure to submit the tax return may result in losing eligibility.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure for new residents in the Canton of Vaud

1. Within three months of arriving in Switzerland, contact one or more recognized health insurers and request a quote for basic LAMal insurance. Coverage is retroactive to the date of arrival. 2. Choose the deductible (CHF 300/500/1000/1500/2000/2500) and, if applicable, the insurance model (free choice, family doctor, HMO, telemedicine), taking into account the premium reductions offered. 3. Send the registration confirmation received from the insurer to the residents’ registration office of the municipality of residence for registration. 4. If you believe you are entitled to cantonal premium reductions, submit the application to the competent service of the Canton of Vaud (generally by mid-year for the following year), attaching the latest tax return. 5. Check annually whether the premium and chosen deductible are still suitable: in autumn, insurers publish the new premiums, and by the end of November (the ordinary deadline provided for by LAMal for standard cancellation), it is possible to change insurer or deductible tier, effective January 1.

Changing insurer: deadlines and forms

Cancelling a LAMal policy follows precise rules: for the standard deductible, the insurer must receive the cancellation by the ordinary statutory deadline, generally three months before the end of the year, effective January 1. For higher deductibles (CHF 2000 and 2500), the cancellation deadline may be different. The form is the one provided by the insurer, and the notice may be sent by registered mail. Once it has received the application, the new insurer is obliged to accept the insured person. Interruptions in coverage are not permitted: the start and end dates must match.

Documents to prepare

To take out the policy, you need: a valid identity document, a residence permit (for foreign nationals: L, B, C, G, as applicable), a certificate of arrival or residence issued by the municipality, and, if applicable, a birth certificate for children to be included in the family policy. For the premium-reduction application: the latest tax assessment decision or tax return.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does the LAMal insurance obligation start in the Canton of Vaud?
The obligation to take out basic health insurance under LAMal takes effect within three months of arrival in Switzerland or birth. This applies to all residents, including B, C, G permit holders and non-lucrative family members. Those who do not comply with the deadline are assigned ex officio to a cashier, with retroactive application of the premiums and possible surcharge.
Which franchises can you choose in the Canton of Vaud?
Adults can choose from six excess brackets: CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 or 2500. The higher the deductible, the lower the monthly premium, but the higher the share of healthcare costs out-of-pocket. After the deductible, the participation rate operates at a cost of 10%, up to the annual maximum provided for by law.
How does premium reduction work in the Canton of Vaud?
Each canton operates its own premium reduction system for lower-middle-income households. In Vaud, the conditions of access (income threshold, family composition, percentage of subsidy) are defined by the cantonal authority. The 2026 parameters must be verified at the Canton's official website. The application must be resubmitted every year, attaching the tax return.
What is the procedure for a new resident in the Canton of Vaud?
Within three months of arrival: contact a recognized health insurance fund and ask for a basic LAMal offer, choose the deductible and any model (free, family doctor, HMO, telemedicine), send the registration confirmation to the inhabitants' control office, and if applicable, submit a request for premium reduction to the competent cantonal service, usually within half a year for the following year.
When can I change sick pay?
For the standard deductible, the cashier must generally receive the cancellation three months before the end of the year, with effect from 1 January. For higher deductibles (CHF 2000 and 2500) the term may be different. The new cashier is obliged to accept the insured and no interruptions of coverage are allowed: the start and end dates must match.
