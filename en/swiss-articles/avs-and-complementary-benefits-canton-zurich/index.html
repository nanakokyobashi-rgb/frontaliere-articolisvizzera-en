Operational details

Practical Implications

The Zurich Cantonal Compensation Fund is a crucial institution for Swiss pensions. The calculation of the AVS annuity is essential to guarantee the minimum living. Contribution gaps and supplementary benefits are essential to cover expenses. The Zurich Cantonal Compensation Fund is responsible for ensuring that Swiss pension beneficiaries receive the minimum living allowance.

The calculation of the AVS pension is based on a complex algorithm that takes into account the beneficiary's income, the number of years worked and the contribution percentage. For example, if a beneficiary has an income of 60,000 Swiss francs per year and has worked for 30 years, their AVS income could be 2,500 Swiss francs per month.

However, contribution gaps and supplementary benefits are essential to cover expenses. For example, if a beneficiary has an income of 50,000 Swiss francs per year and has worked for 25 years, their AVS income could be 2,000 Swiss francs per month, but they may need supplements to cover expenses of around 500 Swiss francs per month.
