Kindergarten Freiburg: childcare, costs (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 September 2026

Child custody in the canton of Fribourg: rates, subsidies available and procedures for applying for cantonal contributions to families.

Context

In brief

  • Childcare in Switzerland is a cantonal responsibility: daycare centers, day families, after-school care
  • Fees calculated based on family income and number of children; cantonal subsidies reduce costs
  • Childcare expenses are partially deductible from federal taxes

Key facts

  • What: Childcare (daycare centers, day families, after-school care) is the responsibility of the cantons
  • When: Available from a few months old until school entry (3-4 years)
  • Where: Canton of Fribourg
  • Who: Cantonal and municipal administrations, private and public facilities
  • Amount: Fees vary by income; cantonal subsidies available

In the Canton of Fribourg, as in all of Switzerland, childcare during the day is a shared responsibility between families, public and private facilities, and cantonal support. There is no single federal system of public daycare centers: each canton regulates the offer, fees, and subsidies independently.

Childcare options include professional daycare centers (public and private), day families (family assistants who care for children in their homes), after-school care for children after kindergarten, and public vs. private communal facilities.

Fees and models vary significantly between cantons. Generally, they are calculated based on family income (with a progressive scale), the number of children in care, the number of days and weekly hours, and the type of facility (daycare center vs. day family). Each canton offers subsidies for families with incomes below defined thresholds to facilitate access.

Operational details

How fares are calculated

Rates for kindergarten and daycare in Switzerland follow standardized models. Most cantons use income brackets: the canton defines income brackets (e.g. CHF <60'000/year, CHF 60'000-80 '000, CHF 80' 000-100 '000, etc.), each with a different daily rate.

The basic daily rate varies depending on the property and canton. In addition, the cost can increase with additional hours (afternoon care, Wednesday afternoon, summer days, evenings), additional days beyond the standard ones, and extra services (specialized canteen, language courses, extracurricular activities).

Daytime family often has lower rates than professional kindergarten, since management costs are lower (less staff, no dedicated building). However, quality and certifications may vary.

Cantonal subsidies and reductions

The Canton of Freiburg and the municipalities offer subsidies to mitigate the burden of costs on families. Subsidies are available for:

  • Families with income below defined threshold (specific amounts and thresholds vary by canton)
  • Siblings in simultaneous custody (progressive reduction: second child 20-30% discount, third even more)
  • Municipal public facilities (often rates already lower than private, with additional subsidies)

Key points

How to find facilities and obtain subsidies: step-by-step procedure

Step 1 – Identify available facilities Request a list of accredited public and private daycare centers from your municipality of residence. Many municipalities maintain online registers. Consult daycare center management associations (Swiss Daycare Center Association, Cantonal Federations). Check if municipalities offer online platforms for searching facilities. Visit candidate facilities, collect educational programs, schedules, contacts, and estimated waiting lists.

Step 2 – Apply for cantonal subsidy Submit a cantonal subsidy application to the competent municipal or cantonal office (ideal timing: 6-12 months before the start of care). Attach income certification: federal and cantonal tax returns for the last 2 years, sometimes an income certificate from the employer. Receive authorization/amount of subsidy in support of the enrollment application to the facility.

Step 3 – Enroll the child Contact the chosen facility, verify availability and timeline. Complete the official enrollment form. Attach required documentation (birth certificate, LAMal health insurance card, copies of parental authorizations if separated). Sign the contract specifying rates (gross and net rate with subsidy applied), schedules, cancellation conditions, responsibilities, and educational plans.

Step 4 – Documentation for tax deductibility Keep all monthly invoices from the facility (main document). Request a payment certificate or annual receipt from the facility. In the first year, attach a copy of the contract and monthly payment certificates. In subsequent years, only the annual payment receipt. Deductibility applies only if one of the parents declares the expense in the federal (IFD) and cantonal tax returns.

Frequently Asked Questions
What custody options are available in the Canton of Fribourg?
In the Canton of Freiburg there are public and private nurseries (with qualified educators), day families (accredited assistants for up to 5 children), and after-school facilities. Each option has different rates and times depending on the facility. Many families choose according to hours, proximity to home/work, child-educator relationship and educational approach (Montessori, Reggio Emilia, etc.). Registrations must be made 6-12 months in advance.
How are daycare and kindergarten fees calculated?
Rates in Switzerland follow income brackets: the canton defines income brackets with different daily rates. Costs increase with additional hours (afternoon, summer days) and extra services. Daytime family is often less expensive than daycare. The final rate depends on the number of children in custody, days per week, type of facility.
What subsidies can I apply for in the Canton of Fribourg?
The Canton and the municipalities offer subsidies for families with incomes below certain levels, siblings in simultaneous custody (progressive reduction: second child 20-30%, third even more), and municipal public structures. Subsidies may be percentage fare reductions, “service vouchers,” or partial refunds. They can be combined with federal tax deductibility.
Are Custody Fees Tax Deductible?
Yes, at the federal level. Expenses for nurseries, day families, babysitters are partially deductible from taxable income. Only one of the two parents can benefit from the deduction. Invoices and receipts must be kept. This benefit is combined with cantonal subsidies. Many cantons offer additional tax deductions or credits.
How to apply for a cantonal custody allowance?
Apply to the relevant municipal/cantonal office 6-12 months prior to commencement. Attach income certification (declaration of federal and cantonal taxes last 2 years). Receive subsidy authorization. Then enroll the child in the facility and keep invoices for tax deductibility. Timing is crucial to ensure optimal seat availability and subsidies.
