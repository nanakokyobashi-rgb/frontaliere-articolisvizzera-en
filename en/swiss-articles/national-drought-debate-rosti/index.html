Drought and heatwave: the debate in the National Council

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 30 September 2026

The National Council is debating twelve parliamentary questions on the drought. Rösti announces a new water strategy for next year.

Bern hosted the National Council’s current affairs debate on heat waves and drought. The twelve urgent parliamentary questions addressed to the Federal Department of the Environment, Transportation, Energy, and Communications brought three related issues to the floor: water shortages, protecting vulnerable populations from heat waves, and measures to support agriculture.

The debate brought together a variety of demands. The Liberal Greens called for a national water management strategy and greater protection of buildings. Corina Gredig (PVL/ZH) pointed to the need to increase water reserves. The Greens criticized Albert Rösti, head of DATEC, for expressing surprise at the severity of the drought.

The Groups’ Positions

Franziska Ryser (SG) brought the health issue to the forefront of the debate:

The heat wave is not just a nuisance, but also a health hazard.

Speaking for the Socialists, Mattea Meyer (ZH) asked why there is no action plan for the period 2026–2030. From the Center Party, Stefan Müller-Altermatt (SO) warned of the possible consequences of the drought on agriculture in the coming years. Christian Wasserfallen (PLR/BE), on the other hand, advocated for greater use of modern technologies and adaptation measures, especially for workers.

Government, Municipalities, and Energy

The SVP defended the actions of its minister. Ernst Wandfluh (BE) praised the speed with which the government adopted measures during the crisis, including the exemption from customs duties on animal feed and the forest health subsidy.

Rösti said he wanted to support cantons and municipalities in their areas of responsibility, citing the climate control of public buildings and the management of reservoirs. For the latter area, he intends to propose a new strategy next year. On nuclear power, he explained that this source is particularly useful in winter, while solar power’s contribution increases in summer. Decarbonization, he added, remains the top priority.

Operational details

The practical value of the debate lies in the distinction between levels of intervention. Water management is not presented as solely an agricultural issue: the concerns extend to reserves, reservoirs, real estate, and public health. For those who live or work in Switzerland, this broadens the scope of the decisions to be made: the DATEC remains the focal point of the national discussion, while cantons and municipalities come into play regarding public buildings and reservoirs.

The most concrete step for local communities is therefore the combination of coordination and local expertise. The national strategy called for by the Liberal Greens and the one that Rösti intends to propose next year outline a general framework; air conditioning in public buildings and the management of reservoirs, on the other hand, fall under the purview of cantons and municipalities. For the reader, this distinction helps avoid confusing a national policy direction with a measure already implemented everywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions
What were the main topics of the debate in the National Council?
The debate concerned twelve urgent interpellations addressed to DATEC. The central issues were water scarcity, the protection of vulnerable people during the heatwave, and support measures for agriculture. The Green Liberals called for a national water management strategy, while the political camp discussed the role of the Cantons and Municipalities in managing water basins and air conditioning in public buildings.
What measures did Federal Councillor Albert Rösti announce?
Albert Rösti, head of DATEC, stated that he wanted to support Cantons and Municipalities in their areas of responsibility, with particular attention to air conditioning in public buildings and the management of water basins. In the latter area, the minister announced his intention to propose a new national strategy by next year. Rösti also emphasized the importance of decarbonization and distinguished the seasonal role of energy sources, with nuclear power useful in winter and solar power in summer.
What was done by the Government for agriculture during the crisis?
According to what the UDC reported in the chamber, the Government adopted rapid measures during the crisis, including an exemption from customs duties on imported fodder and the disbursement of a targeted contribution for forest health. During the discussion, representatives of the Centre nevertheless highlighted the need to consider the possible prolonged consequences of drought for agriculture in the years to come.
