Nurses in Lugano: Social Institutes are looking for staff

The Social Institutes of Lugano are currently seeking qualified nurses for various positions. Available roles include both full-time and part-time opportunities.

Applicants must possess a good command of the Italian language and hold a degree in Nursing. Knowledge of additional languages, such as German and French, is considered a plus.

The demand for nurses is increasing due to the growing elderly population and the need for more complex care. The Social Institutes of Lugano offer a stable career path and opportunities for professional growth.

Available positions include:

  • Pediatric Nurse: Providing care for children aged 0 to 18 years
  • Geriatric Nurse: Caring for elderly patients
  • Day Nurse: Assisting patients during their stay at the Social Institutes

Interested candidates must undergo a selection process, which includes an interview and a professional skills assessment.

Key points

Nurse Wanted in Lugano: Social Institutions Seek Staff

The Social Institutions of Lugano are seeking new staff to strengthen their team of nurses and assistants. To apply, please send your curriculum vitae and a cover letter to the Social Institutions of Lugano. Applications must be submitted by June 30.

This announcement is a great opportunity for those who love healthcare and want to work in a dynamic environment. The Social Institutions of Lugano are an important reference point for public health in the Canton of Ticino, based in Lugano. The facility offers a wide range of care and assistance services for patients, including rehabilitation, therapy, and prevention.

To apply, you must have a degree in nursing and at least 2 years of work experience. Knowledge of German and Italian is required, as the Social Institutions of Lugano work with patients of different languages and cultures. In addition, you must have a good knowledge of computer systems and medical technologies.

The Social Institutions of Lugano offer a dynamic and stimulating work environment, with a team of professionals committed to providing high-quality services to patients. The facility is equipped with modern equipment and offers excellent ongoing training to ensure that employees are always up-to-date on the latest technologies and procedures.

For more information, applicants can contact the Social Institutions of Lugano directly to schedule an interview.
