The public transport fare system in the Canton of St. Gallen follows the organizational model widespread in Switzerland: a fare community coordinates fares across different geographical areas and guarantees the compatibility of season tickets between different public carriers. This integrated approach represents one of the pillars of Swiss mobility, allowing residents and workers to travel on public networks without tariff fragmentation.

Zone structure: how it works

The zone structure is the basis of the Swiss and Canton of St. Gallen tariff system. Each zone corresponds to a defined geographical area, and the ticket price depends on the number of zones crossed. Those who move within a single area pay less than those who cross several. This logic makes public transport flexible and convenient for short journeys within the city or to neighboring municipalities, while season tickets significantly contain the costs for long journeys and daily journeys.

The fare community guarantees that a passenger can use tickets and passes on buses, trams and trains from different operators without purchasing separate tickets for each line. This integration is fundamental for those who travel on multiple lines every day and change carriers during the journey: for example, a commuter who uses the bus to reach the station and then the train to the city can use a single ticket or season ticket.

For regular passengers, season tickets represent the most convenient solution compared to purchasing single tickets. In the Canton of St. Gallen, as in other cantons, monthly passes are available, ideal for those with variable mobility, and annual passes, which spread the cost over twelve months guaranteeing significantly better rates. Those who travel regularly on a daily basis - commuters, workers with fixed routes, students who attend school every day - benefit most from annual subscriptions, recovering the initial investment within a few months of use.

Two national options integrate perfectly with regional travelcards: the Half-Fare travelcard, which reduces single tickets and many travelcards by 50% on the entire Swiss public rail and bus network, and the General Travelcard (GA), which covers the entire national rail network and most local transport. In the Canton of St. Gallen, it is possible to combine the regional season ticket with these national solutions, creating tariff flexibility that adapts to different mobility profiles.

Operational details

Annual and monthly subscriptions: which to choose

The annual subscription in Canton St. Gallen represents the most convenient long-term option for those who plan to use public transport regularly. By paying a single amount, the passenger spreads the cost over twelve months and accesses lower fares compared to the sum of twelve monthly subscriptions purchased individually. This solution is ideal for residents who use public transport daily: commuters who reach their workplace, students attending universities or upper secondary schools in the city, workers with fixed and regular journeys.

The monthly subscription offers greater flexibility instead. Those who do not travel consistently throughout the year, those who know they will change destination in certain months (for example, during school breaks), or those who want to test public transport before committing long-term, find the monthly option most suitable. Monthly prices are higher than one twelfth of the annual cost, but this difference covers and compensates for the advantage of contractual flexibility.

Half Fare and General Subscription: the national options

The Half Fare is a Swiss national card that reduces by 50% all single tickets and many subscriptions across the entire railway network and most of the Swiss public bus network. In Canton St. Gallen, those who hold a Half Fare card can apply it to the regional subscription, drastically reducing the overall cost. The initial investment in the card pays off quickly for those who travel frequently, making it worthwhile already after a few months of intense use.

The General Subscription (GA) is the most comprehensive solution available in Switzerland: it guarantees unlimited access to trains, buses, trams, and boats across the entire national network. In Canton St. Gallen, a GA holder does not need a separate regional subscription, as the GA already covers local travel. However, those who want a cheaper subscription limited to the canton or a specific geographical area choose a local subscription combined with the Half Fare to obtain significant reductions.

Combination strategies and evaluation of the choice

The real convenience emerges from the possible combinations between local and national solutions. A passenger could:

  • Purchase a St. Gallen annual subscription + Half Fare for savings of up to 50% compared to single tickets
  • Opt for the GA if travel regularly involves all of Switzerland and other regions
  • Alternate monthly subscriptions during periods of low mobility and annual subscriptions when travel increases (for example, during school months)

The choice depends on the individual mobility profile and travel habits. Those who travel only within the canton and surrounding areas should conveniently choose the local subscription. Those who work in different Swiss cities or travel frequently at a national level for work or study reasons should seriously evaluate the GA option. Key points

Discounts for students and apprentices

Students and apprentices in the Canton of St. Gallen benefit from dedicated and reduced fares. Middle schools, high schools, and universities often offer discounted school subscriptions at reduced costs, as do vocational training centers and apprenticeship providers. These discounts usually range between 20% and 40% compared to standard fares for the same duration and geographic validity zone.

To access dedicated discounts, official supporting documentation is required: a school card, a letter from the educational institution with a stamp, or an apprenticeship contract. Some carriers require annual renewal of the documentation to maintain the discount, thereby verifying the continuation of the student or apprentice status.

Procedure to obtain a subscription

1. Identify your travel zones: Determine whether you travel within a single zone (single city) or across multiple zones (toward neighboring municipalities, work or study areas). This information is available from the transport operators.

2. Check updated prices: Visit the website of the main transport operator in the Canton of St. Gallen or access the fare lists directly at railway stations and authorized points of sale to compare prices of monthly and annual subscriptions.

3. Evaluate the duration: Analyze your regular travel patterns. If you plan to use public transport almost every day for a year, the annual subscription is economically advantageous. If you are unsure or expect variability, start with monthly subscriptions for 2-3 months to test.

4. Check for any discounts: If you are a student or apprentice, gather the required documentation in advance (school card, institution letter, apprenticeship contract) so you can request the dedicated discounts at the time of purchase.

5. Purchase: Go to authorized points of sale (railway stations, newsstands, carrier ticket offices) or buy online if the carrier offers this option. Keep the purchase documentation and the subscription serial number.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the tariff community in the Canton of St. Gallen?
The fare community is an organization that coordinates the prices and fare conditions of public transport on different lines and geographical areas. It guarantees that the tickets and subscriptions purchased are valid on buses, trams and trains of multiple carriers and operators without the need to buy separate tickets for each company. In the Canton of St. Gallen, this system integrates the regional network into a single coherent tariff structure.
What is the real difference between annual and monthly subscription?
The annual subscription covers twelve months with a total cost of less than the sum of twelve monthly subscriptions purchased separately. It suits those who move daily all year round. The monthly subscription offers flexibility: you can only activate it in the months of need and it costs more than a twelfth of the annual one, but it allows variability and adaptation to changes in mobility.
How does Half-price work and when is it convenient?
Half-price is a national card that reduces the cost of individual tickets by 50% and many subscriptions on the entire railway network and most of the Swiss public bus network. In the Canton of St. Gallen, you can apply it to your regional subscription to save significantly. The cost of the card quickly depreciates if you travel frequently, making it advantageous even after a few months.
What is the difference between AG (General Subscription) and St. Gallen Subscription?
AG covers the entire railway network and most of Switzerland's public transport, allowing you to travel anywhere in Switzerland. The St Gallen subscription covers only the canton and the geographical area of validity chosen. Choose the AG if you regularly travel outside the canton for work or study; otherwise, a local subscription is significantly cheaper.
What specific discounts do students and apprentices have?
Students and apprentices receive 20-40% reductions on standard subscriptions. To access, you must present official documentation (school card, school letter, internship contract). The discounts vary according to the carrier and are renewed annually with the presentation of updated documentation to prove the maintenance of status.
