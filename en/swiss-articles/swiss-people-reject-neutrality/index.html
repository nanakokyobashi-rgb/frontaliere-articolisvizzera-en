Initiative on neutrality rejected by the Swiss people (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

The neutrality initiative was rejected with approximately 70% voting no; it did not obtain a majority of the cantons after the count in 15 of them, while Ticino shows a slight divergence.

The Swiss people rejected the neutrality initiative at the ballot box today. After the count in 15 Cantons, it is certain that the text did not obtain a majority of the Cantons. Ticino partly diverged from the clear national rejection indicated by the overall result.

The proposal was supported by Pro Svizzera and UDC. This is not the first time this year that Switzerland’s main party has failed to convince citizens of its proposals. Today’s result is in fact part of the sequence of votes recalled by the source.

Projections and experts’ comments

The result is not a surprise: polls had left little room for the initiative’s proponents to hope. Projections indicate around 70% no.

“UDC failed to mobilize beyond its own base,” said political scientist Urs Bieri of the gfs.bern institute on SRF radio.

According to the expert, apart from supporters of the Swiss People’s Party, there were few or no votes in favor of the initiative. Bieri’s proposed reading therefore concerns UDC’s ability to expand support beyond its core supporters.

The source also recalls two highly significant previous votes. In March, the proposal to lower the radio-TV licence fee to 200 francs was rejected with 61,9% no. In June, the initiative against a Switzerland of 10 million received 54,8% no.

Even the strong support, especially financial, from former Federal Councillor Christoph Blocher was not enough. Bieri observed that money alone does not make it possible to win: coalitions are needed, and the issue must be perceived as urgent.

How to correctly verify the result

Four steps to avoid forcing the data

1. Start with the certain outcome. The initiative on neutrality was rejected at the polls and did not obtain a majority of the Cantons after the count in 15 of them. This is the firm point of the news.

2. Separate the projection from the result described as certain. The percentage of no votes around 70% is indicated by the projections. It should not be presented as data different from what the source attributes to the ballot count already considered.

3. Keep the national level distinct from the Ticino level. Ticino partly differs from the clear national rejection, but the text does not provide a local percentage. It is therefore not possible to derive a more precise measure from the source.

4. Always verify the attribution of the comments. The assessment of the SVP's mobilization comes from political scientist Urs Bieri, of the gfs.bern institute, who spoke on SRF radio. The reference to financial support, on the other hand, concerns former Federal Councillor Christoph Blocher.

This method makes it possible to use the comparisons indicated by the source without mixing them up.
