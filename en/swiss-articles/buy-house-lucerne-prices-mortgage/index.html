The purchase and sale of homes in the Canton of Lucerne is subject to numerous rules and regulations. According to the law of the Canton of Lucerne, the purchase and sale of a property requires the consent of the cantonal administration. This means that buyers must submit a request for authorisation to the cantonal administration before proceeding with the sale.

The law of the Canton of Lucerne establishes that the purchase and sale of a property must be recorded in the real estate serial number of the Canton of Lucerne. This is a public register containing information about property ownership in the Canton of Lucerne. The sale of a property must be recorded within 30 days from the date of the sale.
