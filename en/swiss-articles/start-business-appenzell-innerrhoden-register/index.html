Starting a business in Switzerland requires compliance with precise procedures defined at federal and cantonal level. In the Inner Appenzell canton, as in the other Swiss cantons, the first step is to choose the legal form that best suits your business project. The decision between a sole proprietorship, simple company, limited liability company (SAGL), public limited company (SA) or cooperative is not purely formal: it conditions personal liability, the minimum capital required, the tax structure and advertising obligations.

Operational details

Three-level taxation: federal, cantonal and municipal

Switzerland applies a multi-level tax system that directly affects every entrepreneur. At the federal level, there is the Federal Direct Tax (IFD) on business income, managed by the Federal Customs Administration (FCA). At the cantonal level, the canton of Appenzell Rhodes-Iodine applies a business income tax according to its own legal rates, which can vary significantly from one canton to another. At the municipal level, individual municipalities multiply the cantonal rate to obtain the final municipal rate: this multiplier mechanism means that two companies in the same canton, but in different municipalities, pay slightly different taxes.

Furthermore, all companies subject to turnover tax above a certain threshold must register for Value Added Tax (VAT), administered at the federal level. The standard VAT rate is 7.7%, but reduced rates of 2.5% for food items and 3.7% for pharmaceuticals and newspapers exist. VAT registration becomes mandatory with annual turnover exceeding a level defined by federal law; below that, registration is optional but convenient if the company has many B2B clients.

Key points

Practical Step-by-Step Procedure

The first step is to consult the commercial register office of the canton Appenzell Inner Rhodes to learn about the exact local requirements, the forms to fill out, and the applicable fees. The standard procedure includes: choosing a legal form and drafting, where necessary, a statute or partnership agreement (it is advisable to seek legal or notarial consultation for SA and SAGL); opening a bank account dedicated to the business; completing the registration application for the commercial register with all required documents (identity document, bank account statement, criminal record extracts if required, certificate of good conduct where required); payment of the registration fee (amount varies by canton); waiting for approval and publication in the official cantonal journal.

After registration with the commercial register, the entrepreneur must register with the federal social security authorities. This means contacting the competent AVS office for the place of residence and submitting a registration application, indicating the estimated business income. For companies with employees, it is mandatory to affiliate with a recognized LPP compensation fund and to take out accident insurance (LAINF/LAA) with SUVA or an authorized private insurer.

Registration Fees and Initial Costs

Registration fees for the commercial register vary significantly depending on the canton and sometimes even by municipality. In the case of Appenzell Inner Rhodes, the exact amount must be verified with the competent office, since there is no uniform figure at the federal level. Similarly, if you choose a SAGL, it is necessary to deposit the minimum capital with a bank and provide a bank certificate; the same applies to an SA, which requires a higher minimum capital.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the minimum capital required to open a sole proprietorship in Appenzello Interno?
A sole proprietorship does not have a minimum capital defined by federal law. The entrepreneur starts the business with the personal means he deems necessary. The choice of legal form depends on the sector: a SAGL requires a minimum capital that must be deposited with a bank before registration and certified, while a public limited company (SA) requires a higher one. The trade register office of the Inner Appenzell canton provides the specific details for each form.
What documents are needed to register in the trade register?
In general, you need: valid identity document of the entrepreneur or the shareholders, statement of the company bank account, completed registration form, and possibly a copy of the articles of association (for companies) or the company contract. Some cantons also require a certificate of good conduct. The Inner Appenzell canton office provides the exact list of required documents. It is advisable to contact the office before submitting the application to check all local requirements.
Do I have to pay VAT if my business is small?
VAT is mandatory if the annual turnover exceeds a threshold defined by federal law. Below it, registration is not mandatory, but it can be convenient if you have business customers (B2B) as it allows you to recover the VAT paid on costs. Those who choose not to register can do so as long as they remain below the turnover limit. Details and registration forms are provided by the Federal Customs Administration (FCA).
What are the social security contributions for employees?
For employees, the AVS/AI/IPG contribution is 5.3% for the employee and 10.6% total together with the employer. Unemployment insurance (AD) is 1.1% up to an annual ceiling. Accident insurance (LAINF/LAA) varies between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the sector. If the company has employees, the LPP (OCCUPATIONAL pension) is mandatory with percentages that vary according to the age of the worker.
How long does it take to get registered in the trade register?
Generally, the procedure takes 1-2 weeks from receipt of the complete application with all documents. Times may vary depending on the workload of the cantonal office. It is advisable to contact the commercial register office of the canton of Appenzello Interno directly to find out the average times and check if there are specific deadlines or reduced working periods.
