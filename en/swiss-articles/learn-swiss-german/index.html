Context

German Swiss, a variant of the German spoken in Switzerland, is a key language for border workers working or living in this wonderful country. This language is spoken in almost every canton in Switzerland, from the Alps to German-speaking Switzerland, and is a valuable resource for communication between border crossers and local residents.

To learn German Swiss, there are numerous options available, including intensive and personalized courses, mobile apps, and online programs with personalized curricula. For example, the “Choose Me” platform offers an intensive German course for border workers, with classes led by communication professionals and a certified final assessment. In addition, the "Duolingo" app offers a free learning program with over 30 languages, including German-speaking Switzerland.

For a better understanding of the cantonal system and its regulations, it is important to be familiar with the official language. For example, the Canton of Zurich has its own version of the civil and criminal code in German, which affects Swiss culture and society.
