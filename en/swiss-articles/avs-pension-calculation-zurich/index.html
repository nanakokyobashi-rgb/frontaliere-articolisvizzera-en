The Zurich cantonal compensation fund plays a crucial role in managing the first pillar of Swiss social security, the AVS. This entity is responsible for calculating the AVS pension, which is essential for ensuring an adequate pension for citizens. The calculation of the AVS pension is based on the contributions paid during working life and can be influenced by contribution gaps. Additionally, complementary benefits are designed to cover the minimum living standard, ensuring that citizens have sufficient income during retirement.

Calculation of the AVS Pension

The calculation of the AVS pension is a complex process that takes into account various factors, including the number of years of contributions paid and the average salary. The Zurich cantonal compensation fund uses this data to determine the pension amount. It is important to note that contribution gaps can significantly reduce the AVS pension amount. Therefore, it is crucial for workers to ensure regular payment of contributions to avoid these gaps.
