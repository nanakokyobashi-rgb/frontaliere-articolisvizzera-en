The health panorama of the Canton of Graubünden has experienced a 2025 marked by economic stabilisation. After a previous year characterized by widespread budgetary difficulties, most of the local hospitals and health centers have managed to reverse course, returning to generate profits or significantly reducing historical deficits. The Cantonal Hospital of Graubünden, the reference health facility for the entire territory, closed the year with a profit of CHF 8.7 million. Management attributed this positive outcome to a rigorous improvement of operational processes and more efficient management of internal resources. This is a significant leap in quality compared to 2024, when the structure had closed with a slight deficit of 339,000francs, despite an operating turnover of 543.4 million francs.

Operational details

Analysis of Management Scenarios

The financial recovery observed in the Grigioni is not uniform, but the result of targeted management strategies. While some structures have benefited from extraordinary public interventions, others have had to implement drastic cost containment measures. The case of the Savognin healthcare center is emblematic: the operating result of nearly 2.4 million Swiss francs was achieved thanks to public contributions and a review of performance. Starting from next year, a new performance agreement will come into effect, which provides for an annual payment of 500,000 Swiss francs by the municipality of Surses, subject to maintaining a liquidity of less than 2.5 million Swiss francs. This safeguard clause represents an interesting model of risk management for local Swiss administrations.

Challenges and Restructurings

Not all structures have had a linear path. The Samedan hospital has faced an extremely complex situation, marked by the failure of two distinct rescue plans. The situation was only resolved after a favorable vote in February, which led to the creation of the 'Sanadura' management entity, active since last April. The system now relies on performance agreements totaling 19.5 million Swiss francs, involving the hospital, the nursing home, and the Spitex service. The dynamics in Alta Engadina demonstrate how the fragmentation of healthcare services can threaten sustainability, requiring consolidation processes such as the integration into the Spital Oberengadin society, linked to the group of the Coira Canton Hospital.

How to interpret the health accounts for citizens and patients in Ticino

For residents in Ticino, the financial stability of a hospital is a key indicator of the quality and continuity of the services it offers. When a facility like the CSVP holds a capital stake of 77%, it ensures a greater ability to invest in technological upgrades and new infrastructure, such as the expansion of the San Sisto Hospital. The transparency of annual reports is essential to understand whether the management is focusing on operational efficiency or an excessive dependence on state subsidies. The monitoring procedure of the accounts, which often involves local communities and cantonal authorities, ensures that resources allocated to LAMal/KVG are used optimally.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which Greyhound hospitals made a profit in 2025?
In 2025, several structures closed in assets: the cantonal hospital of Chur obtained 8.7 million francs, the hospital of Davos 12.8 million francs, the Bregaglia Health Centre over 1.1 million francs and the CSVP over 2.6 million francs. The Savognin health centre also ended the year with a positive operating result of almost CHF 2.4 million.
Why did Samedan hospital have difficulties?
Samedan hospital faced a difficult 2025 due to the failure of two rescue plans. The situation was resolved with a vote in February, which led to the creation of the 'Sanadura' entity and integration into the Spital Oberengadin company, active since 1 April 2025.
What are the prospects for the hospital in Davos?
Thanks to the profit of 12.8 million francs recorded in 2025 and the municipal approval for the assumption of 14 million francs of previous debts, the renovation project of the structure has been started, which will require about 70 million francs. Voters will be asked to vote on the project next year.
