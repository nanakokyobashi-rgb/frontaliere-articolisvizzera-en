Internet blackout: in Switzerland you can pay by card (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 August 2026

The emergency plan for internet-free digital payments in Switzerland has been presented. Operational by the end of next year for basic goods.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Presented emergency plan for digital payments
  • Working by the end of next year

Key facts

  • What: Plan to ensure digital payments without internet
  • When: Presented on Monday
  • Where: Bern
  • Who: Guy Parmelin, Martin Schlegel and Roland Pfister
  • Amount: Two million daily card transactions

In Bern, an emergency plan was presented to ensure the continuity of digital payments even in the event of an internet outage. The announcement was made by the president of the Confederation Guy Parmelin, together with the president of the general management of the Swiss National Bank Martin Schlegel and the country's economic supply delegate Roland Pfister. This initiative aims to make the system operational by the end of next year, aligning Switzerland with other Northern European states that already own a similar device. The need arises from concrete estimates that indicate about two million daily transactions carried out in the Confederation through the use of physical cards, making a practical and solid solution indispensable for the resident population and for the commercial activities of the national territory.

Operational details

The operational restrictions provided for by the emergency protocol establish that the offline network cannot be used for any type of purchase, but will be strictly limited to basic goods. This measure will mainly involve grocery stores, pharmacies and gas stations, with the stated aim of safeguarding the daily supply of the resident population. The plan also provides for the introduction of specific spending limits and a maximum number of transactions allowed for each user or terminal. These limitations serve to mitigate the main risk linked to this mode of operation, namely the temporary impossibility for the merchant to verify in real time the financial coverage available on the customer's account, a control that under normal conditions takes place instantly before the approval of the digital payment.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the contingency plan for digital payments in Switzerland?
The contingency plan presented in Bern by the President of the Confederation Guy Parmelin, together with Martin Schlegel and Roland Pfister, aims to ensure the continuity of digital payments even in the event of an internet disruption. The system will allow merchants to accept payments via physical debit or credit cards by entering the pin code, operating offline and temporarily storing the data in the store system until connectivity is restored.
