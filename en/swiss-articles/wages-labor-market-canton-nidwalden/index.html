The labour market in the Canton of Nidvaldo fits into the economic framework of central Switzerland, characterized by specific leading sectors that offer diversified employment opportunities for residents and professionals. The evolution of the sectors that employ the most in this region includes sectors related to the specialized manufacturing industry, the advanced tertiary sector, tourism and administrative services. The Federal Statistical Office (FSO/SBB) and the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) regularly monitor employment trends, providing aggregated data useful for understanding the dynamics of available jobs at the cantonal level.

Operational details

Salary levels in the Canton of Nidvaldo vary considerably according to the degree of training, professional experience and sector of activity, generally aligning with the average parameters of central Switzerland. There is no federal minimum wage valid for all cantons, but some professional categories are governed by specific Collective Labour Agreements (CLAs) that establish binding minimum wages for participating or subject employers. For employees, gross pay is subject to mandatory social deductions established at the federal and cantonal levels, including contributions for Old Age and Survivors Insurance (AVS), Disability Insurance (AI) and Profit Loss Allowances (IPG), whose overall rate for the employee is 5.3%, while the total rises to 10.6% including the employer's share. To these are added unemployment contributions (AD/AC) equal to 1.1% up to the annual ceiling established, and accident insurance (LAINF/LAA) which varies between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the sector to which they belong.

Key points

For those intending to settle or work in the Canton of Nidwalden, managing practical aspects requires compliance with administrative procedures and deadlines established by federal and cantonal legislation. Regarding property rentals, regulated by the Code of Obligations (CO art. 253 et seq.) applied uniformly throughout Switzerland, the security deposit cannot exceed three months' rent and must be deposited in a blocked account in the tenant's name. Any termination of the contract by the landlord must be notified exclusively using the official form approved by the canton, and can be contested within thirty days at the competent conciliation authority.

Health aspects and operational procedures

From a healthcare perspective, residents in Switzerland are required to take out basic health insurance (LAMal/KVG) within three months of their arrival or the start of their residency. This policy is private, managed by recognized health insurers, and provides for per capita premiums calculated on a cantonal or regional basis, as well as the possibility of requesting a cantonal subsidy for premium reductions based on income. Deductibles for adults can be chosen from standard amounts provided by federal law, such as 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000, or 2500 francs. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main sectors offering employment in the Canton of Nidvaldo?
The labour market in the Canton of Nidvaldo includes opportunities in the specialised manufacturing industry, the tertiary sector, administrative services and the tourism sector. Companies often require professional qualifications certified by the Swiss system such as the Federal Certificate of Capacity (AFC).
How do mandatory social wage deductions work in Switzerland?
Compulsory contributions are levied on the gross salary including AVS/AI/IPG with an employee share of 5.3% (10.6% total with the employer), AD/AC unemployment insurance at 1.1% up to the annual ceiling and LAINF/LAA accident insurance between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the productive sector.
What are the rules for renting an apartment in the Canton of Nidvaldo?
Leases follow the Federal Bond Code. The maximum security deposit is equal to three months' rent on a restricted account in the tenant's name. The cancellation of the landlord requires the use of the official cantonal form and must be challenged within 30 days to the conciliation authority.
