In Canton Uri, compulsory health insurance (LAMal) represents one of the most significant recurring expenses for those residing in Switzerland. Unlike direct federal tax or contributi previdenziali (AVS/LPP), LAMal premiums are not a wage tax, but a private collection, calculated per capita and modulated by personal choice of deductible and insurance model.

Every resident of Switzerland is required to take out health insurance within 3 months of arrival. In the Uri Canton, as in all cantons, the premiums vary according to three essential parameters: the age group, the choice of deductible, and the coverage model. Currently, the allowable deductibles for adults are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 or 2500 per year — a choice that directly affects the amount of the monthly premium.

Operational details

Reduction of premiums: the cantonal subsidy

The reduction of LAMal premiums in the Uri Canton takes place through the cantonal subsidy , a redistribution tool that lowers the tax burden on low- or lower-middle-income households. Unlike social security contributions (AVS/AI/IPG at 5.3% for the employee), the LAMal subsidy is not automatic: those who are eligible must submit a formal application to the competent cantonal office, attaching tax documentation.

# Grant Access Criteria

The allowance is calculated based on the household's gross annual income and the number of dependants. Each canton sets its own access thresholds and coverage percentages. In Canton Uri, as in all cantons, the subsidy covers a percentage of the premium (frequently from 20% to 100%, depending on family income), drastically reducing the monthly burden.

An illustrative practical scenario: a worker with an annual income of CHF 60,000 and two dependent children could be entitled to a subsidy that reduces the monthly premium by CHF 150–200, a substantial difference on an annual basis (CHF 1,800-2,400 per year). Key points

Procedure to access premium reduction in Canton Uri

For those wishing to access premium reduction in Canton Uri, the procedure follows standardized steps and precise deadlines. First, it is necessary to verify whether you meet the cantonal income criteria by contacting the competent administration office or consulting the official cantonal portal.

Step-by-step: reduction application

1. Gather documentation: income tax return (last 2 years), salary certificates, family composition certificates, bank statements (if requested by the office). 2. Download the application form: the official form is available at the Uri Canton Health Service office or online on the cantonal portal. 3. Complete and submit: the typical deadline is late February or early March for the current fiscal year (verify each year with the office, as dates may vary). 4. Receive the decision: the cantonal administration processes the request within 4–6 weeks and notifies you of the approved subsidy amount. 5. Transmission to the insurer: the subsidy is paid directly to the health insurance fund or reimbursed to the applicant, depending on the cantonal method.

Timing and critical deadlines

In Canton Uri, as in almost all cantons, the application window typically opens in January and closes between February and March. Submitting the application after the deadline risks exclusion from the benefit for the current year. For those who change residence during the year and register with the canton, there is sometimes an opportunity for an additional application within a short period (e.g., 30–60 days) after administrative registration. Always verify with the local office the applicable time windows.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the adult allowances available in Canton Uri?
Allowable deductibles for adults are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 or 2500 per year. Each deductible carries a different monthly premium: lower deductible (CHF 300) carries higher premium but more protection; higher deductible (CHF 2500) carries lower premium but more personal risk. The choice depends on your health profile and financial resources.
How to apply for premium reduction in Canton Uri?
Submit a formal application to the Canton Uri Health Service office with: income tax return, salary certificates, family composition certificates. The typical deadline is February/March. The subsidy covers a percentage of the premium based on income. Check the exact criteria and annual expiry dates on the official cantonal portal.
What is the difference between HMO model, network and base?
Basic model: free choice of doctor/hospital, higher premiums. HMO: Obligatory attending physician, reduced costs. Network: Limited access to contracted suppliers, even lower premiums. Telemedicine: consult priority telematics, reward content. The choice impacts both the monthly premium and the practicality of accessing care and services.
When do I have to register for a health insurance fund after I arrive in Switzerland?
Within 3 months of first domicile/residence in Switzerland. If you do not sign up within the term, the insurance may refuse retroactive coverage. Always check the exact date with the cantonal administration Uri. A timely registration is crucial to avoid coverage gaps and loss of rights.
Are LAMal premiums tax deductible?
LAMal premiums are tax deductible as medical expenses or social security contributions, according to cantonal rules. In Canton Uri, tax treatment follows federal and cantonal rules. Check with your tax advisor or cantonal tax office about the precise deduction methods for your situation.
