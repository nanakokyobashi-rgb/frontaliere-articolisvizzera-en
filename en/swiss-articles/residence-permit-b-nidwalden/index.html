Residence permit B in Canton Nidvaldo: how to request it, what documents are needed, duration and renewal procedure with migration office contacts.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Permit B = renewable residence permit for foreigners
  • Requires economic stability, active LAMal, no security hurdles
  • Other than L (short duration) and C (permanent address)
  • Periodic renewal at the cantonal migration office

Key facts

  • What : Renewable, fixed-term residence permit
  • Issuer: Cantonal Migration Authority (SEM)
  • Category L: Short duration up to 1 year
  • Category C: Permanent domicile (10 years residence, 5 for EU/EFTA)
  • Renewal: According to deadlines and conditions established by the canton
  • Who can apply: Foreigners who want to live permanently in Switzerland

The B residence permit is the residence permit for foreigners who wish to live in Switzerland for a fixed and renewable period. Unlike the L permit, valid for up to one year and often linked to seasonal or short contracts, the B permit represents an intermediate category between short duration and permanent residence (C permit). It is the most widespread category among foreigners not originating in the EU/EFTA who establish residence in Swiss territory.

According to the federal regulation on foreigners (SEM), the B permit is subject to periodic renewal under the conditions established by the cantonal authorities. Operational details

General requirements for permit B

The requirements for obtaining and maintaining the B permit in Canton Nidvaldo include general criteria applied throughout Switzerland, as well as any cantonal specifications.

Economic stability : You must have sufficient income to support yourself during your stay. This may result from a stable employment contract, self-employment, investments, or other verifiable sources of income. The cantonal migration office will assess the stability of the source of income.

Health insurance (LAMal): The obligation of sottoscrivere un'assicurazione malattia privata (LAMal) within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland is imperative. This is mandatory for all residents and is premium insurance per capita — not a payroll tax or contribution deducted from the paycheck. Premiums vary by canton, region of residence and deductible level chosen (standard adult deductibles are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000, 2500, depending on your choice). The cantonal authorities also offer premium reduction subsidies for low incomes.

No obstacle to public or social security: There must be no serious criminal record, conduct that puts Swiss public security at risk, or violations of significant federal/cantonal laws.

Key points

Procedure for Renewing the B Permit

The renewal of the B permit typically takes place before the current authorization expires. The migration office of the Canton Nidwalden usually communicates the deadline for submitting the renewal request.

Phase 1: Expiration Check

Check the expiration date printed on the B permit document. The cantonal authorities usually initiate the communication process 2-3 months before the expiration date, sending a notice to the holder.Phase 2: Collection of Updated Documentation

Prepare documentation that demonstrates the maintenance of the requirements: a still valid employment contract (or updated proof of income), current residence certificate, valid health insurance, recent bank statements if required, and a certificate of no tax debts.

Phase 3: Submission of the Renewal Request

Submit the application to the migration office of the Canton Nidwalden. Many cantons accept online, postal, or in-person requests; it's best to verify the preferred channels with the office.Phase 4: Notification of the Outcome

The office examines the documentation and notifies the outcome. In the event of approval, a new B permit is issued for a further period. If the office requires clarification or additional documentation, they will contact the applicant.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between a B permit and a C permit?
Permit B is a fixed-term renewable residence permit, while permit C is a permanent residence. To progress from B to C, you must have resided in Switzerland for at least 10 consecutive years (5 years for EU/EFTA citizens), in addition to meeting the specific requirements of the canton and municipality of residence.
What happens if I let my B permit expire without renewing it?
If the B permit expires without renewal, you will lose your legal right to stay and work in Switzerland. It is important to contact the Canton Nidvaldo migration office before the deadline to start the renewal. If the deadline has already passed, you can still submit a late application, but the office will evaluate the case specifically.
Is health insurance (LAMal) mandatory for permit B?
Yes. Health insurance is mandatory for all residents of Switzerland, including B-permit holders, and must be taken out within 3 months of arrival. It's private insurance at per capita premiums, not a payroll tax. The prizes vary according to the canton, the region and the level of deductible chosen.
Can I work in Switzerland with a B permit?
Yes. Permit B entitles you to reside, work and study in Switzerland for the period of validity of the permit. A separate work permit is not required; the B permit includes the right to work in Swiss territory.
What income requirements do I have to meet to get a B permit in Canton Nidvaldo?
You need to demonstrate sufficient economic stability to support yourself. The exact amounts and specific criteria of the Nidvaldo Canton should be checked directly with the cantonal migration office, as each canton may have its own guidelines.
