Permit B Nidvaldo: guide to requirements and renewal

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 23 August 2026

Residence permit B in Canton Nidvaldo: how to request it, what documents are needed, duration and renewal procedure with migration office contacts.

Context

In brief

  • Permit B = renewable residence authorization for foreign nationals
  • Requires financial stability, active LAMal, no security obstacles
  • Different from L (short-term) and C (permanent residence)
  • Periodic renewal through the cantonal migration office

Key facts

  • What: Fixed-term and renewable residence permit
  • Issuer: Cantonal migration authority (SEM)
  • Category L: Short-term stay of up to 1 year
  • Category C: Permanent residence (10 years of residence, 5 for EU/EFTA)
  • Renewal: According to deadlines and conditions established by the canton
  • Who can apply: Foreign nationals who want to live permanently in Switzerland

The B residence permit is the residence authorization for foreign nationals who wish to live in Switzerland for a fixed period that can be renewed. Unlike permit L, which is valid for up to one year and is often linked to seasonal or short-term contracts, permit B represents an intermediate category between short-term residence and permanent residence (permit C). It is the most common category among foreign nationals from outside the EU/EFTA who establish residence in Switzerland.

Under federal regulations on foreign nationals (SEM), permit B is subject to periodic renewal according to the conditions established by the cantonal authorities. In the Canton of Nidvaldo, as in all cantons, the migration office handles applications for the issuance, renewal and revocation of residence permits. Renewability depends on maintaining specific requirements throughout the entire validity period.

The three main categories of Swiss permits

Switzerland regulates the entry and residence of foreign nationals through three main categories. Permit L is a short-term authorization, valid for up to one year, intended for temporary stays or seasonal work. Permit B allows holders to reside, work and study in Switzerland for a fixed period (the specific duration is set by the canton), with the possibility of renewal. Permit C is permanent residence, normally obtainable after 10 years of continuous residence in Switzerland (5 years for citizens of EU/EFTA states), provided that additional cantonal and municipal requirements are met.

From permit B to permanent residence

A permit B holder can progress to permit C by fulfilling the continuous-residence requirements. After residing in Switzerland for 10 consecutive years (or 5 years if an EU/EFTA citizen), it is possible to apply for an upgrade to permit C. In addition to the minimum duration, cantonal and municipal authorities require compliance with requirements specific to the place of residence: maintained financial stability, no impediments to public security, and the ability to integrate locally.

Operational details

General requirements for permit B

The requirements for obtaining and maintaining permit B in the Canton of Nidwalden include general criteria applied throughout Switzerland, in addition to any cantonal specifics.

Economic stability: It is necessary to have sufficient income to support oneself during the period of residence. This may derive from a stable employment contract, self-employment, investments, or other verifiable sources of income. The cantonal migration office will assess the stability of the source of income.

Health insurance (LAMal): The obligation to sottoscrivere un'assicurazione malattia privata (LAMal) within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland is mandatory. This is compulsory for all residents and is a per-capita premium insurance — not a payroll tax nor a contribution deducted from the payslip. Premiums vary according to the canton, region of residence and level of deductible chosen (the standard deductibles for adults are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000, 2500, according to one's choice). Cantonal authorities also offer premium reduction subsidies for low incomes.

No obstacle to public or social security: There must be no serious criminal record, conduct that endangers Swiss public security, or significant violations of federal/cantonal laws.

Suitable accommodation: Suitable housing must be available. If rented (governed by the Swiss Civil Code, art. 253 et seq., with identical federal law in every canton), the security deposit may not exceed 3 months' rent and is deposited in a blocked account in the tenant's name. Landlord terminations are valid only on an official cantonal form; challenges must be submitted within 30 days to the conciliation authority.

The documents typically required for an application for permit B vary depending on the personal situation. As a rule, the migration office of the Canton of Nidwalden requires:

  • Official application form for permit B (provided by the cantonal office)
  • Valid passport or national identity document
  • Certificate of residence or registration form for the canton's civil register
  • Proof of income (employment contract, payslips, income statement, recent bank statements)
  • Proof of accommodation (signed lease agreement, proof of ownership, or letter from the owner)
  • Certificate of good conduct (extract from the criminal records of the country of origin or previous residence)
  • Proof of enrollment in health insurance (LAMal) with a confirmation letter from the health insurer
  • If applicable, declaration of absence of debts to the tax administration

The exact documentation and specific standards of the Canton of Nidwalden should be verified directly with the cantonal migration office, as each canton may provide for variations in procedural details.

Key points

B Permit Renewal Procedure

Renewal of the B permit generally takes place before the current authorization expires. The Migration Office of the Canton of Nidwalden usually communicates the deadline by which the renewal application must be submitted.

Phase 1: Checking the expiry date Check the expiry date printed on the B permit document. The cantonal authorities usually begin the notification process 2-3 months before expiry, sending a notice to the holder.

Phase 2: Collecting updated documentation Prepare documentation demonstrating that the requirements continue to be met: a still-valid employment contract (or updated proof of income), a current residence certificate, proof of valid health insurance, recent bank statements if requested, and a certificate confirming the absence of tax debts.

Phase 3: Submitting the renewal application Submit the application to the Migration Office of the Canton of Nidwalden. Many cantons accept applications online, by regular mail or in person; it is advisable to check the office's preferred channels.

Phase 4: Notification of the outcome The office reviews the documentation and communicates the outcome. If approved, the new B permit is issued for a further fixed period. If the office requests clarification or additional documentation, it will contact the applicant.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between a B permit and a C permit?
Permit B is a fixed-term renewable residence permit, while permit C is a permanent residence. To progress from B to C, you must have resided in Switzerland for at least 10 consecutive years (5 years for EU/EFTA citizens), in addition to meeting the specific requirements of the canton and municipality of residence.
What happens if I let my B permit expire without renewing it?
If the B permit expires without renewal, you will lose your legal right to stay and work in Switzerland. It is important to contact the Canton Nidvaldo migration office before the deadline to start the renewal. If the deadline has already passed, you can still submit a late application, but the office will evaluate the case specifically.
Is health insurance (LAMal) mandatory for permit B?
Yes. Health insurance is mandatory for all residents of Switzerland, including B-permit holders, and must be taken out within 3 months of arrival. It's private insurance at per capita premiums, not a payroll tax. The prizes vary according to the canton, the region and the level of deductible chosen.
Can I work in Switzerland with a B permit?
Yes. Permit B entitles you to reside, work and study in Switzerland for the period of validity of the permit. A separate work permit is not required; the B permit includes the right to work in Swiss territory.
What income requirements do I have to meet to get a B permit in Canton Nidvaldo?
You need to demonstrate sufficient economic stability to support yourself. The exact amounts and specific criteria of the Nidvaldo Canton should be checked directly with the cantonal migration office, as each canton may have its own guidelines.
