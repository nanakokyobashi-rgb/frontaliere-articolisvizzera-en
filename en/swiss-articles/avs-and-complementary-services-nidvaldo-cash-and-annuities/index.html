The Swiss social security system rests firmly on the first pillar, represented by old-age and survivors' insurance, known as AVS. In the territory of the Canton of Nidvaldo, the application of federal and cantonal regulations passes through specific administrative bodies, including the cantonal compensation fund. This institution manages the collection of contributions and the payment of pensions, ensuring that each affiliated person receives the social security support due based on their work and contribution history. Understanding how this pillar works is essential for planning your financial future and avoiding unpleasant surprises when you retire.

Operational details

The calculation of the AVS annuity represents a crucial moment in the life of every policyholder. The amount of the pension does not depend solely on the last salary received, but is based on the individual's entire contributory career. The years of contribution completed, the average income achieved during the working years and any credits for educational tasks or assistance to family members are taken into account. In the Canton of Nidvaldo, as in the rest of Switzerland, the presence of contribution gaps can significantly reduce the amount of the monthly income paid by the compensation fund. A gap is formed whenever an individual fails to pay the prescribed minimum contributions for an entire calendar year, due to periods of uncovered unemployment, extended stays abroad, or inactivity not properly recorded.

How to fill contribution gaps

To avoid a permanent reduction of the AVS pension, Swiss legislation provides for the possibility of filling the contribution gaps within a period established by law. Once this period has elapsed, it is no longer possible to pay the contributions due for past years, resulting in a proportional reduction in the annuity. Residents of the Canton of Nidvaldo can check their contribution situation by requesting a statement of the individual account at the competent compensation fund. Key points

When the AVS pension and other personal income are not sufficient to cover essential living expenses, complementary benefits come into play. In the Canton of Nidwalden, these benefits are designed to guarantee the coverage of the vital minimum for residents who find themselves in a situation of financial need. Access to this support is not automatic, but requires the submission of a formal application to the compensation office or the designated cantonal office, attaching complete documentation regarding income, assets, rent expenses, and LAMal health insurance costs. The body then proceeds with a detailed calculation, comparing computable income with expenses recognized by law. If total expenses exceed income, the difference is bridged through the monthly payment of complementary benefits.

Procedure and support tools

To apply for complementary benefits in the Canton of Nidwalden, it is necessary to fill out the official forms provided by the cantonal administration and gather all necessary supporting documents. It is advisable to act in advance, as the processing of the application requires thorough checks on the financial and income situation of the applicant and any cohabiting family members. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the role of the clearing house in the Canton of Nidvaldo?
The cantonal compensation fund manages the registration of taxpayers, the calculation of social contributions and the disbursement of AVS annuities and supplementary benefits, applying the federal directives established by the UFAS.
How do contribution gaps affect AVS income?
Each year of missing contribution creates a gap that permanently reduces the amount of the monthly AVS annuity. These periods can only be cured within the strict time limits provided for by federal law.
Who is entitled to the complementary services at Nidvaldo?
Supplementary benefits are due to residents of the Canton of Nidvaldo whose total income, including the AVS income, does not cover the minimum vital requirement defined by current legislation.
