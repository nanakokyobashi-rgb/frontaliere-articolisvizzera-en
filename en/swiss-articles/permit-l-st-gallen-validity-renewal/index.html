St. Gallen permit: validity and extension (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 September 2026

Short-term permit L up to 1 year, renewal procedures, constraints on the employer, transformation into permit B. Practical guide.

Context

In brief

  • The short-term L permit is valid for up to 1 year in Switzerland
  • It is strictly tied to the employer specified at the time of issuance
  • Renewal is not automatic: request 2-3 months before expiration
  • Can be converted to a B residence permit after 12-24 months of regular stay

Key facts

  • What: Temporary residence permit for foreign workers
  • When: Valid for up to 1 year from the date of issuance
  • Where: Applicable in Switzerland, including the Canton of St. Gallen
  • Who: Regulated by the SEM (State Secretariat for Migration)
  • Legal basis: Federal Law on Foreign Nationals (LStrI) and cantonal ordinances
  • Renewal: Formal request before expiration, case-by-case evaluation

The short-term L permit represents the most common form of temporary stay in Switzerland for foreign workers intending to exercise a professional activity for a limited period. This permit is regulated at the federal level by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) and issued by the competent cantonal authorities, including the Canton of St. Gallen. The main feature is the maximum validity of 1 year: the document expires automatically at the end of this period if not renewed or converted into another type of permit.

A decisive aspect of the L permit is the tie to the employer. The holder can work exclusively for the employer specified at the time of issuance and for the positions declared in the application. A change of employer requires a modification request submitted jointly by the interested parties to the cantonal authorities. This tie significantly differentiates the L permit from higher category permits, such as the B (residence) permit, which allows greater professional freedom. Operational details

Conditions and formal procedure for extension

The extension of the L permit does not happen automatically. About 2-3 months before the expiry date, the permit holder and the employer must jointly submit a formal renewal application to the cantonal authorities of the Canton of St. Gallen. The documentation must include: an updated copy of the employment contract, an official statement from the employer confirming the continuation of the employment relationship and the absence of regulatory violations during the previous stay period.

The cantonal authorities will evaluate specific criteria before approving the renewal:

  • Continuity and validity of the employment contract;
  • Adequacy of the salary according to Swiss standards;
  • Persistence of the initial conditions (the position remains a priority and cannot be filled by available Swiss personnel);
  • Absence of legal violations during the previous stay (violations of labor law, administrative or criminal problems).

If all conditions remain satisfied, the L permit is renewed for an additional period, generally up to 1 year. If the renewal application is not submitted on time or is denied, the holder usually has a grace period of 30-60 days to leave Switzerland. Failure to renew exposes to administrative expulsion procedures and registration in records that make it difficult to obtain future authorizations. Key points

Step-by-step procedure for renewal in the Canton of St. Gallen

If you hold a permit L and wish to renew it, follow this ordered procedure:

Step 1: Check the expiration date. Consult your original permit L to identify the exact expiration date. Plan the renewal well in advance: it is crucial to submit the application at least 2-3 months before to avoid administrative complications.

Step 2: Notify your employer. Inform your employer of your intention to renew the permit and ask for their cooperation in preparing the documentation. The employer must formally sign the continuation of the employment relationship.

Step 3: Gather the necessary documents. Prepare:

  • Recent and signed copy of the employment contract (or official letter from the employer confirming the continuation);
  • Valid original permit L;
  • Valid identity document (passport or national identity card);
  • Recent certificate of residence (from the municipality of residence in the Canton of St. Gallen);
  • Written statement from the employer confirming: (a) continuation of the employment relationship, (b) absence of regulatory violations, (c) regular payment of salary, (d) compliance with workplace safety regulations.

Step 4: Submit the application to the competent office. Contact the migration office of the Canton of St. Gallen competent for your district of residence. Check with the office whether it is possible to submit the application online or if personal presentation is required. Some offices require a prior appointment.

Step 5: Await evaluation. The cantonal authorities, in coordination with the SEM, will examine the application. The processing time generally varies from 2 to 4 weeks. A face-to-face interview with a migration office official may be necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long is the short-term L permit valid for?
The L permit is valid for a maximum of 1 year from the date of issue. The exact duration generally corresponds to the length of the employment contract signed. If the contract ends before the end of the year, the permit automatically expires on that date. The permit is NOT automatically renewed: you must submit a formal renewal application at least 2-3 months before the expiry date.
Can I change employer during the L leave?
The L permit is closely linked to the employer specified at the time of issue. A change of employer is only possible through a permit change request, submitted jointly by the old employer, the new employer and the holder to the cantonal authorities. The authorities will assess whether the exchange is compatible with local economic conditions. If a few months of validity remain, it is also possible to apply directly for a B permit.
What is the main difference between L permit and B permit?
The L leave is temporary (maximum 1 year) and linked to a specific employer. The B permit is permanent, renewable for 5 years, and is much less tied to the individual employer, offering greater professional freedom. The transition from L to B is possible after 12-24 months of regular and continuous stay, if the economic and stability conditions are met. Permit B offers greater legal protections than permit L.
What happens if the L permit expires without renewal?
If the L permit expires without renewal, the stay becomes illegal. The data controller loses the right to reside and work legally in Switzerland and may be subject to administrative expulsion proceedings. It is not possible to renew a permit after the expiry date: the holder must leave the country and, if he wishes to return, he must re-enter a completely new application from abroad with all the related times and risks.
How do I apply for renewal of the L permit in the Canton of St. Gallen?
The application for renewal must be submitted to the competent migration office of the Canton of St. Gallen (district of residence), at least 2-3 months before the expiry of the permit. You will need: updated employment contract, original L permit, valid ID, certificate of domicile and employer statement. Check with the cantonal office whether the procedure allows online submission or requires in-person submission.
