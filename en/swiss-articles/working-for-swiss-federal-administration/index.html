Competitive advantages and flexibility

The profile of the public employer in Switzerland is distinguished by a structured offer that aims at the balance between private and professional life. The Federal Administration adopts flexible forms of work, a strategic choice that responds to the modern needs of the labour market, allowing employees to manage their tasks with greater autonomy. This approach is supported by a performance-based remuneration system, which recognizes and values the individual contribution to the achievement of institutional objectives. Professional growth does not stop at hiring: the employer constantly invests in enhancing the skills of its staff through continuous training programs. This strategy ensures that employees are always up to date on developments in the public sector and the needs of the country, while ensuring job stability that few other sectors can offer. ### Opportunity Comparison Those who evaluate a public career must consider the added value of continuing education and networking. Compared to the private sector, the Federal Administration offers a multidisciplinary environment in which the comparison between different departments is constant. Frequently Asked Questions
What types of training does the Federal Administration offer?
The Federal Administration provides about 50 different vocational training courses. These pathways are designed to grow candidates' skills, offering new career opportunities and allowing them to actively contribute to the future of Switzerland.
Are there programs for those who have just finished their studies?
Yes, the Federal Administration offers a wide range of internships specifically dedicated to recent graduate students. These programs allow you to gain valuable experience in the field, expand your network of professional contacts and make the most of your talent for the public good.
What are the benefits offered as an employer?
The institution offers several advantageous conditions, including flexible forms of work, performance-based remuneration and access to lifelong learning programmes. These offers make the Federal Administration an attractive employer for professionals in different career fields.
