Operational details

Kindergarten in Canton Uri is subject to several rules and regulations, which parents must know and comply with to ensure effective management of their family life. Among the first things to know, there is the need to submit an application for enrollment within 6 months of the child's age, as established by the federal law of June 20, 2008, which provides for free kindergarten services for children under 1 year of age.

In addition, parents must provide a certificate of vaccination and a health certificate, both valid and issued by a licensed physician. The cost of kindergarten varies depending on the child's age and length of stay, and can range from a minimum of CHF 800 per month for a child under the age of 1, to a maximum of CHF 1,200 per month for a child over the age of 3.

In case of special needs, parents can request a specialized nursery, which offers personalized services adapted to the specific needs of children. For example, the "Sunflower" kindergarten in Altdorf, Canton Uri, offers a specialized kindergarten service for children with disabilities, which guarantees personalized care and assistance.

Key points

If you need kindergarten or childcare in Canton Uri, you can contact the Department of Education and Culture of Canton Uri. You will be able to request information about the costs and options available. In addition, you will be able to inquire about cantonal subsidies available to families.

Canton Uri offers several daycare and childcare options, including public and private facilities. Public facilities are operated by Canton Uri and offer affordable childcare and daycare services. Private facilities, on the other hand, are run by private companies and offer daycare and childcare services at varying prices.

According to 2022 data, the Uri Canton has 15 kindergarten and childcare facilities, of which 10 are public and 5 private. Public facilities have a total capacity of 300 seats, while private facilities have a total capacity of 150 seats. 📊

The costs of daycare and childcare in Canton Uri vary depending on the facility and type of service offered. However, on average, public nursery costs are around CHF 1,500 per month per child, while public childcare costs are around CHF 2,000 per month. The costs of private facilities are higher and can range from CHF 2,500 to CHF 4,000 per month. 💸
