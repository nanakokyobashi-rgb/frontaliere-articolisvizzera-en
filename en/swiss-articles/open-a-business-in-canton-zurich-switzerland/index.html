Key points

To open a business in the Canton of Zurich, you need to register with the trade register. The procedure for registration in the commercial register is specified in the cantonal law. It is important to check the requirements for registration in the trade register, including the minimum capital and registration fee.

According to the cantonal law of 2020, the minimum capital to open a business in the Canton of Zurich is 100,000 Swiss francs. The registration fee in the commercial register is 2,000 Swiss francs. You can request additional information from the Chamber of Commerce or the Ministry of Economy.

The procedure for registration in the trade register is as follows:

  • Check the requirements for registration in the trade register
  • Prepare the necessary documentation, including the social contract and the tax return
  • Submit the documentation to the Chamber of Commerce or the Ministry of Economy
  • Pay the registration fee to the trade register
  • Receive registration in the trade register

Here is a concrete example: if you want to open a restaurant business in Zurich, you must register with the trade register within 30 days of the business opening date. The registration fee in the commercial register is 2,000 Swiss francs, while the minimum capital is 100,000 Swiss francs.
