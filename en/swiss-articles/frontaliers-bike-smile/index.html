Operational details

Smiles for border workers, 1.7 million for border mobility

The SMILE project, which stands for Sustainability Inclusive Mobility Along European Borders, is an ambitious initiative aimed at improving sustainable mobility along Swiss borders. This project involves the creation of safe and well-defined cycling routes that allow those who work close to the border to use the bicycle to go from home to work, crossing customs.

The SMILE project also envisages improving accessibility to railway stations, schools and rest areas, as well as awareness-raising campaigns to promote more sustainable lifestyles. In particular, the project focuses on the Graubünden region, where traffic is particularly heavy due to its proximity to Italy and Austria.

Cantons involved and amounts

The SMILE project involves the cantons of Graubünden, Ticino and Urni, which have allocated an amount of 1.7 million Swiss francs to finance the construction of cycle paths and rest areas. This amount will be used for the construction of about 10 km of cycle paths, as well as for the creation of parking areas and parking spaces for bicycles.

The SMILE project aims to improve cross-border mobility between Italy and Switzerland, in particular for citizens of the cantons of Lugano and Tessino. According to data from 2022, about 200,000 people cross the border between Italy and Switzerland every day to reach work, school or for reasons of care. The Cross-border Action Plan foresees the creation of a common information system for cross-border mobility, which will include information on road traffic, safety and accident prevention.
