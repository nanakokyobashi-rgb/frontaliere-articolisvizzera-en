Migros egg recall: suspected salmonella contamination (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 22 August 2026

Migros recalls Nostrani del Ticino eggs, code 1-CH-603, for salmonella risk. Here are the refund procedures for consumers.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Precautionary call for Nostrani eggs from Ticino
  • Suspected salmonella contamination
  • Product code concerned 1-CH-603
  • Migros invites you not to consume eggs

Key facts

  • What: Precautionary food recall
  • When: August 21, 2026
  • Where: Ticino, at Migros branches
  • Who: Migros on behalf of Uova Ticino SA
  • Product: Eggs from Ticino
  • Code: 1-CH-603
  • Risk: Suspected Salmonella contamination

The recall of food products from the Swiss market represents a standard safety procedure managed by the authorities and distributors to protect consumers. On 21 August 2026, the Migros distribution chain, acting on behalf of the supplier Uova Ticino SA, issued an official notice relating to eggs marketed under the "Nostrani del Ticino" brand. The decision was made following a finding of suspected contamination with salmonella, a bacterium that can cause serious gastrointestinal disorders. The specific product that is the subject of the alert is identified by the code 1-CH-603. The official communication underlines that, although this is a suspicion, a risk to consumers' health cannot be completely excluded. As a result, Migros management strongly urged all customers to refrain from consuming the eggs belonging to the indicated batch. Operational details

Security and risk management procedures

The management of a food recall in Switzerland follows well-defined protocols involving both food business operators and supervisory authorities. When a distributor such as Migros identifies a potential risk, as in the case of Nostrani del Ticino eggs, an immediate withdrawal procedure from the shelves is activated, accompanied by an information campaign aimed at consumers. This process is not only a commercial choice, but an obligation deriving from the strict regulations in force on food safety. The primary objective is to isolate the product from the market before it can be ingested, thus reducing the likelihood of disease onset. Salmonellosis, in fact, is one of the most common toxins transmitted through contaminated food, characterised by symptoms such as nausea, diarrhoea and headache. For those who have already consumed the product, the indications of the health authorities are clear: in case of onset of symptoms, it is necessary to immediately consult a doctor to receive the appropriate treatment. The reimbursement system managed by Migros allows customers to return the affected packages to any branch, guaranteeing the return of the sales price paid. Frequently Asked Questions
Which eggs are affected by the booster?
The recall specifically concerns eggs marketed under the 'Nostrani del Ticino' brand, identified by batch code 1-CH-603. The Migros distribution chain, which issued the warning on behalf of the supplier Uova Ticino SA on 21 August 2026, invites customers not to consume the product at all due to suspected contamination with salmonella, a bacterium that can cause gastrointestinal disorders.
What should I do if I bought these eggs?
If you have a package with the code 1-CH-603, do not consume it and do not try to cook it to eliminate the risk. You must return the entire packaging, even if partially used, to any Migros branch. The staff will refund the purchase price without requesting the receipt. For purchases made through the Migros Online digital channel, customer service will directly contact the users concerned to manage the refund process.
Is there a consumer support number?
Yes, if you have any questions or require further technical details regarding this food recall, you can contact the dedicated toll-free number 0800 84 08 48. The service is active to provide specific assistance and clarification to consumers regarding the withdrawal procedure from the market and the expected reimbursement methods.
