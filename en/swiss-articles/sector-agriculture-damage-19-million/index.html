Lead

Drought is hitting Swiss farmers hard: the Swiss Hail insurance, which protects the sector in case of weather adversity, forecasts damages of at least 19 million francs, with an upward trend.

The drought is causing significant damage to summer crops, such as potatoes, corn, sugar beets and sunflowers. Most of these crops have not yet been harvested, so it is not possible to quantify the overall extent of the damage. However, for some farms, the losses will be large.
