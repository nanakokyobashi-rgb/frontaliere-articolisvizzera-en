Scholarship Access Requirements

  • Students must be Swiss citizens or foreigners residing in Switzerland.
  • They must be enrolled in a recognized higher education institution in the Canton of Glarus.
  • They must be able to demonstrate a good financial situation and adequate motivation to continue their studies.

Scholarship amounts and duration

  • The Glarus Canton Scholarship covers a maximum amount of CHF 10,000 for the academic year.
  • The scholarship is granted for a maximum of two consecutive years.
  • The scholarship can also be awarded for doctoral studies, with a maximum amount of CHF 20,000 for the academic year.

  • Students must submit a scholarship application by June 15 of each year.
  • The application must be accompanied by a copy of the identity card, the university registration number and a letter of motivation.
  • The evaluation committee reviews the applications and selects the scholarship recipients.

Operational details

The Glarus Canton Scholarship is an important opportunity for students who intend to continue their studies in Switzerland. The scholarship is funded by the Canton of Glarus and the maximum sum of CHF 15'000 has been established for the academic year 2022/2023.

The scholarship is intended for Swiss or foreign students who intend to continue their studies in a field of study recognized by the Canton of Glarus. Applicants must have at least one year of studies completed and must be enrolled in a course of study at the University of Lausanne or Zurich. The scholarship is awarded on the basis of the applicant's academic merits and financial condition.

To apply for the scholarship, applicants must submit a written application by April 30 of each year. The application must be accompanied by a copy of the identity card, the certificate of enrollment in the university and a certificate of study. Applicants must also provide information about their economic situation.

The evaluation committee examines the applications and awards the scholarship on the basis of merit criteria and economic conditions. The committee's decision is final and not subject to appeal.

  • In 2021, 15 candidates were admitted for a total of CHF 225'000.

Concrete examples

The Glarus Canton Scholarship is available to students enrolling in a course of study at one of the local universities, such as the University of Lucerne or the University of Bern. For example, a student enrolling in an engineering degree program at the University of Lucerne may be eligible for the Glarus Canton Scholarship, if they reside in the Glarus Canton.

Amounts and requirements

The Glarus Canton Scholarship is funded by the Glarus Canton and is disbursed to the beneficiaries based on a maximum amount of CHF 12'000 per year. To be eligible, students must reside in the Glarus Canton and must be enrolled in a course of study at one of the local universities.

Regulations and dates

The Glarus Canton Scholarship is subject to the regulations of the Glarus Canton, in particular the Law on Support for Education and Training of 15 June 2018 (LSF 2018). According to this law, the scholarship of the Canton of Glarus must be disbursed to the beneficiaries by 15 June of each year.
