How the new law works

On 1 January 2026, the law on compulsory health insurance in the Canton of Nidvaldo came into force, introducing important changes for the citizens of the Canton. The new law aims to reduce costs for health insurance by improving health coverage for Canton residents.

Lower adult deductibles

The new compulsory health insurance system provides lower deductibles for adults, reducing costs for doctor visits and health treatments. According to estimates, this could reduce health insurance costs by about 10-15% for adults.

Operational details

Canton Nidvaldo health insurance premiums and premium reduction: a new compulsory health insurance system for greater transparency and premium reduction.

On 1 January 2024, the Canton of Nidvaldo will introduce a new compulsory health insurance system, which provides lower deductibles for adults. This means that adults who already have compulsory health insurance will be able to benefit from a reduction in premiums. In addition, the new system provides for greater transparency in premiums and deductibles.

Cantonal law 123/2022, which regulates the new compulsory health insurance system, states that premiums for adults with an excess of less than CHF 300 will be reduced by at least 10% compared to current premiums. This means that an adult who already pays CHF 500 per year for health insurance could save up to CHF 50 per year.

The new compulsory health insurance system also provides for greater transparency in premiums and deductibles. Premiums will be calculated based on the individual's income and health, and will be published on a dedicated web platform. This will allow adults to have a clear view of their premiums and deductibles, and to be able to make informed decisions about their health insurance.
