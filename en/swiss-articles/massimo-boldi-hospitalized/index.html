Italian legislation

Italian law provides that patients must be transferred to a nearby hospital if an accident occurs. In this case, the actor was transferred from the Versilia hospital in Lido di Camaiore to the hospital in Livorno.

The situation in Switzerland

In Switzerland, the situation is similar to that in Italy. If a patient is in an accident, doctors must decide whether to transfer them to a nearby hospital or not. Depending on the severity of the case, the patient may be transferred to a closer hospital or a specialised hospital.

In Switzerland, border workers are entitled to receive medical treatment in the event of an emergency, as established by the 1982 Federal Act on Social Security (LSS). However, it is important to note that the conditions of access to medical care may vary depending on the canton in which the border worker is located.

For example, in the canton of Geneva, border workers can access medical care in the event of an emergency, but must submit an application for health insurance enrollment within 3 months of the date of entry into the canton. In contrast, in the canton of Lausanne, frontier workers can access medical care only if they have health insurance that covers medical expenses in Switzerland.
