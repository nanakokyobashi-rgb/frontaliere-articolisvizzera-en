Alliance Care is ceasing its activities and initiating bankruptcy proceedings. In Bern, the nursing council and the assembly of delegates adopted the decision after an in-depth discussion.

The organization cites financial difficulties as the cause of the closure. Financial results were below expectations, and the measures launched in April 2026 to stabilize the accounts did not make it possible to ensure continuity of operations.

A crisis that emerged in stages

In June, the first signs of the crisis had already emerged, with 17 of Alliance Care's 91 employees being laid off. Associazione svizzera infermieri, which promoted the project, had sought to provide financial support to the new organization through several loans. Despite this, the financial problems had not been resolved.

The failure to increase membership was another factor. Growth in new members was one of the foundations of the association's development prospects, but membership did not reach the hoped-for levels. Alliance Care thus became unable to ensure long-term financial stability. The difficulties affected both the accounts and the expected membership growth.

An investigation published last July by the newspapers of the Tamedia group had shown that membership was significantly below forecasts. This was part of the series of difficulties that culminated in today's decision.

Founded in June 2025, Alliance Care aimed to bring together in a single organization all professionals in the care sector, including healthcare workers without a nursing diploma. Just over a year after its founding, the representation project is closing with the initiation of bankruptcy proceedings.
