Key facts

  • What: Foreign driver's license conversion in Switzerland

The regularisation of one's motoring position in the Canton of Bern is a fundamental step for anyone who moves to the Swiss territory. According to the federal provisions in force, anyone who establishes his residence in Switzerland is obliged to convert his license to drive abroad within a maximum period of 12 months from the time of registration. The competent authority to manage this procedure in Bern is the Road Traffic Office, which is responsible for verifying the suitability for driving and the validity of foreign licenses presented.

Key points

To initiate the conversion process, the resident must follow a well-defined procedure that ensures the correct transmission of data to the Road Traffic Office. The first step is to go to your municipality of residence for registration, a document necessary to prove the start date of residency. Subsequently, you must download the dedicated form from the official website of the Bernese Road Traffic Office. Ensure you have your original driving license, a valid identity document, and your residence permit (permit B or C) issued by the competent authorities. In case of doubts regarding the documentation, it is useful to contact the offices of the competent authority directly to avoid delays in processing the application.

Operational steps

1. Dossier preparation: collect the foreign driving license, passport-sized photo, medical certificate (if required by the form), and a copy of the residence permit. 2. Form completion: enter personal data and information regarding the foreign license held. 3. Submission or delivery: forward the dossier to the Road Traffic Office of the Canton of Bern. 4. Receipt of the license: after verification and any necessary control test, the license is sent by mail to the indicated address.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long do I have to convert my foreign driver's license in the Canton of Bern?
Federal law states that the conversion must take place within 12 months from the date of notification of your residence in Switzerland. After this period, the foreign license is no longer considered valid to circulate in the Swiss territory.
Do I need to take an exam to convert my driver's license?
Not always. The need to take a control examination, theoretical or practical, depends on the country of issue of the original licence and the current recognition provisions between Switzerland and the State of origin. The Bernese Circulation Office will inform the applicant after the analysis of the dossier.
What documents are required for the procedure?
You must submit the completed official application form, a valid original driving licence, a recent photograph in card format, an identity document and a Swiss residence permit. In some cases, a medical certificate is also required.
