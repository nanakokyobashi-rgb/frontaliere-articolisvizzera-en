Key facts

The KOF Barometer is an economic indicator that anticipates the evolution of the Swiss economy. The July figure is higher than expected and confirms a level above the multi-year average. The parameter stood at 103.5 points, 1.4 points more than in June.

The KOF Barometer is composed of several data, among which it currently includes hundreds of sub-indicators. It is presented monthly by the Center for Economic Research of the Zurich Polytechnic and anticipates the evolution of the economy. It also publishes quarterly its own economic forecasts on the Swiss and international economic situation, as well as a survey of economists (Consensus).

The KOF Barometer is an important indicator for understanding the economic situation in Switzerland and for making informed decisions. For example, data from the KOF Barometer can help Zurich entrepreneurs evaluate the opportunity to invest in new projects or expand their business.
