To work as a custodian in Ticino, a person must have a fixed-term or permanent employment contract with a Swiss employer. The contract must last at least 12 months and include a guaranteed minimum wage of CHF 4,500 per month, as established by the Federal Labour Act of 24 March 1943 (LFP, RS 822.11). The employer must also ensure that the custodian has sickness and disability insurance.

G permit

The G permit is a residence permit that allows cross-border workers to work as custodians in Ticino. It is issued by the Immigration and Integration Section of the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDFA) for a period of 12 months. To obtain it, an applicant must submit an application to the FDFA and provide identity, residence and employment documents. The G permit costs CHF 140 for cross-border workers and CHF 280 for non-cross-border workers.
