Requirements to work as a custodian in Ticino

To work as a custodian in Ticino, you need to have a fixed-term or permanent employment contract with a Swiss employer. The contract must be concluded for at least 12 months and must include the payment of a guaranteed minimum wage of CHF 4,500 per month, as established by the Federal Labour Law of 24 March 1943 (LFP, RS 822.11). In addition, the employer must ensure that the caretaker has sickness insurance and disability insurance.

Permission G

The G permit is a residence permit that allows border workers to work as a custodian in Ticino. The G permit is issued by the Immigration and Integration Section of the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDFA) for a period of 12 months. To obtain the G permit, you must submit an application to the FDFA and provide identity, residence and work documents. The G permit costs CHF 140 for border workers and CHF 280 for non-frontier workers.
