Frequently Asked Questions
What documents are required to request the aggregation of AVS and INPS contributions?
To request the aggregation of AVS and INPS contributions, you must submit work certificates attesting to the contribution periods, identity documents and application forms specific to AVS and INPS. It is advisable to contact the competent offices for assistance in filling out the forms and submitting the documentation.
How long does it take to apply for a pension in Switzerland and Italy?
The timing of the pension application varies between Switzerland and Italy. In Switzerland, the application can be submitted a few months before reaching retirement age, while in Italy the times can be different. It is important to inquire with the social security institutions of both countries to know the precise deadlines and the documents necessary for the application.
