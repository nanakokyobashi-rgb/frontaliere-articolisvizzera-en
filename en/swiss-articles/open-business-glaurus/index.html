Legal form

The Canton of Glarus allows you to choose different legal forms for commercial activities, including:

  • Limited liability company (SRL)
  • Limited liability company (SRLI)
  • Joint stock company (SA)
  • Cooperative society (SC)

Minimum capital

The minimum capital required to open a business in the Canton of Glarus is CHF 10,000. You can increase the minimum capital up to CHF 50,000.
