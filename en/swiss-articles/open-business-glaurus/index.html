The Canton of Glarus requires registration in the commercial register to open a business. It is necessary to choose the legal form, determine the minimum capital, pay the registration fees and fulfill the insurance obligations. The commercial register is administered by the Canton of Glarus.

Choosing the legal form

When deciding to open a business in the Canton of Glarus, it is necessary to choose the legal form best suited to one's needs. The most common legal forms are:

  • Limited liability company (LLC)
  • Joint-stock company (JSC)
  • Cooperative company (CC)

Each of these legal forms has its own characteristics and obligations, so it is important to choose the one that best suits your needs.

Registration in the commercial register

Registration in the commercial register is a mandatory step for opening a business in the Canton of Glarus. It is necessary to submit an application for registration, accompanied by a series of documents and information relating to the business.

The documents required for registration in the commercial register include:

  • Company's articles of association
  • Deed of incorporation
  • Identity documents of the partners
  • Identity documents of the directors
  • Information relating to the business's registered office

You can consult the website of the Canton of Glarus for up-to-date information on the registration process.

Minimum capital

The minimum capital required to open a business in the Canton of Glarus varies depending on the legal form chosen. For example, for a limited liability company, the minimum capital is 20.000 Swiss francs. For a joint-stock company, the minimum capital is 50.000 Swiss francs.

Registration fees

The registration fees for opening a business in the Canton of Glarus vary depending on the legal form chosen. For example, for a limited liability company, the registration fee is 500 Swiss francs. For a joint-stock company, the registration fee is 1.000 Swiss francs.

Insurance obligations

The insurance obligations for opening a business in the Canton of Glarus vary depending on the legal form chosen. For example, for a limited liability company, it is necessary to insure the civil liability of the partners and directors. For a joint-stock company, it is necessary to insure the civil liability of the partners and directors, as well as the professional liability of the directors.

Insurance obligations

The Canton of Glarus requires commercial businesses to have liability insurance. You can purchase liability insurance from a Swiss insurance company.
