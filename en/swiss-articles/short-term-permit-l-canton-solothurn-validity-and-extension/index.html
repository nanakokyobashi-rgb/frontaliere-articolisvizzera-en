Validity of short-term permit L

The short-term L permit is a type of residence permit granted by the Canton of Solothurn and other Swiss cantons, which allows foreign nationals to stay in the country for a limited period of time. The validity of this permit is for a maximum of 1 year, starting from the date of issue.

Conditions for the extension of the short-term L permit

To obtain the extension of the short-term L permit, you must submit a request to the Immigration Service (SEM) of the Canton of Solothurn. The application must be submitted within 3 months of the expiry of the current permit. It is important to note that the extension can only be granted if the stay of the foreign national is still compatible with the conditions of the short-term L permit.

Concrete examples of extension of short-term permit L

For example, a foreign national with a short-term L permit issued for a 6-month stay in Canton Solothurn could apply for the extension for a further 6-month period. In this case, the total stay of the foreign national would be 12 months, which is still compatible with the conditions of the short-term L permit.

# When Short Term L Permit applies

The short-term L permit is granted to foreign nationals who wish to stay in Switzerland for work, study or other legitimate purposes. This permit is granted for a period of one year and may be extended for a further year.

How the extension of the short-term L permit works

The extension of the short-term L permit is managed by the Immigration Service (SEM) of the Canton of Solothurn. Foreign nationals wishing to extend their permit must submit an application to the SEM within 30 days prior to the expiry of the permit. The application must be accompanied by all necessary documents, such as work or study documentation, and payment of the extension fee.

Practical analysis

The short-term L permit is useful for border workers who must work or study in the Canton of Solothurn. It is important to submit the extension request in time so as not to lose the validity of the permit.

The short-term L permit is regulated by article 14 of the Federal Law on Foreigners (LFA) of 16 December 2008, which states that border workers can obtain a short-term residence permit for work or study reasons. The L permit is valid for a maximum of 120 days within a 180-day period.

To apply for the L permit, border workers must submit a written application to the Immigration Office of the Canton of Solothurn. The application must be accompanied by documents proving their identity, their border status and their reason for staying in the Canton of Solothurn. The required documents may vary depending on the specific case, but typically include:

  • A valid passport
  • A copy of the birth certificate
  • A copy of the marriage certificate (if applicable)
  • A letter of acceptance from an employer or an educational institution
  • A health insurance certificate

Once the application is submitted, the Immigration Office of the Canton of Solothurn will evaluate the application and may request further documents or clarifications. If the request is accepted, the L permit will be issued to the border worker.
