The Canton of Lucerne has implemented the compensation fund system to ensure that all citizens can access supplementary benefits, regardless of their living conditions. This system is based on the principle of solidarity and fairness, where taxpayers pay a fixed monthly amount that is used to finance supplementary benefits.

Under the Supplementary Benefits Act 2015, the taxpayer must pay a monthly amount of CHF 450.00. This amount may vary depending on the taxpayer's living conditions, such as the number of dependent children or the presence of a disability. The law also states that taxpayers who have an income of less than CHF 20,000.00 per year may request a reduction in the monthly amount.
