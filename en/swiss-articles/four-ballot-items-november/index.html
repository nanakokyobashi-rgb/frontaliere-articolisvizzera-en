November 29 vote: VAT, OASI and four proposals

On November 29, voters will vote on four proposals: an increase in VAT for the 13th AHV pension, two popular initiatives, and an amendment to the law on war materiel.

BERN – On November 29, voters will return to the polls to vote on four new ballot measures. The ballot includes an issue linking the VAT increase to the 13th AVS pension, two popular initiatives, and a legislative amendment.

The first item is described as the VAT increase for the 13th AVS pension. The report does not give the amount of the increase or any other economic parameters: the available information is the explicit link between VAT and the 13th pension.

The two popular initiatives follow. Their titles distinguish the topics: «For a limitation on fireworks» on the one hand and «Yes to fair federal taxes for spouses too» on the other. These are the wordings used in the presentation of the questions; the text adds no details about the effects or the positions for and against.

The fourth item concerns an amendment to the Federal Act on War Material. Here too, the report does not describe which articles of the law would be amended or what the concrete effect would be. It does, however, clearly state that the issue will be part of the same electoral event.

Four topics on the same ballot

The overview presents voters with four separate choices: one on VAT linked to the 13th AVS pension, one on limiting fireworks, one on federal taxes for spouses, and one on the Federal Act on War Material. Two items are explicitly described as popular initiatives; the others concern the VAT increase for the AVS pension and an amendment to a federal law. The report does not attribute positions to parties, authorities or committees and does not anticipate the outcome. It does not give percentages, recommendations or results of previous consultations.

Alongside the report on the vote, readers are directly invited to say whether they already have an opinion and whether they know how they will vote by taking part in the new Tamedia survey. The initiative is described as quick: just a few minutes and a few clicks are enough. The survey is the action proposed by the report to gather the public's intentions on the four topics.

The words used already indicate four directions: increase, limitation, taxes and amendment. The reference to spouses also appears in the title of the second initiative.

Where the text stops

The informational limit is clear: the text does not say what the amount of the VAT increase would be, does not quantify the effect on the 13th AHV pension, does not specify what type of limitation of fireworks is being proposed, and does not explain the tax measure intended for spouses. Nor does it explain the content of the amendment to the law on war materiel. These details cannot be derived from the list of items alone.

The practical takeaway for anyone who needs to find their bearings is therefore the scope of the choice: four items, four titles and a common date. The news item makes it possible to know on which topics to express an opinion, not to calculate amounts or individual effects. Adding numbers or consequences that are not present would change the news item rather than rewrite it.

Ask yourself whether you already have an opinion on the issues and whether you know how you will vote: these are the two questions posed to readers. 4.
