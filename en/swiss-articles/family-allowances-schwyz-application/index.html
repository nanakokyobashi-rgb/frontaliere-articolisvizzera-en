Regulatory framework for family allowances in Switzerland

Family allowances in Switzerland are regulated at the federal level by the law on family allowances, which establishes the common principles valid in all cantons, leaving the cantons themselves the right to provide more generous benefits and to regulate the methods of implementation. Amounts, dependent child criterion and cantonal peculiarities

The source does not report precise amounts for family allowances in the canton of Svitto or specific income thresholds for the definition of dependent child: for the values in force, it is necessary to consult directly the competent family compensation fund or the responsible cantonal office, which publish the updated amounts annually. However, the structure of the benefit remains that defined by federal law, which provides for a child allowance and a higher training allowance for children in training over the age of 16.

The "dependent child" criterion is not based on specific income thresholds, but on objective elements provided for by law: the status of the child, the age and the condition of training. In particular, the right to the training allowance extends as long as the child continues training, generally within an age limit defined by federal law. Any additional conditions, such as the obligation to live together or the limits for children who exercise a gainful activity, are governed by the cantonal implementing provisions and must be verified on a case-by-case basis at the competent fund.

Step-by-step procedure to claim family allowances in Canton Schwyz

1. Employer verification: when starting a new employment relationship, the employee must check that the employer is affiliated with a recognised family compensation fund in Switzerland. This information is usually shown on the payslip or in the employment contract. 2. Gathering the documentation: the employee prepares the civil-status documents for the children (family record, birth certificate, and, where applicable, a training certificate for dependent children over 18 who are still in education). For children in education over the age of 16, confirmation from the school is required. 3. Submitting the application: the application is submitted to the employer, who forwards it to the designated family compensation fund. For self-employed workers, the application must be sent directly to the competent family compensation fund in Canton Schwyz. 4. Verification and payment: the fund checks the eligibility requirements and, if the outcome is positive, communicates the amount due. Payment is normally made through the employer, together with the monthly salary. 5. Periodic updates: in the event of significant changes (change in the number of children, switch from child allowance to education allowance, termination of entitlement), the employee must promptly inform the employer so that the file can be updated.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who can receive family allowances in the canton of Switzerland?
In Canton Svitto, family allowances may be paid to employees and self-employed persons subject to the AVS contributory obligation, as well as to persons without gainful activity who pay AVS contributions, provided that the income from gainful activity does not exceed certain federally defined thresholds. The right exists as long as the child is considered dependent and meets the personal criteria provided for by law.
How do I apply for a family allowance in the canton of Schwyz?
The application is submitted to your employer, who transmits it to the designated family compensation fund, using the forms provided and attaching the personal documentation required for the identification of the children. For self-employed workers, the application must be forwarded directly to the competent compensation fund in the canton of Schwyz.
What is the difference between child allowance and training allowance?
The Federal Act on Family Allowances distinguishes between two main categories: the child allowance, which is paid for every child up to the age of 16, and the education allowance, which is intended for children who continue their education beyond that age, usually until they reach the age of 25. The education allowance is generally higher than the child allowance.
How is the family allowance system funded?
The system is mainly financed by employers, who are required to join a recognized family compensation fund and pay the contributions required by law, and, to a lesser extent, by self-employed workers. Contributions paid to family coffers do not replace AVS/AI/IPG, but are in addition to them.
Where to find the updated amounts of family allowances in the canton of Schwyz?
The source does not report precise amounts for family allowances in the Canton of Schwyz. In order to know the amounts actually in force, the updated forms and the list of recognized funds, the operational reference is the family compensation fund designated by the employer or the competent cantonal office.
