Requirements to work as a beautician in Ticino

To work as a beautician in Ticino, frontier workers must meet certain requirements. First, they must hold a diploma in beautician issued by a recognized training institution in Switzerland or abroad. In addition, they must have a good knowledge of the Italian language, as it is the official language of the Canton of Ticino.

# Border Work Permit

Border workers wishing to work as a beautician in Ticino must apply for a work permit. This permit is necessary to be able to work legally in Switzerland. The application for a work permit must be submitted to the Swiss Consulate or the Immigration Office of the Canton of Ticino. The cost of the work permit is CHF 150 for the application and CHF 50 for the validation.

Requirements to work as a beautician in Ticino

To work as a beautician in Ticino, you must have a qualification recognised in Switzerland. This means that you must have completed a training course in a related field, such as aesthetics, hygiene and safety, or a similar discipline. The qualification must be recognised by the Canton of Ticino or another competent authority in Switzerland.

Permission G

The G permit is required to work in Switzerland as a border worker. The G permit is a document that confirms your border status and allows you to work in Switzerland. To obtain the G permit, you must submit an application to the immigration office of your canton of residence. You will be required to provide documents such as your identity card, a birth certificate, a marriage certificate (if applicable) and a recognised certificate of study.
