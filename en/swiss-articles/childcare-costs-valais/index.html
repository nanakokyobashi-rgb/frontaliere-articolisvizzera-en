In the Canton of Valais, the issue of child care costs revolves around three solutions: daycare centers, family daycare providers, and after-school programs. The table presents them together because families’ concerns extend beyond the mere availability of a service to include how rates are determined.

Income Is the Key Criteria

The rule for Valais is clear: fees are calculated based on income. Therefore, there is no single flat rate applied indiscriminately to those seeking a daycare center, a family daycare provider, or an after-school program. The first factor to focus on when comparing options is the income used to calculate the fee.

In addition to the income-based fee, the table lists available cantonal subsidies. However, this support should not be treated as a standard amount or an identical reduction for everyone: the availability of subsidies must be assessed based on the specific situation. When evaluating costs, it is therefore essential to distinguish between two elements: the service fee and any cantonal support.

For those living in the Canton of Valais, this approach shifts the focus from a simple question about price to a more detailed assessment. It is necessary to identify the type of childcare, inquire how the income criterion is applied, and determine which cantonal procedure allows for verifying the subsidy. The three options remain the starting point, while income and cantonal support are the two economic factors that must be considered together.

The asilo nido guide can be used as an initial reference to organize the comparison, without confusing the availability of a subsidy with a predetermined amount.

Operational details

The cost of childcare should be viewed as a line item in the family budget, but without mixing it with other components of the cost of living in Switzerland. The service fee and the cantonal subsidy are part of the childcare cost comparison; premiums, contributions, and taxes, on the other hand, follow separate mechanisms.

Key points

To turn this overview into a practical decision, the evaluation can follow a brief process. The goal is not to estimate a theoretical price, but to compare information gathered using the same criteria.

Four Steps Before Making a Choice

1. Define the option to be compared. Specify whether the search concerns a daycare center, a foster family, or an after-school program. These three categories constitute the scope of the comparison in the Canton of Valais. 2. Inquire about the fee calculation method. The key question is how the cost is calculated based on income. Note the specific income figure used, so as not to compare a rate based on a different criterion. 3. Verify cantonal subsidies. After identifying the service, inquire at the cantonal level about which subsidies are available for childcare. Do not assign a standard amount to the subsidy before verifying the details. 4. Separate budget items. Keep the childcare portion distinct from other Swiss expenses, including premiums, contributions, and taxes. The final comparison must show the fee and support separately.

This sequence of steps is useful because it prevents you from starting with an isolated figure. First, determine the subject; then the economic criterion; next, the subsidy; and finally, the overall budget. If the reference income changes, the fee calculation is the item that needs to be rechecked; there is no need to replace it with an average amount unrelated to the situation at hand.

For Valais, no fixed amounts or operational deadlines are specified in the table. It is therefore correct to work with a comparison sheet and leave the fields that must be verified at the cantonal level blank, rather than filling them with generic numbers. This caution makes the result more useful even for those living in Switzerland who need to factor child care into their cost of living.
