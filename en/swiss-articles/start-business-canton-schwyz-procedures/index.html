Complete guide to starting a business in the canton of Switzerland: legal forms, registration in the commercial register, minimum capital, tax and social security obligations.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Legal forms: single company, SNC, SA, SARL/GmbH, association
  • Commercial register: mandatory federal registration managed cantonally
  • Three tax tiers: Federal IFD, Cantonal Taxes, Municipal Taxes
  • Social security: AVS/AI mandatory, LPP optional, LAMal mandatory

Key facts

  • What: Business registration in the Swiss trade register
  • When: Before starting self-employment
  • Where: Office of the Registrar of Commerce of the Canton of Switzerland
  • Who: Entrepreneurs, sole proprietorships, joint-stock companies
  • Tax levels: Federal (DFI + VAT), cantonal, municipal
  • AVS/AI contributions: 9.8% of net income for self-employed
  • Minimum capital: CHF 100,000 for SA (CHF20,000 paid-in), CHF 20,000 for SARL

In the canton of Swabia, as in every Swiss canton, anyone wishing to engage in an independent economic activity must register with the commercial register. This is a federal rule implemented at the cantonal level: the register is centralized at the competent office of the canton, where the data of all companies active in the territory converge.

Registration in the commercial register is not just an administrative formality: it represents the moment when the company acquires civil rights, allows you to open a company bank account and — crucially — activates tax and social security obligations. This is why it is essential to register BEFORE starting the activity, not after.

Operational details

The three-tier tax system

The most important element in running a Swiss business is understanding the three-tier tax system. The Swiss Confederation applies direct federal tax (DFI) on the net income of the company. Each canton adds a cantonal income and profit tax. Each municipality, in turn, applies a municipal tax with a multiplier applied to the cantonal rate. The result is that the tax burden varies significantly between cantons and municipalities.

In the canton of Svitto, as in all cantons, the cantonal administration sets the cantonal rate; the municipalities then establish their own multiplier. This means that two identical companies could pay very different taxes depending on where they are located. It is therefore essential to check the specific rates at the cantonal tax office and at the municipality where you intend to locate the registered office.

Value added tax (VAT) is managed at the federal level by the Federal Administration of Customs (AFC/ESTV). A business must register for VAT if it exceeds CHF 100,000 in annual turnover. The ordinary rate is 8.1%. Registering for VAT entitles you to the deduction of taxes paid on goods and services purchased (accounting savings), but requires more complex quarterly statements and annual returns.

Procedure step-by-step: from idea to registration

To start a business in the canton of Schwyz, you must follow a precise procedure. Here are the essential steps.

Step 1: Choose the legal form. Consult a notary, accountant or the cantonal commercial office to understand which form is most suitable. The choice depends on available capital, number of partners, and risk profile. If you are unsure, speak with a professional.

Step 2: Register with the commercial register. Complete the registration form and deposit it with the Schwyz Commercial Register Office (decentralized offices, if present, accept registrations). It is mandatory to register BEFORE starting the activity. Registration creates your federal business identity (IDE number, Business Identification Number).

Step 3: Open a business bank account. Once registered with the commercial register, open a business bank account in the company's name. Banks in Switzerland require a copy of the commercial register as proof of the company's existence.

Step 4: Register with the AVS. Go to the competent municipal office (the municipality where you reside if you are self-employed; the municipality of the legal seat if you are a company) and register with the AVS. You will receive a business AVS number and will receive notifications on the contributions due.

Step 5: Sign the health insurance (LAMal). Contact a health insurance fund within 3 months of the first day of activity. You can choose from various deductible and insurance models. Do not wait: the obligation is immediate, and the lack of coverage exposes you to penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between a sole proprietorship and a SARL in the Canton of Switzerland?
A single company has unlimited liability: personal assets are liable for company debts. A SARL (limited liability company) has limited liability to paid-in capital. The SARL requires CHF 20,000 of minimum capital and more administrative requirements (annual balance sheet, registers), but offers asset protection. The choice depends on the expected size of the company and the risk profile.
How much does it cost to register with the commercial register in the Canton of Schwyz?
Registration costs vary by canton and by legal form. Please contact the Commercial Register Office of the Canton of Schwyz directly for current rates. Generally, registration involves a one-off fee of a few hundred francs, which varies depending on the type of company.
Do I have to register for VAT immediately after registering?
VAT registration is mandatory only if the annual turnover exceeds CHF 100,000. You can also voluntarily register if you stay below this threshold (to deduct VAT on costs), but it is not mandatory. If you plan to exceed CHF100,000, register with the Federal Administration of Customs (AFC/ESTV) within the first month of operation.
Is social security (AVS, LPP, insurance) mandatory for an independent?
AVS (old age and disability) is compulsory for all, with contributions at 9.8% of net income. Health insurance (LAMal) is mandatory by federal law. The LPP (pension funds) is optional for the self-employed, although recommended. Accident insurance (LAInf) is mandatory only if you hire employees; otherwise optional.
In which office should I register my company?
Register the company at the Commercial Register Office of the canton where your company has its registered office. In the case of the Canton of Svitto, at the competent office of the canton itself (at central level or in decentralised offices). The registered office does not necessarily have to coincide with the personal domicile, but must be located in the canton chosen for registration.
