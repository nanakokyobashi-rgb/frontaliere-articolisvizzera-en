Fight against undeclared work: consultation on the new law kicks off (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 11 August 2026

The Federal Council proposes amendments to the law against undeclared work to strengthen controls and cooperation between authorities by 16 October 2026.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Consultation initiated on 19 June 2026 by the Federal Council.
  • Objective: to strengthen controls against undeclared work and abuse.
  • Deadline for the consultation procedure set at 16 October 2026.

Key facts

  • What: Amendment of the federal law against undeclared work (LLN).
  • When: Start June 19, 2026, end October 16, 2026.
  • Where: Switzerland, at the federal level.
  • Who: Federal Council.
  • Legal basis: Candinas Motion (24.3202).

At its meeting on 19 June 2026, the Federal Council officially put a draft amendment to the federal law against undeclared work (LLN) under consultation. The legislative intervention responds to the precise indications contained in the Candinas motion (24.3202), entitled "More possibilities to combat abuses in the federal law concerning measures to combat undeclared work". The main purpose is to combat in a more incisive way the phenomenon of undeclared work and other forms of abuse that affect the law of the commercial register, as well as enforcement and bankruptcy procedures.

Practical Implications for Territorial Controls

One of the most important innovations envisaged by the project concerns the direct access to the data of the information system on admission to circulation (SIAC) managed by the Federal Office of Roads. Currently, supervisory authorities are often in difficulty when they have to operate outside the company premises, as in the case of inspections on construction sites. With the proposed new legal basis, inspectors will be able to verify the identity of employers and workers directly through on-site company vehicles. This step will greatly simplify on-site assessment procedures, reducing downtime and increasing the operational capacity of cantonal control teams.

Integration with corporate transparency

The project also aims to fill a regulatory gap that has emerged in relation to the Law on the Transparency of Legal Entities. The proposal provides that the enforcement bodies, active in the prevention and fight against insurance fraud and undeclared work, can report to the register for transparency any discrepancies detected during their activities. This integration will make it possible to maintain a more accurate and consistent database, facilitating monitoring work on a national scale. Key points

For employers and professionals operating in Switzerland, staying up to date on the evolution of the legislation against undeclared work is essential to ensure the full regularity of their business. The ongoing consultation procedure, which will end on 16 October 2026, is the moment when the social partners and institutions define the operational boundaries of the future law. It is advisable to monitor the developments of the calcolatore stipendio and the official guides to understand how the new rules may affect the correct classification of employees and the management of payroll.

What to do in case of checks

It is essential to always keep employee documentation and proof of affiliation to the AVS/AI/IPG compensation funds available. With the introduction of access to SIAC data, consistency between declared means of transport and business activity will become an immediate point of verification for inspectors. Therefore, each company must ensure that the vehicles used are correctly registered and that the information in the trade register is constantly updated. In case of doubts about the correct interpretation of the regulations in force or the management of employment contracts, it is always advisable to consult the directives of the SECO or contact the competent cantonal offices for the fight against undeclared work in your canton of residence or place of work.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the main objectives of the amendment of the law against undeclared work?
The project aims to intensify the fight against undeclared work and other abuses by improving collaboration between cantonal control authorities and trade, enforcement and bankruptcy registry offices. It is also intended to facilitate external controls, such as those on construction sites, through access to data from the SIAC system.
When will the consultation process end?
The consultation procedure, initiated by the Federal Council on 19 June 2026, will end on 16 October 2026.
How will field controls change?
Thanks to the proposed new legal basis, supervisory authorities will be able to directly access data from the Federal Office of Roads' Admission Information System (SIAC). This will make it easier to verify the identity of employers and employees during checks carried out outside the company premises, such as on construction sites.
