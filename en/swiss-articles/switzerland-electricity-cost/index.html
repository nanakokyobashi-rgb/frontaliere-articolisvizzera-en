Switzerland should invest CHF 4 billion to ensure stable energy reserves.

Context

The Birr reserve power plant should be preserved. Economiesuisse's estimate is that Switzerland should invest four billion francs to ensure stable energy reserves. This investment would be essential to ensure the country's energy security and prevent future shortages.

Switzerland is already one of the nations most at risk of energy shortages in Europe, with domestic electricity production covering only 60% of its needs. Recent years have seen a significant increase in the demand for electricity, mainly due to the increase in population and urbanization.

According to a report by consulting firm EY, Switzerland could risk an energy shortage by 2025 if no action is taken. The situation is particularly critical in the cantons of Central Switzerland, where electricity demand is higher than domestic production.

Economiesuisse's estimate predicts that investments of four billion francs will be needed to ensure the country's energy security. This investment could be used to:

  • Develop new backup power plants
  • Improve the energy efficiency of existing power plants
  • Develop new technologies for the production of electricity
  • Improve the electricity transmission network

The report highlights that Switzerland has already invested over 10 billion francs in the construction of power plants, and that these plants could still be used for many years. In addition, the report stresses that Switzerland has sufficient electricity storage capacity to cover the needs of more than 10 years.

The Finance Commission of the National Council criticized the fact that the measure did not consider cheaper alternatives, such as the construction of smaller power plants or the promotion of energy efficiency. The Commission also criticised the fact that the measure did not provide for a savings plan to cover any additional costs.
