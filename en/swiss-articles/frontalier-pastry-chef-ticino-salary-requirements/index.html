Requirements to become a pastry shop frontier

To become a pastry frontier, you need to meet certain requirements. First of all, professional training of at least 3 years in the pastry industry is required, such as a pastry chef diploma from a recognized training school. In addition, it is necessary to have a good knowledge of the French language, since the confectionery border work takes place mainly in the canton of Geneva and in the cities of Geneva, Lausanne and Freiburg.

Salary of pastry border workers

The salary of pastry border workers may vary depending on experience and professional training. However, according to 2022 statistics, the average salary of a confectionery frontier worker in Switzerland is around €45,000 per year. It is important to note that this salary may vary depending on the city and canton where you work.

Operational details

Key facts

  • What: New Frontier Agreement
  • When: 23 December 2020
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: Frontiersmen
  • Amount: €10,000 (deductible)

The new Frontier Agreement was signed on 23 December 2020 and introduced significant changes for frontier workers in Switzerland. This agreement aims to simplify and standardise the rules for frontier workers, ensuring greater protection of their rights.

Salary and requirements

The new Frontier Agreement establishes that frontier workers must have a monthly income of at least €2,400 in order to be considered frontier workers in all respects. This amount may vary depending on the canton and city where the job is located. For example, in Zurich, the minimum monthly income is €2,800, while in Bern it is €2,500.

Recognition and registration

To be recognised as frontier workers, workers must register with the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) within 30 days of the date of commencement of work. Registration is free and can be done online or at the local SECO office.

Operational Checklists

To ensure an effective registration process, border guards must provide the following documents:

  • A valid passport or ID
  • An employment contract or a certificate of employment
  • An income statement
