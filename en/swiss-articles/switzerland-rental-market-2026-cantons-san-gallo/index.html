Tenant's Rights

Tenants in Switzerland are entitled to a maximum security deposit of 3 months' rent, which must be paid into an escrow account in the tenant's name. This means that the landlord cannot use the deposit for other purposes and that the tenant can request a refund within 3 months of the end of the contract.

Landlord Cancellation

The cancellation of the landlord is valid only if it is presented on an official form of the canton. The landlord must notify the tenant at least 3 months in advance, if there are no grounds of urgency. If the tenant does not renew the contract, the landlord may request the payment of a penalty of up to 3 months' rent.

Security deposit in Switzerland

The security deposit in Switzerland is regulated by the Code of Obligations (OR) of 10 March 1911. The Code states that the security deposit may not exceed 3% of the annual rental price. For example, if the annual rent is CHF 24'000, the maximum security deposit cannot exceed CHF 720.
