Swiss Parliament Approves Central Credit and Crypto Standard

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

131,3 million francs for reserve power plants, 126 votes against 61 on crypto-assets and hotel VAT at 3,8% until 31 dicembre 2031.

131.3 million francs for future reserve power plants were approved by the National Council in Berne by 118 votes to 74. The matter now goes to the Council of States.

Of the credit, 100 million are intended for planning and preparatory activities for the reserve power plants; the remaining 31.3 million will finance the Monthey (VS) plant, a transitional solution until 2030.

Plants, crypto-assets and VAT

By 126 votes to 61, the National Council approved a federal decree extending to crypto-assets the review mechanism for implementation in compliance with the standards for the automatic exchange of information. The matter has been definitively adopted. dichiarazione delle imposte is a separate track: the source does not indicate personal documents or deadlines.

For the hotel sector, by 101 votes to 86, with 7 abstentions, the National Council decided on second reading to consider the Federal Council's bill concerning the special rate of 3.8%. It approved the commission's subsidiary proposal to extend it until 31 December 2031; the matter returns to the Council of States.

Agriculture and agenda

The motion by Katja Riem (UDC/BE) on ecological compensation was approved. It calls for the legal bases and directives to be revised in order to better reconcile nature protection and biodiversity with the sustainable maintenance of agricultural production potential. The Council of States must still express its view.

Two motions by Jacques Nicolet (UDC/VD) and Pierre-André Page (UDC/FR) were also approved. They call on the Federal Council to adapt the provisions of the Ordinance on Low-Voltage Electrical Installations, taking animals' electrosensitivity into account in the mandatory inspections of a farm's electrical installations, and to promote the training of specialists on stray currents and measures to protect livestock.

The session opened with a tribute to the victims of the Zug attack 25 years ago and addressed Question Time. Roberta Soldati (UDC/TI) took the oath as a national councillor, succeeding Piero Marchesi, who is about to take up the office of State Councillor. Tomorrow, Tuesday 29 September, from 08.00 to 13.00, the agenda includes the Federal Act on Health Insurance for Detained Persons and the Health Insurance Supervision Act.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the amount approved for reserve plants and how is it divided?
The National Council approved a loan of CHF 131.3 million for future reserve power plants. Of these, 100 million are intended for planning and preliminary activities, while the remaining 31.3 million will finance the Monthey (VS) transitional power plant, a solution valid until 2030.
How was the vote on crypto-assets and what is the status of the dossier?
With 126 votes in favour and 61 against, the National Council approved the federal decree extending the verification mechanism for the standards-compliant implementation of automatic exchange of information to crypto-assets. The dossier is definitively adopted and no longer returns to the States.
What is the status of the proposal on hotel VAT and how long is the special rate expected?
At second reading, the National Council approved the commission's subsidiary proposal to maintain the 3.8% special rate for the hotel sector until 31 December 2031. The dossier now goes back to the States for the final decision.
