How much does a border painter earn in Ticino?

The border painter in Ticino earns between CHF 40,000 and CHF60,000 per year. The amount is affected by commissions and sales of the paintings. Border painters can opt for the CHF 10,000 deductible.

The border painter is an artist who works in a border area between Switzerland and Italy, particularly in the canton of Ticino. This type of artist can earn a lot of money thanks to commissions for works of art sold to tourists and local residents.

According to Swiss federal law, border painters are subject to the tax rules of the canton in which they work. In Ticino, for example, border painters are subject to the canton's tax rules, as established by the cantonal law of 15 June 2018, which provides for a CHF 10,000 deductible for annual income.

A concrete example of how a border painter can earn in Ticino is as follows: if an artist sells 10 paintings a year for CHF 5,000 each, he will earn CHF50,000. However, if the artist were to pay 20% commissions to gallerists or local merchants, his net income would be CHF 40'000.

Furthermore, if the frontier painter decides to opt for the CHF 10'000 allowance, his net profit would be CHF 30'000, as he would not be required to pay income taxes in excess of CHF 10'000.
