Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 11 July 2026

The new law on border workers recognizes the title and salary of teachers in the Canton of Ticino.

Context

In a nutshell

The new law on border workers recognizes the title and salary of teachers in the Canton of Ticino, giving them official recognition and establishing an adequate salary level. This law will come into force on 1 January 2024, after a long legislative journey that required collaboration between stakeholders, including teachers, employers and local authorities.

Border teacher school Ticino salary requirements

Teaching border workers will be able to access a G permit for the school sector, recognising their added value and contribution to the community. The law also establishes that border teachers are entitled to an official title, attested by a certificate issued by the Ticino Region. This official recognition will give teaching border workers greater social protection and a higher position than non--border colleagues.

The expected salary for border teachers is CHF 140,000 gross per year, plus a supplement for travel and transfers, which will vary according to grade and professional experience. This salary has been calculated taking into account the high quality and added value offered by these teachers in the Ticino community.

Below is a concrete example with real numbers: a teaching frontier with a professional diploma (DP) and a degree in a stem discipline (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics) earns an estimated gross annual income of 155,000 Swiss francs, thanks to the 25% increase provided for cross-border commuter teachers depending on grade and professional experience.

Operational details

The law on border workers, signed on 23 December 2020 and entered into force on 1 January 2024, recognizes the title and salary of teachers in the Canton of Ticino who carry out activities as border workers. Frontier teachers, or "Frontalschulle", are entitled to a G permit for the school sector.

Key points

CROSS-BORDER COMMUTER SCHOOL ASSIGNMENT TICINO - SALARY REQUIREMENTS

For border workers who intend to teach in Switzerland, the G permit is a fundamental requirement to work legally. Here is a detailed guide on the requirements to obtain the G permit for border workers teaching in Switzerland at the cantonal level Ticino.

PROXIMITY

The procedure for obtaining the G permit for border workers is subject to a transitional regime until 2033. Therefore, there may still be a transition period during which border teachers may still encounter some difficulties or uncertainties in the procedure.

DOCUMENTS TO BE SUBMITTED

To apply for the G permit, border teachers must submit an application to the competent cantonal authority. The application must be accompanied by a copy of the employment contract and a statement of employment.

COPY OF EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

The copy of the employment contract must be signed by the employer and must show that the frontier worker has an insured occupation in Switzerland.

COMPANY STATEMENT

The declaration of employment must be signed by the employer and must confirm that the border worker has an insured occupation in Switzerland and that he has no criminal record, in particular those relating to safety at work.
