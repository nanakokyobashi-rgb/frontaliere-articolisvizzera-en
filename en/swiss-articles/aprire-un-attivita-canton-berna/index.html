The process of registration in the commercial register in the Canton of Bern

The process of registration in the commercial register in the Canton of Bern is mandatory for all companies that intend to operate in the cantonal territory. The first step is to choose the legal form of the company, which can be a limited liability company (SRL) or a limited liability company (SARL).

Choose the legal form of the company

The choice of the legal form of the company depends on the needs of your company. The limited liability company (SRL) is a more common legal form and easier to manage, while the limited liability company (SARL) is more suitable for companies that have a higher volume of activity.

Operational details

Registration in the commercial register in the Canton of Bern requires the submission of a complete application, which must be accompanied by a number of documents, including the social contract, the specifications and the minutes of the meeting. In addition, you must pay a registration fee of CHF 500. The registration process can take up to 3 months and requires the presence of a legal representative of the company.

Choice of legal form

Choosing the legal form for your business is a fundamental step before opening a business in Bern. There are several options available, including:

  • Limited liability company (SRL): this is the most common legal form for small and medium-sized enterprises. The SRL requires a minimum share capital of CHF 20,000 and a maximum number of shareholders of 50.
  • Limited liability company (SRI): This is a simpler legal form than SRL, but requires a minimum share capital of CHF 10,000.
  • Joint stock company (SPA): it is a more complex legal form than SRL and requires a minimum share capital of CHF 50,000.
