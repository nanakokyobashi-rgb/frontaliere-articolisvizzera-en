Operational details

The practical analysis of the sustainability of the mortgage loan represents the crucial step to avoid risks of over-indebtedness in the medium and long term. Swiss financial institutions calculate mortgage sustainability by applying a theoretical interest rate, usually set at a prudential level above current market rates, to verify that the total fixed costs of the home do not exceed a certain percentage of the buyer's annual gross income. This simulation serves to protect the owner from any future increases in interest rates set by the SNB, ensuring that the household budget can support spending even in less favourable economic scenarios.

Sustainability and calculation of necessary income

In addition to mortgage interest calculated on the theoretical rate, the sustainability calculation includes mandatory debt amortization and estimated maintenance costs of the property. Depreciation usually provides for the repayment of a significant part of the bank loan within a pre-established period of time, often linked to reaching retirement age according to the provisions of the AVS/AHV pension. The costs of maintenance and provision for the conservation of the value of the property are estimated as a fixed percentage of the estimated value of the asset. Key points

The operational phase of purchasing real estate in the Canton of Schwyz requires compliance with precise deadlines and the handling of administrative and notarial formalities. Once the desired home has been identified and a price agreement has been reached with the seller, the parties proceed to draft and sign the preliminary contract or directly the final deed of sale. The notarial deed must be executed by an authorized public notary in the Canton of Schwyz, who also handles the official registration of the transfer of ownership at the competent cantonal land registry, the moment that legally establishes the transfer of property rights.

Additional costs and real estate taxation

Purchasing a home involves incurring additional costs that vary depending on the regulations of the Canton of Schwyz and its municipalities. The main items include notarial fees for drafting documents and land registry fees, in addition to the real estate transfer tax applied at the cantonal or municipal level according to current rates. On the ongoing tax front, the owner will be subject to property wealth tax and imputed rental income tax (rental value), integrated into the three-tier taxation system which includes direct federal, cantonal, and municipal taxes. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the minimum own funds required to buy a home?
Swiss lenders generally require that at least twenty percent of the value of the property be covered by own funds. Of this share, an important part does not necessarily have to come from the second LPP pillar, but can be composed of free savings or funds from the third pillar 3a.
How is the sustainability of the mortgage calculated?
Sustainability is verified by applying a prudential theoretical interest rate to the borrowed capital, adding a fixed fee for debt amortization and an estimated percentage for the maintenance of the property. Total costs must not exceed a set fraction of annual gross income.
What fees and expenses are paid during the notarial deed?
During the deed and registration in the land register in the Canton of Schwyz, notary fees, registration fees and property transfer tax are paid, the rates of which vary according to the regulations of the canton and the municipality of location of the property.
