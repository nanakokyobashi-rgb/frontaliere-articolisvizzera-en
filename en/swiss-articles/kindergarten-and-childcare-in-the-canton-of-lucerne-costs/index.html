Operational details

Kindergarten and childcare in the Canton of Lucerne are subject to different requirements and procedures. The choice of daycare or daycare depends on the specific needs of the family and the financial resources available.

Costs of a nursery in Lucerne

According to 2022 data, the average costs of a kindergarten in Lucerne are around CHF 800-1,000 per month for a child under the age of 3. However, these costs may vary depending on the facility, quality and service options offered. For example, a kindergarten with a child-to-adult ratio of 1:3 might cost more than one with a ratio of 1:5.

Child care in Lucerne: options and costs

Childcare in Lucerne can be entrusted to different facilities, including nurseries, day families and babysitting services. The costs of these services can vary greatly depending on the options and needs of the family. Here are some examples of costs:

  • Nursery: 800-1,000 CHF per month
  • Day family: CHF 600-900 per month
  • Babysitting: CHF 20-30 per hour

Regulations and requirements for nursery school in Lucerne

According to the cantonal law on educational institutions of 1 January 2019, kindergartens in Lucerne must comply with the following requirements:

  • A child-adult ratio of at least 1:5
  • A qualified and sufficient number of personnel
  • A safe and healthy environment for children
  • An educational activity

Key points

Regarding booking a place in a public nursery, it is advisable to contact the Department of Education and Training of the Canton of Lucerne (DEFL) at least 6-12 months before the start of the school year. In this way, you can guarantee the availability of a place and plan your family's future with greater certainty.

However, it is important to keep in mind that the demand for places in public nursery schools can be high, especially in cantons with a low birth rate, such as the Canton of Ticino or the Canton of Valais. In these cases, it is possible that places are limited and that the reservation must be made well in advance.

For childcare, it is possible to entrust them to day families or private nurseries, but it is important to check the credentials and working conditions of the day families or private nurseries. In Switzerland, the law obliges day care families and private nurseries to respect certain quality and safety standards, such as the presence of qualified staff and the availability of safe spaces for children.

According to DEFL regulations, day care families and private nurseries must be registered and authorized before they can welcome children. Additionally, day care homes and private nurseries need to be able to provide quality care for children, including meal preparation, cleaning and child safety.

Childcare costs can vary greatly depending on the type of service you choose and the area where you live. In general, public daycares are cheaper than private daycares, but may be less accessible for low-income families.

For example, in the Canton of Lucerne, the cost of a place in a public nursery can vary from CHF 600 to CHF 1,200 per month, depending on the age group of the child and the duration of the reservation. Private nurseries, however, can charge a cost of CHF 1,500 to CHF 3,000 per month for a child under 3 years of age.
