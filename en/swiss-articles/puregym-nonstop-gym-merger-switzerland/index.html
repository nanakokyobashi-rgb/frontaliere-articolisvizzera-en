A merger that expands the network

In Zurich, British competitor Puregym acquired the Nonstop Gym chain from Invision, a holding company based in Zug. The transaction is reshaping Switzerland’s fitness sector and effectively doubling the English group’s presence in the country.

Puregym currently operates 49 gyms in Switzerland, while Nonstop Gym has 48. The stated objective is to exceed 100 facilities by the end of 2026, extending the network to German-speaking Switzerland, French-speaking Switzerland and Ticino.

Two brands, a national outlook

The complementarity highlighted in the announcement is primarily geographical. Puregym also intends to make use of Nonstop Gym’s operational experience and organization. The acquired brand was founded in 2014 in Geneva and is intended to disappear in the long term.

The announcement issued on the occasion of the acquisition states:

“Together, we are creating an exceptional nationwide club network that could become number one in Switzerland and reach even more members”

For the British group, the acquisition represents a further step in its international expansion and pursuit of a leading position in the Swiss fitness market. Puregym is based in Leeds, England, and operates in six countries: it has 467 gyms in Great Britain, 128 in Denmark and 61 in the United States. Through a franchise partner, it also operates 23 fitness centers across Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates.

The scope described is national: the planned network includes the three areas mentioned in the announcement and aims for a broader and more coordinated presence across Switzerland.
