The federal framework for tenancy

Security deposit and blocked account

The security deposit required for a rental in Switzerland may not exceed three months' rent. The limit is expressed in months' rent: the maximum stated is three months' rent. The amount must also be placed in a blocked account in the tenant's name. For anyone looking for a home in 2026, there are therefore two immediate checks: compare the deposit with the rent and check the account's designation.

The reference given is CO Art. 253 et seq. The rules form part of federal tenancy law, which is the same in every canton. For the canton of Bern, the practical takeaway is therefore the same basic protection as in the national comparison: as a rule, the deposit does not change merely because the canton changes. Moreover, the account must remain in the tenant's name and blocked.

Landlord's termination notice

The second step concerns the landlord's termination notice. Its validity requires the official cantonal form. The fact that the form is cantonal does not turn the rule into different rules for each canton: the framework remains federal, while the document to use is the official one of the canton concerned.

If the tenant wants to challenge the termination notice, the stated deadline is 30 days. The authority to contact is the conciliation authority. The procedure described is clear on three points: the party giving notice is the landlord, which form is required, and which deadline must be observed. These are the elements to verify before taking action, including in the canton of Bern.

For the 2026 real estate market, the national point of comparison is therefore concrete: a maximum deposit of three months' rent, a blocked account in the tenant's name, an official form, and a 30-day deadline. Operational details

Bern in the national cost comparison

The Canton of Bern should be considered together with the other cantons, but the useful comparison does not involve rent alone. To estimate the available margin, it is necessary to separate gross salary from the items that affect net income. The source does not indicate a federal minimum wage; some cantons have their own. This makes the national picture different from a single rule: the minimum wage, when it exists, belongs to the cantonal level.

Another item is LAMal/KVG. For residents, health insurance is mandatory and must be taken out within three months of arrival. Premiums are per capita and depend on the canton and region; premium reductions are a cantonal subsidy. The premium is neither a tax nor a wage contribution. The housing budget must therefore be kept separate from payroll deductions and rent.

Items affecting net income

At the tax level, Switzerland has three levels: direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each canton has its own law and multiplier; the municipality applies its own multiplier to the cantonal tax. AFC/ESTV concerns direct federal tax and VAT, while cantonal administrations handle cantonal and municipal taxes. This distinction helps to assess the cost of living without adding different items together.

Key points

Operating procedure for home seekers

Five checks before closing the case

The verification can be organized into a brief sequence, without confusing federal rules with cantonal administrative elements.

1. Measure the deposit in relation to the rent. The limit indicated for tenancy is three months: a request exceeding this does not correspond to the parameter stated for the security deposit.

2. Check where the sum is held. The deposit must go into a blocked account in the tenant's name. This check concerns the account, not only the amount requested.

3. If a notice of termination from the landlord arrives, check the form immediately. Validity requires the official cantonal form; for those living in the canton of Bern, the document to check is therefore the official one of the canton concerned.

4. If you want to contest the termination, consider the 30-day deadline and contact the conciliation authority. The deadline and the authority are the two procedural references indicated for the challenge.

5. Complete the monthly budget with items outside the rent. A resident must take out LAMal within three months of arrival; premiums are per capita, vary by canton or region, and may provide for a cantonal reduction.

From a tax perspective, then separate direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each Canton has its own law and multiplier, while the municipality applies its own multiplier to the cantonal tax. Frequently Asked Questions
How much can the security deposit amount to in Switzerland?
The security deposit can reach a maximum of three months of rent. It must be placed on a escrow account in the tenant's name. The limit belongs to the federal lease law referred to in CO art. 253 et seq. and applies in every canton, including the canton of Bern. The parameter indicated is therefore the number of monthly payments.
When is the landlord's notice of termination valid?
The landlord's cancellation is only valid if the official cantonal form is used. If the tenant intends to dispute it, they must comply with the 30-day deadline and contact the conciliation authority. The framework of the lease remains federal, while the requested form is the official one of the canton concerned.
Do rental rules change in the canton of Bern?
The federal lease law is the same in each canton, so the basic rules on security deposit and contesting cancellation also apply in the canton of Bern. The official form used for the cancellation and the administrative elements related to the canton concerned remain cantonal.
Which items should be considered in the budget besides rent?
For residents, LAMal is mandatory and must be stipulated within three months of arrival. The premiums are per capita, vary by canton and region and may provide for a cantonal reduction; they are not a tax or a wage contribution. The budget must also distinguish federal, cantonal and municipal taxes and consider AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC, LAINF/LAA and LPP/BVG.
