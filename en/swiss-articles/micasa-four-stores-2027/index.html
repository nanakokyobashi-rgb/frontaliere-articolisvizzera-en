Micasa closes four stores by January 2027

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 October 2026

Micasa will part with four stores by January 2027: Crissier will close by the end of 2026. Langendorf, Brügg and Buchs involved; no comment on jobs.

Last year, Micasa's 30 branches, four of them in French-speaking Switzerland, were taken over by the managers of the former subsidiary. The transaction came after the brand left the orbit of the Migros group in 2025. Now the network is being scaled back: the economic news site Inside Paradeplatz revealed that Micasa will part with four stores by January 2027.

Four locations involved

The Crissier store, in the canton of Vaud, will close by the end of 2026. The other three sites listed are Langendorf (SO), Brügg (BE) and Buchs (SG). The news therefore concerns locations spread across several cantons, while the Crissier store is the one for which the source already indicates a precise deadline.

The figure of 30 branches makes it possible to gauge the scope of the announcement without confusing it with a complete exit from the market. The source refers to four stores that Micasa will part with, while it does not describe the closure of all the branches.

The sequence described by the source is not limited to a reduction in outlets. The ownership issue is also being reopened. The results of the management entrusted to the executives of the former subsidiary reportedly did not meet expectations. The company was therefore reportedly taken over urgently by a group of Swiss investors. The names of the investors have not been disclosed.

Micasa operates in the mid-range segment of furniture and decoration. The brand attributes the difficulties to the current economic situation and the changing market conditions. This is the reason given by the company, not an additional assessment of individual stores or individual cantons.

The employment issue remains open. At the time of publication, no comment had been released on any possible job cuts. Therefore, the source makes it possible to establish locations, timelines and ownership changes, but not to quantify the consequences for staff. Anyone wishing to follow job opportunities in Switzerland separately can consult the annunci di lavoro, without attributing to Micasa positions or redundancies that have not been announced.

Operational details

For those who live or work in the affected areas, the most useful distinction is between a firm deadline and a time window. Crissier has a specified deadline, the end of 2026. For Langendorf, Brügg and Buchs, the source sets only the limit of January 2027. The text does not provide the exact day for any of the three locations: the practical reference therefore remains the indicated window, not an invented date.
