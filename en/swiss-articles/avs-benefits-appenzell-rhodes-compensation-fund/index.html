AVS and complementary services: External Appenzello (cross-border guide)

First pillar in the Canton of Outer Appenzell: how the compensation fund works, calculation of the AVS pension, management of contribution gaps and access to supplementary benefits to cover the minimum living wage.

Social security in Switzerland rests on three pillars. The first pillar — the AVS — is the foundation: a mandatory federal insurance financed by salary contributions and reserved for every resident working in Swiss territory. The second pillar is occupational pension (LPP), supplementary and mandatory for those who work as employees. The third pillar is individual pension, optional, for those who want additional savings for retirement.

Operational details

How the cantonal clearing house works

The compensation fund of the Canton of Appenzello Outer is the intermediary between the insured and the federal AVS system. Every month, the employer pays the employee's contribution (5.3% of the wage bill) and his own contribution (5.3%) to the relevant fund. This amount is not a direct federal tax, but a mandatory contribution intended solely for the financing of social benefits under the first pillar (AVS, AI, IPG — Profit Loss Allowance).

The compensation fund maintains an individual insurance account for each worker, in which it records the contributions paid year after year. This account is publicly available: the insured is entitled to receive a copy of the account statement at least every five years, free of charge. This way you can check if the contributions have been recorded correctly and if there are any gaps to report before retirement.

Calculation of annuity: the role of contributory duration

The AVS pension is not calculated on a flat-rate basis, but on the basis of two main factors: the duration of the contributory career and the average amount of income declared. In the canton of Outer Appenzel, the compensation fund applies the federal method provided for by the UFAS/BSV (Federal Office of Social Insurance).

The minimum length of contributory careers to obtain a full income is set at the federal level.

Key points

Procedure for requesting AVS pension in the Canton of Appenzell Outer Rhodes

To receive the AVS pension in the Canton of Appenzell Outer Rhodes, the insured person must submit a request to the competent compensation fund. The application should be submitted normally two or three months before reaching the federal pension age. Delaying the request does not result in benefits: the pension begins on the first day of the month following the request (in some cases, from the month of presentation, according to federal regulations).

The procedure involves filling out an official form, available at the cantonal compensation fund or downloadable online from the UFAS/BSV website. It is necessary to attach a copy of the passport or identity card, and if necessary, certificates of unregistered periods (e.g., to request fictitious contributions for illness or injury recognized by the professional insurance).

Requesting supplementary benefits and deadlines

For supplementary benefits, the application should be addressed to the competent cantonal office (usually the Office for Supplementary Benefits of the Canton of Appenzell Outer Rhodes, integrated into the cantonal administrative structure). Here, too, an official form is filled out and documents such as income, assets, and current expenses (rent, health insurance, utilities, medical expenses) are attached.

There is no fixed annual deadline for submitting the AVS application: it is recommended to submit it 2-3 months before reaching the pension age. Delays in submitting the application may result in a delay in the start of the pension.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the compensation fund in the Outer Appenzell Canton?
The compensation fund is the body that coordinates workers' AVS contributions in the canton. It collects monthly payments from employees (5.3%) and employers (5.3%), records them in the individual insurance account and, at the time of retirement, calculates the amount of the pension on the basis of the contributory duration and declared income. It is a decentralised federal institution at cantonal level, which applies the rules set by the UFAS/BSV (Federal Office of Social Insurance).
How is the AVS annuity calculated?
The AVS income is based on two factors: the duration of the contributory career and the average amount of income paid over time. Those who have paid contributions throughout their working lives receive the maximum amount. Each year of contribution gap (year without payments or with incomplete payments) proportionally reduces the amount of the pension permanently. The amount is indexed annually by the Federal Assembly to maintain purchasing power.
How do contribution gaps affect retirement?
Each contribution gap directly reduces the amount of the AVS annuity. If, for example, there are two years left in a 43-year career, the annuity will be calculated on 41 years, with a proportional reduction. In some cases it is possible to fill the gaps by making voluntary contributions (before retirement) or by taking advantage of figurative contributions (for example, during sickness or unemployment). It is advisable to check with the cantonal clearinghouse for specific details.
Who is entitled to supplementary benefits?
Supplementary benefits are granted to those who, although receiving an AVS annuity (possibly supplemented by the LPP annuity of the second pillar), do not have sufficient resources to cover basic expenses: rent, food, utilities, transport, health insurance. Access is subject to a means test: the applicant must not have assets above the thresholds established by the canton. Benefits are calculated as the difference between the minimum living allowance and total income.
When and how to apply for the AVS annuity?
The AVS pension application must be submitted to the competent compensation fund normally 2-3 months before reaching the federal retirement age. The application must be completed on an official form (available at the register or on the UFAS/BSV website) and a copy of the identity document must be attached.
