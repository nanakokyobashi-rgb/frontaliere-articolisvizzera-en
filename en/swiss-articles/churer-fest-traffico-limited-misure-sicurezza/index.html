The 35th edition of Churer Fest will take place from 14 to 16 August in Chur. Traffic will be restricted in the city centre and law enforcement will take several measures to ensure the safety of the event.

The Churer Fest is an event of great importance for the Graubünden region. The event attracts between 90,000 and 100,000 people every year, who come together to celebrate the culture and history of the region. The police of the city of Chur take several measures to ensure order and safety within the festival area.

Among the measures taken are the presence of police officers in every corner of the city, the installation of surveillance cameras and the creation of control areas to ensure access for visitors. In addition, the police will also have a team of agents specialised in managing mass events, who will be responsible for managing any emergency situations.

Operational details

Churer Fest is one of the biggest festivals in Graubünden and attracts between 90,000 and 100,000 people every year. The measures are based in principle on the security situation in Switzerland, as defined by the Confederation.

The Swiss Confederation has established that public safety is a shared responsibility between federal, cantonal and local authorities. According to the federal law of 21 March 2001 on public security, local authorities are responsible for the prevention and repression of crimes (LFP 1: Art. 5). The Federal Law of 20 December 1946 on Cantonal and Local Police (LFP 2: Art. 1) states that local authorities must ensure public safety and prevent crime.

Police in the city of Chur have developed a detailed security plan for Churer Fest. The plan involves placing concrete blocks near the roads leading into the old city to prevent cars or other vehicles from being thrown into the crowd. In addition, police have established checkpoints to control access to the festival area and to ensure that organisers and visitors are aware of security measures.

Key points

Law enforcement is asking all road users to avoid the city centre and, if possible, to cycle or walk there. Traffic detours are signposted in the direction of the Engadinstrasse, Tivolibrücke, Gürtelstrasse, Rheinstrasse and Ringstrasse until the bifurcation of the Masanserstrasse. The city police expect considerable inconvenience for traffic from Friday to Sunday.

The Churer Fest is a very important event for the city of Chur, in the Canton of Graubünden. Thisyear, more than 100,000 people are expected to attend, who will pour into the city centre to attend concerts and events. However, local authorities have established security measures to ensure the safety of users and participants.

Among the security measures envisaged are traffic control, the presence of police officers and the reporting of security areas. Law enforcement also asked participants to abide by traffic rules and not to park in prohibited areas.

The city police have also established special parking areas for participants, which will be signposted with direction signs. Participants are advised to use these car parks to avoid parking in prohibited areas and to avoid obstructing traffic.
