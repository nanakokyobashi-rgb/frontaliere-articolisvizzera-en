The Swiss public transport network

Switzerland has one of the most extensive public transport systems in the world: a coordinated network that connects cities, towns and valleys through railways, regional buses and urban trams. In Basel Countryside, as in all Swiss cantons, public transport is organised through a fare community — an association of transport companies that agree on uniform fares over a defined geographical area.

The canton of Basel-Country, located in northwestern Switzerland, has developed its own fare structure that allows residents and workers to easily access the regional bus, urban tram and train services of the federal network (SBB/CFF). Operational details

Annual and monthly subscriptions

Residents and workers of Basel Campagna can choose between different types of subscriptions, each calibrated to different mobility needs.

The annual subscription is ideal for those who move regularly throughout the year. Purchasing for twelve months, the average price per trip is lower than repeated monthly purchases. The subscription is personal and non-transferable; it generally includes unlimited access within the chosen areas.

The monthly subscription offers more flexibility to those who do not use transport on a constant basis — for example, those who work remotely some days of the week. The monthly cost is proportional to the average number of trips planned. Next to fixed subscriptions there is the option of single tickets, ideal for those who move occasionally. However, regular travelers quickly find that subscriptions offer considerable savings.

Half-price and GA: loyal discount systems

At the Swiss national level, the half-price (Halbtax / Demi-tarif) is an affiliate subscription that grants a 50% reduction on all Swiss public transport tickets and subscriptions. It is not a mobility subscription in itself, but a loyalty card to be associated with a ticket or regular subscription. Key points

Choosing the subscription in Basel Region

The decision depends on the frequency of use and the average distance traveled. Here is a practical procedure to guide you:

Step 1: Calculate monthly trips. Count how many days a month public transportation is used. If it's 10 days or less, a single ticket for each trip might be the most economical choice; from 15 days onwards, a monthly subscription starts to make sense.

Step 2: Identify the zones traveled. Check the number of zones covered by the usual route (e.g., home-work-home). Basel Region and neighboring cantons offer online calculators or apps to determine the number of zones and the corresponding price. The accuracy of this step directly affects the final cost.

Step 3: Evaluate half-price and GA. If at least 5-6 trips are expected per month, compare the cost of a standard subscription with the cost of a half-price subscription added (which halves the price). If frequent trips are made across cantons, simulate the cost of a GA subscription.

Step 4: Check specific discounts. If you belong to the student, apprentice, unemployed, or elderly categories, contact the cantonal transport office or local transport company for dedicated discounts.

Where to check official tariffs and zones

Tariffs and zones in Basel Region are updated periodically. To obtain precise and up-to-date information:

  • Cantonal transport portal: The Basel Region and public transport companies provide official websites with interactive calculators to determine the cost of the route.
  • Mobility apps: Many Swiss cantons have developed apps that allow you to search for routes, calculate tariffs, and purchase tickets directly from your phone.
  • Local counters: At train stations and regional bus terminals, you can pick up informative brochures or speak with expert staff.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between monthly and annual subscription in Basel Campaign?
The annual subscription offers a lower average price per trip than the repeated purchase of monthly subscriptions, but requires a twelve-month commitment. The monthly subscription is ideal for those who do not use transport every day (remote work) or who have interruptions. By calculating your average commutes, you can determine which one is more convenient. Those who make 15+ days of travel per month quickly discover that the annual is convenient.
How does the half-price system work and should you buy it?
Half-price is a national affiliation that reduces the cost of tickets and season tickets on all Swiss transport by 50%. It has a modest fixed annual cost. It is convenient if you make at least 5-6 trips per month; the initial investment is quickly amortized with those who regularly use transport. For those who move frequently, the half-price is almost always convenient.
What reductions do students and apprentices have on transport in Basel Countryside?
Students and apprentices receive significant discounts on subscriptions — generally from 30% to 50% depending on category and age. The exact benefits depend on the training institution (school, university, apprenticeship centre) and the type of subscription. It is advisable to check with the cantonal transport office with a proof of registration.
What does the GA cover and is suitable for those who live in Basel Countryside?
The GA (Generale Abonnement) is an annual subscription that allows unlimited travel on all Swiss public transport: railways, buses, trams and some funiculars. It is convenient if you make frequent inter-cantonal movements (for example, those who work in Zurich or Bern but reside in Basel Countryside) or if you use transport almost daily in several cantons. For those who only move locally, a regional subscription with a half-price is generally cheaper.
How does the zone system work in Basel Countryside?
The zones are concentric distance bands: each additional zone traveled increases the price of the ticket or subscription. Those who move within a single zone pay less; those who cross more zones pay progressively more. Online calculators allow you to enter departure and destination to immediately determine how many zones cover the route and its cost.
