Partners Group starts negotiations to acquire Aroma-Zone (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 11 August 2026

Exclusive negotiations with Eurazeo, which will maintain a minority stake; possible valuation around 2 billion euros

Context

Partners Group, a Swiss investment company based in Zurich, has announced the start of exclusive negotiations to acquire Aroma-Zone, a well-known French brand of natural cosmetics. Parisian investment company Eurazeo will retain a minority stake after the transaction. The valuation of the company could be around 2 billion euros.

Aroma-Zone is a leading company in the French natural cosmetics market, with a significant presence in other European countries as well. The company has a history of over 20 years and has developed a wide range of products that respect the environment and human health. Aroma-Zone products are available in over 10,000 stores in France and other European countries.

The transaction could be one of the most important acquisitions in the natural cosmetics sector in Europe in recent years. The investment company Partners Group has already acquired several companies in the cosmetics and beauty sector, including the Swiss cosmetics company Dr. Barbara Sturm.

The valuation of Aroma-Zone could be around 2 billion euros, a figure that represents one of the most important acquisitions in the natural cosmetics sector in Europe. Investment company Eurazeo, which will retain a minority stake after the transaction, has already invested over €1.5 billion in Aroma-Zone over the years.

Operational details

Aroma-Zone is a fast-growing reality, with a turnover tripled since 2021 and a number of points of sale multiplied by seven. The active clientele has exceeded 5 million people, with a share of male buyers that now represents about a quarter of the customer base.

The Aroma-Zone group, a leader in the Swiss market for the distribution of wellness and cosmetic products, has experienced a significant acceleration in its growth in recent years. In 2021, the turnover was about 150 million Swiss francs, while in 2022 a turnover of about 450 million Swiss francs was reached. This increase was made possible by the group's expansion strategy, which saw the opening of new outlets in cities such as Zurich, Geneva and Lausanne.

The share of male shoppers has increased significantly, rising from 10% in 2021 to 25% in 2022. This increase was made possible by the growing focus on men's health and wellbeing, as well as the group's marketing strategy, which saw the creation of advertising campaigns targeted at male audiences.
