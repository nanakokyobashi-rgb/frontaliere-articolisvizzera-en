Concrete implications at national/cantonal level

In Switzerland, the profession of podiatrist is regulated at national and cantonal level. The Canton of Ticino, in particular, has adopted a series of regulations to ensure the quality and safety of podiatry services.

As of January 1, 2020, the federal health professions legislation (LPF) came into force, which introduced a new system for the recognition of health professions, including podiatry.

In Ticino, cantonal legislation on health professions (LPST) was updated in 2022 to comply with the new federal provisions. The LPST establishes the requirements for the exercise of the profession of podiatrist in the Canton of Ticino, including training requirements, continuing education and professional qualification.

Before/After Comparisons

Before the entry into force of the LPF and the LPST, the profession of podiatrist in Ticino was regulated differently than in the rest of Switzerland.

As of 2020, Ticino podiatrists must be registered with the Confederation and obtain a certificate of professional qualification (CQP) in order to practice their profession in the Canton of Ticino.
