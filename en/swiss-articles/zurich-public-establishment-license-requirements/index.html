In the Canton of Zurich, the public business license must be sought following the cantonal, municipal or competent institution authority, not a single national procedure. In fact, public businesses are among the services whose requirements, deadlines and amounts must be verified in the official source of the Canton, the Municipality or the competent institution.

To research the requirements and application for a public operating license in the Canton of Zurich, the first point is therefore to identify the authority that deals with the practice and follow the instructions relating to restaurants and clubs. The corner also includes any training or responsibilities required, but does not authorize turning a general rule into a requirement valid for every activity.

The scope of the question

The verification must cover three linked steps. First, the requirements applicable to the type of operation. Second, the presence of training or responsibility required by the relevant documentation. Third, the application, with the deadlines and amounts indicated by the authority. For this reason, the reference remains the competent cantonal or municipal level.

This perimeter avoids confusing licensing with taxation. In Switzerland, taxation is divided into three levels: direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each canton has its own law and its own multiplier; in the Municipalities the multiplier applies to the cantonal tax.

Institutions also have different roles. AFC/ESTV deals with IFD and VAT, while the cantonal administrations follow the cantonal and municipal side. UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS, AI and BVG, and does not set taxes; UST/BFS produces statistics and does not establish rates.

The same principle appears for commercial timetables and other cantonal services: requirements, deadlines and amounts may depend on the Canton, the Municipality or the competent institution. The procedure for Zurich must therefore remain anchored to the territory and to the official source indicated for the procedure.

For the general fiscal framework, dichiarazione delle imposte is also available, but the reference does not replace the official source responsible for the license.

Personnel matters follow a separate timetable. The stated maximum working time is 45 hours per week for industry, office work and retail, and 50 hours in other sectors. Annual leave is at least four weeks, or five for those under 20. Maternity leave lasts 14 weeks at 80% through IPG; paternity leave lasts two weeks. Notice periods are one, two or three months, respectively, during the first year, from the second to the ninth year, and from the tenth year onward.

Payroll contributions also follow a precise grid: AVS/AHV, AI and IPG amount to 5,3% for the employee and 10,6% in total including the employer; AD/AC is 1,1% up to the annual ceiling; LAINF/LAA ranges from 0,7% to 1,5% depending on the sector. For LPP/BVG on the coordinated salary, the stated rates are 7% between ages 25 and 34, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54, and 18% from age 55 until the reference age.

Indexed figures, such as the AD/AC ceiling and the LPP coordination threshold or deduction, should not be treated as fixed amounts: their value must be checked for the reference year. This prevents preparing a budget using an outdated figure.

The cost of personnel should then be considered together with the minimum-wage rule: there is no federal minimum wage, while some cantons have their own. For people residing in Switzerland, LAMal/KVG is compulsory and must be taken out within three months of arrival; it is private, with per-capita premiums, not a tax or a payroll contribution. Premiums vary by canton and region, with premium reductions administered at cantonal level. To check the components of a remuneration, busta paga svizzera can be used as a supplement.

Key points

From verification to application

To turn the verification into an orderly application, it is advisable to work in five steps and keep each area of responsibility separate. The goal is not to reconstruct a standard national application, but to follow the procedure specified for the Canton of Zurich.

Five-step procedure

1. Define the activity. State whether the application concerns a restaurant or an establishment and locate it in the Canton of Zurich. Then look for the official page of the competent authority: the matter may fall under the canton, the municipality, or a competent institution. Do not start from a template valid for another territory.

2. Check the requirements. In the official source, read what concerns requirements, any training or responsibilities, deadlines and amounts. These are the fields to verify for the licence and should be read together with the guidance from the authority handling the application.

3. Prepare the application. Use the channel described by the competent cantonal or municipal authority and follow the order of the procedure. Do not replace the application with a tax request: AFC/ESTV handles IFD and VAT, while cantonal administrations handle cantonal and municipal taxes. For guidance on general taxation, consult the dichiarazione delle imposte.

4. Organize the staff. Before calculating labor costs, check the applicable working-time limit, holidays, leave and notice periods. Then separate AVS/AI/IPG, AD/AC, LAINF/LAA and LPP/BVG: they have different percentages and conditions, while maximums and indexed thresholds require data for the correct year.

5. Check health coverage. For a resident, LAMal/KVG must be taken out within three months of arrival. The premium is per capita and is not a payroll tax; premiums and premium reductions follow the canton and region. This check belongs to personnel management, not to licence verification.

At the end, reread the application, comparing each requirement with the indicated official cantonal, municipal or institutional source. The same method applies to business hours and other services that vary according to the competent level: first the authority, then requirements, deadlines and amounts, and finally tax and personnel obligations.
