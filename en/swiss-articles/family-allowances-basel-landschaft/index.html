Family allowances in the canton of Basel-Country are part of the Swiss federal family support system. These are periodic benefits intended for parents for each fiscally dependent child. The entitlement to family allowances and the related monthly amounts vary depending on the canton of residence and the economic situation of the family. In the canton of Basel-Country, as in the other Swiss cantons, there is a territorially competent clearing house in charge of handling claims and payments.

Operational details

Application Procedure and Deadlines

To claim family allowances in the canton of Basel-Country, you must contact the territorially competent compensation fund. The procedure can be carried out online (if available), or by means of a paper form to be filed with the local office of the clearing house or with the municipal administration of residence.

The application must include identity documents, children's birth certificates, school or training certificates (for training allowances), and documentation of the family's financial situation, if requested. It is advisable to acquire the exact list of documents at the competent office, as requests may vary depending on the type of benefit and family situation.

Monthly amounts per child

The amounts of family allowances in the canton of Basel-Country are fixed by cantonal law. In the federal system, each canton independently defines the monthly amounts for each dependent child. These amounts may vary depending on the birth order of the child (first child, second child, third child and subsequent) and may be differentiated by standard family allowances and training allowances.

How to Submit the Application: Step-by-Step

Step 1: Identify the Competent Compensation Fund Verify with your municipality of residence or on the cantonal administration's website which compensation fund is territorially responsible for the Basel-Country area where you reside.

Step 2: Obtain the Application Form Download the application form from the compensation fund's website or the cantonal administration's website, or pick it up from the physical office. The form is usually available in PDF or hard copy format.

Step 3: Complete the Application Fill in the form with personal data, children's information (name, date of birth, AVS insurance number, if applicable), economic and employment situation. Attach the requested documents (birth certificates, school certificates, training certificates, income documentation).

Step 4: Submit the Application Send the completed application electronically (if the compensation fund offers online services), by regular mail, or deliver it in person to the compensation fund's office or the competent municipal office.

Step 5: Follow Up on the Application The compensation fund will communicate the outcome of the request by letter. The first payment usually occurs within 30-60 days of the application's acceptance, based on the fund's internal procedures.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the amounts of family allowances in the canton of Basel-Country?
The amounts of family allowances in the Canton of Basel-Country vary depending on the number and order of birth of the children. Each canton independently establishes the monthly amounts according to its own legislation. To know the exact current values, you must contact the competent cantonal clearing house or consult the website of the cantonal administration of Basel-Country.
How do I apply for family allowances?
The application must be submitted to the territorially competent clearing house in the canton of Basel-Country. You can fill out the form online or in paper form. Identity documents, birth certificates of the children and documentation of the economic situation must be attached. The complete procedure varies depending on the cashier, so it is advisable to check at the local office.
Who is entitled to family allowances in the canton of Basel-Country?
Parents and others entitled under federal and cantonal law are entitled. Generally, they must be residents of the canton and have dependent children. The specific legal conditions (minimum income, age of children, type of training) are established by cantonal legislation. Check the details at the clearinghouse.
What is the difference between family allowances and training allowances?
Family allowances are paid for each dependent child up to a set age. Training allowances are paid when the child is enrolled in school, vocational training or university, generally up to a maximum age set by cantonal law. The amounts and conditions vary depending on the type of service.
When does the payment of family allowances begin after the application?
The right to family allowances starts from the date of birth of the child or from the date of residence in the canton. The first payment is usually made within 30-60 days of the acceptance of the application, according to the procedures of the cantonal clearing house. For precise timing, please contact the relevant office.
