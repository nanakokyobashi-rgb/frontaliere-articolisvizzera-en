Renting quickly is easy. Renting well is another job. The Ticino market is moving, but more demand does not automatically mean fewer risks. Between a published ad and a well-rented property, there are price, presentation, speed, selection, and dossier control. Publishing four photos, writing “bright apartment,” and waiting for the phone to ring is not a real estate strategy. Sometimes it works. Sometimes it brings fifty messages in one evening, twenty requests without information, eight confirmed appointments, and three people who do not show up. Other times it brings a tenant quickly, but not the right one. And when the choice is wrong, the speed with which you rented immediately stops seeming like a success.

Because the real goal of an owner is not just to fill an apartment. It is to protect their income. To safeguard the value of the property. To choose a reliable person. To reduce downtime. To avoid mistakes that can accompany the lease relationship for years. This is the difference between renting and renting well.

The market is running, but not running the same way everywhere

The last official census available, referring to June 1, 2025, records 48,455 empty homes in Switzerland, equal to 1% of the housing stock. The empty units offered for rent had decreased by 8% compared to the previous year. In Ticino, the rate of empty homes fell from 2.08% to 1.92%, the sharpest contraction among the Swiss major regions.

Operational details

The Ticino market is not a single block. It's a multi-speed market. An analysis published in March 2026 describes sustained demand in the most attractive areas, particularly in Lugano, but highlights strong differences between regions, municipalities, neighbourhoods and types of properties. An attic in the centre of Lugano does not position itself as a large suburban apartment. A furnished two-room apartment does not speak to the same audience as a family home. A valuable item can't be presented as just any listing. And a correct price on a street can be wrong just two neighborhoods away.

The market is moving, yes. But it moves especially for those who know how to read it. The difference between renting and renting well lies in the ability to understand the dynamics of the market and adapt to its needs. This means not only publishing a listing, but also presenting the property in the right way, selecting potential tenants carefully and carefully checking their dossier.

Practical Implications for Owners

For property owners, renting well means protecting their investment. This means choosing reliable tenants who regularly pay their rent and respect the property. In addition, it is important to reduce downtime between tenants to maximize income. Hosting well takes time and attention, but the long-term benefits are significant.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure to host well

1. Accurate Presentation : Post detailed listings with high-quality photos and accurate property descriptions. 2. Selection of tenants: Carefully check the files of potential tenants, checking references and documents. 3. Lease Agreement: Draw up a clear and detailed lease agreement, specifying the rights and duties of both parties. 4. Periodic inspection: Carry out periodic inspections of the property to ensure that it is maintained in good condition. 5. Communication: Maintain open and transparent communication with tenants to resolve any issues in a timely manner.
