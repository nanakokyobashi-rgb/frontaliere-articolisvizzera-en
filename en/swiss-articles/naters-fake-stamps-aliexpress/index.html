A woman from Naters in Valais has been sentenced by criminal order after importing counterfeit Swiss stamps into Switzerland. The purchase began as a search for convenience on AliExpress, but the savings turned into a total outlay of 800 francs.

From the reduced price to the discovery of fakes

The newspaper «Le Nouvelliste» reports that the order placed by the woman on the Chinese platform included 100 Swiss stamps of 1.20 francs each. The list price of the entire order was therefore 120 francs, while on AliExpress the cost was 48.59 francs. The difference, described in the source as a saving of just over 70 francs, seemed to make the operation particularly convenient.

The decisive point came with the shipment. The package, coming from China, was intercepted by Swiss customs. The stamps were not original, but counterfeit. Following the discovery, Swiss Post brought the case to justice.

The reconstructed sequence is precise: the order left from a Chinese platform, the package was stopped at customs and the case arrived before justice. It wasn't just a difference between two prices: the goods were identified as counterfeit, with economic consequences separate from the cost of the purchase.

The amount of the sentence

The criminal order set a fine of 300 francs, to which 500 francs in court costs were added. The total reached 800 francs, a figure much higher than the savings initially sought.

The story is part of an episode already reported in the summer of 2025. After offers of counterfeit stamps appeared on AliExpress, Swiss Post had already filed a criminal complaint against unknown persons with the Office of the Attorney General of Switzerland. A spokeswoman had specified that, in the presence of forgeries, criminal proceedings are systematically initiated.

Recognizing counterfeiting, however, is not easy. A philatelic expert consulted by «20 Minuten» at the time had examined the carnets sold on the platform, finding them completely identical to the originals. Copies so accurate that they can fool even a careful eye.

The comparison that changes the result

Looking only at the purchase price, the deal seemed obvious. The price on the platform was lower than the list price and the difference was just over 70 francs. Looking instead at the full story, the cost documented by the source also includes a fine and legal costs. The final result is therefore not a small difference from the official price, but an amount of 800 francs.

For someone living in Switzerland, the case does not justify a general rule about AliExpress. The useful point is the distinction between apparent savings and the economic risk linked to the specific case: the item was a Swiss stamp, the package was intercepted by Swiss customs and Posta brought the matter before the courts. This sequence turned the reduced price into a much higher cost.

The story does not allow us to claim that every online offer is counterfeit. It does, however, show why the lowest price is not enough, on its own, to assess an expense. In the case examined, even authenticity was not easy to verify at a glance: the philately expert had judged the booklets identical to the originals, enough to deceive a watchful eye.

A checklist before ordering

AliExpress showed 48,59 francs: the difference from the listed price was just over 70 francs. 3. Consider the shipment’s origin. In the incident, the package came from China and was intercepted by Swiss customs. 4. Do not use appearance as the only guarantee.
