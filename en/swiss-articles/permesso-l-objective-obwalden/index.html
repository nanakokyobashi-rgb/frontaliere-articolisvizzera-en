What is Short-Term L Permit?

The short-term L permit is a type of residence permit granted by the Swiss Confederation for a maximum of 1 year. This permit can be granted for work, study or other activity and is valid for a maximum of 1 year.

Why was Short Term L Permit introduced?

The short-term L permit has been introduced to promote the mobility of workers, students and people who intend to carry out economic activities in Switzerland. This permit allows you to stay in Switzerland for a limited period of time, without having to apply for a longer residence permit.

How to get Short Term L Permit
