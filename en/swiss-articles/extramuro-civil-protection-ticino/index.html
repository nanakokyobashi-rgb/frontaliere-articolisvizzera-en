Civil Protection: 9 days between Ticino and Como

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 1 October 2026

Extramuro 2026 takes place from October 1 to 9: two days in Pollegio and five in the province of Como, featuring Italian and Swiss operators.

From October 1 to 9, 2026, regions in Lombardy and Switzerland will host “Extramuro 2026,” the second edition of the joint training program dedicated to cross-border civil protection. Following its debut in 2024, the initiative aims to conduct field tests of the emergency response system for major emergencies between Italy and Switzerland.

Two Phases in Ticino and the Province of Como

The program is divided between Switzerland and Italy and consists of two distinct phases. Activities on October 1 and 2 are concentrated in Pollegio, in the Canton of Ticino. From October 5 to 9, the focus of the exercise shifts to the province of Como, involving the municipalities of Erba, Merone, and Pusiano.

The training camp is located at the Erba Multipurpose Emergency Center, which also serves as the exercise headquarters. This location thus becomes the focal point for the phase taking place in the province of Como.

The portion of the exercise titled “Switzerland in Italy” is designed to train the Civil Protection Militia of the Canton of Ticino, following the reverse-scenario module conducted in June. The stated objective is to improve synergy and integration between emergency responders from the two countries in preparation for real emergencies.

Field operations are coordinated by the Lombardy Region’s General Directorate for Security and Civil Protection. The collaboration involves the Fire Department, AREU Lombardia, the Province of Como, and the Chiasso Police and Customs Cooperation Center.

During the activities, members of the public will see Italian and Swiss responders moving throughout the area in their respective vehicles and wearing their standard-issue uniforms. The event will officially conclude on Friday, October 9, with a closing ceremony at the CPE in Erba. The program thus combines an initial phase in Ticino with a second phase in the Como area, with a continued focus on joint emergency response training.
