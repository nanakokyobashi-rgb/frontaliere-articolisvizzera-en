G Permit Requirements

To obtain the G permit, border guards must comply with the following requirements:

  • Be at least 18 years of age;
  • Hold a university degree or a high school diploma issued in Switzerland or in a country that is a member of the Frontier Agreement;
  • Have at least 3 years of work experience as a consultant to clients in a country that is a member of the Frontier Agreement;
  • Have a good knowledge of the Italian language and another official language of Switzerland;
  • Have an employment contract with an authorized employer in Switzerland.

To obtain the G permit, frontier workers must comply with the requirements established by the New Frontier Agreement. In addition, they may have access to a qualification recognition. For more information, please refer to the salary calculator.

The New Frontier Agreement was approved on 6 April 2019 and enters into force on 1 May 2020. The G permit is reserved for border workers who intend to work in Switzerland as customer consultants, with a minimum salary of CHF 40,000 per year.

To be eligible, candidates must have at least one year of work experience in a similar role and demonstrate a fluent knowledge of the Italian language. In addition, they must have a professional quality certificate issued by a recognized organization.

Border workers who intend to work in Ticino must also comply with cantonal regulations on labour and immigration. In particular, they must register with the Federal Office of Immigration (UDI) and obtain a residence permit.

The G permit is valid for a period of one year and can be renewed for a maximum of three years. Border workers intending to work in Switzerland must also pay a fee of CHF 200 for registration with the UDI.
