Consultant to customers in Ticino: requirements and permit G

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 20 July 2026

Border workers working in Ticino as consultants to customers must comply with the requirements for the G permit and may have access to a recognition of the qualification

Context

In brief

  • The New Frontier Workers Agreement was signed on December 23, 2020, and entered into force on January 1, 2024.
  • Frontier workers who work in Ticino as client advisors must meet the requirements for the G permit.

Key facts

  • What: working as a client advisor in Ticino as a frontier worker.
  • When: January 1, 2024.
  • Where: Ticino.
  • Who: frontier workers.

Requirements for the G permit

To work as a client advisor in Ticino, frontier workers must meet the requirements for the G permit. These requirements include:

  • Having at least 3 years of work experience in a related field.
  • Having a basic knowledge of written and spoken German.
  • Completing a training course of at least 60 hours for the field of work.
  • Complying with occupational safety and health regulations.

Operational checklist

For frontier workers who wish to work as client advisors in Ticino, here is an operational checklist:

  • Check whether the requirements for the G permit are met.
  • Submit the G permit application within 30 days of the work start date.
  • Complete the training course of at least 60 hours for the field of work.
  • Comply with occupational safety and health regulations.

Concrete examples

Here are some concrete examples of how the requirements for the G permit may be applied in practice:

  • A frontier worker with 3 years of work experience in a related field of client advising may submit the G permit application without completing additional training courses.
  • A frontier worker with a basic knowledge of written and spoken German may meet the language requirement without additional training courses.
  • A frontier worker who wishes to work as a client advisor in a field different from the one in which they previously worked must complete a training course of at least 60 hours for the field of work.

Comparison of practical scenarios

Here are some comparisons between practical scenarios for frontier workers who wish to work as client advisors in Ticino:

  • If a frontier worker has at least 3 years of work experience in a related field, they may work as a client advisor in Ticino without additional training courses.
  • If a frontier worker does not have at least 3 years of work experience in a related field, they must complete a training course of at least 60 hours for the field of work.

Regulations and amounts

The regulations concerning the G permit for frontier workers who work as client advisors in Ticino are as follows:

  • The Frontier Workers Agreement of December 23, 2020.
  • The Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration of December 16, 2008.
  • The Ticino cantonal law on immigration of June 17, 2019.

The amounts for the G permit for frontier workers who work as client advisors in Ticino are not specified.

Conclusions

In summary, frontier workers who wish to work as client advisors in Ticino must meet the requirements for the G permit. These requirements include having at least 3 years of work experience in a related field, having a basic knowledge of written and spoken German, completing a training course of at least 60 hours for the field of work, and complying with occupational safety and health regulations.

Customer Advisor in Ticino: Requirements and G Permit

Cross-border workers who work in Ticino as customer advisors must meet the requirements for the G permit. The New Cross-Border Commuter Agreement was signed on December 23, 2020 and entered into force on January 1, 2024.

Requirements for the G permit

To obtain the G permit, cross-border workers must meet the following requirements:

  • Be at least 18 years old;
  • Hold a university degree or high school diploma issued in Switzerland or in a country party to the Cross-Border Commuter Agreement;
  • Have at least 3 years of work experience as a customer advisor in a country party to the Cross-Border Commuter Agreement;
  • Have a good command of Italian and another official language of Switzerland;
  • Have an employment contract with an authorized employer in Switzerland.

Allowance and exemptions

Cross-border workers who already held the G permit before July 17, 2023 are exempt from the €7'500 allowance, while new cross-border workers must comply with the €10'000 allowance. 📊

Here are some concrete examples:

  • A 35-year-old customer advisor with 5 years of work experience in a country party to the Cross-Border Commuter Agreement can apply for the G permit without having to pay the allowance.
  • A new 25-year-old customer advisor with 2 years of work experience in a country party to the Cross-Border Commuter Agreement must pay the €10'000 allowance to obtain the G permit.

Relevant cantons and cities

Client advisor in Ticino: requirements and G permit

To obtain the G permit, cross-border commuters must meet the requirements established by the New Cross-Border Commuter Agreement. In addition, they may have access to recognition of their educational qualification. For further information, consult the salary calculator.

The New Cross-Border Commuter Agreement was approved on April 6, 2019, and comes into force on May 1, 2020. The G permit is reserved for cross-border commuters who intend to work in Switzerland as client advisors, with a minimum salary of 40,000 CHF per year.

To be eligible, candidates must have at least one year of work experience in a similar role and demonstrate fluent knowledge of Italian. In addition, they must have a professional quality certificate issued by a recognized organization.

Cross-border commuters who intend to work in Ticino must also comply with cantonal regulations on employment and immigration. In particular, they must register with the Federal Office of Immigration (UDI) and obtain a residence permit.

The G permit is valid for a period of one year and can be renewed for a maximum of three years. Cross-border commuters who intend to work in Switzerland must also pay a fee of 200 CHF for registration with the UDI.
