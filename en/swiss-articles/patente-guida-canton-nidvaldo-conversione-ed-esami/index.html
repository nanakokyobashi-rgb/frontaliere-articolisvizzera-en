Canton of Nidvaldo: requirements and procedures

The Canton of Nidvaldo requires the presentation of a certificate of good conduct, issued by the local police, for an amount of CHF 50. In addition, you need to take a theoretical exam on Swiss road legislation, which can be taken in Italian, French, German or English. The theoretical exam consists of 40 questions, with a time limit of 60 minutes. The passing threshold is 60% of correct answers.
