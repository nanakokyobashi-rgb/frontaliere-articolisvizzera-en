F-35, the SP calls for a Parliamentary Committee of Inquiry

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 14 September 2026

The Socialist Party launches the process for an ICC on the purchase of F-35 fighters, aiming to clarify costs, procedures and transparency towards Parliament.

Context

In brief

  • The SP requests a Parliamentary Investigation Commission on the F-35s
  • Objective: to clarify the procurement procedure and any potential errors
  • Suspicions of distorted costs and incorrect information provided to parliamentarians
  • Council of States member Franziska Roth is leading the political initiative

Key facts

  • Proposing party: Social Democratic Party (SP)
  • Subject: Purchase of F-35 fighter jets
  • Key figure: Franziska Roth (Council of States member)
  • Precedent: Similar request by Pierre-Alain Fridez
  • PIC objective: Full clarification on costs and procedures

The Social Democratic Party has officially announced its intention to request the establishment of a Parliamentary Investigation Commission (PIC) to fully shed light on the acquisition of the F-35 fighter jets. The decision, which follows what was already anticipated by the Sonntagszeitung, aims to ascertain whether the critical issues that emerged during the selection procedure are attributable to individual errors or institutional shortcomings within the Federal Department of Defence, Civil Protection and Sports. The SP parliamentary group intends to submit a formal initiative after completing the necessary internal discussions.

Commission objectives

The request for a PIC is not limited to a superficial check, but aims at an in-depth examination of the treatment reserved for the various bidders during the tender phase. The SP expresses strong doubts about the transparency of the process, asking to verify whether the acquisition and operating costs were deliberately manipulated to favor the F-35 over other available options. A central point of the inquiry will concern the information provided regarding dependence on the foreign supplier: the socialists intend to ascertain whether the Department disseminated incorrect data, thereby influencing the decisions made during parliamentary voting. Council of States member Franziska Roth, an expert in security policy, has been designated as the main political promoter of the initiative, arguing that complete clarification is the only way to restore trust in institutions. As early as last Thursday, former National Councillor Pierre-Alain Fridez had raised similar suspicions, speaking openly about the possibility that a deliberate will to influence political consensus lies behind the management of fixed prices. The proposal now awaits parliamentary debate, where the bourgeois majority may offer resistance.

Operational details

The Socialist Party's initiative raises institutional issues that go far beyond the mere technical choice of the combat aircraft. At the center of the debate is the correctness of the decision-making process that led to the approval of the purchase of the F-35s, a multi-billion-dollar contract that weighs directly on the federal budget and, consequently, on the national tax burden. Concerns regarding the potential manipulation of operating and procurement costs directly affect the effectiveness of parliamentary control, a fundamental pillar of the Swiss democratic system. If the hypotheses of incorrect information transmitted to the Chambers were confirmed, the implications for administrative transparency would be significant, calling into question the responsibility of departmental leadership in managing such complex international supply contracts.

Impacts on institutional trust

The need for a Parliamentary Investigation Commission (PIC) arises at a time when trust in the actions of the Department of Defense is the focal point of the political dispute. For those living in Switzerland, the impact of such events is reflected in the perception of public resource management. Transparency in state procurement processes is essential to ensure that taxpayers' money is used according to criteria of efficiency and competitive fairness. The analysis conducted by the SP focuses on the risk of discrimination among bidders, a practice which, if ascertained, would alter the principles of equality that should govern federal public procurement. The clash with the bourgeois majority in Parliament promises to be arduous, as the latter has so far supported the government's line on the fighter jet purchase. However, the possible establishment of an inquiry commission would allow access to documents and minutes that would otherwise be confidential, offering a clearer view of the chain of command and the decisions made during crucial phases. The issue is not only the fixed price mentioned by Fridez, but the entire information framework provided to the competent parliamentary committees. More rigorous management of procurement is not just a political request, but a necessity to ensure that the federal administration operates within verifiable limits of correctness. Key points

The procedure for establishing a parliamentary inquiry commission follows a precise legislative process within the Federal Parliament. Once the parliamentary initiative is submitted, it must pass the scrutiny of the competent committees and subsequently be approved by the plenary of both Chambers. The support of the Greens, already deemed probable, might not be sufficient to guarantee the necessary majority, making negotiation with the other parliamentary groups the most critical step for the SP. For citizens interested in following the evolution of this dossier, it is essential to monitor the sessions of the Federal Chambers and the reports that will be produced by the Security Policy Committee. This is not an issue that will be resolved quickly, but rather a verification process that could last for months, leading to possible hearings of officials and heads of the Department of Defense.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why is the Socialist Party calling for the establishment of an ICC?
The SP aims to clarify whether, during the selection and purchase procedure of the F-35 fighters, individual mistakes were made or whether there were institutional deficiencies in the Federal Department of Defence, Population Protection and Sport. The objective is to ascertain whether the information provided to parliamentarians, including data on purchase and operating costs, has been manipulated, thus influencing the decisions taken during the vote.
Who is leading the political initiative for this inquiry?
The key figure promoting the initiative is the advisor to the States Franziska Roth, an expert on security policy. Roth argues that full clarification is the only possible method of restoring trust in institutions. Previously, the former national councillor Pierre-Alain Fridez had also raised similar suspicions regarding the management of fixed prices and the potential willingness to condition political consensus during the process of approving the contract.
What is the process envisaged to establish the Commission?
The establishment of a parliamentary committee of inquiry follows a strict legislative process. After the presentation of the formal initiative by the Socialist Group, the proposal must pass the scrutiny of the competent committees and subsequently obtain the approval of the plenum of both Federal Chambers. This is a complex process in which the PS will have to negotiate with the other parliamentary groups, given the possible resistance of the bourgeois majority that has so far supported the government line.
