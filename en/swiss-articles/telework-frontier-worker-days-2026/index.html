Analysis of the New Rules

The new rules on teleworking for frontier workers represent a significant change in the management of remote work for this category of workers. The maximum limit of 25% of working days in teleworking will be a challenge for many border workers who have adopted remote work as their main mode of work.

Adapting Companies Companies will need to adapt to the new rules and make sure they comply with the provisions. This may require organisational changes and investment in technologies to support remote working.

The new rules could also offer benefits for frontier workers, such as greater flexibility and reduced travel times. However, it will be crucial to ensure that frontier workers are aware of their responsibilities and tax obligations.

The Swiss and Italian authorities will need to work together to ensure that the new rules are enforced effectively and that border guards are able to comply with them. This may require greater cooperation and exchange of information between the two nations.
