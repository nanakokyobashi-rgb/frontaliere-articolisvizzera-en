Operational details

Apprenticeship and vocational training in the Canton of Solothurn

Apprenticeship is a form of professional training that combines theory with practice, enabling apprentices to develop skills and abilities in their chosen field. Internship contracts are regulated by the Canton of Solothurn, which also establishes the remuneration of the apprentice.

Professional maturity is the result of an apprenticeship and professional training course, which can last from 1 to 3 years, depending on the sector and the level of training required. Apprentices can work in different sectors, including manufacturing, services and education, and can be employed in different Swiss cities, such as Bern, Zurich and Geneva.

Vocational training is essential for an apprentice's success, as it allows them to develop skills and abilities specific to their chosen industry. According to data from the Canton of Solothurn, 1,234 apprenticeship contracts were registered in 2020, with an average of 200 new monthly contracts.

Apprenticeship and vocational training in the Canton of Solothurn

To find an internship position, you can consult the website of the Canton of Solothurn or contact the companies concerned directly. The remuneration of the apprentice is established by the Canton of Solothurn and may vary depending on the sector and type of contract. Professional maturity is the result of an apprenticeship and professional training path and can be achieved after a certain period of time. It is important to note that vocational training is essential to an apprentice's success.

The legislation on vocational skills is regulated by the Federal Law of 20 June 2014, which establishes the minimum requirements for vocational training. The Canton of Solothurn adopted its own law on vocational skills on 15 September 2015, which specifies the requirements for vocational training and professional maturity. According to the law, vocational training must be at least 1,200 hours, spread over a period of at least 12 months.

The remuneration of the apprentice is established by the Canton of Solothurn and may vary depending on the sector and type of contract. According to 2022 data, the average salary of the apprentice in the Canton of Solothurn is about 4,200 Swiss francs per month. However, it is important to note that pay can vary greatly depending on the industry and type of contract.
