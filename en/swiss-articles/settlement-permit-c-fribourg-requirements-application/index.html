Permit c canton Freiburg: requirements and application

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 22 September 2026

Rules, residence times, integration criteria and procedure for obtaining residence permit C in the Canton of Freiburg.

Context

In short

  • Ordinary release after 10 years of residence, reduced to 5 for EU/EFTA citizens.
  • Language skills and respect for public order are key requirements.
  • The application involves the municipal inhabitants control and the cantonal authorities.

Key facts

  • Type of document: Residence permit C (SEM)
  • Ordinary years of residence: 10 years
  • Years for EU/EFTA citizens: 5 years
  • Reference federal authority: SEM
  • Applicable tax system: federal IFD, cantonal tax and municipal multiplier

The transition from residence permit B to residence permit C represents a fundamental step for foreign residents established in the Canton of Fribourg. The Swiss legal framework, coordinated at a federal level by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) and applied by the individual cantons, gives the C permit an indefinite residence status, not subject to the existence of specific employment contracts or time constraints for annual renewal.

Length of stay and basic requirements

For citizens belonging to the European Union or EFTA, federal practice establishes the possibility of submitting an application after 5 years of uninterrupted and regular residence with a B permit in Switzerland. For citizens of third countries, the ordinary minimum duration is set at 10 years of uninterrupted residence. In certain circumstances, an early concession can be assessed upon completion of 5 years of residence even for particularly integrated third-party citizens.

The Friborg authorities rigorously examine the integration process. The applicant must demonstrate that he respects public safety and order, shares constitutional values ​​and does not depend on social assistance. The absence of criminal proceedings and total regularity in the execution register extract are essential conditions. Furthermore, active participation in economic life, through stable employment or training, is verified through tax and social security certificates, in synergy with the contributions paid to the first AVS/AI pillar and the second LPP/BVG pillar. To verify the impact on disposable income and withholdings, it is possible to consult the [salary calculator] (nav:calculator) to evaluate the financial flows before starting the process.

Operational details

Integration criteria and language skills

In the Canton of Fribourg, a bilingual area with a strong French- and German-speaking presence, knowledge of the official language spoken in the municipality of residence is a central requirement for granting domicile. The candidate must certify their oral and written skills according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages, by presenting linguistic certificates recognized at federal level (such as the FIDE language passport or equivalent diplomas).

For the ordinary issuing of the C permit, oral skills corresponding to at least level A2 and written skills equal to level A1 in the language of the municipality (French or German) are normally required. In the event that early release is requested for successful integration after 5 years, the linguistic requirements become more stringent, requiring the achievement of a B1 level in oral and A2 in written.

Financial stability and social security compliance

Financial stability represents a further pillar of evaluation. The administration examines the history of tax payments at all levels: direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax calculated via the local multiplier. The timely fulfillment of tax obligations and the total absence of enforcement orders in the last three years are binding requirements. The compulsory health coverage through [KVG health insurance] (nav:health), stipulated upon entry into Switzerland, must be regularly paid, without certificates of lack of assets to be paid by the applicant.

Key points

Procedura operativa per la domanda a Friburgo

L'inoltro della richiesta di rilascio del permesso di domicilio C nel Cantone di Friburgo segue un iter strutturato che inizia a livello comunale. Di norma, l'ufficio cantonale della popolazione invia al cittadino straniero un modulo di proroga o trasformazione in prossimita' della scadenza del permesso B. Qualora cio' non avvenga in modo automatico, il titolare puo' sollecitare l'apertura del fascicolo direttamente presso il controllo abitanti del proprio comune di domicilio.

La documentazione da allegare comprende:

  • Copia del documento di viaggio in corso di validita' e del permesso B attuale.
  • Estratto recente del casellario giudiziale e certificato dell'ufficio esecuzioni e fallimenti.
  • Contratto di lavoro valido o ultime buste paga a riprova dell'indipendenza economica.
  • Certificato linguistico riconosciuto (FIDE, DELF/DALF o Goethe-Zertifikat).
  • Attestazione di non percezione di prestazioni di aiuto sociale rilasciata dai servizi sociali competenti.

Una volta ricevuta la documentazione dal comune, l'autorita' cantonale friburghese per la popolazione e le migrazioni esamina la conformita' del dossier, in consultazione con la SEM. La decisione viene comunicata per iscritto. In caso di esito favorevole, il richiedente viene convocato per l'acquisizione dei dati biometrici necessari alla stampa della carta formato carta di credito del permesso C.

Il titolare del permesso di domicilio C gode di una parita' di trattamento quasi completa rispetto ai cittadini svizzeri nel mercato del lavoro e nell'accesso agli alloggi, e non e' piu' soggetto all'imposta alla fonte, transitando verso la tassazione ordinaria con obbligo di dichiarazione delle imposte. Il possesso ininterrotto del permesso C costituisce inoltre il prerequisito fondamentale per avviare, dopo 10 anni complessivi di dimora federale, la procedura di naturalizzazione ordinaria.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the minimum residence times to apply for the C permit in Freiburg?
For citizens of the European Union or EFTA, 5 years of uninterrupted and regular residence with B permit are required. For third-country nationals, the ordinary period is 10 years, which can be reduced to 5 years in cases of early concession for successful integration.
What language level is required for the ordinary C permit in the Canton of Freiburg?
In the Canton of Freiburg, depending on the official language spoken in the municipality of residence (French or German), an oral competence of level A2 and a written competence of level A1, attested by recognized certifications, are required for the ordinary procedure.
How does taxation change with the transition to residence permit C?
With the issuance of the permit C, the worker leaves the system of drawing the tax at source and goes on to ordinary taxation, filling out the income tax return annually for direct federal, cantonal and municipal tax.
