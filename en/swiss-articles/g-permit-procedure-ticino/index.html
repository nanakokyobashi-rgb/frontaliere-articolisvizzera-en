Context

  • Mandatory notification before starting any new work activity.

  • Penalties provided for in the event of delay in submitting the application.

Key facts

  • What: New G permit request for border guards.
  • When: Before the start of the activity or job change.
  • Where: Migration Office of the Canton of Ticino.
  • Who: Foreign workers without residence in Switzerland.
  • Base cost: 15 CHF for biometric data and 10 CHF for residence card.

The start of a lucrative activity in Switzerland by a foreign national who does not intend to transfer his residence to the Swiss territory requires obtaining the G permit. This authorization is strictly linked to the exercise of an employee activity at an employer with operational headquarters in the Canton of Ticino. Current legislation requires that any new recruitment, as well as any change in the workplace, must be notified in advance to the Migration Office.

Notification and Residency Obligations

Operational details

The document control procedure

A fundamental step for the regularization of the worker's position is the verification of the identity. Workers who intend to operate in Ticino must go in person to one of the designated Gendarmeria posts for the control of their own identity document. This operation is a prerequisite for the release of the permit. The document, once verified, will be accompanied by a certified copy in the original issued by the Gendarmeria post itself. Such certification must be strictly attached to the official request to be sent to the Migration Office.

Obligations and Career Management

The management of your career as a border worker in Ticino requires constant monitoring of administrative deadlines. In addition to the initial phase of requesting a permit, every change of employer triggers the obligation to notify again. It is not allowed to start a new activity without first obtaining formal approval from the Migration Office. Timely planning is therefore the critical factor in avoiding pecuniary sanctions. In case of doubts about the documents to be attached or the specific requirements for particular professions, the legal bases and general information provided by the cantonal administration represent the primary reference guide.

Frequently Asked Questions
What happens if the G permit application is submitted late?
Failure to submit the application within the established deadlines, or before the start of work, may result in the application of a fine. It is essential to notify the Migration Office of the commencement of each new activity or job change prior to recruitment to avoid penalties.
Does the border worker have to return home every day?
The border worker must return at least once a week to his main domicile abroad. If he does not return daily, he is obliged to notify his presence to the Swiss municipality where he stays during the working week.
What documents are required for the G permit application?
You must go to a Gendarmerie post to check your identity document. The copy of the document, certified in original by the Gendarmerie, must be attached to the application for the issue of the permit to be sent to the Migration Office before the start of the work.
