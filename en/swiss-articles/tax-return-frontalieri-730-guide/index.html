Border Income Statement 730: Complete Guide (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 25 June 2026

Find out how to fill out the 730 tax return for border workers, the necessary documents and deadlines.

Context

In short - The 730 tax return is mandatory for frontier workers. - The model 730 is specific for frontier workers. - The deadline for submission is September 30. - The work certificate is essential. ## Key facts - What: Tax return 730 - When: Due 30 September - Where: Italy - Who: Border workers - Amount: Unspecified The tax return for border workers is a crucial step to avoid penalties and penalties. Form 730 is specific to frontier workers and must be filled out carefully. The deadline for submission is September 30, and the work certificate is critical to prove hours worked and income earned. The tax credit allows you to deduct the taxes paid in Switzerland from the Italian tax return, facilitating the completion of the tax return. It is important to meet this deadline to avoid penalties and penalties. In case of delay, it is possible to submit a supplementary declaration, but it is advisable to do so as soon as possible. To facilitate the compilation of the tax return, you can use the online service of the Revenue Agency.

Operational details

Documents Needed To complete the 730 tax return, frontier workers need to collect a set of documents. Among them, the work certificate is essential to prove the hours worked and the income received. Other required documents include the cud template, which reports income earned during the year, and any certifications of medical expenses or other tax deductions. It is important to have all the documents in order to avoid errors in the compilation of the declaration. ### Compilation Procedure The procedure for filling in the tax return 730 can be carried out online through the Revenue Agency service. This service allows you to fill in the declaration in a simple and fast way, avoiding common errors. You can also use the support of an accountant or CAF to make sure the return is filled out correctly. In any case, it is important to follow the instructions provided by the Revenue Agency to avoid errors that could lead to penalties.

Key points

Deadlines and Penalties The deadline for filing your 730 tax return is September 30. It is important to meet this deadline to avoid penalties and penalties. In case of delay, it is possible to submit a supplementary declaration, but it is advisable to do so as soon as possible. Penalties for late filing can be significant, so it's critical to meet deadlines. ### Use of the Tax Credit The tax credit is a mechanism provided for by the Italian-Swiss convention against double taxation. This credit allows you to deduct the taxes paid in Switzerland from the Italian tax return, facilitating the compilation of the tax return. It is important to use this credit to avoid paying double taxes and to optimize your tax situation. ### Conclusion The 730 tax return is a mandatory step for frontier workers and must be completed carefully. By using the online service of the Revenue Agency and following the instructions provided, you can fill out the return easily and quickly. It is important to meet deadlines and use the tax credit to avoid penalties and optimize your tax situation.
