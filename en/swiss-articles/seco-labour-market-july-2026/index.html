Dry labour market: 3% unemployment in July 2026 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 13 September 2026

SECO data for July 2026 published: unemployment rate at 3%, unemployed increasing by 7.8% on an annual basis and 45,156 registered vacancies.

Context

In brief

  • 7.8% increase in unemployed persons year-on-year
  • Unemployment rate at 3% in July 2026
  • 45,156 job vacancies registered with regional employment centers (RAV/URC)

Key facts

  • What: Swiss labor market surveys
  • When: July 2026
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: State Secretariat for Economic Affairs SECO
  • Amount: 3% unemployment rate

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs SECO has published the official surveys regarding the labor market situation in Switzerland for the month of July 2026. Compared to the same month of the previous year, the number of unemployed persons increased by 10,122, registering a percentage growth of 7.8%. In July 2026, the unemployment rate increased by 0.1 percentage points compared to the previous month, settling exactly at 3.0%. Analyzing the seasonally adjusted data, the number of unemployed persons increased by 959, equal to a 0.7% increase compared to the previous month, reaching a total of 148,002 people. In the same period, the seasonally adjusted unemployment rate remained unchanged at 3.1%. These data provide a detailed overview of national employment trends, highlighting the variations affecting different age groups and various Swiss productive sectors.

Operational details

The analysis of the data provided by SECO for the month of July 2026 also allows for a careful examination of the situation regarding job vacancies and the phenomenon of short-time work in Switzerland. In detail, the job vacancies announced at the ORCs in July 2026 amounted to 45,156, registering a decrease of 2,088 units compared to the previous month, equal to -4.4%. Of these, 29,738 positions, representing 65.9% of the total, are subject to the notification obligation. Compared to the same period of the previous year, there is an increase of 5,351 job vacancies, equal to 13.4%. Net of seasonal variations, for the month of July 2026 there is a decrease in job vacancies equal to 275 units, or -0.6% compared to the previous month, for a total of 43,244. Regarding the exhaustion of the right to unemployment benefits, the data available at the end of July 2026 indicate that the persons who exhausted this right in May 2026 were 2,629. This is 46 more people, equal to an increase of 1.8% compared to April 2026. Due to the payment practice, unemployment fund data relating to the exhaustion of rights and short-time work are published with a delay of two to three months and may be subject to subsequent changes.

Key points

Regular consultation of data published by SECO is a fundamental tool for understanding the evolution of the Swiss labour market, offering workers, businesses and economic operators valuable indicators on employment trends. Those facing a situation of professional transition or unemployment in Switzerland must carefully follow the procedures established by the Regional URC Placement Offices, strictly respecting the deadlines and obligations of active job search provided for by federal legislation. It is essential to register promptly with the URC of territorial competence to activate the practices related to unemployment benefits and access the support and professional reintegration services offered on Swiss territory.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the unemployment rate in Switzerland in July 2026?
In July 2026 the unemployment rate in Switzerland stood at exactly 3.0%, registering an increase of 0.1 percentage points compared to the previous month. Compared to the same month of the previous year, the number of unemployed increased by 10,122 units, marking a percentage growth of 7.8%.
How many vacancies are registered at the URCs?
The vacancies announced at the URC Regional Placement Offices in July 2026 were 45,156, registering a decrease of 2,088 units compared to the previous month, equal to -4.4%. Of these, 29,738 places are subject to the announcement obligation. On an annual basis, there was an increase of 5,351 vacancies.
What is the situation of youth unemployment and unemployment for older workers?
In July 2026, there were 12,294 unemployed young people between the ages of 15 and 24, an increase of 4.7% compared to the previous month and a youth unemployment rate of 2.8%. For older workers between the ages of 50 and 64, the total number of unemployed is 38,745, with a rate of 2.7%.
