Swiss more likely to spend. Especially for travel

Switzerland is known for its stable economy and its ability to attract investment from all over the world. However, despite its stability, Switzerland is not immune to the spending flows of its citizens. In fact, according to PostFinance's consumption indicator, the travel sector was the driving force behind the increase in consumption in July, with an increase of 1.1%.

This increase has been observed across the country, but with some regional differences. For example, in the Canton of Ticino, the travel sector saw an increase of 2.5% compared to the same period of the previous year, while in the Canton of Bern the increase was 1.5%.

Leisure spending also increased, with an increase of 3.2% compared to the same period of the previous year. This increase has been observed across the country, but with some regional differences. Operational details

The travel sector recorded a significant increase, reaching the highest since the beginning of the measurements. Leisure spending has also increased, with dinners at the restaurant among the preferences of the Swiss. However, sports activity remained contained.

According to data recently published by the Federal Statistical Office (OFS), the travel sector recorded an increase of 15% compared to the same period last year. This increase is particularly significant considering that 2020 was a difficult year for the tourism industry due to the Covid-19 pandemic.

Leisure spending has also increased, with dinners at the restaurant among the preferences of the Swiss. According to a survey conducted by the marketing company "Swiss Market Research", 60% of Swiss said they had consumed at least once a month in a restaurant or bar.

However, sports activity remained contained. According to OFS data, only 12% of Swiss said they had practiced a sport at least once a week. This is probably due to the fact that many Swiss have had to reduce their spending to maintain their financial stability.
