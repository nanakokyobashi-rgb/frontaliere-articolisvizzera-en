Food initiative: decisively rejected by the cantons

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 September 2026

The food initiative was decisively rejected by the Swiss cantons. In Ticino, those opposed reached 68.66%.

The vote in the Cantons

On 16 August 2024, the initiative was submitted with 112'736 valid signatures. The Keystone-ATS news report states that the text was rejected by all 16 Cantons for which counting had been completed. The opening of the report thus rules out profound structural change in Swiss agriculture over the next ten years.

In Ticino, votes against amounted to 68,66%, one of the lowest shares indicated. In Graubünden, rejection reached 75,01%. Rejection was more decisive, with over 80% of votes against, in Uri, Schwyz, Appenzell Innerrhoden and Obwalden: respectively 80,82%, 81,02%, 80,29% and 80,26%. Nidwalden expressed a slightly lower rejection, amounting to 79,85%.

The proposal and the campaign

The exact name was «For a secure food supply – by strengthening sustainable domestic production, more plant-based foodstuffs and clean drinking water (Food Initiative)». Behind the text were Franziska Herren of the association «Acqua pulita per tutti» and six other people. The initiative called for significantly greater Swiss agricultural production, in order to reduce dependence on foreign countries and increase food security. It also aimed for more production and consumption of plant-based rather than animal-based foods, for the net self-sufficiency rate to rise from the current 45% to at least 70% within ten years, and for better protection of the environment and drinking water.

Political scientist Lukas Golder stated on Swiss television SRF that the issues were interesting, but their implementation too rapid and radical. The text received no support from the political establishment. The small committee conducted much of the campaign on its own: after initial support, environmental associations distanced themselves; Greenpeace Svizzera and the Greens allowed a free vote, while all other parties recommended voting no. The latest poll before the vote forecast a 64% rejection.

Operational details

What the result means

The most concrete way to read the outcome is to distinguish between the starting point and the proposed target. In the source, 45% is the net self-sufficiency rate indicated as the current level; 70% was the minimum threshold that the text called for reaching within ten years. For those living in Switzerland, this difference makes clear that the higher number does not describe an already achieved situation: it is the political objective of the initiative rejected in the 16 Cantons where counting had been completed.

The second point concerns the direction of change. The proposal was not limited to calling for more Swiss foodstuffs. It linked increased production to reduced dependence on foreign countries, a greater share of plant-based foods than animal-based ones, and better protection of the environment and drinking water. The source described implementation as too rapid and radical; its outcome does not open up, over the next ten years, the profound structural change indicated at the outset.

How to use the result

Anyone who wants to verify the news can follow a five-step sequence, all anchored in the reported data.

A five-step procedure

1. Note the origin of the initiative. The date is 16 August 2024, with 112'736 valid signatures. In your notes, it should be indicated as the filing date, because that is how the source presents it. 2. Separate baseline and objective. Write 45% next to the current net self-sufficiency rate and 70% next to the minimum objective; add the ten-year horizon. 3. Order the territorial outcomes. Start with Ticino, at 68.66%, and Grisons, at 75.01%; then record the threshold of over 80% and the values for Uri, Schwyz, Appenzell Innerrhoden and Obwalden, as well as Nidwalden's 79.85%. 4. Check the scope. The news concerns the 16 Cantons with the count completed. Do not automatically turn that block of results into data referring to Cantons not mentioned. 5. Distinguish forecast from vote. The 64% belongs to the last poll before the vote. It must be kept separate from the cantonal results actually reported.

This sequence makes it possible to avoid using 70% as if it had already been reached, or 64% as if it were a result. It also helps avoid attributing to individual Cantons motivations that the source does not set out. Frequently Asked Questions
When was the food initiative filed?
The initiative was officially filed on August 16, 2024 with a total of 112,736 valid signatures collected by the committee led by Franziska Herren of the association Clean Water for All and Six Other People. The text aimed to radically change the Swiss agricultural sector.
What were the results of the vote in Ticino and Graubünden?
In the 16 cantons for which the stripping was completed, the initiative was rejected. In Ticino the votes against reached 68.66%, while in Graubünden the rejection stood at 75.01%. Even clearer hiccups, exceeding the obsolete threshold of 80%, were recorded in Uri, Unwit, Appenzello interno and Obvaldo.
What exactly did the rejected proposal provide for?
The text called for increasing Switzerland's net self-sufficiency rate from the current 45% to at least 70% within ten years. It also provided for a significantly higher agricultural production of plant foodstuffs than animal foodstuffs, aiming at reducing dependence on foreigners and better protection of the environment and drinking water.
