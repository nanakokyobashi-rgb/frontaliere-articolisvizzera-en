Cantons and rates: a 2026 comparison

Switzerland is known for its complex tax system, which includes federal and cantonal taxes. Each canton has its own tax rates and rules, which can vary greatly from canton to canton. For example, the canton of Zurich has a cantonal tax of 5.3% for income from work, while Italy has an IRPEF of 23% up to 28,000 euros.

Concrete examples

  • An employee earning CHF 100,000 per year in Zurich will pay about CHF5,300 in cantonal taxes, while a colleague earning the same sum in Italy will pay about CHF 23,400 in IRPEF.
  • An entrepreneur earning CHF 200,000 per year in Geneva will pay about CHF10,600 in cantonal taxes, while a colleague earning the same sum in Milan will pay about CHF 46,000 in IRPEF.
