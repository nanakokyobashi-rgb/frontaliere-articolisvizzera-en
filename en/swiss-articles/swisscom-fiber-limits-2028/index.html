Optical fiber: Swisscom prices frozen until 2028

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 October 2026

Swisscom prices unchanged until the end of September 2028 Two-year extension of tariff limits Maximum 24 francs per month, excluding VAT No increase for the items

Context

TL;DR

  • Swisscom prices unchanged until the end of September 2028
  • Two-year extension of tariff caps
  • Maximum of 24 francs per month, excluding VAT
  • No increase for one-off charges

Key facts

  • Expiry: end of September 2028
  • Extension: two years
  • Maximum: 24 francs per month, excluding VAT
  • Operators: Sunrise, Salt and Init7
  • One-off charges: no increase

Until the end of September 2028, Swisscom will keep unchanged the prices it charges other telecommunications operators for use of the fiber-optic network. The extension was agreed with Price Surveillance and extends the tariff limits already in force by two years.

The previous regulation was due to expire at the end of the month. Before extending the agreement, Mister Prezzi analyzed updated information provided by Swisscom on the costs and revenues of the fiber-optic network. The outcome was announced today in a newsletter issued by Stefan Meierhans's office.

What remains unchanged

For Swisscom's competitors, including Sunrise, Salt and Init7, the financial benchmark remains unchanged: they will continue to pay what they have paid to date, with a maximum of 24 francs per month, excluding VAT. This figure concerns the price Swisscom charges other operators for use of the fiber-optic network.

The confirmation also covers one-off charges. The federal office told the Awp news agency that no other prices of this type will be increased, including the fee for a new activation.

The agreement thus extends the existing arrangement without changing the monthly cap and without adjusting the one-off charges listed in the communication. The decision stems from the updated data examined by Mister Prezzi, not from a new amount announced for the service.

For those who live or work in Switzerland, the distinction to keep in mind is the nature of the price: the source refers to the sums paid by competitors to Swisscom, not to an amount presented as a direct tariff for the end customer. The time frame indicated is precise: the current conditions remain in force until the end of September 2028.

The shift from the end-of-month expiry to the new 2028 deadline is at the heart of the decision. The communication contains no new cap: the limits already applied are being extended, along with confirmation that the one-off charges will not increase.

Operational details

The before-and-after comparison

The practical result of the extension is the continuity of the framework for operators that use Swisscom's fiber-optic network. The difference compared with the previous situation is not a new amount, but the continuation of the agreement beyond the expiry that had been scheduled for the end of the month. The period indicated extends the current caps until the end of September 2028.

The boundary between network and offering

The economic figure should be understood within its scope. The 24 francs per month, excluding VAT, are what Swisscom's competitors pay for the use of the fiber-optic network. The text does not present this figure as an amount paid directly by the end customer. Therefore, the extension alone does not make it possible to describe the price of a subscription or a bill.

The same applies to one-off charges. The new activation is cited as an example of a price that will not be increased, but it remains separate from the monthly cap. Separating the two elements avoids improperly adding an initial charge to a recurring amount.

For Sunrise, Salt and Init7, the communication indicates that the payments already in effect will continue, within the maximum cap. The source does not link the extension to a specific commercial offering from the operators. Key points

A checklist for reading the data

Anyone who wants to correctly apply the information to an offer or communication from their operator can start with the items cited in the source. The goal is not to confuse the extension of the limits with a single tariff for everyone.

Four steps

1. Identify who the price applies to. Verify that the item concerns what Swisscom charges other operators for the use of fiber optics. This is the scope of the agreement.

2. Separate the billing frequencies. The limit of 24 francs concerns the monthly payment and is indicated excluding VAT. The fee for a new activation, on the other hand, belongs among the one-off items.

3. Identify the operators mentioned. Sunrise, Salt and Init7 are the examples given in the communication. The wording indicates that they will continue to pay what they have paid so far, up to the specified maximum.

4. Note the deadline. The date to keep as a reference is the end of September 2028. Until then, the extension maintains the current tariff limits described in the source.

This sequence makes it possible to read a potential commercial text without assigning a different meaning to the data. The monthly amount must not be automatically added to the activation fee, and the one-off fee must not be treated as a recurring charge. The condition that VAT is excluded must also remain visible in any numerical comparison.

The procedure does not require adding figures or conditions that the communication does not indicate. If the information concerns a competitor other than the three examples mentioned, the certain fact remains the general one: the extension concerns the other operators and maintains the current limits.
