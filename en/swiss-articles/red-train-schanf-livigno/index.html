In a nutshell

The Canton of Graubünden wants to work together with the partners of Lombardy to create a railway connection between the Engadine and Alta Valtellina. The project involves the construction of a route of over 14 kilometres to connect S-chanf and Livigno in about ten minutes by rail.

Operational details

The idea of creating a railway connection between Engadine and Alta Valtellina is not new. In July, the Redas Engineering studio had presented a project to build a route of over 14 kilometres to connect S-chanf and Livigno in about ten minutes by rail. The current journey time is around 49 minutes by car and over two hours by public transport.

The project, financed by the Canton of Graubünden, involves an investment of 250 million Swiss francs, of which 100 million for the stretch from S-chanf to Zernez, and 150 million for the stretch from Zernez to Livigno. The Canton of Graubünden has already approved the implementation of the project and has established a financing plan that provides for the collection of funds through a loan of 50 million Swiss francs with an interest rate of 2.5% per year.

The railway route will be carried out in collaboration with the Railway Company of the Canton of Graubünden (RhB), which will operate the line and provide the trains. The company has already started working on the project and has planned a realization time of about 5 years.

The rail link between S-chanf and Livigno will be an important step forward for the region, which will finally be directly connected to the Swiss rail network. The project will also be an important investment for the region, generating new jobs and stimulating local economic development.
