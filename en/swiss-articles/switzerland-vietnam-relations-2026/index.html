On October 17, 2026, in Hanoi, President of the Swiss Confederation Guy Parmelin was received by President Tô Lâm and Prime Minister Lê Minh Hưng. The official visit focused on bilateral relations between the two countries. In this context, agreements were concluded in various political areas. The signing of the free trade agreement bringing together the four EFTA members and Vietnam is also scheduled for Sunday, October 18.

A relationship placed on a new footing

Switzerland and Vietnam have maintained diplomatic relations for 55 years. In 2026, the establishment of a global partnership gave relations between the two countries a new direction. Since 2008, Vietnam has been one of Switzerland's priority countries for economic cooperation. The programme for the 2025-2028 period supports sustainable trade and innovation, promotes financial-sector stability and advances industrial and urban development compatible with climate protection.

Both sides highlighted the opportunities associated with the free trade agreement. The signing is also described as a political signal in favour of open, rules-based trade. The talks touched on the interest in concluding negotiations for a modernized agreement on investment protection, with the aim of strengthening legal certainty for investments.

The international situation and the two countries' cooperation as members of the UN Human Rights Council were also on the table. The delegations also discussed negotiations on cooperation against climate change, pursuant to Article 6 of the Paris Agreement.

During the visit, Switzerland and Vietnam signed several bilateral conventions, including the launch of two development-oriented economic cooperation projects. These initiatives are geared towards implementing the free trade agreement and sustainable urban development; they also aim to improve the framework conditions for trade and investment and strengthen long-term competitiveness. Some declarations of intent concern the labour market, employment and innovation. Following the meetings with Tô Lâm and Lê Minh Hưng, talks with Trần Thanh Mẫn and other government representatives are planned. The Swiss-Vietnamese economic forum and the signing of the agreement are also scheduled for October 18; Parmelin is accompanied by a delegation from the business community.
