Operational details

SECO offers a stimulating working environment, where responsibility, open-mindedness and personal initiative are valued, and opens up new perspectives for employees who wish to help shape Swiss economic policy.

In particular, SECO is a federal agency that is responsible for promoting the economic growth and development of the country. Its mission is to create an environment favourable to business and economic development, through the promotion of competitiveness, the reduction of bureaucracy and the enhancement of research and development.

SECO offers its employees the opportunity to work in a dynamic and stimulating environment, with a culture of open-mindedness and collaboration. SECO employees are committed to developing their skills and contributing to the achievement of the agency's objectives.

SECO is based in Bern, but operates throughout Switzerland. Its presence is particularly strong in the cantons of Zurich, Geneva and Lausanne, which are the country's main economic centres.
