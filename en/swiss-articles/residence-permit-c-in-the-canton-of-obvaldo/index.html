Context

Residence permit C in the Canton of Obvaldo

The Canton of Obvaldo, one of the southernmost regions of Switzerland, is known for its cultural wealth and strategic location on the borders with Italy. However, for border workers residing in the Canton of Obvaldo, obtaining residence permit C is an essential requirement in order to enjoy the rights and opportunities offered by Switzerland.

Requirements and procedures

The procedure for obtaining residence permit C is similar to that for residence permits B and C, but requires some additional requirements. Border workers must demonstrate that they have a permanent residence in the Canton of Obvaldo and that they are able to support themselves and their family. In addition, they must present a bank guarantee of at least 30,000 Swiss francs and prove that they have sufficient income to cover their expenses.

Concrete examples

For example, an Italian citizen who resides in the Canton of Obvaldo and works as an employee in a company in Lugano could apply for a residence permit C. The company must submit a statement attesting to the worker's income and the bank guarantee must be submitted by a Swiss banking institution.

Requirements to obtain residence permit C

To obtain the residence permit C, it is necessary to submit the application to the Canton of Obvaldo. The application for residence permit C must be submitted in person or through a representative. It is important to note that the C residence permit assessment procedure can take up to 6 months, so it is important to plan ahead.

# Residence Permit Requirements C

To obtain residence permit C, the following requirements must be met:

  • Have a monthly income of at least 5,000 CHF
  • Have valid health insurance
  • Having a stable and safe place to stay
  • Not have a criminal record
  • Do not be a danger to public safety

# Residence permit assessment procedure C

The procedure for evaluating the residence permit C is as follows:

  • Submission of the application for residence permit C to the Canton of Obvaldo
  • Evaluation of the application by the immigration service of the Canton of Obvaldo
  • Request for documents and additional information if necessary
  • Final assessment and decision on validation of residence permit C
