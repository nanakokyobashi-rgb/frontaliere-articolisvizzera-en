The topic does not concern a single piece of information. It includes the organization of the service, obligations, call-ups, compensation and the responsible authority. These are connected elements, but not interchangeable. A call-up concerns the relationship with the service; compensation requires a separate verification; requirements must be considered within the remit of the competent authority.

For those who live or work in Switzerland, this distinction avoids searching for a generic answer when the question concerns Lucerne. Call-ups and compensation must be verified with the competent authority. The practical reference is therefore the information provided by the authority responsible for the service.

Key points

To address the issue without adding unverified data, the procedure can be organized into four steps.

1. Define the scope. If the question concerns the Canton of Lucerne, start with the cantonal organization of civil protection. Federal law remains the reference framework, but the operational research must remain connected to the cantonal service.

2. Separate the questions. Do not put organization, obligations, call-up and allowances in the same request. Formulating the points separately helps clarify what information is being sought and which answer must be verified.

3. Verify the call-up. The call-up must be read together with the instructions from the competent service authority. This is the step to use to check the applicable requirements and the obligations connected to one's position, without deriving criteria from sources that do not refer to Lucerne.

4. Verify the allowance. The method to be considered must not be based on assumptions. Allowances must be verified with the competent authority: the request should therefore be directed to the person responsible for the service.
