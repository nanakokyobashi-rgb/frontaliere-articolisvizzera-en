Who can request the NASPI?

Border workers who have lost their job can apply for NASPI if they have worked in Switzerland for at least 12 months in the 2 years prior to the date of application. In addition, they must have worked at least 2 hours per day and 4 hours per week for at least 26 weeks in the year prior to the date of application.

How does the NASPI calculation work?

The NASPI calculation is based on the insured's income and contribution requirements. Income is calculated based on the hourly wage and the number of hours worked. The contribution is calculated based on the hourly wage and the number of hours worked.

The Federal Law of 25 June 1982 (LFP 2) establishes the requirements for the provision of the Naspi allowance. According to Article 1 of the Act, the allowance is connected to work carried out in Switzerland. This means that frontier workers must have worked in Switzerland for at least 12 months to be eligible for the allowance.

Naspi Allowance Requirements

To claim the Naspi allowance, frontier workers must meet the following requirements:

  • Have worked in Switzerland for at least 12 months;
  • Have Swiss social insurance;
  • Be between the ages of 20 and 64;
  • Not being able to work due to a disability;
  • Not be in possession of a sufficient income to ensure a decent livelihood.

Calculation of the Naspi allowance

The calculation of the NASPI allowance is based on the average income of a Swiss worker. According to the Federal Law of 25 June 1982 (LFP 2), the allowance is calculated on the basis of the average income of a Swiss worker, which is currently CHF 4,650.00 per month.
