Family allowances in the canton of Basel-City: amounts and application

The family allowance is an important benefit for families with minor children in the Basel-City Canton. As of the 2022/2023 school year, the monthly amount per child has been set at CHF 240. This increase was decided by the State Council to support families facing economic hardship.

Monthly amount per child

The monthly amount per child is CHF 240. This means that for a family with two children, the total monthly amount will be CHF 480. It is important to note that this amount is subject to change depending on the economic situation of the family.

Family allowance application

The application for family allowance must be submitted within 3 months of the beginning of the school year. You can apply online or at the location of the placement office of the Canton of Basel-City. It is important to have all the necessary documents for the submission of the application.

Required documents

To submit the application for a family allowance, you must have the following documents:

  • Applicant's identity document
  • Children's identity document
  • Proof of residence in the Canton of Basel-City
  • Proof of income
  • Proof of age of the children

Operational details

The family allowance in the Canton of Basel-City: amounts and application

The increase in the family allowance was decided to support families with minor children and to promote the birth rate in the Canton of Basel-City. The monthly amount of CHF 240 per child has been established after a careful assessment of the needs of the families and the available resources. The application process has been made easier and faster thanks to the introduction of a single form. This will allow families to apply more efficiently and without having to go through a complex bureaucratic process.

The new legislation, which came into force on 1 January 2023, provides for an increase in the family allowance of CHF 100 per child compared to last year. This increase was possible thanks to the resources added in the municipal budget for 2023. The total monthly amount for a family with two children, therefore, will be CHF 480 (CHF 240 per child).

The application for family allowance can be submitted by all families residing in the Canton of Basel-City and who have children under the age of 18. The application procedure has been made easier and faster thanks to the introduction of a unique form, which can be downloaded from the website of the Canton of Basel-City. The form must be filled in clearly and precisely, and must be accompanied by all the required documents, such as the tax return and the copy of the employment contract.

Key points

Family allowances in the canton of Basel-City: amounts and application

To apply for the family allowance, families must fill out the single form and submit it within 3 months of the beginning of the school year. The form can be downloaded from the website of the Canton of Basel-City or can be requested from the municipal administration office. Families must provide the information necessary to demonstrate their eligibility for the family allowance. This includes the submission of documents attesting to their residence in the Basel-City Canton and their family status.

The amounts of family allowances vary depending on the composition of the family and the income of the parents. According to the law of the Canton Basel City, the maximum amounts for the year 2023 are as follows:

  • Only child: CHF 1,400 per year
  • Two children: CHF 2,200 per year
  • Three children: CHF 2,800 per year
  • Four or more children: CHF 3,400 per year

It is important to note that these amounts may be subject to review and may change over time. In addition, the amounts may vary depending on the region and city where you live. For example, in Basel, the capital of the Canton Basel City, the amounts may be slightly higher than in other cities in the Canton.
