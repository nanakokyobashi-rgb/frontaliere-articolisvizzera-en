Vocational training in the Canton of Bern is structured through the consolidated dual system, which alternates practical activity in the company with school attendance at a vocational school. The system is regulated at the federal level and managed by the cantons, guaranteeing uniform quality standards throughout the national territory. Students who choose this path acquire practical skills directly in the field, benefiting from early entry into the world of work. The training course aims to achieve the Federal Certificate of Capacity (AFC), a qualification recognized throughout Switzerland that opens up numerous career opportunities and future specialization.

Operational details

The analysis of the Bernese training system highlights the centrality of the internship contract, which represents the legal fulcrum of the relationship between the company and the apprentice. Unlike a normal employment contract, this document assumes a pedagogical value, since the company undertakes to train the young person according to the national educational programs provided for the specific profession. The apprentice's salary is not set by a single federal law, but follows sectoral recommendations and business practices. It is important to remember that compulsory withdrawals for social insurance are made on gross salary, including AVS/AI/IPG and, when the expected thresholds are reached, LPP/BVG contributions.

Integration between study and work

The Bernese system encourages professional maturity, an additional path that allows apprentices to deepen their theoretical knowledge by gaining access to professional university schools. This path can be followed in parallel to the apprenticeship, requiring additional commitment but offering significantly broader professional growth prospects. Compared to the past, the integration between training companies and vocational schools has become closer, favouring training that is increasingly aligned with the needs of the Swiss labour market. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the requirements to enter into an internship contract in the Canton of Bern?
The contract must be concluded in writing between the training company and the apprentice (or his legal representative if a minor). It must be approved by the cantonal vocational training office, which verifies its compliance with federal and cantonal provisions.
How is an apprentice paid during training?
The salary is set out in the internship contract. It varies according to the sector and the year of training. The company is obliged to pay the mandatory social contributions (AVS/AI/IPG) on the gross salary, complying with the current regulations on Swiss social security.
Is it possible to achieve professional maturity during an apprenticeship?
Yes, professional maturity can be achieved in parallel with basic professional training. This path requires an additional commitment and the agreement of the training company, allowing direct access to professional university schools at the end of the internship.
