Forward with Ticino&Lavoro: 2027 Government list

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 3 October 2026

Sergio Savoia, Amalia Mirante, Valentina Mühlemann, Evaristo Roncelli and Giovanni Albertini are on the list for the State Council on 11 aprile 2027.

On Saturday 3 October, at the Talete Center in Lamone, Avanti con Ticino&Lavoro presented its team ahead of the Council of State elections on 11 April 2027. The event gave shape to the list and accompanied the selection of candidates with an appeal for participation and more transparent politics.

The composition of the list

The names listed are Sergio Savoia, Amalia Mirante, Valentina Mühlemann, Evaristo Roncelli and Giovanni Albertini. The text presents them as a team for the Government, without associating any individual candidate with a distinct role or specific portfolio. The stated common element is therefore their candidacy on the same list.

The group chose The courage to change as its slogan. The wording frames the presentation around change and is accompanied by a call for a different way of doing politics. The announcement speaks of opening the windows of the rooms of power to let in light and fresh air.

«It is time to choose another way of doing politics, to open the windows of the rooms of power and let in light and fresh air».

The second part of the appeal directly addresses the citizens of the canton. They are credited with the ability to choose another political path, leave behind a page described as sad and dreary, and build the country’s future together. These are the political points stated in the text.

The reference to the Council of State places the candidacy within the event indicated by the list. 11 April 2027 is the electoral date reported in the announcement, while the Talete Center in Lamone identifies the location of the presentation. No other events connected to the candidacy are indicated.

For the reader, the scope of the announcement is limited: a list of five people, an election date, a presentation venue and a communications platform centered on courage, participation and transparency. The source, however, does not report individual proposals from the candidates. The news concerns the presentation of the team, not the explanation of specific measures.

The words used to describe the political past and the desired future belong to the formation’s communications. The source does not attribute the quotation to a single candidate and does not indicate who uttered each sentence. The message should therefore be read as the position of the announcement and the list as a whole.
