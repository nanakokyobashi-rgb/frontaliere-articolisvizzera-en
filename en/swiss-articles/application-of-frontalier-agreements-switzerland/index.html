Federal Councillor Karin Keller-Sutter met with Italian Finance Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti in Rome today. The meeting took place immediately after the decision of the Ticino Council of State to suspend the payment of rebates for the current year to Lombardy as a precautionary measure. A move that according to the federal councillor weighs on the current agreement on cross-border workers. The talks focused on international financial and tax issues as well as bilateral relations. The revision of the double taxation agreement dating back to 1976 remains of fundamental importance to Switzerland. "We believe that a revision is in the interest of both states. At the same time, we are aware that other issues must be resolved first," Keller-Sutter said today in front of the media in Rome, after the meeting with Giorgetti. However, there are "major challenges" in cross-border cooperation and bilateral relations with cross-border workers. The authorities on both sides intend to work closely with the regions concerned," explained the Federal Councillor.

Operational details

The decision of the Ticino State Council to cautiously block the payment of refunds for the current year to Lombardy was taken after the northern region of Italy had announced that it wanted to request a so-called "health tax" from border workers working in Switzerland. "We have taken note of the decision of the canton of Ticino to partially withhold the compensatory payments due under the 2020 border agreement. This move obviously puts the existing agreement with Italy to the test, "said the head of the Federal Department of Finance (DFF). Key points

The Federal Council deplores the move of the canton of Ticino. "It is in Switzerland's interest that the cross-border commuter agreement is properly implemented," the St. Gallen minister reiterated. At the same time, the Federal Council believes that solutions must be found in this area. "We are aware that there are special issues and interests in border areas. We support the canton of Ticino, but at the same time we want to find a way out."
