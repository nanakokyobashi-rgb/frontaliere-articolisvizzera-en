The procedure for claiming family allowances in the Canton of Solothurn is as follows:

1. The parent must submit the application within 3 months of the birth of the child. 2. The application must be submitted to the Cantonal Administration. 3. The Cantonal Administration will assess the application and determine whether the parent is eligible for family allowances. 4. If the parent is eligible, the Cantonal Administration will determine the amounts of family allowances. 5. Family allowances will be paid monthly.

Child benefits in the Canton of Solothurn: amounts and application. If you are a parent with a child born in the Canton of Solothurn and want to request child benefits, you must submit the application within 3 months of the child's birth. The application must be submitted to the cantonal administration of the Canton of Solothurn, located in Olten. If the parent is eligible, the cantonal administration will determine the amounts of child benefits and pay them monthly. You can check if you are eligible for child benefits by contacting the cantonal administration of the Canton of Solothurn. It is also important to note that the amounts of child benefits vary depending on the number of children and the parent's income. For example, in 2022, child benefits in the Canton of Solothurn were 2,500 Swiss francs per month for parents of one child, 3,500 Swiss francs per month for parents of two children, and 4,500 Swiss francs per month for parents of three children. Parent with an annual income of 100,000 Swiss francs and three children aged 8, 10, and 12: child benefit of 4,500 Swiss francs per month.
