Uri: specific sick pay premiums and subsidies (cross-border guide)

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 28 August 2026

The Canton of Uri applies specific rates for compulsory health insurance premiums (LAMal). Access to the subsidies takes place through the reduction of premiums, with flexible deductibles for adults.

Key facts

  • What: Compulsory health insurance premiums and premium reductions.
  • Where: Canton of Uri.
  • Who: Recognised health insurers resident in the Canton.
  • When: Annual updates on premiums and subsidy requirements.

In the Canton of Uri, compulsory health insurance premiums (LAMal/KVG) follow a specific rate structure, which varies according to the region of domicile within the canton itself. This system aims to reflect regional differences in healthcare costs and population density, directly influencing the monthly cost to residents. Compulsory health insurance is a pillar of the Swiss health system, imposed by law on all residents and to be taken out within three months of arrival in Switzerland. Unlike a tax or wage contribution, LAMal premiums are set on a per capita basis and are not tied to the individual insured's income or salary. This feature clearly distinguishes LAMal from other social contributions, such as those for AVS/AI or LPP, which are instead calculated as a percentage of income. Operational details

The possibility of access to a reduction in premiums represents an important support for low-income families and individuals in the Canton of Uri, as well as throughout Switzerland. The premium reduction system, managed at cantonal level, aims to ensure that the financial burden of compulsory health insurance premiums remains sustainable for all residents, regardless of their economic situation. The requirements to benefit from this reduction are closely linked to the income and net worth of the household. The cantonal authorities set precise thresholds, updated annually, below which residents are entitled to a subsidy. These parameters are defined taking into account the cost of living and average LAMal premiums in the canton. The aim is to prevent health insurance from becoming an unsustainable financial burden, thus ensuring universal access to health care, a key principle of the Swiss system. The procedure to request the reduction of premiums is usually structured through the annual tax return, where the necessary income and capital data are collected. The competent cantonal offices, such as the Social Insurance Office or a similar body, then process the applications and determine the amount of the benefit due. Key points

For residents of the Canton of Uri wishing to inquire about health insurance premiums and the possibility of obtaining a reduction, the process begins with verifying the specific requirements established by the competent cantonal authority. Typically, detailed information on current year premiums and the income and asset thresholds for accessing subsidies is published on the official cantonal websites, particularly those dedicated to health or social insurance. It is advisable to consult your health insurance provider to find out the exact rates applied in your region of residence within Uri and to receive information on the different insurance models and deductibles available. Regarding the application for premium reduction, the procedure is often integrated into the annual tax return. Taxpayers are required to complete the specific forms provided by the cantonal tax administration, indicating the income and assets of the household. The cantonal authorities will use this information to calculate the amount of subsidy due, which will then be credited directly or deducted from the premiums owed. It is crucial to adhere to the deadlines for submitting the tax return and any additional documents requested, in order not to lose the right to the subsidy. In case of doubts or specific questions about your situation, you can contact the responsible cantonal office directly (e.g., the Uri Cantonal Social Insurance Office) or an authorized insurance advisor. Understanding your options in terms of deductibles and insurance models, combined with assessing your eligibility for subsidies, allows for optimized management of costs related to mandatory health insurance.
