Temu and SKV: promoting Swiss SMEs' access to the online market (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 24 July 2026

The Chinese online commerce platform Temu and the Swiss Association of SMEs (SKV) have decided to undertake a collaboration to promote Swiss companies' access to online commerce.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Chinese online commerce platform Temu and the Swiss Association of SMEs (SKV) have signed a statement of intent. - The planned initiatives include training courses, exchange of information and practical examples of Swiss companies already selling on the Internet.

Collaboration details

The declaration of intent between Temu and SKV aims to promote the access of Swiss small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to the online market. The collaboration involves the implementation of training courses and workshops for Swiss companies, with the aim of providing them with the knowledge and skills necessary to sell effectively online.

The planned initiatives also include the exchange of information and the organisation of networking events for Swiss companies that already sell on the Internet. This will allow SMEs to share their experiences and learn from the best practices of other companies.

Concrete examples

An example of a concrete initiative is the training course "Selling effectively on the Internet", which will be carried out in collaboration with the University of Zurich. The course will be free and accessible to all Swiss companies wishing to sell online.

Operational details

The collaboration between Temu and SKV aims to break down barriers to entry in e-commerce and promote the digitisation of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in Switzerland. In addition, the collaboration should facilitate SMEs' access to new customer groups.

Switzerland is known for its dynamic economy and the presence of numerous small and medium-sized enterprises. However, these companies often face barriers to entry into e-commerce, such as a lack of experience in the digital sector or a shortage of financial resources. 📊

The collaboration between Temu and SKV aims to overcome these barriers and provide Swiss SMEs with the resources and skills needed to address the networked market. To do this, Temu and SKV offer a range of services and tools, such as the creation of a professional website, the management of online sales channels and the management of payments and shipments.

A concrete example of how this collaboration can help Swiss SMEs is represented by the Stans fabric company in the Canton of Nidwalden. This company had difficulty reaching a wider audience, but after using the services of Temu and SKV, it managed to increase its sales by more than 20% thanks to the possibility of selling its products online. 💡
