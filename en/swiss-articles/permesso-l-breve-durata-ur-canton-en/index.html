The short-term L permit is a type of permit that allows foreign nationals to work in the Canton of Uri for up to 1 year. It is necessary to obtain the L permit before starting work and the employer must have a legitimately established company in the Canton of Uri.

Conditions of application

To obtain the short-term L permit, the following conditions must be met:

  • The employer must have a legitimately established company in the Canton of Uri. This means that the company must have a legitimate economic activity and a physical address in the Canton of Uri.
  • The employer must be able to demonstrate that the company has the necessary means to support the employment of the foreign worker.
  • The foreign worker must be in possession of a valid residence permit and a written employment contract with the employer.
