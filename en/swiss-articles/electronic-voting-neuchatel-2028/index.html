Operational details

The experimentation of electronic voting in the Canton of Neuchâtel represents a significant step forward in the modernization of the Swiss electoral process. This system, already successfully used in other cantons, aims to facilitate the participation of voters, especially those living abroad. The decision of the Federal Council to authorize this experimentation until November 26, 2028, demonstrates a commitment to innovation and efficiency in the Swiss democratic system.

Key points

To participate in the electronic voting experiment in the Canton of Neuchâtel, voters must follow a specific procedure. First, they must register through the "Guichet Unique", the electronic portal of the canton. Once registered, they will receive the voting instructions electronically and the voting certificate by mail. It is important to note that the voting certificate can only be used to vote electronically or in person at the polls.
