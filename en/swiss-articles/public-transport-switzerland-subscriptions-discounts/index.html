The current situation

Currently, public transport ticket prices in Switzerland are among the highest in Europe. For example, a single ticket for a 2-hour train journey between Zurich and Bern costs CHF 67.40. This means that a Swiss citizen who regularly uses public transport to get to work could pay up to CHF 1,000 per month for tickets.
