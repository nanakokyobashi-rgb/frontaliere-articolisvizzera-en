Key points

For more information on the Compensation Fund and supplementary benefits, please consult the website of the Canton of Lucerne or contact the relevant office. The Compensation Fund is a fundamental institution to ensure the coverage of the pension fund gaps of Swiss workers.

The Supplementary Benefits Act 2005 (SBA) provides that employees with an annual income of at least CHF 60,000 are obliged to participate in the Compensation Fund. This means that workers with an income below this level are exempt from the participation obligation.

Example: A worker with an annual income of CHF 50,000 is exempt from the obligation to participate in the Compensation Fund.

However, it is important to note that workers who do not participate in the Compensation Fund can still benefit from supplementary benefits through other forms of savings. For example, they may participate in pension funds or individual savings plans.

The most common supplementary benefit is the individual pension fund (IPF). The FPI allows workers to save for their retirement and benefit from unemployment insurance. Contributions to the FPI are paid monthly and can be deducted from taxable income.
