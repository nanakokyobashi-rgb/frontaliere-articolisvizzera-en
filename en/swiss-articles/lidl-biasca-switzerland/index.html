Context

The new Lidl store in Biasca was inaugurated today, offering customers a wide range of high-quality products at competitive prices. The store was opened by a group of local investors, who have worked to create an innovative shopping experience accessible to all. The opening of this new outlet represents a significant investment in the Ticino region, where Lidl continues to expand its presence. The Biasca store joins the numerous other outlets already present in cantons such as Zurich, Bern, and St. Gallen.

'We are excited to bring our offer of high-quality products at affordable prices to the Biasca region', said a Lidl representative.

The investment in this new store amounts to several million francs, creating new job opportunities for the local community. In line with Swiss regulations, such as the Federal Law on Environmental Protection of 7 October 1983, Lidl has implemented measures to minimize the environmental impact of the new store. The new Biasca outlet offers a complete range of products, including fresh produce, meat, fish, and non-food items, with prices that can be up to 20% lower than other local supermarkets.

Operational details

The new Lidl store in Biasca was inaugurated with a big celebration, attracting many customers and locals. The store offers a wide range of products, including groceries, home goods, and personal care items. Prices have been set competitively, allowing customers to purchase high-quality products at affordable prices.

"We are excited to bring our quality offering at low prices to the Ticino region," stated a representative of Lidl Switzerland.

The new store adds to the existing distribution network in Switzerland, which already includes over 170 stores across the country, including those in Zurich, Bern, and Geneva.

The Biasca store creates new job opportunities for the local community and is committed to adhering to Swiss labor and environmental regulations, such as the Labor Law (Lavoro) of March 13, 1964, which governs employment relationships in Switzerland.

The opening of the new store strengthens Lidl's presence in Ticino and provides customers in the region with an additional choice for their daily shopping at competitive prices, in line with the national average.

Customers can order online through Lidl's website or mobile app and receive delivery notifications via SMS or email. The delivery service is also available for customers with disabilities, thanks to collaboration with various assistance societies.
