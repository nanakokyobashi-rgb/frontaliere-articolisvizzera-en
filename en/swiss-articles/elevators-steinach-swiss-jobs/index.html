Frequently Asked Questions
What are the requirements to work as an elevator technician in Steinach?
The main requirements include a technical certification recognized by the canton, specialized training in elevator installation and maintenance, and compliance with cantonal regulations for entry into the technical sector. Foreign professionals must also obtain specific work permits.
How is LAMal health insurance assessed for border workers?
Border crossers are entitled to the option for the LAMal, with an adult deductible that can vary between CHF 300 and CHF 2500. The assessment depends on income class and work location, with specific conditions for workers who live in Italy but work in Switzerland.
What are the tax differences for border workers in the elevator sector?
Old frontier workers (before 17/7/2023) have a contribution exemption of 7,500 euros and a transitional regime 2024-2033. The new frontier workers have an exemption of 10,000 euros for border tax, with contributions withheld exclusively in Switzerland.
