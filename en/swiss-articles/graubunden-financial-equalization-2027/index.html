The Canton of Graubünden strengthens financial support to its municipalities in 2027. Equalization contributions will reach CHF 73.2 million, representing an increase of CHF 1.5 million compared to 2026. This was announced today by the Department of Finance and Municipalities, according to sources from the Keystone-ATS news agency.

Of the 73.2 million, the canton will make available 41.7 million, while the financially stronger municipalities will contribute 31.5 million. Financial equalisation is a crucial tool to reduce economic disparities between municipalities, ensuring that those in distress receive support from stronger local administrations.

Operational details

The tax base of the Grison municipalities is divided into several components. Alongside taxes on natural persons, tax revenues from legal entities bring in €114.1 million. Another contribution comes from water fees and indemnities, amounting to 60.9 million, while the tax values of the properties, calculated with a coefficient of 1.5 per thousand, add 138.5 million.

The distribution of these amounts between the different categories of income reveals the economic structure of the canton: on the one hand, the strong role of personal taxation; on the other, the significant contribution of the real estate base and local commercial activities.

Strong municipalities and redistributive logic

Altogether, 40 Grison municipalities are classified as "strong" from a financial point of view and therefore pay contributions to equalisation. Among these, St. Moritz emerges as the main contributor with 15.1 million, reflecting the economic solidity of the well-known tourist resort. Conters in Prettigovia, on the other hand, is the only municipality classified as "neutral", neither paying nor receiving significant contributions.

On the other side of the equation, Landquart is the municipality that receives the most support, accessing over 4.7 million francs. This distribution ensures that municipalities with lower tax capacity can maintain essential services for the community.

Key points

How does equalization work in 2027

The mechanism of financial equalization in Grisons is based on a logic of solidarity between local communities. The wealthier municipalities contribute a proportional share to their tax capacity, while those with lower resources receive transfers that bridge the gap, allowing them to maintain comparable service levels.

In 2027, this equilibrium redistributes 73.2 million francs: since the canton contributes 41.7 million, the remaining amount of 31.5 million comes from the 40 local administrations classified as 'strong'. The growth of the total resource potential of 2.9% fuels this increase in availability compared to 2026, ensuring greater room for maneuver for the beneficiary municipalities.

For those living in Grisons municipalities, the effect of equalization translates into the availability of public services and infrastructure investments financed with redistributed funds. Municipalities such as Poschiavo and Roveredo, which receive the most significant contributions, can allocate their own resources to local development initiatives without relying exclusively on local tax pressure.

What does it mean for cantonal and municipal taxation

Financial equalization is an integral part of the Swiss tax system, where taxation at the cantonal and municipal level follows principles of differentiation. Unlike the federal direct tax, which is uniform across the entire national territory, cantonal and municipal tax rates vary according to local decisions. Equalization represents a corrective tool that mitigates these disparities, preventing municipalities with fewer economic resources from suffering structural imbalance.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the total increase in Grison's financial equalisation in 2027?
In 2027, the municipalities of Grison will receive 73.2 million francs in the form of financial equalisation contributions, representing an increase of 1.5 million compared to 2026. The canton will provide 41.7 million, while the financially strong municipalities will contribute 31.5 million. This increase reflects the growth in total resource potential, which rose to 990.5 million (+2.9% vs 2026).
What are the main sources of income of the Grison municipalities?
Taxes on natural persons (income, substance, at source) represent the primary source with 676.9 million, equal to more than 68% of the total potential. Taxes on legal persons (114.1 million), real estate tax values (138.5 million, calculated with a coefficient of 1.5 per thousand) and water fees plus compensation (60.9 million) follow.
Which municipalities pay and which receive equalisation in the Italian Graubünden?
In the Italian Graubünden, the municipalities of Bregaglia, Brusio, Buseno and Soazza pay equalisation contributions. All other municipalities receive support, with Poschiavo (about 1.2 million) and Roveredo (about 1.6 million) benefiting from the highest contributions in the region.
Who is the largest contributor to the Grison equalization?
St. Moritz is the main contributor with CHF 15.1 million, followed by another 39 municipalities classified as financially strong. Altogether, these 40 municipalities pay 31.5 million of the 73.2 million total earmarked for equalisation in 2027.
What is the role of the canton in financial equalization?
The Canton of Graubünden will make available 41.7 million of the 73.2 million total equalization in 2027, representing the cantonal public contribution. The remaining share comes from financially strong municipalities, creating a system of redistribution of resources among local communities.
