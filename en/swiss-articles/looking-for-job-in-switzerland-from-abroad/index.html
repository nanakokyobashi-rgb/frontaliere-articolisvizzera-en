What is the New Cross-Border Workers Agreement

The New Cross-Border Workers Agreement is an agreement between Switzerland and Italy to avoid double taxation of cross-border workers’ income. This agreement was signed on December 23, 2020 and comes into force on January 1st, 2024.

Who are the cross-border workers

Cross-border workers are people who live in Switzerland and work in Italy, or vice versa. They are subject to a specific tax regime, which requires income to be declared both in Switzerland and Italy.
