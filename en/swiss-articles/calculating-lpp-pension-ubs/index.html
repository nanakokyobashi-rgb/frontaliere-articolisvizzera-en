How to calculate the LPP - UBS annuity (cross-border guide)

Learn how to calculate your LPP annuity with UBS. Complete and practical guide for border workers.

Context

In short - The LPP annuity is calculated based on the salary and the duration of the contribution. - UBS offers tools to calculate the LPP annuity. - The LPP pension is complementary to the AVS pension. ## Key facts - What: LPP pension - When: During the pension phase - Where: In Switzerland - Who: Workers with LPP insurance - Amount: Depends on the salary and the duration of the contribution The LPP pension is a crucial element of Swiss pension, complementary to the AVS pension. UBS offers practical tools to calculate the LPP annuity, allowing workers to better plan their financial future. The LPP pension is calculated on the basis of the salary and the duration of the contributions paid, offering additional economic security during old age.

Operational details

How the calculation of the LPP annuity works The calculation of the LPP annuity is based on several key factors. First of all, the annual salary determined (SAD) is a fundamental element. This salary is subject to a mandatory contribution to the pension fund, which varies depending on the canton. In addition, the duration of the contributions paid directly affects the amount of the annuity. UBS provides online tools that allow you to enter this data and obtain an accurate estimate of the LPP annuity. ### Practical example Suppose a worker has a determined annual salary of CHF 80,000 and has contributed to the pension fund for 20 years. Using the UBS calculation tool, the worker can obtain an estimate of his LPP annuity. This hypothetical example shows how concrete data can be used to better plan for retirement. ### Comparison with the previous situation Before the introduction of online calculation tools, workers had to rely on financial advisors to obtain estimates of their LPP income. Today, thanks to tools such as the one offered by UBS, workers can obtain accurate and up-to-date information independently, saving time and money.

Frequently Asked Questions
How can I calculate my LPP annuity with UBS?
You can use the UBS calculator available on their website. Enter your determined annual salary (SAD) and the duration of contributions paid to get an accurate estimate of your LPP annuity.
What factors influence the calculation of the LPP annuity?
The calculation of the LPP pension depends mainly on the annual salary determined (SAD) and the duration of the contributions paid. Other factors may include the conversion rate of the pension fund and any additional benefits.
Why is it important to calculate the LPP annuity?
Calculating the LPP annuity is essential for planning your financial future. This annuity is complementary to the AVS pension and provides additional economic security during old age.
