In the Canton of Basel-Stadt, public transit passes are organized through a fare zone system and a division into zones. This comparison covers monthly and annual options, combinations with Half-Price and AG passes, as well as discounts for students and apprentices.

These categories define the order in which to approach the decision. First, consider the area covered by the zones; then the duration of the pass; and finally, any combination with half-price passes or AG passes. The passenger category completes the picture: students and apprentices are the groups eligible for discounts.

For those who live or work in Switzerland, the zone is not a mere formality. It is the key factor to consider alongside your usual commute before selecting a fare. A monthly pass and an annual pass are part of the same offering but cover different time frames: the former for one month, the latter for one year. This distinction helps prevent comparing different durations as if they were the same thing.

Key points

How to Prepare Your Decision

You can prepare your decision using a simple worksheet based on the five data points listed for Basel-Stadt: zones, duration, half-price card, AG pass, and personal category. The goal is to arrive at a consistent comparison of the available options.

Practical Procedure

1. Identify the zones. List the zones relevant to your use of public transportation in the Canton of Basel-Stadt. Don’t start with the duration: both monthly and annual passes must cover the same area. 2. Choose the timeframe. Compare the monthly and annual passes. Ask yourself whether your travel needs are concentrated in one month or spread out over the year; duration alone does not automatically confer an advantage. 3. Check the combination. Check separately whether a Half-Fare Card or General Travelcard (AG) is included and factor it into the comparison. Do not add or subtract values: first clarify which combination you are evaluating. 4. Check for discounts. If you are a student or apprentice, look for the discount option before comparing the results. Your personal category is part of the decision and must be kept separate from the duration.

Once you’ve completed the first worksheet, review the results in the same order. If you change zones, don’t mix that comparison with that of another combination; if you change the duration, don’t treat monthly and annual options as equivalent. This rule helps keep your selection clear without basing a fare on assumptions.

For those who live or work in Switzerland, the next step is to link the cost to your personal planning. A monthly pass can be treated as a monthly expense, and an annual pass as an annual expense. Half-Price and AG passes should be noted separately, as should any student or apprentice discount.

Before finalizing your selection, check that all five items are included: zones, duration, Half-Price, AG, and discount. If your personal profile is that of a student or apprentice, you must verify your eligibility for the discount before making your final comparison. Frequently Asked Questions
What elements make up the Basel-Stadt season ticket comparison?
The comparison starts with the fare network and the zones. Then it considers the duration, distinguishing between monthly and annual travelcards. The check also includes the combination with Half-Fare travelcard and GA travelcard. Finally, the personal category comes in, because students and apprentices are listed as recipients of the discounts. These items must be read together to make the choice.
What is the difference between a monthly and annual subscription?
The distinction concerns the duration of the travelcard. The monthly formula refers to one month, the annual one to one year. To compare them correctly, they must be linked to the required zones and to the use of public transport itself. The duration alone does not indicate which option is cheaper: the combination with the Half-Fare or GA travelcard and any personal discount must also be considered.
How should Half-Fare travelcards and GA travelcards be considered?
Half-Fare travelcards and GA travelcards are an independent part of the comparison, because the tariff angle takes into account their combination with the travelcards. They do not have to be added at the end as a separate detail. First, the zones and the duration are identified, then it is checked which combination applies to the travelcard in question. The comparison thus remains readable and does not confuse different elements.
Can students and apprentices get a discount?
Yes. Students and apprentices are listed among the categories for which the comparison considers discounts. The check must be done before choosing between monthly and annual and after clarifying the zones, so that the discount is linked to the formula actually evaluated and the combination with Half-Fare or GA travelcard.
