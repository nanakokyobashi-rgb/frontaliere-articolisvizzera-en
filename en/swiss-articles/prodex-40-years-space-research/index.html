The press release of October 16, 2026, celebrates the first 40 years of Prodex, the European Space Agency (ESA) program dedicated to experimental space research. Since 1986, Prodex has provided resources for the development and use of scientific instruments and experiments.

Switzerland played a decisive role in the creation of Prodex and, through the program, finances its own space experiments. The program thus aims to support standards of scientific excellence in ESA missions and those of international partners such as NASA, JAXA and CNES.

Why the program was created

Space research is considered one of ESA's pillars. In general, the research institutes of member states develop the Agency's scientific experiments, while the respective states finance their implementation. States without their own national funding instrument therefore lacked an equivalent opportunity.

To address this need, in 1986, the Swiss and Belgian delegations launched Prodex, working with prominent figures from the scientific and administrative worlds. Currently, 17 member states of the Agency participate in the program.

ESA's experience enables Prodex to send highly complex scientific instruments and experiments into space, used both in the Agency's missions and those of international partners; these include the space agencies of the United States, Japan and France.

The anniversary was celebrated in Zurich from October 14 to 16, 2026, with a conference and a ceremony. The opening address was given by State Secretary Martina Hirayama. During several roundtable discussions, delegations from the participating states engaged with representatives of ESA and figures from the space research and industry communities.

Over the past 40 years, 130 development projects for space experiments have received support in Switzerland, carried out by more than 20 university research centers. Through specific mandates, more than 50% of the contributions were allocated to Swiss industry.
