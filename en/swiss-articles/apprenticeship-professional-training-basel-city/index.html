Operational details

Apprenticeship in Basel City is governed by the cantonal law of 12 October 2018. This law establishes that young border workers can find an internship at local companies. The internship contract must be stipulated by writing and must contain the working conditions and remuneration of the apprentice.

The remuneration of the apprentice is established by cantonal law and depends on the duration of the traineeship and the type of activity carried out. For example, an apprentice doing a 6-month traineeship at a company in Basel City may receive a monthly salary of CHF 2,500, as established by cantonal law.

It is important to note that apprenticeship in Basel City is also regulated by the International Labour Convention of 1937, which states that apprentices must be treated with equal dignity and respect with other workers. The cantonal law of 12 October 2018 is based on this convention and states that apprentices must be protected against all forms of exploitation or abuse.
