School system in the Canton of Geneva: cycles, enrolment and calendar (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 September 2026

How compulsory school works in Geneva: cycles, enrolment age, teaching languages and transition to high school.

Context

In brief

  • Geneva's public school system covers compulsory education through cycles organised by age group
  • Teaching is predominantly in French, with English introduced in the later cycles
  • Enrolment takes place through the cantonal service responsible for resident families
  • The transition from the final cycle to upper secondary schools follows established cantonal criteria

Key facts

  • What: Organisation of compulsory public schooling in the Canton of Geneva
  • When: School year defined by the cantonal calendar (specific dates not indicated in the source)
  • Where: Canton of Geneva, French-speaking Switzerland
  • Who: Cantonal Department of Public Education (Département de l'instruction publique, DIP)
  • Cost: not yet specified in the source (school fees in cantonal public schools are generally free for compulsory schooling)
  • Languages: French as the main language of instruction; English and German as second languages
  • Structure: Successive cycles (cycle élémentaire, cycle moyen, cycle d'orientation)
  • Compulsion: Compulsory schooling for cantonal residents, according to federal and cantonal legislation

The public school system in the Canton of Geneva is organised into progressive cycles covering the entire span of compulsory education. Unlike other Swiss cantons, which refer to 'primary school' and 'secondary school', the Geneva model groups the school years into three distinct cycles, each with specific pedagogical objectives and variable duration. This structure reflects the approach of the Swiss Conference of Cantonal Ministers of Education (CDIP/EDK), which coordinates minimum standards at national level through the HarmoS agreement.

Operational details

Comparison with other cantons

The cycle system adopted in Geneva differs from that of other Swiss cantons. In German-speaking cantons, for example, the typical scan is “Primarschule” (primary school) followed by “Sekundarstufe I” (lower secondary school) with differentiated levels (Realschule, Sekundarschule, Bezirksschule or Gymnasium). In Geneva, the division into cycles instead groups the primary and lower secondary arches under a single pedagogical direction, attenuating — at least formally — the separation between the two moments.

This choice reflects the pedagogical will to avoid early labelling of students. In fact, the cycle model aims to evaluate skills in progress, distributing orientation decisions over several years. In cantons with strong differentiation of levels from the 7th to the 8th year, separation occurs at an earlier age.

Languages of teaching and multilingualism

French is the language of instruction in the Canton of Geneva, consistent with its location in French-speaking Switzerland. English is the first foreign language introduced, followed by German at a later stage. The cantonal objective is the learning of at least two second languages in addition to the mother tongue.

For children of parents from other cantons or abroad, the system provides for reception devices and transition classes dedicated to intensive learning of school French.

Key points

Practical procedure to enrol a child

Step 1 — Civil registration. The family registers their residence with the residents' registration office (contrôle des habitants) of the Geneva municipality where they intend to settle. Without a residence certificate, it is not possible to start the schooling procedure.

Step 2 — Contact with the school service. Once the residence certificate has been obtained, the family contacts the school secretariat of their district or the DIP central service to begin the enrolment process. It is advisable to attend with civil registration documents and the previous school record (if available).

Step 3 — Assessment and assignment to a cycle. The school service checks the pupil's age and previous schooling, then assigns the class corresponding to the appropriate cycle. For non-Francophone children, a language assessment is provided, with possible placement in transition programmes.

Step 4 — School calendar and timetables. The family receives the calendar for the current school year, with start dates, holidays and administrative deadlines. Timetables and the organisation of school days are defined by each individual institution within the cantonal framework.

Step 5 — Transition between cycles. At the end of each cycle, the family takes part in review meetings organised by the school. Progression to the next cycle is automatic unless the class council decides otherwise with due justification.

Step 6 — Guidance to upper secondary education. In the final year of the orientation cycle, the family and the pupil are supported through the guidance process towards the collège, the école de culture générale or vocational training.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the structure of the compulsory school in the Canton of Geneva?
The compulsory public school in Geneva is organized in successive cycles (cycle élémentaire, cycle moyen, cycle d 'orientation). French is the main language of instruction, with English and German as second languages. The structure is consistent with the CDIP/EDK HarmoS agreement for the harmonization of compulsory schooling at the national level.
At what age does compulsory school start in Geneva?
The school obligation follows the cantonal provisions and the HarmoS agreement, which sets compulsory school entry generally at around 4 years (cycle élémentaire) for Romandy, according to the procedures applied by the Department of Public Instruction of the Canton of Geneva. For the exact age of enrollment of your cohort, it is recommended to consult the calendar published by the DIP.
How do you enroll a newcomer in the Geneva public school?
The procedure requires registration with the municipality of residence, then school registration with the dip service responsible for the district. The minor's identity document, proof of domicile and, if available, the previous school year are required.
Is the final diploma of the Geneva compulsory school recognized in other cantons?
Yes, the diploma awarded at the end of compulsory schooling in Geneva is recognised in the cantons that are signatories to the HarmoS agreement, which harmonises the minimum standards at national level. Any specific checks of equivalence for transfers between cantons with very different systems remain unaffected.
Where to find the official school calendar of the Canton of Geneva?
The school calendar is published by the Department of Public Instruction (dip) of the Canton of Geneva. Holiday dates, start of the year and administrative deadlines are updated year by year on the official website of the dip and municipal school services.
