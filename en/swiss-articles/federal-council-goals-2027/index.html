The Federal Council presents the objectives for 2027 (cross-border guide)

President Guy Parmelin outlined in Bern the strategic priorities for 2027, the fourth year of the 2023-2027 federal legislature.

Key facts

Press release date -> September 14, 2026 Presented by -> President of the Confederation Guy Parmelin Presentation venue -> Council of States, Bern Reference period -> Year 2027 Legislative program -> 2023-2027

President of the Confederation Guy Parmelin officially presented the objectives set by the Federal Council for the year 2027 to the Council of States. The speech took place in Bern and outlined the strategic priorities for the fourth and final year of the legislative program covering the 2023-2027 four-year term. The Swiss executive plans its activities annually through concrete and measurable goals, the results of which are subsequently verified and reported in the corresponding official management report.

Federal strategic planning

The definition of these objectives constitutes a central element of the Confederation's institutional planning. It allows federal authorities to coordinate legislative and administrative activities in a transparent manner. For 2027, the Government has structured a series of targeted interventions ranging from economic promotion to social reforms, outlining a programmatic framework extending to various federal departments and areas of Swiss public life. Annual programming serves to give concrete implementation to the four general political guidelines established at the beginning of the current legislative term, orienting state action toward objectives of sustainability, cohesion, security, and environmental protection.

Underlying political guidelines

During the 2023-2027 legislative term, the Federal Council's action is based on four fundamental strategic directions. The first direction establishes that Switzerland must ensure its prosperity in a sustainable manner, fully seizing the opportunities offered by digital technology. The second direction promotes national and intergenerational cohesion within the country. The third direction guarantees security, actively promotes peace, and ensures coherent and reliable action at the international level. Finally, the fourth direction is dedicated to climate protection and the safeguarding of the nation's natural resources. These four pillars are accompanied by twenty-five specific thematic objectives valid for the entire duration of the legislative term, regularly monitored through official publications that associate individual measures with different thematic areas.

Operational details

Economic and financial priorities for 2027

During 2027, the Federal Council's agenda will see the adoption of major legislative and financial acts intended to influence Switzerland's economic framework. Among the planned interventions, the adoption of the dispatch concerning the promotion of the economic hub for the 2028-2031 quadrennium stands out, a strategic document whose stated purpose is to further increase the country's attractiveness for investments and businesses. In the same year, the Government will make fundamental decisions regarding financial indicators relating to the large-scale multi-year financial decrees that will characterize the next legislative term, thereby defining the margins of maneuver for the federal public budget.

Infrastructure, transport and social reforms

Another key sector affected by the 2027 planning is transport, with the planned approval of the Transport '45 dispatch. This measure aims to promote targeted, coordinated and efficient development of the national railway network, the road network and agglomeration traffic. In the social security and welfare sector, the Federal Council will adopt despatches relating to reforms of major institutional and social significance, namely the old-age and survivors' insurance reform known as OASI 2030 and the integration reform. These measures will directly affect the future balances of the Swiss welfare system and the management of social benefits at the federal level.

Security, agriculture and climate policy

The 2027 program also includes major interventions on the front of internal and international security, emergency management and environmental sustainability. The Government intends to adopt the Strategy against domestic, sexual and gender-based violence and proceed with the update of Switzerland's Strategy for the fight against terrorism. To strengthen international cooperation and the capacity to react to unpredictable events, the adoption of the dispatch on Switzerland's accession to the Union Civil Protection Mechanism is planned. On the agricultural and environmental front, the Agricultural Policy 2030+ dispatch will legally define the framework conditions of agricultural policy for an eight-year period, while the revision of the CO2 Act will define Swiss climate policy for the period after 2030, charting the Confederation's ecological course for decades to come.

Key points

How to follow the implementation of federal objectives

For citizens, businesses, and economic operators residing in Switzerland, following the evolution of federal planning requires attention to consultation procedures and the parliamentary progress of the messages announced by the Federal Council. The measures planned for 2027, such as the launch of public consultations and the submission of messages to Parliament, offer stakeholders and cantons the opportunity to express their evaluations. The periodic publication called Federal Council Objectives constitutes the official reference tool to verify the progress of individual measures associated with the various federal departments.

Consultation of official documentation

Complete documentation presented by the President of the Confederation Guy Parmelin to the Council of States can be consulted through the official channels of the Confederation. The official press release and the attached technical documentation, published on September 14, 2026, offer details of the individual thematic objectives and the related administrative deadlines planned for the coming year. To explore the impact of these decisions on the general economic situation, public finances, and spending prospects, it is useful to regularly monitor updates provided by the federal administration and competent departments.

Financial planning and future prospects
