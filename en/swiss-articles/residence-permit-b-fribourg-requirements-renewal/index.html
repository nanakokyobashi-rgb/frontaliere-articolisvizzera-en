Residence Permit B Freiburg: Requirements and Renewal (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 September 2026

Permit B in Freiburg: requirements for EU/EFTA and third countries, renewal procedures, necessary documents and roles of the SEM and cantonal administration.

Context

In brief

  • The B permit is a renewable residence permit for foreigners in Switzerland
  • The State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) regulates the criteria at the federal level
  • Renewal: the concrete procedures are managed by the Fribourg cantonal administration
  • Documents and requirements vary for EU/EFTA citizens and third countries

Key facts

  • What: B residence permit — renewable residence and work permit
  • Who: State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) — federal authority
  • Where: Canton of Fribourg — cantonal migration administration
  • Duration: Renewable according to federal and cantonal decisions
  • Category: EU/EFTA foreigners and third countries

The B permit is the most common residence permit for those living and working in Switzerland, including the Canton of Fribourg. Unlike the L permit (short duration up to one year), the B permit is renewable and allows for a more stable stay in the Swiss territory. The State Secretariat for Migration (SEM), a federal body, establishes the general criteria and competencies; however, the concrete procedures for application and renewal remain with the Fribourg cantonal administration.

Who can apply for the B permit in Fribourg

The legal status varies between citizens of the European Union and the European Free Trade Association (EU/EFTA) and foreigners from third countries. Both categories can access the B permit, but with sometimes different requirements and documentation. The main difference concerns access to the labor market and the conditions of stay in the territory: those with EU/EFTA status benefit from more favorable conditions compared to third countries.

Operational details

Renewal of the B permit: procedures and timelines

Unlike other permit categories, the B permit is not unlimited: it has a fixed duration set by federal and cantonal authorities at the time of initial issuance. When this duration is about to expire, it is necessary to request a renewal from the competent cantonal administration, in this case, the offices of Fribourg. The renewal procedure generally follows the same guidelines as the initial application: it is necessary to demonstrate that the original conditions persist (employment, self-financing, health insurance). However, the administrative process is sometimes more streamlined, as the authorities already have historical information about the applicant.

How to obtain information on deadlines and procedures

The first step is to contact the cantonal administration of Fribourg directly. Online, it is possible to find: official forms for renewal, list of required documents (identity card, employment contract, health insurance certificate, criminal record extracts if necessary), cantonal and municipal administrative costs, and indicative processing times. This information is essential for planning the procedure in advance.

Implications for those working and residing in Fribourg

For those who already hold a B permit and reside permanently in Fribourg for work reasons, renewal is a crucial aspect of medium-term planning. Self-financing and cost of living are factors that the administration evaluates carefully. A lack of timely renewal can lead to the loss of legal residence status and, consequently, to occupational and tax complications. It is therefore essential to schedule the expiration date and start the renewal procedure at least 2-3 months before the deadline to avoid bureaucratic delays.

Key points

Practical checklist: how to proceed with the renewal

Here are the concrete steps for those who need to renew their B permit in Fribourg:

1. Identify the expiration date: Check the expiration date written on the current B permit. This is the absolute starting point.

2. Contact the Fribourg administration: Find the phone number and address of the Fribourg cantonal migration office through the official website of the canton.

3. Request the updated forms: Ask which are the official forms for renewal and the exact checklist of required documents.

4. Gather the documents: Generally, a valid passport or ID card, current employment contract or letters from the company confirming employment, Swiss health insurance certificate, residence certificate (sometimes issued by the municipality), documentation of income level if relevant, and criminal record extract (mandatory in some cantons) are required.

5. Submit the complete dossier: Start the renewal procedure at least 2-3 months before the expiration date. Submit the complete dossier to avoid requests for additional information that prolong the process.

6. Monitor the status of the application: The administration will communicate the status of the application by mail or email. Respond promptly to any requests for clarification.

7. Pick up the new permit: Once approved, the new B permit will be available for pickup at the cantonal office.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between permit L and permit B?
The L permit is a short-term permit (up to one year) normally intended for seasonal or frontier workers. Permit B is a renewable residence permit suitable for those who intend to reside permanently in Switzerland. The B permit allows a longer stay and less restricted access to the labour market than the L category.
How long before the deadline do I need to start the renewal?
It is advisable to contact the cantonal administration of Freiburg at least 2-3 months before the expiry date written on the permit. This margin allows you to collect all the documentation and avoid administrative delays that could affect the continuity of legal residence.
If my employer changes before the B permit expires, what happens?
A change of employer may require a change or supplement to the documentation submitted. It is not necessarily an acquisition of a new permit, but the cantonal administration must be informed of the change. Communicate directly to Freiburg the details of your new employment situation.
Can I reside in Freiburg with a B permit even if I work in another canton?
Yes, you can. The B permit is linked to the residence (in this case, Freiburg), but the place of work may be in another Swiss region. However, you should check that there are no territorial limitations written on your permit or in the cantonal conditions of issue.
Is health insurance (LAMal) mandatory for the renewal of permit B?
Yes, Swiss health insurance is mandatory for any resident of Switzerland, regardless of the type of leave. You must sign a contract within 3 months of arrival and keep it active. A copy of the insurance certificate must also be submitted for the renewal of the B permit.
