Operational details

The increase in passengers in July is due to the beginning of the summer holidays and the increase in transit passengers. The number of local passengers grew by 1.9%, while the turnover achieved by the airport's shops and restaurants grew by 1.2%. Planes transiting from Kloten increased by 4.3%.

The share of transit passengers in overall traffic is 27.3%, 0.3 percentage points more than a year ago. This increase is due to the increase in transit passengers, which grew by 3.8%. The canton of Zurich recorded a 5% increase in air traffic compared to the same period last year, amounting to 1,854,000 passengers.

Revenue from the airport's shops and restaurants grew by 1.2% compared to the same period last year to CHF 63.2 million. This change is attributable to a 5.9% increase in the runway side area, as opposed to a 6.3% decrease in the city side area. The city of Zurich recorded a 10% increase in sales at the airport shops compared to the same period last year.

Zurich Airport recorded a further increase in passengers in July, with an increase of 2.4% compared to the same month in 2025. This increase is due to the increase in direct flights to tourist destinations such as the Côte d 'Azur and Croatia.
