Uri: lease fees and rights

The Canton of Uri is known for its strict lease laws. The maximum security deposit is 3 months of rent, as required by the cantonal law of the URI. This means that tenants must pay a deposit of at least 3 months' rent at the start of the lease.

The cantonal law of the Uri also provides for the possibility of reducing the security deposit for leases of less than 3 years. For example, if the lease is 1 year, the security deposit could be reduced to 1 month's rent.

The cancellation of the landlord must be sent using the official cantonal form. The landlord must provide at least 3 months' notice before leaving the apartment. If the landlord does not comply with this obligation, he will have to pay a penalty of 1 month's rent.
