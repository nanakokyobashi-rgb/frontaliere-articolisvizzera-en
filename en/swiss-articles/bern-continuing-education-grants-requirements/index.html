Continuing education in the Canton of Bern: requirements and contributions

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 October 2026

In the Canton of Bern, requirements, deadlines and amounts of continuing education contributions follow cantonal rules and must be verified against the official source.

In the Canton of Bern, continuing education grants follow cantonal rules and offices. This is the operational point to start from: the requirements, application deadlines and amounts must be verified in the official source of the Canton. The support should therefore not be understood as a national amount to be automatically transferred from one region to another. For anyone seeking a grant in Bern, the first reference is the cantonal level, not a standard figure valid throughout Switzerland.

The inquiry also concerns recognized programs. The source does not provide a list of offerings, an income threshold, a submission deadline or a standard amount for the Canton of Bern. These elements should not be filled in with assumptions. Verification must remain anchored to the official cantonal source and to the cantonal office or competent institution indicated by that same channel.

The institutional framework

The distinction matters because Swiss areas of responsibility are not interchangeable. AFC/ESTV administers direct federal tax and VAT; cantonal administrations deal with cantonal and municipal taxes. UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG, and does not set taxes. UST/BFS produces statistics and does not set tax rates. These references help avoid confusing a cantonal continuing education measure with taxation, social security or statistics.

For Swiss readers, the consequence is concrete: a page or indication referring to another canton does not replace the Bern source. The name of the measure may seem similar, but verification remains that of the canton concerned. This framework does not authorize inferring either eligibility for the grant or the amount solely from enrollment in a program.

The available information is therefore clear about the method, not about the values to be entered. For an applicant, the correct question is not which national figure to apply, but which cantonal rule applies to the recognized program and to their application. The answer must be sought in the official source of the Canton. The office to contact must also be identified through the same channel, without replacing it with a federal body.

This separation is also useful for anyone checking their own social security: continuing education grants should not be presented as AVS/AHV or LPP/BVG. To explore that second area, readers can consult guide AVS/LPP/rendita, while keeping separate the cantonal verification described here.

Operational details

Why the cantonal rule changes the verification

The first practical effect of cantonal jurisdiction is the comparison of sources. A requirement read for another Canton must not be automatically transferred to Bern; likewise, an amount stated elsewhere cannot become the amount for the Bern application. The source provides a simple rule: for contributions, requirements, deadlines and amounts, the Canton’s official source must be checked.

Verification can be organized without inventing a uniform national procedure. Start with the recognized program, then check the requirements, then the application deadline and finally the expected amount. These four items do not have the same meaning: recognition concerns the pathway; requirements concern access; the deadline establishes when to act; the amount concerns the support. The source does not assign specific values, so the comparison must be limited to what is published for Bern.

In a hypothetical scenario, an applicant finds online a page describing support for continuing education. Before using it, they must ascertain that it concerns the Canton of Bern and that the page indicates the program, requirements, deadline and amount. If any of these elements is missing, it is not correct to fill the gap with data from another Canton or with a national figure.

The distinction also applies to the household budget. LAMal/KVG premiums are linked to compulsory health insurance for residents, are per capita and vary by Canton or region; the premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy. They are not, however, the same item as contributions for continuing education. Anyone assessing the cost of living can therefore keep the two pathways separate and use guide LAMal/cassa malati for the first, without treating it as evidence of the requirements for the second.

The same criterion protects against tax errors: AFC/ESTV, direct federal tax and VAT belong to a different scope from the cantonal rules on education. Verification of the contribution remains anchored in Bern, not in a generic calculation.

Key points

Operational procedure for the application

1. Define the request. State that you are seeking a contribution for continuing education in the Canton of Bern. This avoids starting from the rules of another Canton or from a different measure.

2. Open the official source of the Canton. Look for the reference to continuing education contributions and identify the indicated cantonal office or competent institution. Do not replace that reference with AFC/ESTV, UFAS/BSV or UST/BFS: they have distinct scopes, and the source assigns the matter to cantonal rules and offices.

3. Check the recognized program. Verify that the course you want to take falls within the recognized programs according to the cantonal guidelines. The source does not offer a general list, so the course name alone does not make it possible to determine eligibility for the contribution.

4. Read the requirements and application deadline. Look for the applicable conditions and the deadline indicated for Bern. Do not use a standard date, a deadline from another Canton or a national rule not reported by the source.

5. Verify the amount and follow the indicated procedure. The amount must be read in the applicable cantonal rule; the application must be addressed to the competent contact indicated by the official source. If a document or a step is unclear, the inquiry should be addressed to the same office, without completing the procedure based on assumptions.

After the cantonal review

This sequence separates the application for continuing education from the other items in the budget. LAMal/KVG premiums remain linked to mandatory health insurance for residents and to premium reduction, a cantonal subsidy; AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG belong to social security. These are different checks, involving different authorities.

Tax matters also follow another route: AFC/ESTV concerns direct federal tax and VAT, while cantonal administrations concern cantonal and municipal taxes. For continuing education, do not start from these references, but from the official source of the Canton of Bern. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the requirements for obtaining a grant in Bern?
For the Canton of Bern, the requirements are not listed as a national list in the source. The stated rule is that contributions to continuing education follow cantonal rules and offices. The applicant must therefore check the Canton’s official source to determine which conditions apply to the recognized program and the application. A requirement from another Canton must not be used automatically.
Where can deadlines and amounts be checked?
Terms and amounts must be checked in the Canton’s official source, together with the indicated cantonal office or competent institution. The source does not set a general deadline or a standard amount for Bern. Therefore, it is not correct to apply a national figure or a date found for another Canton.
Does AFC/ESTV administer contributions for continuing education?
No. AFC/ESTV concerns direct federal tax and VAT. UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG, while UST/BFS produces statistics. Contributions to continuing education instead follow cantonal rules and offices, so verification must be carried out using the official source of the Canton of Bern.
Are contributions for continuing education the same as the reduction in LAMal premiums?
No. LAMal concerns mandatory health insurance for residents; premiums are per capita and vary by canton or region. Premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy. This is a separate item from contributions to continuing education, which must be verified according to the rules and offices of the Canton.
