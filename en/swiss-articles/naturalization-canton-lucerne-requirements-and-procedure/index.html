Naturalization Requirements

To obtain Swiss citizenship in the Canton of Lucerne, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Have a valid C residence permit
  • Have spent at least 10 years of residence in the canton of Lucerne
  • Have a stable and sufficient income to support yourself
  • Have a good knowledge of the German language and Swiss education
  • Have exemplary moral and social behavior

Naturalization procedure

The naturalization procedure in the canton of Lucerne involves the following steps:

1. Submission of the application: The applicant submits the application for naturalization to the Council of States of the Canton of Lucerne. 2. Assessment of the application: The Council of States assesses the application and checks whether the applicant meets the requirements for naturalization. 3. Interview: The applicant is summoned for an interview with the Council of States to discuss his application and requirements. 4. Language and education test: The applicant undergoes an examination to check his or her knowledge of the German language and Swiss education. 5. Assessment of morality and social behaviour: The Council of States assates the moral and social behaviour of the applicant to see if they meet the requirements for naturalisation. 6. Decided: The Council of States decides whether to grant or reject the application for naturalization.

Required documents

To start the naturalization process, you must provide the documents required by the competent authorities. These include:
