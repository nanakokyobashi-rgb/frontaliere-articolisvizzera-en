Guide to the compulsory health insurance premiums in the Canton of Uri, bands, deductibles and requirements for the reduction of premiums.

The Swiss health system provides that compulsory health insurance is a fundamental pillar for all residents. In the Canton of Uri, as in the rest of the Confederation, every resident must stipulate a basic coverage within three months of their arrival or birth. This regime ensures access to essential medical benefits established by federal law, ensuring uniform treatment for basic care. The insurance is of a private type but strongly regulated by the State, with premiums fixed on a cantonal and regional basis that vary according to age and the chosen insurance model.

Regulatory aspects and cantonal competence

The management of subsidies and forms for the reduction of premiums is the responsibility of the authorities of the Canton of Uri. The Federal Office of Public Health supervises the overall framework at national level, while the practical execution of grant applications and the verification of income requirements take place through the competent cantonal offices. Operational details

Deductibles and alternative models in the Canton

To contain the economic impact of monthly premiums on family spending, policyholders in the Canton of Uri can choose from several deductible options and alternative insurance models established by the Federal Health Insurance Act. For adults, the deductibles available for the LAMal range from a minimum of CHF 300 to a maximum of CHF 2500, passing through the intermediate levels of CHF 500, 1000, 1500 and 2000. Opting for a higher deductible allows you to significantly reduce your monthly premium, but with a greater financial contribution in the event of actual medical expenses during the year. For example, a resident of Altdorf who chooses to switch from the minimum to the maximum deductible of CHF 2,500 can get a reduction in the annual premium of up to 40-50 percent, a savings quantifiable in several hundred Swiss francs, although he requires to set aside enough liquidity to cover the first healthcare expenses out of his own pocket.

Alongside the ordinary deductible, residents can opt for alternative insurance models such as the family doctor, the HMO model or telemedicine exchanges. These formulas offer percentage discounts on the basic premium in exchange for a procedural constraint: the patient agrees to consult his/her trusted doctor in advance or to call the designated hotline before going to one

How to request the reduction of premiums and deadlines

The LAMal premium reduction mechanism represents a concrete help for low-income policyholders residing in the Canton of Uri. To access this measure, you must submit a specific request to the Cantonal Office of Contributions and Subsidies of Altdorf, strictly respecting the federal deadline of October 31 for the following year. The verification is based on the economic and fiscal situation of the family unit, taking into account the determining income and the substance according to the criteria defined by the Cantonal Health Insurance Law.

Before submitting the application, it is advisable to check your situation through online simulators and consult the guide to health funds active in the Uranian territory, such as Visana or Helsana, to compare the deductible options. Those who comply with the requirements receive a reduction directly credited to the insurer.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the deadline for entering into the LAMal in the Canton of Uri?
Compulsory health insurance must be taken out within three months of arrival in the Canton of Uri or birth.
What are the allowances available for adults?
The annual deductibles for adults amount to CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500, allowing you to modulate the monthly premium according to your needs.
Who should I contact to reduce premiums in the Canton of Uri?
The application for the reduction of premiums must be submitted directly to the offices of the cantonal administration responsible for health insurance benefits.
