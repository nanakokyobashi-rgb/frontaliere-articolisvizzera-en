Lex Netflix brought more money to Swiss cinema in 2025, with a 25% increase in investment in domestic filmmaking compared to the previous year. According to the Federal Office of Culture (UFC), a total of 19.9 million francs were allocated to works and support measures for Swiss audiovisual production. This increase is a direct consequence of the new Cinema Law (LCin), passed by popular vote in 2022 and coming into force on 1 January 2024, commonly known as “Lex Netflix”. The law imposes an investment obligation for the production of "made in Switzerland" films, recalled today in an official note by the UFC.

In addition to investments in cinema, the source provides related data on the economic performance of the same companies obliged by Lex Netflix. These 22 companies achieved a decisive turnover of CHF 817 million last year (2024), marking an increase of 9% compared to the previous year. This indicates sustained growth in the streaming and related services sector in Switzerland, despite regulatory pressures.

The UFC adds an important clarification: "The streaming market continues to grow. On the other hand, the turnover deriving from advertising spaces registers a slight decrease". This suggests a possible shift in income patterns within the industry, with greater reliance on subscriptions and less on advertisements, a trend also observable internationally.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Lex Netflix and when did it go into effect?
Lex Netflix is the common name of the new Cinema Law (LCin), passed by popular vote in 2022 and coming into force on 1 January 2024. It imposes an investment obligation for the production of "made in Switzerland" films on companies that generate gross income in Switzerland.
How much should companies subject to Lex Netflix invest?
The companies concerned must devote at least 4% of their annual gross income earned in Switzerland to national film production. In 2025, 22 companies were subject to this obligation, with a determining turnover of CHF 817 million, corresponding to a theoretical obligation of CHF 32.7 million.
How much was actually invested in Swiss cinema in 2025 thanks to Lex Netflix?
In 2025, companies subject to Lex Netflix actually invested 19.9 million francs in Swiss audiovisual production, an increase of 25% over the previous year. Of this sum, 12 million went to the production and acquisition of Swiss works, with 7.4 million for series and 4.6 million for feature films, mainly fiction.
