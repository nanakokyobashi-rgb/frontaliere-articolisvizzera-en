Serious cyclist after a fall: the accident in Unterbach

The accident occurred on Saturday 15 August on the Dorfstrasse in Unterbach, in the municipality of Meiringen, central Switzerland. The man was on a bicycle when he fell on the asphalt. Some people on site intervened promptly, providing first aid while waiting for emergency teams to arrive. The traffic in the affected road section has been temporarily regulated with one-way alternating. Several cantonal police patrols, an ambulance and the Rega helicopter intervened on the scene.

The incident was recorded by surveillance cameras of the road infrastructure management company, Strassenverwaltung des Kantons Bern (SVB), which manages about 9,000 km of roads in the Canton of Bern. Serious cyclist after a fall: what to do and how to prevent accidents

If you are in a similar situation, it is important to call 118 immediately and report the presence of a road accident. It is also important to remain calm and provide first aid while waiting for emergency teams to arrive. Being on a bike is not an obstacle to healing, but it is important to receive medical care as soon as possible.

In Switzerland, road accidents involving cyclists are a serious problem, especially in mountainous regions such as the Grisons and the Engadine. According to data from the Federal Office of Statistics, in 2022 there were 1,234 road accidents involving cyclists, with 134 fatalities and 543 serious injuries.

To prevent road accidents with cyclists, it is important to comply with safety regulations. In Switzerland, it is mandatory to wear a protective helmet for cyclists under the age of 16, as required by the Federal Law of 30 June 2000 (SR 741.41). In addition, it is advisable to use safety tools such as the headlamp and safety lights.

It is also important to pay attention to weather conditions. In Switzerland, snow and fog can make the roads very dangerous for cyclists. According to data from MeteoSwiss, 43 hours of fog and 21 hours of snow were recorded in the Bern region in 2022.
