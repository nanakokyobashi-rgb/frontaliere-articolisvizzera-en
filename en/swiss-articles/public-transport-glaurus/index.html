Zones and Rates

The Canton of Glarus is located in the central region of Switzerland, with a population of about 39,000. To facilitate public transport, a system of zones and specific tariffs has been established. Passengers can choose between annual or monthly passes, depending on their needs.

Zone 1 includes the city of Glarus and neighboring municipalities, while Zone 2 covers the outermost areas of the Canton. Rates vary depending on the area and the type of subscription chosen. For example, an annual subscription for zone 1 costs CHF 460, while a monthly subscription costs CHF 38.

Combinations and Promotions

In addition to annual and monthly subscriptions, you can purchase half-price and AG (Glarus Association) combinations. These combinations offer further savings for passengers who use public transport regularly.

For example, an annual subscription for zone 1 with a half-price combination costs CHF 230, while a monthly subscription with a half-price combination costs CHF 19.

Operational details

The public transport pass in the Glarus Canton allows passengers to move easily within the region. Prices are specified by area and the Canton of Glarus also offers annual and monthly subscription options. In addition, you can combine the half-price and AG subscription to get additional benefits.

Zones and Rates

The Glarus Canton is divided into several zones, each with its own tariffs. Zone 1 includes the town of Glarus and neighbouring municipalities, while Zone 2 covers the surrounding mountainous region. Prices for zone 1 are CHF 2.50 per day, while for zone 2 they are CHF 4.00 per day. You can purchase a daily ticket or a monthly or yearly subscription.

Subscription options

The Canton of Glarus offers several subscription options, including:

  • Monthly subscription: 60.00 CHF per month for zone 1 and 80.00 CHF per month for zone 2
  • Annual subscription: CHF 720.00 per year for zone 1 and CHF 960.00 per year for zone 2
  • Student subscription: 30.00 CHF per month for zone 1 and 40.00 CHF per month for zone 2

Half-price and AG combinations

You can combine subscription with half-price and AG to get additional benefits. For example, if you have an annual subscription for zone 1, you can buy a daily ticket for zone 2 at half price, i.e. CHF 2.00 per day instead of CHF 4.00.

Canton Glarus is divided into several subscription zones, each with specific rates. The zones are:

  • Zone A: includes the city of Glarus and the surrounding municipalities.
  • Zone B: includes the municipalities of the district of Glarus North.
  • Zone C: includes the municipalities of the district of South Glarus.

Membership fees vary depending on the area of residence and the age of the membership holder. For example, a monthly subscription for zone A costs CHF 120, while an annual subscription costs CHF 1,440.

Half-price and AG combinations
