Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 19 August 2026

The Swiss manufacturer Burckhardt Compression announces cuts for 220 total jobs, of which 150 in Winterthur and 70 in international locations.

In a nutshell

Key facts

  • What: Announcement of a new job cut
  • When: First half of the 2026/27 financial year for charges
  • Where: Winterthur and international locations
  • Who: Burckhardt Compression and CEO Fabrice Billard
  • Amount: 220 total jobs involved

Compressor manufacturer Burckhardt Compression has officially announced a new job cut, motivated by lower production capacity and the need to simplify its organisational structure. A total of up to 220 jobs across the group are involved. Of these, up to 150 jobs are located in the Winterthur headquarters, while the remaining 70 affect the company's international offices. The announcement comes after the company had already announced cuts in November, with further clarifications provided in January regarding 68 positions also affected in Winterthur. Currently, the company employs just over 3,300 employees, exceeding the threshold of 1,000 employees at its Winterthur headquarters. Operational details

Business analysis and reorganization

The strategic decision announced by the top management of Burckhardt Compression involves a series of important organizational maneuvers that directly affect the cost structure and operational management of the industrial group. CEO Fabrice Billard outlined the new measures needed to ensure Burckhardt Compression's competitiveness and sustainable success in the international economic landscape. The company has expressed full awareness of the climate of uncertainty that inevitably arises for employees potentially involved in this complex phase of transition and restructuring. To mitigate these impacts, management has ensured that the consultation procedure will take place in an open and constructive manner, respecting the deadlines established until 9 September. In parallel with the staff cuts in Winterthur and in foreign offices, the business plan provides for an internal reorganisation aimed at the grouping of specific company functions and the overall optimisation of production and administrative processes. A central element of this strategic evolution is the strengthening of the organization in India, a pole clearly considered key to the future positioning of the group on the global compressor markets. Key points

Financial impacts and future prospects

From a strictly economic and financial standpoint, the restructuring plan announced by Burckhardt Compression will entail substantial initial costs, followed by significant structural benefits in the medium and long term. To implement the described measures, the company expects to record, during the first half of the 2026/27 financial year, one-off restructuring charges estimated at approximately 3 million francs. Once the entire reorganization is completed and the new organizational guidelines are implemented, the industrial group expects annual cost savings estimated at around 20 million francs. Despite the extent of the job cuts in Winterthur and abroad, the company has fully confirmed its financial outlook for the entire fiscal year. Regarding the 2026/27 financial year, Burckhardt Compression expects to record revenues between 900 million and 1 billion francs, accompanied by an expected EBIT margin of approximately 12%. These figures confirm the underlying strength of the group despite the need to correct production capacity.
