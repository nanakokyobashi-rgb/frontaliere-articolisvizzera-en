Permit B Valais: requirements and renewal (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 5 September 2026

How to renew the residence permit B in Valais: the requirements, the necessary documents and the procedures at the Cantonal Office of Migration.

Context

In brief

  • Permit B is a renewable long-term residence permit for foreigners
  • Valais has specific cantonal requirements and procedures
  • Renewal must be requested before expiration from the competent office
  • Required documents vary depending on the work situation

Key facts

  • What: Residence permit B for regular foreign residents
  • Where: Canton of Valais (Cantonal Migration Office)
  • Who: Foreigners living and working in Switzerland
  • Duration: Renewable periodically according to federal provisions
  • Federal authority: State Secretariat for Migration (SEM)
  • Nature: Federal administrative authorization managed by the cantons

The residence permit B represents a form of authorization for foreign residents who wish to settle in Switzerland for an extended period. Unlike the permit L, valid for a short duration of up to one year, the permit B allows for long-term residence and is renewable according to the conditions set by federal and cantonal authorities. In the Canton of Valais, as in all Swiss cantons, the management of this administrative procedure is entrusted to the Cantonal Migration Office, which applies the federal principles established by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) and adapts them to local regulations.

Operational details

Fundamental Requirements for Permit B in Valais

The procedure for granting the residence permit B in Valais is based on several fundamental pillars verified by the cantonal authorities. The first is regular employment: the applicant must have a valid employment contract recognized by Swiss law. If the work is self-employed, it must be registered with the competent commercial authorities. A second requirement is the availability of suitable housing: the Cantonal Migration Office verifies that the accommodation meets Swiss standards of health, square footage, and compliance with building and health protection regulations. A third element concerns the financial situation: the applicant must not represent an economic burden on the Swiss social security system, so they must prove sufficient financial means. Finally, public order conditions must be met: no ongoing criminal proceedings, no convictions for serious crimes, and no expulsion orders in force.

Key points

How to proceed: step-by-step guide for renewal

The first step is to gather all the required documentation, checking the expiration dates and validity of each document. Passport, updated employment contract, criminal record, proof of residence, and financial documentation must be in order before contacting the office. In the second step, you need to contact the Canton of Valais Migration Office to verify the specific submission methods for your case and to receive the official renewal form. The office will provide a detailed checklist of the documents required according to your personal and work situation. In the third step, fill out the renewal form in all its parts, attaching the required documents and signing where necessary. If possible, request a deposit receipt at the time of submission as proof of the application delivery. After submission, monitor the status of the request by periodically contacting the office or consulting the online portal if available. In case of requests for additional documentation, respond promptly: delays in submitting supplementary documents can prolong processing times or, in extreme cases, result in the rejection of the application.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between permit B and permit L in Valais?
The L permit is valid for up to one year and is intended for temporary stays and fixed-term employment contracts. The B permit is for long-term residence and is renewable; it is granted to those who intend to settle permanently in Switzerland. In Valais, as in all of Switzerland, the B permit is the most common permit for foreign workers who usually reside in the canton.
When do I need to apply for a B permit renewal in Valais?
It is advisable to submit the renewal request at least three months before the expiry of the current permit. This allows the Valais Cantonal Office of Migration to process the application seamlessly in your residence permit. Delays in filing may compromise the continuity of legal residency.
What documents are absolutely mandatory to renew the B permit?
Essential documents are: valid passport or ID, current employment contract, updated criminal certificate, and proof of compliant accommodation. To these you can add tax and bank certificates to certify financial solvency. The Cantonal Office of Migration provides a complete list based on your personal situation.
Can I renew the B permit if my employment contract is about to end?
Normally, renewal of the B permit requires regular employment and a valid employment contract. If your contract is about to expire, you must notify the Cantonal Office of Migration and prove that you have an agreed new occupation or a certified alternative source of income. Significant changes in the work situation must be communicated promptly.
How long does it take to process a renewal application in Valais?
The average processing time is four to eight weeks from the complete submission of the application. However, if the Cantonal Office of Migration requires additional documentation or thorough checks, the timeframe may be extended. It is for this reason that it is advisable to submit the application well in advance of the deadline.
