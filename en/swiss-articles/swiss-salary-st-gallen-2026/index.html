5.3% of AHV/IV/EO contributions are paid by the employee, while the total with the employer reaches 10.6%. It is the first step to read the average salary by profession in Switzerland in 2026 without confusing the gross with what remains in the paycheck in the canton of St. Gallen.

For statistics, the reference is FSO/BFS. The median salary is one of the figures indexed each year: the precise amounts must be checked against the correct year and the official source. An unverified value cannot become a 2026 figure attributed to a profession or the canton of St. Gallen.

The tax perimeter

The tax framework follows a separate logic. In Switzerland, there are three levels: direct federal tax, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each canton has its own law and multiplier; the municipality applies a multiplier on the cantonal tax. FTA/ESTV manages IFD and VAT, cantonal administrations cantonal and municipal taxation. FSO/BFS produces statistics, does not set rates; FSIO/BSV concerns social security and does not set taxes.

Payroll Entries

The transition from gross to net requires isolating contributions. AHV/IV/EO is 5.3% for the employee and 10.6% overall with the employer. AD/CA is 1.1% up to the annual ceiling. UVG/LAA is indicated between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the sector. BVG is calculated on the coordinated salary: 7% between 25 and 34 years old, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54, 18% from 55 years old up to the reference age.

The minimum wage completes the reading of the labor market: there is no federal threshold, while some cantons have their own rules. Working conditions also matter: the maximum working time is 45 hours per week in industry, offices and sales, or 50 in other sectors; holidays are at least four weeks, five under the age of 20. Maternity lasts 14 weeks at 80% through EO and paternity leave two weeks.

Operational details

Three accounts not to be mixed

A useful comparison for the canton of St. Gallen should put three levels on the same sheet: salary data, deductions and departures that are not withheld. The median salary is among the figures indexed each year; for 2026, only the value for the correct year should be used. A salary statistic does not describe the net or the cost of living on its own.

The easiest place to make mistakes is the KVG. For residents, it is mandatory and must be taken out within 3 months of arrival, but it works with per capita premiums: it is not a tax and it is not a wage contribution. The premiums depend on the canton and region, while the premium reduction is cantonal. For adults, the deductibles indicated are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500.

For the same gross, the result may change if the relevant age group for the BVG, the canton or region of the KVG and the tax municipality change. In the canton of St. Gallen, the municipal multiplier must therefore be kept separate from the cantonal law: the municipality applies its own multiplier on the cantonal tax. Without an updated official rate, an invented percentage does not have to be entered. VAT also remains within the federal tax perimeter of the FTA/ESTV, not among the withholdings to be confused with AHV, ALV, UVG or BVG.

Key points

How to check an offer for 2026

Five-Step Procedure

1. Define the scope. Write down the profession, industry, canton of St. Gallen and year 2026. Look up the statistical data at FSO/BFS and check that any median salary refers to the correct year. Do not replace a missing figure with an estimate.

2. Start from gross. Separate AHV/IV/EO, ALV/CA, UVG/LAA and BVG. Record 5.3% payable by the employee for AHV/IV/EO, 10.6% overall with the employer, 1.1% ALV/AC up to the annual ceiling and 0.7–1.5% UVG/LAA depending on the sector. For BVG, use the coordinated salary and the expected age group.

3. Add the KVG. If you live in Switzerland, you must take out insurance within 3 months of arrival. The premium is per capita, not a tax or a wage contribution; check canton or region, adult deductible and possible cantonal reduction. The deductibles indicated are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 or 2500.

4. Reconstruct the tax system. Divide direct federal, cantonal and municipal tax. For IFD and VAT, the reference is FTA/ESTV; for cantonal and municipal taxes, the cantonal administrations. Consider the multiplier of the municipality, without assigning rates to FSO/BFS or FSIO/BSV.

5. Check the contract. Check the minimum wage: there is no federal threshold, but some cantons have their own. Then compare maximum hours, holidays, maternity, paternity and termination: 45 or 50 hours depending on the sector, at least 4 weeks of vacation, 14 weeks of maternity leave at 80%, 2 weeks of paternity leave; the termination is 1 month in the first year, 2 from the second to the ninth and 3 from the tenth.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does Switzerland have a federal minimum wage in 2026?
The text indicates that there is no federal minimum wage. However, some Cantons have their own minimum wage. For an offer in the canton of San Gallo, it is therefore necessary to check the applicable cantonal level and distinguish the general rule from the local one. The comparison also remains tied to the profession and sector: the absence of a national threshold does not provide a wage valid for every activity.
Which contributions reduce gross salary?
For the employee, AVS/AI/IPG is 5.3%; the total with the employer is 10.6%. AD/AC is 1.1% up to the annual maximum. LAINF/LAA ranges from 0.7% to 1.5% depending on the sector. LPP concerns the coordinated salary and provides for 7% between ages 25 and 34, 10% between 35 and 44, 15% between 45 and 54, and 18% from age 55 up to the reference age.
How does LAMal affect salary comparisons?
For residents, LAMal is mandatory and must be taken out within 3 months of arrival. It operates with per-capita premiums, is not a tax and is not a salary contribution. Premiums vary by canton or region; a cantonal premium reduction exists. For adults, the deductibles indicated are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500.
How are taxes divided among the Confederation, cantons, and municipalities?
Taxation comprises federal IFD, cantonal tax and municipal tax. Each canton has its own law and multiplier, while the municipality applies a multiplier to the cantonal tax. AFC/ESTV handles IFD and VAT; the cantonal administrations oversee cantonal and municipal taxes. UST/BFS is responsible for statistics and UFAS/BSV for social security, not for setting tax rates.
