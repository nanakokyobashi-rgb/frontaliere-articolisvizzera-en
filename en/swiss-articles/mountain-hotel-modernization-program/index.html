Mountain hotels: 100 million for modernization (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 September 2026

The Federal Council launches a program of 100 million francs over eight years for the energy rehabilitation of mountain hotel facilities.

Context

In brief

  • The Federal Council has approved an additional message for the sector.
  • An impulse program is planned to modernize mountain hotels.
  • A total of 100 million francs will be allocated over eight years.
  • The management of the funds is entrusted to the Swiss Hotel Credit Society.

Key facts

  • What: Impulse program for the modernization of mountain hotels
  • Who: Federal Council and Swiss Hotel Credit Society (SHCS)
  • Amount: 100 million francs
  • Duration: Eight years
  • Objective: Energy renovation of the facilities

The government has formally adopted the additional message relating to the total revision of the federal law on the promotion of the hotel sector. The initiative aims to support the modernization of accommodation facilities located in mountain regions, particularly encouraging energy renovation measures. The entire program, which provides for a total allocation of 100 million francs, will be managed through the Swiss Hotel Credit Society (SHCS).

…

Operational details

The introduction of this stimulus program represents a significant change from previous executive assessments. In a previous phase, the Federal Council had expressed reservations about the implementation of the Stöckli motion. The concerns were justified by the federal financial situation, the operational complexity of the program, and the fact that no immediate urgency had been identified for additional support subsidies. However, parliamentary debate led to a re-evaluation of strategic objectives, culminating in the decision to integrate the requested resources.

Key points

For hotel companies operating in mountainous regions, the implementation of the program involves an access procedure to funds through the Swiss Hotel Credit Company (SCA). Interested companies are invited to monitor the regulatory developments related to the total revision of the federal law, as the operational details for submitting requests will be defined in the implementing decrees accompanying the adopted message. Compliance with energy efficiency criteria will be the fundamental requirement to benefit from the incentives provided by the stimulus program.

Tools and Perspectives for Operators

It is advisable for hotel owners to stay updated through the official channels of the Confederation and the SCA to understand the methods of project submission. Since the program has a limited duration of eight years, the planning of interventions must be timely to fall within the defined funding scope. The modernization of infrastructure represents a key step to maintain high service quality in an extremely competitive global market. For those operating in the sector, evaluating the economic impact of such investments requires a thorough analysis of operating costs and expected long-term benefits, also considering other cost factors such as rental expenses, labor, and social contributions.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the new pulse program?
The programme provides for a total allocation of CHF 100 million over eight years. The main objective is to support the modernization of accommodation facilities located in mountain regions, with a specific focus on energy rehabilitation interventions. This measure aims to improve the efficiency of buildings, reduce environmental impact and optimise management costs related to energy consumption, making the facilities more competitive in the international tourism landscape.
Who manages the funds allocated for hotels?
The operational management of the funds was entrusted to the Swiss Hotel Credit Company (SCA). The SCA will act as a financial lever, facilitating access to credit for operators in the sector in areas where private investment could be more complex. The companies concerned will have to deal directly with the SCA for access to financing, following the procedures that will be defined in the implementing decrees of the revision of the federal law on the promotion of the hotel sector.
What are the eligibility requirements for incentives?
The basic requirement to be eligible for the incentives is compliance with the energy renovation standards required by the program. Since the measure is aimed at the enhancement of physical assets and energy efficiency, companies will have to submit projects that comply with defined ecological criteria.
