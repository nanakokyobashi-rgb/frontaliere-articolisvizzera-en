Operational details

Article 253 of the Swiss Civil Code regulates the right of tenancy. The maximum security deposit is 3 months rent. The lessor's cancellation must be submitted on an official cantonal form. The Canton of Svitto does not specify the average fees per area.

Swiss law provides that the landlord must provide the tenant with a written lease agreement, which includes the rental conditions, the rent and the security deposit. The contract must be signed by both parties and must be kept for at least 10 years.

The security deposit is a sum of money that the tenant pays to the landlord at the time of signing the lease. This sum is returned to the tenant at the end of the contract, unless there is damage to the property. The maximum security deposit is 3 months' rent, as provided for in Article 253 of the Swiss Civil Code.
