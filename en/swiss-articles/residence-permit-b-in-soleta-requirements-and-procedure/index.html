# Residence Permit Requirements B

To obtain a residence permit B in the Canton of Solothurn, you must meet the requirements established by cantonal law. These include:

  • Be at least 20 years old
  • Have enough income to support themselves financially
  • Not be a danger to public order or social security
  • Not having committed serious crimes in Switzerland or abroad
  • Have an employment or pension contract with an employer in Switzerland
  • Have basic health insurance in Switzerland

Procedure for renewal of residence permit B

Residence permit B is renewable every 5 years. The renewal procedure is managed by the cantonal migration office. To renew the B residence permit, it is necessary to submit the application within 3 months before the expiry of the permit.

Costs and timing

The cost of the application for renewal of the residence permit B is CHF 300. The renewal procedure can take up to 6 months. You can speed up the procedure by paying a surcharge of CHF 100.
