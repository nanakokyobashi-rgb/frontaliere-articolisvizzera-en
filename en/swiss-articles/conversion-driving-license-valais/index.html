Foreign driver's license conversion in the Valais Canton: guide (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

Obligation to convert the foreign license within 12 months for new residents in Valais: here is the procedure and documents required by the cantonal authority.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Mandatory conversion within one year for those who settle in the Valais.
  • The competent authority is the Valais Canton Traffic Service.
  • It is necessary to pass exams if the license is not from a partner country.
  • The procedure requires original documents and updated medical certificates.

Key facts

  • What: Conversion of foreign driving license.
  • When: Within 12 months of the official transfer of residence.
  • Where: Canton of Valais, Switzerland.
  • Who: Cantonal traffic service.
  • Amount: Variable according to the administrative rights in force.

The Valais Canton Circulation Service regulates the procedures for converting foreign driving licenses for residents in the Valais territory. Anyone who transfers their residence to Switzerland is obliged to convert their foreign driving licence within a maximum period of twelve months. This legislation, applied at cantonal level in accordance with federal provisions, aims to ensure that all drivers on Swiss roads possess the necessary skills, verified according to Swiss standards. The administrative procedure is divided into several phases, which vary significantly depending on the State of origin of the original license. For citizens from countries that have signed specific mutual recognition agreements with Switzerland, the process is usually more streamlined.

Once residency is obtained, the citizen should not consider the foreign license as an unlimited document. It is advisable to plan the conversion immediately after registration with the municipality of residence, avoiding waiting until the twelve-month expiration date approaches. This promptness allows for the management of any requests for additional documents without the risk of being left without a valid driving title. The substantial difference lies in the recognition of skills: while in some cases the change is purely administrative, in other scenarios the Road Traffic Service requires a practical verification. This check should not be seen as an obstacle but as a guarantee of safety for all road users. Differences between conversion procedures For those settling in Switzerland, comparing their situation with the directives of the Road Traffic Service is a necessary step to avoid complications. A central aspect concerns the conformity of documents. If the foreign license is not written in one of the national languages or in English, an officially recognized translation may be required, an element that often extends the processing time of the procedure. It is also useful to remember that the Swiss driving title, once obtained, follows the validity and renewal rules provided by federal law. In case of doubts about one's driving category or the restrictions possibly present on the original document, the official website of the canton or direct contact with the Valais office represent the only certain sources. Anyone who has to go through this process would do well to promptly verify the status of their license and the presence of any deadlines, regularly consulting the canton's portal for updates on procedures or administrative fees in force.

Key points

To proceed with the conversion of the driver's license in the Canton of Valais, the user must follow a defined procedure, ensuring they have all the necessary documentation. The first step is to obtain the official form from the Road Traffic Service of the Canton of Valais. It is essential to complete the form in its entirety, attaching the required documentation, including the original driver's license and the medical certificate of fitness to drive. The certificate must be issued by a recognized doctor who certifies the absence of contraindications to driving. Once the documents are gathered, the application must be submitted in person or through the official channels indicated by the canton. It is important to pay attention to the photograph, which must meet specific quality standards, similar to those required for passports. After submission, the office will verify the validity of the foreign license and determine if a control exam is necessary. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the deadline to convert the foreign license to Valais?
Those who transfer their residence to the Valais Canton are obliged to convert their foreign driving licence within a maximum period of twelve months from the official transfer. It is essential to request the conversion before the expiry date, since at the end of this tolerance period the validity of the foreign permit ceases, making it impossible to drive on the national territory until obtaining the Swiss title.
What documents are required for the conversion process?
For the conversion, the user must submit the official application form of the Valais Canton Traffic Service, a colour photograph compliant with the technical requirements, the medical certificate of suitability issued by a qualified professional and the original of the foreign license. If the document is not written in a national language or in English, a recognised official translation may be required.
Is it always necessary to pass driving tests for conversion?
The procedure varies depending on the State of origin of the license. For citizens from countries with mutual recognition agreements, the practice is often limited to the presentation of documents. For those coming from countries without such agreements, the procedure may include passing theoretical or practical tests, aimed at ascertaining knowledge of the local road code and actual driving skills, as established by the Traffic Service.
