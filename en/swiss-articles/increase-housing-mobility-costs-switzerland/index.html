Operational details

Rising housing and mobility costs have a significant impact on the middle class in Switzerland. Ticino, in particular, is the region most affected by this increase, with an annual increase of 2.1% in the housing and mobility sectors. At the national level, the most marked increases concern fuels, up 12.7% compared to a year ago. Energy costs for heating also increased significantly (+11.4%). In particular, fuel oil rose by 37.1%, while firewood increased by 8.8%. 📊 Concrete examples : In Bellinzona, one of Ticino's largest cities, electricity prices increased by 15% during the year. This has led to an increase in home heating costs, which now represent a significant part of the household budget. In addition, natural gas prices increased by 10%, causing heating and cooking costs to rise. 💡 Regulations and dates: Switzerland has recently introduced new regulations for home heating, which include the installation of solar panels to reduce energy consumption. However, rising costs have made these solutions less affordable for many families.
