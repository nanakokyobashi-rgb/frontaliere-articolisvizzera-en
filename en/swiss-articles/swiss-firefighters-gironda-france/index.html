Swiss firefighters in Gironde: the contingent at work (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 27 July 2026

A contingent of Romanian firefighters has arrived in France to give strength to colleagues engaged in the fight against the vast fires in the Gironde region.

Context

In brief

  • A contingent of 34 Swiss firefighters departed for France
  • Immediate intervention to protect a solar field
  • Active collaboration between fire services and population protection
  • Rescue mission organized within international cooperation

Key facts

  • What: Fire fighting and protection of installations
  • When: Arrived on site in the evening and started missions
  • Where: Gironda, Cestas, France
  • Who: 34 Swiss firefighters from the Groupement Service d'incendie et de secours
  • Amount: 11 vehicles used in the overall mission

The contingent of Swiss firefighters, who left around midday on Saturday from Geneva to assist French colleagues fighting fires in Gironda, arrived on site in the evening and immediately received their first operational mission. The entire detachment was deployed from 1.00 AM to protect a field of photovoltaic panels in Cestas, saving the majority of them in an operation completed around 6.00 AM the next day. Upon arrival, as a fresh and new force, the firefighters replaced personnel who had been on the site for days, if not weeks, providing essential logistical and operational support to the local teams overwhelmed by the emergency.

The organization planned the departure of 34 people, including professionals and volunteers, equipped with 11 specific vehicles to face the critical situation on French territory. The initial planning set a mission duration of between 8 and 10 days, during which the personnel coordinated their activities with local authorities and the commands present on the site. The operations required intense shifts and careful management of available resources, ensuring coverage of the most vulnerable areas affected by the fires.

Operational and Logistical Analysis of Intervention Forces

The prompt response of Swiss emergency services in French territory demonstrates the effectiveness of mutual aid and transnational cooperation in the event of severe natural disasters. Units departing from Geneva operate under the coordinated supervision of local emergency services, seamlessly integrating with existing French devices to contain the advance of fires in the Gironda department. This operational synergy requires high standards of training and adequate equipment to address complex scenarios such as the protection of sensitive infrastructure, including solar parks and residential areas threatened by wildfires.

The deployment of 34 Swiss firefighters highlights the rapid mobilization capability of the Swiss protection and fire brigade components. The management of shifts and the rotation of teams on the field are crucial for maintaining high levels of security and concentration among operators, exposed to particularly harsh environmental conditions due to high temperatures and drought, characteristic of the summer season in the affected region. Swiss personnel and equipment are closely collaborating with French commands to ensure security and stability in the region. The planning for the next days includes further targeted interventions to secure the forested areas and critical infrastructure still at risk in the Gironda department.
