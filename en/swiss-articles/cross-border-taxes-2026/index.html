The introduction of the new tax rules from 2024 represents a substantial change for border workers between Italy and Switzerland, with implications that go beyond the simple deductible. This difference translates into concrete tax savings, especially for those with annual incomes between 20,000 and 50,000 euros. Before the agreement, border taxation provided for a withholding tax in Switzerland with lower deductibles and more complex procedures. Now, the withholding takes place in Switzerland, avoiding double taxation and simplifying administrative procedures. Switzerland applies the withholding tax rate at source, while in Italy the worker can request the tax credit in the annual return.
