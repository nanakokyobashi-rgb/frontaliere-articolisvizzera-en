The Swiss Chair of the OSCE organized a conference in Bern to discuss the origins of the Conference on Security and Cooperation in Europe (CSCE) and the importance of its founding principles for European security. The conference, held on 3 September 2026, brought together political figures, diplomats, historians and experts. Participants include OSCE Chairman-in-Office Ignazio Cassis and OSCE Secretary-General Feridun H. Sinirlioğlu.

Context and Objectives

The conference was organised by the FDFA in collaboration with the Dodis (Swiss Diplomatic Documents) Research Centre. The event aimed to reflect on the origins of the CSCE and the importance of its founding principles for European security today and in the future. During the conference, the OSCE issued a press release available at the following link: OSCE Press Release, 03.09.2026.

Operational details

Practical Analysis

The conference represented an opportunity to reflect on the evolution of European security and the importance of the founding principles of the CSCE. The participation of prominent figures such as Ignazio Cassis and Feridun H. Sinirlioğlu underscored the international significance of the event.

Implications for Switzerland

For Switzerland, the conference offered an occasion to strengthen its role in promoting security and cooperation in Europe. The country's active participation in such events is crucial for maintaining a constructive dialogue with other European nations and contributing to the stability of the continent.

Contribution of the FDFA and the Dodis Research Center

The FDFA and the Dodis Research Center played a crucial role in organizing the conference. Their contribution made it possible to bring together experts and prominent figures to discuss topics of great relevance to European security. The collaboration between these institutions demonstrated Switzerland's commitment to promoting international cooperation.
