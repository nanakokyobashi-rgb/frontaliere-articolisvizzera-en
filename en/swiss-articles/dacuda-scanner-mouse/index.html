The product that had attracted attention

The start-up, founded in 2009 as a spin-off of the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Zurich (ETH), had attracted attention with a device that combined the functions of a computer mouse and a portable scanner.

It was straightforward to use. All it took was moving the mouse over documents, images or newspaper clippings to digitize them. Special software automatically combined the individual captures and transformed them into a composite image. The process therefore made it possible to combine the different parts captured while moving over the material into a single result.

The technology also found applications outside the original device, including in the scanner mice of the electronics group LG. Dacuda subsequently developed Pocketscan, described in the source as a portable scanner.

The company's development also included 3D scanning. In 2017, Dacuda sold its 3D scanning division to the US company Magic Leap. That same year, some former employees founded PXL Vision in Zurich, a company specializing in digital identity verification. The publication in the Commercial Register now marks the dissolution of the original company.
