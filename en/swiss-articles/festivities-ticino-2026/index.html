Discover the main public holidays in Ticino in 2026 and how they will affect the daily life of residents and cross-border workers.

Context

In Brief

  • Public holidays in Ticino are observed according to the Swiss federal calendar.
  • Key holidays include New Year’s Day, Good Friday, Easter, Easter Monday, Labour Day, St. John’s Day, Assumption of Mary, All Saints’ Day, and Christmas.
  • Some of these holidays may impact public services and shop opening hours.

Key Facts

  • What: Public holidays in Ticino.
  • When: 2026, various dates.
  • Where: Ticino, Switzerland.
  • Who: All residents and cross-border workers.
  • Amount: No direct financial impact.

Public holidays in Ticino in 2026 will be celebrated according to the Swiss federal calendar. It’s important to be aware of these dates to best plan your daily life, especially for those who work or study in the region.

The first holiday of the year will be New Year’s Day on January 1st, 2026. Other important dates include Good Friday (March 29th, 2026), Easter (March 31st, 2026) and Easter Monday (April 1st, 2026). Labour Day on May 1st, 2026 and St. John’s Day on June 24th, 2026 are also significant dates.

Impact on Daily Activities

Public holidays can influence shop opening hours, public services, and transportation. For example, during major holidays such as Christmas (December 25th, 2026) and All Saints’ Day (November 1st, 2026), some services may be reduced or closed.

Operational details
