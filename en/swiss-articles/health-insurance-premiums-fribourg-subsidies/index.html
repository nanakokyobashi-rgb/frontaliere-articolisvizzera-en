KVG premiums Fribourg: deductibles and subsidies 2026 (cross-border guide)

KVG deductibles in the Canton of Fribourg, how to choose, procedures for accessing the premium reduction and official deadlines 2026.

KVG (compulsory health insurance) is the health protection system that protects every resident of Switzerland, including the canton of Fribourg. Unlike pension or unemployment insurance, KVG is entirely privatised: it is not a federal tax, but a policy taken out directly with an authorised commercial insurer. Every resident has a legal obligation to take out within three months of arriving in the canton; those who fail to do so lose their right to cantonal subsidies and risk administrative fines.

Why each canton manages subsidies differently

The federal system sets the basic rules for KVG (standardised deductibles, minimum coverage, equity monitoring), but the canton of Fribourg manages premium reduction subsidies itself. This means that the income criteria, the amount of the benefit, the application deadlines and the required documents vary from canton to canton. An income that entitles you to the subsidy in Fribourg may not entitle you in another region. For this reason, direct consultation with the Fribourg administration is essential to learn about the specific rules for 2026.

The deductibles available in Freiburg

All licensed insurers in Switzerland, including those operating in Fribourg, offer six deductible options for adults: CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500. The deductible is the amount that each insured pays in full out of pocket before the insurance company starts covering medical costs. A deductible of CHF 300 implies a higher annual premium, as the insurer covers almost all costs over CHF 300. Conversely, a deductible of CHF 2500 requires a much lower premium, because the insured assumes a higher risk. The choice depends on medical consumption habits, economic situation and family needs.

The prizes change every year and the exchange procedure

Every autumn, the individual insurers communicate the new premiums for the following year. In the canton of Fribourg, as in the whole of Switzerland, anyone wishing to change insurers or increase their chosen deductible (to save on premiums) is entitled to cancel their policy by 30 November of the current year. Termination must be made in accordance with the contractual procedures (usually by registered letter or cantonal form). The effect of the termination is 1 January of the following year: the contract ends and the new one begins. This mechanism allows everyone to adapt their coverage to the new premium levels without penalty.

Operational details

How the premium reduction subsidy works at cantonal level

The canton of Fribourg finances a subsidy for residents who are unable to pay the KVG premium for economic reasons. This subsidy (also referred to as "premium reduction" or "contributions" depending on the canton) is an annual payment based on income and assets. Unlike other federal social benefits (such as AHV/AHV or unemployment), entitlement to KVG benefits is managed exclusively by the canton; there are no uniform federal rules. Each Swiss canton therefore sets its own criteria, amounts and procedures independently.

Who is entitled to the Fribourg cantonal subsidy

The entitlement is triggered when the annual KVG premium exceeds a certain percentage of the declared gross income. In most cantons, this threshold varies from 5% to 8%, but the canton of Fribourg sets the precise value each year and publishes it on its official website. In addition, the canton considers movable and immovable assets. Couples, families with minor children and single people have different income thresholds; the canton applies growth factors for each family member. The data declared in the cantonal tax return of the previous year are used to assess eligibility.

Practical scenario: who benefits

Imagine a single person residing in Fribourg with a gross annual income of CHF 32,000 (about CHF 2,666/month). If you choose a deductible of CHF 500, the annual premium could be CHF 2,800. The premium-to-income ratio is 2,800 ÷ 32,000 = 8.75%. If the Fribourg cantonal threshold is set at 7%, this person exceeds the threshold and is eligible for the subsidy. The canton could cover CHF 400-600 per year, reducing the actual cost of the premium and making coverage more affordable.

What happens if the subsidy is refused

If the canton denies the subsidy, the reason must be communicated by official letter. The refusal can occur if the income is above the threshold, the assets are excessive, or the documentation is incomplete. In these cases, the applicant has the right to lodge an appeal with the cantonal appeal authority within the deadline set by the canton (check the official deadline with the office). The appeal must justify the office's error: misdeclared data, miscalculated threshold, documents not considered correctly. If the application is incomplete, the canton usually notifies and asks for the missing documents before refusing.

Key points

How to apply for the KVG subsidy in Fribourg

Step 1: Check your pre-eligibility Calculate the ratio between the annual KVG premium (visible in the bulletin received from the insurer) and your gross income declared on the last cantonal tax return form. If the report exceeds the threshold published by the Canton of Fribourg (check the official website of the social benefits section), you are potentially eligible.

Step 2: Gather the required documentation Prepare: copy of the KVG policy confirmation letter (insurance certificate), last premium slip received from the insurer, tax return from the previous year (Fribourg cantonal form), copy of identity card or passport, and ownership documents or rental agreement (if applicable).

Step 3: Fill out the cantonal form Download the application form from the official website of the Canton of Fribourg (section dedicated to social benefits or social assistance). Fill it out legibly, including: name and address, KVG insurance number, gross income of the previous year, declared assets.

Step 4: Submit the application to the relevant office Send the completed form plus attachments to the Fribourg cantonal office responsible for social benefits. The methods vary: delivery in person at the office (during opening hours), sending by traditional mail (registered mail recommended), or sending by certified email (if the cantonal website allows it).

Step 5: Wait for the official response The canton examines the application and communicates the decision by official letter within the stipulated time frame (usually 2-4 weeks). If eligible, you will receive a communication with the annual amount of the subsidy. If rejected, the letter must state the reason.

Step 6: If refused, appeal You have the right to lodge a written appeal with the Fribourg cantonal appeals authority within the official cantonal deadline (check with the office upon receipt of the rejection letter). The appeal must justify the error, attaching new evidence or clarifications if available.

Important deadlines and contacts

The application for the benefit must usually be submitted by the end of February of the year for which you apply for it (to receive the benefit from January). Changes in deductible or insurer can occur by cancelling the policy by November 30 of each year, with effect from January 1 of the following year. You can submit a new application for the benefit each year if your economic situation changes.

For exact contacts, up-to-date information on the 2026 income criteria and specific Fribourg deadlines, consult the official website of the Canton of Fribourg (social benefits section). Frequently Asked Questions
What are the deductibles available in Freiburg and how do they affect the premium?
In Freiburg, as in all of Switzerland, you have 6 adult deductible options: CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500. A lower deductible (CHF 300) implies a higher annual premium, because the insurer covers more medical costs. A higher deductible (CHF 2500) reduces the annual premium, but increases the expense to the doctor up to that limit. The choice depends on your medical needs, frequency of visits and personal financial situation.
How do I know if I am eligible for the premium reduction subsidy?
You are eligible if the annual LAMal premium exceeds a certain percentage of your gross income (usually between 5% and 8%, according to the official rules of the Canton of Fribourg). Calculate the ratio: annual premium ÷ gross income. If you exceed the threshold, you can apply. The canton also considers the declared heritage. To know the exact threshold of the Canton of Fribourg 2026, consult the official site of the canton, social benefits section.
When and how do I submit the application for the subsidy?
Fill out the official form available on the website of the Canton of Fribourg. Attach: copy of the KVG policy, latest premium slip, tax return from the previous year. Delivery to the competent cantonal office by the end of February to receive the subsidy in the current year. The canton responds within 2-4 weeks. If refused, you have the right to appeal within the cantonal deadline.
Can I change insurer or deductible if the premium goes up?
Yes, by 30 November each year you can cancel the policy (change insurer) or increase your chosen deductible (same insurer). The effect is from the following January 1. There are no exchange penalties. Similarly, you can submit a new grant application every year if your income changes.
What happens if I am refused the subsidy?
If the refusal is motivated by income above the threshold or excessive assets, you can still appeal if you believe that the data has been misrepresented. File a written appeal with the cantonal appeal authority within the official deadline (verification at the office). In the meantime, you can increase your chosen deductible to reduce your premium, or see if you benefit from other social benefits.
