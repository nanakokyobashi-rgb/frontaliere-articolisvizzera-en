Votes 27 September: heavy defeat for neutrality (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 18 September 2026

According to the gfs.bern poll for the SSR, 63% of the electorate plan to reject the initiative on neutrality at the polls on 27 September 2026.

About ten days before the federal votes on September 27, 2026, the neutrality initiative is in a difficult position and heading towards a ballot defeat. According to the second poll by the Swiss Broadcasting Corporation (SSR), conducted by the gfs.bern research institute between September 2 and 9 among a sample of 12,916 voters with a margin of error between +/-2.8 percentage points, 63% of the electorate plans to reject the text launched by Pro Switzerland. Only 34% support the proposal and 3% remain undecided, while the no front has lost nine percentage points in a month.

Operational details

Analysis of voting dynamics and comparison with the food initiative

The federal votes of September 27, 2026 present a complex picture involving multiple items submitted to a popular vote. Alongside the debate on Swiss neutrality, which aims to enshrine a stricter and more restrictive conception in the Constitution, the electorate's attention is also polarized on the initiative asking the Confederation to promote the production and consumption of plant-based foods. This second text has also seen a drastic decrease in support compared to the previous survey conducted by the gfs.bern institute on behalf of SSR.

The text on plant-based food

While in mid-August the supporting and opposing camps were still neck and neck in the public debate, almost two-thirds of the people interviewed, equal to 64%, now intend to vote no ahead of the September 27, 2026 deadline. Only 33% support the plant-based food initiative, while the share of undecided voters stands at 3%. This drastic trend reversal highlights how the information campaign and political debate have eroded initial consensus, shifting the absolute majority of voters toward the rejection of the federal regulatory proposal.

Key points

Participation and Appointments with Federal Ballots

The final phase of the campaign ahead of the federal votes on September 27, 2026, requires eligible citizens to exercise their political rights in compliance with the deadlines established by cantonal and federal authorities. Participation in voting represents the fundamental pillar of Swiss direct democracy. Voters receive the official voting material at home, including ballot papers, explanatory booklets prepared by the Federal Council, and voting recommendations from parties and referendum committees. It is essential to verify the correctness of the documentation and promptly mail the postal vote or go to the polling stations according to the provisions established by your Municipality of residence.

Procedural Aspects of Postal Voting

For those who choose the postal route, which represents the method widely preferred by the population residing in Switzerland, it is necessary to sign the entitlement card and insert the completed ballot papers into the official transmission envelope, strictly respecting the delivery deadlines indicated on the envelope itself to prevent the vote from being declared null and void due to lateness. Municipal administrations coordinate the vote counting at the local level, guaranteeing the transparency and secrecy of the vote cast on all scheduled federal issues, including constitutional debates on neutrality and food promotion proposals.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will the next federal elections be held in Switzerland?
Federal voting will take place on September 27, 2026. The Swiss electorate is called upon to express itself on several federal objects, including the popular initiative on neutrality launched by Pro Switzerland and the text calling on the Confederation to promote the production and consumption of plant-based foods.
What are the percentages of the second SSR neutrality survey?
The second survey of the gfs.bern institute, conducted between 2 and 9 September 2026 on a sample of 12,916 voters and electricians, indicates that 63% of the electorate plans to vote no, while support stops at 34% and the undecided are 3%. Compared to the previous survey, the contrarian front has grown significantly.
What are the voting forecasts for the plant nutrition initiative?
The initiative calling for the promotion of foods of plant origin also registered a sharp decline in support. Almost two thirds of the people interviewed, equal to 64%, intend to vote no on September 27, 2026. Those in favour fell to 33%, while the share of the undecided stood at 3%, overturning the parity situation recorded in mid-August.
