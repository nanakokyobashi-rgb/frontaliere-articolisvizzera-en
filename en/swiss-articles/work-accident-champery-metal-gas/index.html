Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Swiss occupational safety legislation for metal smelting operations?
Swiss legislation requires employers to comply with the safety standards established by the Occupational Safety Act (Lasi). This includes the use of protective equipment, regular checks and emergency procedures. The legislation also applies to the management of industrial gases and fusion operations.
What should employers do to prevent similar accidents?
Verification of equipment, use of personal protective equipment, training of personnel and compliance with safety procedures must be ensured. Regular equipment maintenance and constant gas monitoring are critical to prevent accidental exposures.
What are the emergency procedures in the event of an accident during the merger?
The procedures include the immediate activation of safety exits, the alert of emergency services, the evacuation of the area and assistance to rescuers. It is important to follow the indications of the emergency plan and work with the authorities to clarify the causes of the accident.
