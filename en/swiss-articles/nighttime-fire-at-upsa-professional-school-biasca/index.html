The fire occurred on the first floor of the building that houses SPAI and SAMS. Tonight shortly after 2 o'clock, a fire alarm was triggered at the UPSA Vocational Training Centre in Biasca. Firefighters from the Biasca Cantonal Rescue Center arrived on the scene with four vehicles and the picket group. After a reconnaissance they found that on the first floor, in a room, there were flames, which they quelled in a short time, preventing them from spreading to adjacent rooms. The Professional Centre is located in Via Stradone Vecchio Sud. The Cantonal Police intervened on the spot for the necessary investigations. They don't complain about being injured or intoxicated.

Fire Details

The fire was located in a classroom located on the first floor of the main building of the training centre. The flames damaged part of the furniture and educational equipment, but thanks to the timely intervention of firefighters, the damage was limited. Preliminary estimates indicate that the cost of repairs could exceed CHF 50,000.
