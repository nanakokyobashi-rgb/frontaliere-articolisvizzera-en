Canton Obvaldo, like other Swiss cantons, offers a high-quality nursery school service. Under the 2013 federal law, childcare is a parental right and must be guaranteed throughout Switzerland. The Canton of Obvaldo has adopted a cantonal law that specifies the requirements for the opening of a nursery and for the training of its operators.

According to the cantonal law of 2018, the costs for kindergarten in the Obvaldo Canton are as follows:

  • Day nursery: CHF 1'200.- per month (CHF 14'400.- per year)
  • Night nursery: CHF 1'800.- per month (CHF 21'600.- per year)
