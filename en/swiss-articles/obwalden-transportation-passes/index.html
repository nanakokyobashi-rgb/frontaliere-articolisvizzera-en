Subscription zones

The canton of Obwalden is divided into three zones: A, B and C. Zone A includes the cities of Lucerne and Emmetten, while zone B includes the cities of Stans and Giswil. Zone C includes the cities of Meiringen and Brienz.

Rules and Regulations

The rules and regulations relating to public transport passes in the Canton of Obwalden are established by the Law on Public Transport of 15 October 2015. The law provides that passes are valid for a period of 12 months and that the price of passes is set annually by the Council of State.
