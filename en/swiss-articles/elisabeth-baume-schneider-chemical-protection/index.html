Baume-Schneider asks for protection against chemical submission

Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 10 August 2026

Federal Councilwoman Elisabeth Baume-Schneider is calling for more protection against chemical sexual violence.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Federal Councilwoman Elisabeth Baume-Schneider calls for more protection against chemical sexual violence. - Claims that recent cases have shown an underestimated phenomenon. - It calls for more prevention, information, rigorous investigations and a national action plan.

Federal Councilwoman Elisabeth Baume-Schneider called for more protection against chemical sexual violence. In an interview published today by SonntagsBlick, Baume-Schneider said that recent cases have shown an underestimated phenomenon.

“Until now, the public knew too little about the fact that chemical submission occurs even within the home walls,” Baume-Schneider said.

The federal narcotics law already forbids administering substances to a person to make them helpless and docile, but according to Baume-Schneider there is certainly room for improvement.

"We need more prevention through information and judicial authorities that prosecute crimes rigorously," Baume-Schneider continued.

The interview was an opportunity to return to the case of the former UDC Argovian grand councilor accused of drugging several women - including a minor - and then raping them.

“The two women who had the courage to go public showed great strength,” Baume-Schneider said.
