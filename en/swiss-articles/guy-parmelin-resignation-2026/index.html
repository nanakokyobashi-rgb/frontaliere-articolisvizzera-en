Guy Parmelin announces his resignation from the Federal Council

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 October 2026

The head of the Federal Department of Economic Affairs will leave office on 31 December 2026. Excluding health reasons: 'I feel a certain tiredness'.

The conference in Bern

On 2 October 2026, at 11 a.m., Guy Parmelin convened a press conference in Bern at short notice. The Federal Councillor, currently head of the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, announced his decision to resign from the Federal Council at the end of the year. He will cease his duties on 31 December.

The first clarification concerned health. In front of the media, Parmelin said that his condition had nothing to do with the choice. Back pain had repeatedly fueled speculation in the media about a possible resignation, but the president of the Confederation separated that issue from the political decision.

"I feel a certain tiredness: the sacred fire is no longer as sacred as it was at the beginning."

Parmelin explained that he had thought long and hard during the summer break about what was best for the country. Progressively, he added, retiring at the end of the year seemed to him to be the most appropriate decision. He also said that he had taken enormous pleasure in leading the department and wanted to make room for new forces in times of great uncertainty.

The President of the Confederation reported that he had informed his colleagues that morning, during the weekly meeting of the executive. However, he did not consider his work finished: he said that he was still motivated, that he wanted to face the remaining challenges and that he would work until 31 December at 23:59. He also recalled that there will be a popular vote and that he intends to work to ensure that the voice of the Federal Council is heard by the population.

For the succession, the Jans hypothesis is open. The text indicates, among the challenges of the future responsible, US tariffs, agriculture and housing. A final decision on the name of the successor is not indicated.

Operational details

The significance of the passage for Switzerland

The news opens a passage at the top of Swiss economic policy, but does not yet describe a concrete change for those who live or work in the country. The source communicates the exit of Guy Parmelin, the end of his mandate and the existence of a hypothesis for succession. However, he does not indicate a new measure, a figure or a personal procedure related to the resignation.

The possible change at the helm of the DFE, indicated as EAER in the titles, must therefore be read through the three dossiers mentioned for the successor: US tariffs, agriculture and housing. These are issues explicitly associated with the phase that is opening, not decisions already taken. The source does not provide amounts, a timetable for measures or indications on the line that Jans would follow. Transforming the hypothesis into an announcement of concrete change would therefore anticipate the available information.

The double passage of 2027

For the reader, the most delicate point is to distinguish the calendar from the political forecast. At the beginning of 2027, according to the source, Parmelin and State Secretary Helene Budliger Artieda will no longer hold their posts. Parmelin does not see a problem with the departure of two key figures in Swiss economic policy: he argues that no one is irreplaceable and that those in managerial positions have replacements.

Parties and associations are divided on the future of the Federal Council, but the text does not report the individual positions. The usable datum is therefore the existence of a confrontation, not its outcome or a precise proposal.

The dossier of the manufacturers can be followed, from the reader's point of view, together with the costo della vita in Svizzera; the source text, however, does not contain figures or forecasts on this topic. The same caution applies to the other two dossiers indicated: until the succession is defined, it is not possible to attribute to the future manager decisions that the source does not announce.

How to follow developments without anticipating decisions

Four useful steps

First: set the certain timetable. The announcement was made on October 2, 2026 in Bern, while the exit from the Federal Council is scheduled for December 31. Parmelin said that he will continue to work until 23:59; this is the operational deadline indicated in the text.

Second: separate the decision from the hypothesis. The resignation was announced directly by Parmelin. Jans, on the other hand, appears as a possibility for the leadership of the EAER. When reading the next updates, it is advisable to keep the certain news distinct from the name still hypothesized.

Third: follow the three issues associated with the successor without turning them into measures already approved. US tariffs, agriculture and housing are presented as challenges. The source does not indicate either a measure or a figure linked to these dossiers, so any assessment must start from what will actually be announced.

Fourthly, to keep the succession separate from the popular vote mentioned by Parmelin. The Federal Councillor has said that he intends to work to ensure that the voice of the Federal Council is heard by the population, but the text does not specify the subject of the vote. It is therefore not possible to add details on its content.

The reference to the new forces concerns institutional replacement, not an announcement of hiring. Those who want to keep the two plans distinct can consult the annunci di lavoro, without presenting the Jans hypothesis as an already defined candidacy.

Frequently Asked Questions
When will Federal Councillor Guy Parmelin officially step down from office?
Guy Parmelin announced at a press conference in Bern on 2 October 2026 that he will step down from his duties as head of the Federal Department of Economic Affairs on 31 December 2026. The Federal Councillor has made it clear that he intends to remain in office and continue working until the last minute, i.e. 23:59 on 31 December.
Are the resignations related to health problems?
No, Guy Parmelin explicitly ruled out health as the reason for his resignation. Although back pain had fueled media speculation, the President of the Confederation clarified that his decision stems from personal reflection that took shape during the summer break, describing his state as a 'certain weariness' and the absence of the initial 'sacred fire'.
What will be the main challenges for the successor at the helm of the Department?
Although no official name has yet been determined for the succession, the source indicates that the future head of the department will have to tackle complex and highly topical dossiers. Among the priority challenges mentioned were US tariffs, the agricultural sector, and the issue of housing. The handover will take place in a context in which State Secretary Helene Budliger Artieda will also leave her position at the beginning of 2027.
