Cross-border commuters working in Switzerland and residing in Italy generally receive their salaries in Swiss francs but often need to convert their wages into euros for daily use. Choosing the right time and the most convenient platform for the CHF-EUR exchange is one of the most critical decisions for those crossing the border daily. Nationally, exchange management involves not only traditional banks but also digital platforms offering services tailored to cross-border workers.

When to exchange

Fluctuations in the exchange rate between the Swiss franc and the euro can directly affect the purchasing power of cross-border commuters. A favorable exchange rate can result in a higher real salary, while an unfavorable rate reduces the effective value of the salary once converted. For this reason, it is essential to carefully evaluate the timing of the exchange, monitoring market trends and the differences between the rates offered by various institutions.
