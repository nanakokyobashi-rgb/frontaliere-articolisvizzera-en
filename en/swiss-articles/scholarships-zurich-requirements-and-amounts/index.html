Scholarships in the Canton of Zurich: requirements and amounts

The Canton of Zurich offers scholarships for Swiss students who enroll in recognized courses of study. These scholarships are intended for students with a precarious financial situation and who are residents of the Canton of Zurich.

# Scholarship Requirements

To obtain a scholarship, students must meet some specific requirements. Here are some of the main requirements:

  • Residence in the Canton of Zurich: students must have been resident in the Canton of Zurich for at least one year.
  • Enrollment in a recognized course of study: Students must enroll in a course of study recognized by the Canton of Zurich.
  • Precarious financial situation : Students must demonstrate that they have a precarious financial situation, which can be assessed based on their income, expenses and overall economic situation.
  • Age: Students must be between the ages of 18 and 30.

Key points

To obtain a scholarship in the Canton of Zurich, students must complete a scholarship application and submit it to the relevant office. Students must also provide documents proving their financial situation and residence in the Canton of Zurich. The scholarship application must be submitted within the deadlines established by the competent office.

Scholarship Access Requirements

To access scholarships in the Canton of Zurich, students must meet the following requirements:

  • Be at least 18 years of age and not older than 35
  • Be resident in the Canton of Zurich for at least one year
  • Be students enrolled in a recognized course of study in the Canton of Zurich
  • Have not already received a scholarship in the Canton of Zurich for the same course of study
  • Have no other sources of financial support covering at least 50% of study expenses
