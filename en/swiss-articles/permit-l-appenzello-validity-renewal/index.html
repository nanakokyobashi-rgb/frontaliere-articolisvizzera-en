The L permit in the Swiss system for the admission of foreigners

The short-term L permit is a key tool of the Swiss temporary admission system for foreigners. Managed at the federal level by the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM), the L permit allows foreign nationals to stay and work in Switzerland for a maximum period of one year.

At the cantonal level, the Inner Appenzell Canton operates like the other Swiss cantons: it receives and processes L permit applications according to federal SEM legislation, coordinating with employers who intend to hire temporary foreign labour.

The L permit is distinguished from other Swiss permits by its purely temporary nature. Unlike the B permit (residence), which allows a renewable stay in the medium term, the L permit is designed for limited work needs

Operational details

Extension and transition to residence permit

A frequently asked question concerns whether the L permit can be extended. According to SEM regulations, the L permit does not extend automatically: each extension requires a new evaluation and formal request. The cantonal authorities of Appenzello Interno evaluate each request for an extension on the basis of the updated documentation provided by the employer and the foreigner.

The transition to more stable living situations is an important aspect. Those who hold an L permit and wish to settle longer in Switzerland can consider switching to the B permit (residence). The B permit is a renewable authorization that allows medium-long term stay, and is designed for those who intend to stay in Switzerland beyond the short period covered by the L permit.

The transition from permit L to permit B is not automatic, but requires a new application and evaluation by the authorities. Generally, the SEM evaluates the continuity of employment, the economic situation of the foreigner, and the support of the employer as relevant elements for the granting of the B permit. Key points

What to do: practical procedure and deadlines

Anyone interested in obtaining a permit L to work in the Canton of Inner Appenzell must follow a well-defined procedure at the cantonal level. The first step is to agree with the employer: the company that intends to hire a foreigner has the responsibility to initiate the application with the cantonal authorities of Inner Appenzell.

The employer prepares the required documentation, which typically includes: job offer or letter of appointment, proof of the company's economic situation, and declaration of the foreign candidate. These documents are transmitted to the competent office of the canton, usually the migration or foreigner admission office.

The cantonal authorities check that the application meets the SEM federal requirements and that the labor market situation is favorable. The SEM at the federal level provides guidelines, but the practical evaluation is up to the cantons.

Once the application is approved, a permit L is issued, indicating the specific duration of stay (up to a maximum of 1 year). The permit holder receives the official document with which they can register with the local authority of residence and start working.

For renewal, the employer and the permit holder can submit a request before the deadline. There is no 'automatic' right to renewal: the decision depends on a new evaluation of the working circumstances and the economic situation.

If a transition to permit B is considered, the procedure changes: a new application is required, and the evaluations of the integration parameters (stable employment, accommodation, relationships) become more relevant. Frequently Asked Questions
How long can I stay in Switzerland with an L permit?
The L permit allows a maximum stay of 1 year. The specific duration of your permit is indicated in the official document issued by the cantonal authorities. Not all L permits have the maximum duration: it depends on the evaluation of the SEM and the cantonal authority on the basis of the work documentation submitted by the employer.
Does the L permit automatically extend when it expires?
No. The L permit does not renew automatically. If you wish to stay in Switzerland beyond the expiry date, the employer must submit a new application to the cantonal authorities of Appenzell Internne. This involves a reassessment of work circumstances and approval is not guaranteed.
Can I switch from L Permit to B Permit during my stay?
Yes, it is possible to switch to permit B (dwelling), but not automatically. You must submit a new application before the L permit expires. The authorities assess whether the employment, economic and housing situation justify a longer stay. Permit B is renewable and offers greater protection.
Who handles the L permit application in the Inner Appenzell Canton?
The State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) provides the federal regulatory framework and evaluation criteria. At the cantonal level, it is the migration or foreigner admission office of the Inner Appenzell Canton that processes and approves applications, in coordination with the employer.
What documents does the employer need to apply for an L permit?
Typically: job offer or letter of appointment, economic proof of the company, personal data of the foreign candidate. Exact documents may vary slightly. It is advisable to contact the cantonal migration office of Appenzello Interno directly for the updated and complete list.
