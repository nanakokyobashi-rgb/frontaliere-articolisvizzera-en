In the canton of Vaud, Migros could close the Centrale Traiteur in Écublens, putting 57 jobs at risk, while the group's restructuring continues. No final decision has been taken: the spokeswoman told the AWP agency that a consultation with staff is underway, confirming a news story published by the Vaud newspaper 24 Heures.

A procedure still open

The most relevant fact, on an employment level, is the distinction between hypothesis and decision. The source speaks of a possible closure and positions in the balance; at the same time he specifies that the process has not reached a conclusion. Consultation is therefore the currently described stage for discussing the future of the unit with staff.

Fresh Food & Beverage Group, an entity created in 2023 from the merger of the Bischofzell and Jowa companies, has the Centrale Traiteur among its food factories. FFB is part of Migros Industrie. The matter therefore concerns an identified production structure and an indicated number of uses.

Migros links the hypothesis to the evolution of market and customer expectations and to the strategic development of the product portfolio. According to the press officer, the analysis carried out on Centrale Traiteur showed that growth and profitability expectations did not materialise.

«The growth and profitability prospects expected for Centrale Traiteur have not materialized».

The possible cessation of activity would be added to the decision, taken by the cooperative in 2024, to close in the industrial area of ​​Migros in Écublens Micarna, a company dedicated to the processing of fresh meat. On that occasion, 84 jobs were cancelled. The possible closure of the Centrale Traiteur would therefore represent a further setback for the same area, while the outcome of the consultation still remains to be known. For the moment, therefore, the certain information concerns the opening of the consultation and the number of jobs exposed, not the final result of the dossier.
