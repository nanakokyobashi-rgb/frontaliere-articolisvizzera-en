On June 17, 2026, the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs SECO published the report on the fight against undeclared work in Switzerland for 2025. The report states that inspectors checked 44 083 people working in sectors such as hospitality or construction.

The report also states that the cantonal inspection bodies have progressively intensified their activities as part of the fight against undeclared work. In 2024, they reached a record number of inspections of companies and people since the relevant law came into force (LLN, 2008). In particular, inspectors from the Canton of Zurich conducted 3 200 inspections of hospitality businesses in Zurich and the surrounding area, while those from the Canton of Geneva checked 2 500 people working in sectors such as construction and maintenance in Geneva and the surrounding area.

In 2025, inspectors conducted 14 450 company inspections, slightly fewer than in 2024 (- 0,5 %). However, it is important to note that the number of inspections increased significantly compared with the 10 000 inspections conducted in 2015.

The report also states that the cantonal bodies forwarded a total of almost 14 150 suspected cases to the competent authorities for further investigation. In particular, the Canton of Ticino forwarded 4 500 suspected cases to the competent authorities, while the Canton of Valais forwarded 2 800 suspected cases.

The report concludes that the bodies responsible for combating undeclared work are effective in tackling this phenomenon and consequently contribute to the prosperity and fairness of the Swiss economy and labor market.
