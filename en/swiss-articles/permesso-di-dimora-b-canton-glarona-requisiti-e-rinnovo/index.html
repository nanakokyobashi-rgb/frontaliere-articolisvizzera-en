Residence permit B is a type of residence permit that allows foreign citizens to stay in the Canton of Glarus for a fixed period of time. This type of permit is regulated by the Italian-Swiss Double Taxation Convention, which establishes the requirements and documents required to obtain the permit.

Requirements to obtain residence permit B

To obtain residence permit B, foreign nationals must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a citizen of a country that has a double taxation agreement with Switzerland
  • Having a stable and regular source of income
  • Have a permanent home in the Canton of Glarus
  • Not be subject to expulsion or deportation procedures

Required documents

Foreign nationals wishing to obtain residence permit B must submit the following documents:

  • An application for a residence permit
  • A valid passport
  • A marriage certificate (if applicable)
  • A child's birth certificate (if applicable)
  • A certificate of employment or salary
  • A certificate of ownership of a residence in the Canton of Glarus
  • A certificate of registration

To apply for residence permit B, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a citizen of an EU country or a country with which Switzerland has a free movement of workers agreement;
  • Have sufficient income to support themselves economically in the Canton of Glarus;
  • Having a safe and adequate accommodation;
  • Have health insurance coverage;
  • Have no history of expulsion from Switzerland or conviction for a criminal offence.
