Who are the border carpenters in Ticino?

Border carpenters in Ticino are employees who carry out carpentry activities in Switzerland and who originate from a Member State of the European Union (EU) or a State party to the Agreement on the European Economic Area (EEA).

Requirements for border carpenters in Ticino

In order to work as a carpenter in Ticino, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Have a baccalaureate degree or a certificate of professional qualification issued by a vocational training institution
  • Have at least 3 years of work experience as a carpenter
  • Be able to speak at least one official language of Switzerland (German, French, Italian or Romance)
  • Have no criminal record or convictions for serious crimes
  • Sign an employment contract with an authorized employer

Recognition of diplomas and qualifications

Diplomas and qualifications awarded by a vocational training institution in an EU or EEA country are recognised in Switzerland, provided they have been awarded under regulations that guarantee a level of quality equivalent to that of Switzerland.
