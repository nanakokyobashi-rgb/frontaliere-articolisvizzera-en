Canton of Schwyz: schooling and enrolment (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 4 September 2026

Compulsory schooling in the canton of Schwyz begins at the age of 6. Learn about cycles, enrollment, calendar, and high school transition.

Context

In brief

  • Compulsory schooling in Schwyz covers 9 years, from 6 to 15 years of age
  • Three main cycles: kindergarten, primary (6 years), secondary (3 years)
  • Enrollment in primary school at the age of 6 in the municipalities
  • German is the main language of instruction

Key facts

  • What: Compulsory school system in cycles in the canton of Schwyz
  • When: From 6 to 15 years of age
  • Where: Canton of Schwyz, Central Switzerland
  • Who: Children residing in the canton
  • Duration: 9 years of compulsory schooling
  • Competence: Cantonal autonomy with federal coordination

The canton of Schwyz, located in the heart of Central Switzerland, regulates its own school system according to federal coordination provisions. Compulsory schooling has a total duration of 9 years, starting at the age of 6 and ending at the age of 15. The system is structured in three distinct cycles: kindergarten (generally 2 years, optional in many municipalities), primary school (6 years, from 6 to 12 years), and lower secondary school, known as secondary school (3 years, from 12 to 15 years).

Operational details

School calendar and organization of the year

The school calendar in the canton of Schwyz extends for about 38-39 weeks of actual lessons during the year. The school year generally starts in mid-August and ends in mid-June. The summer holidays last about 6 weeks and fall in the months of July and August. In addition to the summer holidays, the canton provides autumn holidays (generally 2 weeks in October), Christmas holidays (about 2 weeks between December and January), a week of winter holidays in February, and a week of spring holidays in April. The exact calendar varies slightly among the different municipalities of the canton, as each municipality has some autonomy in organizing the holiday dates.

Teaching languages and linguistic skills

The main teaching language in the canton of Schwyz is German, both in the standard Swiss form and in the local dialect. Swiss German is taught as a language of daily communication and as a school subject. Starting from primary school, other languages are introduced: Italian is generally taught as the first foreign language (although this is not mandatory at the federal level), while English becomes a curricular subject starting from the fourth grade of primary school and continues throughout the middle school cycle. This multilingual approach reflects the Swiss educational system's desire to prepare young people for communication in a multilingual and globalized society.

Key points

Enrollment Procedure for Schools

Enrollment in primary school is done at the municipal administration of the place of residence, usually by March 31 of the year before the start of school. Parents must fill out an enrollment form provided by the municipality and submit it within the set deadlines. Standard documents required for enrollment include: the child's birth certificate, proof of residence, and vaccinations (according to the Swiss federal vaccination schedule, although with some cantonal exceptions). In the canton of Schwyz, as in the rest of Switzerland, vaccination against measles is widely recommended, while other vaccinations follow the guidelines of the Federal Office of Public Health.

At the time of enrollment, parents receive information about the school's organization, costs (compulsory schooling is free in Switzerland, while school materials and excursions represent moderate additional costs), and payment methods. Some schools in the canton conduct informative meetings with parents before the start of classes.

Transition to Secondary Schools and Beyond

At the end of middle school (around 15 years old), students have several options to continue their education. The main choice is between vocational training (dual apprenticeship) and secondary school (gymnasium or cantonal polytechnic school). The transition occurs during the third year of middle school, when teachers and parents support the student in choosing based on abilities and interests. In the canton of Schwyz, vocational training is particularly developed, with many companies offering apprenticeship positions. Secondary schools mainly lead to the federal maturity certificate, which allows access to universities.

Frequently Asked Questions
At what age does compulsory schooling begin in the canton of Swabia?
Compulsory schooling in canton Svitto begins at the age of 6. Although kindergarten (2 years, 4 to 6 years) is generally optional, many municipalities offer it for free. The real school obligation begins with primary school at age 6 and continues until age 15.
How is the school system structured in the canton of Schwyz?
The Svittese school system is structured in three cycles: kindergarten (2 years, optional), primary school (6 years, compulsory, from 6 to 12 years), and middle or secondary school (3 years, compulsory, from 12 to 15 years). After middle school, pupils can choose between vocational training and high school.
What is the procedure for enrolling a child in primary school?
Registration takes place at the municipal administration of the municipality of residence, generally by 31 March of the year before the start of school. Parents fill out a form provided by the municipality and submit documentation including birth certificate, proof of residence and vaccination record. Compulsory schooling is free.
What is the main language of instruction in Svitto?
The main language of instruction in the canton of Swabia is German, in the local and standard Swiss form. Italian and English are taught as foreign languages. English becomes a curricular subject from the fourth grade of primary school and continues throughout middle school.
When does the school year in Svitto start and end?
The school year in canton Svitto generally starts in mid-August and ends in mid-June, covering about 38-39 weeks of actual tuition. Summer holidays last about 6 weeks (July-August), while other breaks include autumn, Christmas, winter and spring holidays, distributed throughout the year.
