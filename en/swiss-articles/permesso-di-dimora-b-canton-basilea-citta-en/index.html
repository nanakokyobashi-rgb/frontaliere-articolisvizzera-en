Residence permit B in Basel City: requirements and procedure

The residence permit B in the Canton of Basel-City is a necessary document for foreign citizens residing in the canton. The cantonal law establishes the requirements and documents necessary to obtain this permit. The cantonal migration office is responsible for the renewal procedure.

Requirements for obtaining residence permit B

To obtain residence permit B, foreign nationals must meet the following requirements:

  • Permanent residence : the applicant must prove that he/she has a permanent residence in the Canton of Basel-City. - Income: The applicant must have sufficient income to support themselves and their family. - Health insurance: the applicant must have a health insurance valid in Switzerland. - Knowledge of the German language: the applicant must demonstrate a good knowledge of the German language.

Required documents

To obtain residence permit B, foreign nationals must present the following documents:

  • Passport: a valid passport with a validity period of at least 6 months. - Certificate of Residence: a certificate of residence of the Canton of Basel-City. - Income statement: a document proving the applicant's income. - Health insurance: a document proving health insurance valid in Switzerland. - German language certificate:

# Residence Permit Requirements B

To obtain residence permit B, you must:

  • Have a residence in the Canton of Basel-City
  • Do not have an annual income greater than CHF 3,500.-
  • Not having an economic activity in the Canton of Basel-City
  • Not be a danger to public order or national security
  • Not having been convicted of a criminal offence
