The tax declaration in Switzerland represents a fundamental fulfillment for all resident taxpayers and for those who have income or assets in the Swiss territory. At the federal and cantonal level, the tax authorities establish the rules and rates applicable to employment income, assets and different forms of social security. For workers subject to tax at source, the withholding takes place directly at source by the employer, although in certain income brackets or in the presence of particular requirements, the completion of an additional or corrective ordinary declaration is required. Operational details

The practical analysis of the tax return in Switzerland requires careful planning of the deadlines and the collection of the supporting documents necessary to access the allowed tax deductions. The main items affecting the final calculation include premiums paid for LAMal health insurance, mandatory contributions to the AVS/AI/IPG social security pillars and the second LPP pillar, as well as documented professional expenses such as transport costs and off-site meals. For those who live in the border reality, the management of double taxation between Italy and Switzerland provides for specific mechanisms, such as the tax credit in the EC framework of the Italian model 730, aimed at preventing the same income from being fully taxed in both countries, considering that the income tax at source is withheld in Switzerland.

Deadlines and methods of forwarding by canton

Each Swiss canton independently defines the deadline for filing a tax return, which usually falls in the first months of the year following the reference tax period. It is possible to request a reasoned extension directly from the competent cantonal offices before the ordinary deadline. Many cantons provide dedicated software or secure web portals for the compilation and electronic submission of data, reducing the margins of error and speeding up the processing of final taxation. Key points

Approaching the tax reporting process in Switzerland correctly means following a structured path based on well-defined steps. First of all, it is necessary to collect all the salary certificates issued by the employers, the certificates relating to the payments for the LPP occupational pension and for the third pillar, as well as the receipts for medical expenses not covered by the LAMal deductible and the documents certifying the movable and immovable assets. Once the documentation has been gathered, access the electronic portal of the reference canton or fill in the official paper form. It is essential to verify the correctness of each figure entered, comparing it with the data reported on the official tax documents issued by the competent bodies. In case of doubts about the correct application of the rules or the management of special deductions, it is advisable to consult the official guides provided by the Federal Tax Administration or contact the cantonal tax offices directly for targeted support. Frequently Asked Questions
Who are the competent authorities for tax management in Switzerland?
The management of taxes is entrusted to the Federal Administration of AFC/ESTV Contributions at the federal level and to the individual cantonal administrations of contributions with regard to cantonal and municipal taxes.
How do you avoid double taxation between Italy and Switzerland for border workers?
Italy avoids double taxation by applying the tax credit mechanism through the EC framework of model 730, taking into account the withholding tax withheld in Switzerland on labour income.
What main deductions can be claimed in the return?
You can claim deductions for contributions to the AVS/AI/IPG and LPP social security pillars, for LAMal health insurance premiums and for duly documented professional expenses.
