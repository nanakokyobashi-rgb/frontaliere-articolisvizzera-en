Operational details

The Villa Negroni Study Centre Foundation is associated with SUPSI

The collaboration between the Villa Negroni Study Centre (CSVN) and the Scuola Universitaria Professionale della Svizzera Italiana (SUPSI) has already been consolidated over the years and is now strengthened by the association agreement. This agreement provides for the mutual involvement of teachers and collaborators, the joint design of new training offers and the organisation of common events.

The main objective of this agreement is to offer local professionals and companies extensive, coordinated training that meets the needs of the market. This means that participants will have access to high-quality courses and workshops, designed to meet the specific needs of the Swiss market.

SUPSI is one of the most important universities in southern Switzerland and offers a wide range of training and specialisation courses. The CSVN, on the other hand, is a foundation that deals with promoting culture and professional training in the Canton of Ticino.

Thanks to this agreement, professionals and companies in the Canton of Ticino will be able to access high-quality courses and workshops, designed to meet the specific needs of the local market. In particular, SUPSI students will benefit from training and specialization opportunities at the CSVN, while CSVN collaborators will have access to high-quality courses and workshops at SUPSI.

Key points

The Villa Negroni study centre foundation is associated with SUPSI

The management of the partnership between the Fondazione Centro Studi Villa Negroni (CSVN) and the Scuola Universitaria Professionale della Svizzera Italiana (SUPSI) will be entrusted to the Directorate of the CSVN and the Directorate of Research, Continuing Education, Development and Knowledge Transfer of SUPSI (RFCST). Alberto Piatti, RFCsT SUPSI Director, said: "The association of the CSVN with SUPSI represents an important step in the development of the training system of our Canton, which allows two already well-established and complementary entities to collaborate in a synergistic way to promote the attractiveness and competitiveness of the Canton of Ticino in the sectors of reference".

The partnership is part of the Action Plan for Vocational Training of the Canton of Ticino, approved in 2020 with Law 12 of 2020, which provides for the investment of 10 million francs for the next five years for vocational training. CSVN and SUPSI have already collaborated in the past on research and training projects, including the “Innovation and Sustainability” project funded by the Helvetas Foundation in 2019 for CHF 250,000.
