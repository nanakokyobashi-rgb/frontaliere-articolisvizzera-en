Energy bills down 4% in 2027 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 8 September 2026

ElCom announces the third consecutive year of decline. The average household will save CHF 53 per year, paying CHF 1,194 for energy.

Context

In Brief

  • Electricity prices fall by 4% in 2027: average bill drops to 1,194 CHF annually
  • Savings of 53 francs for an average family consuming 4,500 kWh
  • Third consecutive year of decline following sharp increases in 2022–2024

Key Facts

  • What: Electricity price decline in Switzerland for 2027
  • When: 2027 (4% reduction compared to 2026)
  • Who: Federal Electricity Commission (ElCom)
  • Where: All of Switzerland (with variation between providers)
  • Savings amount: 53 CHF annually per average family
  • Average bill 2027: 1,194 CHF
  • Cause: Reduction in energy procurement costs
  • Trend: Third consecutive year of decline

The Federal Electricity Commission (ElCom) announced on Tuesday a new decline in electricity prices for 2027. Rates will fall by 4% compared to the current year, confirming a downward trend that has now been ongoing for three years.

An average Swiss family consuming 4,500 kilowatt-hours (kWh) per year will pay 53 francs less than in 2026. The annual bill will therefore be around 1,194 francs. The figure, officially released by ElCom, represents a relief after years of tariff pressure that affected household budgets between 2022 and 2024.

What caused the decline

The decline is primarily attributable to the reduction in energy procurement costs. However, ElCom emphasizes a crucial point: these are median values. Prices, in fact, vary considerably from one provider to another due to significant differences in supply, the energy source used, and network distribution costs.

The context of the energy crisis

Until 2024, Switzerland faced an unprecedented energy crisis. Electricity prices skyrocketed during 2022–2023, creating shockwaves in household budgets. Since 2025, the trend has reversed: each year shows a consistent decline. This 2027 continues in that direction.

Operational details

Energy and household budget: practical implications

The 4% decline is not an abstract figure. For an average Swiss family, 53 francs in annual savings represents approximately 4-5 francs per month on an already stabilized bill. In terms of purchasing power, it is sufficient to cover other recurring expenses in the household budget or to add to emergency funds.

This relief comes at just the right time. The energy crisis of 2022–2023 had produced a double negative effect: substantial price increases and, in parallel, a reduction in economic confidence among Swiss consumers, who in many cases had to redirect resources toward heating and electricity. Three years of consecutive decline (2025, 2026, 2027) signal a return to tariff stability after the peak of volatility.

Geographic variability and local providers

However, ElCom's data conceals an underestimated complexity. Prices vary considerably at the local level. A family living in the canton of Valais, where hydroelectricity is abundant, will pay significantly less than one in the canton of Geneva. Even within the same canton, the difference between local providers can be considerable. The 4% decline is a Swiss average: some customers will receive larger reductions, others smaller ones.

ElCom itself admits that prices sometimes vary considerably from one provider to another due to large differences in supply. This means that the annual benefit could range between 30 and 70 francs, rather than the 53 average.

Key points

How to Monitor and Leverage the Tariff Reduction

The 4% reduction is not automatic. It depends on your local energy provider and the type of contract you have signed. Here is the procedure to ensure that the benefit applies to your bill.

Verify Your Rate with Your Provider

The first step is to contact your regional network operator — the one shown on your bill. Provide them with your customer number (found in the document) and ask these questions:

  • Does the 4% reduction announced by ElCom apply to my contract starting in 2027?
  • When does it come into effect exactly (usually starting from January 1st, but implementation varies)?
  • Are you on a fixed-price or variable-price contract?

If you have a fixed-price contract, the reduction may not apply until the contract expires. If you are on a variable-rate plan, the update typically occurs automatically at the beginning of the calendar year.

Compare Providers (where possible in Switzerland)

Not all cantons allow switching energy providers. However, in those that permit choice, the 4% reduction may vary considerably among alternative providers. Check if your region offers energy market liberalization:

  • Check the website of the main provider in your area for information on switching possibilities
  • If choice is available, compare 2027 rates from at least two providers before deciding
  • Remember that switching has minimal administrative costs but requires a few days of processing

Frequently Asked Questions
What exactly is the energy savings for my family in 2027?
ElCom reports that an average household consuming 4,500 kWh per year will pay CHF 53 less than in 2026, for an annual bill of CHF1,194. However, prices vary considerably from operator to operator due to large differences in supply and network costs. If you consume 3,000 kWh, the savings will be about CHF 35; if you consume 6,000 kWh, about CHF 70. Contact your local operator for a personalised estimate based on your historical consumption.
Why do prices fall when the wholesale market is rising?
ElCom explains that the tariff drop in 2027 is based on the energy purchases already structured for thatyear. Swiss operators buy energy well in advance (months or years in advance), so the current increase in the wholesale market has virtually no impact on 2027 purchases. The repercussions of the current tensions in the international market will only be visible in the coming years, so 2028 onwards could present tariff volatility.
When exactly does the 4% drop go into effect?
ElCom announced the decline for 2027. As a rule, the new prices come into effect from 1 January. However, the exact methods and dates vary locally. Please contact your local billing provider to confirm the exact date of application on your bill and how to bill.
If I don't live in a big city, does the decline still apply to me?
Yes, the decline affects the whole of Switzerland, including rural areas. However, ElCom points out that these are median values and that prices vary significantly from one operator to another. Your savings could be less than or greater than the average CHF 53, depending on the energy source and distribution costs in your region. Contact your local manager for the exact impact.
Does this drop also apply to natural gas and other energy services?
ElCom's official announcement focuses exclusively on electricity. The source does not specify impacts on natural gas, district heating or other energy services. Check directly with your local provider which services are affected by tariff reductions in 2027.
