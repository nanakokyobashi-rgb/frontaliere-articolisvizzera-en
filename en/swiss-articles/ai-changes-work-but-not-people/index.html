Key points

Artificial intelligence (AI) technology is revolutionizing the workplace, but it's not replacing people. This is the message of Denis Machuel, CEO of Adecco, one of the leading employment services companies in Switzerland. The company is aiming for ambitious milestones, with the goal that by the end of the year more than 50% of its revenue will be covered by AI.

“Our strategy is to use AI to increase efficiency and productivity, but not to replace people,” Machuel explains. “The winners in our industry will not be those who use AI the most, but those who use it most effectively to create measurable value.”

Switzerland is a leading country in AI technology and Adecco is one of the companies that takes most advantage of this trend. The company has already implemented AI in several areas, including human resource management and the provision of work services.

For example, Adecco uses AI to analyze candidate data and select the most suitable people for a given role. This process, called “matching,” has already been implemented in several Swiss cities, including Zurich and Geneva.

AI is also changing the way Adecco provides its services. The company is using AI to build apps that help candidates find jobs and to provide help for companies to manage their human resources.
