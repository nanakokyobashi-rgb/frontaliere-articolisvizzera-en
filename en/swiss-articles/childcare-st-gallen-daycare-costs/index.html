In Switzerland, extra-familial childcare is regulated at the cantonal level, albeit within the framework of the Federal Law on Financial Aid for Family Supplementary Childcare (LAF), which since 2023 supports the Cantons that increase subsidies to reduce costs borne by parents. The Canton of St. Gallen is among those that have progressively expanded the offer of nurseries, day families and para-school facilities to meet the demand of working families.

Operational details

# How kindergarten fees are determined

One of the hallmarks of the Swiss system is the link between kindergarten fees and family income. In general, the facilities apply a fee calculated on a scale that takes into account the taxable income of the parents and the number of children received, as well as the employment rate of the place. This model, adopted in many Cantons, aims to make custody accessible also to families with medium-low incomes, containing the expense borne by the nucleus.

When the Canton or Municipality intervenes with subsidies, the fee actually paid by the family may be significantly reduced. The criteria for access to benefits — income threshold, core components, parental work activity — are defined at the local level. For this reason, the same structure may apply very different rates depending on the economic situation of the family and the public support received.

Daytime and after-school families: operational differences

Day families represent an alternative to traditional daycare, with often lower costs and greater flexibility of hours. Their operation is based on cantonal accreditations and on direct agreements between the daytime family and the parents, with hourly or daily rates. Also in this case, public subsidies, when provided for, can reduce spending.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure for families

Families interested in a place in a nursery or an after-school facility in the Canton of St. Gallen can follow a process divided into several stages.

The first step is to survey the available facilities in the municipality of residence, consulting the official websites and the lists published by the cantonal services. Many municipalities publish an updated list of accredited nurseries, day families and after-school programmes, with indications on hours, age groups and indicative costs.

The second step is the admission application, which must be submitted directly to the chosen facility. It is advisable to start the search well in advance of the desired start date, because available places, especially in urban areas, may require waiting lists of several months.

The third step concerns the verification of subsidies. Families who believe they are entitled to a public contribution for childcare must contact the relevant services to obtain information on requirements, necessary documentation and payment times. The subsidy application, as a rule, must be submitted before the start of childcare or in any case within the deadlines set by the Canton or the municipality.

Useful documents and information

For the subsidy application, the following are generally required: proof of taxable income (recent tax assessment), composition of the household, confirmation of the place in the facility, proof of the parents' employment. Timelines and forms vary depending on the municipality.

Frequently Asked Questions
How does childcare work in the Canton of St. Gallen?
Extra-family custody is organized by the Canton and the Municipalities, within the framework of the Federal Law on Financial Aid for the Supplementary Custody of Children (LAF), active since 2023. The offer is divided into kindergartens for pre-school age, accredited day families and after-school families for school age. Rates and subsidies are defined locally.
How are kindergarten fees calculated in St. Gallen?
The facilities apply a fee calculated on a scale based on the taxable income of the parents, the number of children welcomed and the employment rate of the place. This model aims to make custody accessible also to families with medium-low incomes, containing the expense borne by the family unit.
What grants are available for custody in St. Gallen?
When the Canton or Municipality intervenes with subsidies, the fee actually paid by the family may be significantly reduced. The access criteria — income threshold, components of the nucleus, work activity of the parents — are defined at the local level, so they vary from Municipality to Municipality.
What is the difference between daytime and after-school families?
Day families offer accommodation in accredited households, with often lower costs and greater flexibility of hours. After-school classes, intended for school-age children, apply monthly flat rates including meals; in some municipalities, attendance is offered at symbolic or free costs for the lowest income groups.
What steps does a family need to take to enroll a child?
It is necessary to register the accredited structures in the Municipality of residence, submit an application for admission to the chosen structure well in advance due to the waiting lists in urban areas, and verify any entitlement to subsidies at the competent services before the start of custody.
