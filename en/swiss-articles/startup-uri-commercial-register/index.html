The start of an economic activity in the Canton of Uri requires the passing of several administrative and legal phases, starting from the definition of the corporate structure. The choice of legal form is the first decisive step, as it directly influences the minimum capital required, the legal liability of the entrepreneur and the applicable tax regime. Each type of company must comply with federal and cantonal regulations to ensure operational compliance.

Registration in the trade register

Registration in the commercial register represents the formal act that confers legal existence on the company. This procedure is not only a bureaucratic fulfillment, but serves to make public the essential data of the company, such as the headquarters, the corporate purpose and the signature powers of the managers. Registration costs vary depending on the legal form chosen and the specific fees applied by the competent office in the Canton of Uri.

Operational details

The analysis of the initial financial structure is crucial for those deciding to invest in the Canton of Uri. The minimum capital is not a single value, but depends strictly on the legal form adopted. For example, capital companies require a minimum payment that must be deposited in a blocked account before the final registration in the commercial register.

Taxation and Social Contributions

Anyone starting a business must consider that the Swiss tax system operates on three levels: the direct federal tax (DFT), the cantonal tax, and the municipal tax. In the Canton of Uri, as in every other canton, the municipal tax is calculated using a multiplier applied to the cantonal tax. Regarding VAT, jurisdiction lies with the Federal Tax Administration (FTA/ESTV).

On the social security front, the entrepreneur must manage social contributions. If the business involves hiring employees, payroll costs include AVS/AI/IPG (10.6% total, of which 5.3% is borne by the employee), unemployment insurance (UI/AC) at 1.1% up to the annual ceiling, and accident insurance (LAA/LAINF) with rates between 0.7% and 1.5% depending on the sector. For occupational pension (LPP), contributions vary based on the worker's age: 7% for ages 25-34, 10% for 35-44, 15% for 45-54, and 18% for those over 55.

Key points

To make the activity operational in the Canton of Uri, it is necessary to follow a strict procedural path that avoids administrative delays. The first phase consists of drafting the statutes and signing the deed of incorporation before a notary, if the legal form requires it. ### Operational steps for launch The path is generally divided into the following steps: - Definition of the legal form and payment of the minimum capital into a blocked account. - Drafting of the statutes and filing of the application for registration in the commercial register. - Registration with the compensation fund for AVS/AI/IPG for social insurance. - Conclusion of the mandatory insurances, including LAINF for accidents and LAMal for health. - Registration with the AFC/ESTV for VAT, if the turnover exceeds the taxable threshold. ### Personnel and premises management If the entrepreneur decides to rent a commercial premises, they must refer to federal law (CO art. 253 segg.). The security deposit may not exceed three months' rent and must be paid into a blocked account in the tenant's name. In case of termination by the landlord, it is valid only if communicated via the official cantonal form, with the possibility of contestation within 30 days before the conciliation authority. Regarding working hours, federal limits provide a maximum of 45 hours per week for industry, offices and sales, while for other sectors the limit rises to 50 hours. Minimum vacation is set at 4 weeks (5 weeks for those under 20 years old). In case of hiring, it is essential to respect the notice periods: one month in the first year, two months between the second and ninth year, and three months from the tenth year of service. To correctly manage these cost items, it is useful to consult the annunci di lavoro service to compare market conditions or use the calcolatore stipendio to define salaries.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the tax levels for an activity in Canton Uri?
Taxation in Switzerland is structured on three levels: the direct federal tax (DFI), the cantonal tax and the municipal tax. Each canton has its own laws and multipliers; municipalities, in particular, apply a multiplier on cantonal tax to determine the final rate.
What are the AVS/AI/IPG contributions for employees?
The total contribution for AVS/AI/IPG is 10.6%, divided equally between employer and employee (5.3% each). Add to this unemployment insurance (AD/AC) at 1.1% up to the annual ceiling and LAINF for accidents between 0.7% and 1.5%.
How does the security deposit work for the rental of a business premises?
According to federal law (CO art. 253 et seq.), the security deposit for the lease may not exceed three months' rent. This sum must be deposited in a restricted account exclusively in the name of the tenant.
