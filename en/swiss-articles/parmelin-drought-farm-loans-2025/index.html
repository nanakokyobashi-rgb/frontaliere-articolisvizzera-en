Operational details

Fodder: the most immediate challenge for the agricultural sector

In addition to financial aid, the main concern now concerns the supply of fodder for the coming winter. Parmelin pointed to this as the most immediate challenge for the industry, faced at the microphones of RTS. "There is climate change, it's obvious. Twentyyears ago it was not possible to plant Merlot in my region, whereas today it is. In the future, the emergency will concern the supply of fodder for the winter and the end of winter, "said the president of the Confederation.

The situation is made more complex by the fact that drought affects a large part of Europe. "Currently there are imports from Canada, because we must consider that drought is a European problem," Parmelin continued. The logistical difficulties are also aggravated by the low level of important European waterways, a detail that complicates the transport of goods and raw materials by river.

Key points

Cosa fare concretamente per le aziende agricole colpite

Le aziende agricole che hanno subito danni dalla siccità estiva possono accedere ai 54 milioni di franchi stanziati dalla Confederazione sotto forma di prestiti senza interessi. Questi prestiti sono pensati per garantire liquidità immediata, non per coprire perdite strutturali. Le aziende interessate devono rivolgersi ai competenti uffici cantonali dell'agricoltura per avviare la procedura di richiesta.

In parallelo, le aziende possono richiedere l'anticipo dei pagamenti diretti, uno strumento ordinario della politica agricola federale che non richiede nuove basi legali. L'anticipo dei pagamenti diretti è una leva operativa attivabile rapidamente e rappresenta una fonte di liquidità aggiuntiva rispetto ai prestiti senza interessi.

Scenario: cosa succede se la siccità prosegue

Se la siccità dovesse prolungarsi nei prossimi mesi, il problema principale diventerà l'approvvigionamento di foraggio per l'inverno, come indicato dallo stesso Parmelin. In questo scenario, le importazioni dall'estero (come quelle già in corso dal Canada) potrebbero diventare la norma, con un conseguente aumento dei costi logistici, aggravato dal basso livello dei corsi d'acqua europei che complica il trasporto fluviale.

In assenza di una base legale per aiuti a fondo perduto, un eventuale ampliamento dell'intervento federale richiederebbe un passaggio parlamentare o l'attivazione del diritto d'urgenza. Al momento, la fonte non menziona l'avvio di tali procedure.

Frequently Asked Questions
How much has the Confederation allocated for farmers affected by drought?
The Federal Council has allocated CHF 54 million in the form of interest-free loans for farms affected by the 2025 summer drought. It is a tool designed to ensure immediate liquidity, not to cover structural losses, and the companies concerned must contact the competent cantonal agricultural offices to initiate the procedure.
Why does the Confederation not grant non-repayable aid as during Covid?
According to Guy Parmelin, President of the Confederation, today there is no legal basis for providing non-repayable aid. During the Covid-19 pandemic, it was operated in the context of the right of urgency, an instrument that is not active today. In the absence of new legislative intervention or a declaration of extraordinary situation, the Federal Council can only move with ordinary instruments: loans, advances on direct payments and liquidity measures.
What is the most urgent challenge for the agricultural sector according to Parmelin?
The president of the Confederation indicated the most immediate challenge in sourcing fodder for the winter. The drought affects much of Europe and imports of fodder from Canada are already underway. The logistical difficulties are aggravated by the low level of important European waterways, which complicates the river transport of goods and raw materials.
What is the role of direct advance payments?
Parmelin recalled that direct payments can be paid in advance to provide immediate liquidity support to farms. It is an ordinary instrument of federal agricultural policy that does not require new legal bases and can be activated quickly, representing an additional source of liquidity compared to interest-free loans.
What is the situation of the federal accounts with respect to this aid?
The president of the Confederation explained that without the measures adopted, the federal budget would present a deficit of 1.3 billion. It was thanks to the available surplus that the Federal Council was able to unlock the 54 million francs earmarked for farmers, while stressing the absence of further room for manoeuvre.
