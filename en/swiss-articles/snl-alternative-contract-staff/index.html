SNL proposes «alternative contract» to reduce impacts on staff

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 September 2026

SNL forecasts losses of between 350'000 and 400'000 francs following the cantonal decision to no longer commission the Locarno-Magadino service

Context

In brief

  • SNL begins the reorganization following the cantonal decision
  • Expected losses: between 350'000 and 400'000 francs
  • An «alternative contract» has been proposed to staff
  • Signing remains possible even after an initial refusal

Key facts

  • Decision → Canton Ticino no longer commissions the service
  • Route → Locarno-Magadino
  • Expected losses → between 350'000 and 400'000 francs
  • Measures → reorganization and cost containment
  • Staff → alternative contract and redundancies reduced to a minimum
  • Developments → operational and commercial scenarios in the coming weeks

Società Navigazione del Lago di Lugano (SNL) has begun a reorganization following the decision by Canton Ticino to no longer commission the navigation service on the Locarno-Magadino route and not to cover any potential deficits. The company maintains that the decision by the Council of State will result in expected losses of between 350'000 and 400'000 francs.

The reorganization and the alternative contract

According to SNL, the cantonal decision made cost containment necessary. The company says it has managed, «at least at this stage», to limit the consequences for staff: measures that are «less burdensome for staff» have been identified, and redundancies have been reduced to a minimum. The people involved were offered an «alternative contract». The possibility of signing it remains open even for those who had initially chosen not to accept it.

The assessment of the decision remains critical. SNL recalls that it took on the service on Lake Maggiore at the request of the Ticinese and federal authorities, providing public transport services over the years. In its view, the withdrawal of cantonal support unilaterally altered the economic conditions on which the service had been structured.

The matter thus affects the company's operations and the continuity of the service. The stated objective is to maintain an adequate level of service without excessively increasing ticket prices. SNL emphasizes that fares had been kept down because the services had been conceived as public transport and not as a simple commercial tourist offering.

«We chose to do everything within our power to protect jobs and, at the same time, continue to guarantee a service to the region», says director Simone Bianchi.

In the coming weeks, SNL will continue to assess various operational and commercial scenarios. The statement thus leaves the way open both regarding staff and the service on Lake Maggiore.

Operational details

Before and after: where the economic risk shifts

The difference between the previous situation and the one described by SNL concerns how the navigation service is supported. Previously, the company says it had taken on the service on Lake Maggiore at the request of the Ticino and federal authorities; the trips were conceived as public transport and fares had been kept down. After the decision by Canton Ticino, the commission on the Locarno-Magadino route is discontinued and, according to the announcement, so is coverage of any deficits.

The economic figure makes the change in arrangement concrete: SNL expects losses of between 350'000 and 400'000 francs and is responding with a reorganization and cost containment. For staff, this means that the announced measure is linked to the sustainability of the service offering. The company says it chose less onerous solutions and minimized layoffs.

The alternative contract should therefore be viewed as the transition point between the political decision and the organization of work. The communication does not state the salary, working hours, duration or other conditions of the proposal: the announcement does not make it possible to determine an individual impact on income. The only explicit operational detail is that signing remains possible even for those who initially rejected it.

For users, there are two consequences to observe. On the one hand, there is the objective of maintaining an adequate level of service; on the other, there is the intention not to increase ticket prices excessively. The company does not indicate a new fare or a definitive operational solution. SNL instead speaks of various options to be assessed in the coming weeks.

Simone Bianchi's reading focuses on the transfer of risk: according to the director, the political decision places entirely on the company an economic risk previously linked to a public service commissioned by the Canton. For the region, the debate therefore concerns not only the company's accounts, but also what level of service can be maintained without turning the trips into a simple commercial tourist offering.

Key points

Four steps to follow the reorganization

To turn the announcement into a concrete verification, those involved can follow a sequence limited to the information made public by SNL. The point is to distinguish what has already been decided from hypotheses still under evaluation.

1. Read the proposal received. The certain reference is the 'alternative contract' intended for the people involved. The evaluation must start from the document actually proposed; salary, hours, and duration should only be considered if reported there.

2. Keep the choice open. SNL clarifies that the possibility of signing the contract remains open even for those who initially decided not to accept it. This is the practical element to verify before considering one's position closed.

3. Separate work and service. The proposal to the staff concerns the reorganization; the operational and commercial hypotheses, on the other hand, concern the sustainability of the offer on Lake Maggiore. SNL will continue to evaluate them in the coming weeks: this is the indicated time frame.

4. Compare only verifiable data. If the situation requires looking beyond the proposal, the site offers job announcements; the comparison must remain separate from the conditions of the alternative contract.

Even those using navigation have a concrete criterion to follow. The reference is not a rate that has already changed, but the stated goal of maintaining an adequate service level without excessively increasing ticket prices. Monitoring must focus on actual updates, distinguishing operational decisions from commercial intentions and not replacing them with uncommunicated dates or conditions.
