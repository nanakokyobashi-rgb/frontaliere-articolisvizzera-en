Frequently Asked Questions
What documents are required to apply to Lidl?
The presentation of an updated curriculum vitae, a letter of motivation specific to the position and work certificates or diplomas previously obtained are required. The documentation should preferably be in digital format and uploaded to the official portal to ensure proper handling by the HR department.
Do you need to know German to work in Locarno?
Although Italian is the main language for customer service in Locarno, knowledge of German is often considered a preferential or necessary requirement, given the multinational nature of the company and the need to manage communications internally or with the Swiss headquarters.
Where can I check for open positions?
Available positions are published exclusively on the Lidl Switzerland career portal. You can filter searches by location, such as Locarno, to only see opportunities that are active in your region of interest.
