On Friday morning Sunrise announced in a statement that it will cut 358 jobs. The announcement of the layoffs dates back to last month, when a reduction of 450 jobs was expected. The communication therefore updates the number indicated above, but the source does not specify when the cuts will be applied or which locations, departments or professional categories will be affected.

The operational framework and the measures announced

Sunrise speaks of a "profound realignment of the operating model".

As part of this realignment, the company claims to have systematically reviewed both contracts with suppliers and external expenditure items. The statement does not go into the substance of the contracts nor does it associate a single item of expenditure with the figure of 358 redundancies.

In the meantime, the consultation with the Syndicom trade union has ended. For those who will lose their jobs, Sunrise indicates a solid social plan. For some employees, it also indicates the possibility of joining a voluntary exit program. The text does not quantify the recipients of these measures and does not specify conditions or deadlines.
