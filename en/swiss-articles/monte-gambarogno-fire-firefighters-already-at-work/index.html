Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 16 August 2026

A forest fire broke out on Mount Gambarogno. The authorities invite you not to go to the affected area.

Context

The fire on Mount Gambarogno was reported by local authorities this morning. Firefighters are already working to contain the fire and prevent it from spreading into the surrounding area.

The authorities invite you not to go to the affected area and to follow the instructions of the fire brigade. "The situation is under control, but it is important that the local population and tourists move away from the area to avoid any security problems," said the head of civil protection of the Canton of Ticino.

The fire was reported at 10:00 this morning and firefighters arrived on site within 30 minutes. The affected area is located in the Gambarogno region, about 20 km from Locarno. The temperature is 25°C and the wind is light.

The authorities have activated the emergency plan and firefighting teams specialised in extinguishing forest fires have been mobilised. "We are ready to intervene in case of need and prevent the spread of the fire," said the head of civil protection.

The affected area was closed to traffic and tourists were advised not to go to the area. "It is important that the local population and tourists move away from the area to avoid any security problems," the head of civil protection reiterated.

The civil protection of the Canton of Ticino has activated the forest fire alarm system and sent teams of specialized firefighters

Operational details

Local authorities are working to contain the fire that hit Mount Gambarogno, a mountainous area located in the Canton of Ticino in southern Switzerland. Firefighters are already on site and are working to put out the fire.

The fire, which was declared on August 10 at 2:30 pm, has already affected an area of about 50 hectares of forest. Authorities have mobilized more than 100 firefighters to contain the flames and protect nearby homes.

According to estimates, the fire could be caused by lightning striking the mountainous area. The fire has been classified as a “major fire” and authorities are working to contain the blaze and protect people and property.

Local authorities are working to contain the fire using the means at their disposal, including helicopters and aircraft. The helicopter was used to dump water on the fire and protect nearby houses.

Authorities are also working to evacuate people from the area. According to estimates, about 100 people have been evacuated. Authorities are working to find safe housing for evacuees.

The fire also caused problems on nearby roads and highways. The main road through the area was closed due to the fire.

Key points

The local authorities invite you not to go to the affected area, while firefighters are working to contain the fire that has developed on Monte Gambarogno, in the canton of Ticino. According to early estimates, the fire has already affected about 10 hectares of land and it is estimated that it can be completely extinguished within the next 24 hours.

Monte Gambarogno is a mountainous area located north of Lake Maggiore, between the municipalities of Contone and Vira, both in the canton of Ticino. The area is particularly sensitive due to the presence of endemic plant species and its importance for local biodiversity.

Local authorities have already activated the emergency plan to manage the situation and ensure the safety of civilians. Firefighters are working in partnership with firefighters and law enforcement to contain the fire and prevent its expansion.

According to the regulations in force in the canton of Ticino, the local authorities are required to inform the public about the alarm status and the security measures to be taken. Ticino law requires local authorities to inform citizens of the security measures to be taken within 24 hours of the declaration of the state of alarm.

The local population has been informed through official communication channels and rescue teams have been activated to assist people who may be involved in the fire.
