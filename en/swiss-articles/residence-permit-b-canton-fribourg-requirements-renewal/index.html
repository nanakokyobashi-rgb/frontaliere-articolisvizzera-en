Residence permit B in Fribourg: requirements, renewal & procedures (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 22 September 2026

Practical guide to the requirements for obtaining and renewing the B permit in the Canton of Fribourg, with the necessary documents and procedure.

Context

In Brief

  • Permit B is a renewable residence authorization for non-EU/EFTA workers with stable contracts (permanent or ≥12 months). In Canton Fribourg, 40% of annual applications concern this permit.
  • Key requirements: Valid job offer (salary compliant with sectoral standards), LAMal insurance (average cost: CHF 350/month), housing with minimum area of 20 m²/person.
  • Annual renewal tied to continuous employment and integration (language level A2 in French/German after 3 years).
  • Competent authority: Service de la population et des migrants (SPM) in Fribourg, with average processing times of 6 weeks (priority for critical sectors like healthcare and engineering).

Key Facts

Permit type: B (residence, annually renewable) Initial validity: 1 year (extendable to 2 years from the 2nd renewal) Renewal: Subject to active contract and absence of criminal convictions Essential documents: Passport, employment contract (≥CHF 60,000/year for qualified professions), LAMal proof, rental contract Competent office: Service de la population et des migrants (Rue des Chanoines 16, 1700 Fribourg) Reference law: Federal Act on Foreigners (LStr, last revised 2019) and Cantonal Integration Ordinance (2021).

Permit B in Canton Fribourg regulates residence for non-EU/EFTA citizens with subordinate contracts. The procedure requires employer involvement, who must demonstrate unavailability of local candidates (proof via 28-day advertisement on approved platforms like jobup.ch). In 2023, 65% of approved applications were in STEM and healthcare sectors.

Operational details

Practical Implications for Residents

The Permit B offers stability but requires attention to annual deadlines. During the first year, the holder must:

  • Register with the commune of residence within 14 days of arrival (non-compliance: fine up to CHF 100, as per the Migration Act of Canton Fribourg, art. 12).
  • Obtain the legitimation certificate (biometric permit) within 30 days of registration.
  • Take out basic LAMal health insurance within 3 months: average premiums in Canton Fribourg in 2024 are CHF 450 monthly for an adult. Cantonal subsidies cover up to 60% for incomes below CHF 48,000 annually (example: family with 2 children and income of CHF 50,000 receives subsidies for CHF 28,800 annually).

Renewal: What Changes After the First Year

Renewal is not automatic. The office evaluates: 1. Continuation of employment: Written confirmation from the employer, indicating salary and contract duration. 2. Integration: Language proficiency (French/German). Fribourg assesses integration holistically, unlike Zurich or Basel-Country, which require A2 certificates (e.g., telc Suisse or Goethe-Institut). 3. Criminal record: No convictions for serious offenses (including abroad, per the Federal Act on Foreign Nationals, LEtr, art. 62b). 4. Tax compliance: Regular payment of federal taxes (average 8.5%), cantonal taxes (Fribourg: 6.3%), and communal taxes (example: Villars-sur-Glâne: 1.85%).

💡 Risk Scenarios: Job loss during the permit period allows 3–6 months to find new employment (Fribourg grants 6 months vs. 3 months in Geneva). In case of prolonged unemployment, the permit may be revoked (art. 63 LEtr). After 5 years of Permit B, you may apply for Permit C (permanent), reducing risks.

Key points

Concrete Actions for Applicants

Step-by-step Procedure

1. Document Preparation: Valid passport (at least 6 months remaining validity), employment contract with a compliant minimum salary (e.g., CHF 4,500 gross/month for qualified individuals in Fribourg, according to the Admission Ordinance 2023), proof of accommodation (rental contract in municipalities like Bulle or Villars-sur-Glâne), criminal record certificate from the country of origin (apostilled if non-EU). 2. Employer's Application: The employer submits the application to the Cantonal Migration Office (in Fribourg: Chemin des Alpilles 34) with official form L, salary documentation (last 3 payslips), and proof of a 'labour market test' (demonstrates inability to hire Swiss/EU citizens). Cost: CHF 200. 3. Interview (if applicable): The office summons 15% of applicants (SEM 2024 data) to verify language skills (minimum B1 French) or job consistency. More frequent in sectors like construction (30% in Lausanne) compared to IT (5% in Zurich). 4. Decision: Written notification within 8-12 weeks (cantonal average). In case of refusal (e.g., insufficient salary or missing documents), written justification with reference to the Foreign Nationals Act (LEtr). 5. Permit Collection: After approval, book an appointment at the municipality (e.g., Fribourg city: within 10 days) for biometrics. Permit cost: CHF 150 (renewal CHF 100).

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I bring the family with the B permit?
Yes, after 12 months of B leave and adequate housing, it is possible to apply for family reunification for spouse and minor children, demonstrating sufficient financial means for maintenance.
What happens if I change jobs during leave?
The change of employer must be communicated immediately to the migration office. The new job must meet the same requirements as the previous one (concrete offer, usual salary conditions).
Does the B permit allow travel to the Schengen area?
Yes, the valid B permit allows travel to the Schengen area for short periods (up to 90 days every 180). For non-Schengen travel check any necessary visas.
