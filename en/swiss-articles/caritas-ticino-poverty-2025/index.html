LUGANO - Caritas Ticino has strengthened its commitment against various forms of poverty, focusing on proximity and relationships as central tools. This emerged from the organization's Annual Assembly, founded in 1942, held last June 16th at the Laudato si’ Center in S. Antonino. The meeting reviewed activities, financial statements, and statutory structure, also offering a reflection on an increasingly complex context.

The most immediate data concerns the volume of work carried out. In 2025, 1,047 people were welcomed into socio-professional integration measures, with a specific composition: 670 unemployed individuals and 377 people in need of assistance. The Social Services opened 358 new cases, a slight increase compared to the 345 of 2024, related to often complex issues. Concurrently, the number of people seeking spaces for meeting and interaction is growing, indicating an increasingly widespread loneliness.

Operational details

Analysis of social and fiscal implications

The strengthening of Caritas Ticino's support network is not merely a matter of social news, but directly affects labor market dynamics and the Swiss social security system. The figure of 670 unemployed women admitted to socio-professional integration measures highlights how active support is crucial for those facing difficult career transitions. These measures, often linked to LPP/BVG regulations, represent a bridge between unemployment and reintegration into the workforce, a central aspect for both cantonal and national economic stability.

The organization's financial structure offers insights into the sustainability of social policies. With a deficit reduced to 255,682 francs and a balanced budget projected for 2026, Caritas demonstrates management capabilities that are becoming increasingly relevant in a context of rising living costs. Dependence on public mandates (40% of revenue) and own activities (53%) indicates a hybrid model that requires a precise balance between state resources and managerial autonomy.

President Roby Noris emphasized the importance of a 'non-ideological' approach. In an era where social tensions are increasingly felt, physical presence and proximity-based relationships become fundamental tools. The fact that the Social Service opened 358 new files, compared to 345 the previous year, suggests an increase in the complexity of social issues that institutions must address. This trend requires constant monitoring by cantonal and federal authorities to ensure that welfare systems remain effective.

Key points

How to act and support initiatives

For those living in Switzerland who want to contribute or better understand the situation, there are concrete actions to take. If you are experiencing economic difficulties or social isolation, socio-professional integration measures and social services represent accessible resources. The source emphasizes the importance of 'meeting and relationship spaces' to combat loneliness, an often overlooked but crucial aspect for psychological well-being.

From a practical standpoint, participating in circular economy activities, such as donating clothing or household appliances, not only supports Caritas' work but also reduces environmental impact. Data shows that large quantities of materials can be reintroduced into the used goods circuit, a sustainable model that aligns with national green economy trends.

For those interested in monitoring their financial situation or evaluating the implications of unemployment, using calculation tools is helpful. Verifying one's income and comparing it to living standards allows for a better understanding of poverty risks and job opportunities. It is essential to be informed about available rights and resources, both at the federal and cantonal levels, to successfully navigate the challenges of the current job market.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many people did Caritas Ticino welcome in 2025?
In 2025, 1,047 people were welcomed into socio-professional integration measures, including 670 unemployed and 377 in assistance. The Social Service opened 358 new files, an increase compared to 345 in 2024.
What is the financial balance of Caritas Ticino for 2025?
The 2025 operating account closes with a deficit of 255,682 francs, an improvement compared to 371,276 in 2024. Total revenues were 9,596,066 francs, with a forecast for 2026 in balance.
What are the main activities of Caritas Ticino?
The main activities include socio-professional integration measures, circular economy (collection of clothing, household appliances, and furniture) and organic farming. The organization also handled 748,000 liters of invasive plants on 936,000 square meters.
