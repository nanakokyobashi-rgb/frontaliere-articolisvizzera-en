The school system in the Canton of Obwalden is divided into three phases: kindergarten, elementary school and secondary school. Nurseries are compulsory for children aged 4-5 and primary school is compulsory for all children aged 6-10.

Kindergartens and primary school

The obligation to enroll in kindergartens in the Canton of Obwalden was introduced with the law on the right to education of 1 January 2015 (art. 8). This law provides that children between the ages of 4 and 5 must attend a kindergarten. 📊

According to data from the school office of the Canton of Obwalden, in 2020, 95% of children between the ages of 4 and 5 were enrolled in a kindergarten. This number is up from 2015, when only 70% of children between the ages of 4 and 5 were enrolled in a kindergarten.

Elementary school is compulsory for all children between the ages of 6 and 11. Children must attend primary school for 4 years. According to data from the school office of the Canton of Obwalden, in 2020, 99% of children between the ages of 6 and 11 were enrolled in a primary school.
