Key facts

  • What: School system of the Canton of Schwyz. - When: Registration generally takes place between 4 and 5 years. - Where: Canton of Switzerland. - Who: Swiss children. - Amount: Not applicable.

The school system of the Canton of Swabia is structured in three cycles: the primary cycle, the secondary cycle and the tertiary cycle. Registration generally takes place between 4 and 5 years.

The primary cycle lasts 4 years and includes primary school. The secondary cycle lasts 5 years and includes middle school. Thelatter cycle is divided into two sections: lower secondary school and upper secondary school.

The tertiary cycle is mandatory and lasts 3 years. During this cycle, students may choose to attend a high school or technical institute.

The school system of the Canton of Swabia is managed by the Department of Education and Culture. The department is responsible for managing the human and material resources needed to ensure the quality of education.
