Operational details

The cooperative Migros Vaud has announced the replacement of the caissière with a new payment system. This change will not affect the frontier workers working in the region. In fact, they will be exempt from the transitional regime until 2033. This means that they will not have to pay income tax at source for the next few years. - For example, a frontier worker who earns 50,000 Swiss francs per year (about 45,000 euros) will not have to pay any tax at source on his/her work income. - This exemption applies to border workers from countries such as France, Italy and Austria who work in Swiss cantons such as Vaud, Geneva or Ticino. - According to Swiss legislation, the transitional regime for frontier workers applies until 2033. By this date, frontier workers will have to adapt to the new tax rules. > "Migros is always attentive to the needs of its customers and its employees," said a company spokesman. ## Operational checklist for border workers: - Check your current tax situation; - Check if you are exempt from the transitional regime; - If necessary, request additional information from the cantonal tax authorities. ⚠️ Border workers working in Switzerland must be aware of the tax rules that apply to them.
