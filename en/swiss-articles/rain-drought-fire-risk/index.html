Over the last 30 days, drought conditions in Switzerland have remained critical, despite the rainfall in recent days. A MeteoSwiss spokesperson told Keystone-ATS that the rain had eased the problem only temporarily and that, in many parts of the country, the rainfall deficit had not changed.

The spokesperson described the overall situation as critical. The rains, therefore, did not alter the overall picture: they eased the drought only for a limited period.

“Over the last 30 days, precipitation has averaged less than 50% of the long-term average”.

The average figure should be read alongside the more pronounced local cases. The source reports that, in some cases, values of only 15-35% of the norm were recorded. The shift from the long-term average to local values shows that the precipitation did not erase the deficit described by the spokesperson.

Watercourses and lakes

In the Jura and Swiss Plateau, the levels of watercourses and lakes continue to be extremely low. At numerous measuring stations, discharges reached values that, statistically, occur only once every 10-30 years. The reference to the stations makes the picture concrete: this is not merely a general assessment of rainfall, but also measurements of discharges.

Another signal concerns the Rhine. Record lows were recorded in the stretch between Lake Constance and Basel. The source thus places the situation in the Jura and Swiss Plateau alongside that of the Rhine along a specific stretch.

Forest fire danger

Drought also affects the danger of forest fires. According to the map of the Federal Office for the Environment (UFAM), a band of high risk stretches from Valais to St. Gallen; in some areas of Upper Valais, the danger reaches the “very high” level.
