Border tinsmiths can work as tinsmiths with a G permit in Ticino. To obtain this permit, they must meet the requirements of the G permit and obtain a qualification recognition for their vocational training in tinsmithing.

To obtain the G permit, tinsmiths must complete the G permit application form, which is available on the Federal Administration Contributions Office Site. In addition, they must present identity documents and work documents, such as identity card and employment contract.

An important requirement to obtain the G permit is the quality certificate in tinsmithing. This certificate must be issued by a recognised organisation, such as the Swiss Society of Roofing Contractors (SSRC). The quality certificate must certify that the tinsmith has completed a vocational training course in tinsmithing and has acquired the necessary knowledge and skills to work as a tinsmith.

The vocational training course in tinsmithing must be at least 3 years and must be completed at a recognized training institution. The course must include the following subjects:
