Operational details

Practical Implications for Swiss Patients

These advances in AI could have a significant impact on the management of heart diseases in Switzerland. Predictive models can help doctors identify patients at higher risk, allowing for timely and personalized interventions. This could reduce the number of hospitalizations and improve the quality of life for patients.

Comparison with the Previous Situation

Before the introduction of these AI models, doctors relied on standard risk scores that did not account for individual variations. With AI, it is possible to obtain a more accurate prognosis, which can directly influence the treatment plan and the frequency of medical check-ups.

Future Scenarios

If these predictive models are adopted on a large scale, they could become a standard tool in daily clinical practice. This could lead to more efficient management of healthcare resources and better allocation of medical services.

Procedure for Patients

Patients who wish to benefit from these new predictive tools should consult their general practitioner or cardiologist. Doctors can use these models to assess individual risk and plan the most appropriate treatment.

Available Tools

The predictive models developed by Inselspital and the University of Bern are already available online as free-access tools for the scientific community. This allows doctors to access these resources and use them in clinical practice.

Conclusion

AI is revolutionizing the field of cardiology, offering more precise and personalized tools for the management of heart diseases. However, it is important to remember that these tools are designed to support doctors and not to replace them. Human supervision remains essential to ensure quality care.
