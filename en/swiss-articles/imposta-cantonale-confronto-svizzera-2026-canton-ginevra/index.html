The rate for income below CHF 50,000 has not been changed. Comparison with the other cantons reveals significant differences in rates and deductions. Nationally, the maximum rate is 11.5%, but Swiss cantons apply different rates depending on income.

In particular, the canton of Zurich applies a progressive income tax, with rates ranging from 0% to 31.5%. The canton of Geneva, on the other hand, applies a progressive tax, with rates ranging from 0% to 22.5%. The canton of Bern applies a progressive income tax, with rates ranging from 0% to 12%.

For example, if a taxpayer has an income of CHF 40,000 and resides in the canton of Zurich, they will pay an income tax of CHF 5,000, considering the rate of 12.5% (CHF 40,000 x 0.125). If the same taxpayer resided in the canton of Geneva, he would pay an income tax of CHF 4,000, considering the rate of 10% (CHF 40,000 x 0.1).
