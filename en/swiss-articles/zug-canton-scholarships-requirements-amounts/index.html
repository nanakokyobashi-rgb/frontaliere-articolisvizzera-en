Scholarships and loans in the Canton of Zug: requirements, amounts and deadlines (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 17 August 2026

Complete guide to scholarships and honorary loans in the Canton of Zug: who can request them, maximum amounts, presentation deadlines and competent office.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The Canton of Zug offers scholarships (non-refundable) and honorary loans (repayable) for resident students
  • Main requirements : cantonal residence ≥3 years (Swiss or B/C permits), gross family income ≤ CHF 120,000 (threshold 2025), enrolment in recognised courses (university, HF, higher vocational training)
  • Maximum amounts 2025: Bachelor (CHF 12.000/anno), Master (CHF 15.000/anno), Doctorate (CHF 20.000/anno). Loans up to CHF 25,000 total
  • Deadline: 30 April 2025 for the academic year 2025/2026
  • Competent office: Cantonal Training and Subsidies Service (Baarerstrasse 16, Zug)

Key facts

  • What: Cantonal scholarships and loans
  • Where: Canton of Zug (municipalities: Zug, Baar, Cham, etc.)
  • Who: Resident students with income limits (excluding children of already subsidised cantonal employees)
  • Amount: Variable (annual maximums set by the Council of State). Example: CHF 15,000 for Master of Engineering at PF Zurich
  • Deadline: April 30 (sending complete documentation by certified mail)
  • Office: Cantonal Training Service (tel. 041 728 32 22)

The Canton of Zug, known for its low tax burden and innovative ecosystem, actively supports higher education through targeted financial instruments. Managed by the Cantonal Training and Grants Service, these grants combine non-repayable grants and zero-interest loans (1% post-study interest).

Operational details

How exchanges and loans work in the Canton of Zug

The Zugano system distinguishes between scholarships (non-refundable) and honor loans (to be returned after graduation). Key requirements include:

  • Uninterrupted residence in the canton for at least 3 years (B/C permit or Swiss citizenship). For example, a student who moved to Baar in 2021 becomes eligible from 2024.
  • Enrolment in full-time courses at Swiss universities (e.g. ETH Zurich, University of Lucerne) or recognized foreigners (e.g. German polytechnics or UK Russell Group).
  • Household income limits: For a family of 4, cantonal taxable income must not exceed CHF 120,000 (2024 value). A family with an income of CHF 115,000 and two student children can apply for support for both.
  • Maximum age: 35 years for master's and doctoral degrees (reference: Cantonal Law on Education, art. 12).

Maximum amounts and duration

The scholarships partially cover the costs of study and subsistence. Amounts 2024:

  • Bachelor: up to CHF 16.000/anno (e.g. CHF 12,000 for taxes + CHF 4,000 for board/lodging)
  • Master: up to CHF 20.000/anno (e.g. CHF 15,000 for academic costs + CHF 5,000 for out-of-pocket expenses)
  • PhD: up to CHF 32.000/anno (including research and conference contributions)

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I apply for the scholarship if I study abroad?
Yes, as long as the foreign institution is recognized and the course is full-time. Zugana residence must be maintained during studies.
Do the loans have to be repaid even if I don't finish my studies?
Yes, in case of abandonment or failure to obtain the qualification, the reimbursement begins after 2 years from the end of studies or from exceeding the maximum age.
How are income limits calculated?
It is based on the cantonal taxable income of the previous year, net of deductions for AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG. Does not include family assets.
