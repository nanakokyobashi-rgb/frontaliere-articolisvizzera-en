The Canton of Freiburg is one of the 26 cantons of Switzerland and has a population of about 288,000. Like many other Swiss cantons, Freiburg also has an efficient and well-organised public transport system, which includes buses, trains and trams. Public transport passes are one of the cheapest and most convenient options for commuters.

Zones and Rates

The public transport subscription system in the Canton of Freiburg is based on different zones and tariffs. There are two types of subscriptions: monthly subscription and annual subscription. The price of the monthly subscription varies depending on the area and the type of subscription chosen.

  • Monthly pass: the basic price is CHF 85.50 (about € 78.50) for zone A, which includes the city of Freiburg and neighboring municipalities. For the outermost areas, the price can reach CHF 145.50 (about € 131.50).
  • Annual subscription: The base price is CHF 1,020 (approximately € 930) for zone A, a 10% reduction compared to the monthly subscription.

Operational details

The Canton of Freiburg has a tariff community that allows residents to use public transport with a single subscription. Annual and monthly subscriptions are available, as well as the combination with the half-booking and the AG. 📊

Tariff Zones

The Canton of Freiburg is divided into three tariff zones: zone A, zone B and zone C. Zone A comprises the city of Freiburg and neighbouring municipalities, Zone B comprises the municipalities of the district of Freiburg and Zone C comprises the municipalities of the district of Gruyère. 📍

Rates

The rates for public transport passes in the Canton of Freiburg are as follows:

  • Zone A annual subscription: CHF 1'200 (approx. € 1'170) (source: TPF, 2022)
  • Zone A monthly subscription: CHF 105 (approx. € 100) (source: TPF, 2022)
  • Zone B annual subscription: CHF 1,800 (approx. € 1,730) (source: TPF, 2022)
  • Zone B monthly subscription: CHF 150 (approx. € 140) (source: TPF, 2022)
  • Zone C annual subscription: CHF 2,400 (approx. € 2,260) (source: TPF, 2022)
  • Zone C monthly subscription: CHF 200 (approx. € 180) (source: TPF, 2022)

Combinations

You can combine public transport subscriptions with half-booking and GA. The half-prenota is a subscription that allows you to use public transport to get to work and back home, while the AG is a subscription that allows you to use public transport to get to the city centre and back home. 🚂
