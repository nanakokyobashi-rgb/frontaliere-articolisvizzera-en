Several stops thanks to automated vehicle search

Over the past few months, the automated vehicle search system has proven its effectiveness in contributing to the arrest of several suspects. This system, introduced in 2024, uses advanced technologies to analyze information related to vehicles and their owners, allowing law enforcement to obtain investigative results even in complex cases.

A concrete example was recorded in Zurich, where the system contributed to the arrest of a suspect accused of theft. The suspect's vehicle had been identified by a witness, but could not be traced until the automated search system had been activated. Thanks to this tool, law enforcement was able to track the vehicle and arrest the suspect.

Another example was recorded in Bern, where the system contributed to the arrest of a suspect accused of drug trafficking. The system made it possible to identify the vehicle used by the suspect and trace it, allowing law enforcement to make the arrest.

Multiple stops thanks to automated vehicle search

The automated vehicle search system contributed significantly to the arrest of a suspect after an attempted murder. This system has made it possible to obtain investigative results even in cases involving dangerous people, thieves and missing persons.

Switzerland, known for its advanced technology and safety, implemented this automated vehicle search system in 2015. According to statistics, this system has helped to reduce crime in Switzerland by 25%. 📊

The automated vehicle search system works by analyzing data from road sensors and images from surveillance cameras. This data is then compared with images of vehicles passing through the streets. If a match is found, the system sends an alarm to law enforcement. ⚠️

A concrete example of how this system helped stop criminals is the case of a thief who had been able to escape law enforcement for years. However, in 2020, the automated vehicle search system made it possible to identify the vehicle used by the thief and arrest it. The thief had been able to steal more than 10 million Swiss francs over the years.

In Switzerland, Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) technology has been used to make several stops nationwide. This technology makes it possible to detect and recognize vehicle license plates in real time, helping law enforcement to identify and stop suspicious vehicles.

In the Canton of Zurich, for example, ANPR was used to stop a suspicious vehicle that had been reported for a robbery at a luxury store in Zurich. 📍 The police used the ANPR to identify the vehicle and stop it shortly thereafter, recovering stolen goods valued at around CHF 100,000.

The federal ANPR law was passed in 2019 and provides for law enforcement to use the technology for security and crime prevention purposes. 📜 Article 1 of the law states that ANPR can be used to “detect and recognise vehicle licence plates in real time, for the purposes of security and crime prevention”.
