Zimmer Biomet cuts up to 580 jobs in Winterthur (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 9 September 2026

The US giant launches the consultation procedure with workers to reduce staff. Procedures and benefits for affected employees.

Context

In brief

  • Zimmer Biomet announces up to 580 job cuts at Winterthur (Zurich)
  • Consultation procedure launched with worker representatives
  • Corporate objective: productive efficiency and long-term sustainability

Key Facts

  • What: Reduction of jobs at Zimmer Biomet Winterthur
  • Where: Winterthur, Zurich canton
  • Who: Zimmer Biomet (American orthopedic medical devices company)
  • Amount: Up to 580 jobs
  • When: Consultation procedure launched today
  • Reason: Simplification of production network, increased efficiency
  • Responsible: Elizabeth Gauthier, Zimmer Switzerland Manufacturing

Zimmer Biomet's announcement

Zimmer Biomet, one of the leading American giants in the orthopedic medical devices sector with a strong global presence, has announced today its intention to reduce up to 580 jobs at its facility in Winterthur, in the Zurich canton. The American company has officially launched the consultation procedure with worker representatives to assess possible alternatives and attempt to limit the occupational impact.

According to the company's press release, the changes are part of a process of continuous development of the production network. The stated objective is to simplify the production and distribution network, increase overall efficiency, and ensure the company's long-term success. Elizabeth Gauthier, head of production at Zimmer Switzerland Manufacturing, stated that management is fully aware of the importance of the Winterthur site, its long tradition, and the valuable contribution of the local team.

Operational details

Implications on the Swiss labor market

Zimmer Biomet's announcement represents a significant employment shock for the Winterthur region and the canton of Zurich. A potential cut of up to 580 jobs in a single company has cascading effects on the local labor market: on one hand, it increases pressure on relocation services; on the other, it can influence demand for specialized skills in the industrial sector of the area.

The consultation procedure announced is also relevant from the perspective of workers' rights in Switzerland. The consultation phase with workers' representatives is a fundamental step provided for by Swiss regulations on staff reduction. During this phase, alternatives are examined with the union and company committees, to seek less drastic solutions such as reduced working hours, early retirements, or transfers to other locations.

Winterthur is an important industrial center in the canton of Zurich, with a long manufacturing tradition. Zimmer Biomet represents a major employer in the area. Such a significant reduction in jobs has direct repercussions on the local economic fabric: reduced purchasing power of residents, possible contraction of related services such as corporate catering, logistics, and local suppliers. There is an added increase in pressure on unemployment support measures managed at the cantonal and municipal level.

Furthermore, local suppliers that depend on consumption by Zimmer employees could suffer significant setbacks, adding further pressure to commercial activities in the area.

Key points

What to do if you are an employee of Zimmer Biomet

Employees of the company who receive a communication of possible dismissal must, first of all, understand the rights that belong to them during the consultation procedure. It is important to actively participate in dialogues with trade union representatives and company committees, to ensure that all alternatives such as reduced working hours, transfers, or early retirement with benefits are truly evaluated.

In case of actual dismissal, the first step is to register immediately with the employment office of the Zurich canton. Registration must occur within the first day of unemployment. With this registration, the employee automatically accesses unemployment benefits and receives support in finding a new position.

Support resources at the national level

In Switzerland, the organization of job searching is managed primarily at the cantonal level. In the Zurich canton, the employment office has professional orientation consulting services and access to job announcement databases. Furthermore, many Swiss trade unions offer legal advice and negotiation support to their members during collective dismissals.

Employees can also turn to outplacement services made available by some companies during mass layoffs. These services include support for CV writing, interview preparation, and access to contact networks for job searching.

Financial planning after unemployment

For those anticipating an occupational transition, it is important to plan your budget. Unemployment benefits cover only a percentage of previous income, so integration with personal savings and possible loans may be necessary. Some cantons offer subsidies for vulnerable categories, such as people over 55 or with dependents.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many jobs will actually be eliminated at Zimmer Biomet Winterthur?
Zimmer Biomet has announced a potential cut of up to 580 jobs. The actual number will be determined at the end of the consultation procedure with workers' representatives, during which alternatives will be evaluated to limit the reduction in employment.
What is the timeline for implementing the cuts at Zimmer Biomet?
The source does not specify a precise date of implementation. The consultation procedure was launched today; the number of days and weeks dedicated to this consultation will be determined during the next meetings between the company and the workers' representatives.
How can I access unemployment benefit if I lose my job at Zimmer Biomet?
You must register with the employment office in the canton of Zurich within the first day of unemployment. Once registered, you will be entitled to unemployment benefit, which covers a percentage of the previous insured income, with a duration that varies depending on the age and duration of the insurance.
Why is Zimmer Biomet cutting jobs?
According to the company, the changes are part of a process of continuous development of the production network. The aim is to simplify the production and distribution network, increase efficiency and ensure the long-term success of the company in the field of orthopaedic medical devices.
What alternatives to eliminating jobs are being studied?
During the consultation procedure with the workers' representatives, the possible alternatives will be carefully evaluated, with the aim of limiting any reduction in jobs. The source does not specify which exact alternatives are under discussion, but they are likely to include reduced hours, transfers and early retirements.
