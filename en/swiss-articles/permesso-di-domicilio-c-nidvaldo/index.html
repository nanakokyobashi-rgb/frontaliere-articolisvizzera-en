Operational details

Residence permit C is a type of permit that allows you to stay in the Canton of Nidvaldo, an area composed of three districts: Stabio, Balerna and Montreux-Vevey. This permit is intended for those who wish to stay in the canton for more than 90 days a year. The application procedure for residence permit C requires the submission of documents and verification of integration requirements. Language skills are an important factor in the evaluation of the application, in particular Italian, English and French are the most used languages in the Canton of Nidvaldo. Key points

To obtain the residence permit C in the Canton of Nidvaldo, you must submit the application within the established deadline and provide all the required documents. It is also necessary to demonstrate a sufficient level of integration in the Canton of Nidvaldo and to have adequate language knowledge. You can consult the website of the Canton of Nidvaldo for more information on the application procedure and the requirements.

The application for residence permit C must be submitted within 6 months from the date of arrival in the Canton of Nidvaldo. It is possible to apply for residence permit C even if you are already resident in the Canton of Nidvaldo, but you must demonstrate a sufficient level of integration and adequate language knowledge.

To demonstrate a sufficient level of integration, you need to:

  • Have a monthly income of at least CHF 4,500 (source: Canton of Nidvaldo 2022 data)
  • Have a stable employment contract or economic activity
  • Have an integration plan in the Canton of Nidvaldo (for example, participation in language courses or cultural integration)

To demonstrate adequate language skills, it is necessary to:

  • Have a basic knowledge of the German language (e.g. CEFR level A2)
  • Have a German language certification (e.g. TELC or DSH)
  • Have proof of knowledge of the German language (for example, a letter of attestation from the employer)
