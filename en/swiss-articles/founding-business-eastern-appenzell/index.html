Opening a business in Appenzello Esterno: a practical guide (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 September 2026

Legal forms, registration, minimum capital, federal and cantonal taxes, social security and insurance obligations to start a business.

Context

In brief

  • Three legal forms: sole proprietorship, simple partnership, public limited company
  • Mandatory registration in the cantonal commercial register
  • Federal, cantonal, and municipal tax and social security obligations
  • Social security: AHV/IV (5.3%), ALV/UVG (1.1%), mandatory health insurance (LAMal)

Key facts

  • What: Starting a business requires federal, cantonal, and municipal compliance
  • Where: Canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden (northeastern Switzerland)
  • Who: Entrepreneurs, self-employed, small and medium-sized enterprises
  • Competence: Cantonal offices for commercial register and cantonal taxes; federal offices for AHV/LPP and VAT
  • Requirements: Legal form, variable minimum capital, mandatory insurances

Starting a business in the Canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden requires compliance with a series of federal, cantonal, and municipal obligations. Choosing the legal form is the first step: sole proprietorship, simple partnership, or public limited company, each with different implications for minimum capital, taxation, and personal liability.

Registration in the cantonal commercial register is mandatory for most businesses. This administrative requirement is handled by the competent cantonal offices and must be completed before starting operations.

Simultaneously, those starting a business must comply with federal, cantonal, and municipal tax obligations. The Swiss tax system operates on three distinct levels: the federal direct tax (BST) managed by the FTA (Federal Tax Administration), cantonal taxes managed by the competent cantonal office, and municipal taxes calculated with a multiplier on the cantonal value. Each canton, including Appenzell Ausserrhoden, has its own laws and rates.

Operational details

Cantonal tax and administrative implications

The canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden, like all Swiss cantons, manages its own cantonal tax regime and sets the framework for municipal taxes. This means that the effective tax rate varies depending on the municipality within the canton. Self-employed individuals and entrepreneurs must file their cantonal and municipal tax returns annually with the cantonal administration. The direct federal tax is managed at the federal level by the FTA, but it is calculated based on a federal rate that is uniform across Switzerland, except for progressivity. For small growing businesses, VAT (Value Added Tax) becomes relevant when the turnover exceeds certain annual thresholds set at the federal level. The FTA manages all VAT-related issues at the national level. An important aspect for self-employed individuals operating from a base in the canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden is the distinction between:

  • Direct federal taxes (DFT): a progressive tax managed by the FTA
  • Cantonal taxes: managed by the competent cantonal office, with specific rates for the canton
  • Municipal taxes: calculated as a multiplier of the cantonal amount, varying between municipalities within the same canton

Self-employed individuals should also register with the social security registry at the competent federal office (UFAS/BSV). This office does not set the taxes but coordinates the payment of AVS/AI/AD/AC contributions and manages pension rights. Registration must occur within specific times from the start of the activity to avoid administrative penalties.

Key points

Practical procedure to open a business in the Canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden

Step 1: Choosing the legal form Before taking any action, decide whether to operate as a sole proprietorship, simple partnership, or corporation. Consult a notary or tax advisor to understand the implications of minimum capital, personal liability, and administrative burdens.

Step 2: Registration with the cantonal commercial register The commercial register of the Canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden requires the registration of any commercial, craft, or agricultural activity that employs staff or generates revenue above a certain threshold (for sole proprietorships). Contact the competent cantonal office (State Chancellery or cantonal commercial register office) for specific details and forms. Registration is done on an official form and generally involves a registration fee, the amount of which varies depending on the canton and legal form.

Step 3: Federal tax registration If the turnover exceeds federal thresholds, the company must register with the Federal Tax Administration (FTA) for VAT and federal direct tax. The FTA provides unique identification numbers (tax number, VAT number).

Step 4: Cantonal tax registration Register with the competent cantonal office for cantonal and municipal taxes. The Canton of Appenzell Ausserrhoden has specific procedures; the cantonal office will guide you through the necessary steps.

Step 5: Health insurance (LAMal) Take out health insurance with an authorized insurer. LAMal is mandatory for all residents of Switzerland within 3 months of starting the activity (if not previously insured).

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the minimum capital to open a business in the Outer Appenzell Canton?
The minimum capital varies depending on the legal form. For a sole proprietorship or simple company, no minimum capital is typically required. For a public limited company (SA), the federal minimum capital is CHF 20,000, with at least 20% paid up at the time of foundation. Check with the specific cantonal office if Appenzello Esterno has additional requirements.
How are cantonal taxes calculated in the Outer Appenzell Canton?
The Swiss tax system operates on three levels: direct federal tax (DFI), cantonal taxes and municipal taxes. The federal tax is the same throughout Switzerland with progressive rates, while the canton of Appenzell Outermost defines its own cantonal rates. Each municipality in the canton applies a multiplier to the cantonal amount, creating variability between municipalities. The competent cantonal office provides multipliers and current rates.
When do I have to register with the cantonal trade register?
Registration in the cantonal trade register must be completed BEFORE commencing operations. For sole proprietorships or simple companies, the term varies according to turnover and the presence of employees. Most activities must be registered within a few days of the start of operations. The cantonal office of the trade register of Appenzello Esterno specifies the exact times and the necessary forms.
What are the social security obligations for a self-employed person in the External Appenzello?
The self-employed must pay contributions to Old Age and Survivors Insurance (AVS) and Disability Insurance (AI) equal to 5.3% as an employee, with integration of the employer component (total 10.6%). They also pay contributions to Unemployment Insurance (AD) and Insolvency Insurance (AC) up to federal annual ceilings. Health insurance (LAMal) is mandatory. Occupational pension (LPP) for the self-employed is optional but recommended.
Where can I find the official forms to register with the trade register?
The official forms and instructions are available at the commercial register office of the canton of Appenzello Outer (usually at the State Chancellery or the administrative office of the canton). The canton also provides online information on official websites. Consult the cantonal office directly for specific details of the procedure, forms and registration fees in your municipality of domicile.
