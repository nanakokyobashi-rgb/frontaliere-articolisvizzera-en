LAMal Obvaldo premiums: deductibles and reduction (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 23 August 2026

Compulsory deductibles, premium reductions and cantonal health insurance benefits in the Canton of Obvaldo. Application procedure and alternative insurance models.

Context

In a nutshell

  • LAMal is mandatory for residents; deductibles range from CHF 300 to CHF 2500 per year
  • Reductions in premiums (subsidies) depend on the income and assets of the household
  • Each canton defines its own parameters and application deadlines
  • Alternative models (GP, bonuses) significantly reduce premiums

Key facts

  • What: Compulsory health insurance with per capita premiums and selectable deductibles
  • When: Obligation within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland; annual renewal of premiums
  • Where: Canton of Obvaldo, following federal directives LAMal in force since 1992
  • Who: Insured with LaMal insurance companies authorised by FOPs/BAG
  • Deductibles: CHF 300 (ordinary) up to CHF 2500 for resident adults
  • Subsidy: Cantonal; variable according to income, assets, family composition

In the Canton of Obvaldo, as in every Swiss canton, compulsory health insurance (LAMal/KVG) represents the foundation of health protection. The Federal Health Insurance Act, which came into force in 1992, guarantees every resident equal access to medical coverage, regardless of initial health status.

Operational details

# Premium Reduction Request Procedure

Access to Obvaldo's premium reduction subsidies requires an explicit action by the insured. This is not an automatic right conferred by LAMal: a formal request must be submitted to the competent cantonal office (generally the social security or social benefits office of Obvaldo), accompanied by complete documentation:

  • Most recent tax return (certified copy or extract)
  • Current certificate of domicile
  • Current composition of the family unit (spouse, dependent children, other dependents)
  • Official cantonal request form (available at the office or online)
  • Recent bank statements, if the equity is significant

Eligibility criteria vary from canton to canton: each cantonal administration applies its own income and assets thresholds, different from the parameters of other cantons. The subsidy may cover a percentage of the premiums (example: 50%, 75%, 100% up to a threshold) or a fixed annual amount determined by local parameters. Some cantons, like many others, set maximum wealth thresholds: exceeding them excludes from subsidy even if gross income is low. It is therefore essential to verify the specific parameters at the cantonal administration of Obvaldo, avoiding assumptions.

Key points

Step-by-step: how to obtain the subsidy

Step 1: Contact the Obwalden cantonal office responsible for premium reduction subsidies (check the cantonal administration website for the exact name and contact details — the designation varies from canton to canton; it could be 'Social Security Office', 'Social Benefits Office', or 'LAMal Subsidy Sector').

Step 2: Request the official application form. Some cantons make it available on their online portal; others send it by mail or distribute it at public counters.

Step 3: Gather the documentation: complete tax declaration for the last two years, bank statements for the last three months, and an updated certificate of residence. If the household composition changes (marriage, birth, inclusion of a dependent elderly person), include the official certificates.

Step 4: Fill out the form in every section (personal data, gross income, assets, current insurance situation, beneficiaries). Errors, omissions, or inconsistent data significantly delay processing.

Step 5: Send the application to the official address (by regular mail or via the cantonal portal, if available). Keep the shipping receipt or protocol number for tracking.

Step 6: Wait for the official notification of admission or rejection. Processing times vary between 4 and 8 weeks, depending on the office workload. If rejected, the canton will communicate the reasons: it is possible to request a reconsideration with supplementary documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the six allowances allowed for an adult residing in Switzerland?
Federal LAMal law allows six levels of adult deductible: CHF 300 (ordinary, no premium discount), CHF 500, CHF 1000, CHF 1500, CHF 2000, CHF 2500. By increasing the deductible, the monthly premium is reduced proportionally. Children under 18 have separate deductibles (typically CHF 100 or CHF 300). The choice of the deductible remains unchanged for 12 months, except for significant changes in professional, family, or cantonal residence status.
How do I claim the premium reduction from Obvaldo?
Contact the social security office of the canton of Obvaldo. Fill out the official application form attaching the most recent tax return, the certificate of domicile, and the list of dependent family members. Submit your application by the annual cantonal deadline (generally March). You will receive an official response in 4-8 weeks with the result of admission or rejection.
Does the reduction subsidy apply to each family member?
Yes, but the calculation takes place per household. The cantonal office evaluates the combined income and total assets of the nucleus, then provides specific subsidies for each member (parent 1, parent 2, child 1, child 2, etc.). Exceeding the maximum cantonal equity threshold may exclude the entire nucleus from the subsidy, regardless of gross income.
Which insurance models offer lower premiums than the standard model?
Models with mandatory GPs (HMOs, structured medical networks) reduce premiums by up to 15-25% compared to full coverage. The bonus model offers progressive annual discounts (1-20%) if you do not use medical services. Each licensed insurance company proposes local variants. A comparison between companies for the same model and deductible can reveal differences of CHF 200-400 per year.
What happens if I don't renew my grant by the annual deadline?
You automatically lose the premium reduction upon renewal of the LAMal policy. There is no automatic or retroactive extension. You will need to submit a new application at the next deadline to recover the benefit. In the meantime, the full premium (without subsidy) will continue to be charged each month.
