The course of the dispute

18,4 million francs: that is the amount of the fine that the Federal Administrative Court annulled in the ruling made public yesterday evening. The penalty had been imposed on Swisscom in December 2023 and arose from the fiber-optic dispute.

The FAC did not find that the telecom operator held a dominant position. This conclusion differs from that of the Competition Commission, which had argued that there was a dominant position and had launched an investigation into the case at the end of 2020. That investigation then culminated in the penalty imposed in December 2023.

The central issue in the matter is therefore the interpretation of a dominant position in the telecommunications sector. On the one hand, there is COMCO's assessment, which led to the fine; on the other, that of the FAC judges, who did not recognize that position and annulled the penalty.

The proceedings do not necessarily end with the ruling made public yesterday evening. COMCO told the Awp news agency that it would examine the judgment in depth in the coming days. Only after this analysis will the authority decide how to proceed.

The two options indicated are clear-cut: accept the judgment of the Federal Administrative Court or appeal to the Federal Supreme Court. In the first case, COMCO accepts the FAC's decision. In the second, the fiber-optic dispute continues before the court indicated by the authority itself.

The report therefore describes one step that has already been decided and another that remains open. The decided step is the annulment of the fine of 18,4 million francs. The open one concerns the Competition Commission's response, expected after the analysis announced for the coming days.
