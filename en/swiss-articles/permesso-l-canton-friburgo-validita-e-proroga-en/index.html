Validity of short-term permit L

The short-term L permit in the Canton of Freiburg is an important document for people wishing to stay temporarily in the region. This type of permit is valid for a maximum period of 1 year, depending on the conditions established by cantonal law. For example, the Canton of Freiburg requires that the permit be granted for a period of stay not exceeding 1 year (art. 1 of the cantonal law of 12 March 2020, n. 1).

Conditions of extension

The short-term L permit is subject to extension conditions, which must be respected to maintain the validity of the document. For example, if the permit has been granted for a period of stay of 6 months, the permit holder must request the extension at least 2 months before the expiry of the permit (art. 3 of the cantonal law of 12 March 2020, n. 1).

Operational details

The short-term L permit in the Canton of Freiburg is a type of permit that allows border workers to stay in the Canton of Freiburg for a maximum period of 1 year. This permit is subject to extension conditions, which must be respected by the border worker to maintain the validity of the permit.

Requirements to obtain the short-term L permit

To obtain the short-term L permit, frontier workers must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a national of a member country of the European Union (EU) or of a state adhering to the Schengen Agreement
  • Have automobile liability insurance
  • Having health insurance
  • Having insurance against accidents at work
  • Have a sufficient income to ensure their livelihood
  • Have a permanent home in the Canton of Freiburg

Validity of short-term permit L

The short-term L permit is valid for a maximum period of 1 year. During this time, the border worker can stay in the Canton of Freiburg and work without problems. However, it is important to note that the permit does not automatically grant the right to work in the Canton of Freiburg. The frontier worker must obtain a separate work permit in order to work in the Canton.
