Physioswiss calls for a 30% increase in physiotherapy tariffs

The request is addressed to the cantonal authorities: fairer tariffs for physiotherapy services, so as to ensure accessible care close to home in the future as well. At the center is the increase of at least 30% in tariff point values.

The reference to geographic proximity accompanies the economic one: physiotherapy should remain guaranteed and close to home, while appropriate conditions for practices should enable fair salaries for professionals. Physioswiss presents these objectives as linked to the viability of the cantonal network. The problem is therefore described both from the perspective of practices and that of patients.

The mobilization and the issue raised

Physioswiss maintains that the current remuneration does not make it possible to adequately cover practices' operating costs and ensure fair salaries for professionals. For the association, inadequate economic conditions would make it more difficult to maintain a widespread care network in urban centers, peripheral regions and rural areas.

The Ticino collection is part of a national mobilization that involved all 26 Cantons. The balance indicated by the mobilization is 127'000 signatures overall in Switzerland, with 4'000 collected in Ticino.

By submitting the signatures, Physioswiss urges the Department of Health to intervene within the limits of its powers, so that tariffs guarantee adequate coverage of the costs of services.

«The support received highlights the importance, for the population of the Canton, of being able to access easily accessible physiotherapy. We hope that the Department of Health will take this signal seriously and work to ensure fair and sustainable funding for physiotherapy».

The association will monitor the development of cantonal tariff procedures and continue to support the sector's demands in discussions with the authorities. Physioswiss also emphasizes the contribution of physiotherapy to healthcare: the services can help avoid surgical interventions, shorten rehabilitation and safeguard patients' autonomy.

Operational details

The decisive point: point values

The debate does not stop at the new tariff system. Physioswiss has presented a system resulting from negotiations, which differentiates physiotherapy services in a more nuanced way. According to the association, however, this innovation alone is not sufficient to improve professionals' remuneration.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Physioswiss's main request in Ticino?
The association calls on the cantonal authorities to introduce fairer tariffs for physiotherapy services, with the specific objective of increasing tariff point values by at least 30%. This measure is considered necessary to adequately cover the operating costs of practices and ensure fair salaries for professionals, preventing the care network from becoming less extensive in urban centers and rural or peripheral areas.
What is the difference between the tariff structure and point values?
According to Physioswiss, the new negotiated tariff structure serves to represent services in a more differentiated manner, but does not itself determine an increase in remuneration. Instead, the tariff point values, established within the framework of cantonal procedures, determine the actual increases. For this reason, the petition focuses on point values to ensure that the tariffs really cover the costs of the services.
How did the mobilization unfold, and to whom was it handed over?
The mobilization was national in scope, involving all 26 cantons with a total of 127'000 signatures, of which 4'000 were collected in Ticino. The petition, entitled “For guaranteed physiotherapy close to home in the Canton of Ticino,” was submitted on 15 October in Bellinzona to Fabio Lonni, head of the political rights service at the State Chancellery, in the hope that the Department of Health would commit to equitable funding.
