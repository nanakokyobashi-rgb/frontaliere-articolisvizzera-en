The residence permit C is an important requirement for cross-border commuters who wish to settle in the Canton of Schwyz. To obtain this permit, cross-border workers must meet certain requirements and follow a specific procedure.

Requirements for residence permit C

To obtain the residence permit C, cross-border commuters must comply with the following requirements:

  • Be at least 25 years of age
  • Have an annual income of at least 80,000 Swiss francs
  • Have valid health insurance in Switzerland
  • Have no criminal record
  • Have stable accommodation in the Canton of Schwyz

Procedure for obtaining residence permit C

The procedure for obtaining residence permit C is as follows:

1. Submission of the application: cross-border commuters must submit the application for residence permit C to the Council of State of the Canton of Schwyz. 2. Verification of requirements: the Council of State verifies whether cross-border commuters satisfy the requirements to obtain the residence permit C. 3. Booking an interview: if the application is accepted, cross-border workers must book an interview with the Council of State. 4. Interview: during the interview, the Council of State verifies whether the cross-border commuters satisfy the requirements to obtain the residence permit C. 5. Decision: after the interview, the Council of State makes a decision on the application for residence permit C.

Costs for the residence permit C

The costs for the residence permit C are as follows:

  • Application submission fees: 500 Swiss francs
  • Interview fees: 200 Swiss francs
  • Registration fees: 100 Swiss francs
