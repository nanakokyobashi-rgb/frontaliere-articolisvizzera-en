Border pensions: common mistakes to avoid in 2026 (cross-border guide)

Di Laura Bianchi · Published on 27 June 2026

Learn how to avoid common mistakes in pension management for frontier workers in 2026, with a focus on AVS and INPS.

Context

In short - Properly coordinating AVS and INPS is essential for frontier workers. - The aggregation of contributions affects the total pension. - Advance social security planning is crucial. ## Key facts - What: Coordination between AVS and INPS for pensions. - When: Changes in force since 2026. - Where: Switzerland and Italy. - Who: Active and retired border workers. - Objective: Optimise retirement. With the approach of 2026, many border workers find themselves evaluating how best to manage their pensions, considering the specificities of the Swiss (AVS) and Italian (INPS) social security systems. The correct understanding and application of the rules on the aggregation of contributions is essential to avoid errors that could negatively affect the amount of the pension received. In addition, it is important to be aware of the deadlines and fulfillments needed to ensure a smooth transition to retirement.

Operational details

Totalization: a crucial step One of the most relevant aspects for border workers is the possibility of adding the contribution periods accrued in Switzerland (AVS) and Italy (INPS) to obtain a unique and more advantageous pension. The totalisation, regulated by the bilateral agreements between the two countries, makes it possible to avoid penalties deriving from the fragmentation of contributions. However, in order to take advantage of this benefit, it is essential to provide accurate documentation and comply with the deadlines. A common mistake is the failure to communicate all contribution periods to the competent social security authorities. This can lead to delays in pension processing or, worse, loss of vested rights. In addition, it is important to keep track of regulatory changes that could affect pension entitlement or its amount. ## # Long-Term Social Security Planning Another crucial aspect is long-term financial planning. Many border workers overlook the importance of evaluating their social security options in advance, with the risk of finding themselves with an insufficient pension to maintain their standard of living. Reaching out to an experienced social security advisor can be a strategic step in getting an accurate and personalized assessment.

What do you mean by total contributions?
With the aggregation, the contribution periods accrued in Switzerland and Italy can be added together to obtain a single pension.
What are the most common mistakes in pension management for border workers?
Failure to register contributions, miss deadlines, and ignore regulatory changes.
