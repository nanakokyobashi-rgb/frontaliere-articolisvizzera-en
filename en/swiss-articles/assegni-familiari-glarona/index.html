The State Council of the Canton of Glarus has established the settings for family allowances in the Canton. Monthly amounts per child were fixed at CHF 250, CHF 350 and CHF 450, respectively for the first two children and for the third or subsequent children. The legal conditions require families residing in the Canton to have an annual income of at least CHF 40,000. The application procedure involves the submission of a specific form, accompanied by copies of identity and income documents.

Application Process Details

The application for family allowances in the Canton of Glarus must be submitted within a period of 3 months from the date of birth of the child. The application form is available from the Social Welfare Service of the Canton of Glarus or can be downloaded from the official website of the Canton. Families must attach copies of identity and income documents, including identity card, child's birth certificate, and annual income certificate, to the form.

Practical analysis

Family allowances in Glarus Canton have been established to help families cover the costs of child support. Monthly amounts per child were set based on family income and number of children. The legal conditions require households to have an annual income of at least CHF 40,000.

The application procedure is simple and can be submitted to the local town hall. It is important to note that family allowances are not a vested right, but a benefit that can be granted or revoked depending on the circumstances.

📊 Here's an example of how the application process works:

  • Family A has an annual income of CHF 60,000 and two children. Their claim for family allowance is assessed and approved, with a monthly amount of CHF 1,200 per child.
  • Family B has an annual income of CHF 30,000 and a child. Their claim for family allowance is assessed and rejected, as the annual income is below the limit of CHF 40,000.

Family allowance amounts

Family allowances in the Canton of Glarus are subject to various conditions and amounts. Depending on the number of children and family income, check amounts may vary. Here are some concrete examples:

  • A household with two children and an annual income of CHF 60,000 could receive a family allowance of CHF 1,200 per month.
  • A household with three children and an annual income of CHF 80,000 could receive a family allowance of CHF 1,800 per month.
