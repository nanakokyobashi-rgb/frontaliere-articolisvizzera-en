Groupe E cuts 12 jobs: executives mainly affected

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

Groupe E confirms 12 dismissals in Fribourg: nine involve managers. The social plan provides benefits and support for new employment.

Context

In brief

  • Groupe E confirms 12 dismissals in Fribourg.
  • Nine of the positions concern managers.
  • The restructuring will take effect next year.

Key facts

  • Company → Groupe E
  • Location → Fribourg
  • Dismissals → 12
  • Managers affected → 9
  • Decision → definitive dismissals
  • Measures → social plan in force and support in finding new employment

Groupe E has defined the outlines of the ongoing workforce streamlining at the Fribourg-based energy company. The decision announced today brings the total to twelve dismissals; nine involve managers. This figure makes clear that the reduction mainly affects positions of responsibility and that this is no longer merely an announced intention.

The company had announced in June its intention to proceed with staff cuts as part of a reorganization. At that time, the scope of the operation had not been specified. The new communication closes that phase: following consultation with employee representatives, analysis of the situation and exploration of individual solutions, the dismissals are now definitive.

The restructuring timetable

The internal restructuring will take effect next year. Groupe E gives three objectives: streamline structures, shorten decision-making processes and exploit synergies. The sequence described by the company thus separates the decision on jobs from the implementation of the new setup: the dismissals have already been finalized, while the organizational reorganization will take effect at a later stage.

The reasons cited in the note concern the energy sector. The company cites changes in the sector and growing pressure regarding efficiency. These are the elements cited to explain the restructuring and the choice to intervene in the internal structures.

For the people affected, Groupe E announces two forms of support. The first consists of the benefits provided for under the social plan currently in force. The second is support in finding new employment. The note therefore not only presents the number of dismissals, but also communicates the social measures intended for the affected employees.

The transition from June to the definitive decision now makes the scale of the operation explicit: twelve positions, with a predominance of managerial staff. Next year, however, will mark the entry into force of the internal restructuring and the organizational objectives indicated by the company. This is the scope of the communication issued by Groupe E.

Operational details

The comparison between announcement and decision

The most significant change compared with June is the transformation of an intention into a decision. This changes the type of information available to employees: the number has been established and the support is linked to measures already indicated. The significance of management staff

Nine of the twelve layoffs concern management staff. This is the figure that best defines the profile of the operation: the main share of the affected positions belongs to managerial levels. The concentration on management staff therefore places the issue of hierarchical organization at the center of the reorganization. The statement links the layoffs to the restructuring of the organizational structures, without separating the employment dimension from the management one.

For the organization, the stated objectives are clear. Streamlining structures describes a more agile internal configuration; shortening decision-making processes indicates the intention to reduce the steps needed for decisions; leveraging synergies points to better internal coordination. These are objectives indicated for the phase that will begin next year, not results already presented by the company.

From an employment perspective, the social measures are the operational point already communicated. The current social plan constitutes the reference for the benefits intended for dismissed employees, while support in finding new employment accompanies the phase following the decision. For those who need to look beyond Groupe E, annunci di lavoro can supplement the support indicated by the company; aziende che assumono offers an additional point of guidance, without replacing the measures provided for by the social plan.

The concrete significance of the news remains linked to Groupe E: twelve layoffs, nine among management staff, a current social plan and an organizational restructuring taking effect next year. These are the elements that make it possible to distinguish the decision that has already been formalized from the objectives still linked to the restructuring phase.

Key points

What those affected can do

The information communicated by Groupe E makes it possible to derive a practical course of action without adding steps not indicated in the note. The first point of reference is the final decision on the twelve redundancies; the second is the social plan currently in force; the third is the support provided for the search for new employment. The restructuring will come into effect next year and constitutes the organizational timetable communicated by the company.

Four steps to follow

1. Separate the initial announcement from the final decision. The June communication concerned the intention to cut jobs as part of a reorganization. The note issued today instead indicates that, following consultation with employee representatives, analysis of the situation and exploration of individual solutions, the redundancies are final.

2. Refer to the benefits under the social plan currently in force. Groupe E assures that the people concerned will receive what is provided for under the plan in force. This is the channel indicated for the social measures connected to the decision.

3. Use the support in searching for new employment. The company states that it is also supporting the employees involved on this front. Consulting annunci di lavoro may be a parallel step for those who want to explore new opportunities, while company support remains the measure explicitly announced in the note.

4. Keep the personal path and the internal reorganization separate. For employees, the immediate issue is the redundancy and the support provided. For the company, the next step is the restructuring from next year, with leaner structures, shorter decision-making processes and synergies to be leveraged. The operational course therefore remains anchored to the two phases communicated: support for the people concerned and internal restructuring.
