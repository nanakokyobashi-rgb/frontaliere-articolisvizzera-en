Work-life balance: the Ticino survey starts

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 October 2026

Pro Familia Svizzera Italiana launches the first cantonal survey to map flexibility, working hours and leave in Ticino companies.

Context

In brief

  • First cantonal survey on work-life balance
  • Promoted by Pro Familia Svizzera Italiana
  • Official start of the survey: 1 October
  • Objective: to map good practices and areas for improvement

Key Facts

  • Promoter: Pro Familia Svizzera Italiana
  • Start date: 1 October
  • Target: Employers in the canton
  • Data analysis: Dr. Philippe Gnaegi

The first cantonal survey dedicated to the issue of work-life balance, an initiative promoted by Pro Familia Svizzera Italiana that aims to involve all employers active in the Ticino area, officially started on 1 October. The primary objective of this survey is to bring out the good practices already implemented within companies and local authorities, while identifying the areas where action is needed to make concrete improvements. The survey, which aims to offer a representative snapshot of the Ticino economic reality, focuses on crucial aspects of personnel management, such as work flexibility, hours, different organizational models, leave policies, career opportunities and gender balance.

A data-driven approach.

The process of analyzing the data collected will be managed by Dr. Philippe Gnaegi, a university professor who has over twenty years of specific experience in the sector. The results will be processed exclusively in aggregate form, ensuring the anonymity of the individual participating realities. The initiative is not limited to the simple collection of data, but intends to provide institutions, the political world and the economic fabric with a solid and concrete basis on which to build future measures to support businesses. In a historical moment characterized by a widespread shortage of qualified personnel, the ability of a company to reconcile professional and personal needs becomes a determining factor for its attractiveness on the labor market.

Operational details

Impact on the competitiveness of the territory.

The reconciliation of work and private life is not only a matter of individual well-being, but a fundamental pillar for the competitiveness of the Ticino territory as a whole. According to the promoters, companies that adopt flexible models that are attentive to the needs of employees are in a position of competitive advantage, being able to attract and retain the talents necessary for business growth more easily. The survey aims precisely to crystallize this added value, making visible the strategies that make organizations more resilient and attractive. For employers in the Canton, participating in this survey means actively contributing to the definition of a regulatory and support framework that is more in line with the real needs of the market. The comparison with industry practices also allows them to assess their positioning compared to the competition. Find out more about personnel management to understand how company policies influence the local labor market. The collection of structured data will allow, in perspective, to develop targeted interventions that can support Ticino companies in overcoming the challenges related to the management of schedules and the enhancement of employees. This evidence-based approach is essential to avoid fragmented interventions, preferring instead support policies that take into account the specificity of the cantonal economic fabric. The analysis conducted by Dr. Philippe Gnaegi ensures the scientific rigor that is essential to translate the responses of companies into operational guidelines, capable of positively influencing the attractiveness of the Ticino economic system for the years to come.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who can participate in the survey?
The survey is aimed at all employers located in the Canton of Ticino. The objective is to obtain a representative picture of the local situation by involving the largest possible number of organizations and institutions.
How is the collected data handled?
The data are analyzed exclusively in aggregate form by Dr. Philippe Gnaegi, a university lecturer with over twenty years of experience in the field. This method guarantees the anonymity of individual participants and the confidentiality of the company information provided during the survey.
What is the ultimate objective of the investigation?
The survey aims to bring to light the good practices already in place and identify areas for improvement. The results will provide institutions, policymakers and the economy with a concrete basis for developing future support measures, making companies more attractive during a period of shortage of qualified personnel.
