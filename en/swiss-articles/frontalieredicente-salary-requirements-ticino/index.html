Concrete Implications

The procedure for recognition of the qualification for border teachers in the canton of Ticino can last up to 6 months, as indicated by Article 10 of the Regulation on Upper Secondary Education of 2019. This period may vary depending on the individual characteristics of the teacher and the complexity of the recognition procedure.

Requirements for border teachers

In order to be able to teach in the Ticino educational system, border teachers must meet the requirements established by the Council of State of the Canton of Ticino. Among these, we find the master's degree or an equivalent degree, as well as a teaching certification for the specific subject to be taught. In addition, teachers must have a good knowledge of the official language of the canton, i.e. Italian.

Salary and working conditions

The salary of border teachers in the canton of Ticino is determined by the Council of State and may vary according to the subject and level of teaching. According to the Upper Secondary Education Act of 2019, the minimum salary for a border teacher in the canton of Ticino is CHF 5,000 per month. In addition, teachers must have the right of access to the canton's pension and health insurance system.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure for the recognition of the qualification of border teachers in Ticino

1. Submit the documentation required for the recognition of the qualification. 📚

  • The border lecturer must provide the documentation necessary for the recognition of the qualification, including:
  • Original degree certificate and certified copy
  • Graduation fee (approx. CHF 100-200)
  • Identity documents and authorizations for teaching
  • German or French language test (depending on the region)

2. Wait for the decision of the recognition commission.

  • The recognition commission of the qualification in Ticino is responsible for evaluating the applications submitted by border teachers. The evaluation process can take a few weeks or months, depending on the complexity of the application.

3. Verification of teaching requirements. 📚

  • Border teachers must meet the requirements for teaching in Ticino, including:
  • Degree in a teaching related field
  • Teaching experience (minimum 2 years)
  • Knowledge of German or French (depending on the region)
  • Certificate of qualification for teaching (approx. CHF 500-1000)

4. Salary and benefits for border teachers. 💸

  • Border teachers in Ticino can receive an annual salary of about CHF 60,000-80,000, depending on experience and qualification. In addition, they can benefit from:
  • Healthcare
  • Pension
