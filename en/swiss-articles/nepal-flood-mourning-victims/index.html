Nepal observes a day of mourning for the victims of the August 26 flood, with at least 1,398 dead and 5,515 missing

Context

In a nutshell

  • 1,398 dead and 5,515 missing due to the August 26 flood
  • Flags at half-mast and religious vigils to commemorate the victims
  • Nepal observes a day of mourning on September 13, traditional to commemorate the deceased

Key facts

  • What: Flooding caused by a glacier
  • When: 26 August 2024
  • Where: Kathmandu and surrounding regions
  • Who: Nepalese population and local authorities
  • Deaths: At least 1398
  • Dispersed: 5515

Nepal is today observing a day of mourning in memory of the victims of the violent flood of 26 August. The date coincides with the Hindu tradition of commemorating the dead on the thirteenth day after death. In the country, flags are at half-mast and religious vigils with candles are planned tonight. In total, at least 1,398 people died as a result of the disaster, probably caused by the collapse of a glacier, while 5,515 are missing.

# Victim Balance Details

In detail, the death toll from the floods in Nepal is now at least 1,355 dead, while another 4,996 people are missing. In neighboring Tibet, according to official data, 43 people have died and another 519 are missing. Some activists accuse the Chinese government of underestimating the real number of victims of the tragedy.
