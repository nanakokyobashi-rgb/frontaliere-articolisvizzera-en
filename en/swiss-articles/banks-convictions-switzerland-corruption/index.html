Operational details

Practical implications for customers and credit institutions The increase in court rulings against financial operators generates concrete repercussions on the daily operations of banks and on the relationship with customers. Customer verification procedures, commonly known as due diligence and anti-money laundering standards, are further tightened. For resident account holders and for those who have financial relations with Swiss institutions, this translates into requests for more detailed documentation on the origin of the funds and the nature of the transactions. Regulatory compliance becomes a crucial factor not only for large banks, but for the entire system which also includes asset management and retail services. A thorough analysis of the economic dynamics and stability of the credit system makes it possible to correctly assess the risks associated with investments and bank deposits. Transparency in contractual relationships and traceability of financial flows are essential elements to operate safely within the Swiss market. Those who manage their assets must face increasingly strict compliance standards, designed to prevent involvement in corruption or money laundering offences. Key points

How to move in the choice of institutions and verification procedures In order to orient oneself correctly in the Swiss banking landscape in light of recent judicial events, it is essential to adopt a prudent and informed strategy. The first step is to verify the solidity of the credit institution to which they entrust their savings, analysing the transparency reports and the certifications of conformity available. It is advisable to carefully review current account contracts and asset management services, making sure that the bank correctly applies all anti-money laundering and financial data protection provisions. In case of doubts about the management of their accounts or the need to transfer funds between different intermediaries, it is advisable to follow the standard procedures established by the supervisory authorities and consult qualified professionals.
