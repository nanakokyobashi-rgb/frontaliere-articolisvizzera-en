Data analysis

The customer satisfaction ranking was conducted on a sample of 1,500 people, representative of the Swiss population. The survey revealed that Migros is the most appreciated operator in Switzerland, with a score of 7.6 out of 10. Historic rival Coop and Digitec Galaxus placed just behind, with a score of 7.4.

Migros: the leader of the ranking

Migros has earned the first position in the ranking of customer satisfaction thanks to its wide range of products and services. The company offers a wide range of food products, as well as services such as home delivery and online sales. Migros is present throughout Switzerland, with over 500 points of sale in 26 cantons.
