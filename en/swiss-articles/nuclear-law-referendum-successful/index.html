In the Federal Chancellery’s press release, published on October 19, 2026, Bern reports the success of the referendum on the amendment to the Federal Act on Nuclear Energy (LENu). The contested text is the amendment to the same Act dated June 19, 2026. The news therefore concerns the formal status of the collection of signatures, not the content of the legislative amendment, which is not described in the press release.

On October 6, 2026, the referendum committee submitted 79 479 signatures. The Federal Chancellery’s verification established that 78 922 of the submitted signatures were valid. Following this finding, the referendum was declared formally successful. The press release does not present this as an outcome on the merits of the amendment: it merely describes the formal success.

The figure certified by the Federal Chancellery

The passage indicated in the source is the verification of the signatures. The Federal Chancellery does not merely report the total submitted by the committee: it specifies how many signatures are valid and links this verification to the formal success of the referendum. The wording delineates the information available and attests to the formal outcome indicated on October 19, 2026, without presenting a judgment on the content of the amended LENu. There is a difference of 557 signatures between the signatures submitted and those recognized as valid: 79 479 minus 78 922. This is a calculation based on the two figures reported by the source, not an additional figure reported separately.

Operational details

The dates distinguish three stages

The three dates in the communiqué do not indicate the same thing. June 19, 2026 identifies the amendment to LENu against which the referendum is directed; October 6, 2026 marks the submission of the signatures by the referendum committee; October 19, 2026 is the date of publication of the announcement from Bern. The sequence therefore sets out in order the contested act, the filing of the signatures, and the communication of the formal outcome.

The comparison that can be made

The source permits a limited chronological and numerical comparison. Chronologically, it links each date to a specific element. Numerically, it provides two different quantities: the signatures submitted and those that were valid. The difference between the two quantities can be seen by comparing 79 479 and 78 922; there is no need to attribute any further meaning to them beyond the validity established by the Federal Chancellery.

The text does not indicate a date for a vote and does not report an outcome on the substance of the amendment. Therefore, the chronology describes the formal status of the referendum as of the publication date, without providing a subsequent timetable.

Key points

The scope of the news

The communiqué makes it possible to establish the formal outcome precisely, but does not broaden the picture beyond the collection and validation of the signatures. For an accurate reading, the source documents that 78 922 of the signatures submitted are valid and that the referendum against the amendment to LENu has formally succeeded. The text also notes the role of the referendum committee, which submitted the signatures on October 6, 2026, but does not state its name.

Limits indicated by the source

The following remain outside the text:

These limits define the scope of the announcement. The source documents a formal success, not a decision on the substance of the federal law on nuclear energy. It does not attribute statements to the committee, the Federal Chancellery or other parties, and does not describe regulatory consequences.
