What are the main requirements to apply?
Institutions are looking for nurses with recognised qualifications and preferably experience, although they are open to recent graduates. It is important to check the specific requirements of each call.
How can I apply ?
You must consult the calls published on the official websites of the social institutions of Lugano and send the required documentation following the instructions provided. The application can also be submitted through job ad platforms.

Recruits are usually posted between January and April, with selections taking place in the second quarter. It is advisable to prepare the documents at the beginning of the year.
