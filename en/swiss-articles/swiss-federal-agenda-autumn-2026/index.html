The decisions announced by the Federal Council

The Federal Council will allocate more funds to integration programmes and is asking Parliament for 334,5 million francs for the period 2028-2032. The Swiss Government portal lists the request among the updated information on the decisions and activities of the Federal Council.

The official website also provides information on the functioning of the Federal Council as a collegial body and on popular votes. For this reason, the same information area also covers internal dossiers, parliamentary work and international events.

In the dossier on rents, the Federal Council rejects the initiative «Yes to protection against excessive rents (Rent Initiative)». It presents an indirect counterproposal to better combat excessive rents. The counterproposal is identified as indirect and concerns the same issue of rents.

For cybersecurity, the Federal Council intends to strengthen protection through a law regulating software and hardware products, data and digital infrastructures. A proposal will be drafted by giugno 2027: the deadline concerns the preparation of the proposal.

The political framework also includes the popular vote of 27 settembre 2026. The portal devotes a section to the results of the vote and the Federal Council's response.

For parliamentary work, the National Council and the Council of States meet from 14.9 to 2.19; the debates are broadcast live.

At the 81st United Nations General Assembly in New York, President of the Confederation Guy Parmelin delivered Switzerland's official speech. He reaffirmed Switzerland's commitment to dialogue and free trade in the face of unprecedented challenges.

At the international level, Switzerland and the EU negotiated a new package of agreements to stabilize and develop bilateral relations. The Federal Council submitted the Switzerland-EU package to Parliament on 13 marzo 2026.

The official website of the Swiss Government thus brings together information on decisions, collegial functioning, popular votes, digital security and international relations, alongside the work of Parliament.

From request to proposal

The most concrete distinction concerns the verb used. In the case of integration programmes, the Federal Council asks parliament for a sum and links the request to a specific period. The reader can then follow the amount, recipient and time frame without confusing the figure with another dossier.

For rents, the transition is different: the initiative is rejected, while the indirect counter-proposal is presented to better combat illegal renting. The topic is directly linked to the costo della vita in Svizzera, but the news mainly describes the comparison between the initiative and the alternative solution.

Cybersecurity has a development horizon: the Federal Council intends to strengthen it with a law regulating software and hardware products, data and digital infrastructure; a proposal will be drawn up by June 2027. For those who follow digital security, the date to be monitored is therefore linked to the preparation of the proposal.

The Swiss-EU package should also be read in phases. First there is the negotiation of the new package of agreements; on 13 March 2026, the Federal Council forwarded it to Parliament. The stated goal is to stabilize and develop bilateral relations.

The same logic helps to follow the popular vote of 27 September 2026 and the debates of the two Councils, broadcast live. Separating the date, institution and action indicated by the portal makes the update more readable for those looking for national news.
