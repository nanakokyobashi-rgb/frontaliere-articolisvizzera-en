Lindt revises estimates: sales and prices decline

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 29 September 2026

Lindt&Sprüngli cuts the 2026 organic growth forecast to 0-2 percent and is considering broader price reductions from January after the impact of cocoa and summer heat.

Context

In brief

  • Organic growth in 2026 falls to 0-2 percent
  • Previous target was 4-6 percent
  • Lindt is considering broader reductions from January
  • The share price fell to 7960 francs

Key facts

  • Organic growth in 2026 → 0-2 percent
  • Previous target → 4-6 percent
  • Broader reductions → starting in January
  • Morning share price → 7960 francs, -6,9 percent
  • Target from 2028 → annual organic growth of 6-8 percent

The new scenario for Lindt

In Zurich, Lindt&Sprüngli revised its revenue target downward for the second time. For 2026, the organic growth forecast is now between 0 and 2 percent, compared with the 4-6 percent indicated so far. At the beginning of the year, the company was still targeting 6-8 percent; that target was abandoned in March.

The announcement was made on Tuesday. The rationale combines two factors: price increases made necessary by rising cocoa prices and the heat wave that hit Europe during the summer. With high temperatures, consumers showed less interest in Lindor balls and pralines.

“No one felt like entering a specialty chocolate shop at 37 degrees,” said CEO Adalbert Lechner.

The increases, according to the company’s explanation, could not be avoided. After an initial decline, cocoa prices rose again and, even after the price adjustments, weighed on the gross margin. Lindt also intends to act on costs, while job cuts are not under discussion.

The raw materials situation then eased somewhat. Lindt is therefore considering broader reductions starting in January, with the aim of obtaining a greater volume of orders for next Easter and the Christmas period. The company says it is confident about the coming months and 2027. For those living in Switzerland, the topic becomes part of the reading of costo della vita in Svizzera, without the communication turning the assessment into a generalized promise.

The group also maintains its goal of improving the EBIT margin by 20-40 basis points in 2026. For 2027, it expects renewed volume growth, supported by an adapted pricing strategy, greater investment in the brand and falling cocoa prices. Financial forecasts for 2027 will be published in the first quarter; from 2028, annual organic revenue growth of 6-8 percent remains confirmed, with an annual improvement in the EBIT margin of 20-40 basis points.

Operational details

What changes for those who buy and those who watch the stock market

Prices, sales channels and confidence

The first practical point for those living in Switzerland is to distinguish what has already been done from what Lindt is still considering. Some reductions have been applied in Switzerland and Germany; a broader measure is instead indicated as possible from January. This distinction avoids reading as a certain price cut a decision that the company still presents as under consideration.

| Step | Reported signal | Practical reading | | Reductions already made | Switzerland and Germany | Observe the price applied | | From January | Possible broader reductions | Check for any change | | Easter and Christmas | Goal of more orders | Monitor demand's response |

The place of purchase also factors into the reading of the data. Demand declined mainly in specialty stores, while the decline was less pronounced in supermarkets. For the Swiss market, the news therefore does not describe identical behavior in every channel: it links sales weakness to the heat, but signals a different impact between specialty stores and supermarkets.

For an individual or family's budget, the point is not to turn Lindt's forecast into savings that can already be quantified. The company speaks of somewhat more relaxed raw-material prices and a strategy aimed at recovering volumes; the benefit for buyers will depend on the concrete application of the reductions. The comparatore CHF/EUR can complement this reading when observing the two markets mentioned, without confusing the exchange rate with the pricing decision.

The stock market offers another perspective. In morning trading, Lindt participation certificates listed in the Midcap SMIM index fell by 6,9 percent to 7960 francs, below the 8000 threshold for the first time since 2021. Analysts fear a loss of confidence and question the group's forecasting ability; according to the prevailing view, Lindt would also have overestimated its own pricing power. For the reader, the decline in the share price measures the market's reaction to the revenue warning, not the price of a single package.

Key points

How to follow developments

Three practical steps

1. Separate the announcement from its application. When buying in Switzerland, check the price actually applied and do not treat the assessment of broader reductions as a discount that has already been generalized. The distinction is between reductions already implemented and measures that Lindt is still examining.

2. Use the occasions indicated by the company as checkpoints. The next Easter and the Christmas period are the windows during which Lindt wants to obtain a greater volume of orders. The reader can compare the price observed before these phases with the one available during purchases, without automatically attributing every change to heat or cocoa.

3. To follow the company, use two reference points: the publication, in the first quarter, of the financial forecasts for 2027 and the medium-term targets confirmed from 2028 onward. The comparison should be made between volume growth, pricing strategy, brand investments and EBIT margin, all indicators mentioned by the company.

This procedure keeps three levels separate: the price paid by the consumer, the volumes that Lindt wants to recover and the indicators included in the financial targets. The first is verified at the time of purchase; the others are read in the company's subsequent updates. In this way, a company target remains distinct from a result already achieved, and an analysts' assessment is not confused with the price charged in stores.

For personal budgeting, noting the actual changes makes it possible to link spending to one's own figures, without using estimates not indicated in the communication. To link spending to your monthly budget, use the calcolatore stipendio.
