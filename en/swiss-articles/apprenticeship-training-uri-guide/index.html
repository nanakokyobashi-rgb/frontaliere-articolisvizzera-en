Apprenticeship and vocational training in Canton Uri (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 28 August 2026

How to find an internship in Canton Uri: contract, salary, professional maturity and concrete steps to apply.

Context

In a nutshell

  • In Switzerland, vocational training is regulated at the federal level by the LFPr
  • The Canton of Uri manages the network of traineeship posts through the cantonal office of vocational training
  • Apprenticeship normally takes place in 3 or 4 years, depending on the profession
  • Federal professional maturity is compatible with the internship and opens the doors to sup

Key facts

  • What: dual vocational training (company + vocational school + intercompany courses)
  • When: 3- or 4-year internship, with the possibility of parallel professional maturity
  • Where: Canton Uri, cantonal vocational training authority
  • Who: UST/BFS for federal statistics, State Secretariat for Training (SEFRI) for legal bases
  • Amount: the apprentice's salary is set by the employer according to the sector and the year of internship (cantonal reference rates, annual amount to be verified year by year)

Vocational training in Switzerland is based on a federal legal basis, the Vocational Training Act (LFPr), and is divided into three places of learning: the training company, the vocational school and intercompany courses. In the Uri Canton, as in the rest of the Confederation, this dual model allows those who finish compulsory school to choose a qualified professional path directly, without necessarily going through high school. The watchword is permeability: after a federal certificate

Operational details

# Finding an internship: concrete channels

The search for an internship position in Canton Uri passes through some standard channels. The first is the Cantonal Vocational Training Office, which publishes the list of available traineeships every year and accompanies young people in their choice. The second is the national platform Orientamento.ch, which gathers offers from all Swiss training companies. The third is direct contact with companies, often the most effective in smaller cantons such as URIs, where the economic fabric is made up of many SMEs.

A good practice is to start the research one year before the start of the internship. Training companies select candidates in the spring for the beginning of the following summer, although deadlines vary from industry to industry. For the craft and technical sectors — particularly present in a mountain canton such as Uri — spontaneous applications are often well received, especially if accompanied by an updated curriculum vitae and a personalised letter of motivation.

Concrete application procedure: step by step

For those who want to start an apprenticeship in Canton Uri, the realistic path unfolds in six steps.

1. Self-assessment and choice of profession. Consult the official list of available professions at the Cantonal Office of Vocational Education and the guidance tools at orientamento.ch. It is also useful to do a trial internship at a company to test the chosen profession.

2. Active search for an apprenticeship position. Send written applications to companies in the sector, register on the cantonal platform for apprenticeship positions, and contact directly the guidance office of the secondary school.

3. Presentation interview. Present yourself with a portfolio of school assessments, internship certificates, and personal references. Small Uri-based companies often also assess personal motivation and knowledge of the local area.

4. Signing of the apprenticeship contract. Check that the contract specifies: profession, duration, salary, weekly working hours, holidays, vocational school, and the contact person at the company. Keep a copy signed by both parties.

5. Registration at the vocational school. The company normally forwards the data to the school, but the apprentice (or whoever holds parental authority, if a minor) must confirm the registration.

6. Registration with social insurance. The company registers the apprentice with the mandatory insurances: AVS/AHV, accident insurance (LAINF/LAA), AD/AC, and pension fund if the coordinated salary exceeds the LPP/BVG threshold. In Canton Uri as in the rest of Switzerland, anyone who changes canton or begins a gainful activity must also take out mandatory health insurance (LAMal/KVG) within three months.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long is an internship in Canton Uri?
The standard duration is 3 or 4 years depending on the profession and is fixed by the respective federal ordinance on vocational training. The two-year professions (AFP federal certificate of practical training) exist for profiles that require fewer school prerequisites. For the exact duration of the chosen profession, it is advisable to consult the Cantonal Office of Vocational Training of Uri.
How much does an apprentice earn in Switzerland?
The apprentice's salary is not set by a federal minimum wage, because in Switzerland a federal minimum wage does not exist and Uri has not adopted a cantonal minimum wage. The salary is defined by the internship contract and follows in practice the recommendations of the professional associations of the sector. The rates vary considerably by year of internship and profession and must be verified at the updated sectoral source.
Can I attend the professional maturity during the internship?
Yes, the federal professional maturity is compatible with the internship and is attended in parallel. After the AFC, it gives direct access to university vocational schools (SUPs) and, with an additional year of passage, also to universities and polytechnics. In Canton Uri, training is provided by the cantonal vocational school.
What social insurance covers an apprentice?
The apprentice is enrolled by the employer in compulsory insurance: AVS/AHV, accident insurance (LAINF/LAA) and AD/AC. The LPP/BVG obligation is triggered when the coordinated salary exceeds the federal entry threshold. In addition, as a resident, the apprentice must take out compulsory health insurance (LAMal/KVG) within three months of arrival in Switzerland.
