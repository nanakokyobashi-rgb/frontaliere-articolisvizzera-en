AMSA inaugurates the first electric buses in Balerna

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 10 October 2026

On Saturday, October 10, in Balerna, Autolinea Mendrisiense (AMSA) inaugurated its first four electric buses. The event marked the official start of the first phase of the fleet electrification project, launched in 2022, and Piero Marchesi’s public debut as the new Director of the Department of the Territory and State Councillor.

His speech came three days after taking office in the Government. On Wednesday, October 7, Marchesi had been sworn in, thus completing the cantonal Executive. Addressing AMSA representatives and the authorities present, he linked the investment to the prospects for mobility in the Mendrisiotto. For those who use mezzi pubblici, the operational detail primarily concerns the routes involved in the first phase.

For Marchesi, today’s event represents “an important milestone” for AMSA, but also for the Mendrisiotto as a whole and, more broadly, for public transport in Ticino.

The institutional debut

Routes 2, 3 and 4 will receive the four new buses. The inauguration therefore marks the start of a transition based on the gradual replacement of diesel vehicles. The announced program sets 2032 as the end of the transformation of the AMSA fleet.

Marchesi also recalled the company’s history. After almost 120 years, he explained, electric power is returning to regional public transport through modern technology suited to current needs. The reference to the past thus accompanies a choice presented as part of the service’s evolution.

A policy involving multiple operators

The speech broadened the picture to include the other public transport companies active in Ticino. The Canton has approved investments to support the electrification of the fleets of AutoPostale, Trasporti Pubblici Luganesi and Ferrovie Autolinee Regionali Ticinesi. According to Marchesi, the public authority has been working for around fifteen years on the energy transition, reducing consumption and improving process efficiency. The first four AMSA buses, he added, will be followed by others, along with new electric vehicles from other Ticino companies.

Operational details

What it means for network users

The practical point is to distinguish between four buses entering service and the fleet's overall transition. The announcement concerns an initial phase: the electric vehicles are intended for lines 2, 3 and 4, while the replacement of diesel vehicles is described as gradual and included in a programme with a 2032 horizon.

For a resident or worker who uses the mezzi pubblici, this timeline provides a precise reference. The effect to observe is linked to the type of bus used on the lines concerned, not to a general change presented for all public transport in Ticino. The source also links the initiative to sustainability, efficiency and service quality, without turning the inauguration into an announcement about fares or other aspects of mobility.

Before, during and after

The before-and-after comparison is primarily technological. Before the project, the AMSA fleet comprised diesel vehicles destined for gradual replacement. With the inauguration, four electric buses become the first operational core of the transition. In the period that follows, the programme provides for the arrival of additional vehicles until the completion indicated in 2032. The source does not describe simultaneous replacement, but a sequence.

The cantonal dimension emerges from the involvement of AutoPostale, TPL and FART. The Canton's support concerns the electrification of fleets and is presented as part of a broader energy and environmental policy. For the reader, this means also viewing the AMSA case as an indicator of the direction chosen for public transport in Ticino, without automatically extending it to every Swiss network.

The economic profile

Marchesi linked technological progress to the possibility of making the transition to sustainable public transport more economically attractive. The message therefore concerns the relationship between investments, consumption, processes and service quality. No amounts or figures are provided: the concrete element remains the announced electrification process, not a figure to be applied to tickets, taxes or household budgets.

Key points

How to follow the change

To turn the announcement into useful information, readers can follow four steps based on the available data.

A four-step verification

1. Start with the line. If your route uses line 2, 3 or 4, it falls within the first operational area identified for the new electric buses. This verification connects the AMSA project to a specific service, without attributing it indiscriminately to the entire network.

2. Separate the dates. October 10 marks the inauguration of the first four vehicles; 2032, on the other hand, indicates the announced timeline for the program’s completion. The two deadlines describe different phases and should not be conflated.

3. Identify the operator. When discussing electrification in Ticino, it is necessary to distinguish AMSA from AutoPostale, Trasporti Pubblici Luganesi and Ferrovie Autolinee Regionali Ticinesi. The Canton has indicated investments to support several companies, while the specific announcement concerns the first four AMSA vehicles.

4. Read the effects through the indicators cited. The project’s evaluation is based on the energy transition, reduced consumption, process efficiency, sustainability, efficiency and service quality. These are the elements mentioned in the institutional speech.

This procedure avoids two excessive interpretations: considering the fleet replacement already complete or attributing economic effects not described to the inauguration. The verifiable fact remains the launch of a planned phase, accompanied by additional buses announced for AMSA and other Ticino companies.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which lines are served by the new electric buses?
The first four electric buses introduced by AMSA are specifically assigned to service on lines 2, 3 and 4. This inauguration marks the start of the first phase of a broader electrification project, launched in 2022, which envisages the gradual replacement of the diesel vehicles currently operating in the Mendrisiotto area.
When will the transition of the AMSA fleet be completed?
The electrification program announced by Autolinea Mendrisiense (AMSA) envisages completing the fleet transformation by 2032. The process is gradual and aims to progressively replace diesel vehicles with electric vehicles, in line with the objectives of sustainability, process efficiency and reduced energy consumption supported by the Canton.
What is the Canton’s role in this mobility project?
The Canton of Ticino actively supports the electrification of the fleets of various public transport companies, including AMSA, AutoPostale, Trasporti Pubblici Luganesi and Ferrovie Autolinee Regionali Ticinesi. The public authority has been working on the energy transition for about fifteen years, promoting investments aimed at improving the quality of service and the overall efficiency of the regional transport system.
