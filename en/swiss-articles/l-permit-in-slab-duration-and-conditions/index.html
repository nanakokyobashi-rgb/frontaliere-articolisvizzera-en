Operational details

L Permit in Slab: duration and conditions

The conditions of extension of the short-term L permit are described in the regulations of the Canton of Solothurn. In summary, the short-term L permit can be extended for a maximum of 1 year, provided that the permit holder is in possession of an employment contract or an economic activity in the Canton of Solothurn. In addition, the permit holder must demonstrate that they have sufficient insurance coverage.

The regulations of the Canton of Solothurn stipulate that the holder of the short-term L permit must have a monthly income of at least CHF 3,500 in order to obtain the extension of the permit. This requirement is provided for in Article 12 of the Regulation of the Canton of Solothurn, dated 1 January 2020. 📊

For example, if a foreign national has an employment contract of CHF 4,000 per month at a company in Bern, they may be able to obtain the extension of the short-term L permit in the Canton of Solothurn. However, if your monthly income is CHF 2,500, it may not be enough to get the extension.

The permit holder must also demonstrate that they have sufficient insurance coverage. According to the regulations of the Canton of Solothurn, the permit holder must have insurance coverage covering at least CHF 10,000 for accidents and CHF5,000 for illnesses. This requirement is provided for in Article 15 of the Regulation of the Canton of Solothurn, dated 1 January 2020.

Key points

The transition from the short-term L permit to the residence permit is an important process for people wishing to settle in the Canton of Solothurn. As described in the regulations of the Canton of Solothurn, the holder of the short-term L permit may request the change to the residence permit if he is in possession of an employment contract or an economic activity in the Canton of Solothurn and if he has demonstrated that he has sufficient insurance coverage. In addition, the permit holder must demonstrate sufficient knowledge of the official language of the Canton of Solothurn.

For example, if a foreign worker has been hired by a company based in Bern and has an employment contract that provides for a duration of at least 12 months, he can apply for the transition from the short-term L permit to the residence permit. In this case, the worker must prove that he has sufficient insurance coverage, for example a health insurance policy with an amount of at least 30,000 Swiss francs per year.
