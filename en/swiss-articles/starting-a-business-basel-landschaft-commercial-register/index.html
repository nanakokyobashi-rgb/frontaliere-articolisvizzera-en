Practical Analysis

Choosing a legal structure is a crucial step for any entrepreneur looking to start a business in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. This decision will affect not only the minimum capital requirement but also registration fees and insurance obligations. For example, a limited liability company (SRL) requires a minimum capital of CHF 20,000, while a corporation (SA) requires a minimum capital of CHF 100,000. Registration fees vary depending on the legal form and the amount of capital invested. It is also important to consider insurance requirements, which may include liability insurance, employee insurance, and other specific coverage. Additionally, the procedure for registration in the commercial register may vary slightly depending on the chosen legal form. It is essential to thoroughly research these requirements to avoid legal and financial problems in the future.

To start a limited liability company (SRL) in Basel-Landschaft, the entrepreneur must contribute a minimum share capital of CHF 20,000. This capital must be fully paid in at the time the company is incorporated. The registration fees for an LLC amount to approximately CHF 500, including the costs for publication in the Swiss Official Commercial Gazette (FOSC). In addition to the share capital, one must also consider the costs for drafting the articles of incorporation and legal consultation, which can range from CHF 1,000 to CHF 3,000.

For a SA, the minimum required capital is CHF 100,000, of which at least 20% must be paid in at the time of incorporation. Registration fees for an SA are slightly higher than for an SRL, amounting to approximately CHF 1,000. In this case as well, the costs for drafting the articles of incorporation and legal consultation can range from CHF 2,000 to CHF 5,000.

It is important to thoroughly research the specific insurance requirements for the chosen legal form. Additionally, it is advisable to consult a legal expert or tax advisor to ensure that all requirements are met. Finally, you can use online tools to calculate the minimum capital requirement and registration fees, as well as to obtain detailed information on insurance requirements.

Choosing a Legal Structure

Choosing the right legal structure is crucial. In Switzerland, the most common options are the sole proprietorship, the limited liability company (LLC), and the public limited company (AG). For example, for an LLC, the minimum capital requirement is 20,000 CHF, while for a corporation it is 100,000 CHF. The commercial registry fee varies by canton, but in Basel-Landschaft it is generally around 200 CHF.

Documents and Registration Fees

Once you have chosen the legal form, you must prepare the required documents. These include:

  • Articles of Incorporation Prospectus Proof of payment of the registration fee Identification documents of the shareholders

The fee for registration in the commercial register in Basel-Landschaft is approximately 200 CHF. You can pay this fee online through the canton’s commercial register website.
