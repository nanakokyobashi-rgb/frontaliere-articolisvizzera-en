Change of presidency at Rotary Club Lugano (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 26 June 2026

Federico Umberto Mion takes over from Ivo Foletti, with new projects to strengthen the dialogue between generations and the bond with the territory.

Context

In short - Federico Umberto Mion new president of the Rotary Club Lugano - Positive balance of the Rotary year 2025/2026 - New projects for the territory and the young generations - The ceremony took place at Villa Principe Leopoldo ## Key facts - What : Change of presidency at the Rotary Club Lugano - When: 22 June - Where: Villa Principe Leopoldo, Lugano - Who: Ivo Foletti and Federico Umberto Mion - Amount: unspecified The handover to the Rotary Club Lugano took place on the evening of Monday 22 June at Villa Principe Leopoldo. Ivo Foletti, who led the partnership in the Rotary year 2025/2026, announced the rotation with Federico Umberto Mion, new president for 2026/2027. Foletti, in his farewell speech, made an assessment of the year just ended, characterized by the theme "The courage to do business. The responsibility of training those who will come". This course has underlined the importance of entrepreneurship, the economic context of Ticino and the role of training, both academic and professional. Among the main projects mentioned by Foletti, the creation of an outdoor gym in the Municipality of Bignasco stands out, financed also thanks to the fundraising of the "Open Arms" event at the Lido of Lugano. The initiative raised 30,000francs for the local community. Foletti expressed gratitude to the members, committees and partners

Operational details

Practical analysis The change of presidency at Rotary Club Lugano represents an important moment for the territory, as it confirms the association's commitment to fostering local development and training initiatives. The choice to focus on a project such as "Thought Workshop" indicates a desire to strengthen the role of the young generations in the social and economic fabric of the region. At the national level, this type of initiative is part of the broader framework of promoting civic responsibility and civic training activities, which are fundamental for strengthening social capital. The collection of 30,000francs through the "Open Arms" event demonstrates how volunteering and community involvement can generate useful resources for concrete projects. The objective of enhancing memory and experience, as indicated by Mion, translates into a strategic investment in continuity between generations, a crucial element to face the challenges of economic and social change. Collaboration between Rotary, educational institutions, and local communities can also serve as a model for other regions, fostering public-private synergy. For the Swiss context, this example underlines the importance of voluntary networks and civic associations as tools for social strengthening and territorial development. First, you can contact your local club or volunteer association to learn about opportunities for involvement. In addition, schools and training institutions can propose or support projects similar to the "Thought Workshop", creating civic and critical education programs. You can work with Rotary Clubs or other volunteer organizations to organize awareness and fundraising events. For citizens interested in contributing to local development initiatives, it is advisable to monitor the activities of their municipality and local associations, participating in public meetings and supporting projects of collective interest. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the central theme of the Rotary New Year 2026/2027?
The central theme is' Guarding the past, interrogating the present, building the future ', which aims to enhance memory and experience, stimulate awareness of changes and promote concrete actions.
Which major project was mentioned by the previous president?
The main project is the creation of an outdoor gym in the Municipality of Bignasco, financed also thanks to the fundraising of the 'Bracciate Aperte' event in Lugano, which raised 30,000 francs.
How can a citizen participate in Rotary Club Lugano initiatives?
You can contact your local club, attend events, support training or volunteer projects, and use online tools like Rotary's membership and donation site.
What is the objective of the 'Thought Workshop' project?
The objective is to develop critical thinking, rhetorical skills and quality of dialogue among young people, with a pilot project in the schools of Lugano, which aims to become a replicable model.
What are the priorities indicated by the new president Mion?
The priorities are to enhance memory and experience, stimulate an aware reading of the changes and translate the discussion into concrete actions for the territory.
