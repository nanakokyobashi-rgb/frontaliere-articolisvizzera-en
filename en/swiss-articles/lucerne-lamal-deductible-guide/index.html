The KVG is mandatory for Swiss residents and must be concluded within three months of arrival. For 2026, the starting point for those living in the canton of Lucerne is not a single national figure: premiums are defined by canton and region. The comparison should therefore be read in the correct territorial category and updated with the official reference of the year.

Why Lucerne should be read by region

The coverage is private and financed by a per capita premium. It is not a tax and it is not a salary contribution. This distinction affects the reading of the cost of living: the KVG premium is not calculated as a percentage of the paycheck and does not coincide with direct federal tax. AHV/AHV and BVG/BVG are not the same item either: they are salary-related contributions, while health insurance follows the logic of the per capita premium.

For an adult, the choice of deductible includes six levels: CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500. In the practical comparison, deductible and premium must be read together, keeping the canton or region fixed. This avoids comparing offers built on different bases. It is not correct to transform the list of deductibles into a national ranking or to assign the canton of Lucerne a single amount without the official value of 2026.

The premium reduction is the second step to be checked: the subsidy is cantonal. If you live in Lucerne, you must therefore look for the applicable cantonal indication and keep it separate from taxes, AHV/AHV and other salary levies. A premium reduction does not change the per capita nature of the insurance and is not an item of direct federal tax.

Within the institutional scope, FTA/ESTV deals with IFD and VAT; the cantonal administrations concern the cantonal and municipal level. FSIO/BSV concerns social security and does not set taxes, while FSO/BFS produces statistics and does not set rates. These references help not to look for the KVG premium in tax or statistical data.

Finally, the cost of living picture must be separated from the minimum wage: there is no federal minimum wage, while some cantons have their own. For the Lucerne reader, the KVG entry remains autonomous and must be compared with homogeneous parameters. To deepen the comparison between coverages, guida LAMal e cassa malati is available.

The method to compare Lucerne

The national comparison is only useful if it does not erase the difference between canton and region. An average KVG premium can offer a general reference, but the average figures are indexed every year: for 2026, the value to be used is the current official value. For the canton of Lucerne, the decisive step is therefore to identify the correct territorial category before judging the premium level.

This criterion also changes the way the deductible is assessed. Without comparable official values for 2026, it is not possible to indicate an automatically optimal choice for every adult. The options must be compared within the same area and the proposal considered. If the territory and deductible are changed at the same time, the result does not show which of the two explains the difference. The analysis must isolate one variable at a time.

Key points

Operating procedure for 2026

Five steps to follow

  • 1) If the obligation arises from an arrival in Switzerland, note the date and arrange for the conclusion of the contract within three months. The deadline concerns compulsory insurance for residents.
  • 2) Identify the territorial perimeter: for Lucerne, use the value referring to the correct canton or region. Do not replace it with a national average premium.
  • 3) Choose the adult deductible from the levels provided and keep the same choice in each comparison. In this way, the reading remains homogeneous.
  • 4) Check the premium reduction on the cantonal channel indicated by the official source. Keep it separate from taxes, AHV/AHV, LPP/BVG and other wage contributions.
  • 5) If you are considering a change of cash register, compare the new and current proposal with the same canton or region and deductible. The three-month deadline should not be used as a general term for the change: follow the official procedure.

The final check concerns the year. The average KVG premiums are among the figures indexed each year; the 2026 value must therefore be taken from the updated official source. Do not use an amount found for another year, nor transform a national average into a certain price for Lucerne. The same caution is needed when calculating the cost of living: the per capita premium is an independent item, not a percentage of the salary.

For the rest of the budget, it separates the premium from taxation and social security. Direct federal tax and VAT belong to the tax perimeter; AHV/AHV and BVP/BVG are linked to salary contributions. The comparison becomes clearer if four data points are noted: territory, deductible, official premium 2026 and verification of the cantonal reduction. A consistent calculation requires constant criteria, not an average taken from another year or canton.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which LAMal deductibles can an adult choose in 2026?
The deductibles indicated for adults are CHF 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500. The choice should be read together with the premium applied to the area of the canton of Lucerne and the proposal under consideration. To indicate the most advantageous option, the official 2026 values by canton or region are needed; it is not correct to replace them with a national ranking.
When must LAMal be taken out after arriving in Switzerland?
Health insurance is mandatory for residents and must be taken out within three months of arrival. The deadline concerns the start of coverage after arrival and must not automatically be turned into a general deadline for every change of health insurance fund. For those living in the canton of Lucerne, the premium to be compared remains the one provided for the canton or reference region.
Is the LAMal premium a tax or a deduction from salary?
No. LAMal is private insurance with per-capita premiums: the source distinguishes it from both taxes and wage contributions. It should therefore not be confused with direct federal tax, AVS/AHV or LPP/BVG. The federal minimum wage, moreover, does not exist; some cantons have their own minimum wage, but this does not turn the health insurance premium into a deduction from wages.
How is the premium reduction verified in the canton of Lucerne?
The reduction in premiums is cantonal. For a person residing in the canton of Lucerne, the check must be carried out through the canton’s official channel, keeping it separate from the comparison of the per-capita premium and from taxes. The reference for 2026 must be the official one for that year and comply with the applicable cantonal guidelines.
