Context

In short - Obligation of fiscal monitoring for foreign accounts held by residents in Italy. - The RW framework of the Income model must be completed for financial assets. - VAT applies to average stocks above 5,000 euros. - Failure to declare results in administrative fines. ## Key facts - What: Tax monitoring obligation (RW framework). - When: Annually, when filing a tax return. - Where: Italy, for accounts held with institutions in Switzerland. - Who: Revenue Agency, for taxpayers resident in Italy. - Amount: VAT due if the average stock exceeds 5,000 euros. Tax monitoring is a fundamental pillar for taxpayers residing in Italy who have financial relationships with banking institutions abroad. Pursuant to current legislation, every citizen tax resident in Italy is required to report their financial assets held abroad, including current accounts opened with Swiss banks. This obligation is expressed through the compilation of the RW framework within the Personal Income form, an essential document to communicate to the Revenue Agency the size of the foreign financial assets. Frequently Asked Questions
Should the Swiss account always be declared in the RW framework?
Yes, residents in Italy who hold financial assets abroad, including current accounts in Switzerland, have a tax monitoring obligation. This obligation exists regardless of the stock, even if the IVAFE applies only to the exceeding of certain annual average thresholds.
What is the threshold for payment of IVAFE?
VAT, the tax on the value of financial assets held abroad, is due if the average annual inventory of the current account exceeds 5,000 euros. In case of exceeding, the tax is applied on the value of the financial assets according to the rates in force.
What happens if I forget to declare the account in the RW framework?
Omission in the RW framework results in administrative fines, ranging from 3% to 15% of the undeclared amounts. You can regularize your position through diligent repentance, paying a reduced penalty before the tax authorities initiate an investigation.
