  • What: Tax monitoring obligation (RW framework).
  • When: Annually, when filing a tax return.

Pursuant to current legislation, every citizen tax resident in Italy is required to report their financial assets held abroad, including current accounts opened with Swiss banks. This obligation is expressed through the compilation of the RW framework within the Personal Income form, an essential document to communicate to the Revenue Agency the size of the foreign financial assets. Operational details

Mandatory Analysis of Fiscal Scenarios

The compilation of the RW chart requires meticulous precision, as any error or omission can trigger audit procedures. In a hypothetical scenario, a border worker with a current account at a Swiss bank must accurately calculate the maximum and average balance of the tax period. These data, extractable from the annual account statements provided by the credit institution, form the basis for the correct presentation of values. A key distinction is the threshold for exemption from the IVAFE payment: if the average balance of the account does not exceed 5,000 euros over the year, the taxpayer is exempt from paying the tax, but may still have the obligation to monitor. However, the 5,000 euro threshold is not the only parameter; it is also necessary to consider the peak maximum balance reached during the year. If the account has a significant movement, the exemption threshold may be exceeded, making it mandatory to calculate and pay the IVAFE. The comparison with the situation before the introduction of the automatic exchange of information highlights a radical change: today, the Revenue Agency has advanced technological tools to cross-reference the data received from foreign banks with the declarations presented by taxpayers. Therefore, the omission of the RW chart has become an easily detectable conduct, exposing the taxpayer to administrative sanctions that vary based on the gravity of the omission and the cooperation provided. Another practical implication concerns the management of currency exchange: the sums held in Swiss francs must be converted into euros at the time of compiling the RW chart, using the official exchange rates published for the reference period. Procedure for Filing and Deadlines

The procedure for fulfilling the obligations of fiscal monitoring follows the ordinary timelines set by the Italian fiscal calendar. The taxpayer must proceed with the completion of the Redditi model or, where applicable, integrate the 730 model with the RW table. It is crucial to pay attention to the codes to be inserted in the specific columns of the RW table, which identify the nature of the activity and the type of financial relationship. The first operational step is to obtain the annual balance sheet extract by 31 December, which contains the detail of the average balance and the final balance. Subsequently, the value to be indicated in euros is calculated. For those who need a clear understanding of their obligations, it is useful to use the calculator to understand how the fiscal burden intersects with the net monthly income. In the filing phase, the failure to indicate a Swiss bank account in the RW table may result in penalties ranging from 3% to 15% of the undeclared amounts, percentages that can significantly increase if the account is held in countries considered to have privileged taxation, although Switzerland is not currently in such a category. The rectification of any omissions can be done through the operative rectification, which allows the position to be corrected by paying a reduced penalty, provided that the violation has not already been the subject of an audit. It is always advisable to consult a professional authorized to manage the complexities of the RW table, especially in the presence of multiple accounts or diversified investments. Frequently Asked Questions
Should the Swiss account always be declared in the RW framework?
Yes, residents in Italy who hold financial assets abroad, including current accounts in Switzerland, have a tax monitoring obligation. This obligation exists regardless of the stock, even if the IVAFE applies only to the exceeding of certain annual average thresholds.
What is the threshold for payment of IVAFE?
VAT, the tax on the value of financial assets held abroad, is due if the average annual inventory of the current account exceeds 5,000 euros. In case of exceeding, the tax is applied on the value of the financial assets according to the rates in force.
What happens if I forget to declare the account in the RW framework?
Omission in the RW framework results in administrative fines, ranging from 3% to 15% of the undeclared amounts. You can regularize your position through diligent repentance, paying a reduced penalty before the tax authorities initiate an investigation.
