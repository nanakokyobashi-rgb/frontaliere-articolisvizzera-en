Operational details

Fraudulent calls on behalf of authorities have been a problem for a long time in Switzerland. The Federal Office of Cybersecurity has worked to reduce the number of these calls. The requirement to mark calls from abroad with spoofed Swiss numbers was an important step in achieving this goal. From January to June 2026, over 400 reports per month of attempted phone scams on behalf of the authorities were regularly recorded. In July there were less than one hundred. This means that the obligation to dial calls has had a positive effect.

In the Canton of Ticino, for example, over 150 telephone scams were reported on behalf of the authorities in January 2026. While only 20 phone scams were reported in July. This represents a reduction of 87% compared to the same period last year.

According to experts, the requirement to mark calls has been a key step in reducing the number of phone scams. “Calls coming from spoofed numbers are much easier to recognize as scams,” explains a representative of the Federal Office of Cybersecurity. “Swiss citizens are more aware of not answering these calls and not providing personal information.”

However, it is important to continue to be cautious and follow safety measures to protect yourself and your family. Some of the safety measures you can take include:

  • Do not answer unknown calls or calls from unrecognized numbers.
