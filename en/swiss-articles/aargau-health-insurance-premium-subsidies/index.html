Health Insurance Premiums in the Canton of Aargau and Premium Reductions (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 2 September 2026

Find out how health insurance premiums work in Aargau, what deductibles are available, and how to apply for a reduction through cantonal subsidies.

Context

In brief

  • Mandatory health insurance, premiums vary by canton in Switzerland
  • Standard adult deductibles range from CHF 300 to CHF 2500 annually
  • Premium reductions (subsidies) managed by the canton based on income

Key facts

  • What: Mandatory health insurance (LAMal) for Swiss residents
  • When: Must be taken out within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland
  • Where: Canton of Aargau (and all Swiss cantons)
  • Who: Cantonal administration manages subsidies
  • Amount: Deductibles from CHF 300 to CHF 2500 annually for adults

Mandatory health insurance, known as LAMal, is an essential part of life in Switzerland for every resident. In the Canton of Aargau, as in all Swiss cantons, premiums vary based on multiple factors, and the canton provides premium reduction mechanisms to ensure coverage access for those with limited resources.

How LAMal works in Switzerland

LAMal is a private insurance system managed by authorized health insurance companies. Unlike most European healthcare systems, it is not a tax or salary contribution but a per capita premium insurance. This means that every person, regardless of age or income, must take out an individual policy with a health insurance company of their choice. In Aargau, as in every canton, the resident has three months from arrival to subscribe to the insurance, otherwise, an administrative penalty applies.

Premiums are calculated based on three main factors: the cantonal territory, age, and the chosen insurance model. Regarding the territory, the Canton of Aargau applies its own premium bands, which vary based on the regional healthcare costs of the area. The age of the policyholder directly affects the premium: children (up to 18 years) and young adults (up to 25 years) pay reduced premiums, while adults and seniors see their contributions increase.

Operational details

How Premium Reductions Work in the Canton of Aargau

Premium reductions, commonly known as subsidies, are contributions provided by the canton to help residents with lower incomes cover the cost of LAMal. Unlike premiums, which are managed privately by health insurance providers, subsidies fall exclusively under the jurisdiction of cantonal administrations. In the Canton of Aargau, the relevant cantonal office assesses each applicant’s financial situation and determines the amount of the reduction based on specific criteria such as annual income, assets, and household composition.

Reductions are not automatic: residents must apply to the relevant cantonal authority. The amount of the reduction varies considerably from canton to canton and, within the same canton, from case to case. In general, subsidies are reserved for individuals or households with incomes below certain thresholds, which vary annually and are indexed to changes in the cost of living. The canton also takes into account available liquid assets, applying an exemption threshold beyond which assets are considered in the assessment of ability to pay.

Eligibility Criteria and Application Procedure

To obtain a premium reduction in the Canton of Aargau, the applicant must meet two fundamental criteria: reside in the canton and have a financial situation that falls within the established thresholds. The canton considers not only gross income but also any social deductions, family expenses, and significant medical expenses already incurred.

Key points

How to Apply for a Premium Reduction in Aargau: A Practical Guide

Residents of the Canton of Aargau who are having difficulty paying their KVG premiums may apply for a reduction. The first step is to ensure that you have properly enrolled in health insurance; those who are not yet covered have three months from the date they move into the canton to register with a health insurance provider. Once in possession of their policy number, the applicant should contact the relevant cantonal office in Aargau responsible for premium reductions (usually called the Health Insurance Office or something similar).

The cantonal administration provides the applicant with an application form, which is generally also available online on the canton’s official website. The form requires detailed information: personal details, KVG policy number, total annual household income, liquid assets, and dependents (children, cohabitants, elderly dependents). The application must be accompanied by a copy of the most recent tax return, pay stubs from previous months if applicable, and any other documentation supporting the declared financial situation.

Timeline and Validity of the Reduction

The application may be submitted at any time of the year, but the reduction usually takes effect the month following the month of submission, unless otherwise specified by the canton. Reductions are generally valid for one calendar year and must be renewed annually. During the period between submitting the application and approval (which normally takes a few weeks), the applicant must continue to pay the full premium; the reduction applies retroactively only if the application is approved, with a refund of any overpaid amounts.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does LAMal mean, and why is it mandatory?
LAMal is the acronym for 'Health Insurance Act'. In Switzerland, every resident must take out health insurance within three months of arrival in the country. It is required by federal law and privately operated by licensed health funds. LAMal covers basic medical care, medication, hospitalization and other essential treatments. It is a per capita premium insurance system, not a tax, and costs vary depending on the canton, age and model chosen.
What deductibles are available in the Canton of Aargau, and how do they affect the premium?
In the canton of Aargau, as in the whole of Switzerland, there are six standard allowances for adults: CHF 300, 500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000 and 2,500 per year. The deductible represents the amount the patient pays out of pocket before the insurance begins to cover medical costs. A higher deductible results in a lower monthly premium, while a lower deductible corresponds to a higher premium. The choice depends on personal medical spending habits and financial possibilities.
How do you apply for a premium reduction in Aargau, and what are the requirements?
To request the premium reduction in Aargau, the resident contacts the competent cantonal office and fills in the application form provided by the administration. The main requirements are: income within the thresholds established annually by the canton, residence in the Canton of Aargau and regular subscription to the LAMal. The application must be accompanied by documentation certifying the income (tax return, pay slips) and the composition of the household. Approval usually takes a few weeks.
Is the premium reduction retroactive, and how is it paid out?
The premium reduction generally starts from the month following the submission of the application and approval. If the canton approves the application, it reimburses the sums paid in excess as a premium in the period between the submission of the application and the approval. The amount of the reduction is transferred monthly or annually to the sick fund, reducing the premium due, or paid directly to the applicant according to the procedures provided by the canton.
