Operational details

Three questions before you travel

First: does the pharmacy appear in the address directory? Second: does its record publish hours? Third: is there verified duty for its area and the stated interval? Only the third answer concerns duty; the other two do not replace it.

Open the correct route, check the snapshot retrieval time and the named source, then call the pharmacy. The page does not automatically interpret a record as open now and does not extend duty coverage to the Italian provinces.

Directory, opening hours and duty: three different data points

  • directory: A directory record means that the official catalogue contains the entry; it does not mean that the pharmacy is open now.
  • opening hours: Opening hours are shown only when the page or source publishes them; they are not inferred from the directory or its timestamp.
  • verified duty: Verified duty means a regional interval published by OFCT or the relevant association source; it is not the same as continuous opening. Check by phone before travelling.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does a pharmacy directory show which branch is open now?
No. The directory identifies locations and contacts; hours appear only when published, and duty must come from the relevant regional source.
What makes duty verified?
It is a regional interval published by the relevant source for the five covered areas: Mendrisiotto, Luganese, Bellinzonese, Biasca e Valli and Locarnese.
Does this guide cover all of Italy?
No. The Italian catalogue is limited to provinces CO, VA and VB and makes no Italian on-duty claim.
