Di Marco Ferrari · Published on 23 July 2026

The new Chairman of the Board of Directors Andrea Bersani distances himself from the past with regard to pressures for hiring.

Context

The new Chairman of the Board of Directors Andrea Bersani stated that there is no reason to take a position on the exchange of emails between State Councillor Claudio Zali and the then Chairman of the EoC Board of Directors, Paolo Sanvido. Bersani said Sanvido did not follow up on any pressure.

The case of the EoC (Eidgenössische Oberschulverwaltung) has raised a number of questions and debates in the Swiss financial landscape. The EoC, which manages Swiss high schools, has recently faced a number of disputes related to resource management and the transparency of financial operations. The issue of the exchange of emails between Zali and Sanvido was particularly relevant, as it raised doubts about the management of resources and the transparency of decisions.

Zali, who was a State Councillor for the Swiss Confederation, accused Sanvido of lobbying for recruitment into the EoC. Sanvido, on the other hand, denied any form of pressure and argued that his decisions were based on criteria of competence and meritocracy.

The case had significant implications for the management of Swiss high schools. The EoC recently published a series of audit reports that examined financial operations and resource management. Operational details

The EoC said it would cooperate fully with relevant bodies, such as the Public Prosecutor's Office, if an investigation was opened into an alleged abuse of power. He also pointed out that the abuse of power is an ex officio punishable offence.

The EoC, the Human Resource Management Entity, has recently declared its willingness to cooperate with the competent authorities in the event of investigations relating to the abuse of power. This is particularly relevant in a context where the EoC has recently addressed issues related to human resource management and transparency in decisions.

The EoC stressed that abuse of power is an ex officio punishable offence. This means that, in the event of suspected abuse of power, the competent authorities can take legal action to investigate and, if necessary, prosecute the offence. This is an important step in ensuring transparency and accountability in organizations.

For example, in the canton of Ticino, the EoC collaborated with the Public Prosecutor's Office in a series of investigations concerning the management of human resources in some public institutions. These investigations have led to significant improvements in human resource management and helped reduce instances of abuse of power.

In another example, in 2021, the EoC collaborated with the Zurich Public Prosecutor's Office in a case of abuse of power within a public institution.
