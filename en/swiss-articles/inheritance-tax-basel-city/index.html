The inheritance and gift tax in the Canton of Basel-City is a cantonal tax that affects the transfer of assets free of charge, both by inheritance and by donation. Unlike imposta federale diretta, che è uniforme in tutta la Svizzera, inheritance and gift taxes are governed entirely by cantonal laws: each Swiss canton has its own tax regimes, with rates and exemptions defined by its own legislation.

Operational details

Rates, exemptions and degrees of kinship in the Canton Basel-City

In the Canton of Basel-City, as in the rest of Switzerland, taxation is divided into stages according to the degree of kinship. The main grades are organized as follows:

First Degree (Spouse and Children): The surviving spouse generally benefits from substantial tax protection. In many Swiss cantons, including the Canton of Basel-City, the spouse enjoys a total exemption or a significantly reduced rate. Similarly, children and direct descendants benefit from very low rates or partial exemptions, reflecting their order of priority in Swiss civil law.

Second degree (parents, brothers, sisters): Relatives in the second degree of kinship are subject to moderate rates, higher than those reserved for the spouse and children, but lower than those of the most distant degrees.

Third grade (grandparents, uncles, cousins) and higher grades: These subjects benefit from an even higher rate. Persons without kinship (friends, acquaintances, strangers) are subject to the maximum rates provided for by cantonal law.

To consult the exact rates applicable in the Canton of Basel-City, it is essential to contact the cantonal tax administration directly, as these rates may be subject to legislative changes and vary not only by degree of kinship, but also by amount brackets.

Procedure: How to Declare an Inheritance in the City of Basel

If you have been appointed as an heir or have received an inheritance in the City of Basel, you must follow a formal procedure to fulfill your tax obligations. Here are the main steps to follow:

Phase 1: Gather Essential Documents

Obtain the following documents:

  • Original will (if exists) or certificate of intestate succession
  • Death certificate of the deceased (extract from the register)
  • Documents related to the inherited assets (bank statements, stock certificates, real estate deeds, insurance policies)
  • Documentation regarding debts or liabilities of the inheritance (mortgages, credits, funeral expenses)
  • Marriage or birth certificates to prove the degree of kinship

Phase 2: Contact the Cantonal Tax Administration

Contacting the City of Basel's tax office is a crucial step. Reach out to the administration to:

  • Request the official forms for the inheritance declaration
  • Clarify the submission deadlines (usually within a few months of the death, but verify exactly)
  • Obtain specific information on the applicable tax rates for your degree of kinship
  • Understand if you are entitled to exemptions or reliefs

Phase 3: Complete the Inheritance Declaration

Fill out the official form, providing:

  • Personal details of the deceased (name, date of death, last residence)
  • Complete list of heirs and legatees with indication of the degree of kinship
  • Detailed inventory of all assets (active and passive)
  • Market value of each asset at the time of death
  • Total value of the inheritance and taxable base

Frequently Asked Questions
Are donations taxed in the same way as inheritances in the Canton of Basel-City?
Donations are generally subject to taxation in the Canton of Basel-City according to the principle of the degree of kinship, similar to succession. However, the terms of declaration, procedures and methods of payment may differ significantly. Contact the cantonal tax office for the exact procedures and to understand how the donations received in your specific case will be treated from a tax point of view.
Is the surviving spouse always exempt from inheritance tax in the Canton of Basel-City?
Many Swiss cantons, including the Canton of Basel-City, grant special protection to the surviving spouse, which may result in a total exemption or a significant reduction in the rate. However, the exact modalities, amount limits and conditions for benefiting from the exemption depend on the cantonal legislation in force. Check the current arrangements at the tax office to understand what protection your spouse is entitled to in your case.
What are the exact terms for declaring a succession in the Canton of Basel-City?
The terms for the declaration of succession vary according to cantonal legislation and may be subject to change. Generally, the return must be filed within a few months of death, but it is essential to verify the exact timeframe and manner of filing with the cantonal tax administration. Failure to meet deadlines results in penalties and default interest, so please contact the office as soon as possible.
What happens if I do not file the succession declaration within the established deadlines?
Failure to comply with the deadlines for the declaration of succession in the Canton of Basel-City entails administrative sanctions, default interest on the amount due and possible compulsory recovery proceedings of the unpaid tax. To avoid negative consequences, it is vital to file the return promptly and contact the tax office as soon as possible after death.
Who can help me in filling out the declaration of succession in the Basel-City Canton?
You can contact notaries, accountants or tax consultants specialising in inheritance law. The cantonal tax administration provides forms and general information, but not personalised legal assistance. A specialized professional will help you correctly calculate the tax, accurately fill out the required documentation and optimize the tax management of the inheritance.
