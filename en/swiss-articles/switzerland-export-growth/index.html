According to data from the Federal Statistical Office (FSO), Swiss exports increased by 10% in 2022 compared to the previous year. This represents an increase of CHF 12.3 billion. This positive trend occurs in a context of increasing global demand and recovering markets.

Export growth is particularly evident in the mechanical and electronics industries. Swiss products are in demand all over the world for their quality and innovation. According to FSO estimates, the mechanical industry saw an increase in exports of 15% in 2022, while electronics saw an increase of 12%.
