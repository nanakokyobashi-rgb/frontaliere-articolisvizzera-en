Employment in Switzerland: +0.8% in Q2 2026 (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 18 August 2026

In the second quarter of 2026, employment in Switzerland grew by 0.8% while the ILO unemployment rate rose to 4.9% according to UST data.

Context

In a nutshell

  • Total employment at 5.406 million, up 0.8%
  • ILO unemployment rate increased from 4.6% to 4.9%
  • Full-time equivalents (FTE) up 0.6%
  • Stable European comparison with 5.9% in the European Union

Key facts

  • What: Swiss Labour Force Survey (RIFOS)
  • When: Second quarter of 2026
  • Where: Switzerland
  • Who: Federal Statistical Office (FSO)
  • Amount: 5,406 million people employed

During the second quarter of 2026, the labour market trend in Switzerland recorded important dynamics disseminated by the Federal Statistical Office (FSO) through the Swiss Labour Force Survey (RIFOS). According to the press release published on August 18, 2026, the total number of people employed in Switzerland reached 5.406 million, marking an increase of 0.8% compared to the same period of the previous year. This evolution highlights the strength of the national economic fabric, although different variations are observed in the different parameters examined by federal statistical analysts.

Operational details

The situation of unemployment according to the ILO

A central chapter of the RIFOS investigation deals with unemployment calculated according to the criteria of the International Labour Office (ILO). In the second quarter of 2026, there were 252,000 unemployed people in Switzerland, a figure that corresponds to 15,000 more people than in the same period of the previous year. Consequently, the unemployment rate according to the ILO rose from 4.6 to 4.9%. This increase reflects a dynamics of greater mobility within the national labor market, which deserves a careful analysis by economic observers and competent authorities.

The comparison with European Union and neighboring countries

The Swiss context is constantly compared with the international and community one. In the second quarter of 2026, the unemployment rate in the European Union remained stable at 5.9%, positioning itself at a higher level than the 4.9% recorded in Switzerland. If we observe the main neighboring countries to the Confederation, the situation presents significant and varied differences. In Germany, the unemployment rate passed from 3.7 to 3.9%. In France, an increase was recorded from 7.2 to 7.9%. On the other hand, Italy showed a decrease, passing from 6.5 to 5.9%. Finally, in Austria the rate was set at 5.6%, recording a slight increase compared to the previous 5.5%. This geographical panorama demonstrates how Switzerland maintains overall favorable performances compared to the European average and most of the neighbors, even recording internal variations.
