The level of the consultation

There are four federal voting dates scheduled each year: this is the first reference point to keep separate when consulting a guide to cantonal voting in the Canton of Lucerne. The federal calendar provides the national framework; the date and instructions for the cantonal vote should be read in the Canton’s official information.

The second step is to identify the instrument. Within the federal framework, a popular initiative requires 100'000 signatures collected in 18 months, while an optional referendum requires 50'000 signatures in 100 days. These are federal thresholds and deadlines. They should therefore not be used automatically to describe the cantonal procedure: for Lucerne, the applicable rule is the one given in the official information about the vote.

If the proposal amends the Constitution, the indicated federal framework provides for a double majority: both the people and the Cantons must vote in favor. This criterion must also be read together with the level of the vote, because a cantonal guide must distinguish the applicable instrument from the general federal rule.

To interpret the content, it is useful to link each topic to the competent administration. The Swiss tax system has three levels: direct federal tax, cantonal taxes and municipal taxes. The AFC/ESTV is responsible for direct federal tax and VAT; the cantonal administrations oversee cantonal and municipal taxation. In municipalities, the multiplier is applied to the cantonal tax.

The same criterion avoids other misunderstandings. The UST/BFS produces statistics and does not set tax rates. The UFAS/BSV concerns social security, including AVS/AHV, AI and LPP/BVG, and does not set taxes. The LAMal/KVG, on the other hand, is compulsory health insurance for residents: it must be taken out within three months of arrival, has per-capita premiums and is neither a tax nor a wage contribution.

To assess the possible impact on a family, reading the question can be supplemented with the guide on costo della vita in Svizzera. The tool helps organize expenditure items; the voting decision must remain anchored in the text and the official information about the vote.

Operational details

Practical effects to distinguish

A guide to a cantonal vote is more useful when it connects the subject of the consultation to the rule that the reader needs to verify. Not all matters that affect daily life belong to the same level: a tax item, an insurance premium, an employment relationship and a rent follow different frameworks.

The subject-matter grid

| Subject | Checkpoint | | Fisco | The AFC/ESTV administers direct federal tax and VAT; for cantonal and municipal taxation, the cantonal administrations are authoritative. The municipality applies its own multiplier to cantonal tax. | | LAMal/KVG | For residents, it is mandatory and must be taken out within three months of arrival. The comparison prevents reading an item of healthcare expenditure as if it were automatically a tax. LAMal premiums depend on the canton and region, while premium reduction is a cantonal subsidy; adult deductibles are indicated at levels of CHF 300, 500, 1'000, 1'500, 2'000 and 2'500. The distinction matters because LAMal is not a contribution withheld from wages.

For a work-related topic, the practical question is whether it concerns a federal rule or a cantonal minimum wage; the absence of a federal minimum does not automatically become valid information for every Canton. For housing, by contrast, the federal nature of tenancy rules coexists with an official cantonal form for the landlord's termination notice. The procedure to follow therefore depends on the precise subject, not merely on the fact that the vote is being held in Lucerne.

The same applies to social assistance and the public services mentioned. Their administration is generally cantonal or municipal, and the conditions must be verified with the competent office. If the topic concerns health insurance premiums, confronto LAMal e cassa malati can help interpret the expenditure item without confusing it with payroll contributions.

Key points

Practical procedure before voting

Five checks before casting your vote

1. Start with the official calendar. Look for the information from the Canton of Lucerne concerning the consultation and note the date given for the cantonal vote. The federal and cantonal calendars should be kept separate. 2. Identify the instrument. Mark whether the text concerns an initiative or referendum. Federal thresholds and deadlines should not be automatically applied to Lucerne: use the rules specified for that level. 3. Verify who is entitled to vote. Use the official guidance linked to the vote to verify who may vote and how the procedure is described. Do not fill in missing data with generic rules. 4. Determine the area of responsibility. For taxes, distinguish direct federal tax, cantonal taxes and municipal taxes; for VAT and IFD, consider AFC/ESTV; for AVS/AHV, AI and LPP/BVG, consider UFAS/BSV. UST/BFS is used for statistics, not tax rates. 5. Check the correct deadline. The three-month period under LAMal runs from arrival; the 30 days concern challenging the termination of a lease. These are deadlines distinct from the official voting date and must not be confused.

A simple method is to note three columns: fact reported in the official information, competent level and practical consequence to be checked. The first column includes the wording of the question and the calendar; the second, the federal, cantonal or municipal administration indicated by the subject matter; the third, any item concerning taxes, premiums, work, rent or public service. This sheet prevents using a UST/BFS statistic as a tax rate or attributing the setting of a tax to UFAS/BSV.

If the consultation concerns taxes, dichiarazione delle imposte may serve to link the subject matter to your own situation, but it does not replace the official instructions. If it concerns a cantonal competence, requirements, deadlines and amounts must be verified with the Canton’s official source, as required for services administered at that level.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the signature thresholds for federal initiatives?
A federal grassroots initiative requires 100,000 signatures collected in 18 months. For an optional federal referendum, the threshold is 50,000 signatures to be collected in 100 days. These are federal thresholds and terms that should not be automatically transferred to the cantonal level of Lucerne, where the specific rules reported in the official information of the consultation apply.
How is the federal vote different from the cantonal vote?
The federal calendar provides for four voting dates per year, offering the national framework. For cantonal voting in the Canton of Lucerne, the date and instructions of the consultation shall be read exclusively in the official information of the Canton. It is essential to keep the two calendars separate and check the specific institution (initiative or referendum) indicated in the official source to avoid misunderstandings.
Who handles taxation and what are the levels?
Swiss taxation has three levels: direct federal tax, cantonal taxes and municipal taxes. The AFC/ESTV deals with direct federal tax and VAT. Cantonal administrations follow cantonal and municipal taxation, where the multiplier is applied to the cantonal tax. UST/BFS produces statistics but does not set rates, while UFAS/BSV concerns social security and not taxes.
What are the terms for LAMal and rent cancellation?
LAMal is mandatory for residents and must be stipulated within three months of arrival. It has premiums per capita and is not a tax. For the lease, the cancellation of the landlord requires the official cantonal form and the dispute must be made within 30 days to the conciliation authority. These terms are subject-specific and should not be interchanged with the official date of the vote.
What does the federal constitutional amendment provide for?
If a proposal amends the Constitution, the federal framework provides for a double majority: both the people and the Cantons must be favourable. This criterion must be read together with the grade level, since a cantonal guide must distinguish the applicable institution from the general federal rule in order to correctly interpret the content of the consultation.
