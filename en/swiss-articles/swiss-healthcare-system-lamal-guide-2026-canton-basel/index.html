LAMal: what it is and how it works

LAMal is a mandatory health insurance that covers all residents in Switzerland. The law was introduced in 1996 and requires all Swiss and foreign citizens to have valid health insurance. LAMal covers medical expenses, including doctors, hospitals, medicines and other health services.

When and how to join LAMal

LAMal registration is mandatory for all residents in Switzerland. This means that they must be registered within 3 months of arrival in Switzerland. The registration procedure varies depending on the canton in which you reside. Some cantons, such as the Canton of Ticino, require registration via the sickness fund website, while others, such as the Canton of Zurich, require the presentation of personal documents at a sickness fund office.

The Swiss healthcare system

The Swiss health system is based on a model of compulsory insurance, called LAMal (Federal Health Insurance Act). LAMal requires all Swiss citizens to be insured against healthcare costs. Health coverage is provided by one of the 138 authorized health insurance funds, which offer different services and different rates.
