Festival Zurich: the golden eye award goes to Luna Wedler in Switzerland (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 7 September 2026

The Swiss actress receives the award for being the first Swiss actress to win the Golden Eye Award.

Context

In brief

  • Luna Wedler receives the Golden Eye Award in Zurich
  • Wedler is the first Swiss actress to win the award
  • Wedler is the youngest recipient of the Golden Eye Award
  • Wedler will be awarded during the festival's opening ceremony
  • Wedler is featured in two films presented at the festival

Key facts

  • What: Awarding of the Golden Eye Award to Luna Wedler
  • When: September 24, 2024
  • Where: Zurich
  • Who: Christian Jungen (CEO of the festival), Elisabeth Baume-Schneider (federal councilor)

Luna Wedler will receive the Golden Eye Award of the Zurich Film Festival (ZFF) for being the first Swiss actress to receive this recognition. At 26 years old, she will also be the youngest recipient of the award. The announcement was made by the festival's CEO, Christian Jungen, in a press release today. Wedler will be awarded during the festival's opening ceremony on September 24 by federal councilor Elisabeth Baume-Schneider, responsible for the Federal Department of the Interior.

Wedler is featured in two films that will be presented in Zurich: as an actress in "Eurotrash" and as a voice actress in the animated film "De Rägebogefisch", adapted from the book also published in Italian with the title "Arcobaleno, the most beautiful fish of all the seas". Wedler's career began eleven years ago with a role in "Amateur Teens", presented at the ZFF. Over this period, she has already received the Swiss Film Award for Best Actress in 2018 and the Marcello Mastroianni Award, awarded to emerging actors and actresses, at last year's Venice Film Festival.
