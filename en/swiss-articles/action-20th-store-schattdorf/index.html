Action opens its 20th store in Switzerland in Schattdorf, Uri, on September 17, with 14 new local hires.

Action accelerates expansion in Switzerland with the opening of its 20th store. The new branch will be inaugurated on 17 September in Schattdorf, canton Uri, within the Tellpark shopping centre. The store will have an area of over 900 square meters and will bring local hiring to 14.

Action has only been present in Switzerland for a year and a half, but has already extended its network to all regions of the country. In addition to Schattdorf, the Dutch brand is present in Taverne and Losone in Ticino, and in Chur in the Graubünden. At the end of 2025, the company had 8 points of sale, and since then the network has more than doubled, reaching the current 20 branches.

The company founded in 1993 in Enkhuizen, the Netherlands, has not yet communicated a precise numerical target for further development in Switzerland. At European level, Action now has over 3,400 stores in 15 countries, making the company one of the most dynamic players in the non-food discount sector.

Operational details

Action accelerates and opens its 20th store

The opening of the 20th Action store in Switzerland represents a further step forward for the Dutch company in the Swiss market. Action's presence in all regions of the country, including the cantons of Uri, Ticino, and Grisons, demonstrates its ability to adapt to the diverse needs of local consumers.

The new branch in Schattdorf, with its 14 new hires, will help consolidate Action's presence in Switzerland and create new job opportunities for the local population. Action's expansion in Switzerland is part of a broader strategy aimed at strengthening its position in the European non-food discount market.

A growing market

Action, founded in 1993 in the Netherlands, has rapidly expanded its presence in Europe, with over 1,600 stores in 10 countries. In Switzerland, the company began its adventure in 2017, opening its first store in Bern. Since then, it has recorded steady growth, with a 20% year-on-year increase in sales.

"Switzerland is a strategic market for us," said Action's CEO, Heiko van der Schee. "The diversity of the regions and the needs of local consumers offer us a unique opportunity to test and implement new market strategies."

Adapting to local needs

Action has demonstrated a notable ability to adapt to the diverse needs of local consumers. For example, in the canton of Uri, known for its active and sporty population, Action introduced a line of products dedicated to outdoor activities and sports. In Ticino, where the population is more international, Action expanded its offer of typical Italian food and beverages.
