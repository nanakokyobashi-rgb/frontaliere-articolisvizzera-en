Canton Ticino, historic news for cross-border apprentices: the G permit is worth more than before

Switzerland, increasingly closer to cross-border apprentices, has brought about a significant reform in the procedure for renewing the residence permit for these young people. Until now, cross-border apprentices, including those attending basic vocational schools, were forced to renew their G permit every twelve months. This procedure, defined as "cumbersome and anachronistic" by Schmid himself, Federal Minister for the Confederation, has caused many inconveniences and delays in the training and working careers of these young people.

The new reform, introduced in the Canton of Ticino, has brought about a significant change. Cross-border apprentices can now renew their G permit at a later time, without the need to appear in person at the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM). The new system involves sending a simple online request, which will be automatically reviewed and approved in just 24-48 hours.

The novelty is not limited to this, however. The renewed G permit, even following its second authorization, continues to be valid for a period of five years, instead of the previous two. This change represents an important recognition for the training and professionalism acquired during cross-border apprenticeships.

To make this innovation even more attractive, Switzerland has also made some rules more flexible. For example, cross-border apprentices can now apply for the G permit also in Switzerland, without this entailing the obligation to present a medical certificate or an insurance policy. Furthermore, Switzerland has extended the training offer for cross-border apprentices, allowing them to access training and work programs in public and private institutions.
