Ticino: G Permit Now More Valuable Than Ever for Frontier Apprentices (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 11 July 2026

The National Council approved the measure extending the validity of the work permit G to cover the entire duration of the internship.

Context

In short, the National Council approved a landmark measure for border apprentices in Switzerland. The new law (LStrI) extends the validity of the work permit G, which allows you to carry out work during the entire traineeship period, even if you have not moved from another Member State. About 700 cross-border apprentices, especially in Ticino, will now be able to benefit from this scheme.

Key facts:

  • What: The extension of the validity of the G work permit for cross-border apprentices.
  • When: Approval of the measure by the National Council.
  • Where: Canton Ticino.
  • Who: Frontier apprentices.
  • Amount: Not specified, but the measure could introduce economic savings for companies and apprentices, reducing the number of bureaucratic steps and the cost of the G permit.

The decision is the consequence of a long parliamentary process, which led to the approval of the amendment to the LStrI with a majority of 132 votes in favour and 59 against. This law, which will come into force immediately, will allow young cross-border workers to work in Switzerland for the duration of their apprenticeship, without the need to renew the G permit after each traineeship period.

Operational details

Canton Ticino, historic news for border apprentices: the G permit is worth more than before

Switzerland, increasingly close to cross-border apprentices, has made a significant reform in the procedure for renewing the residence permit for these young people. Until now, cross-border apprentices, including those attending basic vocational schools, were forced to renew their G permit every twelve months. This procedure, described as "cumbersome and anachronistic" by Schmid himself, Federal Minister for the Confederation, has caused many inconveniences and delays in the training and career of these young people.

The new reform, introduced in the Canton of Ticino, has brought about a significant change. Border apprentices can now renew their G permit at a later date, without the need to appear in person at the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM). The new system involves sending a simple online request, which will be reviewed and approved automatically in just 24-48 hours.

The novelty is not limited to this, however. The renewed G permit, even after its second authorization, continues to be valid for a period of five years, instead of the previous two. This change represents an important recognition for the training and professionalism acquired during cross-border apprenticeships.

Key points

NEW OPPORTUNITIES FOR BORDER APPRENTICES IN CANTON TISZENTE

The new regime for border apprentices in Ticino, with the introduction of the G Permit, represents a historic turning point for the Canton's youth work sector. This permit, valid for the entire duration of the apprenticeship, which can vary from two to four years, will allow border apprentices to free themselves from the need to renew their residence permit every twelve months. This decision was taken by the National Council after a long parliamentary process and responded to the needs of companies and young people in training.
