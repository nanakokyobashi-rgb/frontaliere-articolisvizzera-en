Canton Valais Scholarships: how to get them (cross-border guide)

Di Redazione Frontaliere Ticino · Published on 6 September 2026

Guide to scholarships in the canton of Valais: general requirements, where to look for information and how to apply to the cantonal authorities.

Context

In a nutshell

  • The Valais provides scholarships and study loans with annual deadlines
  • Requirements and maximum amounts change every year
  • The competent cantonal office is the main point of contact

Key facts

  • What: Scholarships and study loans in the canton of Valais
  • Who pays: Cantonal Authority of Valais
  • When: Deadlines defined annually
  • Where: Competent cantonal office
  • Requirements: Economic situation, residence, academic merit (generally)

The canton of Valais, like every Swiss canton, has its own system of scholarships and study loans. Financial resources for student support are managed at the cantonal level, which means that the requirements, maximum amounts payable and deadlines for submitting applications vary from other cantons and are updated annually.

The Swiss cantonal system of study support

Scholarships in Switzerland do not follow a uniform federal standard. Each canton independently defines the access criteria, the maximum amounts, the duration of the subsidy and the methods of submitting applications. The Valais, as part of this federalist system, has exclusive competence over its policy of support for young people in training.

This means that a student who resides in Valais and attends a recognized educational institution can apply directly to the competent cantonal office.

Operational details

General requirements that influence access

The Swiss cantons, in defining their criteria for access to scholarships, generally consider some common factors. The economic factor is the main criterion: the family's financial situation determines eligibility. Each canton sets an income or wealth threshold below which it is assumed that the student is entitled to a grant. Valais, like other cantons, applies specific economic capacity tests. If you want to understand how your tax situation affects potential eligibility, consult the tax return guide. Since these values change annually (often in relation to inflation and the cost of living), it is essential to consult the cantonal office directly to know the current thresholds.

The academic factor is considered by almost all cantons: students must have sufficient school results. It is not necessary to obtain excellent grades, but it is important to demonstrate a serious commitment and consistent progress in the course of study.

The residence factor represents another crucial aspect. Normally, the student must reside in the canton and be a Swiss citizen or, if foreign, have specific residence requirements depending on international agreements and cantonal regulations.

Finally, the study level factor: Valais supports students in different educational cycles, from those in vocational training to university students. However, the amounts and methods may differ depending on the type of training followed.

Key points

Step-by-step procedure to find specific information

Since the specific details (exact amounts, precise deadlines, complete list of required documents) change every year and are only available from the cantonal authorities, here is how to proceed.

The first step is to identify the exact name and contact of the Valais office responsible for scholarships. This office may vary in name and organizational structure, but it generally depends on the Department of Education or a similar authority. Searching on the official website of the canton of Valais will provide the email address and phone number.

Once the office is identified, request the official documents for the current academic year. These documents will include the official application form, a detailed description of the current requirements, maximum amounts per student category, submission deadlines, and a complete list of documents to be attached (income certificates, bank statements, report cards, etc.).

The required documents generally include tax certificates, documentation on the family's economic situation, and documents proving enrollment in a recognized educational institution. Valais may also require a declaration of the family's income and assets. Deadlines are annual and communicated by the cantonal authorities: it is essential to respect the communicated terms, as late applications may not be considered.

After submission, the cantonal office will review the application and communicate the decision. This process can take several weeks.

Part-time work during studies

Many students supplement the support from scholarships with part-time work. If you are interested in this option, consult job listings in Switzerland to find positions compatible with study schedules.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the maximum amounts of a scholarship in the canton of Valais?
The maximum amounts payable change each year in relation to economic factors and cantonal political decisions. It is not possible to provide a fixed amount without consulting the current rules of the competent cantonal office. We recommend that you contact the Valais Department of Education directly to find out the amounts in force for the current academic year.
When should scholarship applications be submitted in Valais?
The deadlines for submitting applications are set annually by the Valais authorities. Generally applications must be submitted within a defined time window (often before the start of the academic year). We recommend that you contact the relevant office at least 2-3 months before the start of your studies to receive the exact timetable and detailed instructions.
Who can apply for a scholarship in Valais?
Generally, students enrolled in recognized training courses (vocational training, high school, universities, research institutes) who reside in the canton of Valais and who are in a financial situation of need can apply. Foreign nationals with specific residency requirements (according to agreements with their country of origin) may be eligible according to local rules. Contact the office to find out if your specific situation is covered.
What is the difference between a scholarship and a study loan in Valais?
A scholarship is a contribution that does not have to be returned: it is awarded on the basis of economic need and merit. A study loan, on the other hand, is money that the student receives but must repay (often with subsidized interest rates) once they finish their training. Valais offers both options, and students can be eligible for one, the other, or both.
If I move to study in another canton, will my Valais scholarship remain valid?
No, generally the scholarship is linked to the canton of residence and the canton where you study. If you move to another canton, you should contact the office of the new canton to apply according to local rules. Some cantons have reciprocity agreements for transfers, but these vary. Check with both offices before making the transfer.
