In the canton of Appenzell Outermost, as in all of Switzerland, child custody is the responsibility of the cantonal authorities. The Canton directly manages some services and regulates those offered by private entities, guaranteeing quality standards and economically sustainable access. There are three main forms of custody available, each with specific characteristics and schedules designed for different family needs.

The forms of case available

Nurseries welcome children from a few months up to three years of age, offering services in dedicated facilities with qualified staff and educational programming.

Operational details

Progressive rates and cantonal subsidies

In the canton of Appenzello Esterno, as in the other Swiss cantons, the fees for nursery school and day families are calculated progressively on the basis of the gross income of the family. This mechanism ensures that the economic burden is proportionate to the contributory capacity of the nucleus. Income brackets determine how much a family pays for the service: higher incomes pay higher percentages, while low-income families benefit from reduced rates. The Canton sets the income thresholds annually and publishes official tables used by all authorized facilities.

Additional cantonal subsidies further reduce costs for nuclei below specific income thresholds. These subsidies are not universal and automatic, but must be formally requested from the competent office of the Canton with a declaration of income and assets. Allocation criteria include annual gross income, declared assets, and number of dependent children.

Federal and Cantonal Tax Deductions

Those who incur childcare expenses may deduct these amounts from their annual tax return. At the federal level (direct federal tax), deductions are limited to maximum amounts fixed and indexed each year. Key points

How to access services: practical step-by-step procedure

The first step is to consult the cantonal information portal or contact the competent office for childcare and obtain the updated list of authorized facilities in your municipality or region of Appenzello Esterno. Many services, especially public kindergartens, have waiting lists; signing up early increases your chances of getting a seat. Contact the facilities of interest directly for information on seat availability, possible start dates, opening hours (pre-holiday hours, summer closures), registration and deposit amounts, and estimated monthly rates. Also ask what documents are required at the time of registration, generally certificates of vaccination and health history of the child.

Application for cantonal subsidies

Once a service has been assigned, promptly contact the cantonal welfare or assistance office to apply for the subsidy (name varies by canton). The application usually requires: proof of income (annual pay slip, tax return for the previous two years), proof of assets (bank statements, savings policies), copy of the contract signed with the custody structure, identity document of the main applicant. Frequently Asked Questions
What custodial services work in the Outer Appenzell canton?
Three main forms: kindergartens for children 0-3 years old with qualified staff, authorized day families (tagesmütter) that offer time flexibility, after-school for schoolchildren until the end of compulsory schooling. The Canton regulates all forms through quality standards, square footage, staff training, opening hours and maximum costs.
How are fees calculated based on income?
The rates are progressive: the Canton establishes annual gross income bands, and each band corresponds to a percentage or amount of the monthly rate. Modest incomes pay lower percentages, high incomes pay more. All authorized facilities apply the same official tables published by the Canton.
How to apply for Cantonal Custody Allowances?
Contact the competent office of the Canton (social benefits or assistance) with a written request. Attach proof of income (previous tax return), assets, copy of the contract with the structure, identity. Subsidies are not automatic, but assessed on a case-by-case basis under income thresholds set by the Canton. Approval time: 4-8 weeks.
What rights does parental leave in Switzerland offer?
Maternity: 14 weeks at 80% of gross salary (compensation for loss of federal earnings). Paternity: 2 weeks fully paid. Both are federal rights paid independently of the canton of residence, financed by contributions paid during work.
How do I enroll my child in mandatory health insurance?
LAMal is mandatory within 3 months of birth or arrival in Switzerland. Contact a private health insurance company with the child's birth certificate. The cashier communicates the monthly premium, which varies by canton, age and choice of deductible. Many cantons subsidize low income premiums: check with the relevant cantonal office.
