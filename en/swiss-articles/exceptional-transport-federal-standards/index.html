Operational details

What changes in practice&#10;&#10; Uniform regulations at the federal level represent a significant change for the exceptional transport sector. With the issuance of the departmental ordinance, all private providers wishing to offer escort services will have to meet the same federal standards, creating a common national reference framework. A logistics company moving exceptional loads from Bern to Lugano will be assured that the ATE escorts encountered along the route will meet the same training and competence criteria. At the same time, private escort service providers will have to comply with uniform regulations, simplifying their operations and reducing the administrative costs associated with complying with multiple regional constraints.&#10;&#10; ### Efficiency as a central element&#10;&#10; The Zurich pilot project has demonstrated that private providers are able to perform this task more efficiently than the public structure. This means shorter waiting times for carriers, less local road congestion, and improved circulation of special vehicles. Key points

How to participate in the consultation

The federal consultation remains open until November 20, 2026. Anyone wishing to participate — public bodies, trade associations, private escort service providers, transport companies, citizens, and industry professionals — still has several months to submit their feedback. Submission procedures will be communicated through the Confederation's official channels.

Implications for transport operators

Heavy transport operators will need to carefully monitor the developments of this consultation. Once the new legislation enters into force, all escort services must comply with uniform federal standards. This means that heavy transport companies will need to verify that their escort providers meet the federal criteria, potentially train internal staff if they offer in-house services, and adapt contractual processes to the uniform requirements.

Next steps

Once the consultation closes on November 20, 2026, DETEC will process the feedback and finalize the departmental ordinance. An exact timetable for the entry into force of the new rule has not yet been communicated, but a few months typically pass between the close of the consultation and the actual application of the regulation. In the meantime, those operating in the sector — escort service providers and logistics companies — should monitor the status of the consultation process through official federal channels, or look for transport sector job opportunities if the new rules create a demand for certified heavy transport escorts.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who is the exceptional transport escort (ATE)?
The ate is a specialized operator who drives the escort vehicle for exceptional transports. It is responsible for alerting other road users and ensuring the smoothest possible journey. It must be trained personnel and, with the new federal standards issued by the DATEC, it must comply with uniform criteria throughout Switzerland.
When do the new federal standards go into effect?
The consultation on uniform regulation is open from 19 August to 20 November 2026. After the consultation closes, DATEC will process the feedback received and finalize the departmental ordinance. The effective date will be communicated later, usually a few months after the end of the consultation.
Why does DATEC switch from police to private suppliers?
The Zurich Cantonal Police pilot project has shown that private suppliers ensure better operational efficiency and significantly lighten police forces, allowing them to focus on investigative and control tasks that are more critical to public safety.
Who can participate in the federal consultation?
The consultation is open to public bodies, trade associations, private suppliers of escort services, exceptional transport companies, citizens and professionals in the sector. The opinions must be submitted in accordance with the procedures communicated by the official channels of the Confederation by 20 November 2026.
