Operational details

Apprenticeship and vocational training in the Canton of Glarus

The main objective of apprenticeship in the Canton of Glarus is to help apprentices develop their skills and find a stable job. Apprentices can choose from several vocational training options, including theoretical training and practical training.

The Federal Apprenticeship Act (LFA) of 1964 states that apprentices must be trained in a work activity and receive adequate theoretical and practical training. According to the Cantonal Apprenticeship Law of the Canton of Glarus of 2018, apprentices must be trained in a work activity for at least 30 hours per week.

Apprentices can choose from several vocational training options, including theoretical training and practical training. Theoretical training can be carried out at a vocational training institution, such as the Vocational Training Institute of Lucerne (IFP Lucerne), while practical training can be carried out at a company or firm.

Theoretical training is an essential component of apprenticeship. Apprentices can choose from several theoretical training options, including vocational training courses, language courses and computer courses. Key points

Apprenticeship and vocational training in the Canton of Glarus

To find an apprenticeship position in the Canton of Glarus, apprentices can contact the cantonal authorities or visit the Canton's official website. Once an apprenticeship position has been found, apprentices must sign an apprenticeship contract setting out the conditions of their training. The salary of the apprentice may vary depending on the type of training and the sector in which he works.

According to cantonal regulations, apprentices can choose from different types of training, including a traditional apprenticeship of 2-3 years, a higher apprenticeship of 1-2 years and a vocational apprenticeship of 1 year. The choice of the type of training depends on individual needs and job opportunities in the chosen sector.
